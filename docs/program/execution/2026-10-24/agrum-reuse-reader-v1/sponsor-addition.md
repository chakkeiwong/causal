## aGrUM requires separate identification, support and counterfactual contracts

The pinned aGrUM/pyAgrum 3.2.0 source is a concrete candidate for a worker
covering graphical models and causal queries. Its existing discovery and
identification reviews remain bounded source evidence. The added review
traces formula division into tensor arithmetic and distinguishes ordinary
zero-sum normalization from conditional-probability-table normalization.
A present symbolic formula cannot establish support for every target stratum.
The proposal retains a reference choice and conditional C++/Python integration.
\citep{exec24_agrum_source,exec24_agrum_choice}

An authored support example leaves an intervention mean between one quarter
and three quarters. Filling an unsupported ratio with zero or a uniform
value selects a number without identifying it. Another exact example shows
that replacing a joint posterior over roots by separate posterior marginals
changes counterfactual success from one to one half. At 1,000 accounts,
80 USD per success and 30,000 USD campaign cost, illustrative net value
changes from 50,000 to 10,000 USD. These are mathematical witnesses and
native test requirements, not observed package failures.
\citep{exec24_agrum_choice}
