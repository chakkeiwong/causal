# Public-evidence design review — 12 October 2026

This is an author review of six proposal designs. It does not certify a final protocol, independent review, held-out score or fitted causal result. Each design binds the available primary documentation and separates a local/generated route from an optional externally constrained route. Final implementations, review of their actual code, resource pilots and prospective registrations remain necessary before any final empirical claim.

**P1: formulation and clocks.** The eight briefs cover eligibility ATE, participation complier LATE, an unsupported contact effect, a leaked pre-call feature, conditional prediction, an ambiguous business question, an unsupported duration intervention and a valid post-call description. Eligibility and participation are different interventions. The covariate-averaged IV numerator/denominator ratio targets a population-weighted complier effect under the stated assumptions, not every household's participation effect. The review explicitly retains exclusion failure and unmeasured confounding alternatives. The inherited cross-sectional control list is not declared proven pretreatment information. The public UCI archive does not supply the untreated sampling and assignment design needed for the proposed contact contrast.

The actual UCI names file explicitly says duration is unknown before a call. It also identifies the last-contact-inclusive campaign count, the special pdays code and the smaller file's overlap with the full data. These distinctions are now in the clock/split policy. Macro aggregates and contact scheduling fields require a release/decision clock and are excluded by default. The deterministic baseline works on supplied normalized fields; it does not parse arbitrary business prose. Ten behavior checks pass, and eight exposed reference briefs replay as expected. These are development conformance checks, not model generalization or live LLM accuracy. The scoring rubric has noncompensable critical errors so a fluent explanation cannot offset a leaked feature or invented causal claim.

**P2: public panels and generated continuity tests.** Two additional official FDIC dictionaries were acquired through the fixed public-source collector. Inspected fields include CERT, REPDTE, ASSET and DEP for financial records, and CERT, YEAR, BRNUM, UNINUMBR, DEPSUMBR and STALPBR for SOD. The financial dictionary defines CERT as the institution identifier and REPDTE as period end; SOD provides branch-key candidates and terse field labels. Those labels do not prove monetary scaling, constant economic perimeter or historical immutability. The current exact dictionaries were acquired after the source cutoff. Observation year and an earlier landing-page update are not evidence of an exact pre-cutoff release hash.

The proposal therefore freezes a bounded conditional extract contract and a fully specified generated primary design. It does not invent a public extract, dollar scaling or merger-adjusted history. The generated route studies continuity corruption and dependent errors at known slope 0.4; the inherited macro study remains available. Dictionary inspection is complete at the selected-field level. Monetary scaling, archival release identity and history mapping remain prerequisites only for their corresponding FDIC empirical claims. This is an acceptable proposal-design disposition, not final public-panel qualification.

**P3: randomized effects and matched baselines.** The project law has superpopulation ATE 50 because the heterogeneous term 10X1 has mean zero. A sample's average potential-outcome contrast is a distinct target. Known randomization, observed information and nuisance folds are matched across estimators. Both potential outcomes are reserved for the evaluator. Observational assignment, weak support and nonlinear nuisance variants are declared stress cases, with the true assignment law available only where the scenario permits it. Wald uncertainty is asymptotic, not a universal exact guarantee. All failed/unavailable intervals count in the coverage denominator. A Monte Carlo interval merely containing 0.95 is explicitly insufficient for qualification. Numeric equality tolerances, implementation identities and resource pilots must still enter the final prospective protocol; this design cannot substitute for that protocol.

**P4: sequential servicing.** Reward depends on the next state, so actions can influence future utility. The observed dropout process occurs after action and before reward; its prefix weights must include the censoring probability for the reward being evaluated. The stated law makes censoring conditionally independent of the next innovation; the adverse case deliberately breaks that restriction. Policy selection uses a separate training sample, and the chosen policy is frozen before evaluation. The comparator set is finite and does not establish universal policy optimality. A large independent simulator run supplies a numerical policy-value reference with its own Monte Carlo uncertainty, not exact analytic truth. Weight instability and failures remain visible. Bank action feasibility and hidden state are not inferred from this generated construction.

**P5: graphs and identity.** The three displayed Gaussian DAGs have identical covariance and no unshielded collider. In the stated sufficient linear family, effects of X on Y are 0 or 0.48. An associational slope of 0.48 does not select the generating orientation. The intervention contrast of 0.96 follows only under the first DAG, and an intervention sample is different information from observational rows. The algebra check reconstructs each covariance directly, with no simulated significance claim.

The linkage law has outcome support [70,160] and ATE 30. Outcome-dependent corruption violates the convenient missing-at-random story by design. The proposal compares exact/fuzzy/candidate-set approaches, retains missing and wrong matches, and requires analysis exports to omit evaluator truth. The truth join is an explicitly privileged benchmark, not an ordinary deployable method. The final code must freeze the identifier generator/edit distribution and demonstrate physical separation of exported analysis fields and hidden labels. Broad claims about real bank entity resolution remain unsupported.

**P6: agent workflow evaluation.** Task families span problem formulation, clocks, diagrams, estimation eligibility, evidence and typed tools. A deterministic builder now freezes 24 development and 48 reserved hypothetical task variants. Development/reserved template groups are disjoint; each template has two context variants, which must remain clustered rather than being counted as two independent semantic discoveries. Reference interpretations are stored separately and omitted from task exports. These cases are unevaluated, authored and not independently adjudicated. The declared random seeds apply to future perturbations, not to this deterministic build. Public benchmarks remain potentially exposed to model training. The P1 router is only one narrow baseline. Retrieval, fixed templates and optional live arms have different capabilities which must be compared under explicit budgets. Refusal, clarification, critical errors and all-request availability are measured separately. The current author is not represented as two independent adjudicators. No backend credential, paid API call or live-model performance result is supplied by a task-design receipt. Offline design can proceed before those optional live-study inputs exist.

All six task contracts are satisfied as designs: relevant evidence and ambiguity are inspected, local alternatives are specified and the remaining execution requirements are explicit. The tasks do not claim to finish Q3 or the manuscript repairs. Numerical prototypes and final studies remain separate work whose conclusions must depend on executed results rather than these design descriptions.
