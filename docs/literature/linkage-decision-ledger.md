# Weak-linkage inference: source and method decisions

9 October 2026; publication cutoff 7 October 2026. No new publication
or current-source search claim is made. All five sources already have
preserved genuine PDFs, bibliographic records, hashes and inspected anchors.

- **staiger1994**: Weak first-stage limits and residual-test inversion; source PDF pages 17–19 / printed pages 15–17 visually reread. Motivation only; does not establish this linkage projection.
- **newey1986**: Theorem 2 mixing/moment and fitted-parameter/bandwidth restrictions; PDF pages 7–8 / printed pages 5–6 visually reread. Supports the observable covariance approximation, not inverse-rank robustness.
- **slawski2025**: Section 2.1 Audit Sample: independent inclusion and accurate labels; inspected lines 225–266 in retained excerpt. Model assumptions differ from paired two-portfolio histories.
- **rambachan2023**: Section 2.5 equations (9)–(11): exact Gaussian and uniform-asymptotic coverage distinguished. The continuation reconstructs a projection inclusion proof, not the source’s optimized inference.
- **guha2022**: Previously inspected Sections 2.1–2.3: joint Bayesian likelihood and linkage/outcome feedback; substantive challenger with different experiment and assumptions.

The sum/difference projection is selected for a transparent coverage proof,
finite analytic topology and reuse of the full calendar covariance. The
candidate-mean rectangle is an executed precision challenger. Joint
ellipsoidal projection and candidate-specific residual tests are derived
as alternatives requiring further optimization/inference work. No unseen
theorem is credited with resolving these requirements. Audit correctness,
pilot invariance and the per-origin linkage law remain substantive limits.

The initial wide candidate-coordinate pilot and all its code are archived.
The transform was chosen before final draws; error allocation, seeds and
regimes remained fixed. The later finite-variance guard repair reruns the
identical registered draws and preserves the earlier outputs.
