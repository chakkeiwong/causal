\hypertarget{master-program-v14-complete-coverage-with-independent-execution}{%
\section{Master program V14: complete coverage with independent
execution}\label{master-program-v14-complete-coverage-with-independent-execution}}

12 October 2026. Literature cutoff: 7 October 2026.

The program is unfinished. The present working monograph has 375 pages,
but the three requested repairs remain incomplete: the software,
LLM/agent and integration parts occupy physical spans of 42, 42 and 12
pages. None of the ten original capability gaps is fully qualified.
Twenty-three of 48 packages have recorded outcomes, including negative
and inconclusive results. Those outcomes must be interpreted within
their stated scope.

The immediate planning defect was real. The previous controller required
the entire software census before accepting any family comparison, every
family comparison before reuse decisions, and the entire reuse package
before an individual adapter. Its fixed priority repeatedly selected
SW01. Saying that independent work could proceed did not change these
executable barriers.

This revision replaces 16 broad dependency edges across nine unresolved
packages. It gives source reviews, individual reuse decisions,
commercial comparisons, chapter development, adapters, registered
trials, scientific arguments and external intake their own
prerequisites. The final package receipts remain substantive acceptance
decisions; completing a small operation does not close a scientific gap
or award product acceptance.

The detailed \href{comprehensive-v14/gap-audit.md}{evidence audit},
\href{comprehensive-v14/work-graph.json}{machine work graph} and
\href{comprehensive-v14/dependency-review.json}{review of every old
dependency} are part of this program. The PDF includes the full audit as
an appendix. Current execution uses
\texttt{python3\ tools/program.py\ status}, \texttt{next} and
\texttt{check}. The historical controller and README are bound to old
receipts and are preserved. Mission Control and the canonical state
point to this revision.

\hypertarget{the-standard-and-the-four-completion-decisions}{%
\subsection{The standard and the four completion
decisions}\label{the-standard-and-the-four-completion-decisions}}

The deliverable is a connected causal-banking monograph, a reusable
product proposal and evidence for the capabilities it actually claims.
It must cover four journeys: funding responses; sequential servicing;
discovery followed by an intervention query; and trustworthy
observations. Its six research/product streams remain identification and
learning, dependent time series and panels, longitudinal and
controlled-state decisions, discovery and uncertain observations,
reusable operations and computation, and financial validation and
economics.

Each claimed use must connect the business decision and information
clock to a causal model and assumptions, identification or an identified
set, estimation and learning, uncertainty and diagnostics, substantive
alternatives, numerical error and monetary consequences. The named
FinMarketing monograph is a standard for a developed scholarly argument.
It is neither a page-padding target nor evidence that a new integration
already works.

Every scholarly citation needs its genuine PDF under
\texttt{docs/papers/}, named
\texttt{Title\ FirstAuthorSurname(year).pdf}. Every cited source,
including official software documentation, needs identity, version/date,
saved bytes, SHA-256, inspected technical anchors and supported claims.
Metadata establishes identity and discovery only. Unavailable originals
stay in the omissions register; an author-written summary cannot replace
them. Sources retrieved after the cutoff must have their applicable
historical revision established or be reported as a separate dated
delta. Banking arithmetic must reproduce.

The four decisions are deliberately separate. Author completion requires
a reviewed argument, evidence and limitations, and can include negative
results. Capability qualification requires valid and useful output in a
declared model, sampling and information regime. External acceptance
requires authentic independent, bank, consumer and operator evidence.
Investment/adoption requires actual decision-value, cost and owner
acceptance. A source inspection, compiled PDF, passing API test or
completed plan cannot supply the later decisions.

\hypertarget{what-the-audit-found}{%
\subsection{What the audit found}\label{what-the-audit-found}}

The appendix accounts for every contractual cell, task, requirement,
chapter, journey and original gap. It reads the existing receipts
against their actual limitations, rather than treating a package label
as a broad success.

Thirty software cells are partially inspected; 72 await individual
review and none is closed. The catalog contains 115 rows including
aliases and umbrella leads, so it is not a count of distinct qualified
engines. All 17 commercial cells remain incomplete. Selected technical
material, including some Stata material, does not yet provide a full
edition-specific competitive comparison.

All 14 LLM tasks have a bounded primary evidence map. That map inspected
selected passages of 26 papers; 82 relevant and 31 adjacent candidates
remain deferred. Four further omissions record search, adoption,
qualification and external evidence limits. The bounded top-25 searches
do not establish saturation. The existing proposal/diagram validators do
not implement general identification or full PAG recognition. The
offline prototype maps a narrow static population ATE workflow; it is
not live causal discovery or longitudinal policy inference. The live
study still lacks exact approved models, final sizes/thresholds and five
authentic input classes.

Integration has stronger local evidence than its old blocked status
suggested: nine advertised operation/method scopes, eleven scientific
parity cases and nine local fault cases have been exercised. These are
still narrow fixed operations. The tested protocol is MCP 2025-11-25
with SDK 1.27.0; modern 2026-07-28 support, general methods,
cross-language execution, actual consumer conformance and
target-platform qualification are open. Eighteen workspace directory
entries were dispositioned from selected read-only observations; that
does not establish exhaustive call-chain or runtime conformance.

All 22 chapter allocations remain partial. Even before excluding
material that does not qualify as substantive prose, the physical
shortfalls against the nominal minima are at least 58 software, 54 LLM
and 28 integration pages. The task is to develop the missing argument,
comparisons and examples; these are lower bounds on missing length, not
a writing quota or accepted measurement.

Four scientific successor arguments remain unresolved. R01 and R03
successes are restricted to their declared information and policy laws.
R02/R04/R05 negative findings remain active evidence. None of the twelve
authentic external input categories has been supplied. No bank truth,
independent sign-off, actual prices, target service levels or measured
ROI can be manufactured by this agent.

\hypertarget{work-lane-1-software-evidence-and-scientific-comparisons}{%
\subsection{Work lane 1: software evidence and scientific
comparisons}\label{work-lane-1-software-evidence-and-scientific-comparisons}}

The 102 source cells are independently addressable. For each, inspect
the existing search record and candidate identities; complete
complementary primary routes and relevant citation/repository chaining;
record cutoff, duplicates, inclusion/exclusion and bounded stopping
rules; acquire genuine originals; inspect exact technical behavior; and
write a reviewed disposition with alternatives and limitations. A cell
with no eligible candidate needs a documented search. An inaccessible
essential source prevents its claim from closing, while other cells
continue.

The 17 area comparisons begin from already inspected evidence. Their
specific questions are in the audit: graph classes, identification
algorithms, estimands, sampling units, nuisance learning,
survival/competing events, policies and logging, cohort/time
aggregation, donor uncertainty, instruments/common shocks, interference,
mediation/sensitivity, transport/missingness, latent states, observation
truth and complete computation. Each comparison needs explained
assumptions, diagnostics, a banking example, substantive alternatives
and typed failure behavior. It must distinguish documentation, inspected
implementation, executed behavior and scientifically qualified
inference.

Final review of an area waits only for that area's needed
language/source dispositions. Commercial analysis is a separate lane, so
missing a vendor manual does not stop a noncommercial area review.
Discovery evidence can feed discovery chapters and one adapter while
panel or longitudinal searches continue.

\hypertarget{work-lane-2-reuse-decisions-and-actual-engine-paths}{%
\subsection{Work lane 2: reuse decisions and actual engine
paths}\label{work-lane-2-reuse-decisions-and-actual-engine-paths}}

Review every catalog row. Confirm whether it is a package, alias, family
lead, generic infrastructure or vendor offering. Choose adopt,
integrate, port, reference, defer or reject, explaining
target/uncertainty semantics, evidence, license/permission, dependency
burden, alternatives, maintenance responsibility, cost and revisit
trigger. A port additionally needs a behavioral oracle and
numerical/statistical equivalence plan. Mere source availability does
not grant a safe integration decision.

The first candidate paths are pcalg in R, y0 in Python, Tetrad in Java
and the grf native C++ core with its R/native boundary stated
explicitly. These are candidate paths selected from existing evidence,
not approved integrations. Their source and license decisions are
independent. If one is rejected or unavailable, preserve that
disposition and review a scoped replacement; the other paths continue.
Shared underlying engines do not count as independent scientific
replications.

For each approved path, specify one concrete causal operation and
conversion contract inside this repository. Bind engine/build version,
dependencies, mathematical object types, coordinates, units, graph
endpoints, effect-set topology, uncertainty, errors and unsupported
cases. Execute deterministic reference and refusal checks against the
actual engine. Then register the matched qualification study before
producing its final results. Compare scientific validity, finite
availability, useful width/loss and failures before runtime; measure
setup, I/O, conversion and total workflow cost. A documentation
inspection or hand-written fixture cannot count as engine execution.

\subsection{Work lane 3: commercial

absent. Public price, quote-required price and measured TCO are
separate. No vendor contact, paid license or purchase is initiated by
this program without applicable authorization.

\subsection{Work lane 4: LLM/agent scholarship and

Develop each of the fourteen tasks in the audit, with particular depth

formulation validity, equivalence-aware graph quality, downstream effect
loss, useful output and complete cost. Final exact models, prices, sizes
and thresholds require the five real input packets and a reviewed
argument/design. Missing live inputs block live calls and performance
claims; they do not block honest scholarship or offline methodological
development.

\hypertarget{work-lane-5-integration-local-journeys-and-operation}{%

workspace directory's fit/no-fit/deferred disposition using permitted

altered units, invalid graph/set conversions, worker death,
resume/cancel, partial publication and complete-workflow accounting.
Local process recovery does not establish multi-host recovery or bank
service levels. Operator workload, scale, custody and accepted service
criteria enable target trials independently of manuscript finalization;
exact-release reconciliation remains required for external acceptance.

\hypertarget{scheduling-review-and-continuation}{%
\subsection{Scheduling, review and
continuation}\label{scheduling-review-and-continuation}}

There is one coordinator and no spawned editing agents. Logical
independence does not require concurrent agents. The machine graph
contains separately addressable evidence obligations, not hundreds of
sequential phases. Reusable cell, decision, trial and chapter templates
keep the work consistent. Final manuscript and product results remain
integrated.

The scheduler selects the least recently served lane that has ready
work, then uses attempt count, priority and stable ID within that lane.
The lanes are software, reuse, commercial, LLM, integration, research,
manuscript, external and release. The first cycle therefore combines
source review, a representative reuse decision, commercial acquisition,
an LLM task, integration design, scientific argument, chapter
development and intake/release preparation. A long source census cannot
monopolize continuation.

Use bounded units and short tool output. Before an operation, inspect
its exact contract and evidence. Do the work, review the result, repair
justified findings and run appropriate checks. Record a pass only for
that stated scope. A negative or inconclusive disposition can be
recorded without pretending a blocked trial ran. A review that rejects
reuse may complete the decision but cannot enable an adapter. An
essential missing source keeps the affected acceptance open.

After recording a result or a blocker, the controller immediately
reports the next eligible operation across all lanes. Continue it
without asking for phase or checkpoint permission. A checkpoint saves
state; it is not a stopping point. Stop dependent work only for a real
missing prerequisite, invalid argument, runtime/permission constraint or
absent authentic input, record the evidence and exact trigger to resume,
and continue unrelated work. If no operations are eligible, report the
remaining real blockers and needed input precisely.

For a long operation, \texttt{progress} records bounded inspected
evidence and the exact next action without awarding completion. It also
rotates lane selection, so an unfinished literature census cannot
monopolize the session. Saving this checkpoint is followed by continued
work in another eligible lane.

The controller validates and schedules work; it is not an autonomous
background writer, scientific prover or study runner. Commands do not
create evidence or send messages. Scientific registration remains
prospective and immutable; acceptance retains original substantive
validators and exact-release gates. Do not treat a ready argument task
as permission to run an unregistered final experiment. New scope after a
resolved outcome requires a reviewed successor with history preserved,
rather than rewriting its receipt.
