## Graph identification needs a precise information and reporting contract

Ananke's selected 0.5.0 distribution supplies a reference for explicit-graph
identification and a possible isolated comparator. Its identification,
supplied-model oracle, missing-data and average-effect interfaces answer
different questions. Source inspection also finds a conditional binary
reporting inconsistency: disabling log-odds reporting changes the point
calculation, while the selected bootstrap branch still transforms binary
replicates. This is a bounded static finding; no downloaded code was run.
\citep{exec24_ananke_source,exec24_ananke_choice}

A proposed worker needs typed graph and expression interchange, distinct
invalid/nonidentified/unsupported/failure states, matching point and interval
units, explicit missingness names and complete replicate accounting. The
selected distribution's conflicting license classifiers and dependency
environment remain unresolved. Direct adoption and a full port are deferred;
public known-law comparisons should precede expensive bank validation.
An illustrative 3,000 USD across 150 requested, 125 finite and 100 accepted
results gives 20, 24 and 30 USD on the respective denominators, not measured
economics or product acceptance.\citep{exec24_ananke_choice}
