but did not establish the requested scholarly or product standard. The root
FinMarketing TeX file is an include map, not the reference's substantive content.

methods with small illustrations. It did not develop a comparable argument.

This is a comparison of argument and evidence, not a target page count. The
reference contains extensive implemented applications. This commission is a
proposal; matching its standard requires comparably reconstructible proposed
construction and honest executed research demonstrations, not an invented
claim to have reproduced its production applications.

## Revised acceptance questions

1. Can a reader derive the principal estimator and explain the identification
   route, nuisance conditions, dependence law and financial quantity it targets?
2. Does a banking study progress from a decision and data clock through fitted
   estimation and uncertainty to a financially interpretable comparison?
3. Are POMDP estimation, temporal discovery and large-scale time-series analysis
   central product capabilities with concrete scientific and engineering work?
4. Are method choices supported by critical comparison of primary sources,
   including adverse cases and current research that may change the choice?
5. Can another repository reconstruct a valid request and interpret a returned
   result, including a missing-input or unsupported-identification outcome?
6. Do cost and scale claims identify what was measured and what remains a
   proposed bank qualification? Is an expert team composing open source a fair rival?
7. Do the final claims remain traceable to preserved originals, derivations or
   executed results, without treating traceability as proof of scientific quality?

## Program review before execution

The revised dependency graph preserves acquisition and software snapshots,
then couples scientific derivations to study design before the main rewrite.
Numerical studies are bounded CPU work. Source additions use the previously
approved collector or existing local PDFs. No new privilege, paid service,
bank data access, sibling mutation or package installation is required.

Risks are scientific: a long text could still be shallow; a hidden-state model
could hide confounding; a financial example could count principal as profit;
a scale benchmark could time arithmetic and imply estimator performance.
The acceptance questions require explicit derivations, counterexamples,
separate accounting and measured workload descriptions to address these risks.
Review must reopen a claim if an adverse result invalidates it. More favorable
random seeds must not replace completed study results.
