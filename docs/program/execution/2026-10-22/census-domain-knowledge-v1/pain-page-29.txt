                   Overall, the findings of this study support the application of these knowl-
               edge systems to causal modeling, specifically in chronic lower back pain.
               Through Knowledge Graphs, researchers can potentially model and capture
               the complex landscape of chronic lower back pain literature to improve the
               quality of context provided to LLMs. Furthermore, researchers and decision
               makers can leverage these knowledge systems to interface between the variety
               of different data types and study modalities characteristic of chronic lower
               back pain research, serving as a bridge between domain knowledge and data
               driven causal analysis.
