Causal banking: product proposal 18 October 2026 A shared causal-analysis service Product and investment proposal 18 October 2026 Working proposal for review 1 The decision this proposal supports A universal bank needs to know when a proposed action is likely to improve a financial out- come, which assumptions make that answer possible, and when the available information cannot distinguish competing decisions. The proposed investment is a small reusable causal-analysis service supporting four journeys: funding responses, sequential servicing, intervention queries after causal discovery, and the construction of trustworthy observations. Its immediate deliverable is a bounded prototype and an evidence-based recommendation about what to build, reuse or leave unsupported. The recommendation is to develop and compare a narrow set of these operations using public records, documented public experiments and generated cases with known truth. Existing local research supplies selected working constructions and substantial negative evidence. It does not yet establish a complete product or a positive return on deployment. Expensive bank experiments should follow a prototype result that makes their cost worth considering. The absence of bank data, institutional sign-off or production access does not prevent completion of the present proposal. (Causal banking proposal project, 2026j) This brief accompanies the 18 October 2026 working monograph, which has 1,060 pages and 751 audited citation records, including 197 genuine scholarly PDFs. The software, LLM/agent and integration comparisons have expanded substantially, but their coverage is still incomplete. Aggregate length does not establish completeness. Literature coverage retains the 7 October 2026 cutoff, with later acquisitions and uncertain historical versions explicitly distinguished. The recommendation remains provisional where evidence or comparison is incomplete. (Causal banking proposal project, 2026q) 1.1 The reusable product and its customer The first user is an analyst who can state a banking decision and inspect its assumptions. The reusable input records a target population, action and comparator, information available before the action, outcome and horizon, measurement units, observation process, causal restrictions and sampling unit. An offer, its receipt and the later balance are different variables. A measured association is not automatically the effect of changing an offer. The output keeps the target attached to its evidence. It may be an identified formula, an estimate with justified uncertainty, a set of compatible effects, an informative refusal or a failed computation. Unresolved graph endpoints, unbounded confidence sets and numerical failures require different responses. The library, command line and MCP interface should preserve these distinctions so that a downstream application can decide whether to act, ask for information or fall back to its existing workflow. A callable endpoint alone does not establish identification, useful precision or interoperability. (Causal banking proposal project, 2026g) The shared layer would organize three workspaces: intervention studies, sequential decisions and dynamic systems. The four banking journeys pass through these workspaces, with observation quality affecting all three. This organization avoids building a separate estimation stack for every 1 Causal banking: product proposal 18 October 2026 business team while retaining each team’s population, timing and economic account. The initial service would require analyst review of consequential scientific choices. No autonomous bank action is proposed. 1.2 A concrete first slice A useful first demonstration begins with a public or generated business brief, constructs an explicit question and alternative diagrams, checks the available identification route, and returns a result whose assumptions and information limits remain visible through the library, CLI and MCP. For a randomized offer, the first target can be the effect of assignment. A receipt effect requires additional compliance and exclusion assumptions. For a confounded observational table, the correct result may be a documented ambiguity rather than a number. The comparison must include an expert-designed template and a directly composed existing- package workflow. The new service earns its place only if the common representation and checks reduce consequential mistakes or repeated effort without discarding valid alternatives. A generated example can expose a wrong answer cheaply; it cannot establish how often the same failure occurs at a bank. 2 What the evidence currently supports 2.1 Funding and observations Earlier registered synthetic funding work found a passing construction under a demanding information design: known randomized action moments, truthful sampled links, known inclusion probabilities and independent calendars. Its six valid cells passed the prespecified minimum- coverage screen. False exogeneity, wrong inclusion probabilities and label errors retained adverse findings. This supports a selected procedure conditional on its information law, rather than a general correction for one dependent bank panel. (Causal banking proposal project, 2026f) The newer joint-dependence argument makes that limitation more explicit. A finite confidence- region construction can use declared nuisance bounds and conservative projection. Its representative precision is inadequate under the reviewed design. An exact persistence argument also shows why an unrestricted claim cannot obtain useful shrinking uncertainty merely by lengthening a single highly persistent series. The practical next choices are to narrow the dependence family, obtain justified additional information, or accept wider and possibly unbounded results. The new derivation has not been turned into a qualified numerical projection service. (Causal banking proposal project, 2026h) Observation linkage is part of the scientific problem. If different customer histories are obser- vationally compatible but imply different intervention values, more computation on the same summaries cannot choose among them. Public/generated linkage cases can test whether the prototype preserves this ambiguity. A later bank study would need representative truth labels and an explicit inclusion mechanism; labels with unknown selection do not establish the same result. (Causal banking proposal project, 2026i,h) 2.2 Servicing and learned policies Selected earlier servicing studies support fixed-policy inference in a restricted nonlinear family under truthful state audits, supplied historical action probabilities and independent customer trajectories. Their wrong-logger and information-leakage cases remain failures. They do not 2 Causal banking: product proposal 18 October 2026 establish the value of an unrestricted learned policy with hidden borrower state. (Causal banking proposal project, 2026k) A new argument handles estimated action and observation probabilities in a finite, prespecified history table with bounded rewards. It separates training from evaluation and makes the support and observation assumptions explicit. Exact calculations check its policy-value identities and counterexamples. Its conservative uncertainty can nevertheless exceed the complete target range at a representative sample size. The public servicing design uses continuous histories and unbounded Gaussian/quadratic quantities, so this finite-table theorem cannot simply be attached to that design. A useful next study needs a reviewed tighter argument, a separately declared bounded target, or a different experiment. It must preserve the failed predecessor rather than change the target after evaluation. (Causal banking proposal project, 2026l) 2.3 Discovery and agent support Graph discovery constrains possible relationships under stated assumptions. It may return an equivalence class whose members imply different interventions. The proposed service must retain that class and distinguish uncertainty in graph learning from uncertainty in an effect. Exact latent-risk examples in the current comparison exhibit identical observed Gaussian distributions with action effects of zero and three fifths. They prove nonidentification within a class containing both models; they do not enumerate the entire identified set. (Causal banking proposal project, 2026a) LLMs are proposed as aids for translating business language, finding missing timing definitions, suggesting diagrams and alternatives, and preparing auditable tool requests. A model-generated diagram is a candidate assumption, not observed causal truth. The evaluation must score false confidence, unsupported orientations, omitted alternatives and appropriate requests for information. Deterministic task fixtures and exact examples already support some checks; no live- model superiority or bank decision gain is established by the new task designs. A live comparison would require a frozen backend, held-out tasks, reference adjudication, costs and equally informed baselines. (Causal banking proposal project, 2026n) The retention argument is likewise a design result with a finite reference. Across ten allowed publication/collection orders, a stale-snapshot collector loses an artifact needed by a live request in three orders. A coordinated recheck against current roots avoids that failure in all ten, with nine successful publications and one safe rejection. This motivates pins, a live-root check and an explicit rule for stale publishers. It is not an estimated failure probability or an implemented storage collector. The prototype should test those semantics under realistic interruption and restart before an operating guarantee is proposed. (Causal banking proposal project, 2026r) 3.2 What Bayesian advice adds, and what it cannot justify A corrected University of Sao Paulo thesis, published before the cutoff, supplies a useful adverse comparison for LLM-assisted graph discovery. Its reported DiBS graph-distance results improve with a GPT prior on Sachs but worsen on MAGIC-7, MAGIC-ORIGINAL and the authored Summer case. A DeepSeek prior improves the Summer result while worsening the two smaller reported alternatives. These are the thesis’s tabulated means, not new experimental runs or significance tests. The integrated discussion retains its selected feedback experiments, omitted adverse trials and appendix reproducibility discrepancies. It therefore supports an experiment com- paring information channels, rather than a claim that LLM advice improves discovery universally. (Videira, 2026) Repeated model answers estimate response behavior under the query mechanism. A model can repeat a correct or an incorrect answer equally consistently. The exact examples in the monograph also show that restricting an edge model to legal graphs changes its marginal probabilities, and that a Bayesian feedback update is justified only by its stipulated likelihood. A proposed deposit-diagram assistant should preserve response frequencies, semantic categories, admitted graph support and the evidence for adviser accuracy separately. Adopt these design lessons now; defer executable DiBS, ancestral-graph or DSPy integration until pinned code, rights, prompts and experiment artifacts have been inspected.
