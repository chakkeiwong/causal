# V6.1 assessment against the commissioned standard

9 October 2026 (Asia/Shanghai); literature cutoff 7 October 2026.
This is a targeted comparative author assessment of the frozen V6.1 delivery.
It rereads the original user requests, benchmark excerpts, current arguments,
reviews and public result code. It is not a complete proof audit or independent
review. Exact input identities and the executed consumer probe are in
[the evidence record](remaining-gaps-v6.1-evidence.json).

## The required standard

The original 7 October request commissioned a thorough literature and current
open-source survey and product proposal, especially for large-scale time-series
analysis, POMDP estimation, causal discovery and automatic data organization.
It was to support other financial applications and an independent offering,
at the standard of FinMarketing. The later clarification named BayesFilter
and CIP and explicitly said the theoretical deficiencies preceded bank evidence.

The [seven acceptance questions](reference-gap-review.md),
[scholarly reset](scholarship-reset-2026-10-08.md) and
[theory map](../program/theory-map.md) make the requirement concrete:

1. A connected, critical scientific survey. Explain each important method's
   target, assumptions, derivation, limitations and substantive alternatives,
   including the reason to select one method for a banking question.
2. A reconstructible mathematical chain: bank decision and information clock;
   structural/observed law; identification or an explicit ambiguity witness;
   estimator and learning remainder; sampling and numerical uncertainty;
   diagnostics; financial quantity; public operation and result.
3. Sustained treatment of the central connections: graphs and instruments;
   identification and causal ML; longitudinal interventions, state-space
   likelihoods and partially observed decisions; discovery and effect inference;
   observation construction and the scientific target.
4. Reproducible, adverse as well as favorable evidence. Preserve failures,
   compare methods on the same target, and distinguish simulation truth,
   empirical unknown truth, numerical agreement and proved guarantees.
5. An implementable common product design and credible investment argument.
   Operations must preserve scientific meaning in composition; scale and
   economic comparisons must cover equal work and identify their assumptions.
6. Primary technical sources and genuine preserved papers with auditable
   identities, versions, anchors and claims; connected exposition and inspected
   final documents.

FinMarketing supplies depth of estimator/remainder/financial construction;
BayesFilter supplies explicit model, likelihood, derivative and approximation
contracts; CIP supplies source-specific method choice and distinct challengers.
Neither length nor citation count is an acceptance criterion. Proposal scope
permits selected implementation and deferred institutional validation. It does
not excuse missing core theory. Conversely, the standard does not require a
new universal theorem for every model or a production system before delivery.

## What the current edition has achieved

The former omissions are substantially repaired. Graph construction and
identification, IV/LATE/weak-IV, influence functions and learning remainders,
and longitudinal/state-space connections now have sustained derivations.
Blocked dependent learning and a sparse-rate construction already exist;
there are sparse/factor comparisons as well as the newer independent-pilot
funding experiment. Finite hidden-state and exact Gaussian estimation,
small graph/effect confidence constructions, common operations and complete
workload measurements also exist. It would be incorrect to repeat the V2/V5
gap lists unchanged.

W00-W04 completion records successful execution of the focused V6.1 plan and
identity of its delivered files. It does not itself establish equal scholarly
depth across the full original ambition. The current author reviews expressly
limit that broader conclusion. The following findings distinguish an actual
defect in the current output from capability boundaries and unresolved evidence.

## G1: scientific qualifications do not survive every public consumer

This is a current implementation/design gap. `run_funding` defaults to
`regular_delta`. Its returned `scope` describes the conditional sampling and
identification assumptions, but `treasury_report` and `application_result`
omit that scope for regular intervals. Both preserve `uncertainty_scope` for
the projection result. A minimal probe using synthetic result objects confirms
the field loss in both consumers; no study rerun is needed to observe it.

The contract and manuscript disclose regular undercoverage; the normal
consumer output does not carry that qualification. The retained strong-regime
coverage is .925, with Monte Carlo interval [.8947,.9488], for a nominal .95
procedure. Numerical success therefore cannot be presented as statistical
qualification. An opaque scientific hash alone does not communicate the limit.

Closure requires a consistent public inference contract carrying the method,
confidence interpretation, assumptions and qualification through both consumer
paths, with explicit behavior for adverse calibration evidence. That does not
require suppressing a legitimate research estimate or pretending the procedure
has no asymptotic argument. It does require the result to preserve its meaning.

## G2: weak-linkage coverage has not established useful financial precision

The projection construction is derived and checked at its stated moment-model
scope. All 2,000 retained sets cover truth, but all weak/singular draws return
the whole real line. Regular intervals are narrower and under-cover in tested
regimes. Current evidence therefore supplies an honest uncertainty answer,
without a reliable informative financial bound in those weak designs.

This is not an algorithmic error merely because a valid set is unbounded.
Nor is it an impossibility theorem for the full observed mixture. Further
product usefulness would require a justified sharper procedure, additional
outcome-linked audit information or other identifying restrictions, with
coverage and informativeness assessed together. A defensible decision to
abstain can close a particular request; a promise of informative weak-linkage
inference remains unsupported.

## G3: the POMDP offering remains finite-state or exactly Gaussian

The current finite-state and Gaussian routes are substantive achievements.
The trained four-decision belief tree is evaluated conditional on an independent
pilot; it does not provide uncertainty for the unknown optimal value. The
manuscript explicitly records no executed nonlinear private-logger comparison.
Larger trees require approximate planning, and particle/quadrature/filter
alternatives have different likelihood, derivative and value errors.

Before broadening the POMDP promise, develop one representative nonlinear
banking route through identification, actual likelihood/score, approximation
reference, fitted value uncertainty and planning loss. A general solution for
all POMDPs is unnecessary. Keeping a finite/Gaussian initial release is a
legitimate scope choice, but should not be described as qualification of the
broader nonlinear capability. Accurate representative audit labels remain
substantive: the retained 10% label-error experiment has zero coverage.

## G4: temporal discovery is not generally calibrated intervention inference

There are existing small Gaussian/temporal confidence constructions. The
multivariate effect adapter, however, accepts a supplied sufficient graph of
at most seven variables and rejects richer ancestral marks. Its envelope is
conditional structural-order sensitivity, not graph-selection confidence.
The newer hidden-variable example evaluates the correct ancestral target,
but finite-data definite-endpoint accuracy is .6605; learned endpoints remain
exploratory. Its front-door effect comes from asserted scientific restrictions,
not automatic validation of a learned PAG.

For an expanded discovery-led decision claim, qualify a bounded graph-uncertainty
to intervention route, or specify the additional experiment/knowledge that
turns the exploratory output into an admissible effect query. Universal
confidence for arbitrary large PAGs is not required. More observations or a
successful discovery runtime alone does not close this gap.

## G5: automatic data organization has a deliberately narrow observation law

Candidate generation, ambiguity questions and wrong-source fixtures exist.
Conditional reconstruction relies on inclusion of the correct interpretation
and truthful supplied facts. A false trusted assertion can yield a unique wrong
acceptance. The calibrated linkage law redraws one-to-one pair maps by origin,
shares them across horizons, and assumes noninformative matching and independent
truthful audits. It does not cover persistent customer aliases, many-to-many
ownership, outcome-dependent matching or selected/noisy audits.

For broader longitudinal data support, select a representative persistent
entity/observation model, derive its effect on identification and joint
uncertainty, and test wrong semantics, audit selection and temporal leakage
against separately specified truth. No method can infer universal semantic
truth from column profiles. The required claim is a defensible bounded
observation procedure, with meaningful abstention and an information request.

## G6: original scale and reuse ambitions exceed the composed reference route

Existing time-series theory and complete response, latent and discovery
measurements deserve credit. The final source-to-money operation nevertheless
uses a known finite nuisance basis, independent invariant pilot, stable common
calendar, two candidate portfolios and fixed economic weights. Its eighteen
partitioned trials are single-host and in-memory; parallel execution is slower
at tested sizes. The earlier sparse/blocked theory is not automatically a
qualification of arbitrary learners substituted into that public operation.

Further breadth requires preserving those statistical conditions when changing
the learner, training design, observations and physical plan, then measuring
the dominant computation of the selected extension. Streaming, storage,
restart/concurrency and multi-host costs remain unmeasured; latent and discovery
work cannot inherit the funding timing. These are limits on large-scale product
claims, not proof that the current serial reference is inadequate for its own
dimensions. Accepted sibling integration and a deployed standalone workbench
are later construction obligations, not missing mathematical proofs.

## Source coverage, economics and the stopping criterion

The source archive, claim trace and critical method ledgers are substantial.
Publisher blocks, preprint/final differences and bounded correction searches
remain disclosed. A revised scientific route needs its own primary/challenger
and version review. The existence of 44 surveyed projects or 127 saved papers
does not establish exhaustive coverage; neither is exhaustive acquisition or
runtime testing of every package a requirement of this commission.

The expert-composition cost model and matched-workflow evaluation design exist.
Analyst savings, realized bank benefits and commercial demand are unmeasured.
They must remain hypotheses. Independent review, confidential bank evidence,
accepted integration and deployment are subsequent institutional work; they
cannot replace unfinished local scientific construction.

The immediate in-scope repair is G1. G2 is an unresolved usefulness/calibration
issue with a valid abstention outcome. G3-G6 describe the distance between the
broader commissioned capabilities and the selected initial offering; closing
each requires a justified bounded route or a substantive scope decision with
alternatives, not a universal theorem. These findings do not withdraw correct
derivations or the frozen artifact identity. They do prevent using phase
completion alone as a blanket claim that the full benchmark standard is met.

The acceptance test remains a reader reconstructing a representative new bank
question through information, identification, estimation, uncertainty,
numerics, financial meaning and reusable output, with the serious alternatives
and failure boundaries explained. This assessment records the next review and
repair obligations; it does not silently initiate a new implementation program.
