               Appendix C. Variable definitions

               PEG score: Pain interference and Pain intensity due to chronic low
               back pain
               Sleep disturbance: assess self-reported perceptions of sleep
               quality, sleep depth, and restoration associated with sleep. The
               score is a summary score on the general perception of sleep quality
               PROMIS depression: affective and cognitive manifestations of
               depression
               PROMIS anxiety: focuses on fear (e.g., worry, feelings of panic),
               anxious misery (e.g., dread), hyperarousal (e.g., tension,
               nervousness, restlessness), and somatic symptoms related to arousal
               (e.g., cardiovascular symptoms, dizziness).
               BMI or obesity or being overweight. This should perhaps be
               described as weight or BMI associations
               Alcohol: alcohol consumption (number of drinks per week)
               Fear avoidance: patient reported questionnaire which specifically
               focuses on how a patient’s fear avoidance beliefs about physical
               activity and work may affect and contribute to their low back pain
               and resulting disability
               Pain catastrophizing score: Pain catastrophizing is characterized
               by the tendency to magnify the threat value of a pain stimulus and
               to feel helpless in the presence of pain, as well as by a relative
               inability to prevent or inhibit pain-related thoughts in
               anticipation of, during, or following a painful event
               CCI: comorbidity index. It measures the number of comorbidities
               Education: level of education of the participant
               Financial strain: level of financial situation of the participant.
               Often a proxy for socioeconomic status
