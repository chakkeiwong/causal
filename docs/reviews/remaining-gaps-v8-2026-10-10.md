# Remaining gaps after executed V8

The reviewed E00–E07 author program is a bounded research increment, not a
completed generally supported bank product. The [author review](reliability-author-review.md)
reconstructs the results and limits. The required standard is unchanged:
connected target-to-identification-to-estimation-to-uncertainty arguments,
financial interpretation, inspected primary evidence, reproducibility,
substantive losing alternatives and preserved interface meaning. Independent,
bank and adoption evidence is separate from author work.

| Remaining gap | What V8 established | How to address it |

Independently trained covariance is diagnostic only. | Register independently known-nuisance, pilot-size scaling and covariance comparisons. Derive relative pilot/evaluation rates, separate bias from variance, then test a frozen repair on fresh draws. Current bank-ready general calibration is not established. |
