The research monograph connects causal diagrams to identification
and IV; identified functionals to influence functions, orthogonal learning
and inverse problems; and longitudinal interventions to controlled state-space
likelihoods, filtering and POMDP decisions. Panels, structural shocks,
discovery ambiguity, observation processes, continuous time, transport,
sensitivity and contemporary methods lead into common software operations,
financial studies and product economics. The sponsor case retains separate investment boundaries.

The inherited funding construction carries a one-month price intervention through
persistent balances, common shocks, pilot-trained controls, calibrated candidate
entity histories and a financial interval. The servicing construction diagnoses
private-logger inference failures, develops audited-trajectory estimation and
evaluates an independently trained belief policy. An ancestral-graph oracle,
identified/unresolved latent queries and conflicting-source fixtures expose
what discovery and automatic organization can actually establish. Two local
consumers use the same composed dynamic result; economic, sampling, source,
data and learning changes invalidate inappropriate reuse.

The inherited V6.1 linkage construction preserves unbounded and disconnected financial
sets instead of forcing a near-singular inverse. All 2,000 registered
projections cover their synthetic truth, but weak/singular mappings yield
the whole real line throughout. Regular intervals are narrower and show
adverse finite-regime coverage. The comparison reports precision and
availability as well as coverage; its moment projection is not a sharp
full-mixture identified set. The [public contract](contracts/dynamic-funding.md)
explains explicit method choice, input rejection and consumer behavior.

V7 adds useful persistent-pair inference when representative audits supply
actual linked outcomes. Its joint projection narrows some strong cases but
retains weak/singular ambiguity. A fresh stable-domain nonlinear comparison
with 640 audits yields 95 covering intervals in 100 requests; this is a
specified-model result with wide Monte Carlo uncertainty. Finite latent
confidence sets need valid experimental restrictions for useful precision.
Three journeys preserve their distinct sampling and inference meanings.
Thirty-two matched complete runs give identical output bytes, with local
recovery checks and no multi-host or analyst-savings claim.

V8 retains funding coverage of .936/.948/.918 across three fresh designs;
longer covariance windows are not a consistent repair. The first joint
pair/calendar design covers .9217 and the richer learner .9033. Separately
registered larger designs both reach .94, with Monte Carlo intervals including
.95; this is conditional evidence, not uniform qualification. Strong valid
encouragement gives .9525 coverage and useful bounded Gaussian effect sets;
false exclusion gives zero coverage. The selected nonlinear variance model
gives 115 covers in 120 requests, equal to its homogeneous challenger, while
an omitted transition leaves every fit unqualified. Eight complete workloads
show dense/chunked numerical agreement, small total-memory reductions and no
consistent speed advantage. See the [reliability contract](contracts/reliability.md)
and [external validation protocol](docs/program/external-validation-v8.md).

V9 attributes a material fixed-pilot target shift and derives a sufficient
pilot/evaluation rate. Joint-regression horizon inference still covers only
550/600 at 720 dates. The fresh fourfold-pilot route covers 576/565/560 of 600
in three designs; all Monte Carlo intervals include 0.95, but paired improvement
is not established. The 1200/4800 cases need three/twelve times the old pilot
history. Corrected local joint-regression workloads at 1.2 million dates use
about 43% of dense peak memory and 83--84% of dense complete time. This does
not qualify general calibration, growing-pilot streaming, bank service levels
or labor savings. See the [contract](contracts/calibrated-funding.md) and
[external delta](docs/program/external-validation-v9.md).

V10 retains three fixed procedures and reports 2,000 fresh requests in each of
eight selected designs. With small audits, their all-request coverage is
.927/.933/.934. Only 451/2,000 near-rank requests return intervals; false
exogeneity gives .229/.2055/.2085 coverage. A selected baseline favors the
pilots over joint regression, but doubling the independent pilot history has
no demonstrated coverage benefit. A dense-equivalent streaming pilot reduces
large-case complete local memory to .544/.599 of dense use; time ratios
.906/1.071 show no consistent speed gain. This does not establish general
calibration, bank service levels, acquisition economics or acceptance.

## Verify or reconstruct locally

For read-only verification and status:

```bash
python3 tools/delivery_manifest.py verify
python3 tools/causal_program.py status
```

File identity does not establish scientific validity. Replaying, editing or
rebuilding bound artifacts intentionally invalidates a frozen delivery. After
an actual change, rerun the affected studies described in the replay guide,
then their appropriate checks and the build/audit sequence:

```bash
python3 tools/precision_exact_replay.py
python3 tools/precision_verify.py
python3 tools/causal_program.py check
python3 tools/causal_program.py build
python3 tools/foundations_audit.py
```

Earlier verification receipts remain applicable only while their bound inputs
are unchanged. Rendering produces images, not an automatic visual acceptance.
Build and render when manuscripts change. Inspect the final pages, update
substantive and visual records, reconcile Mission Control, then use
`python3 tools/precision_delivery.py check`, then
`python3 tools/precision_delivery.py freeze` and verify the new delivery.
Do not repair stale
scientific evidence by merely replacing its recorded hashes. Source-only
wording changes need review and affected manuscript checks, not unchanged
expensive Monte Carlo studies.

The active build uses `causal_foundations` and `sponsor_reconstruction`, with
LaTeX shell escape disabled. Installed Python/NumPy/SciPy, BibTeX, Poppler and
Pillow support local checks/rendering; `.venv-scope` contains the study runtime.
No network is needed to rebuild the preserved corpus.

## Authorization, product boundary and history

Routine research and local builds are authorized. The existing narrow public
collector command is `/home/ubuntu/workspace/causal/tools/public_sources.py fetch`.
It performs bounded public HTTPS reads from registered manifests, never uploads
local data or executes downloaded code. Use `login:false` and existing allowed
forms. No blanket rule or managed-policy relaxation was introduced.

The design supports selected observed-history, finite-state, exact Gaussian
and audited nonlinear routes. General nonlinear private-state inference, uniform boundary
or near-unit inference, large-PAG confidence, arbitrary dependent ML and
universal semantic reconstruction remain scientific extensions. Richer
persistent entity maps require their own model. Bank-specific observation,
assignment and audit qualification, production workspaces, accepted sibling
interfaces, independent review and measured economics remain separate work.
