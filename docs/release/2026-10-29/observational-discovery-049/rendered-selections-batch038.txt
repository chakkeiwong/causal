Chapter 37

Learning a causal model from
observational data

A discovery method can narrow the plausible economic relationships without settling the effect
of a proposed action. The comparisons here follow that remaining question through specific
implementation choices. A supplied graph can support a checked adjustment candidate; a
learned structural model adds assumptions about functions and disturbances; hidden borrower
circumstances change the graph information that must be retained. The practical choice
depends on those assumptions as well as the computation.
   The first studies compare a restricted pgmpy adjustment route, causal-learn search and the
different maintenance and numerical obligations of CausalNex, DAGMA and Causica. Native
and other-language studies then examine graph representations, latent-variable methods and
the meaning of their scores. The final bnlearn and LiNGAM comparisons carry the argument
into intervention, counterfactual and bootstrap summaries. Each answers a different part of
the same banking request; successful graph construction alone does not complete that request.
The detailed DAGMA study includes bounded execution of a saved source module, while the
other passages retain their stated source-inspection and exact-reference evidence scopes.

37.3.3    A conditional archival route and its alternatives

The inspected optimizer consumes the returned solution vector and can issue a nonconvergence
warning before returning a graph. It does not promote optimizer success into a structured
scientific guarantee. A prospective archival wrapper would capture termination and warnings,
test finite output and acyclicity, and retain an unqualified candidate when these checks fail.
It would preserve column identities, transforms and tabu directions, and report timeouts or
environment failures as unavailable results. An edge weight would never be repurposed as a
confidence level. These requirements follow from the selected source and from the meaning of
the proposed output; no CausalNex runtime qualification is claimed here. (mckinsey/causalnex,
2026b)
   Direct adoption would transfer unsupported lifecycle obligations to the product. Porting
the optimizer would additionally transfer numerical behavior, dependency adaptation and
testing to the local team; there is no demonstrated embedding requirement that warrants this
ownership. An isolated historical benchmark is the narrower route when a concrete comparison
requires it. Other discovery engines discussed in this survey need their own pinned decisions,
because constraint tests, score searches and latent-variable representations answer different
questions. Their names are not aliases for CausalNex, and a separately reviewed pgmpy version
does not qualify the older dependency used here.
   For a small banking prototype, an explicit expert DAG and an exactly enumerated reference
model provide useful comparators: they expose assumptions and intervention arithmetic while
leaving discovery accuracy open. The cost comparison should include retained environments,
license and dependency review, preprocessing, failed requests, analyst correction, future fixes
and retirement. No observed price or maintenance-cost estimate is available from the present
static review. Reconsideration of CausalNex would require a unique archival need, a named
owner, a permissible resolved environment and executed conversion and failure checks. Public
or generated examples can support those initial checks without purchasing access to bank data.

37.5      Causica: a generative reference with explicit admission
          boundaries
An analyst wants to simulate a larger servicing effort for a borrower, compare it with the
observed treatment, and then aggregate the result into a contact policy. A learned structural
model makes these operations convenient: one model can represent a graph, conditional
mechanisms, noise and interventions. Convenience does not settle whether the graph is
identified, whether a counterfactual refers to the same borrower, or whether the reported
uncertainty includes the relevant source of error. Causica is useful precisely because its explicit
abstractions let the proposal examine these questions together.
   The selected source is Microsoft’s Causica revision c3b7dd2b09fe, dated 24 August 2026,
declaring package version 0.4.5. Its DECI path combines an approximate distribution over
graphs with learned functional relationships and noise modules. This is a proposed reference for
a structural-model component. It is not an enabled estimator in the local six-operation service.
The distinction matters when comparing the proposal with an existing library: describing
a model in a monograph does not implement its training, inference, diagnostics or failure
handling. (Microsoft Causica contributors, 2026; Causal banking proposal, 2026e)


37.5.1    What the original result does and does not justify

Geffner et al. formulate DECI using a nonlinear additive-noise model with no latent confounders.
Its graph distribution and function/noise parameters are learned by maximizing an evidence
lower bound. Their Theorem 1 is an infinite-data result for a maximizing solution. Appendix
A assumes minimality, structural identifiability, correct specification, causal sufficiency and
integrability of the log likelihood. Structural identifiability means that another graph in the
admitted model family cannot generate the same observed distribution. It is an assumption
with substantive content, not a property created by calling the optimizer. (Geffner et al., 2022)
  These conditions expose four separate obligations for a banking use. The variable set must
represent the relevant common causes; the structural family must distinguish the intervention
query from observational alternatives; the fitted mechanisms must approximate the admitted
law; and the numerical procedure must achieve an adequate solution. Universal function
approximation does not verify the first two obligations or make a finite training run a global
maximum. The paper explicitly says that the theorem does not cover its mixed-type and
missing-value extensions. A practical implementation may support those inputs without
inheriting that theorem for them. (Geffner et al., 2022)
   After fitting, graph-averaged intervention simulation answers a model-based question. In the
selected source, graph draws share the learned functional and noise modules. Those draws are
therefore not samples from an independently specified posterior over all function parameters.
Their variation is also not a frequentist confidence region for the true graph. The product
should report the fitted model, graph distribution and numerical simulation error separately,
keeping any unresolved structural ambiguity visible. (Microsoft Causica contributors, 2026)

37.9.4    JavaScript graph analysis is a separate component

DAGitty’s JavaScript core can analyse supplied graph and query objects, including selected
adjustment operations on DAG, PDAG, MAG and PAG types. It can therefore help an analyst
inspect a diagram or compare admissible adjustment choices after discovery. Its function call
does not establish the diagram from repayment data. The selected validator explicitly leaves
proper MAG and PAG validation as unfinished work, while performing some checks such as
the absence of semidirected cycles. An affirmative result from that validator cannot alone
certify every graph invariant required by the proposal. (jtextor/dagitty, 2026)
   An internal PAG-to-PDAG transformation maps unresolved endpoint classes to ordinary
edge classes for downstream graph operations. A transformation may be useful inside a justified
calculation without being an acceptable export of the original causal knowledge. The service
should retain the input graph, its endpoint marks, the query and the transformation’s purpose;
it should not return the transformed object as a newly learned unique DAG. The selected
tests include PAG and conditioning examples, providing useful prospective fixtures. The root
GPLv2 text requires a distribution-specific review before reuse; a browser or process boundary
does not itself settle those obligations. (jtextor/dagitty, 2026)
   Selection needs its own diagnostic beside latent-confounding examples. Let A and Y
be independent fair binary variables, and retain records only when S = 1{A = Y }. In
the full population the causal effect of intervening on A is zero. Among retained records,
P (Y = 1 | A = 1, S = 1) = 1 and P (Y = 1 | A = 0, S = 1) = 0. A lender analysing a
channel selected jointly by allocation and repayment can therefore encounter a large conditional
association with no treatment benefit. This declared mechanism tests whether the interface
retains the selection rule; it does not establish a general finite-sample discovery guarantee or
satisfy every algorithm’s faithfulness assumption. (Causal banking proposal, 2026ai)

37.10.4     Bootstrap strength is a statistic with a sampling law

The selected bootstrap frontend defaults to 200 replicates, a sample size equal to the number
of input rows and conversion to a CPDAG for arc strength. The generic backend samples rows
with replacement. A customer’s repeated visits or a common calendar shock are therefore not
automatically respected as independent sampling units. In an authored mean example with
20 independent customer clusters, 25 visits per customer, unit marginal variance and within-
customer correlation 0.6, the mean variance is 0.0308. Treating all 500 visits as independent
gives 0.002. That arithmetic is not a coverage theorem for graph search; it explains why a
