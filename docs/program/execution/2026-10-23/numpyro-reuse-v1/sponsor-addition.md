Observation, intervention and replay move values with different meanings. In
the inspected intervention handler, the sampled value retained under the
original treatment name can differ from the treatment value propagated to
descendants. An interface must preserve both roles. The independent finite
reference gives an association of four response units and an intervention
effect of two under one supplied confounded model. Two further constructions
have the same intervention success marginals of one half but cross-world
conditional success probabilities of one and zero. A sampler cannot resolve
that missing structural coupling from the shared marginals.

A second exact example connects hidden-state uncertainty to the banking
decision. A customer alert has probability 5/12 and changes the probability
of a high-benefit customer type from 1/3 to 3/5. With stipulated action
payoffs of USD 100 for that type and USD -200 otherwise, posterior averaging
values the action at USD -20 and selects no action. Substituting the most
probable type instead reports USD 100 and selects the action, losing an
expected USD 20 under the specified posterior law. These are authored
proposal amounts, not bank returns or an observed NumPyro failure.
\citep{exec23_numpyro_reference}

The proposed worker must distinguish posterior state samples from MAP
decoding, stored latent values from regenerated outcomes, deterministic
