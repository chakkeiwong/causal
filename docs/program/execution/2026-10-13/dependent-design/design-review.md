# Dependent learning: final-study feasibility review

Author review, 13 October 2026, for `SCI.DESIGN.R05S`. The independent-pilot argument is useful proposal material. It is **not approved as a final qualification-study design for the preserved R05S obligation**. This is a reviewed negative design result, not an unperformed Monte Carlo campaign. The result does not block software, manuscript, agent or integration work.

## Supported contribution and mismatch with the original experiment

The conditional PLIV moment is correctly derived: after freezing an independent pilot, the numerator and denominator errors are respectively `E[delta_Z delta_Y]` and `E[delta_Z delta_A]`. Simultaneous norm bounds therefore control the candidate score by `e_Z(e_Y + |theta| e_A)`. The instrument error cannot be replaced by an outcome–action error product. Independent bounded residue classes give the stated joint moment rectangle when the **whole observed vector** is stationary and has a known finite dependence range. Projecting this rectangle retains zero-denominator, disconnected and unbounded cases without a strong-instrument division.

The small references check a complete 16-world marginal law, an orthogonal exact lasso solution with a separate optimizer, and 18,685 projection membership comparisons. These references support their own arithmetic and the restricted proof. They do not certify a full fitted workflow, random-pilot cone conditions, optimization error, arbitrary selection boundaries or repeated-sampling performance.

The candidate changes the predecessor's information law. Gaussian AR controls and unbounded responses do not satisfy known finite dependence and bounded observations. The fitted pilot is independent of the evaluation sequence, whereas the predecessor separated training/evaluation blocks within one trajectory. Features are fixed beforehand and the displayed lasso penalizes an intercept; the old procedure standardized on training data, used an unpenalized intercept and selected a shrinking penalty on an internal validation block. None of those distinctions may disappear from a final protocol. Clipping predictions is justified; clipping observed outcomes changes the target and cannot supply a missing bound silently.

The new sparse certificate requires justified support and approximation bounds, a sparse cone certificate and numerical control of the actual optimizer. Its high-dimension premise is not supplied by a full Gram condition number or a small KKT residual. The calibration alternative avoids sparse certification but requires an independent sample and valid response ranges. Neither alternative has been implemented and profiled as a complete successor in the proposed final design.

## Precision is an obstacle even before fitting

Deterministic planning re-enumerates the existing authored law, with independent fair signs `X,V,H,E`, `Z=.3X+V`, `A=.8Z+.2X+H`, and `Y=1.2A+.4X+E`. Its exact bounds are `B_Z=13/10`, `B_A=56/25`, `B_Y=511/125`; its moments are `Q=4/5`, `M=24/25`. These are stipulated generated units, not bank measurements. The calculation grants exact conditional means (`e_j=0`) to isolate the evaluation bound. It holds the sample moments at their population centers and assigns evaluation error .03. It is therefore a planning calculation, not prospective power or a fitted-study outcome.

At 640 evaluation rows the displayed rectangle contains zero in both coordinates for each planning dependence range `d=0,4,19`. The projected set is the whole line even with exact nuisance means. Using 640 rows is already generous relative to the old 640-date sample, whose folds retain fewer evaluation observations. For independent evaluation, 2,075 rows first make the denominator interval positive **at this center**. This threshold does not imply finite availability with any specified probability.

A stipulated decision threshold of 1.15 is only .05 below the true coefficient 1.2. The centered rectangle establishes an effect above that threshold only if

`8 B_Z (B_Y + 1.15 B_A) sqrt(log(4(d+1)/.03)/(2 floor(n/(d+1)))) < M - 1.15 Q = .04`.

For `d=0` this conservative calculation first passes at 7,344,273 evaluation rows. Positive dependence increases the requirement. This is not a recommendation to collect millions of bank rows; it demonstrates the poor precision of this particular global-range concentration bound near the stipulated decision boundary. A sharper valid variance-sensitive construction or more separating information should be investigated before a large final campaign. A favorable center alone cannot establish the all-request availability or useful-width rate.

The uncorrected calibration alternative has a separate nonshrinking floor. Even with an oracle learner and infinitely many calibration draws, setting every residual-variance lower bound to zero leaves squared-loss limits `1`, `41/25`, and `2101/625`. The resulting denominator bias radius is `sqrt(41/25)`, larger than `Q=.8`; the numerator radius also exceeds `M=.96`. The centered moment rectangle stays uninformative even when evaluation noise vanishes. This is conservatism in the risk certificate, not actual oracle prediction error. A justified residual-noise lower bound can remove this floor in a generated design; bank residual truth cannot be inferred from training error alone. Separate calibration, estimation and any certificate selection require their joint error budget.

## Preserve the negative baseline and distinguish the screens

R05-v2's eight cells and both fitted methods remain immutable. The sparse learner has 499/512 coverage in high dimension and 493/512 with larger support, missing the simultaneous .95 lower-bound screen. Nonlinear omission gives 0/512. The weak-IV cell covers 512/512 but has median width 3.836470. These are nominal 99% intervals, so comparison with the first attempt's 95% intervals cannot isolate an implementation repair. The old high-dimension and larger-support results are failed qualification screens; they do not prove a universal impossibility theorem.

For the preserved 512-request, sixteen-comparison coverage screen, exact rational binomial tails and an independent beta-quantile calculation agree that at least 500 successes are needed. Per-cell pass probabilities at true coverages .95, .97, .98 and .99 are approximately .001849, .234929, .770054 and .997670. No joint power is inferred by multiplying cells. A theorem guaranteeing only .95 does not by itself justify a high probability of passing this screen. For a conservative method whose actual coverage is higher, the margin must be assessed alongside precision and availability.

The .99 finite-availability rule is a raw fraction: 507/512 passes. Replacing it with a .99 lower confidence bound would be a new criterion; even 512/512 has lower bound .988797 under the existing sixteen-way error allocation. No original criterion is changed here. The predecessor has no bank width threshold. The 1.15 decision threshold above is explicitly a proposal scenario, not retroactive bank acceptance.

## Actionable route and unresolved generalization

1. Implement a **separately labeled bounded prototype** with a fully specified stationary finite-dependent generator and independent pilot/calibration samples. Keep the old Gaussian AR failures as the broader negative baseline. Use fixed ordinary and nonlinear dictionaries, and retain high dimension, larger support, misspecification, zero/weak relevance, absent certificates and falsely declared dependence.
2. Choose an actual certificate route. For a small sparse design, provide exact or conservative cone and numerical certificates for the implemented fit. For calibration, compare the zero-noise-lower-bound baseline with a justified known-noise generated design; allocate all calibration errors and retain unavailable/whole-line outputs. Oracle truth belongs in a clearly labeled diagnostic comparator, not in an apparently feasible algorithm.
3. Derive and review a sharper evaluation region, such as a valid bounded variance-sensitive construction within independent residue classes. Any estimated variance and optional certificate selection need their own proof and error allocation. Carry nuisance products and denominator topology through the full argument. Profile on development inputs before fixing replication budgets; do not spend a final campaign merely demonstrating that a worst-case bound is wide.
4. Match fixed-dictionary, certified sparse and calibrated-learner comparisons on information and training/evaluation resources. Preserve full fit, transfer, failed-request, inference and restart costs. Define all-request coverage, finite availability, useful width and decision stability separately. After the executable and proof are reviewed, freeze the actual code, dependencies, fixtures, fresh draws, replication budget, stopping rule and the operating characteristics of the final screen.
5. General Gaussian/mixing panels with unknown dependence, arbitrary learned features and approximation, weak-information useful precision, and scientific instrument validity remain outside the established theorem. Existing public observations cannot prove those causal/dependence premises. These require a new argument or explicit additional information; a narrower success cannot qualify them.

The first four items are concrete proposal/prototype work that can proceed without authentic bank data. They are a development sequence, not approval of an unspecified final protocol. Final R05S registration remains unjustified until the actual learner, observable certificate, dependence law, fresh design and useful-width/power proposal are reviewed against every preserved obligation. No claim of independent acceptance or original-gap closure is made.
