The banking question is the incremental value of a feasible outreach policy to a declared population. A graph of customer identities, a guarantee relationship and an influence mechanism are different inputs. Spatial feedback, exposure-specific outcomes and time-window experiments offer substantive alternatives; their outputs become comparable only after fixing the intervention, observation clock, beneficiary population and costs. The integrated relationship and commercial-network chapters develop the following comparison. This synthesis completes the scientific comparison obligation without treating all software coverage as complete. \citep{exec17_s7_math,exec20_network_reference}

## A response matrix is conditional on a causal model

For receiver-row weights W, stipulate the structural equilibrium
\[
Y=\rho WY+\beta a+\gamma Wa+U,
\qquad S(W)=(I-\rho W)^{-1}(\beta I+\gamma W).
\]
Interpreting S as an intervention derivative requires an invariant mechanism, exogenous action, appropriate fixed W and invertible feedback; |rho|r(W)<1 is a sufficient stability condition. A regression with the same conditional-mean algebra does not supply these premises. The own derivative trace(S)/n, total derivative 1'S1/n and response S e_j to one selected branch answer different questions. \citep{exec20_network_reference}

In the exact two-branch reference, W has off-diagonal ones, rho=1/2, beta=10 and gamma=0. Then S has diagonal 40/3 and off-diagonal 20/3. At an assumed USD 12 intervention cost, using the coefficient gives net -2, the own-branch response gives 4/3, and the two-branch portfolio response gives 8. Removing the true path from branch 1 to branch 2 gives portfolio net -2. This is an authored model comparison, not an observed vendor failure. In a three-branch star, row normalization changes the model and intervention responses; common scalar normalization with the corresponding rho rescaling preserves them. Missing relationships and normalization cannot be repaired merely by choosing a robust covariance option. \citep{exec20_network_reference}

For the total T=beta/(1-rho), the gradient at that point is (2,40). The assumed positive-definite parameter covariance with diagonal (1,1/100) and off-diagonal 1/20 implies delta variance 28. Omitting its off-diagonal gives 20. Thus coefficients and separate standard errors are an insufficient shared result for this calculation. An adapter must retain the covariance, parameter ordering, sample and W identities, normalization, uncertainty conditions and numerical refusal states. These covariance values are illustrative inputs, not estimated coverage evidence. \citep{exec20_network_reference,exec20_network_stata_impacts}

## Exposure support and graph uncertainty require different information

With two connected borrowers and exactly one treated, own/neighbor exposures (1,0) and (0,1) each have probability 1/2, while (0,0) and (1,1) are impossible. A direct effect at untreated-neighbor exposure is not recovered by reweighting a missing cell. Independent half-probability assignment instead gives each cell probability 1/4. Exhaustive enumeration of the stipulated outcome law Y(z,e)=5+2z+3e verifies Horvitz–Thompson expected cell means (5,8,7,10), direct contrast 2 and spillover contrast 3. This point-mean identity does not supply a usable variance from two borrowers; joint exposure support and the actual design remain necessary. \citep{exec20_network_reference}

Even correct marginal edge probabilities may not supply the exposure law. Let U,V denote the two real influence links to one beneficiary, each with marginal probability 1/2, and theta=P(U=V=1). The coherent joint laws allow theta in [0,1/2]. Independent contact actions with probability p yield exposure probability
\[
q(p)=p-p^2\theta.
\]
Increasing p from 1/5 to 3/5, with USD 100 exposure benefit, USD 20 per contact and an incremental USD 16 investment, gives net value 8-32theta and the sharp set [-8,8]. Independent edge completion picks theta=1/4, but that is extra scientific information. Auditing only one randomly chosen edge per neighborhood has the same data law for all theta, however many neighborhoods are observed. Joint audits or a justified restriction are required. \citep{exec17_s7_math}

Policy design offers a different remedy. Write r=P(A1=A2=1), retaining equal marginal action probability p and graph-independent allocation. Then q(p,r)=p-r theta. Against the same old all-or-none policy p0=r0=1/5, each new plan has p1=3/5 and identical expected contact costs. Independent coins (r1=9/25) give value set [0,8], all-or-none outreach (r1=3/5) gives [-12,8], and dispersed allocation (r1=1/5) gives the point value 8. The comparison has a common reference policy. An engine that accepts scalar p for independent allocation cannot be assumed to evaluate the dispersed joint law. Exact enumeration validates these statements; no model fitting or bank benefit was observed. \citep{exec17_s7_math}

MossSpider's conditional network estimator and inferference's grouped interface address different parts of this problem. A shared result must retain the graph or admissible graph class, exposure mapping, full joint action law, population weights and numerical integration error. Conditioning on one completed graph cannot silently close the outer value set. Cross-group guarantees, shared graph errors and repeated requests on one network do not become independent units through an API conversion. \citep{v13_sw_mossspider,v13_sw_inferference,exec17_s7_paths}

## Time-switching is a design alternative with its own failures

The inspected legacy switchback specification uses time-window assignments, exposure timestamps and burn periods, with separate resampling of treated and control buckets 10,000 times and percentile intervals. For the authored law Y_t(z)=100t+2z, observing control at t=0 and treatment at t=1 gives difference 102 although the immediate effect is 2. With carryover Y_t=2Z_t+4Z_(t-1) and alternating assignment, the observed difference is -2, the immediate effect is 2 and the sustained effect is 6. Resampling cannot manufacture randomization or washout. A prospective comparison must freeze schedule randomization, attribution, carryover assumptions and the effective sampling unit before reading outcomes. These are counterexamples to unrestricted interpretations, not recorded commercial failures. \citep{exec20_network_statsig_types,exec20_network_statsig_switchback,exec20_network_reference}
