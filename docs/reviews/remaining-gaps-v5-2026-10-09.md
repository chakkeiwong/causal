# V5: remaining gaps against the commissioned standard

Assessment date: 9 October 2026. Literature cutoff: 7 October 2026.
This is a targeted comparative author assessment requested after delivery,
not an independent proof audit. It leaves the frozen V5 files intact. The
inspection receipt is [remaining-gaps-v5-evidence.json](remaining-gaps-v5-evidence.json).

## Judgment

V5 is substantially closer to the requested standard. The original omissions
of diagram construction, instrumental variables, semiparametric/causal machine
learning and longitudinal/state-space connections are no longer a fair
description of the manuscript. The influence-function and remainder arguments,
inverse/proxy distinctions, censored-history score, validated private-logger
factorization and Gaussian score recursions are substantive scholarship.
The source archive and honest adverse results are also real strengths.

I would nevertheless not claim consistent equivalence to the three reference
monographs across the product's full proposed scope. The remaining problem is
uneven development of the chain from a bank's information and intervention to
a selected method, usable uncertainty, numerical construction and reusable
operation. Several individual constructions meet that test within their
restrictions. Their coexistence does not establish the same closure for the
larger product.

The previous statement that the V5 program was completed is accurate about
execution of T00–T08. It is too strong if read as saying that only independent
review and bank implementation now remain. Some scholarship, comparative
evaluation and product-specification work remains within proposal scope.
This assessment does not make phase completion a substitute for that judgment.

## What the comparison actually requires

The reread FinMarketing uplift passage connects a loss/score to its identified
target, conditional bias and rate conditions. Its panel example carries a
single branch-cohort design through reused-control covariance, event-time
support and a financial linear combination. Its engineering chapter explains
direct-analyst, application-user and custom-builder journeys, including a
numerically valid but economically incompatible composition.

The reread BayesFilter passages carry model-map, initialization, mask and
filter derivatives through the likelihood actually evaluated. Its filter
choice register connects each approximation to a target-specific validation
sequence. The CIP passage assigns a primary method and distinct challengers
to one fixed scientific problem, explaining what each restriction buys.

These are standards of argument and construction, not requirements to match
their length or implement their entire subject matter. Nor are the reference
monographs treated as independently certified truths. The recorded passages
were inspected; this is not a new audit of every chapter of all four books.

## V5-G1. Complete method decisions for the central dynamic use cases

**Material progress.** The static ML chapter now supplies explicit gradients,
remainders, local versus average rates and target changes. Responses have a
same-target covariance comparison; panels have system/difference moment and
initial-law derivations. These should be retained rather than rewritten as
if they were absent.

**Remaining.** Method choice is still most complete for the deliberately
separated reference experiments. In `07b_inference_choice.tex:124`, a bank
facing common shocks, persistence, uncertain rank, aggregation and breaks gets
a sensible hierarchy, but not a comparably complete worked selection and
inferential construction for a representative combination. The long-window
comparison establishes behavior on its declared generator; it does not supply
a justified rule for selecting bandwidths/regimes after looking at the bank's
data. A new uniform theorem over every such process is not required.

The panel treatment is also uneven. `06_panels.tex:160` derives the DR ATT
functional and notes the additional influence terms from normalizers and
aggregation, but does not carry that general construction through as fully as
the static ATE. The synthetic-control branch at line 278 explains the factor
error and alternatives without a similarly developed selected inference
route. The existing deposit study does contain a complete simpler cohort
analysis and financial account; the gap is in the broader supported choices,
not the absence of any panel example.

**Proposal closure.** Develop a representative dynamic banking dossier with
a fixed economic target and observation law, explicit permissible information
regimes, a derived primary procedure and substantive alternatives. Carry
data-dependent selection, learning and dependence into its uncertainty or
declare precisely which choices are fixed externally. Explain why important
adjacent routes are excluded. This requires depth for chosen capabilities,
not a fitted implementation of every method in the survey.

## V5-G2. A resolved estimation/inference strategy for latent decisions

**Material progress.** `10b_validated_private.tex` establishes a genuine
measurement-supported identification and two-sample likelihood argument.
`10c_continuous_estimation.tex` differentiates an actual privately logged
Gaussian model, including the parameter-dependent reward transform.
`11b_continuous_histories.tex` derives the censoring/history score and evaluates
a policy learned in an independent pilot. These repair major V4 omissions.

**Remaining.** The main finite private-logger inference problem is unresolved.
Nominal 95% coverage is .850/.875 across 80 repetitions per validation size,
counting four/five unavailable fits in those denominators. The separate
199-refit challenger has 17 invalid/boundary fits and withholds its interval.
The text appropriately separates this from floating-point and start-value
sensitivity, but it does not yet diagnose the failure sufficiently to select
a usable alternative, sampling regime or evidence-acquisition design. Eighty
repetitions and one bootstrap dataset also limit the precision of the
assessment; the results should not be described as disproving the asymptotic
theory.

The exact continuous reference uses a scalar state, five unknown coefficients,
known noise/initial laws and calibrated measurement scale. The nonlinear
discussion at `10c_continuous_estimation.tex:119` lists appropriate numerical
obligations, but has not carried one selected approximation through the same
likelihood/derivative/value-error comparison. The finite-state validation
sample also supplies true state labels and invariant emissions; a bank-facing
measurement design has yet to explain what those labels mean and how that
extra identifying information would be obtained.

The observed-history, latent and proxy routes are mostly developed on separate
experiments. The independently trained policy is an immediate-reward rule,
not a dynamically optimized belief policy. That is a valid fixed-policy
evaluation experiment, but supports a narrower decision capability.

**Proposal closure.** On a common servicing question, compare the information
required by observed histories, validated states and proxies; investigate the
finite-state inference failure; and select a workable bounded estimation and
uncertainty route. If a nonlinear route remains in initial scope, develop one
selected approximation and credible numerical reference. Otherwise make its
research role and the resulting product limitation central to the offering.
A negative or unavailable result can meet scholarly standards when its cause,
decision consequence and alternative are adequately developed. Solving general
POMDP identification is not a completion requirement.

## V5-G3. Discovery evaluation must match the graph and effect being claimed

**Material progress.** There are real PCMCI+/LPCMCI executions, source-specific
temporal graph distinctions, preserved endpoints and a graph-to-fresh-data
effect adapter. Unsupported marks are refused, and conditional envelopes are
not mislabeled confidence bands.

**Remaining.** In `08b_temporal_choice.tex:38`, hidden-case precision/recall are
computed against observed structural-DAG adjacency. The manuscript expressly
acknowledges that this is not the PAG target. Consequently those numbers do
not tell a reader how well the hidden-confounder method recovers its intended
ancestry/endpoint information. The limitation is honest but leaves the main
challenger unevaluated on the property motivating its inclusion.

The effect adapter enumerates sufficient-graph orders for at most seven
variables and rejects the richer LPCMCI result. It therefore demonstrates
one useful route, not the latent-discovery-to-query route. The search cap
and the conditional effect envelope also have distinct inferential costs.

**Proposal closure.** Define the correct finite-window latent projection or
other explicitly chosen graphical target for the known mechanism. Evaluate
adjacency and endpoint/ancestry information against that target. Work through
at least an identifiable hidden-variable query and an unresolved query with a
clear explanation of the additional information needed. Selection uncertainty
can remain qualified or exploratory; a general confidence theorem for large
PAGs is not required.

## V5-G4. Automatic organization still has a substantial measurement gap

**Material progress.** `09b_generated_observations.tex` generates hypotheses
from profiles, clocks and bounded keys. It resolves renamed synthetic schemas
with explicit trusted answers and rejects an unsupported three-field key.
The linkage example propagates an independent audit's uncertainty and shows
that a biased audit remains biased after variance correction.

**Remaining.** Candidate completeness and answer correctness are assumptions
of the conditional proof, not established properties of new feeds. The
experiments rename/reorder fixtures within the generating vocabulary and use
oracle answers; they do not yet characterize plausible but wrong surviving
interpretations under conflicting or incomplete source documentation.

The linkage calculation is an independent random-replacement model with one
scalar mismatch rate. Richer probabilistic linkage is described at
`09b_generated_observations.tex:108`, but candidate assignments, their calibration
and their propagation into a causal analysis have not been developed to the
same depth. Minimizing downstream effect spread to choose metadata questions
also creates an analysis-selection issue that is stated but not completed in
an executed workflow.

**Proposal closure.** Define a bounded multi-source banking observation model
with realistic entity relations, revisions and conflicting metadata; propagate
plausible mappings and calibration uncertainty through the final estimand.
Evaluate wrong acceptance and useful abstention on independently specified
fixtures, and separate metadata acquisition from final evaluation. Neither
automatic discovery of economic truth nor perfect analyst answers is a
reasonable general guarantee.

## V5-G5. Demonstrate the shared product at a stronger composition boundary

**Material progress.** The scientific language, result types, rejection rules
and inspected sibling mappings are concrete. `18b_composition.tex` implements
graph-to-adjustment-to-score composition, two static uses and a matched
DoubleML call. The history route reuses trajectory-score reduction; temporal
effects preserve a different output type. This is more than a wrapper census.

**Remaining.** The strongest actual reuse is still concentrated in static
adjustment, ratio construction and scalar/trajectory reduction. More difficult
interactions—an uncertain observation plan feeding a temporal model, changed
sampling assumptions invalidating a cached inference plan, or latent fitting
feeding a decision with the correct information—remain distributed across
separate constructions. Six dispatched reference recipes and correct matched
ATE semantics do not by themselves show that a custom application can compose
the central dynamic capabilities through stable public operations.

**Proposal closure.** Give a complete new scientific brief to the common
interfaces, carry it from source view through identification and fitting to
a financial report, and show a second consumer reusing those same components.
Include a supported configuration change and an economically incompatible
request. This can be a local reference journey; a deployed workbench, universal
causal compiler and accepted changes to sibling repositories are unnecessary.
The build/reuse/defer decisions should then follow the observed composition
cost and the scientific boundaries.

## V5-G6. Literature closure needs stronger evidence for decisive choices

**Material progress.** Citation custody is strong: genuine scholarly copies,
versions, hashes, inspected anchors and claim traces exist. Current additions
make several substantive method distinctions. More papers or pages alone
would not remedy the remaining problem.

**Remaining.** `scope-search-ledger.md` records topic searches returning only
15 results from much larger match sets, capped forward searches, and zero-match
index ambiguities. Some decisive results use preserved preprints while later
journal identities are known. These are disclosed limits, but the record does
not establish that the strongest alternatives or relevant changed results for
each central decision were adequately cleared. The general correction/version
disclaimer is weaker than a decision-specific account.

**Proposal closure.** For the core choices in G1–G5, trace the primary result,
its closest substantive challengers and any relevant later version/correction
to a common comparison of target, identification, sampling, approximation and
failure. Reconcile the decisive versions where available, and explain the
claim consequence where unavailable. A bounded stopping rationale based on
scientific alternatives is sufficient; an exhaustive field-wide systematic
review and access to every historical original are not required.

## V5-G7. Financial and scale evidence is improved but misses the central combination

**Material progress.** The public 401(k) analysis is a real financial-data
application with preserved data, distinct eligibility/participation targets,
two nuisance choices and matched DoubleML semantics. The new timings include
actual fitting and uncertainty work with verified thread counts. Earlier
synthetic banking studies already contain financial accounts and adverse
outcomes.

**Remaining.** The public example is cross-sectional. It does not exercise
the proposed product's central combination of long histories, changing bank
actions, observation reconstruction and temporal dependence. Its missing
survey design and uncertain control timing are acknowledged but not pursued
through a fuller design-sensitivity analysis. This is a gap in the empirical
demonstration, not evidence that its unknown causal truth is wrong.

The largest new search has 24 variables/eight lags; the largest finite-state
workload has six states/3,000 paths/24 decisions. There are two fresh processes
per case on one host. `19b_scope_scale.tex` gives useful partition and cost
arguments, but there is no corresponding complete parallel/distributed
qualification of fitting, calibration, nuisance splits and inference together.
The older precomputed-score reducer is genuine component evidence, not that
complete qualification. No estimate of labor savings or demand is measured.

**Proposal closure.** After the theoretical choices above, reproduce a public
dynamic financial analysis, or clearly justify a synthetic counterpart where
access prevents it. Compare matched scientific work using existing software
directly. Exercise the dominant partitioned computation against its serial
reference while retaining the same statistical experiment. Production service
levels, actual buyer demand and bank adoption can remain future institutional
validation; they are not prerequisites for scholarly sufficiency.

## Priority and stopping criterion

The first priority is G1/G2: sustained method-choice and inferential depth on
the central dynamic questions. G3/G4 follow because discovery and automatic
organization must be evaluated against the objects they claim to recover.
G5 should integrate those constructions; G6 accompanies each question rather
than becoming another separate literature-count phase. G7 tests the resulting
construction after its theory is settled.

The acceptance test is a reader reconstructing a new representative bank
question from available information through an identified target, justified
method, uncertainty, numerical behavior and reusable result—while seeing what
the major alternatives would change. Independent review can then challenge
that chain. It cannot replace writing the missing parts.

This assessment does not require universally valid inference under arbitrary
nonstationarity, identification of every hidden-state model, calibrated
confidence for every large PAG, autonomous semantic truth, runtime validation
of all 44 reviewed packages, or confidential data from a named bank. Those
would move the goalposts. V5's concrete improvements remain valid even though
the commissioned standard has not yet been met evenly across the proposal.
