## Skeptical review

The previous inventory sometimes prescribed complete installations before a
proposal recommendation. That confuses proposal evidence with product evidence.
The repaired comparison may recommend conditional reuse on inspected scientific
and source grounds while reserving distribution and numerical qualification for
the prototype. Conversely, a vendor landing page cannot establish identification,
performance, redistribution rights or a price. Those require appropriate named
technical and contractual evidence.

Each result must answer a decision question. Source counts, interface agreement,
successful compilation and additional pages are diagnostics rather than
scientific acceptance. Do not manufacture broad survey closure from a few
additional sources. Independent comparisons continue when another source is
unavailable. Stop the affected acquisition for challenge pages, corrupt files,
identity mismatch, post-cutoff scholarly material or exhausted attempts.
