## Judgment and comparison standard

The revision now supplies two connected banking constructions that were
missing from V5: a funding intervention with common calendar shocks,
persistent balances, learned controls and uncertain entity histories; and
a servicing decision with private historical actions, measurement-supported
estimation and a dynamically trained observable policy. Each connects its
information requirements to a target, estimator, uncertainty calculation,
failure behavior and reusable result. These are concrete proposal closures
for the seven assessed findings, with the restrictions below. They do not
establish a universal causal-analysis engine or a deployed bank product.

The benchmark passages were reread in the preserved
`v5-gap-assessment-excerpts/` directory. FinMarketing's uplift passage derives
the nuisance remainder before stating rates; its retention panel preserves
reused controls and financial exposure; its component chapter tests an
economically incompatible composition. BayesFilter's score chapter follows
initialization, masks and the actual evaluated likelihood; its filter-choice
chapter conditions an approximation on a numerical reference. CIP's method
choice assigns substantive challengers to one fixed scientific problem.
These are tests of reasoning and construction, not page-count targets.

Against those tests, funding now derives the exact pilot remainder, its
finite-basis rate and its date-level covariance; servicing carries the
information distinction through an actual belief policy; and the shared
operation composes source reconstruction, learning, calibration and a
financial account. The exact Gaussian and broad foundational chapters are
retained rather than replaced by simpler examples. No new nonlinear filter
is advertised without the BayesFilter-style likelihood/derivative/value
comparison. No assertion is made that every surveyed method has equal
implementation depth or that these reference monographs have themselves
been independently certified.

## Mathematical and scientific reconstruction

**Funding, cohort effects and donor inference.** In
`07c_dynamic_funding.tex`, the intervention changes one monthly price
innovation, leaving the future natural price rule intact. Recursive
substitution gives the response curve. Conditional residualization yields
the partialling-out moment; expansion with pilot errors gives
E[d_e d_h] − theta E[d_e²], including the often omitted second term.
Pilot independence alone does not make this zero. A fixed correct basis,
bounded penalty candidates and a proportional independent pilot give the
needed product rate under the stated stable law. Calendar influence vectors
retain overlapping-horizon and common-shock covariance before financial
aggregation. The financial account charges the higher first-month rate on
both baseline and induced balances. Baseline exposure, margins and discount
weights are fixed inputs, not estimated economic facts.

The stationary asymptotic argument and the simulation's zero start followed
by a 500-date burn are now distinguished explicitly. Dependence, stability,
fixed horizon and pilot-law invariance are substantive restrictions. Choosing
a favorable regime, horizon or bandwidth from evaluation outcomes invalidates
this inference. External IV, lag augmentation, panel moments, mixed-frequency
measurement and donor methods require different information; the chapter
explains the conditions for switching methods. It does not infer validity
from a generic learner interface.

`06c_panel_influence.tex` completes both quotient derivatives in normalized
DR cohort ATT and the estimated-cohort-share derivative. It combines reused
controls before variance reduction. Point double robustness at a misspecified
nuisance limit is distinguished from orthogonal inference at the truth.
The donor route fixes the randomized eligible region set and refits the same
procedure for every possible assignment. Its rank proof is exact for the
sharp null; inversion is for a sharp constant-effect model. The conformal
challenger trades assignment information for explicit temporal prediction
and stability assumptions, rather than inheriting randomization validity.

Four hundred funding repetitions per date regime retain the full draws.
Financial coverage is .945/.9375 at 360/1,440 origins, while treating branches
as independent calendar information gives .230/.2175. Matched statsmodels
residual-regression/HAC computation agrees numerically. These are finite
regime observations with Monte Carlo error, not uniform validity proofs.
Normalized ATT/cohort-share directional derivatives and the complete
20-assignment donor rank calculation supply separate finite checks.

**Private-state servicing and dynamic decisions.** In
`10d_audited_decisions.tex`, the original nine unavailable fits are reproduced
at their actual seeds and traced to transition-logit boundaries. Conditional
coverage, bias, empirical spread, mean standard error and availability are
reported separately. In particular, the 600-label usable fits do not mainly
show underestimated standard errors. Increasing emission calibration alone
does not add scarce state/action transitions. This is a supported scarcity
diagnosis, not a claim to have proved a unique cause or a global likelihood
maximum. The failed 199-refit bootstrap remains failed.

Two distinct alternatives are developed. With 3,000 observational paths and
600 emission labels, all 120 new hidden-likelihood fits are available and
coverage is .9417 in the same mechanism. Fully audited trajectories instead
change the observation design: operationally defined predecision states,
accurate representative audits, Markov sufficiency and production invariance
are required. Row-frequency/reward estimators have whole-path influences,
preserving covariance among initial law, emissions, logger, transitions and
rewards. An empty category makes interior inference unavailable; it is not
hidden by pseudocounts.

The four-decision policy is optimized on an independent 2,000-path audit
pilot. Its full signal-history tree is frozen before evaluating it. Actions
cannot use evaluation state labels or cash settled after the horizon.
Bellman recursion removes the private logger under intervention; the
fixed-tree value is evaluated by unnormalized state-mass propagation.
Independent hidden-path enumeration and count/value perturbations check
the value and derivatives. The interval is conditional on training, not an
interval for the unknown optimal value. At 1,200 evaluation audits all 400
fits are available and learned-policy coverage is .950. At 300 audits, 54
empty-category failures reduce all-request coverage to .8275. Accurate
labels are consequential: a registered 10% random misclassification example
has zero coverage and large bias.

On that same learned policy and mechanism, the exact public-history
g-formula gives −35.647 against the interventional contrast 25.029. Thus
the observed-history challenger fails without estimation error. Validated
hidden likelihood, complete-state audits and proximal bridges are compared
as different information designs. The Bennett source's hybrid-law proxy
exclusions, allowed policy information and indicator in the outcome bridge
were checked against the primary text. One public signal is not relabeled
as two validated proxies. Arbitrary nonlinear private-state inference stays
outside the initial offering; exact Gaussian and bounded finite-state
routes remain supported reference constructions.

**Latent graph and measurement targets.** `08c_ancestral_targets.tex` defines
an explicit two-period DAG with six observed variables and a finite initial
law. The maximal ancestral margin uses all observed separating subsets and
true ancestry. All 240 d-separation queries agree between independent
NetworkX and Tigramite implementations. Population LPCMCI recovers twelve
adjacencies with twelve correct definite endpoints and twelve circles.
Circles are not scored as incorrect orientations. This is a finite episode
experiment, not a silent truncation of an infinite stationary process.

Finite data remain adverse: 40 repetitions give adjacency precision .9977
but definite-endpoint accuracy only .6605. Weak population partial
correlations explain a concrete difficulty without establishing a unique
failure cause. A binary front-door example recovers .18 against the naive
.42, under an asserted scientific graph; an observationally indistinguishable
two-SCM example has effects 1 and 0. The front-door result is not falsely
attributed to a learned PAG. General graph-selection confidence is not
claimed, and more estimation data do not certify selected graph assumptions.

`09c_linked_histories.tex` distinguishes trusted provenance from true
economic meaning. Five manually specified source-owner fixtures give
correct interpretation, conflict, missing metadata, joint-owner abstention
and a unique wrong acceptance under a false trusted assertion. These are
independently specified relative to the algorithm, but synthetic and
authored within this project; they are not independent bank validation.
Provenance flags simulate asserted authority, not cryptographic verification.

The linkage construction uses a calibrated one-to-one pair map with two
different portfolio responses. It inverts a stratum-specific mixing matrix
and propagates both the full calendar covariance and the independent audit
Jacobian. Near rank failure, regular inference is withheld. The map is
redrawn by origin and shared across that origin's entire response window;
it is not a persistent customer alias across the entire calendar. The
candidate basis omits the potentially misassigned lagged balance, with its
conditional marginalization justified under the declared Gaussian driver
law. General persistent or outcome-dependent linkage needs a new map law.
Independent audit information and pilot-fixed choices prevent evaluation
outcomes selecting metadata questions in the executed design.

Financial coverage is .9625/.950 over 400 repetitions with 100/400 audits
per stratum. The calibration variance contribution is modest relative to
calendar variation in this experiment, and is reported as such. Naive map
bias and biased-audit failures remain visible. The actual Guha journal PDF
supports a substantive joint-posterior challenger, whose outcome feedback,
population, no-duplicates and covariate restrictions differ from this model.

**Common product and public/computational evidence.**
`18c_dynamic_composition.tex` follows raw candidate records and arrival
revisions into the fitted temporal response and its financial interval.
Predecision availability and retrospective outcome extraction use different
clocks. The corrected public operation accepts only the account unit and
received-day interpretation qualified by its materializer. Source assertions,
extraction cutoff, actual data, pilot/audit arrays, economic target and
learning/covariance procedure are bound to the analysis identity. Changing
those scientific inputs invalidates cached inference. Two consumers receive
the same result, with an externally fixed per-account denominator. A margin
change is supported; stock-to-flow and independent-branch requests are
rejected. This is a local reusable library journey, not accepted sibling
integration or a generic identification compiler.

The full partitioned experiment reduces calibration, pilot fits and tuning,
response moments and globally centered lag covariance with cross-partition
halos. All 18 fresh-process cases preserve the same experiment and agree
with a separate linked-response implementation to below 5e−10. Worker count
is excluded from scientific identity. Serial execution is faster at these
sizes and is the selected default. Parent RSS is a whole-process maximum
including untimed verification, not isolated workflow or simultaneous total
worker memory. Multi-host throughput and production economics are unmeasured.

`20c_public_dynamic.tex` preserves and reproduces a 203-quarter monetary
dataset with four fixed design sensitivities and matched OLS/HAC computation.
Changing controls or pre/post-1984 origin windows changes the projection
substantially. Quarterly averaging, private policy information, measurement
regimes and the distinction between M1 and bank deposits block the claimed
bank intervention interpretation. The inspected H.8 description likewise
lacks the pricing assignment and entity-linked audit evidence required here.
This is a concrete design investigation and empirical projection, complemented
by the synthetic causal experiment; unknown empirical truth is not invented.

No unresolved blocking finding remains for the selected V6 proposal
constructions. This judgment is narrower than claiming the full original
ambition implemented or every surveyed route equally developed. General
nonlinear POMDPs, large-PAG confidence and richer linkage are scientific
extensions, not items that become valid merely by passing bank acceptance.
If one is promoted into a supported release, its missing theory, numerics
and adverse evaluation must be developed first.

The next institutional tranche is independent challenge of the derived
arguments, a bounded bank study with real observation/assignment evidence,
agreed sibling interfaces and matched workflow economics. Those are distinct
from the proposal scholarship executed here. User acceptance, external
review, confidential bank validation and deployment have not occurred.
