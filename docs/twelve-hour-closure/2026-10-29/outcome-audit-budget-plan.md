# Equal-resource comparison for random history recovery

The independent-pilot study reduces width but gives the assisted method5000 extra initial records and about775 extra recoveries. A new comparison must charge those resources. It may not relabel the previous unequal-resource result as cost superiority.

For each of500 replications under each routine observation mechanism, generate one7000-customer cohort, with independent random25%recovery among all incomplete records. All methods receive exactly the same initial records and recovered histories. Compare:

1. Reward-weighted recovery using all7000 customers.
2. Pilot-assisted score with5000 training and2000 evaluation customers, matching the previous allocation.
3. Pilot-assisted score with2000 training and5000 evaluation customers.
4. Pilot-assisted score with1000 training and6000 evaluation customers.
5. Two-fold cross-fitting: each half of3500 customers fits the score for the other half;average all held-out scores. Report an ordinary pooled-score interval only as an empirically evaluated candidate. Conditional independence of every evaluation score given one common pilot no longer applies;no automatic import of the split-sample exact argument or general cross-fit qualification.

Threshold0.5 and never act stay fixed. Same quadratic regression basis,known assignment probabilities,known random completed recovery,all masks and observation laws are inherited. Train/evaluate partitions are fixed by index before outcomes. Newly seeded replications;no retuning split fractions after results. Report paired interval-width changes,coverage,bias,RMSE,positive-lower-bound frequency,actual initial/recovery counts and mean squared estimated standard error versus empirical variance. Truth references unchanged with numerical uncertainty shown.

Equal realized resources make the comparison informative for constant per-record/per-recovery costs. Do not invent bank prices or assume all historical records have the same real cost. Fits and runtime are recorded but local time is not a bank operating-cost estimate. No claim of global optimality among split fractions,no ranking by noise,all declared alternatives retained. A fixed500-replication count cannot prove uniform95%coverage.

Review: the split-sample methods have conditional unbiasedness with known action and recovery laws. Cross-fitting preserves unbiasedness of each held-out contribution but introduces dependence between contributions through fitted models;the pooled variance formula needs its own asymptotic argument or empirical qualification and remains labelled as such. This experiment tests the construction rather than assuming it works. No policy selection,no future-data leakage,no bank observations.
