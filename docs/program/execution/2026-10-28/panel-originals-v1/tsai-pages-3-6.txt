treatments. This is achieved through being the most selective with the control group,
thus I trade some statistical power for a more credible parallel trend assumption.

    Additionally, Fricke (2017) and Roller and Steinberg (2023) both study a two-treatment
DiD in a two-period setup, Hull (2018) study a two-period setup with multiple treatment
arms. Finally, Matthay et al. (2022) and Griffin et al. (2023) document the correlation
among state policies and their potential confoundedness.

All proofs are collected in Appendix A. Theorem 1 shows two sources of bias. First,
