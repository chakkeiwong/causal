C. More Visualization Results
C.1. Table-Centric Original Visual Representation Visualizations

We present extended PCA visualizations of LLaVA’s original visual representations across different
network depths. All visualizations follow the same color scheme as main text: green dots represent
"dining table" tokens, while red dots indicate its top-10 co-occurring objects in instruction data.
Across all layers, we observe three key patterns:

      1. Early-stage entanglement: Layers 0-5 maintain tight clustering driven by projector-induced
         biases, consistent with main text findings (figs. 7 and 8).
      2. Mid-layer persistence: Surprisingly, strong co-occurrence patterns persist until layer 25
         despite increasing semantic abstraction (figs. 9 and 10).
      3. Final-layer retention: Even at the final prediction stage (layer 40), dining tables retain
         significant entanglement with co-occurring objects (fig. 11), confirming the lasting impact
         of training data biases.

These results reinforce our main conclusion that instruction data biases create persistent represen-
tational structures that survive through most stages of the vision-language understanding pipeline.
The progressive visualization sequence provides empirical validation of how initial representational
biases propagate through network depth, resisting the expected semantic disentanglement in later
processing stages.
