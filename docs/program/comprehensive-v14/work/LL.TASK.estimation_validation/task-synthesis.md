# Estimation and validation for agent-assisted causal banking

## Scope and evidence status

This synthesis addresses the estimation-and-validation lane of the proposal. It is a literature and source inspection result, supplemented by exact project derivations; it is not a live model evaluation, a bank-data validation, or a production qualification. The primary inspected texts are Chou et al., *A Human-Augmenting Agentic Workflow for Observational Causal Inference* (2026, arXiv v1), Leban and Sun, *CausalDS: Benchmarking Causal Reasoning in Data-Science Agents* (2026, arXiv v1), Alaa et al., *Large Language Models as Co-Pilots for Causal Inference in Medical Studies* (2024, arXiv v1), and Zhang et al., *Evidence-Grounded Auditing of Identification Assumptions in Climate-Policy Causal Evaluations* (2026, arXiv v1). Their genuine PDFs and source audits are retained in `docs/papers/` and `docs/program/repair-v12/work/LA01/source-audit.json`; the cited performance figures remain author-reported evidence. The selected OCI and CausalDS source trees are pinned, but neither package was executed here.

The appropriate standard is a typed sequence of decisions. First specify the target population, action, outcome, horizon and information clock. Then decide whether that target is identified under the declared graph and measurement law. Only then choose an estimator and assess finite-sample calibration. Diagnostics describe the data and fitted procedure; they do not prove identification. An agent may assist each step, but a completed-looking number is not a successful answer when a preceding state is unknown, nonidentified or malformed.

## What the frontier evidence supports, and what it does not

OCI supplies a useful human-principal, actor/critic workflow for point-in-time effects under supplied unconfoundedness. Its paper reports 231 ACIC datasets, a ten-dataset scaffolding comparison and disguised LaLonde analyses. These are promising source findings, not local replication. Its notebook trims observations outside a propensity interval and then uniformly averages retained AIPW pseudo-outcomes while serializing the result as ATO. That is a trimmed-population target; conventional overlap weighting uses weights proportional to $e(X)(1-e(X))$. The proposal must require the agent to print the target, trimming rule, retained denominator and weighting measure before reporting an effect. The notebook runner also uses an unbounded timeout, so it is not admissible as an unrestricted CLI or MCP operation.

CausalDS offers the strongest evaluation pattern for this lane: narrated SCM scenes, a separate observation model, private ground truth, identification-gated Rung-2 and Rung-3 tasks, and first-class abstention. Its reported 100-scene realistic exam shows that symbolic success, interval quality, abstention and cost can diverge across models; those figures are dated author results, not a bank forecast. The inspected scorer computes both uncapped and capped normalized interval scores, plus coverage and width. The dashboard must retain both. Its identification helper can default missing metadata toward identifiable, so the adapter must use three states—identified, nonidentified and unknown—and must distinguish malformed output from principled abstention.

Alaa et al. motivate a causal-question co-pilot: eligibility, baseline time, incident versus prevalent treatment, follow-up and outcome definitions are clarified before analysis. Their examples are illustrative study-design assistance, not longitudinal-policy validation. ARGUS shows why evidence retrieval status must remain separate from scientific credibility: a missing passage may reflect weak retrieval rather than absent support, and its five-paper human pilot is in-sample and preliminary. The banking design therefore records retrieval quality, unresolved assumption and adjudication state separately.

## Worked banking question and exact baseline

Suppose a bank asks whether an early restructuring review offered at an account's first qualifying arrears date changes discounted net recovery over 180 days. The unit is the eligible account at that date. Let $A$ be the offer, $Y$ the net recovery with the offer cost defined once, $X$ the record available immediately before the offer, and $P$ the declared eligible population. The initial estimand is
\[
\tau_P=E_P[Y(1)-Y(0)].
\]
An acceptance or eventual restructuring term is a different action. A hardship flag entered on day 11 cannot be a day-10 covariate merely because its event date is earlier. This clock rule is enforced before identification.

A simple exact fixture has three strata with masses $(1/2,3/10,1/5)$, treatment propensities $(1/20,1/2,19/20)$ and conditional effects $(0,4,12)$. The full-population effect is $18/5=3.6$. If the predeclared rule retains only propensities in $[0.1,0.9]$, the retained-population effect is $4$. Conventional overlap weighting gives
\[
\frac{\sum_s p_s e_s(1-e_s)\tau_s}{\sum_s p_s e_s(1-e_s)}=\frac{1656}{433}\approx3.82448.
\]
All three numbers are valid answers to different questions. A report that calls the uniformly averaged trimmed result “ATO” without displaying the rule answers neither the full-population nor conventional overlap target.

The baseline estimator is a cross-fitted AIPW estimate only after the target and assumptions pass review: consistency, no unmeasured confounding for the declared action, positivity on the target population, correct time ordering and an appropriate sampling/interference law. A balance plot, overlap plot or placebo can diagnose problems; none certifies hidden confounding. If a graph leaves the effect nonidentified, the correct result is nonidentifiable or unknown, not a numerically precise estimate.

## Validation contract and failure cases

Every request stores: target ID, unit, time zero, action contrast, outcome and horizon, eligible population, graph/measurement version, identification state, estimator, split/seed, interval method, diagnostics, all-request denominator and failure state. Results have separate states for `identified`, `nonidentified`, `unknown`, `unsupported`, `malformed` and `failed`. A critic may select a report for review, but selection is not conditional coverage; publish coverage over all requests and over selected requests, with the selection rule and refusals.

A validation fixture should include: (i) a known randomized effect, (ii) a confounded but identified back-door effect, (iii) an unmeasured-confounding graph with a nonidentified effect, (iv) a proxy observation model in which conceptual identification does not imply identification from the noisy column, (v) invalid time ordering, (vi) no-overlap strata, and (vii) malformed or infinite interval endpoints. CausalDS's interval score is useful only after finite endpoint and ordering checks; retain uncapped tails because capping can reverse method rankings. For example, normalized interval-score pairs $(1,100)$ and $(9,9)$ have uncapped means $50.5$ and $9$, so the second procedure is preferable on the uncapped mean. Applying the source scorer's cap of $10$ changes the means to $5.5$ and $9$, reversing the ranking. This is an exact comparison of admissible nonnegative score values, not a model run. Similarly, six covered intervals among six accepted requests after two refusals yield selected coverage $1$ but all-request return-and-cover $3/4$; neither number alone establishes nominal conditional coverage. The all-request denominator must include refusals and failed executions, while the estimand-specific denominator remains visible.

The next prototype should adapt the OCI specification and critic artifacts behind fixed, bounded operations; retain CausalDS's private truth/scoring separation after independently auditing its generator and scorer; and use a deterministic analyst baseline with the same briefs, clocks and fixtures. Direct package integration is not yet admitted. A live provider comparison requires named model/version, custody, pricing, fixed prompts and independent judgments. A bank study remains conditional on prototype evidence identifying a decision-relevant uncertainty and positive value of information.

## Remaining gaps and research actions

A targeted reassessment of the inherited omissions register and the later source census changes the disposition of two leads: ORCA (candidate 241) and *How to Make Causal Inferences Using Texts* (candidate 175) now have inspected original PDFs and citation records in the later source audit. ORCA offers a shared relational state, but its PAG-to-DAG choice adds assumptions and its reported completion comparison does not establish effect accuracy. The text study is relevant to constructing proxies or covariates from documents, not proof that textual adjustment removes hidden confounding. Their older omission entries remain historical evidence of the earlier acquisition state, not a current claim of missing originals. The epidemiology causal-agent paper (31), weighted-estimand robustness (256), StatsPAI (266), text-confounding review (269) and other unmapped candidates remain uninspected in this lane; metadata titles are discovery leads, not technical evidence. The logged Crossref/OpenAlex searches sampled only ten ranked results per broad query, with no pagination or saturation claim. The immediate gap is coverage and independent replication, not a reason to infer superiority. The package gaps are executable environment closure, dependency/license closure, target-preserving adapters, unknown-state handling, finite interval guards and selection-aware validation. The bank gaps are semantic ownership, assignment evidence, feasible actions, interference, transport and independent adjudication. Each is recorded as a later input rather than silently filled by synthetic evidence.

The recommended decision is to build a narrow, fail-closed prototype around the typed contract and exact fixtures. It may recommend, refuse or defer an analysis; it may not convert a predictive fit, a retrieved passage or an agent confidence score into causal truth. Success for this proposal is a reproducible design and bounded evidence portfolio that justifies the prototype investment. Bank validation, production reliability and measured ROI remain later claims.
