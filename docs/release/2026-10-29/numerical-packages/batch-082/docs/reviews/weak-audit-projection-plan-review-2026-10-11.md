# Pre-execution challenge of the weak-audit projection plan

11 October 2026. Author plan review, completed before fresh final draws.

The construction answers a narrower question than general calibration. Exact
audit inversion is attractive where the V10 Wilson rank guard refused 1,549
of 2,000 near-rank requests, but it does not make the unknown candidate response
law exact. The plan therefore allocates error separately and conditions its
asymptotic conclusion on the dependent score CLT, nuisance rate and origin-swap
law. The Monte Carlo study must retain false exogeneity to demonstrate this
boundary, and must report infinite width whenever projection reaches rank one.

The candidate score must be formed on the *observable* mixed responses before
audit inversion, with the fixed monetary weights and stratum denominator.
Using the already inverted scalar funding score would make the primitive bands
depend on a near-singular estimated audit probability and defeat the design.
The four marginal response bands need a simultaneous familywise allocation;
two exact audit intervals get a separate allocation. The direct cost is fixed,
not estimated. Independence between pilot and evaluation is required; serial
dependence within either is allowed by the qualified law.

Numerical review requires exact-binomial endpoint checks, corner and rank-one
projection tests, dense score and lag-covariance comparisons, and explicit
rejection of invalid counts, declarations, dimensions and nonfinite histories.
An empty projected set remains a failure or incompatibility outcome; it may not
be silently replaced with the whole line. An unbounded set may cover but cannot
meet a finite bank precision threshold. Conditional coverage among finite sets
may be descriptive only; all-request coverage is primary.

The 600-request precision does not resolve small differences around 0.95.
Five fixed designs and paired draws support a bounded comparison, not universal
selection of the new procedure. The baseline interval remains a comparator,
not a gold standard. A wrong exogeneity declaration can invalidate both
methods. The V10 monograph and manifest must remain byte-identical; the new
supplement has its own receipt. Approved for execution with these conditions.
