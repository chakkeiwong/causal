# Three local journeys with common result semantics

`JourneyRequest` requires the family, sampling law, population, unit, information
clock, inference method and fixed monetary scale. `run_journey` binds the request,
data and implementation identities and returns a qualified result. Treasury and
application consumers retain the same inference description. The application
consumer adds a fixed-account rescaling without replacing the underlying set.

| Family | Sampling and target | Required information | Refusal |
|---|---|---|---|
| `persistent_funding` | Independent complete pairs; focal randomized unit-action response | Persistent candidate histories, one audit label per selected pair, known positive inclusion probabilities | Calendar shocks, unknown label error, invalid probabilities or incompatible clock |
| `nonlinear_servicing` | Independent main and audited trajectories; fixed pilot-policy value | Signal, private action, reward and accurate independent state-labelled audits | Current hidden state in the policy, incomplete histories, boundary/unresolved numerical fit |
| `discovery_decision` | Independent finite Gaussian Z,A,Y episodes; intervention derivative | Declared latent/observed/encouragement structural class | General dependent calendar inference or treating a selected graph as confidence |

For servicing, state audits identify and fit the model. The evaluated threshold
policy uses the current observable signal and date. It cannot access that
customer's current audit label or future records. Monetary reward units are
declared before inference. A result marked unavailable may retain a research
point calculation, but its interval is withheld.

Graph ambiguity may yield the whole line. The result requests validated
encouragement/exclusion or sufficient measurements; it does not orient a graph
because a favorable answer is desired. Empty, disconnected and unbounded sets
retain their topology in JSON. A numerical result is conditional on the supplied
scientific facts; neither a hash nor an authenticated assertion establishes
semantic truth.

`BlockedIVRequest` supplies a separate bounded dependent learner family, with
temporal separation, explicit rates and training-only tuning. It cannot be
composed with persistent-pair inference without a new joint sampling argument.
The local interfaces are demonstrated with new eligible requests and two
consumers. Sibling mappings remain proposals until their owners accept them.
