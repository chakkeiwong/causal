1    The decision this proposal supports

A universal bank needs to know when a proposed action is likely to improve a financial out-
come, which assumptions make that answer possible, and when the available information cannot
distinguish competing decisions. The proposed investment is a small reusable causal-analysis
service supporting four journeys: funding responses, sequential servicing, intervention queries after
causal discovery, and the construction of trustworthy observations. Its immediate deliverable is a
bounded prototype and an evidence-based recommendation about what to build, reuse or leave
unsupported.
The recommendation is to develop and compare a narrow set of these operations using public
records, documented public experiments and generated cases with known truth. Existing local
research supplies selected working constructions and substantial negative evidence. It does not
yet establish a complete product or a positive return on deployment. Expensive bank experiments
should follow a prototype result that makes their cost worth considering. The absence of bank data,
institutional sign-off or production access does not prevent completion of the present proposal. [1]
This brief accompanies the 19 October 2026 working monograph, which has 1,105 pages and
802 audited citation records, including 207 genuine scholarly PDFs. The software, LLM/agent
and integration comparisons have expanded substantially, but their coverage is still incomplete.
Aggregate length does not establish completeness. Literature coverage retains the 7 October
2026 cutoff, with later acquisitions and uncertain historical versions explicitly distinguished. The
recommendation remains provisional where evidence or comparison is incomplete. [2]

1.1   The reusable product and its customer

The first user is an analyst who can state a banking decision and inspect its assumptions. The
reusable input records a target population, action and comparator, information available before
the action, outcome and horizon, measurement units, observation process, causal restrictions and
sampling unit. An offer, its receipt and the later balance are different variables. A measured
association is not automatically the effect of changing an offer.
The output keeps the target attached to its evidence. It may be an identified formula, an estimate
with justified uncertainty, a set of compatible effects, an informative refusal or a failed computation.
Unresolved graph endpoints, unbounded confidence sets and numerical failures require different
responses. The library, command line and MCP interface should preserve these distinctions so
that a downstream application can decide whether to act, ask for information or fall back to its
existing workflow. A callable endpoint alone does not establish identification, useful precision or
interoperability. [28]
The shared layer would organize three workspaces: intervention studies, sequential decisions and
dynamic systems. The four banking journeys pass through these workspaces, with observation
quality affecting all three. This organization avoids building a separate estimation stack for every
business team while retaining each team’s population, timing and economic account. The initial
service would require analyst review of consequential scientific choices. No autonomous bank
action is proposed.


1.2   A concrete first slice

A useful first demonstration begins with a public or generated business brief, constructs an explicit
question and alternative diagrams, checks the available identification route, and returns a result
whose assumptions and information limits remain visible through the library, CLI and MCP. For
a randomized offer, the first target can be the effect of assignment. A receipt effect requires
additional compliance and exclusion assumptions. For a confounded observational table, the
correct result may be a documented ambiguity rather than a number.
The comparison must include an expert-designed template and a directly composed existing-
package workflow. The new service earns its place only if the common representation and checks
reduce consequential mistakes or repeated effort without discarding valid alternatives. A generated
example can expose a wrong answer cheaply; it cannot establish how often the same failure occurs
at a bank.

2.2   Servicing and learned policies

Selected earlier servicing studies support fixed-policy inference in a restricted nonlinear family
under truthful state audits, supplied historical action probabilities and independent customer
trajectories. Their wrong-logger and information-leakage cases remain failures. They do not
establish the value of an unrestricted learned policy with hidden borrower state. [32]
A new argument handles estimated action and observation probabilities in a finite, prespecified
history table with bounded rewards. It separates training from evaluation and makes the support
and observation assumptions explicit. Exact calculations check its policy-value identities and
counterexamples. Its conservative uncertainty can nevertheless exceed the complete target range at
a representative sample size. The public servicing design uses continuous histories and unbounded
Gaussian/quadratic quantities, so this finite-table theorem cannot simply be attached to that
design. A useful next study needs a reviewed tighter argument, a separately declared bounded
target, or a different experiment. It must preserve the failed predecessor rather than change the
target after evaluation. [33]


2.3   Discovery and agent support

Graph discovery constrains possible relationships under stated assumptions. It may return an
equivalence class whose members imply different interventions. The proposed service must retain
that class and distinguish uncertainty in graph learning from uncertainty in an effect. Exact
latent-risk examples in the current comparison exhibit identical observed Gaussian distributions
with action effects of zero and three fifths. They prove nonidentification within a class containing
both models; they do not enumerate the entire identified set. [34]
LLMs are proposed as aids for translating business language, finding missing timing definitions,
suggesting diagrams and alternatives, and preparing auditable tool requests. A model-generated
diagram is a candidate assumption, not observed causal truth. The evaluation must score
false confidence, unsupported orientations, omitted alternatives and appropriate requests for

3.1   What the expanded interface work now demonstrates

The proposal now develops request admission, result interpretation, publication integrity, retention
and capacity as parts of the same workflow. A local standalone-client check made six actual
library and CLI calls across three requests: a conditional expression, an unsupported sampling
setting and a contradicted premise. Their scientific meanings agreed. Four effect-set examples
were preserved response replays; they were not four fresh calculations. Five malformed replies
were rejected. This evidence supports the inspected local examples and their declared schemas.
It does not establish a running notebook kernel, a newly executed MCP integration, another
repository’s adoption or production service levels. [44]
The retention argument is likewise a design result with a finite reference. Across ten allowed
publication/collection orders, a stale-snapshot collector loses an artifact needed by a live request
in three orders. A coordinated recheck against current roots avoids that failure in all ten, with
nine successful publications and one safe rejection. This motivates pins, a live-root check and an
explicit rule for stale publishers. It is not an estimated failure probability or an implemented
storage collector. The prototype should test those semantics under realistic interruption and
restart before an operating guarantee is proposed. [45]

3.3    R identification and framework evaluation

The expanded R comparison distinguishes graphical adjustment, symbolic ID/IDC, counterfactual
identification, restricted sharp bounds and Bayesian causal queries. These operations answer
different questions. In an exact authored repayment example, a point-identified average effect
coexists with a nontrivial counterfactual interval, while two admissible priors give different posterior
summaries. The supplied assumptions and result type therefore need to survive any language
adapter. Selected pinned sources support the comparison; native R execution, broader census
and complete dependency/rights qualification remain unresolved. The causaleffect disposition is
reference use with executable integration deferred. [48]
Two additional originals help specify a framework comparison. The selected autonomous-agent
survey organizes profile, memory, planning and action, and discusses prompt fragility, hallucination,
information leakage and efficiency. PromptBench provides a documented modular evaluation
design and custom metric hooks; its historical findings do not show that one prompt is best for
every task. These sources support a proposed evaluator whose references check the banking target,
assumptions and valid nonidentification responses. They do not establish a qualified causal agent,
current model ranking or licensed software integration. [49, 50]
An exact bookkeeping illustration separates completion from correctness. Across forty fixed
requests in twenty paired families, stipulated system A has 22 correct, two wrong and sixteen
unanswered requests; B has thirty correct, six wrong and four unanswered. Accuracy among
completed requests favors A, whereas correct responses per planned request favor B. With assumed
value and error costs, their net-value ranking can reverse. These are authored outcomes and
prices, not observed model or bank performance. A justified scientific refusal can be a completed
correct answer; an execution failure remains in the planned denominator. The comparison should
preserve both distinctions and pairing. [51, 52]

A new exact servicing example separates causal attribution from incremental value. The stipulated

rules. Public and synthetic evidence can establish whether the proposed interfaces preserve those
contracts and whether further experiments are worthwhile. The truth of a bank’s assignment and

and a counterfactual structural model are separate deliverables. No installed native engine or
upstream test was run. Other C++ candidates remain open. [85]
The selected counterfactual builder reconstructs from the observational Bayesian network and
replaces selected parentless-node priors with their individual posterior marginals. Conditioning
can induce dependence among those roots. An exact authored example has independent fair
roots U,V, and observed profile A=0,Y=1 requiring U=V. The joint posterior equality probability
is1; multiplying the separate root marginals instead gives1/2. Two structural worlds share
the same observed and marginal intervention laws but have individual counterfactual success
probabilities1and0 for this profile. Thus neither an intervention average nor a stable numerical
answer supplies missing cross-world coupling. This is source-motivated arithmetic, not a measured
native defect. [85, 86]
With an assumed USD100 success payoff and USD20 action cost, those answers imply conditional
values of USD80, USD-20 and USD30. They are not incremental returns against a measured
baseline. Retain structural functions and joint abduction, justify a sufficient posterior factorization,
provide a justified set over couplings, or refuse the individual answer. The Rcpp dosearch alternative
also needs typed timeout versus nonidentification states and its supported environment. Reuse
remains deferred pending independent-oracle conformance, exact graph identity, numeric support,
wrapper and rights checks. [85, 86]

3.14    EconML: a reference choice with target and history safeguards

The selected EconML source, at a 1 October 2026 commit declaring version 0.17.0, is a useful
reference for heterogeneous effects and selected dynamic-panel comparisons. Its effect model and
supplied prediction population determine what is averaged. A flexible nuisance learner cannot
correct an omitted effect modifier by itself. The proposed adapter must preserve the action
contrast, pre-action adjustment, effect-function class, population and monetary units. This is a
completed proposal choice; executable integration remains deferred. [90]
An exact authored example assigns half the customers to each of two observed strata. Offer
probabilities are one half and one tenth, while stratum effects are USD 0 and USD 100. The
population effect is USD 50. A deliberately misspecified constant residual regression instead
targets USD 450/17, about USD 26.47, because it weights strata by treatment variation. At an
assumed cost of USD 35, the population decision has net value USD 15; mislabelling the projection
gives about USD -8.53. This is target misspecification, not a reported library error under a correct
effect model. [91]
The selected dynamic path infers periods from row occurrence inside a customer, requires equal
history lengths and ignores supplied sample weights and sample variances with a warning. The
inspected bootstrap call path resamples time rows inside fixed customer groups, so it can duplicate
or reverse a history. An authored two-date enumeration preserves the original ordered history in
only one of four equally likely row draws. No installed EconML bootstrap was run, and this is not
a measured coverage failure. Group-aware nuisance folds and within-unit covariance also do not
establish protection against common calendar shocks across customers. [90, 91]
Start with a narrow public or synthetic static prototype, using exact nuisance and target references.
Then test chronological unit/time conversion, weight refusal, actual bootstrap draws, warnings
and declared sampling laws before admitting a dynamic adapter. Preserve applicable MIT and
BSD notices, lock package and native dependencies, and measure fitting, resampling, failed-run
and maintenance costs. A Python boundary is a candidate; a wholesale port is not justified by
the present evidence. Public conformance can establish whether this route merits later bank trials.
[90]

5    Economics and the next experiment

Let N be the number of attempted decision tasks in one year, D the incremental gross monetary
utility of the proposed action relative to the chosen baseline, F fixed incremental cost, c variable
incremental cost per attempted task, and R an additional risk cost not already counted in D. A
transparent planning account is

                                     V = N E[D] − F − N c − R.


                                                   13
Causal banking: product proposal                                                  19 October 2026


The baseline must be a feasible alternative under the same information. A correct refusal may
leave the baseline action unchanged while still costing review time. A misleading recommendation
can have negative utility. Loss must not be counted in both D and R, and unacceptable actions
remain excluded by constraints irrespective of their average financial payoff. [92]


5.1   A reproducible illustration, with assumed inputs

The following one-year illustration uses USD solely as a declared planning unit. Assume 10,000
attempted tasks, fixed incremental cost of USD 100,000, variable incremental cost of USD 6 per
task and no separate R. All volumes, prices, gains, probabilities and signal likelihoods below
are assumptions; none is a measured bank benefit or vendor quote. The calculation excludes
discounting because its horizon is one year. [92]


           Regime      Prior    Gross gain/task         Net value   Pr(positive signal)
           High           0.4          USD 40       USD 240,000                     0.8
           Moderate       0.4          USD 20        USD 40,000                     0.5
           Adverse        0.2          USD −2      USD −180, 000                    0.1


Expected incremental value is USD 76,000 before further information. This number alone cannot
justify a real investment because its inputs are assumed. Under the same assumptions, an
experiment produces a positive signal with probability 0.54. Expected net value conditional on
that signal is approximately USD 150,370, and conditional on a negative signal it is approximately
minus USD 11,304. Choosing to build only after a positive signal gives expected value USD 81,200
before the experiment’s own cost.
The expected value of sample information is consequently USD 5,200. An experiment costing USD
8,000 loses USD 2,800 in expected information value; at USD 3,000 it gains USD 2,200, assuming
identical signal informativeness. Perfect information has value USD 36,000 in this illustration.
That is an upper bound on the information component of spending for this particular decision
and prior, not a universal experiment budget. The prototype must help assess the assumed signal
quality before these calculations can inform an expensive study. [92]
Holding other assumptions fixed, break-even gross gain is USD 16 per attempted task. Halving
volume raises it to USD 26; doubling volume reduces it to USD 11. An additional USD 4 of
review cost per task raises the 10,000-task threshold to USD 20. These sensitivities are arithmetic
consequences, not estimates of demand or analyst effort. Refusals and failed requests still incur
applicable costs and remain among the attempted tasks. [93]


5.2   Costs to measure in the prototype

The measured account should separate data acquisition and cleaning, labels and identity audits,
model and tool calls, analyst formulation and checking, package setup, integration and maintenance,
compute and storage, incident recovery and independent validation. Each cost needs its unit,
quantity, date/version, price source and incremental share relative to the baseline. The current
actual-price fields remain unfilled. Public examples can reveal setup burden and local resource
use without establishing bank-scale service levels, customer outcomes or willingness to adopt. [92]
A later bank experiment should state the decisions it distinguishes, the uncertain assumption that
matters, the target population and horizon, expected signals, losses from wrong decisions, and the
full cost of obtaining the information. It should compare cheaper public/generated alternatives
first. If no feasible experiment has plausible positive net information value, narrow or defer the

The software comparison still has 74 open and 26 inconclusive cells in its 102-cell coverage matrix.

and rendered visual review of the exact release. Source audits, exact arithmetic and all-page
text bounds are narrower checks and cannot substitute for visual acceptance. Missing bank data,
platform access or signed adoption remain later conditional needs; they do not stop independent
proposal research. [2, 94]
The evidence routes are also different. We know how to complete missing source comparisons,
write and test selected adapters, run registered generated benchmarks and measure local workflow
costs. Their success still requires execution and review. We do not presently have a general useful
solution for arbitrary latent graphs, unrestricted persistent dependence, hidden-state optimal
policies or arbitrary nuisance learners. The proposal can be complete with explicit bounded claims
and negative findings in these areas; it cannot claim their scientific resolution. [2, 30, 33]
The present recommendation is therefore to test the narrow common service against existing
composition, use that evidence to refine the investment scenario, and request a bank experiment
only for a consequential question that the cheaper evidence cannot settle. Independent scientific
acceptance, target-bank validation, production reliability and adoption are later decisions with
their own evidence. They remain distinct from completing a credible proposal and a useful
bounded prototype.
