    Whether the issue at hand is researcher ambiguity, reader disagreement, or some combina-
tion of both, my framework assumes that it can be represented by a class of alternative weights
Λ. To make this modeling assumption a practical one, I restrict attention to classes Λ that have
the following structure.

Assumption 2. Λ ⊆ W is nonempty, compact, and convex.

    This structure is useful for establishing the existence and uniqueness of solutions to upcom-
ing optimization problems. I now provide examples of classes Λ that satisfy Assumption 2 and
map to various empirical contexts.
