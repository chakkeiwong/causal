Chapter 40

Identification, counterfactuals and
effect sets

A bank can know that an offer increases average completion and still be unable to say which
completed applications the offer caused. It can identify the effect of sending an encouragement
without identifying the effect of making every customer receive the service. It can also possess
a valid identifying formula whose numerical evaluation is too fragile to support the proposed
decision. These are different limits on what a causal product can say. The choice between
software components depends on preserving those limits through the calculation.
   This chapter develops that choice around a binary servicing action and a binary successful
outcome. The examples are project-authored probability laws, evaluated exactly, with a
stipulated value of USD 100 per success and USD 12 per action received. They are neither
estimates of bank behavior nor measurements of upstream software. Their role is to make
a proposal testable: an implementation can be asked to preserve the target, recover the
appropriate answer or set, and explain a refusal without confusing a scientific obstacle with a
computational one. (Causal banking proposal, 2026ca)
  The argument first separates adjustment, general identification, individual counterfactuals
and encouragement effects. It then develops the full binary instrument table to show how
changing an assumption changes a particular identified set. The language comparisons translate
those distinctions into symbolic results, search termination and model-specific calculations.
The closing Ananke and DoWhy GCM studies examine two further boundaries: the scale of a
reported effect and the information needed to retain an individual’s noise. Each comparison
therefore asks what information a returned quantity uses, before asking which engine should
compute it.

40.10       Choose software by the scientific route it preserves
The detailed software comparisons distinguish graphical adjustment, general intervention
identification, estimation and supplied structural-model simulation. The synthesis here uses
three inspected routes to show how a product should choose between them; it is not a claim

40.11      A bounded prototype can resolve implementation choices
The six exact law families supply complementary tests for a proposed public/generated
prototype. The adjustment family checks population weighting and the distinction between
total and controlled direct effects. The extended bow family checks conditional identification
without an identified joint. The coupling family checks refusal to turn identified experimental
marginals into identified personal attribution. The encouragement family checks LATE, the
observable assignment law and the population effect bounds. The consistency family checks
sharp extreme completions. The rare-event family checks whether an otherwise valid formula
survives numerical evaluation. Each family has its own expected scientific answer; agreement
on one does not replace the others. (Causal banking proposal, 2026ca,by,bz)
   Compare components only on a declared shared domain. An adjustment-only route should
be assessed on adjustment queries; its refusal of a nonadjustment query should not count as a
false identification. For supported ID/IDC routes, retain the graph, intervention and condition
sets, positivity domain, formula or witness, and exact expected result. For a supplied structural-
model simulator, retain the additional mechanisms and exogenous coupling; agreement with
one selected model must not be scored as identifying the whole observational class.
  A meaningful result distinguishes at least a point functional, a supported effect set, a valid
nonidentification witness, an unsupported scientific route, invalid input or support, numerical
unavailability, and an execution failure. These distinctions guide action. Extra assumptions
can shrink a scientific set; a fuller algorithm may support an unimplemented route; a software
repair may restore a valid answer; additional observations may reduce sampling uncertainty.
Confusing them would direct the bank toward the wrong investment.
   The initial public prototype can use generated mechanisms with known responses and
permissible public covariates to make the information structure realistic. Such covariates do
not reveal unobserved potential responses. Before broader comparisons, the proposal needs
a fixed domain, version and rights decision for each candidate, matched output semantics
and a separate registered finite-sample study wherever statistical performance is claimed.
Exact identities and deterministic error envelopes are a necessary reference, not that study’s
outcome. (Causal banking proposal, 2026bz)
   Several limits have a known next step without a known positive answer. Additional primary-
source review can expand software coverage, but uninspected candidates cannot yet receive
an adoption decision. Generated comparisons can reveal whether the adapters preserve the
mathematical distinction, but cannot establish a bank’s assignment truth. Domain work and
later intervention evidence can assess plausible instruments, delivery versions, interference and
harm restrictions. Independent review, accepted loss thresholds, actual precision requirements
and real costs are later inputs. The proposal can specify those questions and the evidence that
would answer them while proceeding with the implementation comparisons whose requirements
are already concrete.

40.22.1     Inspecting recursion without mistaking it for a correctness proof

The native do-calculus source constructs backdoor and frontdoor expressions, then provides a
general recursive identification path. Its branches restrict the graph to outcome ancestors, add
irrelevant remaining variables to the intervention, partition observed nodes connected through
latent co-children, and multiply or marginalize the resulting kernels. A single remaining full
confounded component triggers a hedge exception. The induced submodel keeps observed
identifiers and retains a latent descriptor only when it still has at least two retained children.
These observations identify the algorithmic objects that a comparison must preserve; they
are not a proof that every legal input reaches the mathematically intended branch. (aGrUM
authors and contributors, 2026)
   Two details deserve explicit qualification before reuse. The conditional-query path builds one
modified graph after removing incoming edges to intervention nodes and outgoing edges from
every conditioning node, then tests candidate promotions within that graph. A comparison
must establish the appropriate multi-variable graph surgery and the conditional query after
each promotion. Separately, when an accumulated kernel is present, the first topological
factor is constructed as a joint-probability AST rather than the numerator expression just
calculated from that kernel. Whether that branch is reachable with a distinct marginal, or
is equivalent under the implementation’s invariant, requires a proof-aligned fixture. Neither
observation alone warrants announcing a software defect. Both are precise obligations that a
root README or successful simple adjustment example would miss. (aGrUM authors and
contributors, 2026)
   The numerical layer has its own boundary. A formula’s identified predicate checks whether
an expression root exists. Evaluation then requires an observational Bayesian network, so a
structure-only answer and an evaluable answer are different states. Division of expression trees
delegates to tensor division. The earlier selected files do not settle zero-denominator support
handling, finite arithmetic or an uncertainty statement. The more detailed comparison in
Section 38.5 resolves a bounded part of that source question: selected tensor-division branches
introduce no support-zero refusal, ordinary zero-sum normalization leaves the tensor unchanged,
and CPT normalization refuses a zero-sum conditioning row. Those static findings specify native
fixtures; they do not establish universal runtime behavior or statistical uncertainty.(aGrUM and
pyAgrum contributors, 2026; Causal banking proposal project, 2026cs) The Python convenience
wrapper retains the model on the returned tensor and maps an unidentified formula to a None
numerical answer; complete exception and lifetime conversions still need direct inspection and

40.24.4     A confidence wrapper inherits the experiment performed by its
            callable

The selected generic confidence wrapper repeats a callable with generated seeds and returns
coordinatewise percentile limits and a geometric-median summary by default. Its default is
20 repetitions. The helper named bootstrap sampling is an alias for partial application: by
itself it does not resample training data or refit mechanisms. Repeating a query on a fixed
fitted model can measure simulation variation while omitting sampling, selection, graph and
misspecification uncertainty. (PyWhy contributors, 2026e)
   The separate fit-and-compute helper clones the model and chooses a fraction of training
rows without replacement, with default fraction 0.75. It refits the mechanisms and optionally
repeats automatic assignment; the graph is still the supplied graph. Without-replacement
subsampling and the ordinary size-n bootstrap are different laws. For authored data (0, 0, 2, 2),
all size-three subsets have means (2/3, 2/3, 4/3, 4/3), whose variance about the empirical mean
is 1/9. The size-four with-replacement empirical bootstrap mean instead has variance 1/4.
These are exact conditional finite-population calculations, not evidence about the coverage
of either interval procedure. (PyWhy contributors, 2026e; Causal banking proposal project,
2026di)
  Subsampling fractions, dependence units, omitted graph uncertainty and estimator-specific
centering or scaling require a justified uncertainty design. A narrow distribution of repeated
simulations cannot settle those questions. Coordinatewise percentile intervals also do not
automatically supply simultaneous coverage for a portfolio of causal queries. (Causal banking
proposal project, 2026di)
   Dictionary outputs require a further alignment contract. The selected wrapper appends
each returned value to its key’s list, then stacks the lists for the joint summary. It does not
locally require every repetition to return the same key set. An authored sequence returning key
a only in repetitions 0, 2, 4 with values (0, 1, 2) and key b only in repetitions 1, 3, 5 with values
(0, 10, 20) has equal list lengths but no joint repetitions. Stacking produces artificial pairs
(0, 0), (1, 10), (2, 20). A proposed worker must preserve repetition IDs, require a fixed output
schema or explicitly represent missing values, and report requested and valid denominators
per query. This is a conditional source deduction, not an executed package failure. (PyWhy
contributors, 2026e; Causal banking proposal project, 2026di)


40.24.5     Graph falsification is a diagnostic with a defined comparison law

The selected falsification routine compares local-Markov-condition violations with those from
node-permuted graphs and separately assesses whether permutations commonly remain in
the graph’s equivalence class. Its result distinguishes evaluability, falsifiability and rejection.
A zero-test case is not evaluable; data-subset evaluation is allowed by default. These states
and the exact tested subset should be retained instead of compressed into a graph-approved
Boolean. (PyWhy contributors, 2026e)
  This diagnostic does not prove graph truth, rule out all hidden causes or identify an
unrestricted intervention. Its conditional-independence tests, dependence law, permutation
budget and power need their own qualification. The linked scholarly justification has not
been newly reviewed in this selected-source decision and belongs in its follow-up register.
Source comments are not a substitute for that paper’s authentic PDF and inspected technical
argument. (Causal banking proposal project, 2026di)
