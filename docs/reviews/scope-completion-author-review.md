## Reconstruction of the main mathematical chains

**Panels and transmission.** Difference moments follow from orthogonality to
both innovations in a differenced error. Level moments additionally require
the initial-departure/individual-effect covariance to vanish. The scalar
decomposition and propagation are explicit, and endogenous regressors need
their own restrictions. The GMM expansion uses entity moments, a full
entity-level covariance and a nonsingular moment Jacobian. Estimated weighting
has a finite-sample derivative even though the zero population moment removes
it from the first-order expansion. The implemented covariance is not called
a Windmeijer correction. The invalid-initial-law and serial-error experiments
demonstrate different failures, retained across all requested repetitions.

The response construction keeps external-IV normalization fixed while changing
the long-run covariance. The prefilter identity follows by summing scores and
bounding the boundary term; feasible consistency additionally controls
estimated-filter error and recolouring. Neither stability diagnostics nor a
longer bandwidth supplies a uniform near-unit theorem. The weak-denominator
sets invert residual tests without dividing by the estimated first stage;
Bonferroni supplies their stated joint construction. Lag-augmented LP has its
own innovation target and source assumptions. Common-shock aggregation counts
calendar information, not customers. Mixed frequency, factors, error correction,
breaks and heavy tails are compared as changes to model/information assumptions,
with unqualified combinations excluded from a routine release.

**Observed histories.** The treatment and censoring ratios change measure to
the full-follow-up policy path. Pairing continuation errors at consecutive
times gives the displayed remainder. Its product bound requires overlap,
fixed horizon and norms controlling policy occupations; cross-fitting alone
does not supply those conditions. The code uses whole-customer folds and
never evaluates hidden postdropout histories or rewards. Exact stopping-path
enumeration checks zero bias with either correct probabilities or correct
outcome regressions; wrong components and omitted censoring produce nonzero
bias. The pilot is independent and held fixed, so reported coverage is
conditional policy evaluation, not learning-algorithm uncertainty or regret.
MNAR reward selection remains an explicit identification failure.

**Private logging and continuous states.** The finite model factors the
unnormalized signal/action/next-signal matrix into validated emissions,
state-action mass and controlled transitions. Left inversion followed by row
division recovers those components only under full rank, positive mass and
invariant measurements. Reward-marked moments identify action rewards under
the same factorization. Joint likelihood scores include validation data and
private logging. The information Schur complement and value gradient explain
why calibration cannot ordinarily be treated as fixed.

The finite-state coverage failure is not repaired by numerical precision:
extra starts change the value by about .0012 and differencing steps change
the model SE by less than 4e-8, yet the repeated-sample coverage remains
.850–.875. Seventeen invalid/bootstrap boundary refits out of 199 exceed the
prespecified availability limit. The interval is withheld, and the usable-fit
standard deviation remains a conditional diagnostic. This is a research
estimation path with an unresolved routine-inference qualification.

The distinct Gaussian reference fixes initial and noise laws and measurement
scale. Initial and lagged cross-covariances identify its five coefficients;
independent action noise separates action response from latent state.
The transformed joint observation density has determinant one but still has
a parameter-dependent residual, whose derivative is retained. A full stacked
Gaussian covariance provides an independent likelihood check. The affine
deterministic policy is singular relative to the historical continuous
conditional action density: its value is identified by the structural model,
not ordinary importance weighting. The nonlinear/particle extension states
the extra integration, differentiation and error-budget obligations instead
of inheriting exact Gaussian validity.

**Discovery and observations.** The review distinguishes finite-window latent
projection, stationarified targets, population completeness, conditioning caps
and finite-sample search. The executed temporal adapter actually consumes
selected graph output and a fresh estimation sample. It preserves directed
constraints and prohibits new unshielded colliders, including those involving
lagged parents. Independent chain, collider and lagged-parent fixtures check
those distinctions. Mixed ancestral endpoints are refused by this limited
adapter. Its order envelope is conditional structural sensitivity, not a
confidence band. The two-direction Gaussian example explains the intervention
or background timing evidence needed to resolve a material price-response
decision. Hidden-case structural adjacency is explicitly not PAG accuracy.

Observation candidates now come from type profiles, identifier membership,
clock roles and a bounded key search. The correctness induction requires
candidate inclusion and truthful constraints/answers. The three-field-key
fixture is outside the grammar and is rejected rather than silently accepted.
The question criterion minimizes worst remaining effect ambiguity and is
explicitly exploratory when it uses outcomes. Linkage attenuation and the
independent-audit variance are derived; joint uncertainty cannot repair a
biased audit. Neither a schema score nor a fuzzy match probability supplies
an identifying assumption.

**Composition, finance and scale.** A supplied DAG determines a sufficient
back-door expression, nuisance roles and a score; an independent-unit
covariance is a separate operation. Public eligibility and participation
queries share these components, while their ratio needs additional IV
assumptions. The learned-history route reuses the trajectory-score reduction,
and temporal effects retain a distinct result type. Matched DoubleML uses
identical folds, predictions, clipping and weight normalization. Its numerical
agreement establishes those semantics, not universal package correctness.

The public 401(k) extract, its missingness rule and all nine controls are
preserved. Eligibility ATEs range 7,968–8,932 and participation LATEs
11,398–12,935 in archived dollars. The original paper's complete learner and
100-split procedure is not claimed replicated. Unknown control timing,
unmeasured selection and absent survey design fields limit the empirical
interpretation. The covariance is labelled an iid working analysis.

The workload grid has eleven cases and 22 fresh processes. Thread counts are
observed, not inferred from an outer thread-limiting context. Complete fitting,
information/refits and reporting are measured at explicit statistical
dimensions. Refits, temporal-search conditioning and covariance halos motivate
different parallel units. Matched DoubleML shares nuisance fits, so its small
incremental time is not a speedup comparison. Distributed throughput and
analyst-time savings remain unmeasured.

## Defects found, repaired and preserved

1. An appended scope chapter repeated the existing narrative instead of
   developing its dependencies. It was removed from active input and retained
   as `history/unintegrated-scope-chapter-draft.tex`; the arguments now live in
   their corresponding chapters.
2. Pilot discovery recall counted only in-window true edges and concealed an
   omitted-lag failure. Final recall uses the full structural truth; the pilot
   is preserved and hidden-case metrics are not labelled PAG accuracy.
3. A supplied-order sensitivity calculation was insufficient evidence for
   graph-to-effect integration. A separate executed adapter now consumes
   actual search output, preserves collider constraints and refuses unsupported
   marks; the two analyses are distinguished.
4. Binary participation regression could leave probability support. The
   public first-stage outcome predictions now have a recorded [0,1] projection,
   distinct from propensity truncation. The previous receipt is preserved.
5. The initial workload's late-loaded numerical pools exceeded the requested
   thread limit. Environment limits now precede imports and every worker checks
   observed pools. The failed thread-accounting receipt and runs are preserved.
6. A draft public LATE range did not match final results. The final text uses
   11,398–12,935 and the verifier reconstructs the ratio and ranges.
7. A deterministic continuous policy could be misread as satisfying ordinary
   density-ratio overlap. The text explicitly states singularity and the
   structural identification route.
8. Proxy injectivity was insufficient to justify bridge existence. The text
   requires range/existence separately and limits adoption of the missingness
   source's general existence discussion.
9. The response comparison implied archived set widths that its aggregate
   receipt does not retain. The text now reports unbounded-set frequencies:
   at least one coordinate is unbounded in 99.25% of weak-case repetitions for
   the longer-window and prefiltered constructions.
10. The short-panel dimension included an initial observation plus eight
    transitions. The manuscript now states that observation convention.
11. A search-chain key used an unregistered Stock–Watson alias. It now resolves
    to `stock2018`; duplicate chain listings and omission schemas were repaired.
    Zero LMTP/index forward matches remain an index limitation, not a negative
    literature claim.
12. The consistency scanner counted preserved history as active manuscript
    labels. History is now excluded without changing archived text. V5 build,
    audit, rendering and delivery tools recognize the active T program and keep
    R/S phases historical.

The final workflow verifier reconstructs retained fitted-run summaries,
coverage denominators, graph adjacency summaries, public ratios and workload
accounting. Inference and linkage receipts preserve aggregate counters with
frozen generators/seeds; the verifier checks their arithmetic and identities,
not a fresh replay of those complete simulations. Exact finite identities and
Gaussian/path likelihood checks are separate evidence. None proves all general
theorems or certifies bank data.
