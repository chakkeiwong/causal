## Julia and Rust workers must preserve the banking clock

Six pinned Julia and Rust survival profiles show why language coverage must be accompanied by a precise service contract. A collections comparison needs a common eligibility clock, treatment strategy, entry and censoring law, event definition and output units. In an exact authored example, two strategies differ by two months of restricted mean survival but have zero event-risk difference at a five-month horizon. A field named risk difference cannot safely represent both quantities. \citep{exec24_survivalother_reference}

JuliaStats Survival.jl, SurvivalAnalysis.jl, SurvivalModels.jl and JointSurvivalModels.jl offer distinct candidates for original Julia workers. The inspected snapshots expose useful right-censored, parametric, Cox, nonparametric or joint-model components. Their proposed admission conditions differ: zero-time handling, censoring representation, tied-event convention, scalar versus vector boundary behaviour, optimisation failures, numerical support and random-generator routing require explicit checks. Direct MIT declarations do not clear every dependency or prove native performance. \citep{exec24_survivalother_juliastats,exec24_survivalother_juliaanalysis,exec24_survivalother_juliamodels,exec24_survivalother_juliajoint}

The inspected Rust survival causal entrypoints raise source-local questions about eligibility versus treatment clocks, administrative censoring, fixed rather than fitted censoring weights, time-valued output labels, bootstrap consistency and half-imputation of censored outcomes. These findings justify withholding direct causal admission for those entrypoints until repair and qualification; they are not executed native failures or conclusions about every component in that repository. The separate turnbull prototype remains reference-only because finite interval-only support, truncation, variance and source licensing are unresolved. \citep{exec24_survivalother_rustcore,exec24_survivalother_rustturnbull}

The investment route is a bounded public/generated comparison of original workers with exact target-specific oracles. The proposed CLI/MCP interface records source and environment, population and clock, event/censoring/entry semantics, units, options, warnings, refused and failed requests, uncertainty and full costs. Twelve initiated requests costing USD 36 with three accepted numeric deliveries cost USD 12 per accepted delivery, not USD 3; these are stipulated accounting values. Whole-library ports require a demonstrated benefit sufficient to cover their additional maintenance and semantic risks. Search tails and native qualification remain open. \citep{exec24_survivalother_reference}
