20: ## Introduction
21: Identifying causality lets scientists look past correlations and uncover the mechanisms behind natural and social phenomena. Although many advanced methods for causal discovery now exist, their varied assumptions and technical complexity often discourage non‑experts from using them.
22: 
23: **Causal‑Copilot** addresses this gap. Guided by a LLM, it automates the full causal‑analysis workflow: data inspection, algorithm and hyperparameter selection, code generation, uncertainty assessment, and PDF report creation— all triggered through simple dialogue. By combining LLM‑driven domain knowledge with state‑of‑the‑art causal techniques, Causal‑Copilot lets researchers focus on scientific insight instead of implementation details.
