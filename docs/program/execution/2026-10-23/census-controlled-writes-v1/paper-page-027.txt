E.5   S ETTING THE T HRESHOLD τ

The internal protocol sets the threshold using the storage ratio in Theorem 5. By the unit-count
consequence of Theorem 5, the unit-count saving of the kernel store is |SO |/|A| = 1/PAu (uniform
reading of the kernel mass). CPUNeSy eligibility should require the saving to materially exceed the
fixed cost of running the deductive engine and the bridge (the certification surcharge priced into α,
Proposition 3). The internal protocol specifies
                           τ = 0.5,        i.e. require     |SO |/|A| ≥ 2,
so that an eligible core at least halves the unit count; domains in the band 1 − PA ∈ [τ /2, τ ) are
declared hybrid-eligible and served with the graceful-degradation protocol (the main-text hybrid
band). A sensitivity analysis over τ ∈ {0.3, 0.5, 0.7} is reported with the results.
