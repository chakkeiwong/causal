5.4 Statistical Tests
All statistical comparisons use the Mann-Whitney 𝑈 test [49] (two-sided, significance
level 𝛼 = 0.05) with Bonferroni correction for multiple comparisons. For 6 pairwise
strategy comparisons, the corrected significance level is 𝛼corr = 0.05/6 = 0.0083. Effect
sizes are reported as Cohen’s 𝑑 [50] (standardised mean difference), interpreted as
small (𝑑 = 0.2), medium (𝑑 = 0.5), or large (𝑑 = 0.8).
    For domain and task comparisons, we additionally report 95% bootstrap confidence
intervals (10,000 resamples) for the mean CAR score within each category.

5.5 Implementation Details
The evaluation pipeline is implemented in Python 3.11. Tokenisation for metric
computation uses whitespace tokenisation with case-insensitive matching and stem-
ming (Porter stemmer) to handle morphological variants of causal keywords. The F1
computation in CIR and CCR uses the set-based formulation (Equations 1–4).
    For CLP, pattern matching uses a curated list of 47 forward causal patterns, 32
reverse causal patterns, and 21 ambiguous patterns. Patterns are matched as regular
expressions with optional intervening words (up to 5) between the cause and effect
terms.
    The complete evaluation code, prompts, gold annotations, and raw results are
released with the benchmark for full reproducibility.
