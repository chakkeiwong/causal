## What changed scientifically

An inspected Aitiotita path freezes a candidate link when dependence persists
after conditioning on all neighbours. Dependence under that larger set does
not rule out independence under a smaller set. The existing source review
identified the problem; this study evaluates a repair and its computational
cost, rather than counting the earlier counterexample again as new work.

Full stable subset search recovered the generating skeleton in all 1,381
declared cases. The matched screened search failed on 49. A depth-two cap
returned 165 incorrect skeletons, all marked incomplete. Independently written
moral-graph and active-path oracles agreed on 3,090 comparisons. Exact rational
arithmetic and a separate residualisation argument agreed on the financial
example: a zero intervention effect, an unadjusted slope of 3/4, a properly
adjusted slope of zero, and an overadjusted slope of -1/2.

The proposal should use full subset search as its reference, exclude the
examined freezing rule from a general Gaussian-oracle adaptation, and report
unfinished searches as such. These finite exact-oracle results are not
measurements of field failure prevalence or Java runtime. They do not qualify
sample-based tests, arrow orientation, bank effects or a deployed product.
