## Synth reference: qualify preparation and numerical status before reuse

The selected R Synth revision is retained as a comparison reference,
with adoption and a full port deferred. Static inspection finds that ordinary
treated predictors use the requested aggregation operator while donors use
the mean, and that signed custom weights can yield a different or invalid
normalization. Solver wrapper returns do not provide a complete numerical
certificate. Independent exact examples expose these boundaries without
running downloaded code. \citep{exec24_synth_source,exec24_synth_choice}

A public prototype must preserve donor eligibility, the intervention clock,
matched predictor definitions, missingness and actual numerical status.
Complete dependency rights, native uncertainty and costs remain conditional.
Illustrative 2,400 USD divided by 120 requests, 100 conformant outputs and
80 useful outputs gives 20, 24 and 30 USD. These are planning inputs, not
measured bank value. \citep{exec24_synth_choice}
