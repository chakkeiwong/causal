During review, the old rendering directory was found to contain both
historical two-digit page filenames and current three-digit filenames.
Its mixed contact sheets were rejected as evidence. The renderer now creates
hash-specific output directories and explicitly orders physical page numbers.
All final contact sheets were freshly inspected after this repair. The
construction tail was shortened without removing acceptance requirements;
proposed SDK/integration wording was also made explicit. A duplicate sponsor
closing section had been removed in the preceding revision.

The final build logs contain no overfull boxes, unresolved references,
citation warnings or BibTeX warnings. Covers, contents, chapter openings,
equations, figures, tables, source concordance and bibliography are present.
Some chapter endings are short; no missing text or clipped figure was observed.
The concordance and software tables are dense reference material, with an
offline searchable source index supplied alongside the PDF.

Contact-sheet review checks page composition, not every glyph. Enlarged
inspection, source review and build checks provide complementary evidence.
This is author review, not independent copy editing, accessibility
certification, scientific peer review or bank acceptance.
