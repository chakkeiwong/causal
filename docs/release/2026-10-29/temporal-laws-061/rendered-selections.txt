Chapter 55

Dynamic panels and structural
funding shocks

A deposit response evolves over time and may coincide with changes in monetary conditions or
customer demand. Dynamic-panel moments, restrictions on structural shocks and state-space
forecasts use different sources of information. A forecast alone does not establish the effect of
changing the bank’s policy.
   The dynamic-panel foundation first develops moment validity, instrument choice, calendar
identity and selection. The language comparisons then examine how implementations preserve
or change those objects, followed by structural-shock and state-space alternatives. Several use
the same stipulated calendar gaps, rotations or missing-state laws as matched comparisons;
repeating their arithmetic does not provide independent empirical corroboration.


55.1     Dynamic-panel reuse: instruments, calendars, and the
         cost of searching for a model
A dynamic-panel coefficient becomes useful to a bank only after its time scale and identifying
moments are justified. Consider monthly account balances following a servicing intervention,
or deposit flows following a change in offered rates. Persistence makes a static regression an
incomplete description. It also makes the distinction between an immediate association, a
structural response and a long-run consequence economically important. The pydynpd package
offers a concrete Python candidate for a bounded linear dynamic-panel service. Its original
paper describes difference and system GMM, several estimation steps, diagnostic tests and
automatic lag-model search. The paper’s historical comparisons motivate inspection; they do
not establish current relative speed, correctness or commercial parity. (Wu et al., 2023)
   The reviewed repository commit predates the 7 October 2026 cutoff. The root license is
MIT. The selected setup file declares Python at least 3.6, version 0.2.1 and dependencies on
NumPy, SciPy, pandas and prettytable, whereas the separate version module declares 5.2.0.
The setup file’s nonempty version takes its own branch. We therefore identify the reviewed
code by its commit and hashes, without pretending that these declarations identify one verified
wheel or supported environment. No package code, benchmark or test was executed. The
following source findings and exact project examples support a reference disposition and a
conditional original-worker design. (pydynpd contributors, 2025)

55.1.1    The equation and the information supplied by instruments

For account i and period t, start with the deliberately restricted model

                                  yit = αyi,t−1 + βait + ηi + εit .                           (55.1)

Here ait is a declared action or exposure, ηi an account effect and εit the remaining disturbance.
Coefficient β is a structural causal response only under a model in which this action and
disturbance have the required intervention meaning. An economic label on a regression column
does not provide that meaning. Differencing removes the fixed account effect but leaves

                                ∆yit = α∆yi,t−1 + β∆ait + ∆εit .                              (55.2)

The lagged dependent regressor generally shares a disturbance with the differenced error.
The classical short-panel argument uses suitable lagged levels as instruments under the
stated moment and serial-correlation restrictions; the original discussion also distinguishes
predetermined from strictly exogenous regressors. The relevant asymptotic regime and cross-
unit sampling conditions are part of that argument. They cannot be replaced by a successful
software call. (Arellano and Bond, 1991)
   Write gi (θ) = Zi′ ui (θ) for account-level moments, where Zi contains the chosen instruments
and ui the transformed residual vector. A GMM calculation minimizes ḡN (θ)′ WN ḡN (θ) for a
declared weight matrix. At least three questions must remain separate: whether the moments
are valid, whether they identify the requested coefficient, and whether the estimated weight
and covariance give useful finite-sample inference. System GMM adds level-equation moments
and hence additional restrictions. For the simple autoregression, the inspected original account
of the method makes the initial-condition restriction E[∆yi2 ηi ] = 0 explicit. High persistence
can make otherwise valid lag instruments weak; adding level moments is justified only when
their extra conditions hold. (Blundell and Bond, 2023)
  A small exact example shows why a moment solver cannot supply these conditions. Let U
be equally likely to be minus one or one, set x = z = U , and let the structural equation be
y = x + ε with ε = U . The true structural coefficient is one, but

                                      E[z(y − θx)] = 2 − θ.                                   (55.3)

The moment criterion is minimized at two because the proposed instrument is invalid. It is
precisely solved and scientifically wrong for the stated target. In another example, x1 = x2 = z
identifies only β1 + β2 . Coefficients (1, 0), (0, 1) and (1/2, 1/2) generate the same fitted values.
A generalized inverse can choose a numerical representative without identifying the two effects.
These are authored algebraic witnesses, not native pydynpd benchmark results. (Causal
banking proposal project, 2026ei)


55.1.2    The calendar and the instrument ledger

The inspected parser requires a lag-one dependent variable and rejects declared dependent-
variable GMM instruments starting before lag two. It derives a corresponding level-lag entry
and makes regressors not assigned to a GMM or IV list into IV entries. Default options select
two steps, a level equation, first differences and uncollapsed instruments. These defaults are
computational conventions, not evidence that a bank’s rate or servicing action is exogenous or
that level moments are valid. The proposed request must carry an explicit instrument ledger:
variable, treatment of endogeneity, equation block, permitted lags, collapse choice, availability
clock and scientific justification. It should reject an implicit scientific classification. (pydynpd
contributors, 2025)

55.1.3    Weights, covariance and numerical termination

The inspected estimator computes weighted normal equations using a pseudoinverse; the
step-result class also pseudoinverts its weight matrix. It accumulates moment outer products
by account and invokes a covariance helper named for the finite-sample two-step correction.
Its Hansen calculation uses the nominal difference between instrument and regressor counts
as degrees of freedom. These paths must be reported as inspected implementation choices.
They do not establish effective-rank adequacy, an asymptotic approximation under arbitrary
dependence, or numerical reliability near singularity. A finite-sample correction cannot repair
invalid instruments. (pydynpd contributors, 2025; Blundell and Bond, 2023)
   Common bank-wide shocks give an elementary warning about the covariance unit. If
gi = U for all one hundred accounts, with a common mean-zero unit-variance shock, then
Var(N −1 i gi ) = 1. Keeping only the diagonal contributions gives an expected value of 1/100.

55.1.4    A search survivor is a selected result

The original paper describes an automatic mode that searches lag combinations and returns
models satisfying specification tests. In the inspected source, candidates are generated from
adjustable lag ranges; ordinary exceptions during candidate processing are caught and skipped.
The acceptance predicate requires the last autocorrelation-test probability to exceed 0.05
and the Hansen probability to lie strictly between 0.05 and 0.99999. Ordinary process
errors, diagnostic failures, rejected candidates and returned models are consequently different
denominators. Some parser errors use process exit rather than the ordinary exception path.
An authorized isolated worker must translate every terminal outcome into a typed result and
preserve all attempted candidates. (Wu et al., 2023; pydynpd contributors, 2025)
   Searching changes the interpretation of a surviving test. For a stipulated collection of twenty
invalid candidates, suppose each independently passes a screening rule with probability 0.1.
The probability that at least one survives is

                                    1 − (1 − 0.1)20 ≃ 0.8784.                               (55.4)

This is not an estimate of the power of Hansen or autocorrelation tests, and real candidate
events need not be independent. It is a finite-law explanation of why a search for nonrejection
is not a certification procedure. Predeclare the economic model and admissible moments
where possible; otherwise retain the full search and address selection in the inferential design.
A held-out or simulation-based check must still be justified for the panel’s dependence and
available information. (Causal banking proposal project, 2026ei)


55.1.5    From coefficients to a proposal for reuse

For a stable scalar linear response with invariant coefficients, the effect of a permanent unit
increase in exposure is β/(1 − α). With a stipulated immediate response of USD 10, persistence
0.8 gives USD 50 and persistence 0.95 gives USD 200. These are model calculations, not
measured bank value. An uncertainty region reaching the pole at one cannot be converted
into a bounded long-run estimate by plugging in the center. Regime changes, intervention
feedback and a general servicing policy lie beyond this calculation. (Causal banking proposal
project, 2026ei)
   The present choice is reference-only reuse, with an original Python worker as a conditional
prototype candidate. Before admission, its typed library/CLI/MCP contract must bind the
equation, estimand, calendar, immutable data, sorted key map, instruments, requested and
diagnostic steps, covariance unit, rank tolerances, search ledger and termination policy. The

55.2      Python dynamic panels, structural shocks and state-space
          forecasts
A treasury team asking how a funding intervention changes future deposits needs more than
a well-fitting forecast. A panel of branches raises a different sampling problem from one
long aggregate time series, and a filtered latent state is not automatically an intervention
variable. The existing pydynpd review already develops dynamic-panel moments, instrument
proliferation, calendar alignment, selection and conditional uncertainty. Its genuine paper
and retained source review remain the starting point here. The added comparison examines
statsmodels VAR/SVAR paths, two further dynamic-panel candidates, a structural-shock
package and a dynamic linear-model implementation. Each candidate is evaluated by its
target, information clock, restrictions, numerical return and failure contract. A discovery result
or an estimator name supplies none of these guarantees by itself. (Wu et al., 2023; pydynpd
contributors, 2025; Causal banking proposal project, 2026ga)


55.2.1    The calendar is part of a dynamic-panel moment condition

For a schematic panel relation yit = αyi,t−1 + x′it β + ai + uit , the interpretation of a lag is
part of the model. Conditions on a two-period-old value and a differenced disturbance refer to
declared calendar periods, not automatically to the second most recent observed record. This
is consequential for branches with missing reporting months. In an authored table with times
(2000, 2002, 2003) and values (10, 20, 30), the previous-observation lag is (NA, 10, 20), whereas
a one-calendar-period lag is (NA, NA, 20). Treating the middle row as if its lag were observed
changes the usable moment rows before any GMM solver runs. This arithmetic illustrates a
contract difference; it is not a native benchmark. (pydynpd contributors, 2025; Causal banking

55.2.3    Proxy alignment, shock normalization and attempted resamples

The retained macroshock revision offers short-run, long-run, sign and proxy-IV branches. The
source contains substantive identification calculations, but a callable branch still needs an
observation-identity contract. In its proxy helper, missing instrument entries are filtered
and the resulting instrument vector is paired with a suffix of residual observations of the
same length. An interior missing instrument can therefore change row correspondence. In
an authored three-row example, instrument values (1, NA, 2) aligned to residuals (10, 20, 30)
produce the matched inner product 1 · 10 + 2 · 30 = 70. Pairing retained instruments with the
last two residuals instead gives 1 · 20 + 2 · 30 = 80. The inspected public model path passes
the arrays to this helper. This witness isolates alignment; it does not assert a package run or
validate exclusion. (patricioariaslpz/macroshock contributors, 2026; Causal banking proposal
project, 2026ga)
   The same helper resets the full impact matrix inside the loop over instruments, then
populates the current column. A corresponding reset appears in the selected bootstrap proxy
branch. The authored two-column reset trace ends with only the second identity column
retained. Thus a multi-instrument use needs explicit native qualification before earlier columns
are advertised as jointly fitted. The impulse-response helper additionally scales the requested
shock by the reciprocal of its own-variable impact coefficient. If that coefficient is two,
a requested own-variable movement of one uses structural-shock amplitude one-half. The
response is not automatically a one-standard-deviation shock, and a zero diagonal is a distinct
refusal case. (patricioariaslpz/macroshock contributors, 2026; Causal banking proposal project,
2026ga)
   The resampling paths have different stopping and state contracts. The normal bootstrap
samples residual rows; the wild path aliases the model residual array and changes its entries in
place. Its loop counts accepted output draws and the inspected loop has no separate attempt
cap. A progress expression uses an integer step derived from one tenth of the requested
replications, creating a small-request boundary to test. The sign-restriction routine is separate:
it does have a rejection-count cap and can return zero-filled summary arrays on that stopping
path. A zero-filled unavailable response must not be reported as an estimated zero policy effect.

input-state hashes. No general dependent-time-series calibration follows from implementing

55.3.3    A resampling distribution must retain failures and shock meaning

The selected svars moving-block bootstrap constructs overlapping residual blocks, draws blocks
and refits a declared structural method. Some method-specific errors are converted to null
results; failed results are subsequently removed before surviving estimates form the covariance
summary. Other branches have different error handling. A service must retain requested,
attempted, failed and retained counts, the block length, design and failure causes. A successful
conditional summary is not automatically an unconditional statement about every requested
fit. Sign and permutation alignment, shared calendar shocks and weak identification remain
substantive qualification questions. (cran/svars contributors, 2025)
   TsDyn provides a complementary nonlinear experiment. Its inspected GIRF path conditions
on histories and shocks, then averages paired simulated paths. The low-level helper with
shock_both set to false replaces the first innovation in one path with the requested shock;
with shock_both true, it adds the shock to that path’s baseline innovation. The averaging
helper used by the public GIRF path defaults to the additive setting. Both paths share the
history and subsequent innovations. If the baseline innovation is one and the specified shock
is two, replacement gives an immediate difference of one, whereas addition gives two. These
are different experimental targets. A natural-language request for a two-unit funding shock
must resolve that distinction before an agent constructs a call. (cran/tsDyn contributors, 2024;
Causal banking proposal project, 2026gd)
   The selected routine samples future residual innovations without replacement and samples
histories and shocks without replacement when they are not supplied. A horizon longer than
the available residual pool therefore needs an explicit input policy and fixture. The public
routine passes the supplied seed separately for each history–shock combination. Its averaging
helper resets that seed once per combination and then repeats the low-level calls without
passing a seed, allowing the random stream to advance within that combination. This can
couple innovation draws across combinations; it does not establish that all repetitions within a
combination are identical. The joint simulation law and Monte Carlo uncertainty must retain
that distinction. The TVAR delegate preserves the supplied starting matrix and innovations,
chooses a threshold regime and rounds selected threshold comparisons and paths. Threshold

55.4.2    Point forecasts and uncertainty are different output contracts

The inspected Smile ARMA fit rejects inadmissible orders and short series, estimates an
auxiliary autoregression and fits lagged observations and residuals by SVD. Its SVD delegate
uses an effective-rank threshold and solves on retained singular vectors. The ARMA routine
subsequently forms a Cholesky factor of the design Gram matrix for coefficient standard errors.
These are different numerical requirements: a finite rank-truncated coefficient calculation need
not imply that the later covariance calculation completes. A proposed test must retain both
outcomes and the installed tensor/LAPACK delegate, rather than infer a universal failure or
success from the initial solve. (haifengl/smile contributors, 2026a)
   The selected multistep forecast copies recent observations and residuals, recursively produces
future values and leaves future residual entries at zero. It returns a point-forecast array. Its
residual variance and coefficient tests are separate objects; the selected return is not a horizon-
specific prediction band. In an authored AR(1) example with coefficient 1/2 and unit innovation
variance, conditional forecast-error variances at horizons one and two are 1 and 1 + 1/4 = 5/4
even with known parameters. A single residual-variance field cannot be silently reused as
both. Estimated parameters, model selection and dependent innovations would add their

   Smile’s inspected ARIMA fit differences by adjacent observations and selects the ARMA,

55.4.4    State propagation through missing observations

JDemetra+ provides a distinct conditional state-space reference. Its inspected observation
wrapper treats nonfinite entries as missing. The ordinary filter skips the measurement update
for such a row but still advances the state. The concrete state delegate applies the transition
to the mean and covariance and adds the process-noise covariance at every advance. Under a

55.6.1    DynamicPanelModels: sorting, differencing and instrument clocks

The Julia panel constructor explicitly rejects duplicate entity/time pairs and groups a sorted
table by entity. Those are valuable safeguards for a bank panel. They do not by themselves
establish one calendar convention throughout the estimation path. In the inspected trans-
formation, lagged regressor values are shifted by row position. A separate first-difference
check admits a row only if its current and previous time labels differ by one. Instrument
construction then indexes earlier positions in the globally observed time-label vector and looks
up the selected value for the entity. These three operations must be compared as a composed
contract. (MichalS16/DynamicPanelModels.jl contributors, 2026)
   An authored example uses dates (1, 3, 4) and levels (10, 30, 40). At date four, the current
and previous dates are adjacent, so the current first-difference gate passes. A row-shifted
lagged regressor contains values 30 and 10 at the two relevant rows, yielding a difference of
20. A one-calendar-period lagged difference would require the level at date two, which is
absent. The same globally observed label sequence can make a nominal second-lag instrument
select date one rather than date two. This does not show that all requests are wrong: regular
complete calendars avoid this particular discrepancy. It shows why the adapter must declare

recorded with the result. The moment conditions also need the usual substantive restrictions

verify those restrictions for bank data.

precise than an informal promise to retain the earliest member of each collinear set. The
subsequent count comparison is useful but is not a proof of identification or valid uncertainty.

55.6.7    A common temporal report preserves different scientific questions

The comparisons support a sequence of decisions. First define the calendar, intervention and
target response. Then justify the moments, structural restrictions or state law that supply
information about it. Only then can a numerical path and uncertainty procedure be compared.
Dynamic-panel GMM, structural shock analysis, local projections and conditional forecasting
remain serious alternatives, with different identifying information and failure modes. A shared
report should make those differences visible instead of inferring a common causal meaning
from similar coefficient or forecast arrays.
  The funding question can also depend on uncertain customer relationships and on whether
a pilot population represents the intended rollout. The next chapter extends the information
question to relationship graphs, transported policies and audit design: what additional
observations would actually distinguish the bank’s competing decisions?
