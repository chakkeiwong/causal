### Reuse choices preserve the exact package identity

The proposed dose-response layer references two distinct R distributions:
causaldrf 0.4.2 for transparent generalized-propensity adjustment and
CausalGPS 0.5.1 for continuous-exposure design, matching or weighting and
diagnostics. These choices depend on the exact inspected revisions and
their separate scientific roles. A name appearing in several catalogues
does not create a new engine or new validation evidence.
[@exec24_dose_causaldrf; @exec24_dose_causalgps; @sp24dosealiases_decisions]

The implementation proposal preserves the dose units, density measure,
population, observation identity, diagnostics and uncertainty convention.
It first uses public or generated causal problems with known targets to
qualify the selected worker, including scale transformations, unsupported
dose regions, extreme or empty weights, join failures and all-request
costs. Reference use supplies a comparison and a test specification;
integration still requires resolved dependencies, rights, maintenance
responsibilities and native conformance. The existing mathematical and
financial examples remain the basis for that proposal, with no new
performance or bank-value claim from reconciling duplicate names.
