The scientific procedures remain fixed: joint regression with horizon covariance,
fourfold independent pilot with horizon covariance, and eightfold independent
pilot with horizon covariance. The latter also satisfies the inherited sufficient
rate and measures the cost/benefit of more nuisance information. At each request,
the two pilot methods use nested prefixes of one independent realization and
share the evaluation histories and audit counts with joint regression. Scientific
identification, regular audit rank and covariance premises are inherited from
V9; computational streaming supplies no new statistical theorem.

The eight designs separate the original 720-date failure, baseline 1200 dates,
4800 dates, smaller/larger audit information, high persistence, near-rank audit
information and false exogeneity. Pilot size varies within every design. This is
a selected one-factor comparison around named cases, not a full factorial or a
bank population. Old omitted-dictionary failures remain preserved in V9.

At 2,000 requests the approximate marginal 95% Monte Carlo half-width near
coverage .95 is 1.96 sqrt(.95*.05/2000)=.00955; its worst-case value is .02192.
Wilson intervals are reported for observed coverage. Paired differences have
sampling uncertainty based on the observed discordances. Simultaneous claims
across the 24 marginal coverage cells use Bonferroni Wilson intervals; ordinary
intervals remain descriptive. Replications are fixed; no early stopping on
favorable coverage. There is no claim to detect arbitrarily small superiority.
Report all-request and conditional coverage, availability, bias, empirical SD,
mean SE, width, paired differences and pilot-history requirements. Pilot/short
rehearsal seeds are disjoint from final seeds and cannot tune a procedure.

Streaming retains fixed-size QR factors for the two chronological pilot halves.
Their orthogonal residual norms give the same standardized validation losses;
stable merged centered sums give training variances. Refit combines both factors
and the chosen ridge penalty. Evaluation uses one pass for residual action sums
and response cross-products, then one for the fixed scalar score and centered
lag products. Only blocks, fixed-dimensional factors and a lag buffer are held.
Mapped inputs, their filesystem storage and resident pages still have costs.
Numerical equivalence needs stable rank and separated penalty losses; exact
repeated penalties choose the first occurrence. A near tie must be exposed by
validation-loss diagnostics, never resolved by the final causal estimate.
