Prepared 10 October 2026. Every external disposition below is **pending**.

## Independent scientific challenge

Owner required: a named reviewer independent of the authors, with causal
inference and dependent-data expertise. Supply the assessed PDF, source/claim
trace, registered designs, all journals (including failed pilots), program
version and immutable delivery manifest. The reviewer must reconstruct five
questions: pair/calendar projection and label-sample rates; audit allocation
information/cost; graph-class causal invariance and weak-rank inversion;
audited nonlinear identification and numerical scope; nuisance representation,
gap and covariance conditions. Coverage shortfalls and useless whole-line
answers must be discussed explicitly.

Required output: `external/review/report.pdf`, signed reviewer identity and
conflict statement, per-question disposition, reproducible counterexamples,
and unresolved findings. Acceptance requires no unresolved material scientific
misstatement in the claimed scope. A disagreeing review is evidence to retain,
not a reason to alter its inputs or replace it with an author review.

## Bounded bank study and independent label truth

Owners required: bank question sponsor, assignment/decision-system owner,
source/clock owner, independent audit-truth owner and privacy/data custodian.
Begin with one population mean action response, not an unrestricted optimal
policy. Before fitting, record monetary units, intervention, time horizon,
eligibility, stable identifiers, first availability timestamps, revisions,
common shock calendar and the actual action-assignment mechanism. Check that
the selected model's identifying assumptions are plausible; API acceptance
cannot establish them. If actions are not randomized as required by the joint
reference, this route is ineligible until a justified score is derived.

Audit units are complete persistent pairs. Supply known inclusion probabilities
and strata/costs, the randomization ledger, both candidate outcome histories,
independent pilot, adjudication procedures and failures. Gold truth must be
representative and independently sampled; record both underlying labels and
observed errors, with enough examples in each direction to challenge symmetry
and conditional independence. Unknown inclusion, selected/asymmetric label
errors, many-to-many ownership or inconsistent availability stop qualification.
They cannot be repaired by merely increasing audit count.

Required files under `external/bank-study/`: `protocol.json` (frozen target,
assignment and sampling laws); `owners.json` (signed attestations);
`calendar.csv`; `pairs.parquet`; `audit-ledger.parquet`;
`gold-labels.parquet`; `pilot-manifest.json`; and `data-manifest.json` with
version, hash and field dictionary. Data remain in approved bank custody.
Use approved local references to these files; this program does not authorize
uploading them or exposing identifiers in public evidence.

Accept only after owner review of the estimand, clock, inclusion and truth
evidence, predeclared diagnostics/precision and a held-out evaluation. Unknown
causal truth is explicit: compare randomization moments, placebo directions,
audit-error sensitivity and financial reconciliation rather than report a
synthetic-style truth-coverage number. Stop if scientific premises fail or
the attainable calendar precision cannot support the banking decision.

## Application interface acceptance

Owners required: one standalone and one application consumer maintainer.
Agree the exact operation, units, sampling law, input identity, refusal,
whole-line/disconnected-set presentation and propagated qualification fields.
Use `contracts/reliability.md` and the local rehearsal as proposed fixtures.
Consumer acceptance requires executed fixtures, including missing/changed
assumptions and unavailable intervals, plus signed versioned acceptance records
in `external/interfaces/`. Source inspection or this workspace's wrapper does
not establish acceptance in a neighboring application. No sibling is changed.

## Matched analyst workflow and investment evidence

Owner required: workflow lead and an evaluator independent of product builders.
Choose comparable complete tasks in advance (question clarification, data/clock
reconstruction, audit design, fitting, review and report). Randomize or balance
task/analyst order, freeze quality/risk grading, record active labor and elapsed
time, rework and defects, infrastructure and audit costs. Include training and
ongoing review. Preserve unsuccessful tasks and examiner disagreements.
Write `external/workflow/protocol.json`, event log, grading ledger and economic
analysis with uncertainty. A faster local fit is not analyst labor savings;
do not claim ROI until matched quality and observed total costs justify it.

## Platform and multi-host qualification

Owner required: target platform operator and recovery/operations lead. Execute
the [saved multi-host protocol](multi-host-next-step.md) on declared hardware,
software and network topology, with equal complete obligations. Add input
integrity, host loss during fit/checkpoint, replay idempotency, missing partitions,
coordinator failure and restored output identity. Freeze throughput, p95 latency,
recovery-time and memory/service objectives before measuring. Required
`external/platform/`: environment manifest, fault schedule, event logs, complete
outputs and acceptance report. Local mapped-input QR measurements are a
rehearsal and cannot stand for these trials.
