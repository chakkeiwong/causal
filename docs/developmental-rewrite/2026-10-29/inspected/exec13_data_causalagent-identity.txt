Abstract
Causal inference holds immense value in fields such as healthcare, economics, and social sciences. However, traditional causal
analysis workflows impose significant technical barriers, requiring researchers to possess dual backgrounds in statistics and computer science, while manually selecting algorithms, handling data
quality issues, and interpreting complex results. To address these
challenges, we propose CausalAgent, a conversational multi-agent
system for end-to-end causal inference. The system innovatively
