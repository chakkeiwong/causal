      The regular funding interval cannot support inference when a representative identity audit

92.4      Financial interpretation and the information still needed
Projection can trade noncoverage or refusal for width. That trade is not automatically useful.
A funding decision based on the sign of V would need the entire defensible set on one side
of the bank’s specified decision threshold. A whole-line set can never meet that rule. Even
the baseline median set width exceeds the synthetic target value (about USD 375,000) by
more than a factor of three. The exercise does not provide a bank threshold, the price of
representative audit labels, or the cost and availability of independent pilot history. Width
conditional on a finite set cannot substitute for all-request coverage and finite-set availability.
   The inherited regular inverse is tighter in selected cases but can refuse near rank and
undercovers under some finite designs. A sharper transformed average/difference confidence
region or candidate-specific covariance inversion is a substantive alternative; it would need its
own simultaneous dependent-score argument and numerical qualification. More representative
audit labels can shrink Ps but cannot cure a false origin-swap or exogeneity premise. New
outcome-linked information may identify targets that counts and candidate responses cannot.
These alternatives require separate evaluation.
   Chapter 93 develops the transformed average/difference alternative and evaluates it on fresh
paired requests, including weak audits, persistence and false exogeneity. That comparison
addresses precision under the same target; it does not acquire the new causal information
required to rule out confounding. Bank use would also require credible histories and audit
labels, an economically meaningful precision threshold, measured costs and operating evidence.
The local experiment establishes neither an adopted service nor institutional savings.

Using shared information to narrow
uncertainty

      Separate response bands retain uncertainty about customer identity but can produce
      broad funding-value sets. Shared information in the two observed candidate responses can
      narrow those sets by forming average and difference scores before covariance estimation. A
      repaired 3,600-request study finds narrower transformed sets in all 2,937 paired finite cases
      of six selected designs, without a general dominance theorem. Weak audit information
      remains the limiting factor: 558/600 near-rank outputs are whole-line; even with eight
      times as many audit labels, 105/600 are whole-line and the finite transformed median
      width is about USD 15.4 million. A pathwise observational-equivalence construction shows
      that false exogeneity cannot be solved by interval algebra alone. The study uses synthetic
      populations; performance for a particular bank requires evidence about its assignment and
      observation processes.

method applies to the institution and decision being considered. The next chapter changes the

candidate assignment is correct. This can resolve the identity ambiguity, while the exogeneity
counterexample continues to apply. Its benefit must therefore be compared with both the new
label requirements and the remaining uncertainty.

What additional information buys

     A causal banking service must return a decision quantity together with the information
     and uncertainty that make it defensible. Funding responses, sequential servicing, temporal
     relationships and verified customer histories reveal different limits on that information.
     A funding experiment uses supplied action randomization and independent calendars
     to remove a fitted-ratio mean shift. A nonlinear servicing extension evaluates a fixed
     observable policy using truthful state audits and supplied action probabilities. Both meet
     a preregistered minimum-coverage screen on selected synthetic grids with conservative
     99% critical values. A joint pair/calendar/gold-label experiment still fails finite calibration
     in important regimes. A corrected temporal graph union retains effects but can return
     the whole real line. A corrected sparse learner fails qualification at higher dimension or
     support. Full linked-outcome streaming agrees with its dense reference and survives real
     local process failures, without a consistent measured performance gain. These are research
     findings from specified experiments. Their use in a bank requires independent review,
     credible assignment and observation information, suitable operating performance and a
     defensible economic case.


95.1     One decision, several kinds of evidence
A bank deciding whether to raise a deposit rate, change a collections policy or act on a
discovered relationship needs a target with units and timing. It also needs an observation
model: a row may not identify the customer whose balance matters; a public signal may omit
the private state used by a historical decision maker; an apparent edge may permit several
intervention effects. Computation cannot supply missing identity, assignment or exclusion
information.
   The experiments ask whether changing the available information improves a stated banking
comparison. A negative result can explain which information is missing. An uncertainty
set can be statistically defensible yet too wide to distinguish a profitable action from a loss.
Scientific validity and useful precision therefore need separate attention.
   Every table cell has 512 fresh Monte Carlo requests. Covers uses all requests, including
refusals. MC lower is a simultaneous one-sided exact-binomial 95% lower bound, Bonferroni-
adjusted over all method/cell comparisons in the study. The minimum screen is .95. These
screens do not prove uniform coverage or validate supplied scientific assumptions. Primary
methods use frozen 99% critical values or covariance regions; some challengers use 95% critical
values. Width and coverage have a disclosed tradeoff.
