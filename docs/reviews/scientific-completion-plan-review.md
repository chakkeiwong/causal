# Review of the scientific completion plan

8 October 2026, Asia/Shanghai. This is author review of the proposed program,
not completion of its mathematical work or an independent scientific review.

The plan responds to each comparative gap with a scientific construction,
selection decision and observable exit result. Its principal change from
the previous program is that warnings and interface requirements cannot
stand in for the estimator or uncertainty argument they call for.

| Gap | Work and closure location | Failure that must keep it open |
|---|---|---|
| Dependent, large-scale causal time series | S01 regimes; S02 derivations and method hierarchy; S07 complete workload | Assumed CLT, unspecified bandwidth/splitting, iid theorem applied to common shocks, or reducer-only speed evidence. |
| POMDP estimation and value uncertainty | S03 model-matched identification, likelihood, parameter/value inference and belief error | Unknown-emission fit supported only by a known-emission result; observed-state error bound transferred without proof; fitted optimal value called evaluated policy value. |
| Discovery to effect report | S04 graph class, discovery, effect sets and statistical uncertainty | Sample splitting called graph confidence; stability frequencies called probabilities; forced graph orientation hides an unresolved effect. |
| Literature selection and scholarship | S01 and sustained S02–S05 primary reading/comparison | Another method census, technical support from metadata, or exclusions made solely to close a phase. |
| Scientific product representation | S06 typed objects, transformations and concrete API paths | Hash-binding treated as semantics, scalar protocol advertised for joint curves/graphs, or unspecified qualification delegated to implementation. |
| Automatic organization | S05 candidate grammar, constraints, ranking/abstention and target consequences | An unexplained mapping score, hard merge of ambiguous entities, or an untestable claim that records reveal economic assumptions. |
| Scale and software | S06 selected executable probes; S07 statistical and full-workflow cost comparisons | Package census treated as compatibility, successful fits only, or cost measured without inference and sensitivity. |

The four study problems anchor a broader 14-domain synthesis; they do not
turn the commission into four narrow demonstrations. Method choices remain
provisional until primary texts and adverse comparisons support them. This
avoids inventing a promised theorem or committing to a package before its
mathematical operation is understood.

The time-series route explicitly includes persistence and cointegration
treatment, a dependent inference construction and a scalable learning path.
The plan therefore cannot close by supporting only independent customer
averages. The POMDP route must complete an identified estimation baseline
even if a more general unknown-emission/private-logger extension remains
conditional. Discovery may legitimately deliver ambiguity, but it must show
how that changes the intervention decision. These distinctions preserve the
user's central capabilities without promising to solve unrestricted open
problems.

The dependency order is feasible for one agent. Source reading, derivations
and small discriminating experiments proceed together; expensive repetitions
and performance runs follow initial mathematical construction. S04 inherits
the applicable dependence argument, S06 follows the scientific phases, and
S08 follows integrated evidence. The first checkpoint contains a completed
scientific route rather than planning alone. No external reviewer or
delegated work is assumed.

Monte Carlo precision and hardware pilots determine repetition counts and
scale limits. The illustrative 500-repetition coverage calculation is a
design aid, not a guarantee that every case is qualified at that count.
Repeated-sample success supplements a theorem and does not replace it.
Unfavorable outcomes can change the method/product choice; they are not
deleted to satisfy a preset success narrative.

Existing source controls and authorization remain adequate for planning and
local scholarship. Selected new package installations need concrete version,
environment and access choices before any genuinely necessary escalation.
The plan does not broaden the allow list, authorize external messages or
permit sibling edits. Every new citation retains the genuine-copy and
source/claim audit requirements.

Disposition: the plan is ready for the S01 scientific specification and S02
time-series construction. The eight scientific phases remain open. S08 must
judge reconstructability against substantive reference passages, not counts,
script success or the existence of chapter headings.

## Execution review: additional defects and repairs

The user authorized execution after thorough review. A second dependency
review found concrete issues in the proposed sequence:

1. S05 was scheduled after the inference phases although observation clocks
   determine their data and targets. Repair: S01 fixes an oracle observation
   contract and adversarial raw-record fixtures; S05 later infers candidates
   against that frozen contract. Estimation cannot silently change it.
2. A single-series result, independent-trajectory result and common-shock
   panel result could still be confused. Repair: assign distinct regime IDs
   and prohibit transferring standard errors between them; document each
   limiting experiment and its effective sample size.
3. The unknown-emission POMDP fit lacked a selected identification design.
   Repair: require a constructive finite-state identification argument under
   exogenous randomized logging before that fitted route can be causal;
   retain private logging as a distinct sensitivity/conditional model.
4. Discovery sample splitting controls one form of reuse but not graph
   coverage. Repair: begin with a finite Gaussian graph class and joint
   covariance confidence construction, mapping all compatible models to
   an effect set. Scalable temporal discovery needs its separate theorem.
5. The study plan could choose bandwidths, caps and favorable laws after
   seeing final results. Repair: version the experiment specification before
   final repetitions; pilots may change settings with an explicit receipt.
6. S06 could postpone every executable feasibility question until after
   drafting. Repair: use installed numerical/data packages and small exact
   references during derivation; qualify specific external operations only
   after their version and semantics have been inspected.
7. Runtime qualification will conflict with an old document-only audit
   assertion. Repair: preserve historical source qualifications and attach
   separate version-bound runtime receipts, updating the active audit to
   validate that evidence rather than banning runtime evidence.

The plan remains suitable after these amendments. They are dependencies and
scientific safeguards, not new approval gates. S01 execution now begins.
