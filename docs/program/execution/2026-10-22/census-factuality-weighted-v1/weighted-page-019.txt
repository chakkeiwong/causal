    The above notion of optimality is a minimax analogue of the notion of a uniformly most
accurate confidence bound (Lehmann and Romano, 2024, Section 3.5). In Appendix B, I show
that η̂1−β is uniformly most accurate in the class of quantile-unbiased estimators that depend
on θ̂ through θ̂′ Qθ̂. In fact, η̂1−β minimizes expected loss in this class under any quasiconvex
loss function that attains its minimum at H(θ), for any heterogeneous θ. The latter optimality
statement is based on results from Pfanzagl (1994) on optimal quantile-unbiased estimation
in models with monotone likelihood ratios.
