# I3 author argument review

13 October 2026. Scope: new workspace/standalone chapter and its preserving
reader delta. This is author review, not independent or consumer acceptance.

The chapter now follows a consumer question through target mapping, clocks,
numerical authority, identification, uncertainty, value and displayed result.
All eighteen inventory directories are accounted for, including both grouped
MacroFinance worktrees, the dsge_hmc container and excluded support/policy
directories. Selected declarations were re-inspected directly. Ten changed
historical source identities are explicitly a dated drift observation; the
four technical files used for new detailed mappings remained unchanged.

The examples were reviewed separately from their proposed adapters:

- Funding uses a negative ten-basis-point action, fixed USD exposure, quarterly
  accrual and an annual effective discount rate. Summed cash flows and the
  affine value mapping agree. The supplied coefficient range is a scenario,
  not a fitted interval. Endogenous balances require a different joint target.
- The finite-state approximation argument defines total variation and proves
  the bounded-payoff inequality. Exact probabilities and a coupling attain the
  monetary error bound. The example is not a BayesFilter run, error certificate,
  posterior interval or frequentist coverage claim.
- Two-stage servicing defines independent structural disturbances and behavior
  propensities, so intervention and weighting identities are justified by the
  generated law. Full rational enumeration and a separate marginal-expectation
  derivation give 29, 43.6 and 14.6 USD per account. The policy clock, costs and
  zero-discount terminal horizon are explicit. No static adapter is relabeled
  as an implemented longitudinal method.
- The CardNPV accounting illustration uses its admitted horizon semantics and
  separates a perfect-execution gap from the feasible-policy contrast. The
  short generated servicing example is not passed as an admitted CardNPV input.
- The two graph models have exactly the same positive observed distribution.
  Their effects and values differ. The set concerns these two specified
  models; it is neither a full latent-model identified set nor a confidence
  region. A timing restriction alone is not presented as excluding confounding.
- Finite-population selection uses fixed outcomes and independent known
  inclusion probabilities. Enumeration verifies unbiasedness, variance 1100
  and the naive mean's expectation 45.625. Zero inclusion is demonstrated by
  indistinguishable worlds. Shared-map variance uses a separate elementary
  dependent-error example and does not claim a covariance estimator.
- Population transport states effect invariance and support explicitly; its
  supplied two-stratum weights reverse a +6 source effect to -6 in the target.
  Estimated weights require joint uncertainty; relabeling the target cannot
  transport an effect. Portfolio aggregation states fixed known membership,
  no interference and a known joint net-value table. Disjoint-group and
  interaction decompositions both yield 2300 rather than the naive 2900.
  Primary CashEntry/Value inspection supports only the declared ownership,
  category and continuation rules, not an executed accounting bridge.

All three complete example scripts reproduce byte-identically. New mathematical
