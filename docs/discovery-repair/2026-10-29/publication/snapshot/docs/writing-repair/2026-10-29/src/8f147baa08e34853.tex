\section{Augmented synthetic control: extrapolation, tuning and uncertainty}
\label{sec:exec24-augsynth-reuse}

A banking intervention may affect a regional portfolio outside the convex
hull of plausible untreated donors. Augmentation can reduce pretreatment
imbalance by combining a constrained donor fit with an outcome model. It
also changes the interpretation of weights and the information needed to
defend the comparison. The selected \texttt{augsynth} implementation is a
reference for a bounded public prototype, conditional on explicit
extrapolation, tuning and uncertainty contracts. This decision grants no
native adapter capability.~\citep{exec24_augsynth_choice}

The author-repository revision of 4 September 2026 declares version 0.2.0,
R at least 3.5.0 and MIT with a license file. The actual MIT text is retained,
including its notice condition and warranty disclaimer. Imports include
\texttt{osqp} at least 1.0.0, \texttt{Matrix}, \texttt{Formula} and several
data-transformation packages. These identify selected originals; installed
dependency rights, numerical behavior and support require separate
qualification. The inherited documentation record is preserved without
extending its claims to uninspected paths.
\citep{exec24_augsynth_source,v12_sw_augsynth}

\subsection{The dispatcher determines the scientific comparison}
More than one finite treatment time selects \texttt{multisynth}, where a
supplied \texttt{progfunc} is ignored with a warning. One treatment time
selects the single-outcome or multiple-outcome route according to the
formula. Several treated units at one date are therefore different from
several treatment dates. The inspected single-outcome prediction averages
treated outcomes; it is not automatically an exposure-weighted portfolio
effect. The banking request must distinguish an equally weighted branch,
account or region from an exposure-weighted aggregate.
\citep{exec24_augsynth_source}

The single-outcome defaults are ridge augmentation, SCM weights and no unit
fixed effect. Preparation splits observations at the intervention date and
uses each unit's maximum treatment flag. This persistent-treatment
representation is not a general treatment-reversal contract. A proposed
adapter must validate unit-time uniqueness, pre/post unit alignment,
missingness, calendar meaning and persistence. A rectangular matrix does
not establish those causal conditions. Covariates are aggregated before
treatment, but their measurement availability still needs an information
clock. Full staggered and multiple-outcome engines remain separate review
targets.~\citep{exec24_augsynth_source,exec24_augsynth_choice}

\subsection{Negative weights represent extrapolation}
For the selected single-treatment ridge route without auxiliary covariates,
let $X$ be the $n_0\times p$ donor matrix after centering each feature on its
donor mean, and $x$ the centered treated vector. With simplex weights
$w^{\rm sc}$ and penalty $\lambda>0$, the inspected update is
\[
 w^{\rm aug}=w^{\rm sc}
   +X(X'X+\lambda I_p)^{-1}(x-X'w^{\rm sc}).
\]
Since $\mathbf{1}'X=0$, the adjustment sums to zero. Individual weights
need not be nonnegative. This argument concerns the specified centered
route; covariates, fixed effects and supplied feature transformations must
have their own matched contracts.~\citep{exec24_augsynth_source,exec24_augsynth_choice}

In an authored example, centered donors are $(-1,1)$, the treated value is
two, $w^{\rm sc}=(0,1)$ and $\lambda=2$. The initial imbalance is one and
$X'X+\lambda=4$. The adjustment $(-1/4,1/4)$ gives weights $(-1/4,5/4)$,
remaining imbalance $1/2$ and absolute weight sum $3/2$. Donor revenues of
80 and 120 thousand USD produce a counterfactual 130; treated revenue 150
gives a gap 20. The simplex gap would be 30. Neither gap is identified by
arithmetic alone: donor eligibility, stable extrapolation and absence of
relevant interference need justification. Truncating the negative weight
and renormalizing changes the estimator; it is not a formatting adjustment.
\citep{exec24_augsynth_choice}

The constrained solver introduces an auxiliary outcome path tied to donor
weights by linear equalities. With a symmetric positive-semidefinite metric,
its objective agrees with donor matching up to a constant and positive
scale. The wrapper requests OSQP relative and absolute tolerances of
$10^{-8}$ and extracts the donor part of the primal vector. At that boundary,
it does not return a complete certificate or locally gate extraction on
termination status. This does not imply that OSQP lacks internal status
information. A proposed adapter must retain status, constraint residuals
and numerical diagnostics. A scaled imbalance also needs an explicit state
when uniform-donor imbalance, its denominator, is zero.
\citep{exec24_augsynth_source}

\subsection{A tuning calendar is part of the estimator}
The actual ridge route calls \texttt{cv\_lambda} and then
\texttt{get\_lambda\_errors}; the generic CV file is not authoritative for
this path. The grid contains $n_\lambda+1$ candidates including its nominal
maximum and minimum. R's \texttt{seq(0:n\_lambda)} returns indices of its
vector argument, so the subsequent exponents are zero through $n_\lambda$.
Maximum four, ratio $1/16$ and two intervals give $(4,1,1/4)$.
The default maximum is the squared leading singular value of the donor
matrix. Selection defaults to the largest lambda within one reported
standard error of the minimum mean loss.
\citep{exec24_augsynth_source,exec24_augsynth_choice}

For $T$ columns and holdout length $h$, the inspected loss helper uses
starts $1,\ldots,T-h$, excluding the last feasible start $T-h+1$.
With four columns and $h=1$, only starts one, two and three are evaluated.
Authored squared losses $(0,0,0,100)$ for candidate A and $(4,4,4,4)$ for B
give selected-window means zero and four, but all-window means 25 and four.
These abstract losses demonstrate a possible ranking change, not observed
package output. A comparison must reproduce or explicitly change that
boundary.~\citep{exec24_augsynth_source,exec24_augsynth_choice}

The helper divides the sample standard deviation of holdout losses by the
square root of their count. Overlap or serial dependence does not make
this a calibrated sampling standard error automatically. It can remain a
tuning convention while its inferential interpretation stays qualified.
Small calendars, invalid holdout lengths, zero variation, singular
covariates and nonfinite losses require explicit tests and refusal states.
Model selection, calendar and failed-candidate accounting should be frozen
before the intervention outcome is inspected.
\citep{exec24_augsynth_source,exec24_augsynth_choice}

\subsection{Finite resampling and confidence-grid boundaries}
The inspected conformal path adjusts treated outcomes under each null,
refits the model and computes residuals; the fitted ridge lambda is retained
in the extra arguments. Its IID branch randomly permutes residuals and the
other inspected branch uses circular shifts. These require different
scientific resampling justifications. Stationarity alone does not establish
every finite circular-shift test as exact. No theorem is borrowed here from
an uninspected package bibliography.
\citep{exec24_augsynth_source,exec24_augsynth_choice}

The tail proportion averages indicators that resampled statistics exceed
or equal the observation. An authored observation three and sampled values
zero, one and two yield zero; a plus-one convention yields $1/4$. Neither
formula alone proves calibration for an arbitrary experiment. Identity draws,
ties, sampling law and complete denominators must be retained. Pointwise
intervals must remain distinct from simultaneous trajectory uncertainty.
\citep{exec24_augsynth_source,exec24_augsynth_choice}

The interval helper appends zero to its null grid, takes extrema of accepted
values, and returns every p-value whose grid entry is zero. If zero was
already present, the returned vector can exceed the three entries expected
by its caller. A zero-width grid is one mathematical case to qualify. If
no value is accepted, the base-language extrema are positive and negative
infinity, respectively. Extrema of a disconnected accepted set give its
envelope, not its complete membership, and a finite grid does not certify
tail closure. The proposed contract retains the full grid and acceptance
set, deduplicates nulls under an explicit RNG policy, and distinguishes
empty, truncated and disconnected outcomes. These conditional static
findings are not observed package failures.
\citep{exec24_augsynth_source,exec24_augsynth_choice}

\subsection{Donor policy and an accountable reuse decision}
The separately inspected staggered donor helper requires donor treatment
strictly after cohort time plus the requested horizon; other helpers
restrict missingness and matching. Inspecting them does not qualify the
full staggered solver. A changed horizon can change the donor population.
The donor-table helper can initiate additional placebo inference when RMSPE
is requested and replace tiny displayed weights by zero. Provenance and
cost records must distinguish these computations and displayed weights
from the fitted result.~\citep{exec24_augsynth_source}

The decision is reference use. Direct R adoption is deferred until complete
rights, numerical status, calendar, tuning and uncertainty are qualified
on matched public or generated examples. A full port is deferred because
it must reproduce indexing, dispatch, centering, refitting and failure
semantics, not merely the ridge equation. A small independent oracle tests
specific contracts but replaces neither the package nor identification.
Constrained synthetic control, augmentation and factor models are distinct
alternatives whose assumptions should be compared explicitly.
\citep{exec24_augsynth_choice}

Scientific owners justify donors and uncertainty; adapter owners preserve
targets, clocks, weights, diagnostics and refusals; platform and rights
owners qualify the installed distribution; evaluation owners preregister
oracles and count every attempt. An illustrative 3,600 USD across 180
attempted, 150 completed and 120 usable tasks gives 20, 24 and 30 USD per
respective denominator. These are planning values, not measured prices or
bank benefits. Tuning, null refits, failures, storage, recovery and
maintenance belong in the cost record. Revisit the decision when a
justified prototype needs this comparison and those contracts can be
tested. Native capability, full dependency rights, uncertainty and final
acceptance remain open.~\citep{exec24_augsynth_choice}
