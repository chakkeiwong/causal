12 October 2026. Literature cutoff: 7 October 2026.

The program is unfinished. The present working monograph has 375 pages, but the
three requested repairs remain incomplete: the software, LLM/agent and
integration parts occupy physical spans of 42, 42 and 12 pages. None of the ten
original capability gaps is fully qualified. Twenty-three of 48 packages have
recorded outcomes, including negative and inconclusive results. Those outcomes
must be interpreted within their stated scope.

The immediate planning defect was real. The previous controller required the
entire software census before accepting any family comparison, every family
comparison before reuse decisions, and the entire reuse package before an
individual adapter. Its fixed priority repeatedly selected SW01. Saying that

This revision replaces 16 broad dependency edges across nine unresolved

## The standard and the four completion decisions

The deliverable is a connected causal-banking monograph, a reusable product
proposal and evidence for the capabilities it actually claims. It must cover
four journeys: funding responses; sequential servicing; discovery followed by
an intervention query; and trustworthy observations. Its six research/product
streams remain identification and learning, dependent time series and panels,
longitudinal and controlled-state decisions, discovery and uncertain
observations, reusable operations and computation, and financial validation
and economics.

Each claimed use must connect the business decision and information clock to
a causal model and assumptions, identification or an identified set, estimation
and learning, uncertainty and diagnostics, substantive alternatives, numerical
error and monetary consequences. The named FinMarketing monograph is a standard
for a developed scholarly argument. It is neither a page-padding target nor
evidence that a new integration already works.

Every scholarly citation needs its genuine PDF under `docs/papers/`, named
`Title FirstAuthorSurname(year).pdf`. Every cited source, including official
software documentation, needs identity, version/date, saved bytes, SHA-256,
inspected technical anchors and supported claims. Metadata establishes identity
and discovery only. Unavailable originals stay in the omissions register;
an author-written summary cannot replace them. Sources retrieved after the
cutoff must have their applicable historical revision established or be
reported as a separate dated delta. Banking arithmetic must reproduce.

The four decisions are deliberately separate. Author completion requires a
reviewed argument, evidence and limitations, and can include negative results.
Capability qualification requires valid and useful output in a declared model,
sampling and information regime. External acceptance requires authentic
independent, bank, consumer and operator evidence. Investment/adoption requires
actual decision-value, cost and owner acceptance. A source inspection, compiled
PDF, passing API test or completed plan cannot supply the later decisions.

| Mandatory repair | Required completion |
|---|---|
| MR01: software | Ten chapters; 100-150 substantive pages; all 17 areas across R, Python, Java, C++, other relevant implementations and commercial products; individual coverage dispositions for all 102 cells; edition-specific commercial comparison; reasoned adopt/integrate/port/reference/defer/reject decisions; representative actual engine qualification or explicit failed/unavailable dispositions. |
| MR02: LLMs and agents | Eight chapters; 96-112 substantive pages; all 14 tasks; substantial frontier literature and package comparison; especially business translation, causal diagrams and discovery; banking cases, alternatives, counterexamples, remedies, evaluation and unresolved research. |
| MR03: integration | Four chapters; indicative 40-60 substantive pages; all 12 requirements; library/CLI/MCP semantics, actual language paths, custody, versions, graph/set topology, recovery, standalone clients, all workspace dispositions, four journeys and authentic consumer evidence distinguished from fixtures. |

All 22 chapter allocations remain binding. Logs, raw catalogs, bibliography,
front matter, empty pages and repeated baseline material earn no substantive
page credit. Shared material is counted once. Physical spans are not accepted
body counts. Any allocation exception requires an explicit substantive coverage
review; it cannot waive a missing subject or essential source.

## Dependency rules that now govern execution

An execution prerequisite must supply an artifact, scientific argument, license
decision, runtime dependency or authentic input actually consumed by that
operation. A final acceptance gate can aggregate all evidence needed for its
claim. A phase number, unrelated family, page target or previous checkpoint
cannot be an execution prerequisite.

## Work lane 1: software evidence and scientific comparisons

The 102 source cells are independently addressable. For each, inspect the
existing search record and candidate identities; complete complementary primary
routes and relevant citation/repository chaining; record cutoff, duplicates,
inclusion/exclusion and bounded stopping rules; acquire genuine originals;
inspect exact technical behavior; and write a reviewed disposition with
alternatives and limitations. A cell with no eligible candidate needs a
documented search. An inaccessible essential source prevents its claim from
closing, while other cells continue.

The 17 area comparisons begin from already inspected evidence. Their specific
questions are in the audit: graph classes, identification algorithms, estimands,
sampling units, nuisance learning, survival/competing events, policies and
logging, cohort/time aggregation, donor uncertainty, instruments/common shocks,
interference, mediation/sensitivity, transport/missingness, latent states,
observation truth and complete computation. Each comparison needs explained
assumptions, diagnostics, a banking example, substantive alternatives and typed
failure behavior. It must distinguish documentation, inspected implementation,
executed behavior and scientifically qualified inference.

Final review of an area waits only for that area's needed language/source
dispositions. Commercial analysis is a separate lane, so missing a vendor manual
does not stop a noncommercial area review. Discovery evidence can feed discovery
chapters and one adapter while panel or longitudinal searches continue.

## Work lane 2: reuse decisions and actual engine paths

Review every catalog row. Confirm whether it is a package, alias, family lead,
generic infrastructure or vendor offering. Choose adopt, integrate, port,
reference, defer or reject, explaining target/uncertainty semantics, evidence,
license/permission, dependency burden, alternatives, maintenance responsibility,
cost and revisit trigger. A port additionally needs a behavioral oracle and
numerical/statistical equivalence plan. Mere source availability does not grant
a safe integration decision.

The first candidate paths are pcalg in R, y0 in Python, Tetrad in Java and the
grf native C++ core with its R/native boundary stated explicitly. These are
candidate paths selected from existing evidence, not approved integrations.
Their source and license decisions are independent. If one is rejected or
unavailable, preserve that disposition and review a scoped replacement; the
other paths continue. Shared underlying engines do not count as independent
scientific replications.

For each approved path, specify one concrete causal operation and conversion
contract inside this repository. Bind engine/build version, dependencies,
mathematical object types, coordinates, units, graph endpoints, effect-set
topology, uncertainty, errors and unsupported cases. Execute deterministic
reference and refusal checks against the actual engine. Then register the
matched qualification study before producing its final results. Compare
scientific validity, finite availability, useful width/loss and failures before
runtime; measure setup, I/O, conversion and total workflow cost. A documentation
inspection or hand-written fixture cannot count as engine execution.

## Scheduling, review and continuation

There is one coordinator and no spawned editing agents. Logical independence
does not require concurrent agents. The machine graph contains separately
addressable evidence obligations, not hundreds of sequential phases. Reusable
cell, decision, trial and chapter templates keep the work consistent. Final
manuscript and product results remain integrated.

The scheduler selects the least recently served lane that has ready work, then
uses attempt count, priority and stable ID within that lane. The lanes are
software, reuse, commercial, LLM, integration, research, manuscript, external
and release. The first cycle therefore combines source review, a representative
reuse decision, commercial acquisition, an LLM task, integration design,
scientific argument, chapter development and intake/release preparation. A long
source census cannot monopolize continuation.

Use bounded units and short tool output. Before an operation, inspect its exact
contract and evidence. Do the work, review the result, repair justified findings
and run appropriate checks. Record a pass only for that stated scope. A negative
or inconclusive disposition can be recorded without pretending a blocked trial
ran. A review that rejects reuse may complete the decision but cannot enable an
adapter. An essential missing source keeps the affected acceptance open.

After recording a result or a blocker, the controller immediately reports the
next eligible operation across all lanes. Continue it without asking for phase
or checkpoint permission. A checkpoint saves state; it is not a stopping point.
Stop dependent work only for a real missing prerequisite, invalid argument,
runtime/permission constraint or absent authentic input, record the evidence and
exact trigger to resume, and continue unrelated work. If no operations are
eligible, report the remaining real blockers and needed input precisely.

For a long operation, `progress` records bounded inspected evidence and the
exact next action without awarding completion. It also rotates lane selection,
so an unfinished literature census cannot monopolize the session. Saving this
checkpoint is followed by continued work in another eligible lane.

The controller validates and schedules work; it is not an autonomous background
writer, scientific prover or study runner. Commands do not create evidence or
send messages. Scientific registration remains prospective and immutable;
acceptance retains original substantive validators and exact-release gates.
Do not treat a ready argument task as permission to run an unregistered final
experiment. New scope after a resolved outcome requires a reviewed successor
with history preserved, rather than rewriting its receipt.


Local study protocols and original parent research protocols must be frozen
prospectively in their applicable scopes; the parent envelope can describe
independent study paths without requiring their builds or results to finish.
The package rollup must bind the actual registered paths and retain negative or
unavailable language scopes. No results are retrospectively called registered.

`deliver` admits a new versioned working book with its exact predecessor,
compiled PDF, source artifacts, source/claim review and text/layout reports.
It preserves prior deliveries and changes the current reader without requiring
another master-program revision. It never grants I02 or scientific acceptance.

## Delivery milestones and review of this plan

Milestones measure integrated evidence, not permission gates or calendar
promises. The first is a usable body of independently reviewed area comparisons,
package decisions, LLM task syntheses and chapter increments. The second is
actual scoped engine and whole-workflow evidence feeding those chapters. The
third is author release of the complete monograph and revised proposal. Scientific
successors and authentic external work advance when their real inputs permit,
alongside these milestones. Final capability and investment decisions are later
evidence gates; their timing cannot honestly be promised now.

The plan review tests coverage completeness, correct interpretation of previous
passes, dependency necessity, fair lane selection, negative/inconclusive routes,
preservation, scientific preregistration and exact-release external acceptance.
Behavioral tests must demonstrate that an unrelated missing vendor/manual or
blocked language path cannot block an eligible adapter or chapter; an unapproved
license decision cannot enable an adapter; a rejected path can be dispositioned
without claiming execution; final acceptance cannot be bypassed; and recording
work returns the next ready lane rather than stopping at a phase boundary.
