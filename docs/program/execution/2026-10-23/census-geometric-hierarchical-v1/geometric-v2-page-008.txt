Theory We now state a general definition of geometric structural causal models. Let xpa(v) denote
the parents of a variable xv in the causal graph, for all v ∈ [V ] ≜ {1, . . . , V }.
Definition 5 (Geometric structural causal model). A geometric structural causal model (GSCM)
Mscm with a group of index transforms (G, ·) is defined by
  1. endogenous structured variables x1 , . . . , xV , where xv ∈ (X v )Ω for v ∈ [V ],

  2. a directed acyclic graph G,

  3. distributions p(ϵv ) over exogenous noise variables ϵv for v ∈ [V ], and

  4. causal mechanisms f v that are equivariant to G,

                                    f v (ϕ(xpa(v) ), ϕ(ϵv )) = ϕ(f v (xpa(v) , ϵv ))             (14)

     for all ϕ ∈ G and v ∈ [V ].
Each endogenous variable is generated as,

for ω ∈ Ω and v ∈ [V ].

Definition 6 (GSCM after a hard intervention). Under a hard intervention do(xv = xv⋆ ), the causal
