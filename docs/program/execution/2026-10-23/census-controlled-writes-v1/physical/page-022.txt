D.3    C ERTIFIED ROUNDING AS A D EDUCTIVE S OURCE

Proof of Lemma 2. Scert collects the conclusions of extracted derivation DAGs all of whose edges
verify. It is finite, since the carrier trace is finite, and effectively listable, since edge verification is
decidable. Its nonemptiness permits the empirical distribution PScert to be normalized. Soundness
of V gives Scert ⊆ Cn(B). Thus the finite, effectively listable source with its empirical distribution
satisfies Definition 2.

D.4    C ERTIFIED C OVERAGE

Proof of Proposition 2. Work on the bias-free query mass specified in the proposition. For the in-
tended derivation to survive rounding, it suffices that each of its n steps be proposed, each of those
steps pass verification, and each of its e exposed premises be formalized. The respective probabil-
ities are at least (1 − µ)n , (1 − η)n , and (1 − εf r )e . Independence in the slip channel makes the
probability of their intersection at least their product. On this event, rounding retains a complete
certified derivation, which proves the claimed lower bound.

D.5    C ERTIFIED -D EPTH OVERHEAD

Proof of Lemma 3. Certified derivations form a subset of all derivations, giving the lower bound.
For the upper bound, let π attain Dd (q | B). Restore each subgoal removed by rounding using its
shortest certified reconstruction from B. Under the recovery-depth accounting of Definition 10, this
adds at most drec (q) to the derivation depth. The resulting derivation is certified, giving the upper
bound.
