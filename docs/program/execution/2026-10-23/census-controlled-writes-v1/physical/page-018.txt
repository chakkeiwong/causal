B.4   P ROOF -VALID R ELIABILITY U NDER P REMISE E RASURE

Definition 5 (Premise erasures and transparent caches Xu (2026c)). Base leaves are independently
unavailable with rate ε due to storage loss. Their source support remains valid. A fixed cache
X ⊆ Cn(B) remains available during the erasure trial. Recovery is evaluated on a fixed hereditary
witness DAG, with each inference requiring all its designated parents. Only this witness is used for
recovery. A leaf is residually exposed if some path from it to q avoids X. Write Dexp (q; X) for these
leaves and Dexp (q) := Dexp (q; ∅).

The residual-leaf law is stated in Corollary 1, and proved in Appendix D.9. The following back-
ground result records the additional module and complexity claims.
Theorem 4 (Module gains and cache-selection complexity Xu (2026c;b)). For shared workloads,
semantic modules yield exact reliability laws under joint and maximal-error criteria; leaf-only
transparent storage incurs a first-order overhead factor 1/ε, reduced in the saturated shared regime
to ρ/(sε), where s premises are protected by one module and ρ is the module-to-leaf cost ratio.
Optimal selection in general derivation DAGs is NP-complete already at depth two; MDS parity
caching is optimal up to one packet against the coded benchmark.
