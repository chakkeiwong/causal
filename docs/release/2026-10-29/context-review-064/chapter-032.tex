\chapter{What the evidence changes about the proposed service}
\label{rewrite:evidence-consequences}

The four journeys distinguish a wrong target, insufficient treatment support,
causal ambiguity and uncertain measurement. Those failures demand different
responses. A common service should make the distinctions visible while
reusing the appropriate statistical procedures. Its initial scope follows
from the evidence below; it does not require every possible capability to
be implemented at once.

\section{A result should change a design choice}
The existing 3,000-replication generated offer study is evidence about target
alignment. Under one specified design, the residual projection averages
26.447 when the equal-population benefit is 50. Its interval covers its own
target in 97.2\% of repetitions and the population target in none. At the
stipulated cost of 40, interpreting it as the population effect rejects the
beneficial offer in all 500 repetitions of that scenario. The design
consequence is to put the population weights and target in the result,
and to compare implementations only after aligning those quantities. A
more precise projection is not a repair for this interpretation error.

The separate 6,000-replication sparse-support study addresses inference
after the target is aligned. Pooling yields 94.7\% conditional coverage
under equal normal variances but 52.2\% under the tested unequal-variance
case. Cellwise Student intervals joined by a union bound preserve the
conditional coverage guarantee in the normal model at the cost of very
wide intervals. Thus the initial implementation should show cell support
and retain an unsupported or inconclusive result. If useful precision
requires unjustified pooling, the next expenditure should improve assignment
support or the information collected. These are existing generated
experiments, not evidence of superiority by a named external package.

The public UCI timing study answers a different question. On 41,188 archived
contact records, later-archive prediction has AUC 0.630 using the candidate
pre-call feature set and 0.793 when duration is added. The latter feature
is unavailable before the call, so its predictive gain cannot justify
pre-call targeting. Differences between early and late archive outcomes
also matter for evaluation. The correct service response is to preserve
availability dates and the evaluation period. Neither score estimates
the effect of making a call. Chapter~\ref{scisyn:evidence} gives the
executed design, comparators, results and qualifications.

The retained public 401(k), monetary, panel and linked-record analyses
provide further empirical and methodological context. Their sampling,
instrument, design and observation restrictions remain attached to their
results in the detailed evidence part. A working independent-observation
covariance in a public application does not validate a bank's common-calendar
uncertainty. A successful finite construction does not settle the unrestricted
linked-history problem. The negative findings remain relevant because
they identify the information a future bank study must supply.

The new one-sample deposit calculation in Chapter~\ref{rewrite:deposit}
completes the numerical route from estimation to contribution. Its callable
and command-line agreement tests the delivery of that particular report.
The four existing library/CLI/MCP cases test semantic consistency for
their own operations, including unsupported dependence. They establish
complementary facts. The later public experiment also carries fitted effect and covariance
summaries through all three interfaces (Chapter~\ref{gap29:public}).
Raw-data fitting and final reporting remain local scripts. These executions
are not a matched analyst trial, a general estimation service or evidence
of improved bank outcomes.

\section{Choose the smallest comparison that can reverse the recommendation}
The first comparison should take the same fixed offer question through
expert composition and a minimal shared integration. The cases should
include correct assignment, heterogeneous effects, unequal treatment
support, unavailable predictors, misrecorded receipt and a dependent
sampling request outside the accepted route. The generated law supplies
truth where that is possible; a public case supplies realistic data handling
without pretending its causal truth is known. A valid refusal counts as
a completed scientific answer when the information is insufficient.

An independent evaluator should judge target correctness, assumption
visibility, uncertainty, interpretation and avoidable errors. Time must
include correction, interpretation and unsuccessful attempts. The estimate
itself is not a success metric when its target differs from the request.
The proposed comparison is not yet an executed analyst study; its purpose
is to discover whether the common representation is worth its expense.
If both workflows reach equivalent answers at equivalent cost, improve
the existing workflow. If the integration reduces consequential errors or
repeated work, a narrow bank-data pilot becomes a defensible next step.


\section{What the newer studies add to that choice}
The public earnings contrast tests the complete chain from observed rows to
uncertainty and an explicit spending hurdle. A positive contrast alone does
not establish that a cost is covered. The servicing comparisons show why a
fitted policy can offer narrow but misleading uncertainty when dropout or
state assumptions fail. The institutional panel separates persistent level
adjustment from a coherent economic unit; the linkage study makes the cost
of a justified error budget visible. These results explain which information
the proposed study description must carry, rather than establish the benefit
of buying the service.

Together they favour a comparison on completed scientific work: whether an
analyst identifies the right target, preserves uncertainty, recognizes an
unsupported premise and reaches the correct conditional decision. The
software choices in Chapter~\ref{scisyn:software} and the investment account
in Chapter~\ref{rewrite:investment-decision} follow from that evidence.

\part{Choosing methods for discovery and identification}
\label{reading:part-methods}
Software is useful when its methods answer the economic question under
credible assumptions. The comparisons begin with the relationships that a
method can discover and the effects it can identify. They distinguish a supplied
causal model from one learned from data and compare alternatives across languages.
