\chapter*{A focused investment in better banking studies}

The proposal is to compare a small shared analytical integration with a
capable quantitative team using established tools. Start with a fixed
deposit-offer question: which eligible households, what assignment, which
price rule, what one-year outcome and what decision threshold? The first
expenditure buys evidence about completed study quality and full effort.
It does not commit the bank to replacing its statistical estate.

The accompanying monograph develops four banking journeys. An offer study
carries an assignment effect through estimation uncertainty into contribution.
A servicing calculation shows why conditioning on information changed by an
earlier contact can alter the policy target. A graph example admits the same
observations with either a profitable or an unprofitable action. An observation
example reverses a financial decision when a known measurement error is
corrected. These cases motivate different scientific capabilities; they
should not be hidden behind one generic treatment-effect command.

\section*{What has been demonstrated}
The generated offer example specifies 8,000 independent households, two
equally weighted strata, normal outcome noise and a particular automatic
repricing rule. The actual estimated additional balance is \pounds1,183.47
with a conservative interval [\pounds1,128.34, \pounds1,238.60]. At the
stipulated annual margin, rate increment and costs, contribution is
\pounds2.55 per household with interval [\pounds1.03, \pounds4.06].
These are constructed results. They demonstrate an estimator-to-financial
report, not an actual bank opportunity or a software return on investment.
The callable example and its command-line process return identical results.

Earlier generated studies, retained in full, show that an accurately
estimated projection can answer the wrong population question and that a
pooled uncertainty calculation can fail when its variance assumption is
violated. These adverse results affect the proposal: every result must
carry its target, treatment support, sampling assumptions and uncertainty.
An unsupported result or a wide interval is a legitimate scientific outcome.

The public UCI timing comparison uses 41,188 archived contact records.
Including call duration raises later-archive predictive AUC from 0.630 to
0.793, but duration is unavailable before a call. That result supports
information-date controls, not a causal claim about calling customers.
Four existing local cases agree across library, CLI and MCP for graph
syntax, conditional adjustment, timing checks and a refusal. The initial
MCP initialization timeout is retained with the successful retry. The
deposit raw-data estimator is not an MCP financial-analysis operation. The
public experiment additionally sends actual fitted estimates and covariance
summaries through library, CLI and MCP with matching intervals; fitting and
report generation remain local scripts.

The public experimental subset contains 445 participants. Its estimated
earnings contrast is about 1,794, with approximate interval [479, 3,109] in
the archive's units. Subtracting an illustrative cost of 1,000 leaves an
interval crossing zero. The study compares native R and Python fits, which
agree within the declared numerical tolerance; it supplies no bank treatment
effect or general package ranking.

The newer generated comparisons sharpen the adverse cases. Servicing policy
improvement coverage falls from 94\% under the maintained model to 6\% with
future-dependent dropout and 61\% with an omitted state predictor. Fixed
effects leave bias when institutional outcomes are reassigned. Linkage bounds
can preserve a stated error budget while becoming too wide to support action.
These are conditional studies, with Monte Carlo uncertainty reported in the
monograph. They explain why observation assumptions and information costs
must remain visible in a completed report.

A follow-up design can buy missing information when it recovers the intended
historical outcome. Random invitations alone are insufficient when completion
is selective. The bounded repayment example derives an honest effect range
under that selection and a conservative confidence set; changing the outcome
during follow-up can instead make the wrong contrast more precise. The cost
of obtaining trustworthy records must accompany any proposed precision gain.

A separate exact-oracle discovery comparison tests a computational repair on
1,381 declared graph cases. Full subset search recovers every reference
skeleton in the suite; an all-neighbour screening shortcut fails on 49.
A depth-two limit leaves 165 incorrect skeletons, all reported as unfinished
searches. These are project computations under exact graph oracles, not a
Java benchmark, a field failure rate or a calibrated bank discovery service. The
finite-sample follow-up shows that completed search can still produce wrong
graphs and unreliable effect intervals. Timing information helps in selected
cases; it does not supply general calibration.

\section*{What to reuse and what to compare}
The monograph retains a seventeen-area software synthesis and the detailed
source comparisons. Selected fitting and uncertainty paths have specific
restrictions that require correction and verification before reuse; source
inspection does not establish native qualification. The first integration
should reuse a transparent statistical procedure with an independently
understandable alternative.
A new native port needs a measured advantage to justify its maintenance
cost. Discovery classes, sequential policies, dependent panels and uncertain
measurements require their own scientific restrictions; qualification of
one does not qualify the others.

Language assistance should initially clarify the question, retrieve relevant
evidence and expose alternatives, with a structured questionnaire and ordinary
search as comparators. Formal identification and numerical calculations remain
with defined procedures. Historical paper results and local scripted cases
do not establish this product's incremental live-model benefit. The literature
cutoff is 7 October 2026. The fixed frontier inventory contains 91 inspected
entries, including one explicit source-quality quarantine, and 22 unread.
The financing and epistemology comparisons refine extraction and explanation
claims without identifying a lending effect. The graph workflow does not
establish calibrated intervention intervals. The pharmacovigilance comparison
shows why relevant text terms cannot substitute for an intervention argument;
text-based adjustment requires sufficient information and the correct
target-population weights.

The proposed analyst comparison includes both answerable questions and
deliberately insufficient designs. Independent assessment covers the final
target, assumptions, uncertainty and financial interpretation. Its effort
measure includes preparation, correction, interpretation and unsuccessful
attempts as well as runtime; a correct refusal remains distinguishable from
an avoidable tool failure. The comparison has not yet measured hours saved
or improvements in bank decisions.

\section*{An economic decision with reversal conditions}
Let $N$ be annual studies eligible for the restricted route, $h$ hours saved
per study at equivalent quality, $w$ loaded hourly cost, $D$ reduced expected
decision loss, $C$ annual operating cost and $K$ annualised development and
adoption cost. The conditional annual benefit is
\[ B=N(wh+D)-C-K. \]
Actual quantities remain unknown. Count the same required outputs across
expert composition, minimal integration, a broad shared service and a
commercial alternative. Include training, maintenance and interpretation,
and avoid counting correction time both as labour and reduced decision loss.
The constructed deposit contribution is not the incremental value of the
software and must not be inserted as $D$.

The comparison must also price useful completed answers. Cheaper model
requests can cost more per accepted study when failures and correction
increase. The monograph therefore separates whole-attempt expense, independently
assessed usefulness and waiting time. Public energy measurements do not supply
bank quality, traffic, tariffs or operating savings.

Equivalent quality and cost favour the existing workflow. A commercial
entitlement providing the needed outputs at lower full cost favours that
alternative. Missing assignment support or unreliable observations favour
better study design and data collection. Reproducible reductions in errors
or full effort favour extending the integration to a narrow bank-data
pilot. Authentic bank information, independent scientific review, responsible
consumer and platform acceptance, and an institutional decision remain
necessary for adoption.

The recommendation is therefore to fund the bounded comparison first. Its
question is whether a common study representation and report improve
completed economic analysis enough to cover their cost. The full monograph
contains the mathematics, alternative methods, existing studies and adverse
findings needed to judge that claim.

\section*{Basis of this proposal}
The accompanying monograph contains the public experimental study, generated
comparisons, retained derivations and comparative source arguments behind this
proposal. Its source audit records original papers, versions, inspected
anchors and supported claim scopes. The sponsor introduces no separate source,
bank measurement, vendor price or deployment claim.
Editorial delivery, fresh visual acceptance, independent scientific
acceptance and bank adoption remain separate determinations.
