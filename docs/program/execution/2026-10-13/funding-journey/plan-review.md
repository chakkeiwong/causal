# Funding workflow design and author review

This is a project-authored design dated 13 October 2026. It adds no literature after the 7 October cutoff and makes no bank-performance claim. The inspected service is the existing local reference service. The operation contract is `IN.JOURNEY.DESIGN.funding`; final service calls belong to a separately registered `IN.JOURNEY.LOCAL.funding` study.

## Target and complete scientific chain

A treasury user is considering a quarter-percentage-point increase in the offered deposit rate. Let beta be the change in the next twelve months' **average retained balance**, in USD millions, per percentage-point increase in the quote. A one-time response of a closing balance is a different target. Scaling a normalized structural response to this quarter-point decision additionally requires stable local linearity and a well-defined intervention. A supplied proxy-moment ratio does not establish either premise.

The raw-data proposal requires a dated household/account map, population/inclusion definition, quote and balance definitions, event time, first usable publication/availability time, decision time, and a target horizon. A feature used for a decision must satisfy event <= available < decision, including revisions. Follow-up outcomes occur after the decision: this feature-clock check must not be misapplied as a requirement that future outcomes already be known. Their later ascertainment, censoring and complete twelve-month follow-up are separate requirements. The local rows test feature clocks only. Correctly ordered timestamps do not establish source ownership, identity, assignment or inclusion truth.

Monthly response origins with overlapping follow-up can be dependent. A reference moment calculation needs a justified joint limit for every numerator and the shared normalization denominator. The supplied covariance must include fitted nuisance and relevant serial/cross-coordinate terms. For a leading score psi = psi0 + A phi_eta, the long-run covariance is the sum over lags of Cov(psi_t, psi_{t-k}); it contains psi0/phi cross-terms as well as both individual long-run covariances. Consistent arbitrary plug-in learners, or data splits alone, do not prove this expansion. Cross-fitting across dependent months needs an explicit dependence argument and leakage-safe construction. R02S–R05S remain separate research obligations.

For candidate beta, invert |sqrt(n)(mean_Y-beta mean_D)| <= z sqrt(omega_YY - 2 beta omega_YD + beta^2 omega_DD). This calculation avoids dividing by a weak denominator. It can produce bounded, disconnected, all-real, empty and singleton sets. A zero supplied variance or empty set is not evidence of perfect causal knowledge or no financial risk. Nominal Gaussian confidence requires assumptions beyond this arithmetic. A sensitivity inequality carries no sampling-coverage probability.

In the proposed decision layer, first review causal and observation premises, covariance provenance and the normalization; then map the entire effect set to the financial objective, preserving adverse tails and unavailable results. Failed prerequisites give an investigation or hold recommendation. The local runtime does **not** implement that decision layer. The registered test will explicitly retain its ability to compute conditionally on unverified identity/inclusion and a declared dependent sampling law. This is an exposed boundary, not demonstrated validity for those inputs.

## Independent references and adversarial cases

The 29 cases and 118 request slots are fixed before registration. Each case runs through the library and CLI, initially and after re-instantiation/process restart. Two further slots change the horizon under an already used idempotency key. All requests, errors and replay results remain in the denominator. No MCP, foreign-language adapter, sibling consumer or bank is invoked.

The reference normal critical is z=2, with alpha=erfc(sqrt(2)) approximately 0.0455003. At n=400 and a fixed denominator of one, a numerator mean of two and supplied variance one give [1.9,2.1]; variance nine gives [1.7,2.3]. For the two-outcome Bonferroni case, family alpha is twice this value, so the same critical applies to each coordinate. Its family nominal level is approximately 0.909, not 0.95. A rectangle formed by these coordinate sets is not a fitted general joint distribution or an optimal joint region.

For the random-denominator case, n=100, means (2,1) and identity covariance give 96 beta^2 - 400 beta + 396 <= 0, hence endpoints (25 +/- sqrt(31))/12. For means (1,0), n=4 and diag(0,1), the inequality is 4-4 beta^2 <= 0, giving two rays. Means (0,0) with identity covariance give -4(1+beta^2) <= 0, the whole line. Zero covariance with means (1,0) gives the empty set; with (2,1) it gives the singleton {2}. These are elementary inequality references, not calls to the service's solver. Borderline floating-point degree cancellation is avoided; endpoint tolerance is fixed to absolute 1e-9 and relative 1e-10. This is not a certified general numerical-error guarantee.

The remaining cases cover covariance asymmetry and indefiniteness, dimension mismatch, too few origins, invalid alpha and simultaneous flags, absent and corrupted artifacts, changed unit/clock contexts, unresolved versus contradicted identity/covariance, continuous and dependent back-door requests, feature clocks, unsupported identity/inclusion audit, unavailable nuisance fitting and design evaluation. The profit break-even sensitivity set solves 3750 beta - 7000 >= 0 exactly, with beta >= 28/15.

An additional exact reference constructs X_i=theta-2 eta0+U_i and eta_hat=eta0+mean(V_i), with independent U_i~N(0,1), V_i~N(0,2), independent across i. The statistic mean(X)+2 eta_hat has influence U+2V and variance nine. Treating eta_hat as fixed keeps only variance one. This intentionally nonorthogonal generated example proves that omitted nuisance uncertainty can matter; it does not prove a theorem for an arbitrary bank learner. It also separates arithmetic conformance on hypothetical dependent-context declarations from this specifically independent Gaussian example.

The household example has truth-known contributions (0,2,4,6). Repeating the first household four times gives a record mean 12/7, while the known-household mean is three. If the map is missing, these same rows can represent different populations. With known positive inclusion probabilities (1,1,1/2,1/2), enumerating all four equiprobable selection states gives expected unweighted mean 13/6 and expected Horvitz--Thompson mean three. This is not a claim that treatment effects become observed; the contributions are generated truth. Unknown probabilities or zero inclusion for a target stratum remain unresolved.

## Financial interpretation and full cost

The authored annual scenario reprices a fixed baseline average balance of USD 2.6 million by 0.25 percentage points, costing USD 6,500. Incremental retained funding earns a 1.5% annual spread **net of its own offered deposit interest**, so incremental profit is 3,750 beta USD before the baseline repricing and workflow costs. An illustrative workflow allowance of USD 500 comprises data/identity preparation 120, integration amortization 150, scientific review 100, computation 30 and monitoring/recovery 100. These figures are scenario assumptions, not measured bank prices. Total net profit is 3,750 beta - 7,000. The narrow variance-one interval implies [125,875] USD, whereas the variance-nine interval implies [-625,1625] USD. A favorable point profit of 500 USD does not settle this decision.

The complete workflow must also budget failed requests and retries, human adjudication, optional live-model acquisition/calls, held-out evaluation, legal/platform review, implementation, security, deployment and ongoing operations. Those categories may exceed the illustrative allowance and require sensitivity analysis. Local measurement covers every attempted invocation including failures, replay, worker/CLI startup, plus active runner time including imports into the artifact store and durable journaling. It excludes inter-segment idle time, initial Python/module startup, authoring, later review/manuscript time and unexecuted activities. Parent and child process high-water RSS are reported separately; they are not simultaneous process-tree memory. No cost-per-bank-decision or ROI is inferred from seconds alone.

## Evidence classes and useful next work

1. The available local computation is a fixed summary-inversion, clock and contract-boundary screen. Its positive acceptance rule is exact slot completion, expected qualifications/errors/topologies and library/CLI/restart parity. Correct refusals count as conformance, not available estimates. No outcome-based stopping or replacement of failed fixtures is allowed.
2. Independent references are hand-derived inequalities and exact finite inclusion/economics sums. The reference calculator does not invoke an estimator or reuse the service root solver.
3. A proposed consumer contract double should require explicit target/clock/identity/covariance/decision fields and withhold funding recommendations when prerequisites are absent. Its execution is not included in this study, and it must never be described as an accepted workspace consumer.
4. An authentic owner-run study later needs accepted unit maps, actual inclusion and instrument/design evidence, independent held-out follow-up and covariance diagnostics, failure traces, full costs and an owner disposition. Its expense should be justified by a scoped prototype result. It is not a prerequisite for the present proposal.

A useful subsequent generated study can jointly generate dependent monthly histories, known identities, censoring/selection and a specified nuisance estimator, then compare matched covariance methods and effect-set/decision performance under a prospective protocol. Public funding panels can inform frequencies, scales and availability clocks but cannot supply intervention, household or assignment truth that they do not contain. These are explicit follow-up items, not reasons to suspend independent program branches.
