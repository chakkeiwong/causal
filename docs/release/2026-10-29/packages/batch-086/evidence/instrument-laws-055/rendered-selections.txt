The selected linearmodels fit defaults to heteroskedastic-robust covariance with the finite-

44.4.1    What the instrument identifies, and which policy it values

Let Z ∈ {0, 1} denote eligibility or a randomized encouragement, D(z) actual take-up under
eligibility z, and Y (d) a fixed-horizon monetary loss under treatment d. Loss is positive when
it costs the bank money. In an independent-encouragement design, assume consistency, no
interference, independence of Z from the potential variables, exclusion of a direct Z effect on
loss, monotonicity D(1) ≥ D(0), and a positive first stage. Write C = {D(1) > D(0)} for the
complier group. Independence and exclusion give

                ∆D = E[D | Z = 1] − E[D | Z = 0] = P (C),
                ∆Y = E[Y | Z = 1] − E[Y | Z = 0] = P (C)E[Y (1) − Y (0) | C],
                 θC = ∆Y /∆D .                                                           (44.6)

The second equality follows because always-takers and never-takers do not change treatment,
while every complier changes from zero to one. It does not identify their unobserved treatment
contrasts, and therefore does not generally identify the population average effect. With a
deterministic score cutoff, global independence of eligibility is unavailable: an appropriate
continuity argument at the cutoff, or an explicitly justified local-randomization window, must
supply the corresponding design. Calling eligibility an instrument does not supply that
argument.
  Consider the following complete, project-authored population. The encouragement is
independent of type and has probability 1/2. All monetary values are hypothetical dollars
over the same horizon. (Causal banking proposal project, 2026bk)

treats only compliers.

44.4.4    Local randomization is an alternative design with its own obligations

rdlocrand assumes local randomization within a supplied or selected window. The window-
selection routine considers nested windows and covariate balance tests. That procedure is
useful evidence for design assessment, but a favorable balance p-value cannot prove assignment
independence. Outcome-driven window search also changes the inference problem. The
proposed request must retain whether the window was fixed before outcome inspection,
selected with pre-treatment covariates, or chosen through a procedure requiring additional
selection analysis. (rdlocrand package contributors, 2026)
   The distinction is visible in a finite reference. Fix six customers’ losses under the sharp null
at (0, 0, 0, 100, 100, 100) dollars. Assume exactly three are assigned treatment, uniformly over
the 63 = 20 allocations, in a window fixed in advance. If the first three receive treatment, the
difference in mean loss is −100. Exactly two allocations have an absolute difference at least
this large, so the two-sided randomization p-value is 2/20 = 0.1. This is complete enumeration
under a Fisher sharp null of no effect for any customer. It does not supply a general test of
zero population average effect under arbitrary heterogeneous effects. (Causal banking proposal
project, 2026bk)
   The pinned rdlocrand documentation also distinguishes requested from effective polynomial
degree. For the documented mean contrasts and fuzzy procedures, positive degree invokes
large-sample normal inference; a randomization p-value is not computed. Some statistic/degree
combinations revert to degree zero with a warning. Fuzzy test inversion can require an explicit
effect grid. Consequently, the adapter must export the effective method, degree, variance choice,
assignment law, seed, repetitions, window and grid, not just the originally requested options.
HC3 standard errors do not confer finite-sample randomization validity, and a TSLS/Wald
label does not remove weak-first-stage concerns. (rdlocrand package contributors, 2026)

44.7.1    Tetrad: follow the observation axis and the parameter lifecycle

At the retained Tetrad revision, Tsls.estimate() constructs a new SemIm, calls its coefficient
procedure and returns that object. The default fixed measurement is chosen by lexicographic
name order unless an explicit loading list is supplied. A loading normalized to one defines
a coordinate for the latent variable. To see the consequence without making a package-
performance claim, write an authored structural relation as Y = 2L + ε. Under the coordinate
L∗ = 4L, the same relation is Y = 12 L∗ + ε. The numeric coefficient changes while the
represented relation does not. A banking adapter must carry the fixed indicator, units
and scaling with the coefficient; a renamed or rescaled indicator must not silently turn a
measurement normalization into a new economic claim. (Ricardo Silva and Tetrad contributors,
2026; cmu-phil/tetrad contributors, 2026a; Causal banking proposal project, 2026fm)
   The earlier source finding concerns a specific measurement-stage loop, not every Tetrad esti-
mator. Its intended vector is the fixed indicator’s column, but it calls getDouble(colIndex,i)
while iterating over cases. The concrete BoxDataSet forwards its first argument as row and
second as column to its data box. DoubleDataBox indexes a row-major array accordingly;
VerticalDoubleDataBox uses column-major storage while retaining the same public row/-
column contract. Thus storage orientation does not repair the call. An exact authored
5 × 3 table Dij = 100i + j, with zero-based indices and fixed column one, has the intended
vector (1, 101, 201, 301, 401). Swapping indices first reads (100, 101, 102) and then requests
columns three and four, outside this table. This is a source-level reachability condition and an
independently reproduced indexing witness. No installed Tetrad invocation was performed. A
useful native qualification must reach this branch on nonsquare data, preserve case identities,
and distinguish a repaired call from a wrapper that simply avoids the branch. (cmu-phil/tetrad
contributors, 2026a; Causal banking proposal project, 2026fm)

The preceding comparisons establish why a common interface must preserve design-specific

45.1     An instrument is a scientific claim
Suppose a bank uses a rule-based funding incentive Z as an instrument for an offered rate
A, with retained balances Y as the outcome and baseline controls W . Software can encode a
regression system, but it cannot establish that the incentive has no direct effect on balances
or that its assignment is independent of omitted balance determinants. Those are properties
of the economic setting and information process. The package comparison begins after the
instrument, target population, timing and exclusion claim have been stated.
   The selected ivreg documentation describes ordinary two-stage least squares and robust
M/MM regression choices. Exogenous regressors in the outcome equation must also appear
among the instruments. An adapter should therefore retain explicit endogenous, exogenous and
instrument column roles rather than constructing the formula from a single list of predictors.
The robust regression option is an estimator choice; it does not supply a weak-instrument
confidence guarantee. The inspected package is ivreg 0.6-8. The separate AER implementation
is compared in Section 44.4.2; shared function names do not make the engines interchangeable.
That bounded review does not qualify the whole AER family. (ivreg contributors, 2026)
  Weak information changes the result contract. The inspected ivmodel documentation

45.2     Regression discontinuity is a family of designs
A credit policy can change at a score cutoff, but several causal questions can be attached to
that threshold. A sharp design studies a deterministic assignment change. A fuzzy design
uses a discontinuity in treatment probability. A kink design studies a change in slope. These
require different estimands and denominators. Treating them as one generic local regression
would make an apparently comparable benchmark compare different scientific problems.
  The inspected rdrobust 4.0.0 documentation exposes sharp, fuzzy and kink settings, local-
polynomial choices, bias correction, bandwidth choices and variance options. The adapter
should bind the cutoff and running-variable units to the result and export the actual bandwidth
and inference settings. The bandwidth for a point-estimation objective and the choices
underlying a bias-corrected interval cannot be silently conflated. The saved 4.0.0 snapshot
predates the literature cutoff. Section 44.4 explains why the subsequently authenticated CRAN
publication time retains that distribution for the client cutoff; it does not settle every upstream
channel’s history. (rdrobust contributors, 2026)
   For a fuzzy design, the treatment jump matters as much as the outcome jump. If a local
effect is expressed schematically as τY /τA , a small τA produces an unstable ratio even when
each fitted regression has a clean numerical convergence report. The bank’s first question
should be whether the score rule changes the specified treatment in the relevant population.
The software trial should vary that first-stage jump and preserve weak cases rather than
selecting only a strong demonstration.
   rddensity supplies a different operation: local-polynomial density or manipulation diagnostics
near the cutoff. Such diagnostics can challenge a design, but failure to reject a discontinuity
does not verify the assignment mechanism or continuity of potential outcomes. An interface
that displays “RD valid” after a density test would convert one diagnostic into a much
stronger scientific statement. A useful report instead gives the test, bandwidth, support and
interpretation limit alongside other design evidence. Discrete scores and mass points require
explicit attention. (rddensity contributors, 2026)
   rdlocrand represents another approach, based on a declared local-randomization window.
Its selected documentation states that a positive polynomial order uses asymptotic inference
and that confidence-set inversion is performed over a supplied grid. Thus a finite grid endpoint
is not automatically a finite confidence bound. If all evaluated values near the boundary

45.3      Sensitivity parameters are not missing truth
Two other R interfaces provide useful sensitivity summaries. sensemakr parameterizes omitted-
variable sensitivity through partial R2 quantities and observed-covariate benchmarks. A large
benchmark asks how conclusions would change if an omitted variable had specified residual
associations; it does not measure the actual association of an unobserved variable. The bank
must identify what makes a proposed benchmark plausible, and the report should retain both
the chosen benchmark and the effect scale. (sensemakr contributors, 2024)
   The selected EValue evalues.RR interface takes a risk ratio and optional confidence limits
and reports E-values for the point and the near-null confidence limit. This is a particular
scale-specific sensitivity operation. A default probability difference, a standardized regression
coefficient and a net present value should not be passed to it as if they were risk ratios.
Any conversion needs an explicit target and assumptions. An E-value does not estimate a
confounding correction or certify an absence of confounding. (EValue contributors, 2025)
   These tools can make a funding or credit recommendation more intelligible: they expose
how much an assumption would need to change before a conclusion changes. They cannot
supply verified instrument exclusion, known ownership, correct record inclusion or a gold audit
label. Their adoption is therefore conditional on a result contract that separates observed data
summaries, user-supplied sensitivity assumptions and causal conclusions.
