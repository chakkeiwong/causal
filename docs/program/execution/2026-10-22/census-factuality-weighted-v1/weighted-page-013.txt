Remark 2 (Reporting Constraints). Since Λ represents the broad range of beliefs and objec-
tives that researchers and readers may have, it is impractical to report (τ̂λ ,CIλ ) for all λ ∈ Λ.
Moreover, if there are confidentiality restrictions or communication costs, reporting the entire
data (θ̂,Σ) may not be a broadly applicable solution.13 With these issues in mind, I will develop
methods that facilitate inferences across λ ∈ Λ based simply on (i) the baseline report (τ̂w ,σw )
and (ii) a supplementary report of low-dimensional statistics, where the latter loosely refers
