# Writing-standard assessment of release batch 085

30 October 2026. Attributed author review; not independent acceptance.

The required standard is a connected research monograph from which a prepared
economist can reconstruct the scientific argument. A chapter should explain the
banking decision and information clock, define its model and notation, state
the assumptions, derive identification or the identified set, construct the
estimator and its uncertainty, examine failure and sensitivity, and explain the
monetary consequence, substantive alternatives and recommendation. FinMarketing
is a benchmark for developed exposition, not a length target. Neither a proposal
label nor lack of bank data lowers this theoretical standard.

The original scholarship review correctly rejected a book that named methods,
listed cautions and passed artifact checks without developing the connections.
That remains the relevant test. Source hashes establish identity; reproducible
arithmetic establishes specified calculations; contextual reading assesses the
argument. None alone establishes scholarly sufficiency.

## Remaining gaps against the standard

**Independent critical reading and editorial acceptance remain absent.** The
author records cover all 101 numbered chapters through exact complete contexts
and explicitly verified component chains. That is useful review provenance,
not a substitute for a new reader testing whether the exposition is navigable,
persuasive and correct. The present pass reads the central arguments in full
source context and selected exact rendered text. It does not freshly reread all
1,941 rendered pages. Length and accumulated specialist comparisons make
readability, repetition and proportionality consequential review questions.
Any proposed condensation must preserve the derivations and negative findings
or identify their accessible destination before deletion.

**Fresh visual acceptance of the exact PDFs remains open.** Compilation,
resolved references and text bounds pass. The exhausted session image allowance

A fresh session should inspect the exact bound reader and sponsor, beginning
with altered passages, dense derivations and long comparison tables.

**The source comparison is bounded rather than exhaustive.** The fixed frontier
has 91 inspected entries and 22 unread originals. One inspected entry is a
source-quality quarantine, so inspection is not positive support. Supporting
method papers and the separately excluded energy comparator do not enlarge
that denominator. TCDF and the original Windmeijer work retain their separate
omission records; an inspected neighbouring method cannot stand in for either
original. Mutable or date-uncertain commercial pages are dated observations,
not certified availability before 7 October 2026. The residual inventory names
each remaining original and its next action. A broader ranking or completeness
claim requires new evidence and another comparison in context.

**General scientific capabilities remain unresolved in the places the text
states.** Useful precision under persistent linkage uncertainty, general
nonlinear hidden-state intervention inference, arbitrary dependent learners,
and general learned-graph effect confidence have not become solved merely
because restricted examples now have derivations. The book supplies negative
findings and bounded supported constructions. This is an acceptable way to
state an open research question; it cannot support a universal capability
claim. If such a claim becomes necessary, the missing construction is a
scholarly task before any bank pilot.

**Implementation and adoption evidence remain separate.** The inherited
software census contains 99 open/inconclusive rows with mixed source, rights,
native and coverage obligations. That is not a count of missing chapters.
Selected native examples do not qualify every version or method. Actual bank
information, matched analyst effort, platform performance and institutional
adoption concern later qualification, not a reason to defer elementary theory.
The source follow-ups for DoubleML, did, causaleffect, MATLAB, HUGIN and the
named causaLens service are already developed in chapter 26; the programme
must not repeat superseded claims that those documentary comparisons are absent.
