   study correlation with expert ratings (Kendall’s 𝜏 = 0.78) provides some validation,
   but more comprehensive human evaluation would strengthen our findings.
2. Gold annotation: Causal keyword sets and CLP patterns are author-defined
   based on textbook sources. While we followed a systematic annotation process,
   broader crowdsourced annotation or expert panel review would strengthen the gold
   standard’s validity.
3. Model selection: We evaluate three open LLMs via the Groq API. Closed-source
   models (GPT-4, Claude) are not included due to API cost constraints and the goal
   of evaluating reproducible, open-weight models. Results may differ for larger or
   more capable models.
4. Modality: CausalReasonBench tests causal reasoning in text. Causal reasoning
   from data, images, graphs, or structured tables is out of scope. Real-world causal
   reasoning often requires integrating evidence from multiple modalities.
5. Zero-shot evaluation: All evaluations are zero-shot. Few-shot evaluation with
   causal demonstrations might significantly alter the relative performance of strate-
   gies and models.
6. Scenario coverage: Twelve scenarios provide systematic coverage but cannot rep-
   resent the full diversity of causal reasoning challenges in each domain. Additional
   scenarios, particularly in under-represented sub-domains, would improve coverage.

8.9 Threats to Validity
Internal validity.
The primary threat to internal validity is the potential for data contamination: if
specific adversarial scenarios (e.g., the ice cream-drowning example) appear in model
training data, performance on these scenarios may not reflect genuine causal reason-
ing. We mitigate this by including novel adversarial scenarios alongside well-known
examples and by testing with multiple models trained on different data.

External validity.
Our results are limited to three model families and may not generalise to all LLMs. The
Groq API’s inference characteristics (e.g., potential differences in precision compared
to local inference) could also affect results, though temperature-0 generation should
minimise stochastic effects.

Construct validity.
The CAR composite score weights three metrics with fixed coefficients determined by
a pilot study. Different weighting schemes could lead to different conclusions about
which prompting strategy is best. We report individual metric scores alongside CAR
to enable alternative aggregation.
