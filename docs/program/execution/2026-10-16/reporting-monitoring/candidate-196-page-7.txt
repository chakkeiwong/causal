 2) Contract-centred taxonomy We develop a corpus-grounded taxonomy for agentic NetOps and
    AIOps, organised around autonomy rungs, tool surfaces, evidence obligations, safety gates, rollout
    constraints, and audit requirements.
 3) Architectural patterns and guardrails We extract recurring design patterns from recent systems,
    including retrieval and evidence management [12], tool-use loops [13], [14], and operational assis-
    tants for monitoring, query recommendation, incident analysis, and network troubleshooting [7], [8],
    [9], [10]. We map these patterns to guardrails such as verification, privilege minimisation, staging,
    and human approval.
