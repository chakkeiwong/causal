## Challenges and repairs

**Scientific meaning versus numeric success.** The original public defect was
observed by actual calls, repaired in the raw result and both consumers, and
tested against archived code. An AST comparison removes only the new metadata
assignments and confirms that the legacy numerical kernel is unchanged. Real
regular and projected executions also have identical numeric fields. Bounded,
disconnected, whole-line, empty and unavailable outputs retain their meaning.
The new explicit-method request prevents a result-dependent procedure choice;
the old default is preserved only as a documented migration path.

**Information at singularity.** C02 cannot be described as extracting precision
from correctness counts alone. Its audit reveals the persistent map and actual
linked outcome history. The pair-level inverse-inclusion identity identifies
the focal moment under that additional law. Conditional expectation is checked
by enumerating audit selection and label flips. Repeated dates are never counted
as independent map labels. Correctly supplied noisy-label correction has an
identification premise; an estimated unknown error rate would need another
influence contribution. This qualification must remain in public reports.

**Projection tails and rank.** Ellipsoid support is analytic at fixed ranks;
convexity/concavity over reciprocal-rank rectangles supplies a global enclosure.
The implementation conservatively returns the whole line when an audit interval
touches zero rank or when the four-dimensional response pivot lacks qualified
rank. It does not silently use a local optimum or arbitrary pseudoinverse.
Independent primal feasibility checks retain all sampled feasible values. The
rectangle challenger preserves some disconnected cases that the ellipsoid
fallback deliberately enlarges. This is not uniform dominance or sharp inference.
Floating-point beta/chi-square evaluation is not a proved numerical enclosure.

**Coverage diagnostics.** Retained strong-design regular coverage is .925;
known audit probabilities give .930 and an oracle linearization .9275. This
shows that estimated audit probabilities alone do not explain undercoverage.
The separate fresh Gaussian primitive experiment has known covariance and
strong-design regular coverage .960. These experiments do not uniquely identify
the cause of the original calendar failure and do not validate its HAC
approximation by analogy. The retrospective comparison is explicitly labelled;
unchanged old draws are not repackaged as fresh confirmation.

**Nonlinear identification and numerics.** Accurate representative complete
state-labelled audits anchor state scale and identify the full-rank regressions,
initial law and clipped logistic logger. Those states are assumed information,
not recovered truth manufactured by the filter. The observed likelihood keeps
the private logger. The policy uses only current observed signal and date;
audited current states and future records are rejected. The exact Gaussian
reduction removes both transition nonlinearity and private-state logging.
Likelihood, score and value are compared separately. Automatic differentiation
is audited as differentiation of the discrete objective; particle finite
differences remain an uncertified Monte Carlo challenger.

**Unavailable nonlinear intervals.** The original final comparison's 45/100
baseline boundary fits and 25/25 misspecified boundary fits are retained. A
fresh registered stable-domain comparison avoids treating an unnecessarily
narrow rectangle as the scientific stability condition. With 160 audits its
stable procedure is available 99/100, covering 94/100 requests; with 640 audits
it is available 100/100 and covers 95/100. The latter MC95 is [.8872,.9836], not
evidence for exact or uniform nominal calibration. The 40 original-domain
bootstrap refits have only 18 available results; they do not supply a qualified
bootstrap interval. The recipe never selects the procedure with the shortest
observed interval. Audit bias accidentally covering in a particular design
does not establish audit truth or robustness.

**Graph selection versus model-class inclusion.** All 40 broad structural
descriptions are retained conservatively. A constructed positive-noise latent
model reproduces the same covariance at any specified finite action effect;
the proof, not a finite effect grid, establishes ambiguity. The valid
encouragement arm declares randomization and exclusion as new information.
Its equation is projected through a simultaneous covariance region and all
allowed graph descriptions. The sufficient-graph interval relaxation may be
very loose; that is numerical conservatism, not population nonidentification.
There is no claim that an observational search learned the exclusion. Finite
independent Gaussian episodes cannot be relabelled as general time-series
graph confidence.

**Dependent learning and physical execution.** The selected public operation
preserves the orthogonal product remainder, full lag/outcome windows, supplied
mixing rate, separation, training-only tuning, nuisance conditions and within-
block score covariance. Its sparse-law coverage .935 has MC95 [.8914,.9649];
the model is conditional and finite-sample behavior remains approximate. An
omitted common interaction gives zero fitted-learner coverage while an oracle
remains near nominal. Passing a learner object cannot establish its rates.
Source materialization has explicit received/decision/outcome clocks. Recovery
checks use a separately computed dense covariance and reject changed data,
procedures and corrupt checkpoint content. Memory-mapped input does not prevent
the nuisance fit from allocating matrices; the manuscript states this limit.

**Fair workload and product claims.** The first timing trial was invalidated
by an incorrect direct-kernel call and unequal public output obligations; it
is preserved and excluded. The repaired suite executes 32 fresh-process
complete workloads with matched sources and byte-identical public outputs,
including two repeats at each size/method. The shared operations are broadly
at computational parity with direct expert composition. Streamed score
checkpointing adds cost: at 12,000 dates its complete runs take about .40–.41 s
versus .25–.26 s for the dense direct route on this host. Cold nonlinear runs
include JIT compilation and use about 0.77–1.15 GiB peak process memory.
These small measured cases do not establish bank-scale throughput, speedup,
analyst savings or demand. The runnable multi-host protocol is a next-step
artifact; its execution is not claimed.

## Comparison with the required standard

The preserved FinMarketing estimator/remainder example requires the actual
identity and its remainder to be explained; it rules out calling learner
plug-ins automatically qualified. The added pair identity and blocked IV
operation meet that requirement at explicit information and rate conditions.
The banking accounts define the action normalization and fixed monetary scale,
including why repeated dates and actual labelled outcomes change information.

The preserved BayesFilter score and derivative contract requires initial,
observation, assignment and transition dependence to be differentiated together.
The new nonlinear likelihood and its tangent recursion explain that dependence,
verify all 15 directions, and compare value as well as likelihood. Its finite
policy subclass and local/numerical limits remain visible. It is not an inferred
integration into BayesFilter.

The CIP method-choice example requires substantive challengers answering the
same target and clear reasons to choose one route. Here the rectangle/ellipsoid,
audit/outcome information, quadrature/Gaussian/particle, broad-latent/valid-IV,
sparse/fixed-basis/oracle and direct/checkpointed alternatives have distinct
restrictions and losing cases. Runtime and institutional economics remain
different evidence classes.

Against the seven acceptance questions: the estimators are reconstructible;
financial units and information clocks are explicit; the nonlinear, temporal
and dependent-learning routes are central scientific constructions; primary
method comparisons and adverse evidence are preserved; new eligible requests
and meaningful refusals execute; complete costs compare equal public obligations;
and all changed claims have source or derivation/execution traces. This supports
author completion of this bounded extension. It does not warrant treating the
full ambitious product as uniformly supported.
