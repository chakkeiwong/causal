## Interference investments require a named population and policy

Borrower-network outreach and branch-allocation interventions need different
scientific contracts. The pinned MossSpider 0.0.3 network implementation and
inferference 1.0.3 grouped implementation are useful references. Equal-group
and equal-person averages, graph direction, eligibility and the treatment of
excluded neighbors cannot be substituted silently. Both packages remain
reference choices; an original-language worker requires a later qualified
public prototype. [@exec24_interference_mossspider; @exec24_interference_inferference]

Eighteen authentic implementation, identity, notice and test files support the
comparison. Exact authored examples distinguish a USD 50 equal-group outcome
from USD 20 per person, and a correctly normalized policy ratio of 1/4 from
1/64 when policy mass is included twice. Selected implementation paths motivate
checks for endpoint refusal, allocation flags and derivatives, missing-group
denominators, negative variance and singular score matrices. These are static
findings and mathematical fixtures, not executed package benchmarks.
[@exec24_interference_reference]

Investment should first fund a bounded public prototype with independently
specified targets, exact direction and allocation oracles, complete failure
accounting and a justified uncertainty regime. Rights review must cover the
actual installed dependencies, beyond the selected MIT notice and GPL
declaration. Scientific, adapter, platform and evaluation owners have separate
obligations. A stipulated USD 2,400 across 100 submissions but 60 usable outputs
means USD 24 per submission and USD 40 per usable result; those illustrative
denominators do not establish observed costs or bank value.
