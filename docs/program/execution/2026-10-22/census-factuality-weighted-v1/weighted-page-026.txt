5.2     Baseline and Alternative Weights
The researcher constructs a vector of baseline weights ŵn ∈ W and a nonempty, compact, and
convex class of alternative weights Λ̂n ⊆ W. The population analogues are (w(Pn ),Λ(Pn )). The
baseline and alternative estimators are τ̂ŵn ,n = ŵn′ θ̂n and τ̂λ,n = λ′ θ̂n , where λ ∈ Λ̂n . The baseline
and alternative estimands are τwn (Pn ) = w(Pn )′ θ(Pn ) and τλ (Pn ) = λ′ θ(Pn ), where λ ∈ Λ(Pn ).
If the baseline and alternative weights are known, then I define ŵn = w(Pn ) and Λ̂n = Λ(Pn ).
    I assume that the sample baseline weights ŵn are uniformly consistent for the population
baseline weights w(Pn ). Moreover, I assume that w(Pn ) is uniformly bounded—i.e., the baseline
weighting scheme cannot place arbitrarily large negative weight on any group.
