# Author review of the integrated master program

11 October 2026. This is a review of the program's scope, executability and
evidence controls. It is not independent scientific acceptance of the product,
nor a finding that the unresolved mathematical questions have been solved.

## Findings and repairs

1. **The canonical program was stale.** Mission Control/state still stopped at
   V10, while three later deliveries added 14 pages and new information-design
   evidence. The new canonical master, state, CLI and README reconcile all four
   deliveries, point to the 259-page reader, and identify the sponsor case as
   the unchanged V10 baseline. Historical evidence is linked rather than
   repeatedly pasted into the current next-action list.
2. **Repeated author completion hid capability debt.** The new state separates
   package outcomes, per-gap capability dispositions, external evidence and
   the final sponsor decision. Review found that the initial new tracker could
   still have inferred gap closure from passing packages. That was repaired:
   each required predecessor must explicitly qualify the gap in its declared
   scope. Conditional progress is insufficient; negative/inconclusive work
   cannot qualify a gap. F01 rejects unresolved predecessor qualifications.
3. **Funding had crowded out the original scope.** The six streams and four
   banking journeys now have explicit contracts. R03/R04/R05 restore nonlinear
   servicing, learned temporal effects and learner-specific inference. D01-D10
   each map to executable tasks and retained generalization limits. A selected
   family is an explicit bounded construction, not closure of an unrestricted
   research ambition.
4. **A global source check damaged a frozen receipt.** Two independent
   supplements both use eq:score, legitimately. The old checker treated the
   repository as one document and wrote its failure over V10's receipt. The
   failed bytes are retained. The original 244 bytes were reconstructed only
   after matching the pre-existing manifest digest exactly. Seven mutable
   control files were preserved; research PDFs and original manifests were
   not changed. All four unchanged nested verifiers pass through the overlay,
   covering 7,609 unique artifacts. No new historical scientific result was
   manufactured by this repair.
5. **The new checks need the right scope.** Source checking now follows input
   closures of five current compiled documents and finds 159 active cited
   works among 188 registered sources. Labels in separate documents cannot
   supply each other's references; duplicate labels within a document still
   fail. Withdrawn roots do not inflate active citation counts. Status and
   ordinary checks are read-only; full historical replay is a separate command.
6. **An executable plan must admit failure and safe revision.** Work cards have
   dependencies, acceptance criteria, concrete outputs and failure actions.
   I01 accepts reviewed negative/inconclusive branches so the monograph can
   report them; F01 requires positive qualified outcomes. Protocols freeze
   before fresh draws. Failed attempts retain contracts, registrations,
   evidence and versioned successor outputs. A resolved downstream conclusion
   prevents an unreviewed reopen beneath it. Other ready branches continue.
7. **Evidence paths alone were too weak.** Passing receipts now need bound
   promised outputs, complete criterion findings and task-contract identities.
   Changed evidence or acceptance rules invalidate receipts. Research pass
   additionally requires the registered protocol, later evaluation start,
   complete bound journal and retained failures. These controls do not prove
   a theorem or independently certify timestamps; the substantive review and
   preserved evidence remain necessary.
8. **Calibration criteria must not be selected for convenience.** Exact-rank
   559/600 coverage is neither general calibration proof nor a demonstrated
   universal defect. The protocol distinguishes scientific guarantees,
   simultaneous finite-grid evidence, conditional/asymptotic claims,
   availability, width and acquisition cost. A nominal level falling inside
   a Monte Carlo interval is not a passing minimum-coverage test. A strict
   lower-bound screen may itself be inconclusive at exact nominal coverage;
   that is reported honestly. No bank width threshold or tolerance is invented.
9. **External work had to be actionable without fabricated participants.**
   Seven owner/input groups have explicit evidence and rejection contracts.
   Intake requires identified external provenance and signed evidence; final
   acceptance is tied to the integrated release. An author receipt cannot
   satisfy an external gate, and X01 specifically requires independence.
   Hash/shape checks do not establish signature authenticity or audit truth;
   the responsible owner must substantiate those facts. No contact was made.
10. **Execution and delivery needed bounded tooling.** Verification runs with
   short tool returns and durable receipts; expensive historical identity
   replay is kept distinct from ordinary checks. The master PDF combines the
   argument, capability/evaluation/input contracts and machine-generated work
   cards. The first build exposed two overfull table phrases; they were
   rewritten for line breaking. Initial rendered review also found sparse
   spill pages between the component documents; removing forced page breaks
   lets the connected program flow continuously. Final rendered inspection
   follows that repair.

## Verification and disposition

The behavior tests exercise dependency cycles/unknown predecessors, missing
gaps, stale evidence/contracts, incomplete outputs, failed prerequisites,
late registration, false external completion, separate label namespaces,
reopening and atomic state conflicts. The test receipt records the exact
suite, result and input identities. The independent historical verifier
replay and the active source check pass. Final build and actual visual evidence
are bound separately in the program delivery receipt.

The preparation wave is executable and suitable for continuation after these
repairs. No remaining blocking defect in the reviewed program design was
identified. This judgment is limited to the declared program and tested
controls; it does not independently endorse its research results.

R01 is the first scientific task: derive a specific correction or changed
independent information/calendar design before registering another final
funding experiment. R02-R05 and E01 remain separate ready branches after M03.
The routes for several research questions are not established solutions, and
the external inputs remain absent. The full bank product is not complete.
