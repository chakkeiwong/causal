## Capability disposition

The [journal reconstruction](weak-audit-sharpening-verification.json) checks
3,600 distinct fresh requests, both sets of response bands and audit intervals,
the projections, all-request coverage and paired finite widths. In the 2,937
requests where both sets were finite, the transformed set was narrower in all
2,937. This selected-law paired finding is useful evidence for the covariance
transform, not a theorem or a decision-value result. Baseline conditional
finite median width fell from USD 1.229 million to USD 1.096 million; the
persistent design fell from USD 3.387 million to USD 2.889 million. The
selected valid-law cells all showed 600/600 coverage for both procedures, with
six-cell simultaneous Monte Carlo intervals saved in the results. Such
synthetic counts do not establish calibration under arbitrary banks or laws.

At the original near-rank size, both procedures returned 558/600 whole-line
sets. Their 42 paired finite sets had conditional median widths about USD
52.0 million and USD 16.2 million. With eight times as many representative
audit labels per stratum, 105/600 remained whole-line; the 495 finite sets
had respective medians about USD 47.8 million and USD 15.4 million. The extra
information improves finite availability in this selected design, but is
unpriced and still far from an established bank decision threshold. A whole-line
set can cover the target without providing useful precision.

Under the deliberately false exogeneity law, candidate and transformed
coverage was 597/600 versus 561/600. The transformed method lost coverage
in 36 paired requests and gained none. Neither fraction validates a false
causal premise. The checked observational-equivalence witness produces the
same observed histories under a class allowing an adjusted nuisance mean,
while one-unit intervention responses and the financial target differ by about
USD 387,575. This is a scoped identification counterexample, not an
impossibility result under every scientific restriction. Assignment truth,
randomization, a valid instrument or another justified restriction would be
new information; narrower interval algebra alone is insufficient.
