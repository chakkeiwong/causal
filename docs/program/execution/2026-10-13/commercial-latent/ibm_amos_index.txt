Overview
IBM SPSS Amos structural equation modeling
IBM® SPSS® Amos is structural equation modeling (SEM) software that extends regression, factor, and variance analysis. It enables intuitive or programmatic modeling of complex relationships. Included in SPSS Statistics Premium, and available to buy as part of Base, Standard and Professional editions, or as standalone; Windows only.
Features
Feature Spotlights
Structural Equation Modeling / Path Analysis
Model complex relationships with structural equation modeling (SEM) and path analysis. SPSS Amos enables you to analyze direct and indirect effects between variables, providing a comprehensive, theory-driven view of causal relationships and improving the accuracy of your insights.
Confirmatory Factor Analysis
Validate theoretical constructs using confirmatory factor analysis (CFA). SPSS Amos helps you test how well observed variables represent underlying latent factors, ensuring your measurement models are statistically robust and aligned with your research hypotheses.
Bayesian Estimation
Enhance model accuracy with Bayesian estimation by incorporating prior knowledge into your analysis. SPSS Amos supports probabilistic inference, enabling more flexible modeling and stronger results, especially when working with complex data or limited sample sizes.
Estimation of Categorical and Censored Data
Work effectively with real-world data using support for categorical and censored variables. SPSS Amos enables more accurate modeling of non-continuous or partially observed data, ensuring your analyses reflect practical conditions and deliver more reliable outcomes.
Latent Class Analysis
Uncover hidden subgroups within your data using latent class analysis. SPSS Amos identifies distinct segments based on patterns in observed variables, helping you better understand behaviors, preferences, and population differences for more targeted analysis.
Spreadsheet-Like Model Input
Build models quickly with a spreadsheet-like interface that requires no programming. SPSS Amos simplifies model creation by allowing users to define variables and relationships in a familiar tabular format, making advanced modeling more accessible and efficient.
