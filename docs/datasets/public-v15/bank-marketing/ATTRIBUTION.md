Moro, S., Rita, P., and Cortez, P. (2014). *Bank Marketing* [Dataset].
UCI Machine Learning Repository. DOI: https://doi.org/10.24432/C5K306.

Custodian: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/222/bank+marketing

The custodian page inspected on 12 October 2026 states the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The saved custodian page and exact SHA-256 are registered in
`docs/program/public-data-v15/source-audit.json`.

The six CSV/text members here are extracted without byte changes from the
downloaded public archive. Original archive bytes are preserved at
`docs/literature/raw/pd15_uci_bank_archive.txt` (a ZIP file saved with the
collector's allowed binary suffix). Descriptive derived counts are in
`docs/program/public-data-v15/bank-profile.json`; no fitted predictions or
causal effects are added to the data.
