\section{Tigramite as a temporal reference: preserving the meaning of the result}
\label{exec23:tigramite-scope}
A bank comparing deposit rates and subsequent balances needs to distinguish a conditional-dependence statistic, an unresolved endpoint, a fitted intervention response and uncertainty about that response. Tigramite offers explicitly differentiated methods for these stages. The selected January 2026 source declares version 5.2.10.1. PCMCI, PCMCI+, LPCMCI, RPCMCI and J-PCMCI+ differ in contemporaneous-link, hidden-variable, stationarity and context assumptions. The conditional-independence test adds its own restrictions: ParCorr uses linear residual correlation, whereas other tests require their own treatment. These are distinct scientific choices within one package, not interchangeable routes to a monetary effect. \cite{exec23_tigramite_reuse_source,exec14_pt_tigramite}

The proposal selects Tigramite as a method-specific reference, with a conditional route to an isolated Python worker. Prior installed-version operations remain relevant within their recorded scope, including the finite-DAG oracle comparison. A matching version string does not establish byte identity with the selected repository tree, and those operations do not qualify every delegate considered here. \cite{runtime_tigramite_scope,runtime_networkx_dynamic} The additional evidence below is static source inspection and independent exact examples, not another native trial.

\subsection{A statistic, a graph and a dollar response}
\label{exec23:tigramite-statistic}
Let $X_t$ denote a centered rate intervention in specified rate units, $Y_{t+1}$ a balance response in dollars and $Z_t$ the chosen history. Consider a stipulated linear population,
\begin{equation}
 Y_{t+1}=100+20X_t+\gamma^\top Z_t+\epsilon_{t+1},\qquad
 \operatorname{Var}(X_t\mid Z_t)=1,\quad
 \operatorname{Var}(\epsilon_{t+1}\mid Z_t)=225.
 \label{exec23:tigramite-dollar}
\end{equation}
Assume independent residualized exposure and error, an intervention-invariant mechanism, a history sufficient for adjustment, and intervention support. The residual covariance is $20$ and the residual variance of $Y$ is $625$. Thus the partial correlation is $20/25=4/5$, whereas the stipulated causal slope is 20 dollars per rate unit. A one-unit change with stipulated cost 12 dollars has net value 8 dollars. Reading $4/5$ as a dollar gain instead gives $-56/5$ dollars. These are authored population values, not observed bank returns; their disagreement demonstrates the units lost in an invalid conversion. \cite{exec23_tigramite_reference}

PCMCI labels its \texttt{val\_matrix} as test-statistic values and its optional \texttt{conf\_matrix} as intervals for those values. ParCorr fits ordinary least-squares residuals and returns their Pearson correlation. Its analytic test uses $T-d$ degrees of freedom for $d$ array rows and $T$ usable observations, returning a nonfinite p-value when degrees of freedom are insufficient. The CI base also supplies a constant-variable shortcut. An adapter must report degeneracy and invalid arithmetic rather than interpret every returned entry as scientific evidence of absence. The \texttt{run\_pcmci} signature defaults FDR to \texttt{none}, although the parameter prose says \texttt{fdr\_bh}; a profile must set this argument explicitly. The correction helper distinguishes lagged-only and contemporaneous-inclusive test families. A corrected edge p-value is not a portfolio-response interval. \cite{exec23_tigramite_reuse_source}

\subsection{Endpoint ambiguity is a substantive output}
\label{exec23:tigramite-graph}
PCMCI+ can return contemporaneous ambiguity and conflict marks; LPCMCI returns a temporal partial ancestral graph. The selected effect class accepts DAG and ADMG variants, including temporal and stationary forms, but does not enable its commented-out PAG graph types. A wrapper cannot bridge these interfaces by replacing circles or conflicts with arbitrary arrows. It must retain the compatible graph family, justify additional scientific restrictions where possible, and determine identification or set identification under those restrictions. A graph-conditioned adjustment fit does not solve that preceding problem. \cite{exec23_tigramite_reuse_source}

Even a summary frequency requires its exact definition. The default graph-summary routine chooses a modal endpoint string but, in a tie, reports the sum of tied modal counts divided by the resample count. Five resamples with one direction and five with the reverse can produce a conflict mark with reported frequency one, although none of the ten inputs contains that conflict mark. In a second stipulated ensemble, four absent links and three of each direction make absence the most frequent individual string, while adjacency has total frequency $6/10$. The alternative adjacency-first aggregation answers the latter question. These finite examples do not establish discovery failure; they show why the counts, aggregation rule and emitted mark must accompany the summary. Such frequency is neither the posterior probability of an arrow nor a calibrated graph confidence level. \cite{exec23_tigramite_reuse_source,exec23_tigramite_reference}

\subsection{An intervention prediction still needs a contrast}
\label{exec23:tigramite-intervention}
For a supplied admissible graph and valid adjustment set $Z$, the unconditional adjusted response is
\begin{equation}
 \mu(x)=\int E[Y\mid X=x,Z=z]p(z)\,dz,\qquad
 \Delta(x,x_0)=\mu(x)-\mu(x_0).
 \label{exec23:tigramite-contrast}
\end{equation}
This requires identifying graph and sampling assumptions; fitting regression does not supply them. The inspected prediction delegate aggregates predictions over stored adjustment observations. For an illustrative zero-mean adjusted history in the linear population, predictions at zero and one are 100 and 120 dollars; the contrast is 20 dollars. Calling 120 the gain includes the baseline. A caller must preserve that baseline, adjustment distribution, units, transforms and conditioning target. The effect class checks adjustment validity by default but exposes a bypass for a user-supplied set. An accepted product profile should not silently enable that bypass. \cite{exec23_tigramite_reuse_source,exec23_tigramite_reference}

The hard intervention sets an exposure value; the source's soft option adds an amount to each observed exposure. For nonlinear responses these policies differ. If $Y=X^2$ and the stipulated reference population places equal mass at zero and two, the hard zero-to-one contrast is $1$, whereas adding one to everyone's observed exposure gives
\begin{equation}
 E[(X+1)^2-X^2]=2E[X]+1=3.
 \label{exec23:tigramite-policy}
\end{equation}
Here soft denotes that additive implementation, not every stochastic intervention in causal inference. Conditional nested regression and alternative estimators need separate review. \cite{exec23_tigramite_reuse_source,exec23_tigramite_reference}

The effect bootstrap refits the selected effect procedure and takes percentiles of intervention predictions, retaining the supplied graph. Discovery bootstrap summaries instead concern graphs and statistics. Neither bootstrap code nor a nonempty interval establishes joint validity after graph discovery, graph or policy selection, or under arbitrary dependence. Specify the uncertainty target and sampling law before choosing which stages to resample. \cite{exec23_tigramite_reuse_source}

\subsection{Calendars, masks and resampling belong in the scientific contract}
\label{exec23:tigramite-calendar}
The data layer constructs lagged arrays from integer reference positions, dataset offsets and a cutoff. Its ordinary twice-maximum-lag cutoff and the effect fitter's maximum-lag cutoff can retain different observations. Masks apply to selected variable roles, separately from missing-value handling. Calendar gaps, reporting delays and multiple panels require explicit time-grid conversion and effective-sample records. The earlier inspected RPCMCI implementation replaces a supplied mask, requiring a distinct admission rule. \cite{exec23_tigramite_reuse_source}

Several static edge cases warrant native oracle tests. The selected data-layer bootstrap draws starts from \texttt{arange(n-L)} for $n$ retained positions and block length $L$. For $n=6,L=2$, these are $0,1,2,3$; the complete nonwrapping set includes start 4. Position 5 is absent from the first expression's reachable indices. This enumeration describes the inspected expression, not an executed failure or quantified inference bias. Nonconsecutive retained positions also need not be adjacent calendar times. \cite{exec23_tigramite_reuse_source,exec23_tigramite_reference}

The missing-value extension constructs later position indices without clipping before assignment. Missing position 5 in a six-position array, extended through lag two, yields $5,6,7$; the last two exceed the bounds. The source writes the used-indices attribute after its dataset loop while concatenating samples from all datasets. A multi-panel adapter must verify complete panel provenance rather than assume this attribute records every panel. These observations motivate narrow replay cases and separately reviewed patches; they do not establish that all package analyses fail. \cite{exec23_tigramite_reuse_source,exec23_tigramite_reference}

\subsection{The reuse choice and the work needed to change it}
\label{exec23:tigramite-choice}
Whole-package adoption is premature because method assumptions, graph conversion, uncertainty and these edge cases require distinct contracts. Porting to R, Java or C++ is not justified by a measured benefit here: orientation rules, tests and resampling would acquire a second maintenance burden. A selected Python worker is the candidate to test, preserving endpoints and counts, effective observations and transforms, warnings and failures, and refusing unsupported graph-to-effect requests. These requirements describe proposed capabilities.

The repository declares GPL version 3 or later and contains GPLv3 text. Setup specifies minimum dependencies rather than a locked environment; optional algorithms add packages. This records licensing evidence, not clearance of a future distribution. Process isolation alone does not settle rights. A maintainer must bind artifacts and versions; a scientific owner must choose the sampling law, target and tests; an operator must address custody, failure budgets and distribution. Comparison should include installation, search, ambiguous and failed queries, resampling, serialization, upgrade regression and human review. Reconsider integration when a permissible pinned profile passes the exact oracles and registered public or synthetic trial. Later bank experiments should be justified by prototype merit rather than required to finish this proposal decision. \cite{exec23_tigramite_reuse_source,exec23_tigramite_reference}
