56.1     An execution identity is larger than a request name
A durable analysis must bind the canonical request, scientific context, input bytes, code,
schema, numerical environment, seed and budget. A repeated job name alone cannot establish
that a result is still valid. The implemented runtime records these identities and rejects
idempotency collisions involving changed requests or execution identities. (Causal banking
research project, 2026bf)
   Artifacts use canonical JSON, content and descriptor hashes, opaque identities and bounded
local reads. Regular-file and nonsymlink checks reduce accidental path redirection. A content
hash establishes byte identity under the relevant assumption about the hash function; it is
not an authenticated endorsement of the input’s scientific accuracy. A local operator can still
supply a false declaration whose bytes remain perfectly stable.
   The scientific request contains no arbitrary shell command, Python import or network
endpoint. Fixed worker entry points execute the supported operations. This makes the scope
of the local tool explicit and allows a review grant to refer to actual operations. The current
controller’s trusted caller object is not a substitute for an institutional identity and approval
service.


56.2     Budgets, durable jobs and cancellation
Scientific workers have wall-time and address-space limits. The memory bound applies to the
worker, not to all memory used by the resident SDK and coordinator. A complete operating
measurement must include both. Small control or error envelopes may exceed an extremely
small requested output limit to explain why the request cannot be fulfilled. These are disclosed
boundaries of the present implementation. (Causal banking research project, 2026bf)
   Report jobs retain a hashed prefix of completed work identities. The local retry rule is
bounded to three attempts under the original deadline. Publication is atomic, and replay
checks request, artifact and checkpoint identities before continuation. The recorded restart
fixture kills a report worker after its first checkpoint and completes all one hundred distinct
work identities once. This is evidence for that local workflow, not a distributed exactly-once
guarantee for arbitrary external side effects.

56.3     Fault evidence and what remains unmeasured
Nine recorded fault cases cover replay with conflicting budgets, wall timeout, output limits,
worker memory, kill/restart with checkpoint, cooperative cancellation, changed artifact bytes,
changed scientific context and changed checkpoints. (Causal banking research project, 2026bf)
The tests distinguish preserving a result from merely restarting a process. An interrupted
computation that silently uses new inputs is a different analysis even if it eventually finishes.
  These fixtures do not measure institution-scale availability, multi-host recovery, adversarial
tenant isolation or a service-level commitment. They also do not show that streaming always
improves elapsed time. The earlier complete local workload comparisons retain small or absent
gains; engineering choices must be justified by the intended workload rather than by the
existence of a streaming interface.
   Operational qualification should measure the complete request: artifact import, scientific
work, uncertainty, serialization, reporting and recovery. A benchmark of one reducer omits
fitting and coordination costs. The comparison needs matched inputs, numerical tolerance
and an explicit accounting of unsuccessful requests, just as a scientific comparison needs a
common target.


56.4     Compatibility is a maintained scientific obligation
A new backend release can change defaults, graph encodings, estimator targets, uncertainty or
failure behavior. A compatible JSON schema alone does not prove scientific compatibility. The
proposed adapter register should bind a version, accepted inputs, method identity, preserved
result types and matched adverse fixtures. A material semantic change requires a reviewed
successor rather than relabeling an old qualification.
   Transport migration likewise needs real client/server tests. The current legacy MCP choice
is useful for its tested environment. Supporting a modern protocol or both eras requires a new
compatibility argument, discovery behavior and failure tests. It should not be advertised by
extending a version string while keeping the old handshake.
   Ownership must also remain explicit. The causal library owns its result contract and
adapters. Numerical providers own their implementations and scientific domains. Consumers
own their target interpretation and displayed decision. The bank owns actual data authority,
assignment records, operational constraints and acceptance. A report can connect those
responsibilities; it cannot replace their evidence.

56.6      Checkpoint invariants and the scope of idempotence
Suppose a report combines deterministic contributions si for a frozen ordered list of work
identities (a1 , . . . , am ). A useful checkpoint is

                             Ck = (J, (a1 , . . . , ak ), (s1 , . . . , sk ), hk ),

where hk binds the preceding contents. The required invariant is that the completed identifiers
form a prefix of the frozen request, each identifier has one committed contribution, and the
public result is a function of those committed contributions. Recomputing an uncommitted
contribution after a crash may waste work; committing it twice changes the answer.
   An exact project construction makes the distinction visible. Three intended contributions
are 100, −40, 70, so the total is 130. A crash and retry can produce the calculation trace
100, −40, −40, 70. Naively appending every returned value gives 90. A map from work identity
to its immutable contribution still gives 130, provided a repeated identity cannot carry changed
contents. These are finite arithmetic results, not measurements of a distributed service (Causal
banking proposal project, 2026m).

56.8      Resource budgets and cancellation at the right boundary
The local worker sets an address-space limit and a wall-time timer. The coordinator starts a
fixed worker entry point, collects its output and validates the canonical response against the
requested output size. This supports a bounded set of local operations. It does not impose
one aggregate memory bound on the worker, coordinator, SDK and all clients, and the output
check is applied after collection rather than as an incremental bound on an arbitrary producer’s
stream (Causal banking proposal project, 2026o).
   For a proposed third-party numerical adapter, admission should therefore include peak
resident memory of the whole workflow, temporary disk use, serialization and parent-process
buffers. A limit that is safe for a short report may be inappropriate for an estimator returning
a large covariance matrix. If a result exceeds the public response size, the design can publish
a separately bounded artifact with explicit custody and expose a small reference. It must
retain the scientific object’s meaning and cannot silently truncate interval components or omit
unsuccessful fits to meet a size limit.
  Cancellation is a race between a request and the computation’s next state transition. For
pure report generation, a useful rule is that an accepted cancellation recorded before the
publication transition prevents a new public result; cancellation after publication returns
the actual completed state. The inspected implementation checks a cancellation flag around
report work and before publication. That cooperative behavior does not guarantee that every
numerical kernel can be interrupted at an arbitrary instruction. A later adapter may need
worker termination and a rule for discarding uncommitted artifacts.
  A timeout should be recorded as an execution outcome. A scientifically unidentified effect
can instead be a completed result with an explicit scientific limitation. Converting both into
one generic failure statistic obscures the remedy: the first may need resource or implementation
work, while the second may need a different target, new information or abstention. A successful
process exit likewise says nothing by itself about whether the effect is precise enough to support
a decision.

56.9     Ownership and a reversible adoption path
Operational ownership follows the claim being made. A method maintainer owns the supported
algorithm and numerical domain. The common-library owner owns the request/result contract,
method-version binding and admitted conversions. The consumer owns the displayed target,
units and business action. A later bank study additionally needs a data owner for the actual
observation and assignment processes and a decision owner for losses and feasible actions.
These are proposed responsibilities, not names or approvals that have already been supplied.
   For example, a deposit report may use a valid backend estimate while a consumer interprets
the coefficient as currency rather than thousands of currency units. The remedy belongs partly
in the shared unit contract and partly in the consuming application’s acceptance examples. A
central numerical team cannot certify a silently altered presentation. Conversely, an application
owner cannot declare a confidence procedure valid merely because the resulting interval is
displayed correctly.
   The proposal should make an adoption decision reversible at its natural boundaries. Retain
the incumbent expert workflow and its artifacts while a new adapter is evaluated on matched
public/generated requests. Publish both methods’ complete outputs with their own identities;
compare discrepancies before replacing the default. A schema migration should have a declared
mapping and refusal cases for information it cannot preserve. Where a new method changes
the estimand, the migration is a new scientific analysis, not a formatting conversion. A rollback
retains access to the previously admitted version and evidence without rewriting its result as
if produced by the new version.
   The same approach applies to workspace consumers. Read-only inspection of a repository
can reveal candidate objects and interfaces. It cannot establish that its owner accepts the
proposed contract, that its launch environment is compatible, or that an integration saves
work. A local mock consumer can test the proposed request and response boundary now.
Actual consumer execution, deployment ownership and institutional operation remain distinct
later claims, with their own narrowly defined studies.

56.11      The next complete qualification
The remaining engineering route is concrete: choose source-audited packages and permitted

across all three surfaces. The trial must include unsupported methods, late observations, graph

56.12      Where a result becomes public after interruption
A recovery specification should identify the transition at which a consumer may rely on a result.
The calculation finishing, the artifact bytes appearing, and the job becoming completed are
distinct events. In the inspected local report worker, a job lock protects the final cancellation
check, artifact publication and update of the job’s completed state and result reference. The
artifact store has its own content and metadata publications. This source structure gives a
concrete basis for reasoning about selected crash points; it does not create a transaction over
every file or an external service (Causal banking proposal project, 2026o).

means the report artifact has been stored, and T means the job record exposes a completed
state and the artifact reference. Before C, uncommitted work may need recalculation. Between
C and A, recovery can reconstruct a report from the checkpoint after revalidating identities.
Between A and T , an artifact may exist without being exposed by a completed job. After

56.12.1     Idempotence does not cross an uncooperative boundary

An external offer illustrates the limit. Suppose an adapter sends a customer message and
loses the acknowledgement before recording success. There are two worlds consistent with the
local observation: the message was delivered, or it was not. Blind retry duplicates the first
world’s action; refusing to retry omits the second world’s action. A local content hash cannot
distinguish them. A cooperating recipient that recognizes a durable action identity, or an
authoritative query that resolves delivery, can change the information available. Without such
a contract, the correct state is unresolved and a reconciliation owner is needed. The current
report service performs no such action, so this example motivates a later interface requirement
rather than alleging an observed product failure.

56.14.1    What an inexpensive complete trial should measure

A public/generated prototype trial can now be specified without pretending to have production
data. Freeze a finite mixture of supported and negative scientific requests, artifact sizes and
budget classes. Record intake, admission, queue entry, worker start, computation completion,
publication and client receipt under one clock convention. Measure peak resources for the
process tree and store, include failed parsing and serialization, and inject declared crashes
before and after checkpoint and publication. The retention trial should operate on a disposable
copy with known dependencies and verify every live reference after collection. The reference
constructions in this section make expected states auditable; they do not substitute for the
subsequent measurements.
   Compare the library, CLI and compatible MCP path under the same request definitions,
while separately reporting their unavoidable launch and transport costs. Use an incumbent
analyst or scripted workflow as a decision comparator only when its task and permitted
information match. Monetary costs should distinguish one-time construction, shared operation,
per-request compute, review, retry and recovery. Avoid counting a worker’s CPU charge again
inside an already inclusive service charge. The prior all-attempt example shows why costs from
failures and fallback decisions belong in the economic denominator (Causal banking proposal
project, 2026m).
