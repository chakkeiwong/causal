# Scientific choices after scope completion

Reviewed 9 October 2026; literature cutoff 7 October 2026. This map relates
method selection to the 14-domain [source-question matrix](source-question-matrix.md).
Exact source identities and inspected anchors resolve through [sources.json](sources.json);
new bounded searches and screening are in [scope-search-ledger.md](scope-search-ledger.md).
The preserved V4 archive retains the earlier S-phase choice map.

| Domain | Primary construction and reason | Substantive challenger or boundary |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Causal semantics | Specify an intervention on assignments/mechanisms before estimation; SCM/SWIG and potential outcomes connect the same banking invitation/receipt example. | A shock, sustained policy, edge intervention and stochastic mediator policy are distinct targets. General network equilibrium is not inferred from a graph. |
| 2. Diagram construction | Include pre-action knowledge, private logging, selection, measurement and availability. Supplied graph restrictions carry substantive provenance. | Discovery suggests an equivalence class under declared graph/test assumptions; lineage and correlations do not certify causal arrows. |
| 3. Identification | Derive adjustment, do-calculus/ID or a model-specific functional; retain failure witnesses. The executed graph-adjustment backend implements a sufficient criterion. | Failure of that backend does not prove general nonidentification. General ID/PAG implementations and executable hedge validation remain separate work. |
| 4. Randomization/adaptation | Preserve known assignment and valid monitoring experiments; evaluate the pilot-learned policy on independent trajectories. | Frozen-policy value differs from learning-algorithm value and regret. Natural logging and an adaptive experiment do not inherit one another's inferential law. |
| 5. Instruments | External-IV moments identify normalized responses; joint ratio inversion retains weak denominators. Eligibility/participation form a separate conditional LATE. | Dynamic LATE requires source-specific complier restrictions (casini2025). CUE robustness (kleibergen2021) may concern a pseudo-target after moments fail; it does not restore causal exclusion. |
| 6. Causal ML | Orthogonal influence scores, explicit remainder/rate conditions and sampling-aware splits determine learner roles. DoubleML IRM is matched numerically. | CATE, partially linear projections and ATE need different theory. Selected boosting fits and favorable coverage do not prove generic rates; dependent/high-dimensional constructions retain their own restrictions. |
| 7. Proxies/transport | Labelled measurement validation or source-specific sequential proxies add identifying information; transport requires invariance and population support. | Bridge existence/range and uniqueness/completeness are separate (zhangprox2024, wei2026); predictive representations cannot certify either. |
| 8. Longitudinal inference | Whole-trajectory cross-fitted continuation, action and censoring nuisance fits give a full-follow-up score with telescoping remainder. | Direct recursion, ignoring dropout and MNAR challengers reveal distinct failures. Irregular observed histories (ran2026) differ from a latent complete path or an intervention on visits. |
| 9. State-space connection | Retain private action likelihood when the logger sees hidden state; replace it only in the intervention recursion. | The calibrated Gaussian model gives exact likelihood/derivative/value checks. Discrete private actions generally destroy Gaussian closure; particle/quadrature extensions need their own error qualification. |
| 10. POMDP estimation | Validated full-rank emissions identify multistate marked kernels; jointly estimate validation and trajectory likelihoods. | Finite-state normal intervals undercovered; the 199-refit bootstrap was withheld. This remains an identified research fit, while the restricted Gaussian reference is a numerical benchmark. General nonlinear identification/planning is not released. |
| 11. Panels and responses | Compare same-target covariance alternatives, date-aggregated scores, and separate difference/system GMM initial-law restrictions. | Long bandwidth helps selected near-unit responses but no method fixes invalid instruments. Lag-augmented LP has a different justified target. No implemented Windmeijer correction; rank/break/heavy-tail/mixed-frequency combinations require specific models. |
| 12. Discovery | PCMCI+ under sufficient stationary temporal assumptions; LPCMCI is a latent-confounding challenger retaining its marks and cap. A bounded graph-to-fresh-data effect adapter executes. | Temporal ancestral targets differ (gerhardus2021, rohekar2023). Conditional order envelopes are not confidence sets. Unresolved PAGs/hidden-effect requests are refused by the limited adapter; background timing or an experiment may resolve a decision. |
| 13. Observation | Generate candidate clocks, keys, values and aggregation from schema profiles; acquire trusted answers to material questions. | Missing grammar interpretations and false answers remain failures. The independent linkage-audit correction propagates sampling variance; a biased audit still fails (slawski2025). No universal semantic discovery is claimed. |
| 14. Product/computation | Compose explicit query, identification expression, nuisance, score and inference operations; reuse qualified DoubleML/Tigramite functions and build cross-method semantics. | Public finance provides reproducibility with unknown truth and incomplete survey design. Twenty-two new process runs retain dimensions and actual thread limits; distributed throughput, analyst savings and accepted sibling integration remain unmeasured. |

The selected methods have different primary/challenger roles because they buy
different identifying information and impose different restrictions. The public
study uses a conditional-exchangeability ATE and an additional-assumption LATE;
it does not select identification from the data. The observed-history route is
preferred where recorded history can defend sequential exchangeability. A
validated latent model is relevant where private information has a credible
measurement experiment; a proxy route is relevant where its exclusion and
bridge assumptions are defensible. Unsupported requests retain an unresolved
scientific result instead of receiving an estimator under an unstated model.

The 44-project document survey and seven selected installed-package records
remain separate. Reuse decisions concern qualified operations, not blanket
package validity. Runtime versions, licenses, dates and tested inputs are
preserved; license/distribution choices and institutional acceptance remain
part of construction. The monograph's investment comparison still uses
explicit planning assumptions, without converting local timing into labor
savings or bank demand.
