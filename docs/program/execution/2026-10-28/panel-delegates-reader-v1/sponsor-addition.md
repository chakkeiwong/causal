## Panel adapters must preserve the question

Ten pinned implementation files now clarify the reuse plan. The selected fastdid branch uses the pre-confounding anchor from the paper's explicit appendix and intersects donor cohorts across both periods. It propagates estimated share contributions through influence functions, but can drop unsupported or failed cells and imposes specific restrictions on unbalanced panels and inference options. These are observable source contracts, not a native qualification. [@exec28_panel_delegates]

The nonabsdid wrapper preserves several useful method-specific choices but does not enforce a common sample. It changes matching and tuning defaults across backends, and its interval conversion needs explicit normal-approximation provenance. The pampe robustness helper reconstructs row positions instead of retaining supplied pre-period indices: a fit using rows 5–12 can be diagnosed on rows 1–8. The adapter must retain the actual information timeline and distinguish fixed-subset from selection-aware sensitivity. [@exec28_panel_delegates; @exec28_panel_delegate_reference]

Exact authored fixtures also show why the stacked tutorial requires a declared balanced design. Equal observation lengths make its triangular-count factors cancel; unequal lengths produce weights 2/3 and 4/3 instead of pooled-row weights 4/5 and 6/5. A numeric adoption date of 1 exposes a separate inclusion-predicate issue. These findings support specific repair and conformance tests, while package execution, dependency rights, full inference and bank acceptance remain open. [@exec28_panel_delegate_reference]
