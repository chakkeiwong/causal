# Evaluate the requested servicing policy before selecting software

The bank's target should name a fixed rule, its initial customer population,
the information available before each action, the horizon and net cash-flow
account. Evaluating a prespecified policy, evaluating a learning algorithm
that changes over time, selecting the best rule in a class, and estimating an
unknown optimum require different experiments. A static effect or a ranking
metric cannot automatically answer a sequence of action questions.
\citep{exec16_cp_matrix}

For a history Ht, logged action probability bt, target policy pi_t and reward
Rt, write V(pi)=E_pi sum_t gamma^t Rt. A full trajectory likelihood ratio is
the product of pi_t(At|Ht)/bt(At|Ht) over the relevant action times. Multiplying
the logged path law by this product replaces the action kernels; it leaves
the environmental kernels intact only under consistency, sufficient histories
for assignment, the appropriate observation law and support wherever the new
policy acts. Correctly recorded probabilities and a clock that excludes future
data are substantive premises. A large number of events cannot repair their
failure. No general hidden-state guarantee follows from a successful observed-
state calculation. The finite construction below checks this identity without
invoking an external engine. \citep{exec16_cp_exact}

## Exact examples expose three different failure modes

First, let A0 be a fair binary draw, L1=A0, and let the logger choose A1=1
with probability 9/10 after A0=0 and 1/10 after A0=1. Set terminal net reward
to 20 A0 + 100 A1 - 120 A0 A1 - 5(A0+A1) dollars. Paths 00,01,10,11 have
probabilities 1/20,9/20,9/20,1/20 and rewards 0,95,15,-10. The policy that
always takes both actions has value -10. Full weighting gives a weight of
20 on path 11 and zero elsewhere, recovering -10 exactly. Adjusting only the
history-conditional final action instead gives 42.5; using only its marginal
probability gives 84.5. Both positive answers change the first-action law and
therefore answer another question. The population weight-moment ratio is
(EW)^2/E(W^2)=1/20; that indicates concentrated information without proving
coverage or a finite effective sample size. \citep{exec16_cp_exact}

Second, changing a policy parameter's meaning changes the target before any
sampling error. With natural action probabilities e1=1/5, e2(0)=1/5 and
e2(1)=3/5, gross reward 20 A1 + 30 A2 - 10 A1 A2 and costs 8(A1+A2), the
natural value is 184/25 dollars. The inspected lmtp ipsi rule for delta=2
maps e to 1-(1-e)/2, giving probabilities 3/5,3/5,4/5 and value 456/25.
An odds tilt maps e to 2e/(1+e), giving 1/3,1/3,3/4 and value 107/9.
Enumeration and separate moment formulas agree. These are stipulated values,
not fitted-package results or measured bank benefits. The interface must name
the complete probability map, the natural proposal if used, and the history
under the already assigned past. \citep{exec22_lmtp_reuse,exec23_policy_reference}

Third, selection needs fresh evaluation. Suppose m policies each have zero
true incremental value and independent estimation error taking -4 or 4 with
equal probability. The maximum estimate has expectation 4(1-2^(1-m)): for
eight candidates it is 127/32, despite all true values being zero. Independent
mean-zero evaluation of the selected index has expectation zero. This is an
exact explanation for freezing the selected policy before final evaluation,
not a universal bias-subtraction formula. A class optimum, the chosen policy's
conditional value and the value of the learning procedure must be reported
separately. \citep{exec16_cp_exact}

## A bounded qualification route

The request must bind the action set, trajectory identity, actual logger,
observation probabilities, policy history and version, population, horizon,
discount and costs. Diagnostics should include support, realized weights,
folds, warnings, convergence and uncertainty for the requested contrast.
Preserve failures and unavailable intervals in the original denominator.
Supplying another inferred logger or hiding weight clipping is a changed
analysis that needs its own specification. Source inspections of gfoRmula,
lmtp and ltmle already motivate adversarial history, truncation, warning and
covariance fixtures. \citep{exec20_gformula_reuse,exec22_lmtp_reuse,exec23_ltmle_source}

The first public/generated comparison should use exact finite laws with
truthful loggers and separately test wrong loggers, unsupported actions,
future-data leakage, unobserved state and selective fit failure. Fit and select
policies on a training partition, then evaluate frozen policies with matched
information and costs on a fresh partition; dependent trajectories require a
justified dependence design. Compare the original engine, its proposed
adapter and the independent oracle before any speed or economic claim. Missing
vendor algorithm details, exact editions, rights and native execution remain
local obligations. Source synthesis supplies the testable proposal; it does
not qualify a general sequential service or prove that an expensive bank
experiment will succeed. \citep{exec16_cp_matrix,exec16_cp_exact}
