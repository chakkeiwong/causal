                          II. O PERATIONAL BACKGROUND : N ET O PS AND AIO PS
   Operations is best understood as a control problem carried out under uncertainty and organisational
constraint. Most incidents are not solved by one brilliant query. They are solved by repeatedly turning
partial signals into safe, reversible actions while keeping the blast radius small. That is why “helpful text”
is only a small part of the story. Key challenges include the collection of evidence, the proposal and
review of changes, and the documentation of decisions for subsequent audit and organizational learning.
Early research on Internet operations demonstrated that even minor configuration errors or policy mistakes
can result in unexpected and widespread failures [45]. As systems increase in scale and software updates
occur more frequently, the likelihood of such issues escalates.

A. A unified evidence-to-action control loop

state xt (network and service state), operators and automation observe it only through tool-mediated
observations ot (logs, metrics, traces, reachability checks), and they apply actions at (changes, mitigations)
that alter future state [1], [10], [8], [18]. A compact abstraction is:
