# Adversarial review of the V6 plan

9 October 2026. Author review before execution; not independent review.
Basis: all seven V5 findings, current latent likelihood/score implementation,
existing time-series/panel code, observation generator, discovery scoring and
the benchmark passages recorded in the V5 assessment.

## Scientific changes required by the review

1. **A larger latent sample is not a diagnosis.** Existing undercoverage can
   mix unavailable fits, biased values, underestimated uncertainty and tails.
   Decompose these first; compare at the true parameter when useful. Retain
   the existing failed bootstrap. A labelled-trajectory alternative changes
   the evidence-acquisition design and must be priced/described as such.
2. **Audited state is an observable definition, not access to metaphysical
   truth.** The underwriting/affordability state must be defined before
   outcomes, and must suffice for action and transition restrictions. Audit
   selection and misclassification require explicit checks/sensitivity. The
   plan now requires operational definitions and an adverse audit case.
3. **Learning a dynamic policy adds an information restriction.** Evaluation
   cannot condition actions on latent states used by the simulator. Freeze
   the pilot-trained signal-history tree and evaluate that tree. Exclude
   later settled reward from its information when that is the chosen clock.
4. **Independent pilot data do not automatically eliminate nuisance bias.**
   Derive the exact score remainder, its rate and the effect of covariance
   estimation; measure failures. State how independent historical regimes
   could be obtained, and why a nearby time split alone need not be independent.
5. **More branches do not resolve few common-shock dates.** Aggregate the
   influence by date before serial covariance; retain cross-horizon terms and
   compare the misleading branch/date calculation explicitly.
6. **A synthetic-control test can answer a different question.** A sharp
   assignment-null test is exact only under the declared randomization;
   observational donor/placebo ranks are not automatically p-values. Keep
   impulse, sustained rollout and aggregate financial targets distinct.
7. **Hidden-variable graph truth is not the observed structural adjacency.**
   Use a finite-window ancestral target; evaluate sound endpoint claims and
   information, not equality to a single fully oriented generating graph.
   An infinite stationary process cannot be silently truncated at its window.
8. **Link probabilities are not Bayesian evidence by declaration.** Specify
   their independent calibration and the entire candidate mapping law. Report
   set uncertainty when mapping probabilities are not identified; do not
   substitute many sampled links for more independent customers or dates.
9. **Self-generated semantic fixtures give circular comfort.** Fixtures and
   metadata conflicts must be written outside candidate-generation code;
   include a plausible wrong interpretation. Answer correctness remains an
   assumption, and failed semantic acceptance remains an observed failure.
10. **Composition must preserve the economic experiment.** Fingerprint
    observation clock, unit, action normalization, target weights, sampling,
    pilot/calibration identity and covariance configuration. Demonstrate both
    legitimate reuse and incompatible requests in actual calls.
11. **Public time series alone are not a causal study.** Investigate available
    financial data and assignment information before claiming empirical
    identification. A synthetic counterpart must have a concrete access/
    design justification and measured matched software comparison.
12. **Version searches cannot become another capped topic census.** Make a
    decision-specific primary/challenger/correction ledger and record the
    consequence of unavailable versions. Reuse inspected copies but do not
    let existing custody substitute for scientific comparison.
13. **Phase completion previously overstated scholarly sufficiency.** Final
    review must revisit the seven findings with argument and evidence, state
    residual proposal gaps separately from bank obligations, and withhold a
    sufficiency claim when any promised chain remains incomplete.

## Feasibility and sequencing

The plan is executable locally with the archived corpus and installed numeric
libraries. U01/U02 have useful independent work, but run sequentially here as
requested. Begin with decomposition of the retained latent failures and
registered funding/servicing specifications. Literature work accompanies each
choice; its final gate follows the selected methods. Integrate source and
product behavior only after those methods have a defensible statistical
experiment. Final rendered review occurs after substantive repairs.

No uniformly valid nonstationary theorem, universal POMDP estimator, complete
PAG confidence construction, bank-data access or production distributed
service is required. These exclusions do not excuse missing derivations or
experiments for the selected capabilities. The review accepts the plan for
execution subject to the thirteen controls above; it does not pre-accept its
eventual results.
