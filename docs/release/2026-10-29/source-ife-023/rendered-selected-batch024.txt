47.17.3     A paired contrast must retain its scale and sampling identities

The delegate’s arithmetic is useful once the incoming influence representation is correct. A
difference subtracts corresponding contributions; a ratio uses the numerator derivative and the
denominator derivative jointly. This retains their covariance. Before combining two objects,
the inspected code requires equal vector lengths and identical weight and ID arrays. Those
checks help, but cluster labels do not uniquely identify the individuals within a cluster. The
delivery contract must bind each influence contribution to the same original observation across
fits, as well as to its cluster and weight. Reordering records while regenerating positional IDs
can pass an array-level check while changing the pairing. Such a change alters uncertainty
without changing either marginal point estimate. (Williams and contributors, 2026)
   The newly inspected lmtp contrast path computes relative-risk and odds-ratio contrasts
on the log scale, obtains a symmetric interval there and then exponentiates its endpoints. It
also exponentiates the point estimate, but leaves the returned std.error column on the log
scale. That is usable when documented; interpreting every numeric column as being on the
reported ratio scale is a conversion error. For a ratio of 1.5 and log-scale standard error 0.2,
the delta-method ratio-scale standard error is 0.3. A conventional normal critical value gives
an exponentiated log interval approximately [1.014, 2.220], compared with [0.912, 2.088] for a
symmetric interval formed on the ratio scale. Neither arithmetic construction proves finite-
sample coverage, especially near a weak denominator. The delegate’s rejection of denominators
smaller than machine epsilon is a numerical guard, not a test that a denominator is statistically
separated from zero. (Williams et al., 2026; Williams and contributors, 2026; Causal Banking
Research Project, 2026b)
   The caller warns when a numeric reference is used because an estimated reference treated as
known loses its uncertainty. Its print method warns about unadjusted multiple comparisons.
The delegate permits a configurable critical value but prints a fixed 95% interval label. A
bank-facing result therefore needs the actual contrast, scale of every uncertainty field, critical
value, sampling unit and comparison family. A method name or a formatted confidence label
should not supply these facts by implication. (Williams et al., 2026; Williams and contributors,
2026)
   The recommendation is conditional reuse of the arithmetic interface after qualifying the
incoming centered influence function, mean-weight normalization, individual pairing and
independent clusters. Compare a known outcome shift, a deterministic reward increment,
unequal cluster sizes, a single-cluster request and log-versus-ratio output against independent
laws before relying on a standard error. A simpler independent implementation of the declared
influence calculation is a serious alternative for a narrow initial worker; it must meet the
same statistical requirements. Native dependency resolution, the numerical variance delegate,
full survival handling, learner behavior and random-number coordination remain separate
qualification obligations. These source findings strengthen the admission conditions for the
longitudinal reference; they do not establish an executed package failure rate, calibrated bank
uncertainty or an observed business benefit. (Williams and contributors, 2026; Williams et al.,
2026; Causal Banking Research Project, 2026b)
