               3.4.4. Direct causal query
                   In addition, we investigated a form of a direct causal query used by Ji-
               ralerspong [13], which is one of the most used forms of queries in the litera-
               ture. We use the direct causal query for completeness and reference to other
               previously investigated causal queries.
               Given the above information/definition/your knowledge/given text,
               which of the following is the most likely?
               A. Changing {Var 1} causes a change in {Var 2}, either directly or
               through one or more intermediate variables.
               B. Changing {Var 2} causes a change in {Var 1}, either directly or
               through one or more intermediate variables.
               C. There is no causal relationship between {Var 1} and {Var 2}.
