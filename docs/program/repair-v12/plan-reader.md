# Master program V12: software, language models and integration

11 October 2026. Baseline literature cutoff: 7 October 2026.

The master program now requires three major repairs before its next integrated
author release: a systematic causal software survey, a substantial treatment of
language models and agents in causal work, and executable library/CLI/MCP
integration with actual consumers. These are mandatory parts of the proposal,
with their own coverage, evidence, implementation and editorial contracts.

This document succeeds the planning scope of V11. It preserves the V11 scientific
contracts, negative results and delivered artifacts. The current research reader
is still the 268-page I01 release, not a completed version of these repairs. Its
three-page sponsor brief remains available. The new release is I02; no I02
monograph, completed survey, commercial superiority, bank validation or adopted
integration is claimed by installing this plan.

## The recovered state and the standard

The saved canonical state has eleven dispositions among sixteen V11 packages.
M00–M03 established the controls. R01 qualified a selected funding construction;
R03 qualified a selected nonlinear fixed-policy construction. R02's persistent
map/calendar construction, R04-v3's temporal graph-to-effect construction and
R05-v2's dependent learner have negative results in material regimes. E01-v2
qualified local linked streaming and recovery; I01 integrated these findings.
None of the ten original gaps has met every scientific and external gate.

The required standard remains a connected scholarly argument from a bank's
decision and information clock through model, assumptions, identification or an
identified set, estimation, uncertainty, diagnostics, numerical behavior and
financial consequences. Notation must be explained; substantive alternatives and
adverse examples must be worked through. The named FinMarketing monograph is a
standard of development and argument. It is not evidence that an integration
works. The new page allocations respond to the user's explicit requested scope;
they do not replace the standard of scholarship.

Every cited scholarly work needs its genuine PDF in docs/papers/, named
“Title FirstAuthorSurname(year).pdf”. Every scholarly, software and official
documentation citation needs a source identity, version/date, local copy,
SHA-256, inspected technical anchors and supported claims. Metadata can establish
identity and discovery; a package name or abstract cannot substantiate an
algorithmic claim. Unavailable originals remain in an omissions register.
Inherited results, new derivations, synthetic examples, hypotheses and verified
implementations must be distinguished. Reproducible banking examples and
inspection of the rendered PDF are acceptance requirements.

There are four separate decisions: author scholarship delivered; a capability
qualified in a declared regime; independent/bank/platform acceptance; and a
signed investment/adoption decision. A critical survey can be excellent while
concluding that a method fails. Neither that survey nor an API can repair missing
identification, establish authentic audit truth or accept itself independently.

## MR01: search every causal area and decide what to use

The current software chapter and its 44-project register are a starting corpus.
They do not provide the requested multi-chapter survey or full commercial
comparison. SW01 begins with 110 unverified search leads, including inherited
software, commercial products, agent frameworks and protocol references. A lead
is neither a verified citation nor an assertion that a current package exists.

The coverage contract crosses seventeen areas with six dimensions: R, Python,
Java, C++, other relevant implementations and commercial products. All 102 cells
must receive a supported disposition. The areas include IID, latent and temporal
discovery; identification and counterfactuals; effect and heterogeneous-effect
estimation; experiments, IV and RD; longitudinal and survival analysis; dynamic
policies; panel/DiD; synthetic controls; dynamic panels/time series; networks and
interference; mediation and sensitivity; transport and missingness; Bayesian/SCM
and state computation; trustworthy observations; and computational workflows.

SW01 will first record search terms, engines/registries, search date, cutoff,
result identities, screening decisions and unavailable sources. Search families
include method names and aliases, official package registries, upstream code and
release histories, primary method papers, vendor manuals, and backward/forward
citation checks. Language wrappers and native engines must be distinguished;
aliases cannot inflate the inventory. Two documented passes cover both the
method families and language/vendor dimensions. A cell without an eligible
candidate needs the searches and exclusion reasons, not a blank. Current mutable
documentation cannot be silently presented as its 7 October version. Later
material is a separately dated delta with an explicit scope decision.

The aim is defensible systematic coverage, not an impossible claim to enumerate
every unpublished or inaccessible program. Each cell records a search stopping
rule, exclusions and residual coverage limits. Essential unavailable evidence
keeps the affected claim or repair open. Public technical manuals may support a
documented capability even when a paid binary is unavailable; they cannot support
claims about measured performance, usability or numerical agreement.

SW02–SW05 develop the mathematical and operational comparison. Each substantive
package dossier explains the target, population, intervention, assumptions,
sampling law, information clock, identification, graph/effect output, uncertainty
and sensitivity, diagnostics, supported runtime and failure behavior. It then
works through an appropriate banking example and compares substantive
alternatives under the same target. A panel fixed-effect regression is not
automatically an identified treatment effect; a selected graph is not an effect
confidence region; a predictive forest is not automatically a causal forest.
The chapter must explain these distinctions through operations and examples.

SW06 compares commercial products by product, edition and dated version. For
each proposed operation it records the vendor's documented claim and evidence
level, our actual state, the capability difference, and a build/buy/partner/omit
recommendation. Our state is one of implemented and qualified in scope,
implemented but conditional, proposed, or absent. The comparison can conclude
that a vendor is stronger. “Not evaluated” is distinct from “absent”, and a
proposed feature is not a delivered competitive advantage. Pricing is recorded
as public, quote-required, unavailable or actually measured; no invented total
cost or vendor contact is part of this program.

SW07 assigns every admitted open-source catalog entry an action: adopt directly,
integrate through an adapter, port a selected operation, use as a research
reference, defer, or reject. A decision needs technical evidence, a substantive
alternative, licensing and maintenance evidence, a bank use case, a reason and a
revisit trigger. Direct adoption requires semantic fit and an acceptable
dependency contract. An adapter must preserve target, units, information clock,
graph ambiguity and uncertainty. A port additionally needs license permission,
an upstream behavioral oracle, numerical/statistical equivalence checks and a
maintenance owner. Rewriting an algorithm is not presumed better than using it.

IN03 implements selected paths in this repository. SW08 then qualifies the
selected R, Python, Java and C++ engine paths under a registered design with
independent small references. Native bindings and subprocess boundaries receive
separate cost and failure evidence. All requests, including crashes, unsupported
operations and unavailable runtimes, stay in the record. An unavailable language
path forbids an executed claim for that path. It does not justify inventing a run
or concealment in the critical survey. SW09 reconciles the findings into ten
connected chapters and closes MR01 only after substantive review.

## MR02: language models within a causal workflow

LA01 maps primary literature and packages across fourteen task families. These
include business translation, variable definitions and measurement, evidence and
expert disagreement, diagrams, IID and latent/temporal discovery, experimental
queries, identification, data/code work, longitudinal decisions, estimation and
validation, reporting/monitoring, agent architecture and evaluation/cost. Search
and source-audit rules are the same as for MR01. Favorable findings, failed
replications, counterexamples and benchmark limitations all belong in the map.
A generic agent framework must not be credited with causal algorithms it does
not implement. A remembered paper title is only a discovery lead until verified.

LA02 starts with four banking journeys: funding response, sequential servicing,
discovery followed by an intervention query, and trustworthy observation
construction. For each, the system must translate a business request into a
decision maker, available actions, target population, outcome and horizon,
estimand, observation/availability clock, feasible intervention and required
information. Ambiguous business wording should produce alternatives or
abstention. It must not invent a unique “correct” causal question.

For example, “reduce delinquency without reducing value” leaves the customer
population, servicing action, timing, loss horizon, cost of contact, constraints
and feasible comparison unresolved. An LLM may help expose those choices and
draft an estimand. It cannot certify that the bank's logging policy is known or
that records contain all time-varying confounders. The casebook will compare
alternative formulations and show how their identification requirements differ.

Every proposed diagram edge must have a statement of variables and time,
evidence type and anchor, assumptions, counterevidence and review status.
Data-supported relations, domain hypotheses and language-model suggestions remain
different objects. Expert disagreement generates explicit alternative graphs.
Deterministic checks catch temporal contradictions, accidental collider
adjustment, impossible interventions and discarded endpoint ambiguity. Passing
those checks establishes internal consistency, not causal truth.

LA03 organizes discovery methods by role: language-only proposals, empirical
discovery, retrieved domain evidence, priors/constraints, hybrid search, active
expert queries and experiments. Each mechanism receives a primary-source account,
its inferential role, a banking example and a counterexample. CPDAG/PAG
equivalence, latent confounding and temporal restrictions must survive suggested
orientations. Downstream identification and effect sets expose the consequence
of graph errors. Agreement among prompts or agents cannot become a calibrated
confidence statement without a justified statistical procedure.

The remedies are specific and testable. Unsupported edges require anchored
retrieval and review; temporal leakage requires explicit availability clocks;
prompt instability requires repeated perturbed evaluations and abstention;
benchmark memorization requires held-out tasks and renamed/restructured cases;
graph uncertainty requires equivalence-aware scoring and effect-set propagation.
Retrieval quality and agreement can still fail, and the chapters must say where.
Observational equivalence requires extra information or bounded conclusions;
more fluent language cannot remove it.

LA04 covers design, identification, preparation, linkage, estimator selection,
code generation, diagnostics, sensitivity, reporting and assumption monitoring.
Each task receives a useful agent role, a non-agent baseline, required evidence
object, failure mode and limited remedy. Reporting must distinguish a theorem,
an assumption, a synthetic run, an unexecuted suggestion and an external finding.
Retrieved content is data rather than authority to invoke tools. Artifact and
permission boundaries must be explicit in the actual integration design.

LA05 builds an auditable local prototype on the shared operation contract. It
carries the problem, graph alternatives, source evidence, tool calls, validation
and refusals end to end. Replayed or mock model responses can qualify contract
behavior; they cannot qualify live model quality. LA06 freezes models/versions,
prompts, tools, inputs, split, costs, repetitions, seed, stopping rule and metrics
before any final empirical evaluation. Comparators include non-agent workflows,
data-only discovery, language-only suggestions and retrieved/hybrid variants
where justified. Metrics cover formulation validity, assumptions, equivalence-
aware graph quality, downstream effect error/coverage, abstention, reviewer time
and full workflow cost. Missing model access or expert labels remains missing.

LA07 integrates eight chapters. Business translation, diagram construction and
discovery receive the greatest combined allocation. The result should explain
what assistance is useful, why failures occur and which remedies have evidence,
without presenting agentic orchestration as a solution to D01–D10.

## MR03: one semantic core and actual consumers

The saved nine-repository documentation index is historical evidence. The fresh
seed inventory observes eighteen workspace directories; that observation alone
does not establish eighteen usable integrations. IN01 must inspect applicable
instructions, revision or content identity, actual entrypoints, documented
schemas and role of each repository, grouping worktrees with their projects.
Every directory receives a relevant, adjacent, unsuitable or deferred disposition
with an inspected reason. Standalone notebook, batch, CLI and service uses are
included. All writing, fixtures and adapters stay in causal.

The inventory will examine likely statistical and banking consumers, protocol
servers and research utilities on their actual interfaces. A familiar directory
name is not enough to assert a function exists. Instructions, source inspection,
documentation snapshots, local contract doubles, actual library/CLI/MCP runs and
signed consumer acceptance are separate evidence levels. Any run that would
modify a sibling's files, environment, services or dependencies remains deferred
unless a safe isolated execution can be established within the authorized scope.

IN02 defines one operation layer for problem specification, graph objects,
identification, design, estimation, sensitivity, audits and reporting. Requests
carry units, population, intervention, time/availability, assumptions and version.
Results preserve provenance, unsupported/error states and finite, disconnected,
unbounded, empty or refused sets. Library calls, CLI JSON/exit codes and MCP
tools/resources map to the same validated operations. An API that turns an
unbounded confidence set into a finite interval fails conformance even if its
transport is successful.

Large inputs use authorized artifact handles with content identity and declared
custody. The contract covers schema/capability negotiation, bounded job budgets,
polling, cancellation, retries, idempotency, checkpoints and recovery. The MCP
specification and SDK version must be locally audited before implementation;
an MCP-shaped JSON response is not a tested MCP server.

IN03 implements representative reviewed language adapters. IN04 implements the
actual CLI/MCP surfaces and calls the server from a real client. IN04 depends on
the common contract, not completion of LLM research. LA05 uses that same contract;
IN05 brings the strands together in registered whole-workflow trials. It covers
all four bank journeys and standalone usage, with at least three relevant
repository contracts selected after IN01. Real eligible clients and engines,
read-only inspection and contract doubles remain individually labeled.

Fault trials include incompatible versions, invalid graphs, unit/clock mismatch,
missing artifacts, permission errors, timeouts, interrupted work and partial
publication. Comparisons include scientific output, uncertainty, error propagation,
recovery and full cost before speed. Maintainer adoption and target-bank platform
acceptance remain X03, regardless of author conformance results. IN06 develops
four operator and architecture chapters from the actual evidence, with diagrams,
client examples, failure behavior and explicit adoption limits.

The detailed machine work cards specify owner, prerequisites, steps, outputs,
criteria and failure action. There are 24 additions, bringing the program to 40
packages. The same local research agent executes them serially; independent
branches are scheduling choices, not authorization to spawn other agents.

The first ready package after P12 is SW01; LA01 and IN01 are also eligible. A
blocked branch does not stop another eligible branch. Searches and primary-source
inspection come before technical claims. Derivation, contract fixtures and
failure review come before final preregistration. Studies retain failed and
unavailable requests. A failed engine or model trial may feed a critical chapter,
but cannot earn a successful implementation claim. Editorial synthesis requires
complete coverage and a review of unresolved essential omissions.

No honest calendar estimate is available until the first inventories establish
source access, runtime availability and pilot costs. Each package records the
remaining work and exact next command or evidence request. Checkpoint records are
for recovery, not reasons to stop execution. Short bounded tool calls, persistent
artifacts and detached/polled builds reduce the risk of stream disconnection.

## Evidence gates and the control migration

There is one canonical state, docs/program/state.json. Frozen V11 controls,
completed work contracts, receipts, attempts and research PDFs remain unchanged.
The successor command is `python3 tools/causal_program_v12.py`; use its status,
next, check, template, register and record subcommands for V12. The old
`tools/causal_program.py status` and `check` remain compatibility checks required
by the repository instructions. Old mutation/render commands do not enforce the
new repair gates and must not be used for V12 acceptance.

The successor adds mandatory repair records, exact new contracts, retained
baseline identities and substantive repair-acceptance validation. Closure needs
a full coverage matrix, traceable primary-source audit, reviewed chapters,
measured substantive page allocation, inspected rendered pages and no unresolved
blocking findings. A page count, a list of packages, a boolean “done”, or a passed
transport test is insufficient. These controls check evidence integrity; an
author remains responsible for the scholarly judgment in the evidence.

I02 depends on SW09, LA07 and IN06 passing their scholarship contracts. Unresolved
X01–X04 and F01 are revised to require I02 and assess its exact manifest. The
preserved core expects a legacy I01 reference in the assessed_release field; the
successor additionally requires assessed_current_release and scope_release_sha256
to bind I02. The legacy field preserves lineage, not acceptance of the enlarged
scope. Original external contracts are archived before revision. An old I01-only
receipt cannot pass the successor validator. External owner/intake identity and
signed evidence are still required; an author cannot sign for a bank or reviewer.

Competitive gaps found by SW06 and unsupported package/model/consumer paths
receive explicit dispositions and successor proposals in I02. No change to a
completed scientific contract is allowed underneath a frozen receipt. A future
scientific successor requires a reviewed revision preserving the failed attempt,
fresh evidence and affected downstream review. The plan does not silently reopen
R02/R04/R05 or declare them repaired through a new library.

## What remains beyond this local repair

The software survey, LLM scholarship, schemas, adapters, local prototypes,
synthetic/reference comparisons and author chapters have known execution routes.
Their actual results are unknown until the work runs. The original gaps need
additional distinctions:

| Obligation | Known next route | Present limit |
|---|---|---|
| D01/D04: identity, assignment and audit truth | Owner-certified inclusion, assignment, ownership and gold labels; test added-information designs. | Truth and access cannot be synthesized from declarations. |
| D02: general nonlinear and optimal-policy inference | Work family by family; separate fixed-policy, learned-policy and estimated-logger uncertainty. | No general theorem or successful universal method is known here. |
| D03: learned latent temporal graphs | Preserve equivalence; derive effect bounds and class-retention guarantees; obtain interventions/restrictions where needed. | Equivalent observed laws can have different effects. |
| D05/D09: joint dependence and useful precision | Derive a justified joint variance/design repair or acquire more independent information; preregister fresh evaluation. | Current R02 result is negative; more computation is not a variance proof. |
| D06: dependent learners | Learner-specific rates, covariance and complete-workload qualification. | Current R05-v2 fails material regimes; arbitrary learners remain unsupported. |
| D07/D08: operation and independent acceptance | Named reviewers, target-platform faults/scale and signed consumer/bank acceptance. | Local trials and author reviews cannot supply these external outcomes. |
| D10: economics | Bank loss/precision thresholds, actual prices and matched held-out workflow value. | Synthetic values and local timings are not institutional ROI. |

Paid product access, inaccessible primary texts, live model access, independent
expert labels and sibling runtime restrictions may block particular comparisons.
Record the exact missing evidence, its owner or acquisition route, impact and
continuation trigger. Use a supported alternative only when it preserves the
question; never replace an unavailable original with an agent-written “paper”.
No purchase, vendor message, data upload or sibling mutation is inferred from
this plan. The user-authorized local research continues wherever those inputs
are unnecessary.

The plan itself closes P12 only. MR01–MR03 stay open until the actual chapters
and evidence meet their gates. The current monograph remains I01 until I02 is
compiled, inspected, reviewed and bound to a new delivery manifest.
