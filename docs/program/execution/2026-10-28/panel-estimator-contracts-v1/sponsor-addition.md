## Estimator boundaries that affect decision comparisons

Nine further pinned estimator and adapter files clarify what a reusable panel
interface must preserve. Selected fastdid branches apply different balanced and
unbalanced nuisance calculations, effective-support refusals and uncertainty
transformations. Two physical observations with weights one and two have an
effective count of 1.8 and fail the selected minimum-two rule. Extreme propensity
clipping can still leave control odds near ten billion. A finite answer is
therefore insufficient evidence of usable overlap or calibrated uncertainty
[@exec28_panel_estimator_sources; @exec28_panel_estimator_reference].

The selected nonabsdid conversions expose additional boundary risks: fractional
horizon aliases are truncated; native event clocks are shifted; method-label
fallbacks can conceal an unknown label; and a zero-variance reference row is
inserted for plotting. Stata import defaults can turn numeric labels into
factors and erase tagged missingness distinctions. Name truncation followed by
collision repair can exceed the intended output-name length before the final
writer is called. The independent witnesses reproduce these source-level
transformations; no imported package, writer or bootstrap was executed.

The proposed investment should first fund a typed public-data adapter trial
that preserves requested and resolved options, original identifiers, clock
units, missingness reasons, interval provenance and every refused cell. Exact
upstream versions, constructor behavior, numerical dependencies and rights
remain to be reviewed. These are concrete proposal requirements, not evidence
of an already qualified product or a reason to begin expensive bank experiments.
