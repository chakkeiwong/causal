# Registered follow-up: joint candidate information and identification limits

11 October 2026 (Asia/Shanghai). The first weak-audit supplement is complete
and frozen. Its 3,000 requests showed high coverage but wide primitive-rectangle
sets. This follow-up is explicitly motivated by that observed result; it is
registered before any of its own final draws. The earlier study remains intact.

Within each stratum, use the fixed linear transform of observable monetary
candidate responses, A=(mu1+mu2)/2 and D=(mu1-mu2)/2. The focal effect is
A+D/(2p-1). Transform the residual outcomes *before* score covariance, so
shared candidate noise and its covariance enter the A/D marginal variances.
Four Bonferroni normal bands across the two strata share .025 error; the same
two exact binomial audit intervals share .025. Project these transformed bands
with the already available `sum_difference_projection` algebra. This is a
different simultaneous primitive rectangle; it need not be contained in or
always narrower than the original candidate rectangle. No dominance is assumed.

Compare the original candidate rectangle and new transformed rectangle on
fresh shared evaluation, independent 4T pilot and audit draws. Retain five
original 1,200-date stress designs; add a sixth near-rank design with 12,800
audit labels per stratum instead of 1,600, holding the true correct-map
probabilities .55/.515 and evaluation/pilot sizes fixed. Use 600 repetitions
per design (3,600 final requests), seeds 10112643 with distinct stream keys.
Six-cell Bonferroni Monte Carlo intervals accompany raw counts. No outcome
driven alteration of covariance, information sizes, repetitions or penalties.

Report all-request coverage, finite nonempty availability, whole-line,
disconnected, empty and unavailable cases, and conditional finite median
width. Record per-request gain/loss and width comparisons; never compare
conditional medians on different finite subsets as a paired precision gain.
Numerical qualification before final draws must compare transformed slopes
and lag covariance against a direct full four-coordinate covariance calculation,
test whole-line/disconnected/empty cases, and retain null-variance refusal.

Derive the false-exogeneity population target for the inherited simulator.
If the structural action innovation is correlated c with the common balance
shock of scale sigma, the observed action response is (beta+sigma*c)*rho^h.
More data shrinks either valid-for-observed-law procedure around this shifted
quantity. Construct the alternative independent-error observational law with
coefficient beta+sigma*c and nuisance mean g(W)-sigma*c*m(W). That exhibits
why the causal target cannot be chosen from these observations without a
scientific restriction, instrument, randomized variation or new truth.

Author plan review: accepted after checking (i) transformation precedes score
covariance; (ii) same error budget, fixed tuning and independent pilot;
(iii) no finite-sample exactness promotion; (iv) comparison preserves all
six adverse/information designs; (v) audit count increase is additional
information, not a free algorithmic gain; (vi) population counterexample is
an identification claim for a class allowing the adjusted nuisance mean,
not an impossibility theorem under every possible restriction. The final
consolidated supplement must preserve both investigations and the real external
requirements for bank usefulness. No sibling modifications or contacts.

## Registered repair before qualified final draws

The first 3,600-request run is preserved in
`docs/studies/weak-audit-sharpening-initial-2026-10-11.zip` and labeled
exploratory/not qualified. An adversarial duplicate-candidate fixture exposed
a deficient numerical guard: roundoff gave the difference score a variance
about $2\times10^{-33}$ of the average-score variance, yet a normal band was
reported. The pre-final numerical check had tested a separate exactly zero
lag accumulator but failed to require refusal for this fitted fixture. This
failure and all original identities remain in the preservation receipt.

Before another final draw, add a fixed scale-aware guard: refuse if either
transformed variance is nonpositive/nonfinite or if the difference-coordinate
variance is at most $(8\epsilon_{\rm machine})^2$ times the positive
average-coordinate variance in either stratum. This is a numerical
qualification, not an identification theorem. Strengthen the adversarial
check to require refusal on the duplicate-candidate fixture. Freeze version 2
of the same six-design specification with fresh seed 10112751, 600 requests
per design and unchanged error allocation. Preserve all version-1 requests as
exploratory; do not relabel or reuse them as the qualified comparison.
