Chapter 56

Uncertain relationships, transported
policies and the information to audit

The chapter develops the information needed to move from uncertain customer relationships
to a policy decision in a new population. Exact examples first separate graph ambiguity,
coordinated action, transport and audit design. The implementation comparisons then ask
which parts of that argument survive linkage, imputation, discovery, historical retrieval and
reporting. Repeated population-weighting and pooling examples provide matched arithmetic
checks; they do not constitute independent evidence of practical performance.


56.1      A banking relationship is an input to a policy’s meaning
Consider an outreach proposal intended to improve repayment through a customer’s business
contacts. A contact may share information, coordinate a payment or pass on an offer. The
bank has candidate relationships assembled from several files, and a pilot population differs
from the proposed rollout population. The question is the incremental value of changing the
outreach allocation. It requires a definition of the relationships through which the action
can operate, the joint allocation of actions, the population receiving the benefit and the
observations that establish those quantities. An accurate estimate conditional on an invented
network can answer the wrong banking question.
   There are three distinct objects. An identity link says that two records refer to the same
entity. A relationship link says that two distinct entities have a specified connection. An
influence link asserts that an action can affect an outcome through that connection. A
common telephone number can suggest the first or second object without establishing the third.
Conversely, a one-to-one customer match does not establish a one-to-one guarantee network: a
firm can have many guarantors. The observation methods in Chapter 21 and the allocation
methods in Chapter 58 address parts of this problem. Here the issue is their composition:
uncertainty in the first objects changes the exposure, target population and monetary value
used by the later methods.
   Let G denote the scientifically relevant relationship graph, L an identity assignment, R the
observation indicators and Z a pretreatment population stratum. For an action vector a, an
exposure mapping hi (G, a) specifies which part of that vector can affect customer i. A policy g
is a joint probability law for feasible action vectors, conditional on information available at its
decision time. It need not factor into independent customer-level coins. A useful population

56.4     A population can change through its graph
Transporting a pilot result requires invariant causal mechanisms or a justified formula combining
the available populations. Selection diagrams express such invariance claims; the pretreatment
admissibility result of Pearl and Bareinboim (2014, Theorem 2 and Corollary 1) allows target
averaging of an invariant conditional intervention response under its graphical conditions.
Equal observed customer characteristics do not establish that the relevant conditional response
is invariant when relationship structure changes.
   An exact finite example makes the distinction visible. Take eight customers with the same
recorded covariates. At the source they form four disjoint undirected pairs, so everyone has
degree one. At the target, four customers are isolated and four form a cycle, so half have degree
zero and half degree two. Both graphs have four edges and mean degree one. Independently
assign each customer an action with probability p. An individual’s stipulated benefit is USD
100 if at least one of its neighbors receives an action; its own action costs USD 20. There is no
direct own-action benefit, and no influence beyond immediate neighbors.
  For degree d, the exposure probability is 1 − (1 − p)d . Averaging over these two actual
graphs gives
                     qs (p) = p,  q∗ (p) = 12 {1 − (1 − p)2 } = p − 21 p2 .          (56.8)
When p rises from 1/5 to 3/5, the source exposure gain is 2/5, whereas the target gain is 6/25.
The expected own-action expense increases by USD 8 in both populations. Net benefits before
a USD 24 investment are therefore USD 32 and USD 16; after investment the decisions have
values +8 and −8 respectively. Global enumeration of all 28 action vectors on each graph

56.6     Completion uncertainty and sampling uncertainty answer
         different questions
The intervals above express compatibility with supplied exact information. They are not
repeated-sampling error bars. When the graph and observation model are estimated, at
least three uncertainty sources must be distinguished: which completions remain admissible,
uncertainty conditional on a completion, and numerical error in averaging action assignments.
More Monte Carlo draws reduce the last component without resolving the first two.
   For an illustrative model-based analysis, suppose the two endpoint graph completions in
(56.5) have posterior weights 1/2 each. Conditional value means are −8 and 8, and conditional
variances are each one square dollar. The law of total variance gives

       Var(v | O) = E{Var(v | G, O) | O} + Var{E(v | G, O) | O} = 1 + 64 = 65.            (56.13)

A four-point construction with independent conditional noise {−1, 1} checks the arithmetic.
Reporting the variance one after selecting a graph omits the between-completion variation.
Computing a value at the mean graph also need not equal averaging the value: (56.2) depends
on the product U V . (Causal banking proposal, 2026cr)
   These posterior weights are supplied model statements. They do not establish frequentist
coverage, a justified prior or authentic probabilities for bank relationships. The joint Bayesian
linkage construction of Guha et al. (2022) illustrates how outcome information can inform
uncertain matching in a common likelihood. Its no-duplicate one-to-one linkage, no-interference
SUTVA and ignorability assumptions must remain attached. It does not supply a theorem
for replacing G by an arbitrary banking influence network. Adding a network outcome model
changes the likelihood and the causal assumptions and needs its own derivation and evaluation.

56.9.2    Missingness recovery and graph discovery require separate admission

The missing-data discovery study of Tu et al. (2019) assumes no confounding or selection bias
relative to its substantive variable set, causal Markov and faithfulness conditions, missingness
indicators that are not causes of substantive variables, faithful observability, no specified
deterministic relations between missingness indicators and no self-masking missingness. These
restrictions matter if unpaid balances are selectively unrecorded or account closure changes
later actions. A value being absent from a table does not establish the source’s missingness
graph assumptions.
   In the pinned causal-learn 0.1.4.8 source, the PC entry point dispatches mvpc=True to its
missing-value path; a supplied Fisher-Z test is replaced by the missing-value variant and
the correction name is passed downstream. The observed commit is 0dacacf36390 from 4
September 2026. (causal-learn contributors, 2026) This establishes dispatch semantics. It
does not establish a theorem for arbitrary nonrandom missingness, unknown identity links or
dependent customer networks. The proposed Python bridge must require a source-compatible
missingness and testing regime, retain the correction choice and preserve graph ambiguity.
Section 56.14 examines the test and correction paths, including their minimum-information,
virtual-data and dispatch restrictions. Their native behavior and validity under the proposed
sampling law still require qualification.
   Agarwal and Singh’s corruption-aware route uses a different information structure: bounded
approximately low-rank signal, mean-zero subexponential measurement errors independent
across rows, and a specified masking model that permits within-row dependence while requiring
independent rows. (Agarwal and Singh, 2022, Section 5.1, Assumptions 5.1–5.3) Repeated
measurements can supply useful structure for selected corrupted covariates. They do not
establish truthful missing edges or independent rows when several table rows share a customer,
household or guarantee. A native reconstruction of the algorithm would retain the same
scientific restrictions. Before adopting this route, define what constitutes a row and show how
the bank’s observation law supplies the required repetition and independence.
   These are substantive alternatives to blind imputation. A compatible missingness model
can identify a target; a low-rank structure can supply additional information; a sensitivity
class can preserve unresolved values; and an audit can observe the missing joint unit. Their
outputs should be compared on a common target. Completing a table first and selecting the
best-looking causal output afterwards would hide which information supplied identification.

56.12      From the information argument to implementation
           choices
Observation uncertainty can change a policy’s exposure and its transported value even when
separate edge probabilities and ordinary population summaries are correct. The needed
information can be a joint neighborhood moment rather than more labels of individual fields.
A feasible coordinated allocation can sometimes remove dependence on that moment; a target-
specific audit can sometimes measure it more efficiently. These alternatives require a common
causal request and distinct statements of compatibility, sampling error, model probability and
numerical approximation. The proposed software composition earns its role by preserving that
meaning through the decision.
   The following comparisons test these requirements against concrete components. They begin
with transport scores and imputation laws, then examine numerical completion, customer
identity and commercial analysis workflows. A component can be useful without resolving the
full composition problem.


56.13      R transport and missingness: preserve the population,
           the missingness law, and the analysis target
A bank moving a successful servicing experiment to a different customer population faces
two related but distinct questions. Which customers should the experimental effect describe,
and what can be learned when outcomes or adjustment variables are unavailable? A trans-
port estimator changes the population over which an identified response is averaged. An
imputation model supplies a distribution for unobserved values under declared restrictions.
Neither operation automatically supplies the assumptions needed by the other. This dis-
tinction governs the proposed R interface and its comparison with generalize, transportr,
causaltransportr, mice, smcfcs, and causalOT. The inspected releases are pinned before the
client cutoff, with precise identities and primary implementation anchors. The comparisons
concern those selected implementations, not every release. No downloaded package was exe-
cuted. (benjamin-ackerman/generalize contributors, 2025; nt-williams/transportr contributors,

56.13.2     Read the score and its complete delegate chain

transportr separates a task, nuisance fits, cross-fitting, and an influence-function object.
Its standard score uses source inverse odds and an average response difference over target
observations. The task exposes population roles, observation indicators, and cluster identifiers.
Nevertheless, its default one-fold setting uses the same observations for training and validation.
More folds activate separate fold construction, with a cluster-aware branch when identifiers
are supplied. Cluster-aware splitting alone does not derive cluster-aware uncertainty. The
selected influence routine hands row contributions to the external ife package without a
cluster identifier in that call; the complete uncertainty delegate remains a qualification
requirement. The documented sampling-weight argument is currently unused. Section 47.17.2

that a particular native search necessarily emits those members. The same source collection
includes a member-graph selection alternative whose output is one of the input graphs, subject
to the underlying search’s own validity. That alternative deserves an explicit adapter choice
rather than a silent swap of scientific meaning. (cmu-phil/tetrad contributors, 2026d; Causal

56.19.1     Missingness assumptions precede record completion

Let A be a binary action, X covariates recorded before that action, Y (a) the monetary
outcome under action a, and R an indicator that the outcome is observed. The record contains
(X, A, R, RY ), with a separate missingness mask so that an absent outcome is never mistaken
for a zero. Consistency connects an observed outcome to Y (A); treatment exchangeability and
positive treatment probability are additional causal assumptions. For the particular problem
in which only Y is missing, a conditional mean restriction sufficient for response adjustment is

                 E[Y | X, A, R = 1] = E[Y | X, A],       P (R = 1 | X, A) > 0.            (56.40)

The stronger familiar MAR restriction makes response independent of the missing outcome
conditional on the observed variables. When covariates are also missing, the pattern-specific
observed information and imputation model must be specified again; equation (56.40) is not a
general multivariate recipe. Administrative censoring, a variable undefined for a customer,
and an outcome lost through nonresponse need distinct observation models.
   Stata’s substantive manual and the SAS assumptions chapter distinguish MAR from MCAR
and describe ignorability using MAR together with distinct parameters for the data and
missingness models. They also explain why an assumption about unobserved values cannot be
verified by inspecting only the observed values. The SAS profile adds distributional restrictions
for particular regression and MCMC procedures. These restrictions must accompany a
procedure name. A good fit to observed records, a plausible-looking imputed distribution or
rejection of an MCAR diagnostic does not establish MAR. (StataCorp, 2026ab; SAS Institute,
2026c)

equally likely to be zero or one, randomize A independently of H, and set R = H. All customers
have Y (0) = 0. Responders have Y (1) = 10. In one complete-data law, nonresponders have
Y (1) = −10; in a second they have Y (1) = 10. The observable joint distribution of (A, R, RY )

56.19.5     Competitive position and a public qualification route

The documented commercial systems already provide substantive imputation, analysis and
diagnostic workflows. The proposal has not demonstrated a native replacement or superior
estimation performance. Its proposed contribution is to preserve the causal question and
its restrictions across those workflows: source and target populations, observation clocks,
missingness reasons, imputation identity, covariance semantics and explicit refusal. Licensed
isolated execution is a candidate integration route; public manuals do not grant redistribution
rights for the engines. EViews, Mplus and graphical-tool profiles cannot be ranked without
the missing originals, and prices remain unknown. (StataCorp, 2026z; SAS Institute, 2026f;
IBM, 2026c)
   A useful first prototype would compare a selected licensed procedure and an already re-
viewed open implementation on the same generated population, missingness law and estimand.
Complete-record analysis, complete-case analysis, single imputation, proper multiple impu-
tation and an explicitly specified response-weight procedure supply substantive alternatives.
Fix the causal target, sample law, interaction structure, number of imputations, diagnostic
and refusal rules before evaluation. Include MAR and MNAR cases, complete and missing
source support, compatible and incompatible imputation models, cluster sampling, changing
coefficient sets and unsuccessful fits. Report bias, coverage under the claimed regime, interval
width, unavailable requests, simulation error and full workflow cost. A sensitivity grid evaluates
robustness; it is not a device for choosing whichever assumption makes a preferred intervention
profitable.
   The library, CLI and MCP interfaces should carry the same request and result meaning.
Original and imputed data need stable unit and imputation identifiers, measurement types,
category references, passive transformations and sampling design. Results need per-imputation

56.19.6     What must be preserved from the audit to the decision

The chapter’s alternatives answer different information problems. Joint audits can distinguish
relationship laws that separate-edge labels cannot. Coordinated actions can sometimes avoid
an unresolved graph moment. Population adjustment uses justified invariance and support;
imputation uses an observation and analysis model; historical retrieval uses identity and
availability rules. Neither a completed table nor a precise conditional calculation establishes
all of those premises.
  A defensible recommendation therefore retains the missing information and its monetary
consequence. It may call for an additional joint audit, a different allocation, a restricted target
population, sensitivity analysis or refusal. The software choices matter because their score,
covariance, mask, clock and failure behavior can preserve or change that recommendation.
The next chapter examines hidden-state and Bayesian computations as further conditional
tools, with their assumptions carried into the comparison.

59.1      Resampling a sequence requires a sampling model
Consider a bank evaluating a sequence of servicing decisions over twelve months. The data
contain repeated observations of each borrower, interventions that depend on previous balances
and competing events such as account closure. The statistical unit might be a borrower history,
a household, a branch or an independent economic calendar. These choices imply different
resampling laws. A routine with “bootstrap” in its name does not determine the appropriate
law.
   LiNGAM’s generic bootstrap resamples rows and refits the model. VARLiNGAM has a
distinct path: it fits a lag model, applies a contemporaneous LiNGAM model to the residuals,
resamples residual vectors and reconstructs a sequence using the fitted autoregressive coefficients.
The inspected loop temporarily sets the lag-selection criterion to None. Consequently, one
must inspect the initial lag/coefficient settings and the loop’s refit behavior before claiming
that it propagates model-selection uncertainty. (LiNGAM project, 2026a)
   Residual resampling can be a sensible candidate under an appropriate innovation law. It
does not automatically preserve persistent residual dependence, unmodeled common shocks or
a different sampling calendar. Row resampling is a different candidate suitable for a different
observational unit. A banking comparison should include a correctly specified innovation
model and an adverse case with persistent residuals, preserving the uncertainty target and
failures in both. Bootstrap edge frequencies should not be reported as a calibrated effect
confidence region without an additional argument.
   pygformula’s inspected bootstrap draws observed subject identifiers with replacement, copies
each subject’s complete history and assigns a fresh replicate identifier to each draw. It updates
histories and refits covariate and outcome models. Expanding to the requested simulation
population occurs in a separate step. These operations distinguish the observed sampling unit
from the Monte Carlo population used to evaluate a fitted regime. (pygformula project, 2026a)

59.2      The intervention clock and competing-event target
The inspected simulation path applies an intervention to the current simulated frame and then
refreshes history variables before outcome prediction. Static schedules index their values by
the absolute time index int_values[t]. Threshold interventions bound the current action
between the supplied limits. Custom interventions receive the current frame, available pool
and time index. The API permits useful regimes, but does not itself certify that a custom
function uses only information available at the decision time. (pygformula project, 2026a)
   For example, “contact once delinquency has persisted for two months” refers to the observed
history before the action, whereas “contact borrowers who will be delinquent next month” uses
future information. Both can be expressed as executable code if future fields have been made
available. A valid interface must enforce the declared information clock and treat prohibited
reads as a contract violation. Thresholds should also retain their monetary units and whether
they change an action, a measurement or an eligibility condition.
   Competing events alter the target even when the same outcome model is used. In a stipulated
discrete-time model, let dt be the probability of a competing event in period t among those
still at risk, and let yt be the outcome hazard conditional on avoiding that competing event. If
competing events occur before outcomes within a period, the cumulative incidence through T
is

59.3     Following an R forest operation into its C++ implemen-
         tation
For grf, the causal-forest binding assigns the same variable index to treatment and
instrument, then calls an instrumental predictor. The predictor factory constructs
InstrumentalPredictionStrategy. The native prediction uses a ratio of centered outcome–
instrument and treatment–instrument moments. When instrument and treatment coincide,
this corresponds to a weighted local covariance divided by a weighted local treatment variance.
This traced route supports the interpretation of continuous-treatment output as a local slope
under its functional assumptions. (grf project, 2026a)
   It does not identify an arbitrary finite change in a nonlinear dose-response curve. A change
from an offer rate of one unit to two units needs the appropriate counterfactual contrast and
exposure support. A slope, an average slope and a finite contrast can coincide in a linear
model and diverge otherwise. The bank must choose the target before comparing software
outputs.
   The native variance routine forms influence-like quantities across tree groups, separates
between-group variation from group noise and calls a Bayes debiaser. These groups concern
the forest’s computation. They are distinct from the observed borrower clusters used by the R
average-effect calculation. Inspecting both paths avoids the false inference that a tree-group
variance option automatically accounts for a persistent banking calendar. The debiaser, forest
sampling, serialization and numerical safeguards require qualification before a direct native
interface is admitted. Section 77.20 develops the selected native representation, variance-field
and persistence contracts; source inspection does not replace their runtime tests. (grf project,
2026a)
   The maintained R interface is therefore a reasonable first integration candidate, subject
to a complete license/dependency and behavioral review. A direct C++ port would have to
preserve the forest representation, prediction strategy, variance method, randomization and
exceptional outcomes across version changes. Avoiding one language boundary does not by
itself justify that maintenance commitment. The open-source decision should compare the
operational cost of an R process or library bridge with the full scientific and technical cost of
a maintained port.

         tions

squares these group contributions and applies a group-count correction. For binary treatment,
the score uses the usual propensity residual divided by eb(1 − eb). For continuous treatment, an
auxiliary regression forest estimates the conditional variance of the treatment; the debiasing

59.6      Implications for reusable causal operations
These examples support a common semantic contract with explicit graph orientation, query/-
failure type, independent observational unit, decision clock, target population, dose units
and uncertainty construction. A business-facing assistant can elicit these fields and explain
unresolved choices, while a deterministic validator checks the declared operation against its
supported combinations. An LLM’s plausible description is not evidence that a package uses
the intended covariance or intervention target.
   The relevant next qualification compares complete causal requests. It retains the same
estimand and information across packages, separates sampling from Monte Carlo error, includes
failed fits and preserves unbounded sets. Numerical agreement in a benign case is useful
evidence about implementation, but cannot establish assignment truth, identification or useful
precision in adverse banking regimes. Those remain scientific and institutional requirements.
  The commercial comparison that follows applies the same criteria to documented vendor
capabilities and to this proposal’s actual evidence. A product feature is useful only for a
specified request; a proposed preserving interface is not yet demonstrated superiority over an
existing engine.
