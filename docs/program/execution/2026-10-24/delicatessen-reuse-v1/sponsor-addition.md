## Estimating equations need qualified roots, sampling units and covariance scales

The Delicatessen reference review authenticates seven selected originals at an 11 August 2026 revision reporting version 4.3. The candidate makes estimating equations explicit, but numerical root finding does not establish causal identification, and a pseudoinverse does not identify an unconstrained parameter. A proposed worker must retain the target, full residuals, rank, nuisance provenance, independent sampling units and covariance scale. \citep{exec24_delicatessen_source,exec24_delicatessen_choice}

Selected source control flow gives a concrete qualification obligation: standalone one-parameter vector and one-row-array inputs can use different observation counts. An authored four-observation calculation produces variance 1/4 through the main estimator contract but 1/16 if the standalone row return receives the documented extra division by four. This is a conditional static deduction, not a native run. Shape, normalization and HC1 fixtures must precede integration. Cluster-summed contributions also preserve cross terms omitted by an independent-row calculation. \citep{exec24_delicatessen_source,exec24_delicatessen_choice}

Probability clipping is a scientific choice. In a fully specified authored population with correct propensity but an outcome model omitting action, clipping changes the AIPW contrast from 3/5 to 27/80. A stipulated 1,000-account decision valued at 100 USD per additional success and costing 50,000 USD changes from 10,000 USD net to -16,250 USD when that pseudo-target is substituted. These are constructed consequences, not observed bank value. The proposed interface records clipping, counterfactual design coding and sensitivity rather than treating a finite standard error as acceptance. \citep{exec24_delicatessen_choice}

The immediate choice is reference only. Conditional integration requires locked dependencies, rights review, root/rank/shape/derivative tests, known-law public trials, independent-unit justification and complete failure and cost reporting. Porting has no demonstrated maintenance advantage. Authored costs of 4,800 USD over 240 requested, 200 finite and 160 accepted results imply 20, 24 and 30 USD per result under different denominators. Native qualification, visual review and final acceptance remain open. \citep{exec24_delicatessen_source,exec24_delicatessen_choice}
