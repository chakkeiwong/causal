Chapter 26

Choosing the capabilities worth
assembling

A bank does not buy an average treatment effect. It buys a better decision: which customers
to contact, which price to quote, which branches to reorganise, or which experiment to fund.
Software is useful when its answer bears on that decision and its assumptions are credible in
the setting. A method that answers a narrower question accurately can be more valuable than
an ambitious system whose output is difficult to interpret.
   The detailed comparisons in this monograph support selective reuse. The first proposal
should assemble a small number of named statistical procedures around a common description
of the decision. It should retain a second implementation where disagreement could expose
a consequential mistake. It should not promise to reproduce every package in one language.
The development expense of such a rewrite is certain; the benefit remains to be demonstrated.
   The comparison below draws on the retained technical sources and their dated versions. It
is a synthesis of those inspections, not a new census of current releases. Research coverage
ends on 7 October 2026; acquisition after that date does not establish historical availability of
a mutable manual. A documented procedure, an executed example and a proposed integration
are different kinds of evidence. Commercial tools are compared by their documented procedures;
unobserved functions remain unknown rather than being treated as absent.


26.1     Discovering relationships and identifying effects

26.1.1    1. Discovery from independent observations

For a cross-sectional customer study, the first choice is the set of assumptions under which
conditional independence or a score can reveal useful structure. The inspected R pcalg, Python
causal-learn and Java Tetrad implementations provide concrete comparison routes for PC. They
expose choices about independence tests, search and orientation. Using the same algorithm
name does not align those choices. A graph with unresolved directions is a legitimate result,
not an unfinished diagram to be completed by managerial intuition. (pcalg contributors;
CRAN source mirror, 2024b; causal-learn contributors, 2026; cmu-phil/tetrad, 2026a)
   A restricted PC comparison needs one primary implementation and another whose choices
can be inspected independently. The independence test, sampling unit, significance level and
treatment of orientation conflicts determine whether their outputs can be compared. Porting
the search into a new language is not justified by the proposal’s present evidence. Score-based,
functional and continuous-optimisation methods belong beside PC as different modelling

26.1.2    2. Hidden common causes, selection and equivalent graphs

An apparent connection between contact and repayment may arise because staff recognise
distress that the database does not record. Under such circumstances an analysis assuming
no hidden common causes can be precise about the wrong model. The inspected discovery
sources distinguish procedures such as PC and FCI and different graph representations. An
algorithmic orientation conflict must not be reinterpreted as an established hidden common
cause merely because the display uses a similar pair of arrowheads. (causal-learn contributors,
2026; pcalg contributors; CRAN source mirror, 2024b)
   The partially directed or ancestral graph determines a range of intervention conclusions
under its stated assumptions. A latent-variable route also needs an explicit interpretation
of its endpoints. Selecting one convenient orientation would instead report an unidentified
effect as identified. This is a rejection of an inference, not of a software family. Where
observationally equivalent models imply different lending decisions, additional timing knowledge
or experimental information is the valuable purchase. Another graph visualiser cannot supply
that information.


26.1.3    3. Time, interventions and changing regimes

Repeated customer observations contain both persistence and common shocks. The retained
Tigramite sources distinguish discovery methods, tests, lag conventions, graph classes and
effect calculations conditional on a supplied graph. Their observation and masking rules matter
when a calendar period is missing. A row after a missing month is not necessarily the next
economic period. Commercial dynamic-network and structural time-series routes are useful
alternatives, but a supplied temporal model and a discovered model answer different questions.
(Jakob Runge and Tigramite contributors, 2026; BayesFusion, LLC, 2024a; StataCorp, 2026e)
   Temporal integration therefore depends on the original calendar, observation availability
and the allowed intervention. Evidence from multiple regimes has a different interpretation
from an assumption that one stationary regime describes all years. A rate change during
a funding crisis need not have the response seen in ordinary months. A lagged prediction
improvement alone does not identify a general temporal effect. The first comparison should
assess whether plausible lag and regime choices change the decision, with graph ambiguity
carried into the effect calculation.


26.1.4    4. Adjustment, identification and counterfactual questions

The bank must distinguish identification of an intervention distribution from evaluation of a
structural model that somebody has supplied. Ananke’s inspected identification and model-
oracle interfaces make this distinction concrete. aGrUM and pyAgrum likewise combine
graphical, symbolic and numerical facilities whose individual meanings must be preserved. A
symbolic expression may still require conditional distributions unsupported by the observed
population. (Ananke community, 2023h; aGrUM authors and contributors, 2026; aGrUM and
pyAgrum contributors, 2026)
  Symbolic identification determines what can be learned before an estimator is selected.

Model-based counterfactual simulation answers a separate question conditional on the supplied
structure. A simulation from that structure therefore cannot establish identification from

26.2      Estimating benefits from actions and repeated policies

26.2.1    5. Average and heterogeneous effects

EconML’s residual-regression formulation illustrates why the final effect model matters as
much as the prediction methods used to remove confounding. In a binary-treatment setting
with sufficient observed adjustment information S, write e(S) = P (A = 1 | S) and τ (S) =
E[Y (1) − Y (0) | S]. With exact nuisance functions and a constant final coefficient, the
population projection is
                                    E[e(S){1 − e(S)}τ (S)]
                               θ∗ =                        .
                                       E[e(S){1 − e(S)}]
The average benefit for the target population is instead E[τ (S)]. These coincide under a
constant effect, but need not do so otherwise. This derivation concerns a restricted specification,
not every EconML estimator. (PyWhy contributors, 2026d)
   An explicit specification separates adjustment variables, effect modifiers and population
weights before an existing estimator is used. R forest and targeted-estimation routes and
Python DML routes should be compared on the same population and intervention, not on
their default output names. The inspected grf source gives a concrete R/native reference, while
commercial Stata CATE procedures provide an additional documented comparator. (GRF
contributors, 2026c; StataCorp, 2025) The proposed service’s contribution is to make these
choices visible and repeatable. It has not demonstrated predictive or causal superiority over
the existing estimators. The generated study in Chapter 28 examines the decision consequence
of an omitted effect modifier.


26.2.2    6. Experiments, instruments and discontinuities

Eligibility rules, capacity limits and randomised offers can supply stronger designs than
adjustment alone. The inspected commercial IV manuals and rdrobust sources provide useful
estimation and diagnostic comparators. Their identifying premises remain substantive: a valid
excluded instrument requires an argument about the outcome, and a discontinuity requires a
credible comparison at the threshold. Bias correction for an RD estimate does not establish a
strong first stage for a fuzzy design. (StataCorp, 2026g; SAS Institute, 2015a; RD Packages
developers, 2026b)
   An existing design-specific procedure gives the research team an informative baseline. A cross-
language comparison is interpretable only with the same threshold, bandwidth, polynomial,
kernel, treatment definition and uncertainty calculation. A weak or uninformative result may
be correct. Converting an instrument coefficient into a portfolio-wide effect instead requires
an additional argument connecting the relevant population. The first investment is usually a
better account of eligibility and assignment, not a second two-stage least-squares solver.

26.2.3    7. Longitudinal actions, survival and competing events

A collections policy changes future customer condition and future treatment. Adjustment
for a condition caused by an earlier action can therefore change the estimand. The retained
R gfoRmula and Python pygformula implementations provide inspectable simulation and
resampling paths for specified longitudinal interventions. Their histories, event ordering and
competing-event options must be compared explicitly. They are not equivalent to fitting a
hazard regression with a time-varying covariate. (CausalInference gfoRmula contributors, 2025;
pygformula contributors, 2026a; SAS Institute, 2015b; StataCorp, 2026j)
   A complete longitudinal procedure follows whole customer histories. Its meaning depends
on whether default, repayment, account closure or death prevents observation or constitutes
part of the outcome itself. An intervention that eliminates a competing event is a different
business question from one that leaves it possible. Java and C++ survival components can
be useful computational ingredients, but the proposal does not infer a complete sequential
causal method from a survival routine. The unresolved comparison is whether the entire policy,
observation and uncertainty calculation survives the chosen route, not whether it returns a
plausible curve.


26.2.4    8. Learning and evaluating a policy

A policy assigns an action using information available at the decision date. Choosing the
best rule on historical outcomes and evaluating it on those same outcomes rewards chance
as well as merit. The inspected R policytree source optimises supplied action scores; the
sources for dynamic-regime packages address different histories and decision sequences. A
commercial offline policy score or an optimum within a supplied decision model has its own
interpretation. (policytree contributors, 2026; DTRreg contributors, 2026; polle contributors,
2026; BayesFusion, LLC, 2024n; StataCorp, 2025)
   Simple prespecified policies expose what a richer rule adds. Score construction, policy search
and independent evaluation answer separate questions; their separation prevents the maximum
historical score from masquerading as an expected bankwide saving. Limited historical support
may restrict the feasible action space or leave some customers unevaluable. Reusing a native
policy learner avoids a rewrite whose benefit has not been measured. The useful next evidence
is an out-of-sample value comparison including action costs and the current decision rule.

26.3.1    9. Panel effects and staggered adoption

The R did source distinguishes cohort, calendar, relative-time and treated-cell aggregation.
The inherited exact example has cohort shares 3/4 and 1/4; the early cohort’s effects are 2 and
4, and the late cohort’s observed effect is 10. The resulting aggregates are 4, 19/4, 4 and 15/4
for treated-cell, group, dynamic and calendar targets respectively. None is an arithmetic error.
Their populations and time weights differ. (Callaway,SantAnna and did contributors, 2026)
   A group-time target and an explicit aggregation turn the business question into a comparison.
R, Python and commercial panel routes can then be assessed using the same control population,
treatment timing, anticipatory period and uncertainty calculation. General fixed-effects
regression is an important comparator, not an automatic substitute for the intended staggered-
adoption target. (Laurent Berge and fixest contributors, 2026; Bernardo Dionisi and differences

contributors, 2026; StataCorp, 2026n) Missing cells can change the population; they must
not disappear into an unchanged label. Reusing an R or Python procedure through another
language is initially preferable to reproducing its entire aggregation and inference logic around
a generic regression solver.


26.3.2    10. Synthetic controls and comparative time series

For a small number of treated regions, donor suitability can matter more than the choice of
optimiser. The synthetic-control construction uses untreated units to represent an otherwise
unobserved trajectory under restrictions on the outcome process and pre-intervention fit. Those
restrictions need a banking interpretation, including whether the proposed intervention affects
the donors. (Abadie et al., 2010)
   Synthetic controls provide a distinct comparison alongside panel methods. Their interpreta-
tion depends on donor eligibility, outcome units, the pre-period and the uncertainty protocol.
The inspected Synth-for-Stata implementation is community software hosted by a commercial
product; licensing the host does not establish vendor authorship or support for the extension.
The R CausalImpact path is a different model-based construction, with its own assumptions
about controls and stability. (Synth contributors, 2026; Google CausalImpact maintainers,
2026a,b) The complete documented route is the natural candidate for a bounded study; a new
port would need evidence of a material deployment advantage. The proposal should compare
sensitivity to donor and model choices before promising robust regional savings.


26.3.3    11. Dynamic panels, structural shocks and common calendars

A branch balance equation can contain lagged balances, rates and common funding shocks.
Dynamic-panel instruments, structural VAR restrictions and local projections address different
questions. The inspected Stata and EViews sources provide concrete commercial comparators;
they do not establish that a branch rate-setting policy is exogenous. (StataCorp, 2026o,q,p;
EViews, 2025c)
   The model acquires an economic interpretation through its instrument restrictions, horizon
and sampling unit. A large number of customer rows does not create a large number of
independent policy dates. The effective calendar determines whether uncertainty is driven by
branches, common dates or both. Forecasting can be valuable in its own right, but a forecast
response to an input change does not by itself justify changing a pricing rule. A new native
implementation should be considered only after the instrument and dependence comparison
changes a specific decision about the first prototype.


26.3.4    12. Networks and spillovers

Contacting one member of a household can affect another; a branch campaign can shift business
across nearby branches. The inspected MossSpider and inferference sources implement different
network and grouped-allocation constructions. Their eligible populations, exposure definitions
and uncertainty calculations cannot be harmonised by renaming columns. (Paul Zivich, 2026;
Bradley Saul, 2026)
  An initial study needs one economic exposure and a defensible assignment mechanism.
The population whose outcomes matter can differ from the population of possible recipients.
The matching statistical method can be compared with a deliberately restrictive no-spillover
analysis. Generic Java or C++ network libraries can represent relationships or simulate
a supplied process; that is not evidence that they identify an effect in the observed bank

network. Connectivity alone therefore does not resolve interference. The highest-value missing
information is often the assignment mechanism and the economically relevant links, not a
faster graph traversal.


26.3.5    13. Mechanisms, mediation and sensitivity

A policy may improve repayment through contact, understanding and a revised payment plan.
The total effect of the policy and the effect of intervening on one of those mechanisms are
different quantities. The retained R mediation paper explicitly distinguishes the additional as-
sumptions needed for causal mediation; randomising treatment alone does not settle them. The
source also distinguishes model and inference routes. (Tingley et al., 2014; Tingley,Yamamoto
and mediation contributors, 2025)
   A specified mechanism question determines which mediation procedure and sensitivity
analysis are meaningful. Controlled and natural effects describe different interventions. A
commercial mediation command is a useful comparator only after the treatment, mediator,
population and scale are aligned. (StataCorp, 2026b,s) A predictive-score decomposition has
no automatic causal-mechanism interpretation. For the investment decision, ask whether the
mechanism would change what the bank funds. If the same action is preferred across the
credible sensitivity range, a more elaborate decomposition may have little immediate value.


26.3.6    14. Transfer, selection and missing observations

Successful results from one branch need not transfer to another. Causal transport requires
assumptions linking the source and target populations; filling missing values within the
source population is a separate task. The retained transportability argument and commercial
imputation manuals support this distinction. (Pearl and Bareinboim, 2014; StataCorp, 2026aa;
SAS Institute, 2026e,f)
   Imputation and target-population weighting require separate justifications. Those arguments
depend on which outcome and assignment information is missing and why it is believed
recoverable. An imputed column does not recover an unrecorded treatment policy or the
potential outcome of a systematically excluded population. Adverse sensitivity results change
the decision just as favourable ones do. Commercial systems already provide substantial missing-
data facilities; the proposed service’s candidate advantage is a clearer causal interpretation and
repeatable comparison, which still needs evaluation. No new universal solution to unmeasured
selection is claimed.

26.4.1    15. Bayesian and hidden-state models

Posterior computation is valuable when an economic model distinguishes hidden credit condition
from noisy observations. It quantifies uncertainty conditional on the model and prior. It does
not establish the restrictions that give a policy intervention its meaning. The inspected Bayesian
and factor-inference sources therefore belong in a different category from observational graph
identification. (PyMC Developers, 2026; stan-dev/stan contributors, 2026; libDAI authors and
mirror contributors, 2012; StataCorp, 2026ac,ae)
  A mature inference engine can evaluate an economically specified model. Its result still needs

prior sensitivity, convergence diagnostics, observation timing and an account of structural
uncertainty. A posterior with narrow intervals can remain conditional on restrictions the

conditional comparators, with numerical inference and causal identification assessed separately.


26.4.2    16. Identity, ownership and information dates

Before a policy effect is estimated, records must represent the intended customer, institution
and decision date. The retained DuckDB, Feast and Polars sources provide useful tools for
queries, historical features and joins. Their technical functionality cannot establish that a
record was available to a decision-maker merely because its economic event date is earlier.
(DuckDB contributors, 2025; Feast contributors, 2026; Polars Contributors, 2026)
   Data-processing components can preserve event time, publication time and the availability
of revisions. Ownership changes and identity links must represent the economic unit required
by the particular target. A rare incorrect link can have a large monetary consequence if
it involves a large exposure. The existing public-data survey makes UCI Bank Marketing
useful for a timing example: call duration is unavailable when deciding whether to place the
call. It does not establish an effect of calling from a representative untreated group. (UCI
Machine Learning Repository, 2026a) Retrospective access to later information would change
the historical decision being evaluated. The unresolved requirement is credible observation
history, not a missing SQL function.


26.4.3    17. Numerical computation and a reproducible study

Java regression libraries, C++ numerical kernels and other-language panel packages can reduce
development effort. Their statistical surroundings matter. The inspected C++ panel sources
sit inside procedures that select samples, define effects and scale uncertainty; a coefficient solver
alone does not preserve those steps. A Julia suite and its individual components likewise must
not be counted as independent corroborating implementations. (Apache Software Foundation,
2026; fixest contributors, 2026; xhdfe contributors, 2026; Selected repository contributors,
2026b)
   A numerical component answers a computational need; a complete statistical procedure also
determines the sample and inference. A port is justified when a measured constraint makes
its expected benefit exceed development, comparison and maintenance costs. A study run
through a library, a command line or an assistant should preserve the same question and result.
Agent frameworks and vendor service clients may help conduct the work, but a completed
call is not evidence that a causal interpretation is justified. (Causal banking proposal project,
2026dh; SAS Institute, 2026i)

26.5.2    When an implementation detail is consequential

The selected routes in Section 26.8 use existing procedures under declared targets and observa-
tion laws. A native port incurs another implementation and validation burden; its benefit must
cover that burden. The inspected integration design develops that cost comparison. (Causal
banking research project, 2026as)
   These are proposal recommendations drawn from the preceding comparisons, not claims
that every integration has been executed. They also explain which software details merit
further investigation. A difference in coefficient ordering is material when it changes reported
uncertainty. A difference in a random-number helper deserves priority only if it could change
the statistical conclusion or adoption choice. The conditional examples establish possible
failure mechanisms; they do not establish default-package defects or measured coverage
failures. (Selected panel package contributors, 2026c; Causal banking proposal project, 2026hb)
   The longitudinal comparisons identify a more specific reason to withhold some profiles. In
the selected pygformula restriction path, deterministic histories enter the fitting population
even though simulation subsequently enforces their known values. In the selected lmtp
uncertainty composition, the order of centering, weighting and aggregation matters when
weights or cluster sizes differ. The Julia survival comparison similarly separates identified
parameter combinations, observation entry and extrapolated tails. These findings concern
which distribution or uncertainty is computed, not merely programming style. They narrow
the proposed reuse contracts without establishing how often failures occur in bank data.
Sections 47.20, 47.17.2 and 47.23.3 develop their source traces, exact constructions and
alternatives. (pygformula contributors, 2026b; Williams and contributors, 2026; Williams et al.,
2026; JuliaSurv contributors, 2026a,c,b)

trial. It does not justify rejecting every method in the same library, nor does a corrected
formula alone establish native qualification. The next comparison must reproduce the intended

26.6      What the commercial comparison changes
Commercial statistical packages are credible alternatives, not an empty space waiting for the
proposed service. The inspected Stata procedures cover distinct treatment, panel, survival and
structural questions; the SAS sources distinguish statistical procedures from service access.
The bank may already possess trained staff and relevant entitlements. A proposal that ignores
those assets overstates the saving from a new platform. (StataCorp, 2026a,n,i; SAS Institute,
2015a, 2026i)
   Compare three feasible alternatives: improve existing analytical practice; connect a small
set of existing procedures; or build a broader common service. For each study, distinguish a
vendor-authored procedure, a community extension inside a commercial host, an open client
to a licensed server, and a general numerical component. They have different support and cost
implications. The comparison has not established exact current prices, complete entitlements,
performance at the bank’s scale or the absence of equivalent features in all competitors. Those
are unresolved commercial questions, not findings of technical superiority.
   The proposed investment is therefore conditional. Its candidate contribution is a disciplined
connection between the business question, assumptions, software choices and financial interpre-
tation. Fund a narrow comparison that can show whether that connection prevents material
errors or saves analyst time. If the existing estate achieves the same result more cheaply, retain
it. If differences in estimands, graph interpretation or information dates repeatedly change
decisions, a shared service becomes more persuasive.


26.7      Existing commercial capabilities and their terms
The public experiment in Chapter 29 demonstrates why reuse is the starting point. Native R
fitting and a separate Python calculation agree on the same observations, regression specification
and HC2 covariance. Rewriting a familiar estimator in another language would add little to
that calculation. The proposal’s contribution must instead be demonstrated in the connection
between the economic question, assumptions, available information and reported decision.

It documents fitfepanel as introduced in R2026b and fitrepanel in R2026a. The retrieved

  The documented treatment of missing identifiers is consequential: a record with an absent
subject identifier is assigned an additional group, while a missing predictor or response removes
the observation from estimation. Those are computational conventions, not evidence that
the economic unit is known or that discarded observations are ignorable. The panel study in
Chapter 16 shows why that distinction matters.
   A matched comparison must specify the institution and time effects, covariance convention,
initial sample and missing-record treatment before comparing coefficients. The inspected pages

possible way to obtain time effects or two-way covariance in the wider MATLAB estate. There
has been no licensed MATLAB execution in this study. The appropriate proposal decision
is to retain it as an existing-estate alternative and test the declared calculation where the

26.7.2    A decision diagram already contains an information structure

HUGIN’s official GUI 9.8 manual describes Bayesian networks, decision and utility nodes,
and limited-memory influence diagrams. Its account makes the information available at
each decision explicit through incoming informational links; it also relaxes the traditional
requirement that every earlier observation remains available. This is directly relevant to a
servicing policy, where later actions must use the information actually observed at their dates.
(HUGIN Expert, 2026a)
   The proposal should therefore compare a graph assistant with an established diagram editor
and a declared decision model. It should not equate drawing an arrow with identifying an
intervention effect, or infer from a manual that the proposed integration has been imple-
mented. The inspected material supports a concrete alternative for representing decisions
and information; it does not establish an automatic solution to latent confounding or missing
outcomes.
   The selected influence-diagram route has a further restriction that matters for a loan
portfolio: its manual excludes continuous chance nodes. The separate conditional-Gaussian
Bayesian-network facility does not remove that restriction. That facility describes Gaussian
variables whose means are linear in their continuous parents, allows the coefficients and
variances to depend on discrete parents, and excludes continuous parents of discrete nodes.
These are different documented model families within the same product. A continuous balance
history therefore cannot be carried into the decision diagram merely because another HUGIN
model accepts a continuous measurement. (HUGIN Expert, 2026a)
   One possible comparator uses explicit balance and arrears categories in a supplied decision
model. Their boundaries, transition probabilities and utilities would then be part of the
economic approximation: customers in a single category need not have the same response to a
contact. A second comparator retains continuous states in a separately justified policy-value
calculation, such as the construction in Chapter 3 and its fitted extensions. The choice
should turn on whether categorisation preserves the decision and its material uncertainty. The
inspected manual establishes the representation boundary; it does not quantify the loss from
coarsening a bank’s records or demonstrate a native servicing trial.
   The vendor’s published licence text makes distribution using its API subject to an OEM
licence. That term changes the proposed reuse route: an internal connection to an entitled
installation and redistribution of a new product are different propositions. The actual order,
deployment conditions and prices remain to be established with the vendor. The current
manual’s exact release date relative to the literature cutoff is also unresolved. (HUGIN Expert,

26.7.3    Which comparisons belong before a prototype

The proposal can justify selecting existing estimators and diagram tools from inspected
methods, alternatives and explicit limitations. A complete installed distribution, matched
adverse-data tests and target-system acceptance are subsequent implementation evidence.
They must be specified before a native adapter is accepted, but they should not be treated as
proof that the economic case for a prototype is unfinished in every respect.
   The useful comparison asks whether an existing procedure answers the bank’s question and
what additional work preserves its assumptions. Three choices illustrate why conditional reuse
can be justified before a new product is built, while the meaning of a reported effect must be
settled immediately.


26.7.4    Reuse the estimator after choosing the population

For an offer made once to each customer, the relevant average may cover all eligible customers
or only those who actually received the offer. DoubleML’s inspected Python interactive-
regression implementation distinguishes these targets, checks for a single binary treatment, and
directs instrumental-variable data to a different model. It forms separate predictions under
treatment and control, processes the propensity estimates, and constructs the corresponding
score. These are useful reasons to integrate the selected original procedure instead of writing
another implementation merely to change language. The inspected revision is 6884b5f, dated
23 September 2026; its declared BSD-3-Clause licence and dependencies are recorded separately
from the earlier installed-version observation. (DoubleML contributors, 2026)
  The recommendation is conditional integration of a specified model, with an unadjusted
or transparent adjusted comparison retained. Neither the choice of predictor nor clipping
a propensity establishes that every customer had a realistic chance of receiving each action.
Sampling dependence, permissible covariates, target weights and the uncertainty calculation
must agree with the study. The full distribution and matched adverse-data trials belong in
the prototype comparison. Porting is justified only if a measured platform or cost constraint
outweighs the burden of maintaining the statistical procedure.
   For phased adoption across branches, did offers a different target: effects for an adoption
cohort at a particular date, followed by an explicitly chosen aggregation. The newly retrieved
DESCRIPTION comes from the same 74b88eb commit as the retained API source. It identifies
version 2.5.1.902, R at least 4.1.0, a GPL-3 declaration and dependencies including DRDID.
Its bytes match the earlier mutable description, closing that specific identity uncertainty. This
is a repository version, not evidence of a stable published release or of a complete deployment
entitlement. A request for a separate LICENSE file returned 404; that does not cancel the
declaration. (Callaway and Sant’Anna, 2026a; Callaway,SantAnna and did contributors, 2026)
   The source’s treatment of time-varying weights matters economically. In a balanced panel
the default comparison uses the earlier period’s weights; choosing varying weights switches
to the repeated-cross-section estimator. In an unbalanced panel, fixing weights at an anchor
date restricts the sample to units observed then. Thus two analysts can agree on the branch
records yet answer different questions after selecting different weighting rules. Imagine two
equally weighted customer groups with hypothetical effects of 10 and −10 units. Their equally

27.6      What causal reasoning benchmarks establish
A banking analyst asks several different questions of an assistant. Can it translate a request for
higher deposits into an intervention and a measurable target? Can it distinguish information
known before an offer from information created by the offer? Can it explain which arrows in a
diagram are assumptions, and recognise when several diagrams fit the same observations? A
correct answer to a narrative puzzle is useful evidence about some of these abilities, but it
does not establish all of them.


27.6.1    Explanations deserve their own test

Wang and Shen examine three constructed scenarios: coin operations, interrupted file downloads
and a hypothetical vaccination calculation. Four variants of each scenario and three prompting
approaches give 36 prompt conditions. Their scoring distinguishes a correct result and
explanation, a flawed explanation, and an incorrect result. This is valuable because a correct
number can accompany a mistaken economic argument. The study reports mixed changes
from examples and guided reasoning, rather than a uniformly helpful prompting intervention.
(Wang and Shen, 2024)
   The terms used in a benchmark also need scrutiny. The paper calls reasoning from an
observed effect to a cause actual causality. That task description does not, by itself, specify a
formal criterion for an individual’s actual cause. Its smallpox case asks which source produces
more deaths in a stipulated population. With the given numbers, vaccination reactions
produce 990,000(0.01)(0.01) = 99 deaths, while disease among unvaccinated people produces
10,000(0.02)(0.20) = 40. Those population counts differ from the per-person risks, 0.0001
and 0.004. Confusing a total with a risk is an estimand error; the arithmetic alone does not
establish a general Simpson-reversal mechanism. These are deductions from the paper’s stated
example, not new medical evidence.
   The banking analogue is direct. A large customer group can contribute more defaults
while offering a smaller default risk per account. A deposit offer can increase gross balances
without increasing net contribution. An assistant should state the denominator and economic
target before presenting the answer. The paper’s prompts provide useful stress cases for that
distinction. The small scenario set and model configurations do not support a current ranking
of assistants for banking work. We have inspected the published methods and results; we have
not reproduced its model calls or inspected every supplementary response.

27.6.2    Scale does not remove uncertainty about the answer key

MDC-Bench broadens the comparison to 8,422 questions across seven disciplines, including
business, economics and management. Its tasks distinguish reasoning, intervention, decision
and counterfactual questions. Questions are constructed from causal narratives extracted from
preprints, with model-generated alternatives and a manual sampling check. The published
protocol identifies model versions, prompt templates, temperature and output limits. (Wang
et al., 2026, Sections 2–3 and Appendix A)
   Its harder condition requires two ranked answers instead of one. For example, the reported
Claude thinking configuration has average accuracy 84.56% in the basic condition and 59.25% in
the harder condition. These are different response requirements on the same benchmark; their
difference is not a measured deterioration in one fixed banking task. The benchmark is useful
for comparing errors by causal structure and question type, provided its answer-generation
assumptions remain visible.
   The detailed prompts reveal an important distinction. Reverse reasoning may use necessity
and sufficiency, or otherwise select an abductive explanation judged probable from background
knowledge. Intervention questions construct an altered causal narrative; the answer alternatives
are ranked by their judged plausibility. These procedures do not supply a known structural
equation system, a randomised treatment effect or an identified posterior over causal diagrams.
Appendix A’s sampling review helps detect poor questions but does not turn model-generated
causal statements into independent economic truth. The authors also discuss weak long-chain
relations and distractors that may be too easy. (Wang et al., 2026, Limitations; Tables 28–29
and 39–40)

(Chen, 2025, Sections 5.6 and 6; Tables 1–4)
   The causal interpretation needs more than those comparisons. Equation (16) factorises a
joint distribution according to a graph. Equation (17) defines a conditional treatment contrast.
Neither statement alone identifies that contrast from the observed tweets. Equation (19)

bank may test whether a language model helps extract a policy variable or whether a simulator
represents a declared mechanism. It still needs a defensible treatment assignment, observation
model and counterfactual comparison before attributing a financial gain to the policy. The
reported architecture is a research lead. Its stated improvements are not evidence that the
proposed banking service already delivers causal or commercial benefits.

   The resulting assessment has distinct outcomes: whether the target is correct, whether a
proposed adjustment is justified, whether an unknown effect remains unknown, whether the
numerical calculation is correct, and how much analyst time the assistance saves. Independent
adjudication is needed for open-ended economic formulations. Generated cases with known
mechanisms can test specific errors before an expensive bank exercise. The public experimental,
servicing and linkage studies in this volume provide numerical cases of that kind, but no live
assistant has yet been evaluated on them.
  The inspected papers therefore sharpen the proposed experiment rather than establishing
a benefit from the proposed service. Repeated agreement among prompts is especially weak
evidence when their errors share a source, as the repeated-measurement calculation shows.
The useful next comparison measures consequential mistakes and time on held-out tasks, with
human adjudication and a fixed cost allowance.


27.7      A positive workflow result and its limits
The retained ORCA study gives a more direct reason to test assistance in a completed
workflow. Twelve participants used a counterbalanced within-subjects design; the baseline
assistant already had tools, step gates and minimal state. The paper’s Figure 5 reports total
task durations of 1,395 seconds for the baseline and 1,107 seconds for ORCA, a reported
reduction of about 21%. Table 1 reports completion rates 0.75 and 0.83. This is positive
published evidence for a structured workflow on the study’s relational, generated causal tasks.
It is not a measured benefit for this banking proposal. (Chung et al., 2026, pp. 3–5)
   The interpretation changes when the outcome definitions and all attempted analyses are
retained. Completion means producing an ATE, regardless of accuracy; discovery success
requires the treatment and outcome nodes to be present. The paper’s confidence-interval
metric is described as an estimated ATE falling within a ground-truth interval, so its reported
0.083 and 0.50 must not be presented as conventional repeated-sample confidence coverage.
The full-data condition comparison for absolute ATE error has p = 0.321; after excluding
configuration-error outliers, the reported OLS comparison has p = 0.020. Both belong in an
assessment of completed work because configuration errors are among the failures a service is
supposed to avoid. The abstract’s claimed 42-percentage-point completion gain also differs
from the displayed 0.75-to-0.83 comparison; this synthesis uses the stated table comparison
rather than reconciling that discrepancy by invention. (Chung et al., 2026, pp. 1, 5, 17–18)
   The proposal should therefore test whether shared intermediate results and question clari-
fication save full effort while preserving scientific quality. It should retain all configuration
failures and report separately the time spent by the analyst, tool execution and correction. The
external study supports that experiment’s plausibility. Its small participant sample, specified
tasks, outcome definitions and analysis exclusions prevent a claim that the same benefit is
established across bank studies.

27.8     Identification, execution and reporting
CLadder evaluates formal causal questions under constructed mechanisms. Its historical model
results concern a supplied task rather than the elicitation of a new bank mechanism. A defined
formal procedure provides the identification comparator: an assisted answer must preserve
both the conclusion and the conditions under which it follows. When identification fails, an
explanation of the missing restriction is useful; an unexplained empty response is not. The
benchmark literature and retained mechanism-clustering critiques also justify retaining failed
cases and the full uncertainty denominator. (Jin et al., 2023)
   For execution, the local interface study establishes equality for four constructed requests.
Assessing assistance requires a further comparison with a scripted workflow, because choosing
the request and correcting mistakes are part of the analytical work. Request construction,
faithful tool use, detected errors and correction effort are the relevant outcomes. The formal
tool’s calculation cannot by itself establish the assistant’s contribution.
   A deterministic reporting template is another strong comparator. It already states the
estimand, assumptions, interval and scope. The proposed additional value is an explanation of
why the result changes a decision, or why it cannot yet support one, that remains faithful to
the result and its sources. An explanation that promotes a set crossing zero into a positive
recommendation is substantively wrong. No local live-model evaluation yet establishes an
incremental benefit for this reporting task.
   Question clarification with evidence retrieval and explicit alternatives is the preferred
first assisted task, evaluated against the questionnaire. Identification and numerical results
remain with defined procedures. Expansion to autonomous discovery, variable construction
or recommendations needs evidence specific to those tasks. The three frontier studies in
Section 27.6 sharpen this distinction. The financing-and-exports study in Section 65.11.4
supports a bounded extraction and explanation comparison; its scores do not identify a lending
effect. The related epistemology discussion supplies no separate intervention estimate. These
findings refine the proposed experiment without establishing a live banking benefit. (Li, 2025;
Mugleston et al., 2025)
   The end-to-end workflow in Section 69.30 turns a prompt-based risk score into sampled
graphs and fitted effect intervals. Its uncertainty interpretation depends on the additional
conditions developed there. The thesis by Ait Lhouss is used only to explain a source-quality
exclusion: Section 4.1 and the selected-study table report ten studies, whereas the abstract
and Tables 5–6 report eighty. These unresolved denominators prevent using its reported review
coverage or productivity results. That limited exclusion implies neither intent nor a formal
retraction. (Molero Pérez, 2026; Ait Lhouss, 2025)
   The pharmacovigilance comparison in Section 66.11.3 shows that useful enriched terms
and classifier-score differences still need an intervention argument. The InferBERT and
DeepCausality methods expose the missing adjustment requirements; the conditional text-
embedding alternative also makes target-population weighting explicit. The fixed frontier
inventory contains 91 inspected entries, including the one source-quality quarantine, and
22 unread entries. Supporting method originals are audited separately and do not enlarge
that denominator. Inspection is therefore distinct from positive scientific support, and the
remaining omissions limit broader claims. A live comparison needs a fixed model version,
held-out questions, matched information, independent adjudication and a budget. (Wang
et al., 2023b, 2021b, 2022; Veitch et al., 2020)

28.5     What better text measurement would have to accomplish
Suppose an assistant extracts distress from customer notes. Let true distress be U , measured
distress W = U + εW , action A = U + εA and outcome Y = τ A + U + εY . All disturbances
are mutually independent, centred Gaussian variables, with unit variance except Var(εW ) = s.

The calculation was independently checked by solving the covariance normal equations. For
τ = −1/5, the adjusted coefficient is zero at s = 1/3 and positive above that threshold. This is
residual confounding from imperfect measurement, even with an arbitrarily large sample. The
retained review of text-based adjustment motivates the measurement question; the equation is
a project derivation. (Keith et al., 2020)
   The useful contribution of a language model would be to improve measurement enough
to alter the effect or decision relative to a credible alternative. It is insufficient to show
that its summary is fluent. Compare human coding, a dictionary, conventional supervised
extraction and the language model on the same permitted notes. Hold out customers and
periods; retain errors and abstentions; examine whether the text precedes treatment or is
itself a consequence of contact. A measurement-error variance below 1/3 is a boundary in this
particular constructed system, not a transferable acceptance threshold for real text.
