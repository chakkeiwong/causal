# A rollout path must preserve its cohort population

The pinned community Stata EventStudyInteract wrapper offers a reference route for interaction-weighted event studies. Its genuine Sun--Abraham preprint supports cohort aggregation under stated trend, anticipation and sampling assumptions. A changing horizon can change the population: authored constant cohort effects of two and ten average to four with shares 3/4 and 1/4, then to two when only the early cohort remains observed. This need not show an effect fading. Last-treated controls must stop being controls before their adoption period. \citep{exec24_eventstudy_sources,exec24_eventstudy_original,exec24_eventstudy_contract}

A future adapter must export the interaction-weighted coefficient and covariance objects, retain cohort shares and actual estimation samples, and qualify the entire covariance calculation. The selected share-covariance call uses a robust option while the outcome regression receives the requested covariance option; the source flags unbalanced-panel normalization for further work. Exact authored covariance and sample witnesses define tests, without claiming native failure or coverage. Pointwise intervals do not establish a simultaneous rollout path. \citep{exec24_eventstudy_sources,exec24_eventstudy_contract}

The acquired pinned MIT license resolves the earlier wrapper-license uncertainty, but does not qualify Stata entitlement or unpinned dependencies. The decision is reference now, with an original Stata worker conditional on matched semantics, rights and public/generated validation. An illustrative 1,800 USD over 90 requested, 75 conformant and 60 useful results gives 20, 24 and 30 USD per denominator. Public tests can establish engineering merit before costly bank work; they cannot manufacture untreated trends or absent long-horizon outcomes. \citep{exec24_eventstudy_sources,exec24_eventstudy_contract}
