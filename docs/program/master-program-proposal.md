## The deliverable and the standard

Produce a coherent scholarly monograph and product/investment proposal, grounded in inspected primary sources, a systematic software/agent/integration comparison, and reproducible bounded public/synthetic evidence. Explain what the proposed capabilities do, what competing approaches provide, how a minimal prototype should work, what its evidence establishes, and which later experiment could change an investment decision.

Retain the FinMarketing standard of developed argument, notation, assumptions, alternatives, derivations, diagnostics and banking examples. Retain real local PDFs for cited scholarly works and identity/version/hash/anchor audits for all sources. The literature cutoff remains 2026-10-07; later acquisition snapshots are labeled. No paper summary is substituted for a paper. Build and inspect the PDF; current rendered visual acceptance requires a fresh session under the inherited image allowance.

The current proposal does **not** require an accepted bank deployment, signed production adoption, authentic bank assignment/audit truth, paid platform trials, institutional approval, or measured bank ROI. Those remain later, conditional claims with their own evidence requirements. An author cannot manufacture that evidence, but its present absence does not block this proposal.

## Acceptance at each stage

| Stage | Required evidence | Completion means |
|---|---|---|
| Proposal now | Developed scholarship; sourced landscape comparisons; concrete architecture; reproducible examples; prototype design and scoped executed evidence; cost/risk/VOI scenarios | A reviewable recommendation to build, narrow, reuse, reject or investigate specific capabilities |
| Bounded prototype | Selected working paths, matched baselines, prospectively registered public/synthetic studies, failure cases and measured local resources | Demonstrated merit and limits in the declared regimes; no inference of bank qualification |
| Conditional bank study | A decision-relevant unresolved question plus a prototype result supporting the experiment; scoped bank data/design/owners | Evidence on target-bank assumptions, precision, transport and operational fit |
| Product and adoption | Independent and institutional acceptance, target-platform reliability, validated workflows and measured economics | Qualified product/investment claim in the accepted scope |

These are distinctions between claims, not mandatory stopping points. Independent proposal and local prototype work continues whenever its inputs are available. A later-stage task cannot become a transitive proposal blocker. Open research may receive a negative/inconclusive disposition and a restricted claim instead of an invented theorem.

The final **proposal** acceptance review will check: (Q1) all three mandatory manuscript repairs against their coverage/depth contracts; (Q2) source and claim traceability and explicit negative findings; (Q3) a bounded prototype portfolio with actual versus proposed behavior distinguished and reproduced evidence for all claimed demonstrations; (Q4) a conditional investment argument and justified next-experiment design; (Q5) integrated sponsor/monograph consistency, compilation, references and rendered review. All five are still open at whole-program level. I02/F01 historical product-oriented rollups are not substituted for this proposal gate.
