With these conditions in place, we show that consistent estimation is possible from finite data.
The key tool is Lindenstrauss’s ergodic theorem [Lindenstrauss, 2001], which extends the law of
large numbers to ergodic distributions and group actions. We use Assumption 4.3 to ensure its
estimator can be computed from finite data.
