## Graphical mechanisms need explicit intervention and counterfactual contracts

The selected DoWhy GCM review retains twelve authenticated originals at the 4 October 2026 revision. GCM is a distinct candidate from the previously reviewed identification API. Its fitted mechanisms, parent order and query paths require their own scientific and operational contracts. Automatic mechanism selection predicts values on a supplied graph; it does not establish graph truth or noise independence.\citep{exec24_gcm_source,exec24_gcm_choice}

Intervention sampling and individual counterfactuals require different information. An authored system with outcome equal to twice action plus noise gives an individual untreated outcome of three when the observed action is one and outcome five; its population untreated mean can instead be zero. Two further authored models have identical randomized observed and intervention distributions but opposite individual counterfactual answers. A proposed worker must retain noise provenance and refuse unsupported abduction.\citep{exec24_gcm_choice}

Uncertainty depends on what is repeated. The simple query helper does not refit; the fit-and-compute helper samples 75 percent of rows without replacement by default. Exact finite examples distinguish that law from an ordinary bootstrap. Dictionary summaries additionally need fixed schemas and repetition IDs, because equal per-key counts can hide disjoint repetitions. Graph-falsification states are diagnostics rather than proof of a valid causal model.\citep{exec24_gcm_source,exec24_gcm_choice}

The decision is reference only, with conditional isolated integration after model, rights, environment and known-law public tests. Explicit positive-class coding preserves financial signs: an authored increase in default probability by one fifth across 1,000 accounts at 100 USD loss per default implies 20,000 USD additional expected loss. An illustrative 5,400 USD over 270 requested, 225 finite and 180 accepted results gives 20, 24 and 30 USD per result. These are stipulated examples, not observed economics. Native, visual and final acceptance remain open.\citep{exec24_gcm_choice}
