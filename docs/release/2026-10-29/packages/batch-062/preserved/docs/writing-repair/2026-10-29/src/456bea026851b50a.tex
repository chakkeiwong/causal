\chapter{What additional information buys}
\label{integrated:master_execution}

\begin{quote}\small
A causal banking service must return a decision quantity together with the information and uncertainty that make it defensible. Funding responses, sequential servicing, temporal relationships and verified customer histories reveal different limits on that information. A funding experiment uses supplied action randomization and independent calendars to remove a fitted-ratio mean shift. A nonlinear servicing extension evaluates a fixed observable policy using truthful state audits and supplied action probabilities. Both meet a preregistered minimum-coverage screen on selected synthetic grids with conservative 99\% critical values. A joint pair/calendar/gold-label experiment still fails finite calibration in important regimes. A corrected temporal graph union retains effects but can return the whole real line. A corrected sparse learner fails qualification at higher dimension or support. Full linked-outcome streaming agrees with its dense reference and survives real local process failures, without a consistent measured performance gain. These are research findings from specified experiments. Their use in a bank requires independent review, credible assignment and observation information, suitable operating performance and a defensible economic case.
\end{quote}

\section{One decision, several kinds of evidence}
A bank deciding whether to raise a deposit rate, change a collections policy or act on a discovered relationship needs a target with units and timing. It also needs an observation model: a row may not identify the customer whose balance matters; a public signal may omit the private state used by a historical decision maker; an apparent edge may permit several intervention effects. Computation cannot supply missing identity, assignment or exclusion information.

The experiments ask whether changing the available information improves
a stated banking comparison. A negative result can explain which information
is missing. An uncertainty set can be statistically defensible yet too wide
to distinguish a profitable action from a loss. Scientific validity and
useful precision therefore need separate attention.

Every table cell has 512 fresh Monte Carlo requests. Covers uses all requests, including refusals. MC lower is a simultaneous one-sided exact-binomial 95\% lower bound, Bonferroni-adjusted over all method/cell comparisons in the study. The minimum screen is .95. These screens do not prove uniform coverage or validate supplied scientific assumptions. Primary methods use frozen 99\% critical values or covariance regions; some challengers use 95\% critical values. Width and coverage have a disclosed tradeoff.

\section{Funding: change information to remove an error}
\subsection{Target, linked audits and pilot bias}
The target is a one-month ten-basis-point increase over $H=6$ future months. With two equally weighted strata,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 V&=\tfrac12(\theta_0+\theta_1)-C,\qquad \theta_s=w'\beta_s,\\
 w_h&=48\cdot10^6\{.002(.99)^h-(.001/12)1\{h=0\}\},\quad C=400{,}000.
\end{aligned}
\]
A balance unit is USD one million and the synthetic account has 48 branches. Future margin is discounted once. Response $2(.82)^h$ gives approximately USD 375,149; persistence .96 gives USD 751,749. These are reproducible accounts, not bank profits.

Let $F_t$ be the focal response and $Y_{t1},Y_{t2}$ candidate histories. A truthful identity audit $R_t$ has known inclusion $q_t>0$. Conditional on complete histories $\mathcal H$, assume $\E[R_t\mid\mathcal H]=q_t$ and set
\[
 B_t=(Y_{t1}+Y_{t2})/2,\qquad Z_t=B_t+R_t(F_t-B_t)/q_t.
\]
Then $\E[Z_t\mid\mathcal H]=F_t$, without division by identity rank $2p_s-1$. Each origin audit resolves a horizon vector, not seven independent labels. Truthful links are additional information beyond correctness counts.

For the inherited independent-pilot ratio, define $d_m=\widehat m-m$ and $d_\ell=\widehat\ell-\ell$. With innovation variance one, the conditional population slope is
\[
 \frac{\theta_s+\E[d_md_\ell\mid P]}{1+\E[d_m^2\mid P]}.
\]
A residual calendar variance does not estimate this pilot-dependent mean shift. Orthogonal-score product conditions support a growing-pilot argument \cite{dml2018}; the cited iid result does not prove finite dependent-calendar calibration here.

\subsection{Known randomization and independent calendars}
Suppose the true assignment is supplied as $A=m(X)+v$, with $\E[v\mid X]=0$ and $\E[v^2\mid X]=1$. The synthetic law uses $m(W)=.5W+.2(W^2-1)$. An independent pilot produces an outcome prediction $\widehat\ell$ and scalar control $\widehat b$. Define
\[
 G_t=v_t\{w'Z_t-\widehat\ell(X_t)\}-\widehat b(v_t^2-1).
\]
Conditional on the pilot, $\E[v\widehat\ell(X)]=0$ and $\E[v^2-1]=0$. Exogeneity and truthful inclusion give $\E[G_t\mid P]=\theta$. The pilot affects variance, but not the mean. The fitted-action error and ratio denominator disappear because assignment information is supplied. The study has equally sampled strata and a common response; unequal sampling would need explicit weights.

With $K$ independent calendars and $L$ consecutive origins each, let $U_k=L^{-1}\sum_tG_{kt}$. Then
\[
 \widehat V=K^{-1}\sum_k U_k-C,\qquad
 \widehat s^2=\frac1{K(K-1)}\sum_k(U_k-\overline U)^2.
\]
The variance contains all within-calendar covariance. Its conditional mean is constant, so no pilot-dependent mean variance is omitted. An iid-calendar CLT as $K\to\infty$ justifies the limit; Student critical values are asymptotically equivalent, not exact pivots for these non-Gaussian means.

The frozen design has $K=24$, $L=50$ (12 for the short cell), a fourfold pilot and 99\% Student intervals. Adjacent blocks of one bank series are not independent calendars, nor are branches sharing a national shock. This requirement has real feasibility and cost implications.

\section{Persistent maps, common dates and imperfect gold truth}
\subsection{One joint sampling law}
After multiplying outcomes by randomized signed actions, let focal and companion responses be
\[
 F_{it}=\beta+U_i+C_t+E_{it},\qquad D_{it}=\delta+V_i+C_t+J_{it}.
\]
Pair effects are iid across pairs; $C_t$ is an independent stationary Gaussian AR(1); cell disturbances are independent. Candidate order persists within a pair. A Bernoulli pair audit $R_i$ has known $q$, and its label independently flips with probability $e<1/2$. An independent representative sample of $M$ gold labels estimates $e$. This is a specific experiment, not arbitrary multiway dependence.

Let $\xi_i=1$ for a correct audit label and $-1$ for a flipped label. At the true $e$, $W_i=R_i\xi_i/[q(1-2e)]$ has mean one and second moment $a=1/[q(1-2e)^2]$. For $d=(\beta-\delta)/2$, the corrected score has decomposition
\begin{align*}
 Z_{it}&=\beta+P_i+C_t+H_{it},\\
 P_i&=(U_i+V_i)/2+W_i(U_i-V_i)/2+d(W_i-1),\\
 H_{it}&=(E_{it}+J_{it})/2+W_i(E_{it}-J_{it})/2.
\end{align*}
Pair and cell projections are uncorrelated. With pair variance $\sigma_p^2$ and cell variance $\sigma_e^2$,
\[
 \Var(P)=\sigma_p^2(1+a)/2+d^2(a-1),\quad
 \Var(H)=\sigma_e^2(1+a)/2.
\]
Thus the known-error variance of the mean is
\[
 \frac{\Var(P)}N+\frac{\sigma_c^2}{T^2}\left[T+2\sum_{k=1}^{T-1}(T-k)\rho^k\right]+\frac{\Var(H)}{NT}.
\]
Gold estimation adds $g^2e(1-e)/M$ at first order, $g=(\beta-\delta)/(1-2e)$. Curvature and random-derivative terms require a fixed rank margin and comparable pair/date/gold growth; their constants diverge near $e=.5$.

Persistent audits contribute to pair variance and cannot be averaged away merely by adding dates. Independent origin audits instead add a diagonal cell component. At $q=1,e=0$ the expression reduces to complete truth. Binary enumeration and direct Toeplitz covariance sums independently check the limits.

\subsection{Feasible precision still fails}
Row-mean variance and date HAC each count cell interaction. Subtracting its double-demeaned estimate once addresses that duplication under this law. It is not a generic dependent-panel repair. Rank uncertainty can cause refusal; Bartlett calendar variance remains biased at high finite persistence.

The reported monetary target is $1000\beta$ USD; interval widths are USD. The challenger retains the inherited double-counted interaction and a 95\% critical value.

\section{Nonlinear servicing with a supplied private logger}
\subsection{State, signal, action and reward}
The selected17 extension adds a nonlinear action reward to the inherited action-variance family:
\begin{align*}
 S_{t+1}&=\rho S_t+\gamma\tanh(S_t)+bA_t+\sqrt{q_{A_t}}\epsilon_{t+1},\\
 O_t&=S_t+\sqrt{v_o}\nu_t,\\
 p_t(1\mid S_t,O_t)&=.05+.9\operatorname{logistic}(\lambda_0+\lambda_sS_t+\lambda_oO_t),\\
 R_t&=\eta_0+\eta_sS_t+\eta_aA_t+\eta_{sa}S_tA_t+
 \eta_{\tanh}A_t\tanh(S_t)+\sqrt{v_R}\zeta_t.
\end{align*}
The private state precedes signal, historical action and reward. A deployed policy observes $O_t$, not $S_t$. Representative truthful full-state audits fix state scale/orientation; logger probabilities are actual supplied records. With full support and positive action probabilities, the transition/reward regressors are linearly independent and their Gaussian conditional laws identify this finite family. A noisy audit or guessed logger is a different experiment.

The complete likelihood multiplies initial, observation, Bernoulli action, reward and transition densities. Hidden-state likelihood fitting remains an efficiency alternative; this estimator uses extra logger information instead. Mechanism replacement supplies the intervention interpretation \cite{pearl2009}; the new numerical/inferential composition is project work.

\subsection{Sequential weighting and uncertainty}
For a policy chosen independently of evaluation,
\[
 W_t=\prod_{j\le t}\frac{1\{A_j=\pi_j(O_j)\}}{p_j(A_j\mid S_j,O_j)},\qquad
 V(\pi)=1000\E\left[\sum_{t=0}^2.97^t W_tR_t\right].
\]
Integrating each historical action cancels its denominator and replaces that action by the policy. This sequential change of measure covers the nonlinear transition and reward under the supplied true logger. It remains valid with a misspecified parametric transition if logger and audit truth remain correct.

One trajectory contributes one discounted weighted reward. Its sample variance retains within-path dependence. The fixed horizon and probability floor bound weights by $.05^{-3}$; Gaussian moments give finite variance. The trajectory CLT motivates 99\% Student intervals, not an exact finite $t$ law. Poor overlap can still make convergence slow. Thresholds $(0,.2,.4)$ are independently frozen; no unknown optimal value is inferred.

Widths are USD per trajectory. The challenger is the same weighting estimator with a 95\%, rather than 99\%, interval.

\section{Temporal discovery: retain the class before answering}
\subsection{A stationary finite temporal law}
Let $X_t=(Z_t,A_t,Y_t)'$ satisfy
\[
 X_t=rX_{t-1}+\epsilon_t,\qquad r\in\{0,.4,.8\},
\]
with invariant Gaussian initialization and iid innovations. Innovation structure is
\[
 z=e_z,\qquad a=kz+l_au+e_a,\qquad y=\tau a+cz+l_yu+e_y.
\]
The latent root and errors are independent. The instantaneous intervention coefficient is $\tau$. In structural form the lag matrix is $r(I-B)$: a real restriction, not an arbitrary VAR. Unknown continuous lag and long-run interventions are different questions.

For every candidate $r$, form $E_t(r)=X_t-rX_{t-1}$. At the true candidate these are iid Gaussian innovations. Nine simultaneous variance bands---three coordinate variances and six signed pair sums/differences---give covariance inclusion by chi-square inversion and Bonferroni. Propagate each covariance box through all compatible graphs and union over all lag candidates.

Only inclusion at the true candidate is needed. Wrong-lag data need not be iid, and no selected lag is falsely treated as fixed. The finite innovation classes contain the inherited 40 broad latent structures or eight encouragement/sufficiency structures per lag. Graphs are removed only when necessary restrictions cannot intersect their covariance region. On true-law inclusion the true graph and effect survive.

With valid encouragement exclusion, $c=0$ and the latent root does not cause $Z$, so $\Cov(Z,Y)/\Cov(Z,A)=\tau$. Interval ratio propagation imposes no positive first-stage floor and preserves disconnected or unbounded sets. Broad latent models admit equal-covariance witnesses with arbitrary effect, giving $\R$. Extra scientific information must exclude those alternatives; selecting a convenient graph does not.

Widths are outcome/action units, conditional on finite returned sets. The challenger selects one lag before propagating its graph class.

\section{A learner whose code matches its argument}
The residualized IV score is
\[
 \E[(Z-m_Z(X))\{Y-m_Y(X)-\theta(A-m_A(X))\}]=0.
\]
Independent-training nuisance errors shift the conditional moment by $\E[\delta_Z(\delta_Y-\theta\delta_A)]$. This is the inherited orthogonal-score principle \cite{dml2018}; dependent separation and actual selection need their own proof.

The corrected procedure has two outer calendar blocks separated by a gap. Inside each training block a further separated split selects a shrinking lasso grid. Inner training controls standardization; selected models are refit using only outer training data. Evaluation labels never tune penalties. Bartlett products stay within evaluation folds, never across the omitted gap. Actual feature/support overrides are honored.

For fixed dimension, a correct linear nuisance, positive Gram eigenvalues and fixed geometric mixing give root-sample moment/coefficient rates. A finite shrinking grid preserves that rate, including at selection boundaries. Products are then second order. A sufficiently increasing gap and growing covariance window supply a conditional asymptotic route. Growing dimension additionally needs restricted-eigenvalue and approximation/product-rate control. Prediction accuracy alone proves none of these. The grid has 20 or 60 controls, support three or eight, 640 origins and ordinarily a 40-origin gap.

Widths are outcome/action units. The challenger fits an unpenalized dense reference on the same permitted training folds.

\section{Keeping uncertainty visible in the reported result}
Each A-D result carries actual bounds or a refusal reason, units, assumptions and capability disposition. Finite intervals, disconnected sets, whole lines, empty sets and unavailable computations remain distinct. Computed sets without bounds reject. Null endpoints denote infinity only in the explicit extended-real schema, never zero or absent evidence.

The full linked method streams both pilot and evaluation pseudo-outcomes. Chronological pilot halves are compressed into augmented QR factors and online target moments. Validation uses the same fixed penalty losses, followed by full-pilot refit; bounded passes then compute estimates and covariance. Twelve dense-equivalence cases span chunks 1/37/257, low audit, identity rank and persistence. Relative tolerance $10^{-9}$ and absolute USD $10^{-6}$ assess numerical equivalence, not bank usefulness.

Complete workloads include input generation, tuning/refit, inference, serialization and total process memory. At 120,000 evaluation and 480,000 pilot origins, dense and streamed runs take about 1.08 and 1.71 seconds, with roughly 409 MiB peak RSS each. The streaming fit is slower. Input generation dominates memory; reduced auxiliary storage does not imply a large total RSS reduction. No consistent performance advantage is claimed, and a dense fallback is retained.

Recovery binds code/input hashes, seed and request count. Local locking, fsync, atomic record rename and a final manifest prevent partial publication. Real process deaths during fitting, a partially written checkpoint and final publication recover the same 12 requests on restart, replay without duplication and reject changed identities. This is local single-process evidence; multi-host coordination and bank service levels need their platform operators.

\section{Financial value and unresolved evidence}
If $\Delta L$ is held-out improvement in bank decision loss over an accepted workflow, then
\[
 \mathrm{Net\ value}=\Delta L-C_{\rm audit}-C_{\rm calendar}-C_{\rm integration}
 -C_{\rm compute}-C_{\rm review}-C_{\rm operation}.
\]
The research supplies synthetic precision and local computing measurements, not bank values for these costs. Linked histories, correctness labels and full-state audits are different services. A label count is not a market price; a faster fit is not ROI. Width, availability and decision loss need matched information and actual prices.

Verified customer histories provide a restricted way to learn funding
responses, and the nonlinear servicing study supports one specified policy
comparison. Several causal graphs can still fit the same observations.
Assignment, audit inclusion and label accuracy require substantive evidence.
Joint dependence and high-dimensional learning retain adverse findings, while
local computing results do not establish bank operating performance. Useful
precision and the full financial case depend on the institution and decision.

A bank study would need the relevant decision makers and data owners
to establish assignment rules, observation quality, required precision and
costs. The information requested should be specific to the proposed study.

\section*{From research output to a bounded bank decision}
A deployment question must first specify the unit, decision time, available records and loss from a wrong action. The funding construction requires certified randomization and link inclusion, plus independent calendars whose economic shocks do not overlap. If those inputs cannot be supplied, its calibrated synthetic interval is not a substitute for a bank answer. The servicing construction requires a frozen observable policy, truthful representative state audits and the actual historical action probabilities; otherwise the reported fixed-policy value has a different target or can be severely biased.

For discovery, a bank must specify the scientific restrictions that remove observationally equivalent intervention effects. If its accepted restrictions still permit the whole real line, the service should return that set and withhold a directional recommendation. For shared calendars and dependent learning, the present finite failures require a new variance design or stronger information before qualification. A platform may transmit these limitations faithfully, but cannot turn them into positive scientific evidence.

Finally, independent reviewers must reconstruct each claimed regime and bank owners must measure useful precision, decision loss and complete acquisition and operating costs. Only a bounded release meeting those conditions should enter an institutional acceptance and investment decision. An adverse scientific or economic conclusion is a legitimate output of this program.
