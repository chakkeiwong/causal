# Writing standard and remaining gaps after the developmental rewrite

29 October 2026. Assessment of the delivered developmental rewrite, SHA-256 e718978d23cdf458912dd1d3f5904453dc8aefe8b7ac783cdfa5cfe89cb90e2f. This is a focused author assessment of the standard and remaining editorial work, not an independent review of every page. No manuscript content was changed for this assessment.

## Verdict

The current monograph does not yet meet the writing standard consistently across the whole book. The rewrite materially improves the economic opening, examples, selected comparisons and investment conclusion. It also protects the substantial scholarly material. It has not completed a chapter-by-chapter developmental and prose revision of the retained comparative and operating corpus.

The earlier statement that the plan was executed describes delivery of its candidate and preservation checks. It does not establish that the writing standard has been met. Calling all four opening journeys complete needs qualification: they are complete worked illustrations of particular decision problems; only the new deposit case carries a fitted sample estimate and sampling interval through to a callable/CLI financial report. The other three demonstrate exact supplied-law arguments. Their wider estimation and inference treatments exist elsewhere and still require better integration.

## The standard

The binding standard is stated in docs/program/master-program.md, lines 22–42, and docs/program/scientific-editorial-repair.md, lines 8–71. It is a developed scholarly monograph and argued banking proposal. FinMarketing supplies the benchmark of sustained mathematical exposition; The Economist supplies a reference for clear economic language. Neither supplies a length target, a requirement to imitate a voice, or a substitute for scientific correctness.

A suitably prepared economist or quantitative reader should be able to reconstruct the argument from the book: what decision is at issue; what the symbols and information dates mean; what assumptions identify an effect or set; why the estimator and uncertainty calculation answer that question; what alternatives would change the answer; and what this implies for action or investment. An unresolved or nonidentified conclusion can satisfy the standard when its derivation and consequences are fully explained.

The concrete requirements are:

1. A cumulative argument. Each chapter has a clear question and an identifiable contribution to the book's conclusion. Definitions and conclusions have authoritative locations; later passages develop, apply or challenge them.
2. Developed mathematical explanation. Introduce economic primitives and notation before using them; explain the assumptions and the steps from identification through estimation and inference. Distinguish a theorem, an authored derivation, a numerical illustration and an implementation result. Retain real proofs, counterexamples and limitations.
3. Comparative scholarship. Explain what serious alternatives solve, where their assumptions or targets differ, what the inspected evidence supports, and why one route is selected or remains unresolved. Naming packages and listing cautions are insufficient.
4. Substantive banking applications. Keep the intervention, population, horizon, units and information clock consistent. Connect uncertainty and sensitivity to a financial decision. A supplied-law example should say what must be learned when those quantities are unknown.
5. Natural, precise prose. Lead with economic or scientific reasoning, use connected paragraphs and purposeful transitions, explain specialized terms, and keep authoring/program status out of the product narrative. Necessary qualifications remain; repeated generic warnings should not carry the argument.
6. Reader-facing evidence and presentation. Attach source support to the claim, make empirical/generated/proposed status intelligible, use useful diagrams and tables, and provide functional cross-references and legible citations. Inspect the rendered result as well as compilation diagnostics.
7. Preservation of substance. Consolidation must account for the destination of each important claim, assumption, derivation, alternative and adverse result. Literal repetition is not necessary to preserve substance, but unaccounted removal is unacceptable.

## Remaining gaps, with evidence and completion tests

### 1. The complete book still needs developmental integration — highest priority

The main argument ends at printed page 224; Parts V–X continue through page 1747 before the bibliography. This length is not itself a defect. The problem is that multiple generations of synthesis remain as separate conclusions. Software recommendations appear in Chapters 22, 28 and 63; assistance conclusions in Chapters 23, 30 and 77; investment arguments recur in Chapters 26, 27, 31, 101 and 102.

Direct inspection confirms repeated reasoning, not just similar titles. Chapter 63 repeats the offer-projection argument and selective-reuse recommendation already developed earlier. Chapter 77 again recommends formulation, diagrams and questionnaire comparison after Chapters 23 and 30. Some repeated passages contain unique examples and must be retained in an appropriate location.

Completion test: assign each principal question one authoritative treatment; merge repeated conclusions while carrying every distinct argument, example and qualification into the relevant chapter or named supporting treatment. The reader should not have to reconcile multiple summaries to discover the current recommendation. Maintain the deletion ledger and claim-level destinations during consolidation.

### 2. The four applications have unequal depth and remain partly disconnected from their advanced treatments — high priority

The deposit chapter provides a specified sample, estimator, interval, accounting map and callable/CLI result. Servicing supplies exact g-formula and weighting calculations, then describes what happens when the law must be estimated. Discovery supplies an exact covariance and compatible models; sampling uncertainty is discussed without a fitted discovery-to-effect study there. Observation correction supplies known K; uncertainty from learning K is discussed rather than carried through the opening calculation. These are useful examples, not errors.

See docs/developmental-rewrite/2026-10-29/journeys.tex, particularly lines 173–217, 246–277 and 289–330. The independent-household deposit illustration also does not replace the required structural/dependent funding treatment, which remains elsewhere in the book.

Completion test: develop each journey's stated scope through the relevant existing estimation, diagnostics, sensitivity and decision material in a coherent reading route. Explain what can be estimated, what remains a set, and what must be refused. Reuse the substantial existing dependent-funding, longitudinal and observation results. A new experiment is needed only if a claim requires evidence absent from the existing work; prose cannot manufacture it.

### 3. Comparison tables do not finish the underlying comparative argument — high priority

The new 17-area matrix is a useful selection summary. Several entries are explicitly conditional design choices, and the wider language/commercial census remains incomplete. A defensible unresolved recommendation is acceptable; an unexplained unresolved comparison is not. The table must be the conclusion of an accessible argument that explains the decisive assumptions, method differences and strongest alternative. At present, much of that reasoning remains distributed among long package-specific treatments.

The same applies to language assistance. The new task synthesis and ORCA discussion are stronger, but they do not establish that the wider retained literature has been reconciled task by task. The inherited count of 82 inspected and 31 unread originals is a coverage description, not a quality score. Material omissions need explicit dispositions against the 7 October 2026 cutoff.

Completion test: for each central recommendation, provide a traceable comparison of target, assumptions, evidence, alternative and reason for the choice. Identify the missing source or result that could overturn it. A source-supported prototype recommendation can meet this test without a new runtime benchmark; a superiority claim cannot.

### 4. Chapter-level exposition, transitions and notation have not received a complete consistency pass — high priority

The rewrite's exact edit ledger documents selected target and title corrections, extensive citation spacing and relocations. That is good evidence of preservation. It is not evidence that every retained chapter has been rewritten for logic, notation and reader understanding. The strong graphical, IV, influence-function and longitudinal derivations must be retained; the old diagnosis that the book lacks mathematics would be incorrect.

Completion test: read each chapter in its final position. Verify its premise, symbol definitions, assumptions, derivational steps, transitions and conclusion. Reconcile duplicated terminology and changing units or horizons explicitly. Resolve only actual gaps; no new proof error is asserted by this assessment. A separate correctness review of the most consequential arguments remains desirable and must not be confused with this exposition pass.

Preserve substantive institutional limits and the evidence for them. These are author-executable editorial corrections, not dependencies on bank approval.

### 6. The prose still shifts between exposition and instructions — confirmed in sampled passages; full extent unmeasured

The selection and retained comparison passages often use commands such as integrate, retain, reject and compare. They can be useful recommendations, but repeated sequences of them read as implementation briefs. Repeated reminders that a result is not bank validation, not identification or not a universal solution can dominate the development when they are not attached to a new specific distinction.

Completion test: turn the core of each section into a connected explanation of why the result follows and why the alternative changes it. Keep precise limitations next to the affected claim and cross-reference established restrictions instead of repeating generic disclaimers. Retain distinctive details and genuine negative results. This requires paragraph-level editing, not a banned-word substitution.

### 7. Navigation, citations and final visual quality need a reader's pass — remaining presentation work

The final contents has 102 numbered chapters. It provides a usable main route, but repeated conclusions and overlapping chapter roles still weaken navigation. All 444 inherited inputs being reachable is a preservation result, not proof that a reader can readily locate the authoritative treatment.

Twenty-one scholarly author fields were repaired. Some displayed software/project citations remain cumbersome; for example, printed page 1065 displays a compressed contributor label and repeated project-year suffixes. Traceability should remain, but the prose need not force the reader to decode project evidence identifiers as though they were scholarly author names.

The final PDF has no unresolved references, overfull boxes, empty pages or out-of-page text in the checks. Fresh visual inspection of this final edition remains pending. The recorded underfull warnings are not themselves evidence of bad appearance; they require judgment where they affect readability.

Completion test: verify chapter roles, precise forward/backward references, first-use definitions, meaningful captions, source display and representative dense pages. Use the prepared previews in a fresh session, keeping the inherited image-safety limit. Mechanical page bounds do not establish aesthetic or editorial acceptance.

## What is not a prerequisite for meeting the writing standard

Authentic bank adoption, signed institutional approval, production deployment, actual vendor prices and a new live-model experiment are not blanket prerequisites for a rigorous proposal. Their absence limits the claims the book may make. It does not excuse unfinished integration, unexplained mathematics or weak comparative synthesis. Conversely, improving prose does not establish these capabilities.

There is no requirement to force every causal question to a positive decision or single winning package. A carefully derived nonidentification result, an adverse experiment or a conditional recommendation can meet the scholarly standard.

## Practical acceptance test

For every major question, a suitably prepared reader should be able to explain the target, assumptions, reasoning, evidence, strongest alternative and decision consequence without reconstructing the project's publication history. The reader should know which argument is authoritative and what observation would change the conclusion. Every substantial consolidation must still pass a semantic deletion audit.

The immediate priority is therefore a chapter-by-chapter consolidation and exposition pass, starting with repeated software/assistance/investment conclusions and the links from the four applications to their advanced treatments. Final visual/human review is necessary, but it is not the only remaining work.
