D.1        Pilot Study for CAR Weight Selection
The weights in the CAR composite (Equation 5) were determined through a pilot
study. Two domain experts (one in AI/NLP, one in causal inference methodology)
independently rated 24 model responses (6 per domain) on a 1–5 Likert scale for
“overall causal reasoning quality.” The inter-rater agreement was substantial (Cohen’s
𝜅 = 0.72).
    We tested 15 weight combinations (varying each weight from 0.2 to 0.5 in steps
of 0.05, subject to summing to 1.0) and selected the combination maximising rank
correlation between CAR and the averaged expert ratings:


       Table D5: Top-5 weight combinations by Kendall’s 𝜏 with expert ratings.
𝑤CIR                    𝑤CLP                    𝑤CCR                     Kendall’s 𝜏

0.40                     0.35                    0.25                        0.78
0.35                     0.40                    0.25                        0.76
0.35                     0.35                    0.30                        0.74
0.40                     0.30                    0.30                        0.73
0.45                     0.30                    0.25                        0.71




   The selected weights (0.40, 0.35, 0.25) achieve the highest 𝜏 = 0.78, and the
top three combinations yield similar correlations, suggesting robustness to moderate
weight perturbations.
