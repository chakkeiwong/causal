## Standard

A prepared economist or quantitative reader must reconstruct the decision, information clock, model and assumptions, identification or set, estimation, uncertainty and sensitivity, monetary consequences, alternatives and recommendation. FinMarketing sets the standard of developed scholarly exposition; clear economic prose is the language standard. Neither page count nor file integrity establishes it. A developed negative conclusion can satisfy it.

The seven writing gaps are integration, connected application depth, comparative scholarship, chapter exposition, stale administration, repetitive prose and navigation/presentation. Author repairs, human editorial acceptance, independent scientific review, prototype qualification and bank adoption are distinct. Missing bank prices or live-model trials do not excuse unfinished writing.
