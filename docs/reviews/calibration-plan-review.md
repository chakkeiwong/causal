# Pre-execution review of the V9 plan

10 October 2026. Author adversarial review, not independent acceptance.

The plan is executable after the following scope and design resolutions.

1. The V8 larger funding design holds its pilot at 1,600 dates while increasing
   the evaluation sample. This is a plausible error source, not a diagnosed
   cause. Compare known action/outcome means separately, the exact conditional
   pilot target and several independent pilot sizes on common evaluation draws.
2. Stationary nuisance formulas must include the whole pre-origin balance
   history, the nonlinear driver square and natural future assignment. A
   one-step conditional mean is not the horizon-specific outcome mean. Derive
   the geometric recursions and check them independently before final studies.
3. Orthogonality gives a product remainder, not immunity to a fixed pilot.
   Derive the explicit ratio target and relative pilot/evaluation rate; retain
   finite-sample bias and audit-inversion contributions rather than attributing
   all error to the covariance window.
4. Joint regression is justified only for the prespecified fixed-dimensional
   dictionary with appropriate rank and causal innovation assumptions. Dense
   partial regression and grouped QR must agree. No general machine-learning
   qualification follows from ordinary least squares.
5. Finite response horizon does not by itself truncate score covariance.
   Prove the additional innovation/filtration condition for this mechanism.
   Incorrect action means or endogenous innovations violate it. Keep those
   adverse laws and preserve an explicit refusal for nonpositive variance.
6. Untapered covariance need not be positive semidefinite in finite samples.
   Qualify the prespecified scalar financial contrast only; do not silently
   advertise a valid full response covariance or repair negative variance by
   clipping it to a favorable number.
7. Independent identity-audit uncertainty must propagate through the inverse
   map. Preserve the existing Wilson rank guard and explicitly mark weak
   information; more dates do not repair near-singular audit information.
8. Use fresh diagnostic and final seeds. Register original, larger, persistent,
   weak-linkage and violated-assumption laws. Do not pick the best final
   bandwidth, discard unavailable requests or interpret Monte Carlo agreement
   as a theorem. A pilot repair requires a preserved failure and fresh final run.
9. QR-only fitting is insufficient for a memory claim: score and lag accounting
   must also be chunked. Verify centered cross-boundary lag products against a
   dense independent implementation, including awkward chunk boundaries. Record
   raw mapped-input memory and complete repeated process cost.
10. The four broad recommendations cannot all receive institutional acceptance
    in this workspace. The concrete funding/finite-dictionary extension can be
    executed; arbitrary model generalization remains separate research, and
    bank/platform/workflow acceptance requires external evidence. Record exact
    missing inputs and rejection criteria without commissioning anyone.

Disposition: execute the bounded plan with these requirements. No present
coverage improvement, performance advantage or independent acceptance is assumed.
