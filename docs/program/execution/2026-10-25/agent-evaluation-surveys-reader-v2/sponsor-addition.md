## Evaluate scientific answers separately from completed agent workflows

Two retained preprints connect agent architecture to practical evaluation. The chemistry survey by Ramos, Collison and White organizes observation/action loops, decision modules and tools while discussing unclear agent success criteria and validation limits. Zhou and colleagues' vulnerability detection/repair survey identifies dated search-scope limitations involving heuristic labels, missing repair tests, granularity and deployment. These are the inspected preprints' own frameworks and syntheses; the underlying studies' performance is not adopted transitively, and their journal-original equivalence remains unverified. [@exec25_cow_chemistry; @exec25_cow_vulnerability]

A bank-facing agent should preserve the business target before generating a graph or calling an estimator. In an authored example, USD70 of incremental gross recovery with USD80 action cost is a USD10 net loss. An answer about gross recovery alone can be computationally correct and economically wrong. A second example assigns proxy scores 0.9 and 0.8 to actions worth minus USD10 and plus USD20; optimizing the proxy createsUSD30 regret. These are finite design witnesses,not observed bank losses or model error rates. [@exec25_cow_reference]

The public qualification plan separates translation,graph and identification correctness,valid refusals,code/tool execution and decision utility. Correlated judges cannot be treated as independent checks,passing sample tests cannot substitute for a specification,and agreement with noisy benchmark labels cannot establish truth. For 100 requests, 80 completed runs and 60 accepted scientific answers with USD200 total cost, the cost per accepted answer is USD10/3 rather than USD2.50 per completion. This constructed accounting motivates complete denominators before expensive bank trials. [@exec25_cow_reference]

Versioned CLI/MCP requests should retain the target,information clocks,assumptions,evidence,worker scope and resource budget;results should bind artifacts,diagnostics,refusals and attempt histories. This remains a proposal,not implemented repository integration. The review supplies a route to public experiments without claiming universal causal identification,contamination-free benchmarks,bank transfer or measured savings. [@exec25_cow_reference]
