## DataRobot propensity reference and causal weighting obligations

The official readmission overview and its genuine pinned notebook distinguish treatment prediction from subsequent inverse-probability weighting. The March2025 notebook revision is authenticated to its Git blob and inspected without execution. Its explanatory text explicitly describes in-sample propensity predictions; the displayed outcome percentages normalize weights within treatment arm. Those facts do not establish a general causal uncertainty guarantee.\citep{exec13_ch_datarobot,exec25_datarobot_notebook,exec25_datarobot_choice}

The proposal retains this workflow as a reference. Missing root accelerator rights, data rights and an exact entitled client/server/model environment defer adoption and porting. A conditional integration needs pretreatment feature review, class and row identity, positivity handling, a declared target and justified uncertainty. Exact authored examples show that multiplying all weights in one normalized arm leaves both its mean and weight-based effective sample size unchanged. Public/generated qualification should test known causal truth, near-positivity failures, stale models and missing predictions before expensive bank work.\citep{exec25_datarobot_notebook,exec25_datarobot_choice}

No vendor run, benchmark or actual price is claimed. A hypothetical USD7,200 complete cost is USD60 per attempt, USD80 per completion and USD120 per admissible result. Predictive model quality, causal validity, execution completion and economic value remain separate acceptance decisions.\citep{exec25_datarobot_choice}
