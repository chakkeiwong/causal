## Statistical design

For each of two strata reduce each candidate response curve by the *fixed*
financial vector. Form simultaneous normal/HAC intervals for these four
observable means, allocating half of the total error by Bonferroni. Invert
the exact finite binomial tails for each independent audit proportion using
the other half. Zero audit observations give [0,1], not a fitted zero-error
calibration. Project the Cartesian confidence region through the two-candidate
mixing equations and then the fixed-weight financial sum. A union-bound
inclusion proof preserves coverage through rank loss. The audit component is
finite-sample; the dependent learned-response component remains asymptotic.
No uniform near-unit-root, increasing-horizon or arbitrary-learner claim is made.

Derive extrema on each side of p=1/2 analytically. At rank one, only a sum is
identified. Do not use a bounded plotting grid, silently select one side, or
report the convex hull in place of a disconnected set. A missing audit can
yield an uninformative set; a missing evaluation stratum cannot support the
observable-response CLT and is rejected. Regular delta inference remains a
distinct procedure, including its availability rule and conditional limits.

Use already preserved primary sources for the weak-IV, score, covariance,
linkage and confidence-region foundations. The particular projection and
binomial-tail derivations are identified as project constructions, not
misattributed to an existing source. No new literature acquisition is needed
unless reading reveals a source-dependent claim that the archive cannot support.

## Pre-final pilot refinement

The first three-draw pilot per case showed that projecting a rectangular
region for separate candidate means discards their strong common-shock
covariance. The mixing matrix diagonalizes into portfolio average and half
difference: the latter equals (2p−1) times half the true effect difference.
Before final draws, select simultaneous bands in these fixed coordinates
and retain the candidate-mean rectangle as a same-data conservative challenger.
Neither rectangular construction dominates for every covariance law. The
original pilot and all bound inputs are preserved in
`docs/studies/linkage-candidate-box-pilot.zip`; no final study outcomes
informed this amendment. Total error and all final regimes/seeds remain fixed.
