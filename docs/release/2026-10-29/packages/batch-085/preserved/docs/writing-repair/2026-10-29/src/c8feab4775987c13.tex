\hypertarget{choosing-a-commercial-latent-model-component}{%
\section{Choosing a commercial latent-model
component}\label{choosing-a-commercial-latent-model-component}}

\label{exec14:commercial-latent-choice}

The practical choice for a bank is a component that answers a specified
question under an explicit information law. A supplied borrower-risk
model, an induced customer segmentation, a discovered class of causal
graphs and an intervention effect are different purchases. Their outputs
can support one another only through an additional modeling argument.
This distinction also prevents the proposed library from taking credit
for functionality that an established commercial system already
documents while our canonical interface still reports an unsupported
operation.

Consider a lending team that wants to assess a concession before the
next credit-loss period. A documented latent-class model can describe
unobserved heterogeneity in repayment patterns. It need not determine
whether a concession caused a change in loss, because the same private
information can influence both the concession and the outcome. The
observational-equivalence and noisy-proxy examples in the preceding
commercial discussion continue to apply. A proposal to combine an
existing fitter with explicit causal assumptions may be useful; it must
not advertise the fitter as a solution to the missing information.

\hypertarget{six-concrete-alternatives-and-five-unresolved-comparisons}{%
\subsection{Six concrete alternatives and five unresolved
comparisons}\label{six-concrete-alternatives-and-five-unresolved-comparisons}}

The inspected GeNIe manual identifies its family as 5.0.R2. Its separate
SMILE programmer manual identifies 2.2.4.R1. Those are two documentation
identities, not evidence that a particular pair of installed binaries
has been tested together. GeNIe documents EM fitting for an existing
network, including initialization, latent variables and discrete
missing-data cases. It explicitly keeps the structure fixed. The stated
continuous multivariate-Gaussian implementation does not accept missing
values on that parameter-learning page. The structure-learning interface
has its own data restrictions. A reuse decision should therefore begin
with a fixed discrete model and its explicit observation law, rather
than infer a universal latent-discovery capability from the EM name.
\citep{exec13_genie_readme,exec13_smile_intro,exec13_latent_genie_structure,exec13_latent_genie_parameters}

BayesiaLab provides two substantive representation alternatives: a Naive
latent factor fit with soft class probabilities and the documented
Multinet procedure with class-specific trees and hard imputation. Their
likelihoods, assignments and downstream uses are not interchangeable. A
comparison should retain the number of classes, variables used to create
them, conditional-independence restrictions and the date each variable
becomes available. A segment trained using the eventual loan outcome
cannot silently become pre-concession information. The inspected help is
a mutable snapshot without a pinned current numbered edition; that
limitation applies to the technical comparison even though the primary
text is available.
\citep{exec13_latent_bayesia_data,exec13_latent_bayesia_variables}

The Stata latent-class example supplies a particularly useful
conventional baseline. Its \texttt{gsem} specification fixes two
classes, fits class-specific response probabilities, and distinguishes
marginal class proportions from posterior membership and thresholded
assignments. Its printed initialization and final-fitting diagnostics
also show why one convergence flag cannot describe all fitting stages.
The example carries a 2025 copyright and release-19 data reference. It
establishes the demonstrated procedure, without establishing a purchased
edition's current platform or automation entitlement.
\citep{exec13_latent_stata_lca}

Mplus should not be reduced to a generic clustering competitor. The
guide linked as Version 8 includes a randomized-trial complier-average
causal-effect example, with compliance information partially observed
and a noncomplier treatment slope fixed at zero. A bank evaluating
randomized assistance offers with incomplete take-up has a direct
methodological comparison here. The offered-treatment effect and the
effect among compliers remain different targets. The same chapter
documents mixture models and complex-sampling options, which deserve
matched comparisons under their stated restrictions. The required
licensed option package and current binary behavior still need separate
evidence. \citep{exec13_latent_mplus}

Netica's inspected help describes counting, EM and gradient fitting of
supplied conditional probability tables. Its TAN discussion is expressly
motivated by prediction and diagnosis, including outgoing links from the
target variable. Those arrows cannot be imported as identified causal
directions. The reference to TAN's introduction in version 5.00 is
historical feature information; it does not establish the current
installed release. Netica remains a candidate supplied-model comparator
after the prior, objective, initialization and edition are fixed.
\citep{exec13_latent_netica_algorithms,exec13_latent_netica_structure}

The archived SAS/STAT 14.1 CALIS documentation supplies a further
baseline for specified measurement and structural models. It documents
multiple modeling languages, parameter restrictions, missing-data
estimation and diagnostic outputs. These are capabilities that our
current canonical service does not provide. The archival technical
source does not identify a current SAS license, prove that every
proposed model is identified, or supply the missing current HPBNET or
PAG contract. \citep{exec13_latent_sas_calis}

Five further candidates remain unresolved: HUGIN Expert, MathWorks, IBM
Amos, LISREL and causaLens. The saved public-source review has
unavailable technical routes or navigation/licensing material
insufficient for the required algorithm and edition comparison. These
vendors stay in the landscape, with a request for authentic versioned
technical text before a ranking or adoption recommendation for this latent-model
question. Chapter~\ref{scisyn:software} separately records the later observed
causaLens service offering; its advertised scope does not resolve the missing
versioned latent-method specification. No lack of capability is inferred from
an inaccessible page. Their omission from
the six developed technical comparisons is an evidence gap, not a
finding that they cannot serve the bank's problem.

This table is evidence about named configurations in the retrieved
vendor page. It is not a runtime benchmark or an authenticated copy of
the page as it stood at the 7 October 2026 literature cutoff. In
particular, the Java Inference column's documented model-modification
entry should be retained even though its name might suggest a narrower
scope. Conversely, the REST configuration cannot be credited with
parameter fitting or discovery merely because another configuration in
the same product family offers those functions.

The same table associates the three Java configurations with model files
on the customer's servers and the REST configuration with model files on
Bayesia servers in France. It describes Java license validation through
the Bayesia License Server, requiring an Internet connection, and
credentials in each REST call. Model residence and license-validation
traffic are distinct architectural questions. Customer storage alone
does not establish offline operation. A bank proposal should decide
whether the intended task is model fitting, model modification or
inference, then examine the corresponding deployment path.
\citep{exec13_bayesia_api}

For the proposed library, an adapter would translate an explicit model
and variable/state map into the selected engine contract. It would
preserve the input version, configuration, objective, initialization,
convergence details, model output and uncertainty meaning. An
inference-only adapter would accept an already fitted model and supplied
evidence. A learning adapter would also need an exact data/observation
specification and fitting controls. Neither adapter would confer causal
truth on an elicited or learned edge. Automatic selection among these
paths remains proposed functionality; no commercial engine was invoked
in this review.

\hypertarget{license-scope-and-total-cost}{%
\subsection{License scope and total
cost}\label{license-scope-and-total-cost}}

SMILE explicitly separates development licensing from
embedding/deployment licensing. Its manual distinguishes server
deployment from end-user program distribution and directs readers to
obtain pricing details. Its academic conditions are not a general
permission for commercial banking use. The documented C++ implementation
and Java, Python, R, .NET and COM wrappers offer plausible integration
routes, but wrapper equivalence and selected-platform behavior have not
been measured here. Reimplementation of proprietary code is not an
adoption route; using a licensed engine through its public contract is
the candidate. \citep{exec13_smile_intro,exec13_smile_license}

BayesiaLab Professional documents purchase and rental, individual and
floating licenses, and elastic licensing. The API page distinguishes
development from production and describes a production basis tied to
simultaneously loaded model instances. Thus a bank that serves many
models concurrently may face a different license requirement from one
that loads models sequentially, even on the same physical machine. This
is a licensing observation, not a claim that either arrangement meets
the bank's latency requirements. The preserved API table links a USD
twelve-month price list but supplies no numeric fee in its inspected
cells. No numerical amount or binding quotation is available for the
compared deployment.
\citep{exec13_bayesia_api,exec13_bayesia_professional}

For the other selected engines, the technical examples do not establish
the needed edition-specific license, redistribution rights or full
deployment price. The comparison therefore distinguishes a
vendor-requested quotation, a public price-list link whose amount has
not been verified, and an unavailable price in the inspected sources.
None means zero cost. A proposed experiment can still use generated data
and an available open-source baseline while price and license evidence
for a commercial trial are acquired later if justified.

Let L be the license expense allocated to the intended analysis period,
I the integration and maintenance allocation, D the data-preparation
cost, H the human review cost, C the computation/storage cost, and O the
monitoring/recovery cost. For a candidate workflow, compare \[
 K=L+I+D+H+C+O
\] at the same accepted output quality. If m valid decisions are
produced, K/m is an accounting rate only when failed requests, manual
corrections and retries remain in K and the definition of a valid
decision is held fixed. It is not a causal return-on-investment
estimate. Faster inference on a supplied model cannot be compared
directly with a workflow that also constructs, reviews and fits that
model. The present evidence supplies some license categories and
configuration constraints; it supplies neither actual vendor workflow
timings nor observed bank prices.

\hypertarget{the-bounded-proposal-and-a-useful-next-comparison}{%
\subsection{The bounded proposal and a useful next
comparison}\label{the-bounded-proposal-and-a-useful-next-comparison}}

The observed local canonical service preserves supplied graph syntax,
declared context, selected simple effect/set operations and explicit
unsupported results. It does not expose a commercial latent fitter or a
general latent-discovery engine. The proposal is to add evidence-linked
formulation, distinct graph and measurement objects, selected adapters
and a decision interface that preserves ambiguity and uncertainty. Those
proposed controls are a reason to investigate an integration; they are
not an observed superiority claim against the vendors.

The matched comparisons answer different investment questions.
First, a supplied finite latent measurement model can compare fitting
and convergence under the same observation law, class count, prior and
initialization budget. Second, a representation task can compare class
probabilities and predictive use while preserving the distinction from
causal adjustment. Third, a graph discovery task needs explicit
latent/selection assumptions, equivalence-class outputs and an effect
query. A fitter or TAN predictor cannot enter the third comparison as if
it produced the required graph class. A randomized-compliance comparison
is a fourth, separate route when the actual design supports it.

For each route, freeze generated observed inputs and reserve hidden
truth for evaluation. Preserve nonidentification, empty cells, label
permutations, failed optimization, weak separation and alternative
missingness mechanisms in the denominator. Compare numerical outputs
only after checking that they represent the same object. Record full
workflow resources and unpriced components, then ask which additional
information or implementation step could change the recommendation. A
successful generated comparison can justify a scoped later licensed or
bank experiment. It cannot retroactively authenticate a vendor version,
create a missing identification assumption or confer institutional
acceptance.
