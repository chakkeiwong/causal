     4.4.1    Action constraints
         Some action constraints are necessary in order to ensure AGFN samples only valid
     AGs. Thus, we restrict actions that would create cycles or almost cycles. An action adding
     a directed edge leads to a cycle, or almost cycle, if and only if a directed path, or directed
     path with a single bidirected edge, exists between its endpoints. Similarly to Giudici and
     Castelo, 2003 and Deleu et al., 2022, we leverage the iterative nature of the generative
     process to efficiently track such paths and mask invalid actions.

     4.5     Expert-In-The-Loop (EITL) Causal Discovery
         After training, we use AGFN-generated samples to find pairs of variables that should
     be queried to the expert such that, the answers would maximally reduce the entropy of
     the distribution 𝑝𝜃 (𝒢 ) over AGs.Expert feedback is then used to update 𝑝𝜃 (𝒢 ), and the
     process is repeated.
