69.35.3     Additional non-Gaussian information: a useful but restricted route

Shi and colleagues develop a two-wave method that can distinguish some causal alternatives
using higher-order cumulants. Their model is

                           X = β1 U + ex ,       Y = β2 U + ηX + ey ,

with temporal ordering, linearity, independent errors and latent component, and specified
non-Gaussian information. Define the fourth mixed cumulants C31 = cum(X, X, X, Y ),
C22 = cum(X, X, Y, Y ) and C13 = cum(X, Y, Y, Y ). The first statistic is

                                      ∆1 = C31 C13 − C22
                                                      2
                                                         .

For this scalar-component model, writing κU = cum4 (U ) and κx = cum4 (ex ) gives

                                  ∆1 = κU κx β1 β22 η(β2 + ηβ1 ).

The proof’s nonzero cumulants and loadings, and the excluded cancellation η = −β2 /β1 ,
matter. Under those conditions the statistic supplies information absent from the Gaussian
example. It does not create information in that example by changing the estimator. (Shi et al.,
2026; Causal banking proposal, 2026ay)
  Two exact checks make the boundary concrete. First, let U take values −1, 0, 1 with
probabilities 1/6, 2/3, 1/6. It is nonnormal, has variance 1/3, and has fourth cumulant
1/3 − 3(1/3)2 = 0. With independent Rademacher errors, X = U + ex and Y = U + X + ey
have a direct effect of one. Nevertheless, C31 = C22 = C13 = −2, so ∆1 = 0; the relevant
third cumulants are also zero. This does not contradict the result with its full nonzero-
cumulant premises. It shows why “nonnormal” alone is an insufficient eligibility test and why
nondetection must remain inconclusive. (Causal banking proposal, 2026ay)
  Second, consider independent Rademacher U, V, ex , ey with

                           X = U + V + ex ,        Y = U + 2V + ey .

There is no direct X → Y path. Direct enumeration of all sixteen equally likely atoms,
independently checked by adding factor cumulants, gives C31 = −6, C22 = −10 and C13 = −18.
Thus ∆1 = 8. After standardizing X and Y , whose variances are three and six, the determinant
is 8/(32 62 ) = 2/81, still nonzero. The two hidden loading vectors (1, 1) and (1, 2) are not
proportional. Although the paper discusses aggregating hidden factors into a global component,
one common scalar component is not automatically an adequate representation of arbitrary
loadings with the required independent errors. This example lies outside that scalar restriction;
it supplies a diagnostic requirement for a proposed banking application, not a counterexample
under all theorem premises. Multiple forms of persistent borrower distress should not be
collapsed into a single latent score merely because that makes the test available. (Shi et al.,
2026; Causal banking proposal, 2026ay)

bootstrap intervals for three statistics with a Bonferroni allocation of α/3. The paper explicitly
treats failure to detect as inconclusive. In one specified weak-signal simulation, reported power
rises from 4.8% at N = 2,000 to 99.5% at N = 200,000. These are useful warnings about
information requirements, not a general sample-size formula for a bank. Calendar dependence,

69.36.2     A valid instrument need not answer the population question

Consider a stipulated servicing experiment. At time zero an eligible population receives a
randomized encouragement Z. Actual servicing A occurs by day seven, and binary repayment
Y is measured by day thirty. Let A(z) denote take-up under encouragement z, and Y (a)
repayment under servicing a. Assume encouragement is independent of the take-up type and
potential outcomes, that it affects repayment only through servicing, and that consistency and
no interference hold. Take-up is monotone: there are no borrowers who accept only when they
are not encouraged. These assumptions define the example; an assistant’s proposed instrument
does not establish them. (Causal banking proposal, 2026w)
  Suppose one quarter of borrowers are always-takers with (A(0), A(1)) = (1, 1), one quarter
are never-takers with (0, 0), and one half are compliers with (0, 1). The two candidate worlds
have the same take-up and differ only in potential repayments:

          Stratum      Probability World A: (Y (0), Y (1)) World B: (Y (0), Y (1))
          Always-taker    1/4             (0, 1)                  (1, 1)
          Never-taker     1/4             (0, 1)                  (0, 0)
          Complier        1/2             (0, 1)                  (0, 1)

Under either world the complete observed joint law of (Z, A, Y ) is the same. For Z = 0, a
quarter have (A, Y ) = (1, 1) and three quarters have (0, 0); for Z = 1, these probabilities are
reversed. Consequently,
                         E[Y | Z = 1] − E[Y | Z = 0]   3/4 − 1/4
                                                     =           = 1.
                         E[A | Z = 1] − E[A | Z = 0]   3/4 − 1/4
The population average effect is nevertheless 1 in world A and 1/2 in world B. Exact enumeration
reproduces every cell and contrast; neither world is fitted to bank data. (Causal banking
proposal, 2026w)
   The reason is visible algebraically. Let C denote the complier stratum. Under the stated
independence and exclusion restrictions, the first-stage difference is P (C): always-takers and
never-takers do not change treatment. For the same reason the reduced-form outcome difference
is P (C)E[Y (1) − Y (0) | C]. Dividing therefore identifies the complier effect when P (C) > 0.
The ratio supplies no new observation of the always-takers’ untreated outcome or the never-
takers’ treated outcome. Here their observed outcomes and the binary restriction permit
a population-effect range [1/2, 1]. Varying the unobserved outcomes, or mixing compatible
subtypes, attains the endpoints and intermediate values in this stipulated class. It would be
incorrect to attach the ratio’s value to the unrestricted population target. (Causal banking
proposal, 2026w)

repayment and a USD 75 servicing cost per treated borrower. A hypothetical policy treating
everyone rather than nobody then has net value between minus USD 25 and plus USD 25
