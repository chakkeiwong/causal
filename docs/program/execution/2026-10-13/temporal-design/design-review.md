# Temporal graph-to-effect study: feasibility review

Author review, 13 October 2026. This reviews `SCI.DESIGN.R04S` against the preserved R04S obligation and R04-v3 evidence. It approves the restricted mathematical contribution as proposal material, but **does not approve the proposed argument as a final qualification-study design**. No final sample is drawn and no prior request is relabeled fresh. The rejection is local to that study; independent manuscript, software, agent and integration work remains eligible.

## What the argument actually establishes

The observation law has a common continuous scalar lag in a known interval, invariant initialization, independent Gaussian innovations and a finite supplied structural family. At the true lag, the nine projected residual sample variances have an exact normal projection argument. Chebyshev plus a union bound covers the covariance constraints at that lag. Union over every candidate lag and graph retains the true tuple on the same event; no claim of validity at every incorrect lag is required. The continuous-lag union repairs one limitation of the old finite supplied lag grid. It does not prove a result for an arbitrary lag matrix, unknown order, nonstationarity, latent histories, selection or arbitrary PAG confidence.

The exact small reference checks covariance polynomials over whole cells, input-error inflation, ratio-set topology and an all-real equal-law witness. Sampled arithmetic tests supplement the analytic enclosure argument; they do not certify the continuum by sampling. The ratio-box outer approximation drops dependence/positive-definiteness constraints and can therefore be substantially wider than the feasible region. There is no resource-profiled final solver, complete observable-input enclosure policy, final protocol or full study implementation in the supplied candidate. The existing author and deterministic reviews are preserved, not overwritten or rerun as fresh evidence.

## Useful availability fails before a Monte Carlo campaign

With nine variance directions, relative tolerance one quarter and observational error .02, the displayed Chebyshev construction requires at least 14,400 residual degrees of freedom, or 14,401 transitions. The predecessor has 240 transitions and 239 degrees of freedom. Its untruncated union bound is 1.20502, providing no useful probability bound. The specified fallback is the whole real line. It cannot meet a 99% finite-availability requirement at that information level, regardless of the number of final simulation replications.

Even perfect numerical projection cannot resolve the unrestricted latent equal-law family. Every real contemporaneous effect has the same observed Gaussian temporal law; a method faithful to that family retains all real effects. At zero relevance, exclusion alone can have the same problem. A valid unrestricted observational procedure and a uniformly finite useful effect set cannot both be obtained from those observations. The proposal needs actual separating information, a substantively defensible restricted target, or an explicit nonidentification disposition. A chosen favorable graph does not supply missing information.

The direct assigned-action experiment can separate effects under its specified independent common-history Gaussian episode law. It requires a common transported effect, equal arm variances and direct action assignment. Replacing those episodes with adjacent records from one trajectory, or replacing direct action with encouragement, changes the argument. Actual bank episodes are later conditional evidence. A generated experiment can develop this branch now, but must be described as added information rather than a repair that recovered the effect from unchanged observational data.

At m=200 per arm, reported effect 20, reported pooled variance 100 and per-offer cost 15 for 3,000 customers, the conservative half-width is approximately 5.78806. Its profit lower endpoint is approximately -2,364.19 USD. Holding the reported variance and effect at those scenario values, m=268 first makes the half-width less than 5, and m=400 gives approximately 4.08761. Those are conditional arithmetic screens, not ex ante power or a recommended bank sample size: pooled variance and the point estimate would themselves vary in a future study. The old protocol has no bank width threshold, and none is invented in this review.

## Correct the interpretation of a preserved count

The persistent R04-v3 cell has 507 finite results among 512 requests. The exact fraction, 507/512 = 0.990234375, **passes the frozen raw finite-fraction threshold of .99**. An earlier statement that persistence itself missed finite availability is too strong under that criterion. Five whole-line results are still relevant evidence; they are not grounds for declaring that raw threshold failed.

This clarification leaves the old results and receipts intact. The weak-relevance and broad-latent cells each have zero finite results and still fail finite availability; false exclusion retains the true graph in zero of 512 requests. The overall broader gap therefore remains open. Hidden-history and selection counts retain their invalid-model caveats and are not qualified by graph-label bookkeeping.

A newly required simultaneous lower binomial confidence bound for availability would be a different criterion. At family error .05 across sixteen cells it is approximately .97130 for 507/512, and even 512/512 gives only .98880. The predecessor separately imposed a lower-bound screen for coverage; it did not impose that new .99 lower-bound rule for availability. Do not silently substitute one for the other.

## Certification-screen power is a separate requirement

For 512 independent final requests and family error .05 across sixteen comparisons, the existing coverage rule requires at least 500 successes to put the one-sided exact lower bound at or above .95. Exact rational binomial-tail sums independently verify the beta-quantile threshold: the tail at 500 under p=.95 is .00184934, while the tail at 499 exceeds .003125. At true per-cell coverages .95, .97, .98 and .99, pass probabilities are approximately .001849, .234929, .770054 and .997670. These are per-cell probabilities for the screen, not measured causal-method coverage. No product of them is a joint power estimate without an independence argument.

Increasing replications gives discrimination above the acceptance boundary; it does not create high power at the exact boundary while preserving the same error control. A candidate needs a defensible coverage margin plus useful availability/width, not merely a nominal .95 label. No threshold or original outcome is changed to create a pass.

## Concrete repair routes and unresolved obligations

1. Implement and independently certify the sharper chi-square variance pivot at the restricted true-lag event, with conservative tail-quantile enclosures and correctly rounded input bounds. Development evaluation at 239 degrees of freedom and the stated nine-way .02 error allocation gives variance multipliers about [.76731,1.34533]. These ordinary numerical quantiles only indicate a feasible direction; they are not certified release endpoints or final performance evidence. Preserve the continuous union and compare with the exact small-class oracle.
2. Profile this bounded implementation on declared development inputs. Freeze time and memory budgets, lag-partition refinement, full-width/unavailable fallback rules and explicit timeout versus nonidentification dispositions. Bind the actual code, dependencies, input enclosures and independent reference before any final registration. A point optimizer's failure cannot exclude a graph.
3. Freeze separate observational and added-information generated regimes. Retain strong, weak and zero relevance; unrestricted latent effects; persistence; false exclusion; hidden history; selection; disconnected, empty and whole-line sets. Directly assigned independent episodes form a separate information scenario, with transported-effect premises and cost categories explicit.
4. Define a decision-useful width or loss criterion as a proposal scenario, not an invented bank acceptance target. Profile power for the actual retained coverage/availability screens and declared final grid. Freeze fresh seeds, stopping rules and all-request denominator after the design review, preserving development exposure. Compare with the existing union and selected-lag challenger under matched information.
5. Keep general lag matrices/orders, non-Gaussian or nonstationary hidden histories, selection, unknown measurement errors, heterogeneous transported effects and arbitrary graph-class confidence as unresolved generalizations. Public data can inform observed clocks, scale and persistence but cannot decide among equal-law causal worlds without additional identifying information.

The first four routes are actionable for a restricted prototype and proposal. The fifth has no general solution established here. Authentic information is unnecessary to continue generated design and scholarly work; it is necessary only when making the corresponding real-bank claim. Final R04S registration must wait for a supported argument, certified executable, observable inputs, explicit useful-width/power design and a review that maps every restriction to the unchanged original obligation.
