### Backend choice preserves scientific outcomes and complete costs

Five pinned source profiles now support a concrete comparison of the
Anthropic and Google GenAI Python clients, the Ollama client, vLLM and
llama.cpp. Their generation, parsing and tool-execution contracts differ.
A valid schema does not establish an acyclic diagram or causal identification;
a remote-call limit does not necessarily bound local function execution;
and a prediction-time setting does not establish an end-to-end deadline.
The proposal retains native stop/refusal states, artifacts, attempts,
validation outcomes and full costs through the library, CLI and MCP.

Public or generated causal questions with exact correctness and refusal
oracles provide the first useful comparison. An authored example reverses
a conditional-quality ranking when all submissions and cost per correct
answer are counted. No real provider is ranked by this invented example.
The proposed route references the inspected implementations and qualifies
original clients or workers before integration. Official OpenAI guide
access, additional hosted platforms, service rights, exact model artifacts,
native conformance and actual prices remain open; an inaccessible document
is not evidence that its service lacks a capability.
