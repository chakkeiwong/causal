3.2. Fine-tuned LLMs
The pre-training of LLMs usually utilizes a great quantity of unlabeled data, and the fine-tuning
involves training these pre-trained LLMs on a smaller dataset labeled with examples relevant to
the target task. By exposing the model to these new labeled examples, the model adjusts its
parameters and internal representations suited for the target task. In this work, we experimented
with two models: (1) BERT [3] to represent small language model (under 1b parameters)
and (2) GPT to represent larger-parameter models.
