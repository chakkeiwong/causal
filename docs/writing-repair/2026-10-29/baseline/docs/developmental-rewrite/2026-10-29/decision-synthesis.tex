\chapter{What the evidence changes about the proposed service}
\label{rewrite:evidence-consequences}

The four journeys distinguish a wrong target, insufficient treatment support,
causal ambiguity and uncertain measurement. Those failures demand different
responses. A common service should make the distinctions visible while
reusing the appropriate statistical procedures. Its initial scope follows
from the evidence below; it does not require every possible capability to
be implemented at once.

\section{A result should change a design choice}
The existing 3,000-replication generated offer study is evidence about target
alignment. Under one specified design, the residual projection averages
26.447 when the equal-population benefit is 50. Its interval covers its own
target in 97.2\% of repetitions and the population target in none. At the
stipulated cost of 40, interpreting it as the population effect rejects the
beneficial offer in all 500 repetitions of that scenario. The design
consequence is to put the population weights and target in the result,
and to compare implementations only after aligning those quantities. A
more precise projection is not a repair for this interpretation error.

The separate 6,000-replication sparse-support study addresses inference
after the target is aligned. Pooling yields 94.7\% conditional coverage
under equal normal variances but 52.2\% under the tested unequal-variance
case. Cellwise Student intervals joined by a union bound preserve the
conditional coverage guarantee in the normal model at the cost of very
wide intervals. Thus the initial implementation should show cell support
and retain an unsupported or inconclusive result. If useful precision
requires unjustified pooling, the next expenditure should improve assignment
support or the information collected. These are existing generated
experiments, not evidence of superiority by a named external package.

The public UCI timing study answers a different question. On 41,188 archived
contact records, later-archive prediction has AUC 0.630 using the candidate
pre-call feature set and 0.793 when duration is added. The latter feature
is unavailable before the call, so its predictive gain cannot justify
pre-call targeting. Differences between early and late archive outcomes
also matter for evaluation. The correct service response is to preserve
availability dates and the evaluation period. Neither score estimates
the effect of making a call. Chapter~\ref{scisyn:evidence} gives the
executed design, comparators, results and qualifications.

The retained public 401(k), monetary, panel and linked-record analyses
provide further empirical and methodological context. Their sampling,
instrument, design and observation restrictions remain attached to their
results in the detailed evidence part. A working independent-observation
covariance in a public application does not validate a bank's common-calendar
uncertainty. A successful finite construction does not settle the unrestricted
linked-history problem. The negative findings remain relevant because
they identify the information a future bank study must supply.

The new one-sample deposit calculation in Chapter~\ref{rewrite:deposit}
completes the numerical route from estimation to contribution. Its callable
and command-line agreement tests the delivery of that particular report.
The four existing library/CLI/MCP cases test semantic consistency for
their own operations, including unsupported dependence. They establish
complementary facts. Neither is a matched analyst trial, a full general
estimation service, or evidence of improved bank outcomes.

\section{Choose the smallest comparison that can reverse the recommendation}
The first comparison should take the same fixed offer question through
expert composition and a minimal shared integration. The cases should
include correct assignment, heterogeneous effects, unequal treatment
support, unavailable predictors, misrecorded receipt and a dependent
sampling request outside the accepted route. The generated law supplies
truth where that is possible; a public case supplies realistic data handling
without pretending its causal truth is known. A valid refusal counts as
a completed scientific answer when the information is insufficient.

An independent evaluator should judge target correctness, assumption
visibility, uncertainty, interpretation and avoidable errors. Time must
include correction, interpretation and unsuccessful attempts. The estimate
itself is not a success metric when its target differs from the request.
The proposed comparison is not yet an executed analyst study; its purpose
is to discover whether the common representation is worth its expense.
If both workflows reach equivalent answers at equivalent cost, improve
the existing workflow. If the integration reduces consequential errors or
repeated work, a narrow bank-data pilot becomes a defensible next step.

\chapter{Which tasks merit language assistance?}
\label{rewrite:assistance-tasks}

Assistance should be selected by the task it improves. A system that answers
questions about a supplied causal mechanism has not thereby shown that
it can discover a defensible mechanism for a new bank. The evidence
synthesis in Chapter~\ref{scisyn:assistance} and the detailed studies
support a restricted comparison, with a questionnaire and ordinary search
as serious alternatives. They do not establish a measured saving for this
proposed product.

\section{Formulation and variable construction}
For formulation, the useful output is a precise intervention, alternative,
population, outcome, horizon and information date, together with unresolved
choices. The retained Kıcıman and causal-learn+ discussions motivate
question clarification and graph critique. Their descriptions support
a prototype candidate, not the truth of bank-specific assumptions.
\citep{v12_la_2305_00050,v12_la_2606_23608}
The strongest elementary comparator is a structured questionnaire completed
by an economist. Measure material omissions found and the time required
to correct the final question. The deposit distinction among assignment,
receipt, acceptance and price is an explicit test case. An eloquent
answer that silently chooses one is a failure.

Variable construction requires a separate assessment. A model can suggest
a record field or an extraction rule, but measurement validity depends
on the relation between that field and the scientific variable. The
retained text-and-causal-inference review discusses text used for adjustment;
it does not prove that a modern embedding removes confounding.
\citep{exec13_census_text_review_arxiv}
Compare any proposed extraction with a documented coding rule and
independently labelled examples, preserving missing and ambiguous cases.
The observation journey shows why high predictive accuracy or apparent
completeness is insufficient. Begin with auditable field definitions and
retrieval assistance; an autonomous scientific measurement claim remains
unsupported without independent validation.

\section{Diagrams and numerical discovery}
For diagram work, require evidence and timing for each proposed relationship
and retain competing explanations. LACR's reported retrieval effects vary
across its examples. The retained inspection records both improvement and
deterioration, including the ASIA skeleton comparison and the SACHS
comparison when PC output is added. This is evidence for evaluating the
retrieval choice, rather than assuming that more context improves the graph.
\citep{v12_la_2402_15301}
Compare the assistant with ordinary search plus an economist and, where
its assumptions fit, a numerical discovery procedure. The inputs must
be matched. Assess false orientations, missed common causes and the
resulting effect or action, rather than relying solely on edge counts.

The first assistant should propose a diagram and competing explanations
for review, then call a named discovery or identification method under
explicit assumptions. Numerical discovery contributes its own evidence;
textual plausibility cannot turn an unresolved equivalence class into
a validated graph. The three-model pricing example is a useful adverse
case because one unjustified orientation changes the financial sign.

\section{A positive workflow result and its limits}
The retained ORCA study gives a more direct reason to test assistance in a
completed workflow. Twelve participants used a counterbalanced within-subjects
design; the baseline assistant already had tools, step gates and minimal
state. The paper's Figure 5 reports total task durations of 1,395 seconds
for the baseline and 1,107 seconds for ORCA, a reported reduction of about
21\%. Table 1 reports completion rates 0.75 and 0.83. This is positive
published evidence for a structured workflow on the study's relational,
generated causal tasks. It is not a measured benefit for this banking
proposal. \citep[pp.~3--5]{exec13_census_orca}

The interpretation changes when the outcome definitions and all attempted
analyses are retained. Completion means producing an ATE, regardless of
accuracy; discovery success requires the treatment and outcome nodes to be
present. The paper's confidence-interval metric is described as an estimated
ATE falling within a ground-truth interval, so its reported 0.083 and 0.50
must not be presented as conventional repeated-sample confidence coverage.
The full-data condition comparison for absolute ATE error has $p=0.321$;
after excluding configuration-error outliers, the reported OLS comparison
has $p=0.020$. Both belong in an assessment of completed work because
configuration errors are among the failures a service is supposed to avoid.
The abstract's claimed 42-percentage-point completion gain also differs
from the displayed 0.75-to-0.83 comparison; this synthesis uses the stated
table comparison rather than reconciling that discrepancy by invention.
\citep[pp.~1, 5, 17--18]{exec13_census_orca}

The proposal should therefore test whether shared intermediate results and
question clarification save full effort while preserving scientific quality.
It should retain all configuration failures and report separately the time
spent by the analyst, tool execution and correction. The external study
supports that experiment's plausibility. Its small participant sample,
specified tasks, outcome definitions and analysis exclusions prevent a
claim that the same benefit is established across bank studies.

\section{Identification, execution and reporting}
CLadder evaluates formal causal questions under constructed mechanisms;
its reported results concern historical models and that supplied task.
It does not measure elicitation of a new bank mechanism.
\citep{v12_la_2312_04350}
For identification, compare assistance with a defined formal procedure,
requiring the assistant to preserve both the answer and its conditions.
An appropriate refusal must explain the missing restriction, not simply
return an empty result. The benchmark literature and the retained
mechanism-clustering critiques give reasons to keep failed cases and
uncertainty denominators in the evaluation.

For execution, the existing local interface study shows equality for four
constructed requests, not model-driven analysis quality. A scripted
workflow is the credible baseline. Compare any assistant on request
construction, faithful tool use, errors detected and correction effort.
Do not give it credit for a calculation performed by the formal tool
while omitting the costs of choosing or correcting the request.

For reporting, a deterministic template is a strong comparator because
it can already state the estimand, assumptions, interval and scope.
The assistant's proposed value is explaining why the result changes a
decision or why it cannot yet support one. Check that explanation
against the actual result and the retained sources. A report that turns
a set crossing zero into a positive recommendation fails even if its
prose is persuasive. No local live-model evaluation establishes an
incremental benefit for this reporting task.

The preferred first assisted task is therefore question clarification
with evidence retrieval and explicit alternatives, evaluated against
the questionnaire. Identification and numerical results remain delegated
to defined procedures. Expansion to autonomous discovery, variable
construction or recommendations requires task-specific evidence. The
frontier census remains 82 inspected and 31 unread; this synthesis does
not claim those omissions have been resolved. A live comparison still
requires a fixed model version, held-out questions, matched information,
independent adjudication and a budget.

\chapter{The first investment and the evidence that would reverse it}
\label{rewrite:investment-decision}

The immediate proposal is a minimal comparative integration for a fixed
offer study, with explicit population weights, information dates and
a complete financial report. Reuse a transparent estimator and retain
a documented alternative. The seventeen-area comparison establishes
which further capabilities are plausible candidates and why they need
separate assumptions. It is not a reason to build all seventeen before
evaluating the first study.

\section{Compare equivalent completed studies}
There are four economically serious alternatives. Expert composition
uses existing methods and bespoke study documentation. Minimal integration
adds a reusable request and result description around selected methods.
A broad shared service adds more methods, languages, maintenance and
operating commitments. A commercial route can provide supported
procedures and existing institutional integration, but its relevant
entitlements, actual prices and matched capabilities are unknown here.
The detailed commercial discussions preserve documented features and
unknowns; an unobserved feature is not evidence that it is absent.

The proposed first comparison selects minimal integration against expert
composition. Its potential gains come from repeated target specification,
information-date checks, error detection and reporting. Fitting a regression
may occupy little of the total work. Any proposed saving must therefore
be measured over preparation, correction, interpretation and failed
attempts. Bespoke causal reasoning remains part of every alternative.

Let $N$ be annual studies genuinely eligible for this restricted route;
$h$ hours saved per completed study at equivalent quality; $w$ loaded
hourly cost; $D$ reduction in expected decision loss per study; $C$ annual
operating cost; and $K$ annualised development and adoption cost. Then
\[
 B=N(wh+D)-C-K.
\]
The conditional break-even study volume is $(C+K)/(wh+D)$ only when
$wh+D>0$. A negative denominator cannot be repaired by doing more studies.
The earlier illustrative investment account in Chapter~\ref{r3:investment}
retains its actual stipulated inputs and arithmetic, including the
26.74-study threshold. That threshold belongs to that account. It must
not be imported as an observed requirement for the newly selected scope.

The deposit example's \pounds3 population contribution concerns one
constructed customer action. It is not $D$, the improvement in decision
loss due to choosing one analytical workflow over another. Counting
that whole contribution as a software benefit would confuse policy value
with the incremental value of analysis. Similarly, correction hours
counted in $h$ must not be counted again in $D$. These distinctions
prevent an attractive worked example from becoming an invented ROI.

\section{A spending sequence with real alternatives}
The first expenditure buys a matched analyst comparison using the
constructed and public cases, with independently assessed scientific
quality and recorded full effort. It asks whether the small integration
changes decisions or reduces work enough to pay for its ongoing cost.
A bank-data pilot follows only if that evidence is favourable and the
bank supplies a defensible target, data access, assignment information
and a meaningful precision requirement. An operating trial then tests
the relevant interfaces and platform under its actual conditions.

Several observations would reverse or narrow the recommendation. Equal
quality and effort favour the established workflow. A commercial
entitlement supplying equivalent outputs at lower full cost favours
that route. Frequent lack of treatment support or reliable observation
truth favours better study design and data collection. A repeated
need for unsupported dependent inference limits the offer route rather
than justifying a broad claim. Evidence of consequential, reproducibly
avoided errors or lower full effort can justify extending the integration.

This is a conditional proposal with a selected next experiment. It
preserves the larger scientific and software programme without treating
its unfinished questions as already solved. The detailed comparisons
and operating evidence that follow allow a reader to inspect those
restrictions and alternatives before funding the next step.
