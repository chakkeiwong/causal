# Known-design cross-fitting for the recovered sequential score

This is a project derivation for the declared IID fixed-horizon,known-assignment,known-recovery setting. It is not a general theorem for estimated propensities,dependent customers,learned policies or arbitrary learners.

Let Z_Q(O) be the observed-data recovery score obtained by applying K+B M/q to D_Q,including V_Q0 in K. For every fixed square-integrable tuple Q, sequential treatment weighting telescopes and recovery conditional expectation gives P Z_Q=V(pi). Let Q* be any fixed deterministic limit and define delta_Q=Z_Q-Z_Q*. Thus P delta_Q=0 exactly,not merely to first order.

Split n IID records into K fixed folds,with K fixed and n_k/n bounded away0. Fit Qhat_-k using records outside fold k. Require ||Z_Qhat_-k-Z_Q*||_L2(P)=o_p(1),and 0<Var(Z_Q*)<infinity. Conditional on the training records,fold-k evaluation records are IID and independent of Qhat_-k. Its remainder R_k=P_nk delta_Qhat_-k has conditional mean0 and conditional variance ||delta_Qhat_-k||²/n_k. Conditional Chebyshev yields sqrt(n_k)R_k=o_p(1). The K remainders need not be independent: their finite weighted sum is still o_p(n^-1/2). Hence cross-fitted mean equals P_n Z_Q*+o_p(n^-1/2),and the IID central limit theorem supplies asymptotic normality.

For pooled-score variance, conditional Markov gives P_nk(delta_Qhat_-k²)=o_p(1). Cauchy-Schwarz bounds the difference of empirical second moments by this L2 term and the tight oracle second moment. Together with mean consistency,this proves pooled variance consistency for Var(Z_Q*) under the stated L2 condition. No claim of finite-n exact coverage follows.

Why the declared finite-dimensional fit has a route to the condition: fixed dimension,horizon and policies;bounded inverse action and recovery probabilities;finite moments of the Gaussian-state/polynomial-reward law;nonsingular population weighted Gram matrices;each training fit has enough independent observations. Weighted least squares converges recursively to deterministic projection coefficients,even if the quadratic class is misspecified. For the threshold policy,substitution of a bounded binary policy action does not destroy finite moments. Coefficient consistency and finite feature second moments then give score L2 consistency. The experiment checks sample rank,not a universal proof of population nonsingularity. Population full rank follows here because continuous X,L have positive density on open sets and each action has positive conditional probability,so a quadratic combination of the nine declared features cannot vanish almost surely except with zero coefficients. Recovery weights do not remove support.

Qualification limits: inverse probabilities known and bounded,complete truthful recovered histories,policy fixed independently of evaluation,finite score variance,correct random recovery and IID customers. If propensity scores are estimated,the exact P delta_Q=0 argument no longer applies and products of estimation errors or a different argument are needed. Cross-fitting alone repairs none of those failures.

This argument explains the specific candidate evaluated in the equal-resource study. It does not turn its94.4% simulated coverage into a finite-sample95% guarantee.
