# From financial text to an intervention question

The acquired Li (2025) original reports financing-constraint classification,
export prediction and expert-rated explanations. The scientific question for
the proposal is whether even perfect extraction of those observed variables,
plus a coherent explanation, determines the effect of relieving a constraint.

Construct two binary structural laws with the same complete observed joint
distribution of a financing-constraint indicator F and export participation Y,
but different intervention contrasts E[Y|do(F=1)]-E[Y|do(F=0)]. In the first,
a latent distress state causes both F and Y and F has no effect on Y. In the
second, exogenous F causes Y. Attach exactly the same deterministic text
channel to (F,Y), so every extraction, prediction and consistency rule on
that information has the same observational law in both worlds.

Use independent Bernoulli inputs and exact fractions: distress probability
1/2; two measurement/response flip probabilities 1/5 in the confounded law;
and a response flip probability 8/25 in the causal law. These are chosen
counterexample constants, not fitted values or recommended defaults. They
produce matching observational risks 17/25 and 8/25. Separately derive the
joint table algebraically and compare with exhaustive enumeration. Verify
normalization, equality of each observed cell and text transcript probability,
nonzero risk difference versus zero intervention effect, and distinct effects
under random assignment. No Monte Carlo or statistical significance claim.

This is a project derivation showing a missing logical implication. It is not
a replication or accusation about the source's data, a proof that text never
helps, or an operational loan-offer effect. A real offer must define take-up,
timing, terms, outcome and target population separately. A failed equality or
arithmetic check invalidates the example and stops its publication. Preserve
the first attempt if it fails. Compute budget: one CPU minute; two local
infrastructure repairs. Run no paid models or downloaded code.

Review: the comparison answers a consequential scientific question absent
from an explanation-quality score. It uses the same observed information and
does not give one method access to hidden truth. Additional independent
information or a justified identifying design can distinguish the worlds;
better prose alone cannot. Link this construction to the monograph's existing
identification argument and source-specific findings, without claiming a new
general theorem or universal failure of language assistance.
