# Pre-execution review of the V8 plan

10 October 2026. Author review; no independent acceptance is implied.

The plan is feasible at the selected scope after the following restrictions.

1. A repeated map contributes one audit unit, while a common shock contributes
   one calendar unit. A joint variance cannot be obtained by choosing whichever
   existing standard error is larger. Derive the joint score decomposition and
   state the relative growth and nondegeneracy conditions before implementation.
2. Pair-plus-calendar covariance can count interaction noise twice. Keeping
   that term is acceptable only with its conservatism and asymptotic order
   explained; exact finite-sample calibration must not be claimed.
3. An estimated label-error rate needs an independent validation design and
   its own influence term. An error-rate confidence region touching one half
   invalidates a regular correction. Unknown selection or asymmetric errors
   remain different models, even when the input validator cannot detect them.
4. Allocate audits from an independent pilot and compare equal expected cost,
   including varying per-stratum cost. Report achieved cost and an unattainable
   precision target explicitly. The pilot is extra information, not free data.
5. Calibration experiments must distinguish an observed mechanism from a
   unique causal explanation of V7 undercoverage. Fresh oracle arms help
   diagnose; they do not retroactively certify the old experiments.
6. Exact Gaussian pivots are available for the finite graph information
   classes. Their confidence events differ from the covariance rectangle.
   Preserve weak-rank topology and the false-exclusion challenger; never
   describe a supplied experimental exclusion as discovered by the algorithm.
7. The nonlinear variance extension needs enough labelled transitions in each
   action group. Check all 16 derivatives and value refinement independently;
   keep boundary and numerical failures. A fixed observable policy is not an
   unknown optimal or full belief-history policy.
8. Chunked QR must preserve intercepts, training folds and feature definitions.
   Matching a dense fit verifies algebra; repeated dependent samples assess
   uncertainty. A richer dictionary can repair one omission without providing
   general misspecification robustness. Memory/time comparisons include the
   whole request and equal reporting obligations.
9. External protocols must define who supplies which evidence, rejection and
   stopping criteria, and the concrete files needed to resume. Local rehearsals
   cannot be recorded as bank, independent or multi-host validation.

The reviewed plan closes an executable next increment. It does not promise a
favorable result or remove the general nonlinear, graph, semantic and learner
research obligations. Separate scholarly and supported-capability dispositions
are required at E07, with a final current gap register.
