A causal product must preserve a chain of reasoning. A bank defines a feasible
intervention and a population. A scientific model describes what that
intervention changes and what information the bank observed. Identification
then either expresses the requested counterfactual through the observed law,
or explains why several answers remain possible. Only after that step do
estimation, machine learning and computational scale enter the argument.

This manuscript reconstructs that chain. Its running example separates a
randomized invitation, actual product receipt, subsequent customer behavior,
and the bank's financial outcome. The distinction leads to graphical
identification and instrumental variables. Identified functionals lead to
influence functions, orthogonal estimation and heterogeneous-effect learning.
Repeated decisions lead to the longitudinal g-formula; partial observation
then changes the likelihood and the policy-evaluation problem. These are
connected mathematical problems, and the product's interfaces must reflect
the connections and the boundaries.

The argument then develops econometric panels and structural shocks,
causal discovery, measurement and observation clocks, continuous-time
models, policy learning, transport and sensitivity. The software survey
compares mathematical inputs and outputs before proposing reusable
operations, execution plans, banking studies and investment economics.
The companion sponsor proposal condenses the construction and investment
decision; it does not replace the assumptions in this monograph.

Scope is explicit. The first product supports bounded identification and
inference routes. General nonlinear hidden-state identification, arbitrary
network equilibrium and universal inference for long dependent histories
are not established here. The inherited 44-project source/document survey, retained as a bounded
baseline alongside the new pinned comparisons, is supplemented
by version-bound executions of selected numerical, columnar, DoubleML and
Tigramite operations. The fitted comparisons distinguish calibrated examples
from adverse coverage and unavailable intervals; a supported likelihood is
not automatically a qualified confidence procedure.
Local reference implementations connect observation construction, inference
and scientific result types. Read-only sibling API inspections and local
mapping examples establish a concrete integration design; accepted sibling
adoption and a complete bank workbench remain separate work.

Every cited paper or book has a preserved PDF in \texttt{docs/papers}, a
source identity and hash, and inspected technical anchors. Source roles and
remaining omissions are recorded in the source--question matrix. The
publication cutoff is 7 October 2026; acquisition and inspection dates
are retained separately. Coverage is a structured, bounded survey, not a
claim to have retrieved every relevant publication. Inherited results,
project derivations, finite counterexamples, fitted synthetic experiments
and proposed behavior are distinguished. Known-truth banking mechanisms are
synthetic; archived household-finance and quarterly monetary applications
have unknown causal truth and explicitly qualified designs. Connected funding
and servicing constructions carry uncertain observations, learning and
dynamic dependence into reusable financial results. A linkage construction connects weak identification to
financial confidence sets, including disconnected and unbounded answers.
The servicing comparison
separates public-history, private-state and audited-state information, and
evaluates a belief policy trained independently of its evaluation sample. Measurements include complete reconstruction, fitting,
uncertainty and reporting workloads as well as the earlier isolated score
reducer. The economic inputs remain planning assumptions. Neither the
synthetic findings nor the local timings establish bank adoption or
enterprise performance.

The bounded extensions distinguish persistent identity audits from correctness
counts, retain covariance and latent-graph uncertainty in outer effect sets,
and develop an audited nonlinear servicing likelihood with explicit policy
and approximation comparisons. Separate dependent-learning and restartable
execution routes preserve their sampling experiments. A completed calculation
may still be uninformative or empirically unqualified; those outcomes survive
the public result rather than disappearing during monetary reporting.

The reliability construction joins persistent pair audits to a common calendar
under a stated projection law and independent label validation. It derives
finite Gaussian null-test effect sets, extends one audited nonlinear transition
variance model and fits a fixed richer dependent dictionary through chunked QR.
Fresh calibration and workload comparisons retain shortfalls and small or absent
cost advantages. Larger information designs provide bounded follow-up evidence,
while independent scientific, bank and platform acceptance remains separate.

The funding calibration argument then derives the exact target shift from a
finite independent pilot and its required information growth. A fixed-regression
alternative admits a two-pass memory construction, while fresh comparisons
retain its small-sample undercoverage and the cost of a growing-pilot route.
Improved attribution and local execution do not imply universal calibration.
A more precise comparison varies audit and pilot information and preserves
weak-information refusals. An equivalent streaming pilot retains chronological
tuning and monetary score inference; its storage benefit is qualified separately
from coverage, elapsed time and institutional adoption.
