2.2 Interventional Markov Equivalence: New Concepts and Results
An intervention at some target a ∈ [p] destroys the original causal influence of other variables of
the system on Xa . Interventional data thereof can hence not be used to determine the causal parents
of Xa in the (undisturbed) system. To be able to estimate at least the complete skeleton of a causal
structure (as in the observational case), an intervention experiment has to be performed based on a
conservative family of targets:
