## Preservation contract

1. The protected reading edition, scientific continuation, research predecessor, old sponsor and their source/evidence files remain byte-identical. Work occurs in a new successor directory and new PDF names.
2. Important mathematics, proofs, assumptions, limitations, negative findings, substantive alternatives, measured results and source boundaries remain in the delivered complete monograph. A file left only in an archive does not satisfy this rule.
3. Moving material is distinct from deleting it. Every moved block needs its original path/hash, destination and compiled reachability. A shorter main reading route is permitted only when the detailed argument remains accessible and its contribution is explained.
4. All edits to inherited text use exact, recorded before/after spans. Each entry states why the text changes, which claims/qualifications survive, where they survive and whether the deletion is administrative, redundant, corrective or substantive. The full removed text is retained in the audit, not merely a deletion count.
5. A substantive deletion is not approved merely because a replacement paragraph is shorter. It requires a claim-level disposition. Unsupported statements may be corrected, but the correction and evidence must be explicit. No known adverse result may disappear because it complicates the recommendation.
6. Check whole-tree membership and content, not just the old display-math counters. Verify citation identities, labels, displayed and inline mathematics, quantitative statements and assumptions. Changed mathematical targets or units require separate review and reproducible arithmetic.
7. The final deletion review runs after the final manuscript edit. It fails on an unmapped input, an unexplained deleted/changed span, missing supporting material, unresolved references, an inconsistent financial result or a changed protected baseline. Do not publish a passing verdict while any such item remains.
