Causal banking: product proposal 19 October 2026 A shared causal-analysis service Product and investment proposal 19 October 2026 Working proposal for review 1 The decision this proposal supports A universal bank needs to know when a proposed action is likely to improve a financial out- come, which assumptions make that answer possible, and when the available information cannot distinguish competing decisions. The proposed investment is a small reusable causal-analysis service supporting four journeys: funding responses, sequential servicing, intervention queries after causal discovery, and the construction of trustworthy observations. Its immediate deliverable is a bounded prototype and an evidence-based recommendation about what to build, reuse or leave unsupported. The recommendation is to develop and compare a narrow set of these operations using public records, documented public experiments and generated cases with known truth. Existing local research supplies selected working constructions and substantial negative evidence. It does not yet establish a complete product or a positive return on deployment. Expensive bank experiments should follow a prototype result that makes their cost worth considering. The absence of bank data, institutional sign-off or production access does not prevent completion of the present proposal. [20, 21] 1.1 The reusable product and its customer The first user is an analyst who can state a banking decision and inspect its assumptions. The reusable input records a target population, action and comparator, information available before the action, outcome and horizon, measurement units, observation process, causal restrictions and sampling unit. An offer, its receipt and the later balance are different variables. A measured association is not automatically the effect of changing an offer. The output keeps the target attached to its evidence. It may be an identified formula, an estimate with justified uncertainty, a set of compatible effects, an informative refusal or a failed computation. Unresolved graph endpoints, unbounded confidence sets and numerical failures require different responses. The library, command line and MCP interface should preserve these distinctions so that a downstream application can decide whether to act, ask for information or fall back to its existing workflow. A callable endpoint alone does not establish identification, useful precision or interoperability. [22] The shared layer would organize three workspaces: intervention studies, sequential decisions and dynamic systems. The four banking journeys pass through these workspaces, with observation quality affecting all three. This organization avoids building a separate estimation stack for every business team while retaining each team’s population, timing and economic account. The initial service would require analyst review of consequential scientific choices. No autonomous bank action is proposed. 1.2 A concrete first slice A useful first demonstration begins with a public or generated business brief, constructs an explicit question and alternative diagrams, checks the available identification route, and returns a result whose assumptions and information limits remain visible through the library, CLI and MCP. For a randomized offer, the first target can be the effect of assignment. A receipt effect requires additional compliance and exclusion assumptions. For a confounded observational table, the correct result may be a documented ambiguity rather than a number. The comparison must include an expert-designed template and a directly composed existing- package workflow. The new service earns its place only if the common representation and checks reduce consequential mistakes or repeated effort without discarding valid alternatives.
