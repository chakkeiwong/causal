# Proposed closure program after V6.1

10 October 2026 (Asia/Shanghai). Status: proposed, with author plan review;
implementation and new scientific experiments have not started. The literature
cutoff remains 7 October 2026. This plan responds to the
[V6.1 assessment](../reviews/remaining-gaps-v6.1-2026-10-09.md).
The frozen V6.1 phase history and delivery remain evidence of that edition.

## What we know how to do

There is a direct repair for G1 and a defensible construction route for a
bounded extension in each of G2-G6. Knowing the construction route does not
establish its useful precision, numerical cost or applicability to a real bank.
Those are explicit questions for the proposed studies. A negative study can
resolve a research question without delivering the desired product capability.

| Finding | Current knowledge | Proposed deliverable | What cannot be promised |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 Public qualifications | Exact defect reproduced; correction is known | Consistent, versioned inference qualification in both consumers and exported results | Metadata repair does not improve interval coverage |
| G2 Linkage precision | Joint-region projection has a coverage-by-inclusion route; additional audited outcomes can add identifying information | Compared projection procedures, undercoverage diagnosis and audit-design decision | Narrow, valid sets from genuinely nonidentifying moments |
| G3 Nonlinear POMDP | A small identified parametric model with separate validation data can be fitted against a numerical reference | One complete nonlinear private-logger estimation and policy-value route | General nonlinear identification, global optimization or calibrated intervals for arbitrary models |
| G4 Discovery to effect | Project a simultaneous observed-law region through a declared bounded structural class, retaining graph ambiguity | One latent/temporal model-class confidence construction and information-acquisition example | General useful, tractable confidence from arbitrary learned PAGs |
| G5 Persistent observations | Model a fixed map across a trajectory and obtain audits at the map's sampling unit | Persistent-map construction with selection/noise assumptions and counterexamples | Semantic truth or unidentified audit bias from column profiles alone |
| G6 Reuse and scale | Existing blocked/sparse theory and partition algebra provide starting points | Qualified learner integration, streaming and restartable local execution, matched workloads | Arbitrary learner validity, multi-host performance or business savings without evidence |

The required standard remains a connected argument from banking question and
information through identification, estimation, uncertainty, numerical error,
financial meaning and reusable output. Every selected route must compare
substantive alternatives and explain how the choice changes the result.
Restricting scope requires this argument; adding an exclusion sentence is
insufficient. The [acceptance questions](../reviews/reference-gap-review.md)
and [theory map](theory-map.md) govern the final review.

## C00-C01: a known repair and a better acceptance process

Preserve the current delivery, the new assessment and its formatter probe
before changing bound files. Define an explicit target, observation law,
estimator, uncertainty claim and failure state for each proposed capability.
Read the primary/challenger passages needed by each construction and retain
their versions and limitations. Existing source ledgers are a starting point,
not a substitute for checking assumptions introduced by a new model.

Introduce one inference description shared by the raw result, treasury report,
application report and serialized export. It must identify the method, nominal
confidence level, finite-sample versus asymptotic claim, sampling/identification
conditions, numerical status and empirical qualification. A successful numeric
calculation and a qualified uncertainty statement are separate properties.

For a revised request version, make the inference method explicit at request
construction, before evaluation. Preserve an explicit regular research route
and disclose its retained adverse calibration. Do not silently switch to the
shortest interval, or call the conservative projection universally qualified.
Existing callers get a documented migration rather than an unnoticed change in
their statistical procedure. Both public consumers must carry the same
qualification after a permitted fixed monetary rescaling.

Replay the reproduced omission; exercise regular, bounded, disconnected,
whole-line, empty and unavailable results; and inspect actual public outputs.
Check that a hash is accompanied by an interpretable scientific description.
This repair needs contract and affected execution verification, not a new
Monte Carlo experiment to discover whether fields were retained.

## C02-C03: persistent linkage, calibration and useful information

### Persistent maps change the experiment

Start with a declared population of independent paired customer trajectories,
fixed horizon and one one-to-one identity map per pair, held fixed throughout
the trajectory. Define the target as a population average focal-customer
response. This is a new sampling design; it is not a claim that the original
single-calendar funding theorem already covers persistent crosswalks.

An audited pair supplies one map label, not one independent label per date.
Infer uncertainty from independent pairs and the actual audit design. Derive
the joint influence or likelihood before resampling whole pairs. A calendar
with shared shocks needs a separate common-calendar or multiway argument;
ordinary pair resampling is not automatically valid in that extension.
Increasing the number of dates for a fixed number of unknown maps cannot be
substituted for increasing independent map information.

Use a known-probability audit design that records the true map and the linked
action/outcome history. Include audit inclusion and its uncertainty in the
estimating law. Contrast this richer observation with correctness counts alone.
If labels are noisy, identify their error law from separate gold-standard
validation or use explicitly justified sensitivity bounds. Estimating a label
error rate from two equally uncertain assertions is not a validation design.
Many-to-many ownership remains separate unless an allocation rule and its
target are scientifically specified.

Fixtures must include persistent wrong aliases, repeated dates, unequal audit
inclusion, label error, a false source-owner assertion and a correct
interpretation outside the candidate grammar. Candidate inclusion and semantic
truth are evaluated against separately specified truth; the algorithm cannot
certify its own accepted answer. The output must preserve unresolved mappings,
their effect consequences and the particular missing fact an owner must supply.

### Improve the projection without promising impossible precision

For the existing law, first isolate the regular interval's undercoverage.
Registered diagnostic arms vary known versus estimated audit probabilities,
oracle versus fitted response information, and linearized versus projected
uncertainty. Diagnose bias, variance, unavailable intervals and coverage on all
requests as well as conditional on availability. These comparisons can reveal
contributors; they must not be presented as a unique cause without evidence.

The first sharper candidate is a joint ellipsoidal region for the four
observable response coordinates, combined with the exact audit regions. The
current rectangle remains the transparent reference. Under a valid joint
response approximation and a prespecified error allocation, projection of
the primitive region preserves coverage by inclusion. An ellipsoid covariance
inverse requires a justified fixed-rank response limit; an arbitrary sample
pseudoinverse is not a remedy for singular response information. Retain the
rectangle or an explicitly justified reduced-coordinate region in that case.
For fixed nonzero map
ranks, financial value is a linear functional of the response vector, so
ellipsoid extrema can be computed analytically. The remaining small audit
parameter search needs global outer enclosures and explicit zero-rank limits.
A local optimizer or a bounded effect grid cannot certify the tails or remove
feasible values. Numerical nonresolution returns an outer bound or an explicit
unsupported state, never a falsely narrow interval.

Joint ellipsoids are not uniformly smaller than rectangles. Register the
procedure before final evaluation and compare coverage, bounded frequency,
component topology, conditional widths, computation and whether the financial
decision is resolved. Keep strong, weak, singular and reversed maps, missing
audits and the existing unfavorable draws. Preserve the full covariance and
the response approximation assumptions. A residual-test inversion is a second
research candidate only after its critical value and nuisance elimination are
derived; it is not presumed valid by analogy with IV.

Then evaluate the extra information from C02's outcome-linked audits in its
own sampling experiment. Compare uncertainty reduction with audit burden at
fixed designs. Increasing correctness-count audits helps distinguish nonzero
rank from zero; it cannot identify a component contrast when the true rank is
zero in the mean model. At zero rank, the defensible remedies are new identity
or outcome information, an externally justified restriction, or a changed
target such as the portfolio total. The last is explicitly a different target.

Closure has two entries: validity/behavior of the method, and usefulness of
the supported information design. If the first passes and the second fails,
record the research result and the unresolved capability separately. Do not
mark the usefulness gap closed merely because whole-line sets cover truth.

## C04: a complete nonlinear POMDP route with identifiable inputs

Select a low-dimensional parametric servicing model. A concrete starting law is

\[
 S_{t+1}=\rho S_t+\gamma\tanh(S_t)+\beta A_t+u_{t+1},\qquad
 O_t=S_t+e_t,
\]

with a Gaussian initial law, independent Gaussian transition/measurement
innovations, and positive unknown noise variances. The observation coefficient
one fixes the audited state's scale. The private logger uses

\[
 P(A_t=1\mid S_t,O_t)=\epsilon+(1-2\epsilon)
 \operatorname{expit}(\lambda_0+\lambda_s S_t+\lambda_o O_t),
 \qquad 0<\epsilon<1/2,
\]

and the monetary reward mean is
\(\eta_0+\eta_s S_t+\eta_a A_t+\eta_{sa}S_tA_t\), with independent
Gaussian reward noise. Bound parameters before data analysis, with
\(|\rho|+|\gamma|<1\), and specify the reward accounting. This is a proposed
reference model, not an estimated description of a bank. State, signal,
action, reward and next state follow that order. The policy may use current
signals and past actions/rewards, but never the audit's current state label.
Use an independent representative audit of state-labelled transitions and
measurements, with both actions supported, to anchor state meaning and identify
the selected parameter family. Derive this identification and any remaining
equivalence before fitting the unlabelled production histories. Full labelled
histories are costly additional information, not something manufactured by a
filter. The initial demonstration assumes accurate audits; noisy/selected
audits are explicit adverse or separately identified extensions.

The identification route is concrete: audited initial states identify their
law; audited signal/state pairs identify measurement noise; transition
regression on \((S,\tanh S,A)\) and reward regression on
\((1,S,A,SA)\) identify their coefficients when the respective design matrices
have full rank; the audited logger regression identifies its coefficients
under its own rank conditions. Retain those conditions and the independent
audit's sample size in inference. An optimizer Hessian from unlabelled data
alone is not a proof of these claims.

Derive the joint observed/audit likelihood, including the private action
mechanism, and its score. For interventional policy value replace the action
mechanism and use only policy-available information. Compare dense adaptive
quadrature as the small reference, a Gaussian approximation as a cheap
challenger, and a particle likelihood as a distributional challenger. Verify
the exact Gaussian reduction where its assumptions hold. Refine quadrature
domain and resolution, check score directions, and use repeated particle
seeds/counts to distinguish approximation bias from Monte Carlo noise.
For the Gaussian reference set the nonlinear transition coefficient and
private-state logger coefficient to zero; discrete actions alone do not
prevent exact filtering when they add no unobserved information conditional
on the measured history. Keeping a state-dependent logistic logger would
invalidate that Gaussian reduction.
Likelihood agreement alone is insufficient: compare derivatives and value.

Joint uncertainty includes estimated audit/calibration parameters and the
whole customer trajectory. Choose audit and main sample sizes jointly; an
audit-only identified direction has the audit's information rate, not
automatically the much larger production sample's rate.
Compare score-based uncertainty and full refits
under the same stated model; neither is assumed calibrated near singular
information or optimizer boundaries. Use multiple starts and report unresolved
local modes. No local optimizer is described as a global maximum certificate.

Train a policy on independent pilot data and freeze it before evaluation.
Validate approximate planning against an enumerable short horizon and derive
the finite-horizon propagation of model, filtering and planning error. Evaluate
the trained policy's value, conditional on training; optimal-value inference
is a separate target. Express numerical error in money alongside sampling
uncertainty. Numerical tolerance comparisons give observed accuracy; only an
actual error bound warrants a certified-error claim.

Registered adverse regimes include weak measurements, scarce state/action
visits, audit bias, longer horizons and nonlinear misspecification. An executed
identified reference can close the bounded nonlinear construction gap even
if a cheap approximation loses. Routine validity for arbitrary private-state
models remains unclaimed.

## C05: retain graph uncertainty through a bounded effect calculation

Define a small time-ordered Gaussian structural class with a specified finite
observation window, at most four observed nodes and a declared possible hidden
common cause. Begin with independent episodes and state that sampling boundary.
Enumerate the allowed structures rather than treating one search output as
truth. Timing, exclusion and any parameter bounds must be supplied scientific
restrictions. An unproved arbitrary bound cannot be used to manufacture a
finite effect set.

For the first linear reference write the unrolled observed vector as
\(X=B_GX+\ell_G U+\xi\), with acyclic time-respecting \(B_G\), one independent
standard-normal latent root \(U\), and independent Gaussian errors with diagonal
covariance \(D_G\). Missing edges fix the corresponding coefficients to zero.
The observed covariance and scalar action-to-outcome derivative are

\[
 \Sigma_G=(I-B_G)^{-1}(\ell_G\ell_G'+D_G)(I-B_G)^{-T},\qquad
 \theta_G=[(I-B_G)^{-1}]_{Y,A}.
\]

These equations define the compatibility and intervention calculations to be
implemented, rather than relying on graph labels alone. The observed mean is
a nuisance. A latent root outside this class, correlated errors beyond the
allowed structure, selection or a longer unobserved past changes the model.

Construct a simultaneous region for the observed covariance. For every allowed
structure retain all compatible structural parameterizations, including latent
ones, and map them into the requested intervention effect. On the covariance
coverage event the true structure and parameters are retained if the declared
class contains them. This gives the route to an outer confidence set without
assuming that thresholded conditional-independence tests selected the truth.
Use exhaustive small references or conservative interval/constraint calculations
to check retention. If computation cannot resolve a component, retain it or
report numerical nonresolution; a timeout is not nonidentification.

Compare this operation with the current exploratory temporal search and
conditional effect envelope. Include hidden confounding, weak graph signals,
ambiguous orientation, a missing true structure and an omitted earlier period.
Some cases should remain uninformative. Add a separately declared information
arm, such as randomized encouragement or an observed confounder with adequate
support, and derive the new target before comparing its precision. A graph
orientation is not credited to discovery when it came from that extra premise.

The construction extends the existing sufficient-Gaussian example to a bounded
latent class. A dependent infinite-history extension requires its own observed
law region and window-boundary argument; the independent-episode guarantee
cannot be relabelled as a general time-series guarantee. Useful large-PAG
confidence and tractable general structural optimization remain research.

## C06-C07: preserve the statistical experiment while making it reusable

Integrate the existing separated-block/sparse-learning route into an explicit
public operation. Preserve whole lag/outcome windows, the separating gap,
training-only tuning, nuisance approximation/rate assumptions and fitted-score
covariance conditions. Its source model differs from the current Gaussian
finite-basis funding construction; reconcile those assumptions before any
shared inference claim. Compare it with the finite-basis reference and an
oracle under aligned targets, plus misspecification and leakage cases.
An empirical mixing diagnostic does not prove a population mixing rate, and
accepting an arbitrary estimator object does not validate its nuisance rate.

Implement streamed source materialization and sufficient-statistic passes
where the selected estimator permits them. Keep fold identity, global
centering, chronological halos and exactly-once contribution accounting.
Checkpoint coherent scientific states; inject interruption, retry, duplicate
partition and partial-write failures. Restarted results must reproduce the
serial reference within declared tolerances with the same statistical sample.
Measure elapsed time, peak memory, I/O and numerical differences across the
dominant response, latent and discovery dimensions. Include source reading,
fitting, uncertainty, consumer output and serialization in complete-workflow
costs, with stage times disclosed separately.

Use the recorded host for local qualification. Develop a runnable multi-host
protocol, but actual network/storage/concurrency service levels require the
corresponding environment. No speedup is an acceptable measured outcome;
it should determine the recommended executor. Repeatedly time matched work
without a concurrent simulation distorting comparisons.

Complete three journeys: funding under uncertain persistent identity, servicing
under private-state information, and discovery leading to either a qualified
effect set or an information request. They share request/result semantics,
provenance and reporting, while retaining different identification and
sampling laws. In particular, the independent-pair persistent-linkage result
cannot be joined to a common-calendar dependent score without a new joint
sampling argument; the interface must reject that unsupported composition.
Demonstrate at least two consumers and a new eligible request
within each supported family. Deliberately incompatible clock, population,
unit, policy-information and inference requests must fail meaningfully.
Existing sibling interfaces can be inspected and mapped locally; accepted
integration needs their owners and is a distinct later outcome.

Compare against an expert composing existing libraries with the same inputs,
target, inference and output obligations. Current local execution can measure
computational parity and reconstruction effort within the project. General
analyst savings need repeated matched tasks by independent users. Do not turn
implementation time or a single author's familiarity into an adoption estimate.

## C08: scholarly and evidential closure

Rewrite the affected argument as a coherent whole. Each capability must explain
its scientific purpose, inherited results, project derivations, alternatives,
adverse evidence and product consequences. Keep execution bookkeeping outside
the product narrative. Review substantive passages against the preserved
FinMarketing, BayesFilter and CIP examples, not against their page counts.

For each changed claim, preserve the genuine cited PDF or official source,
identity, version/date, hash, inspected anchor and exact support. New searches
use the existing 7 October cutoff and disclose coverage limits. Unavailable
originals stay in the omissions register. Complete affected mathematical and
execution checks, source audit, build and rendered inspection before binding
the new delivery. Changed evidence must be revalidated; replacing stale hashes
is not a scientific replay.

Use two dispositions for each gap: whether the scholarly construction and
comparison are complete, and whether the requested capability is supported,
conditional, uninformative, unsupported or still unresolved. Another reader
must be able to reconstruct a representative new eligible request and its
financial conclusion or refusal. Independent review is subsequent challenge,
not a substitute for this author work.

## What is not known, and how it can be resolved

| Open question | Why it is open | Next way to learn or legitimate outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Will sharper linkage inference be financially useful? | Geometry can reduce conservatism; it cannot create rank information. Finite response calibration also remains conditional. | C03 compares methods and audit designs. An unbounded set or unresolved decision remains possible. |
| Can the original data identify a focal contrast at exact singularity? | The displayed mean equations leave it free. This is a limitation of those restrictions, not a proof about every full mixture model. | Add outcome-linked identity information or new model restrictions, or return ambiguity/change the target explicitly. |
| Will nonlinear policy intervals remain calibrated beyond the selected model? | Weak information, model misspecification and numerical approximation interact; a likelihood fit alone settles none of them. | C04 supplies a bounded reference and adverse evidence. A broader claim needs another identification/approximation argument. |
| Can arbitrary learned latent graphs yield useful confidence sets cheaply? | Observational equivalence, unmeasured causes, selection and model search may make the set large and optimization hard. | C05 closes a small declared class. No general scalable solution is established in this project or promised by this plan. |
| Can semantics or audit bias be recovered without trustworthy extra evidence? | Competing meanings or biased label processes can fit the same observed records. | Seek source/measurement evidence, randomize audits where possible, or retain sensitivity/abstention. |
| Will this outperform expert composition or scale on the bank's platform? | Workload, access patterns, user behavior and infrastructure are not observed here. | Run the matched-user and real-platform experiments; retain or narrow the product if it loses. |

The research phases have a staged commitment: first identification and source
review, then an independent small numerical reference, then a preserved pilot
for cost and failure discovery, then a registered final comparison. Pilot
outcomes can revise a design before registration, with the original preserved;
final outcomes cannot be used to choose favorable seeds, regimes or intervals.
Choose repetitions and numerical tolerances from stated precision and error
budgets. For example, about 2,000 independent draws give roughly a one-percentage-
point 95% Monte Carlo half-width near .95 coverage; a more expensive smaller
study must report its wider uncertainty. A favorable coverage point estimate
alone is not a pass criterion or a theorem.

No reliable schedule for the research branches follows from the current files.
Pilot timings and optimization diagnostics should determine resource estimates.
Known repairs proceed first; an unresolved branch must not keep already
qualified capabilities from being described accurately. External participants
are needed for independent review, authentic bank data/semantics, accepted
interfaces and workflow economics. Their participation is not assumed or
requested by tools as part of preparing this proposal.
