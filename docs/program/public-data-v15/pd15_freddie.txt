Single Family Loan-Level Dataset
As part of a larger effort to increase transparency, Freddie Mac is making available loan-level credit performance data on all mortgages that the company purchased or guaranteed from 1999 to 2026.
Loan-Level Dataset Files
To access the Single-Family Loan-Level Dataset, register and sign-in to Clarity Data Intelligence. The full dataset, Standard Dataset by year, Non-Standard Dataset, sample files, and RPL Mapping file can be downloaded via the SFLLD Data Download page.
Access Historical Data
Access Historical Data
The availability of this data will help investors build more accurate credit performance models in support of ongoing risk sharing initiatives.
The Standard and Non-Standard datasets cover approximately 56 million mortgages originated between January 1, 1999 and March 31, 2026. Monthly loan performance data, including credit performance information up to and including property disposition, is being disclosed through March 31, 2026. Specific credit performance information in the dataset includes voluntary prepayments and loans that were Foreclosure Alternatives and REOs. Specific actual loss data in the dataset includes net sales proceeds, MI recoveries, non-MI recoveries, expenses, current deferred UPB, and due date of last paid installment.
The information in the historical dataset is unaudited and subject to change. Freddie Mac cannot guarantee the dataset is complete or error free. Read our
disclaimer
. The historical dataset is not to be construed as securities disclosure.
In addition, Freddie Mac requires a
licensing agreement
for commercial redistribution of the data in its Single-Family Loan-Level Dataset. Use of the dataset continues to be free for non-commercial, academic/research and for limited use, subject to the applicable terms and conditions.
