Chapter 75

Evaluating causal assistance without
changing the question

The question throughout this chapter is whether assistance preserves a banking decision while
improving its resolution at an acceptable cost. The opening defines the comparison; the
coverage screen and prospective experiment then show what a finite evaluation could establish.
The source studies test the reference answers, exposure history, adjudication and scoring rules
on which that experiment would rely. Finally, the evaluator, memory and toolkit comparisons
follow those requirements into a working interface. Their different failure mechanisms cannot
be reduced to a single accuracy score.


75.1     The benchmark unit is a causal problem
An agent can produce a plausible report, execute valid code and still answer the wrong causal
question. Evaluation must follow the entire chain from business meaning through assumptions
and identification to uncertainty, availability and decision loss. A benchmark of text fluency,
graph edit distance or successful tool calls measures only part of that chain.
   CLadder demonstrates the value of formal questions with varied verbalizations, including
commonsense and deliberately unfamiliar or conflicting stories. The inspected paper also
identifies language-to-formal-tool formulation as a continuing challenge. Its historical binary-
answer accuracy is evidence about those tasks and models; it is not a measurement of effect
coverage, decision loss or banking readiness. Jin et al. (2023)
  CausalDS expands the evaluation object to narrated synthetic mechanisms, data and a
separate observation model. Its inspected results show why symbolic success, uncertainty
quality, abstention and cost should be reported separately. Its author-reported exam and
repeated runs are useful design evidence, not model evaluations conducted in this project. A
generated world with known truth also remains different from authentic bank data whose
semantic and assignment mechanisms must be established externally. Leban and Sun (2026b)
  The proposed experimental unit is therefore an independently generated or independently
adjudicated causal mechanism and question. Several phrasings, label conditions or stochastic
model calls can be nested within that unit. They do not create new independent worlds.
Repeating one problem fifty times can measure response variation for that problem; it does
not provide fifty independent demonstrations of general causal competence.

open-loop policies, and the paper explicitly says finite held-out predictive tests do not establish
causal identification. The banking design retains attempt counts and numerical computation
costs and separates prediction, experiment choice and effect identification.

formal/numerical checks, source-grounded human review and selective assistance. They do
not establish one best model, general causal competence or positive bank ROI. The reported
results remain literature evidence from distinct tasks, populations and model settings.


75.10.3     Reusing benchmarks without inheriting their labels blindly

The inspected CLadder source is pinned to commit 3d2d1169b4b9 (2024-05-29), with an
MIT notice. Its query generator contains a Wald-ratio IV template and uses ATE bounds in
constructing answers. An unrestricted IV graph does not identify a population ATE through
the Wald ratio. Additional assumptions may identify a different target or a sign; the full
generator and item assumptions must be checked before admitting a label. Quarantine IV-as-
population-ATE cases and resolve repository/paper dataset-version differences. Retain the
generator as a candidate source of formal fixtures after that review.
   The inspected CausalDS source is pinned to c74671d0a092 (2026-07-17), with Apache-2.0
code and a CC0 data notice. Its separation of conceptual SCM, observed proxy, private truth
and scorer is useful. The inspected grader can default missing identification metadata toward
identifiable and distinguishes intentional abstention from malformed output. It computes both
uncapped and capped normalized interval-score summaries; publish both. Require explicit
identified/nonidentified/unknown states, finite endpoint validation, preserved observation
semantics and raw tail losses before reuse. These are static source findings, not installed-
package results or a completed dependency/license audit.
  The first prototype should import reviewed fixture definitions and expected outputs through
a narrow adapter, then compare the adapter against an independently written oracle on tiny
cases. The trusted scorer must remain outside the agent’s writable task environment. Freeze
hashes of code, data, prompts, retrieval corpus and scoring rules. Impose tool-output and
runtime limits while retaining the failure status and incurred cost. Exposed development
examples are useful for debugging but cannot also be the hidden quality test. Neither upstream
package has been executed for this synthesis. (causalNLP/cladder; andleb/causalds, 2026)

75.12      Selective adjudication is an observation mechanism

outputs, disputed graphs or expensive decisions may be an efficient way to find defects. The
error proportion in that selected set is not automatically the error proportion among all
assigned cases. The review policy is part of the observation model, just as an account-selection
rule is part of a banking data analysis.

75.16.3    What software-repair evidence contributes

Zhou and colleagues’ preprint reviews 58 primary studies within its stated search scope.
Its synthesis identifies heuristic vulnerability labels, repair datasets lacking tests, limited
input granularity and offline evaluation/deployment gaps. Those are findings of that dated
review, not current universal absence claims. Its threats-to-validity discussion acknowledges
terminology and search-selection limits. We use the review as primary evidence of its own
synthesis and proposed roadmap; individual model-performance results remain dependent on
their original studies. (Zhou et al., 2024)
   The connection to causal software is direct at the level of validation logic. A generated
adapter may compile and pass a sampled test while reversing an intervention flag, dropping
failed requests or joining future information. For a finite example, let the required function
be f (x) = x on {0, 1}. A constant-zero patch passes a test at 0 and fails the specification at
1. The number of passing tests cannot be interpreted without their coverage and oracle. For
a separate label example, predictions can agree with all four heuristic reference labels and
disagree with all four stipulated true labels. These are exact constructions, not allegations
about a particular benchmark. They motivate independent scientific oracles and adverse cases
for generated code. (Causal banking proposal project, 2026fg)
   The vulnerability survey’s discussion of training/evaluation overlap also matters to a public
causal-agent study. A proposal should bind task provenance, source versions, temporal or
structural splits, and known exposure limits. Public accessibility makes a benchmark useful
for reproducibility but does not establish that a model has never encountered it. A new
natural-language paraphrase need not create a new causal problem. Splitting by graph family,

   Equation (10) also fails in its stated class of bounded losses. Set ℓ(y, a) = 1/2 for every
label and action, retain the fair model and true law, and use ψ(h) = (1 − exp(−h))/2. This

The global entropy–risk assumption therefore holds. The total-variation Lipschitz assumption
holds with LD = 1, although its minimal constant is zero. Sensitivity and collapse gap are zero,
H = log 2, and λ = 1 satisfies the condition. The claimed upper bound is 1/4, while actual
self-risk is 1/2. This is a counterexample to the general bounded-loss claim, not a proposed
banking loss. (Causal banking proposal project, 2026cm)
   The proof text reveals the algebraic difficulties. Adding and subtracting ψ(H) does not
permit dropping the positive addend from an upper bound. The condition λH ≥ ψ(H) gives
−λH ≤ −ψ(H), opposite to the direction used to justify a larger right-hand side. Replacing a
supremum by a smaller expectation also tightens an upper bound without justification. Its first
step compares the original distribution’s loss at a different modal action with loss at its own
modal action in the wrong direction for zero–one loss. A valid narrow statement is possible,
but these steps do not prove the entropy improvement. (Hiremath and Hiremath, 2026)
   A second finite example isolates expected versus worst perturbations. At the reference
input the model is a point mass; at one adverse perturbation it is fair. Give that perturbation
probability 1/100 and the reference 99/100, so the two-point law has full support. Expected
total variation is 1/200, whereas its supremum and worst self-risk are 1/2. Small average
sensitivity need not certify worst-case behavior. (Causal banking proposal project, 2026cm)

75.25      Controlled model writes preserve a conditional proof
           boundary
A credit agent may correctly execute a rule after incorrectly reading its premise. For example,
an admitted statement that an account is in arrears may trigger a valid collection rule even when
the underlying record does not support that statement. CPUNeSy addresses the admission
boundary between language-model proposals and a symbolic executor. Its inspected September
2026 arXiv original separates source checking, restricted predicate writes, derivation verification
and selective serving. The paper’s running header claims a 2027 conference publication; that
self-description is not independently verified acceptance as of the literature cutoff. The evidence
here is the versioned original and its explicit assumptions and reported observations. (Li et al.,
2026e)


75.25.1     What can be certified, and what remains a premise

Let St be admitted source units, Γt = At ∪ Rt the active facts and rules, and Cn(Γt ) their
closure in a declared decidable Horn fragment. The source mode of the proposed gate checks
type and source support before admitting a unit. Its derived mode requires a complete acyclic
witness rooted at the claimed conclusion, checks every edge and refuses a witness exceeding a
declared depth budget. Certificates retain dependencies and checker versions; a source erasure
requires eviction or revalidation of affected cached consequences. The paper’s state-soundness
result assumes a sound source checker and a sound fragment verifier. Its induction establishes
the stated invariant under those assumptions. It explicitly says that source soundness has not
been established for the experimental natural-language checkers. (Li et al., 2026e)
   A narrower invariant is available without statistical independence. If the allowed source
vocabulary Ω excludes every rule-head predicate, a direct write of an intermediate or target
predicate is rejected. It does not follow that every admitted leaf is true. In an independently
authored toy rule, arrears ⇒ deny, admit only arrears as a writable source predicate.
Directly writing deny is then impossible, but a false admitted arrears premise still derives
it. The proposed banking contract should therefore record both derivation status and source-
support status. Neither status establishes an intervention estimand, an adjustment set or
the truth of a borrower causal diagram. (Li et al., 2026e; Causal banking proposal project,
2026bq)
   The voting argument likewise retains its sampling assumptions. It separates a shared-error
query mass β from a regime with independent slips. Conditional independence across traces is
required for its concentration bounds, including within the shared-error regime. Two agreeing
calls do not estimate β or establish those assumptions. A stipulated example with β = 1/5, a
common wrong answer on that mass and independent slip probability 1/10 elsewhere gives
two-vote wrong, correct and abstained probabilities 26/125, 81/125 and 18/125. Answered risk
is 26/107. Three-vote majority error is 139/625; arbitrarily many independent votes within
each regime still leave error floor 1/5. More calls can reduce random slips while preserving a
shared semantic mistake. (Li et al., 2026e; Causal banking proposal project, 2026bq)

75.25.3    A prospective banking protocol and its cost boundary

The internal hypothesis protocol has no independently verified preregistration timestamp.
Complete per-query certificate archival was not instrumented in every adapter; model caches
lack consistent sampling dates and immutable revisions. The paper describes an offline audit
package, but the inspected PDF does not establish custody of that package in this project. Its
coverage bound is not empirically instantiated from step-level channel rates, and independence
remains unverified. Candidate-pool restriction and token savings must also be separated from
physical storage compression: reported bounded redundancy is 37.1% in the mathematical
corpus and 0.60% in the legal corpus, below the internal 50% target. Physical index-byte
savings and the scheduler-specific crossover remain unmeasured. (Li et al., 2026e)
   A bounded prototype can nevertheless test a concrete question. Freeze a public or generated
banking rule corpus with explicit leaf facts, source spans, admissible predicates and known
interventions before held-out queries. Compare an unrestricted translator plus solver, restricted
writes, fixed-trace source rechecking and a separately labeled fallback. Inject both prohibited
target writes and false but well-typed leaves. Test dependency deletion, changed-source cache
invalidation, missing evidence, contradictory records, abstention and full failure reporting.
Independent labels must judge source faithfulness separately from formal derivability. This is a
prospective proposal, not an implemented source checker or live-model trial. (Li et al., 2026e)
