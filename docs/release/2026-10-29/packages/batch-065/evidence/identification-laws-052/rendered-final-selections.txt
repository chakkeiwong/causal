40.22.1     Inspecting recursion without mistaking it for a correctness proof

The native do-calculus source constructs backdoor and frontdoor expressions, then provides a
general recursive identification path. Its branches restrict the graph to outcome ancestors, add
irrelevant remaining variables to the intervention, partition observed nodes connected through
latent co-children, and multiply or marginalize the resulting kernels. A single remaining full
confounded component triggers a hedge exception. The induced submodel keeps observed
identifiers and retains a latent descriptor only when it still has at least two retained children.
These observations identify the algorithmic objects that a comparison must preserve; they
are not a proof that every legal input reaches the mathematically intended branch. (aGrUM
authors and contributors, 2026)
   Two details deserve explicit qualification before reuse. The conditional-query path builds one
modified graph after removing incoming edges to intervention nodes and outgoing edges from
every conditioning node, then tests candidate promotions within that graph. A comparison
must establish the appropriate multi-variable graph surgery and the conditional query after
each promotion. Separately, when an accumulated kernel is present, the first topological
factor is constructed as a joint-probability AST rather than the numerator expression just
calculated from that kernel. Whether that branch is reachable with a distinct marginal, or
is equivalent under the implementation’s invariant, requires a proof-aligned fixture. Neither
observation alone warrants announcing a software defect. Both are precise obligations that a
root README or successful simple adjustment example would miss. (aGrUM authors and
contributors, 2026)
   The numerical layer has its own boundary. A formula’s identified predicate checks whether
an expression root exists. Evaluation then requires an observational Bayesian network, so a
structure-only answer and an evaluable answer are different states. Division of expression trees
delegates to tensor division. The earlier selected files do not settle zero-denominator support
handling, finite arithmetic or an uncertainty statement. The more detailed comparison in
Section 38.5 resolves a bounded part of that source question: selected tensor-division branches
introduce no support-zero refusal, ordinary zero-sum normalization leaves the tensor unchanged,
and CPT normalization refuses a zero-sum conditioning row. Those static findings specify native
fixtures; they do not establish universal runtime behavior or statistical uncertainty. (aGrUM
and pyAgrum contributors, 2026; Causal banking proposal project, 2026cs) The Python
convenience wrapper retains the model on the returned tensor and maps an unidentified
formula to a None numerical answer; complete exception and lifetime conversions still need
direct inspection and execution. Preserve an expression with its graph, variable domains and
support conditions instead of returning an unqualified floating-point success. (aGrUM authors
and contributors, 2026)
