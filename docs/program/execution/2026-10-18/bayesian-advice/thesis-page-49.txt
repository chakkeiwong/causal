    Overall, for the four datasets, the best SHD results were from the prior distributions
alone; for both the MAGIC-7 and Summer datasets, using DeepSeek alone yields the best
overall performance in terms of both E-SHD and AUROC. This indicates that modern
LLMs are highly effective at solving simpler CD tasks, particularly discovering small
graphs in well-known domains. Thus, these methods have caveats. LLMs should be used
on domains which they are expected to have knowledge of. And, when using BSL, one
should be sure the assumptions of the structure learning method hold in the data.
    One limitation of using (E-)SHD as a metric is that a DAG with some correctly placed
edges and some incorrect ones may have the same SHD as a graph in which all edges are
incorrectly placed. Additionally, it does not reward accurate estimation of uncertainty.
