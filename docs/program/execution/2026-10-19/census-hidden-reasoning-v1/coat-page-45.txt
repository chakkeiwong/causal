Proof [Proof for Prop. 3] From Eq. 5, we have

                        H(Y | h[k] (X); wk+1 (X)) < H(Y | h[k] (X));                     (13)

Since Y ⊥
        ⊥ h[k+1]\S (X) | hS (X), we have

                       H(Y | hS (X); h[k+1]\S (X)) = H(Y | hS (X)).                      (14)

Therefore:
                       H(Y | hS (X)) = H(Y | hS (X), h[k+1]\S (X))
                                         = H(Y | h[k+1] (X))
                                                                                         (15)
                                         = H(Y | h[k] (X), wk+1 (X))
                                         < H(Y | h[k] (X))
Also note that

                   H(Y | hS (X), X) = H(Y | X) = H(Y | h[k] (X), X),                     (16)

therefore:

      H(Y | hS (X)) − H(Y | hS (X), X) < H(Y | h[k] (X)) − H(Y | h[k] (X), X),           (17)

which is Eq. 6.
