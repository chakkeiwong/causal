window.set_minicart_scroll = function() {
        if ($('.minicart-items-wrapper').height() > 412) {
            $('.minicart-items-wrapper').addClass('overflowed');
        } else {
            $('.minicart-items-wrapper').removeClass('overflowed');
        }
    };

    window.load_minicart_func = function() {
        $.get('/store/customer/section/load/?sections=cart&force_new_section_timestamp=true',function(data,status) {
            var total_nums = data.cart.summary_count,
            subtotal   = data.cart.subtotal,
            product_items = data.cart.items;
            $('span.counter').text('('+total_nums+')').show();
        
            if (product_items.length > 0) {
                $("#cartCount").html(total_nums);
                var pro_item,content;
                var itdsc = total_nums == 1 ? 'item' : 'items';
                content = '<div class="minicart-block"><div class="minicart-total"><span id="total_num_tp">'+total_nums+'</span> '+itdsc+' in cart</div>';
                content +='<div class="minicart-items-wrapper" style="max-height:410px;overflow-y:auto;">';
                content +='<ul class="minicart-items">';
            
                for (var i in product_items) {
                    pro_item = product_items[i];
                    content += '<li><img class="productImg" src="'+pro_item['product_image']['src']+'"/>';
                    content += '<div class="product-item-details"><div class="product-item-name-block">';
                    content += '<a href="javascript:;" class="product-name">'+pro_item['product_name']+'</a>';
                    content += '<dl class="product options list"><dt class="label" style="display: none;">Quantity:</dt><dd class="values"></dd></dl></div>';
                    content += '<div class="details-qty qty">Qty: <span>'+pro_item['qty']+'</span></div>'; 
                    content += '<div class="product-item-pricing">'+pro_item['product_price']+'</div>';
                    content += '</div></li>';
                }  
                
                content += '</ul>';
                content += '</div><div class="subtotal"><span class="label">Subtotal: </span><span id="subtotal_price">'+subtotal+'</span></div>';
                content += '<div class="toolbar"><button class="blue_btn action primary">View cart</button></div></div>';
            
                $('.minicart-content').html(content);
                $('button.primary').unbind().click(function() {
                    window.location.href="/store/checkout/cart/";
                });
                set_minicart_scroll();
            } else {
                $("#cartCount").html();
                $('span.counter').hide();
                $('.minicart-content').html('<strong style="font-size: 14px; display: block; text-align: center; font-weight: 500; font-family: Inter; color: #475569;">Your cart is currently empty</strong></div>');
            }
        });
    };
    
    load_minicart_func();

    $(document).ready(function() {

                $('.welCome').show().text('Hello, ' + data.customer_name);

        load_minicart_func();
    });

What is a single-user license?
A single-user license for Stata is an identified-user license. The
   registered user is the only authorized user of that license.
A single-user license allows Stata to be installed on multiple
   computers as long as the authorized user is the only one with access
   to the software.
Single user licenses may be either annual or perpetual.
Annual licenses
An annual license entitles you to receive updates and new releases
       to Stata software throughout your license term, all at no additional
       cost.
An annual license lets you enjoy uninterrupted access to technical
       support from a dedicated staff of expert Stata programmers and
       statisticians.
An annual license is a one-year license. The term for an annual
       license begins the day we process your order. Prior to the one-year
       expiration date, you may renew your license to ensure continued access
       to Stata software.
Renewals are easy. Choose "Renew my license automatically" to
       subscribe to Stata, or renew your license quickly online.
Perpetual licenses
A perpetual license will not expire.
Perpetual licenses include one year of maintenance to ensure you
            maintain access to the latest features and updates. You will
            receive a convenient reminder to renew the maintenance prior to
            the one-year expiration.
Student perpetual licenses do not include maintenance. If
                   you own a student perpetual license and would like to
                   upgrade to a new version of Stata, you may purchase
                   an upgrade separately.
If you decide to not renew your maintenance, upgrades to new
            versions of Stata must be purchased separately.
A single-user license may be upgraded to a new edition of Stata for an
  additional cost, for example, from Stata/SE to Stata/MP. If it is an annual license, the license will maintain its original
  expiration date.
Prof+ Plan
and
student
discounts are available to qualified
  individuals.
All Stata licenses include
PDF documentation
.
View the single-user license terms and conditions.

What is a volume purchase of single-user licenses?
A volume purchase of single-user licenses is a purchase of multiple
single-user
licenses placed
at the same
  time
in order to take advantage of falling per unit costs.
Purchasing multiple copies of Stata under a volume discount does
  not mean that the copies are connected. They are independent licenses.
A volume purchase discount decreases the overall price paid for multiple
  single-user licenses purchased together.
View the single-user license terms and conditions.

Pricing

What is a network license?
A network license allows a specific number of concurrent users.
A network license allows Stata software to be installed on multiple
  computers within a single geographic location or physical site.
The minimum size network is a 2-user license. If the license is on
  the current version of Stata, the size of the license may be increased
  for a reduced per-user charge. For example, if you currently own a
  5-user network license, the price of adding another user would be the
  price of adding user number 6.
A network license may be annual or perpetual.
Annual licenses
An annual license entitles you to receive updates and new releases
          to Stata software throughout your license term, all at no additional
          cost.
An annual license lets you enjoy uninterrupted access to technical
          support from a dedicated staff of expert Stata programmers and
          statisticians.
An annual license is a one-year license. The term for an annual
          license begins the day we process your order. Prior to the one-year
          expiration date, you may renew your license to ensure continued access
          to Stata software.
Renewals are easy. Choose "Renew my license automatically" to
          subscribe to Stata, or renew your license quickly online.
Perpetual licenses
A perpetual license will not expire.
Perpetual licenses include one year of maintenance to ensure you
            maintain access to the latest features and updates. You will
            receive a convenient reminder to renew the maintenance prior to
            the one-year expiration.
If you decide to not renew your maintenance, upgrades to new
            versions of Stata must be purchased separately.
A network license may be upgraded to a new edition of Stata for an
  additional cost, for example, from Stata/SE to Stata/MP. If it is an annual license, 
  the license will maintain its original expiration date.
All users of a network license have access to
PDF documentation
.
View the network license terms and conditions.
Site
Quote request
A site license for Stata provides your organization or university with 
  access to the most
  current version of Stata. All users work together
  seamlessly, both within and across departments, always with the most
  up-to-date version of the software.
If a new version of Stata is available, a site license receives
     priority access to the new version at no additional charge.
A site license ensures continued access to updates—including
     enhancements, bug fixes, and security patches.
A site license can be customized to
include or not include usage on personal devices or usage on
     university-owned assets only,
cover an entire university or just a single department,
grant access to both faculty/staff and students, or to either group
     independently.
A site license automatically qualifies your university for the
Stata Prof+ Plan
.
A site license is an annual license.
A site license has only one serial number to maintain and usage
  is not monitored throughout the year. It conveniently centralizes Stata
  purchases into a single annual payment.
A site license provides uninterrupted access to technical support from
  a dedicated staff of expert Stata programmers and statisticians.
All site license users have access to
PDF documentation
.
Access to Stata on the Wharton Research Data Services (WRDS) cloud
  environment is available to all site license users that subscribe to the
  WRDS service. For more details, please visit
https://wrds-web.wharton.upenn.edu/

What is a Student Lab license?
A Student Lab license is appropriate for student use for classwork or
  for use by faculty for teaching. It is not appropriate for research,
  government, or corporate training.
The minimum-size Student Lab license is
10
. A 10-user Student Lab
  license is a license for 10 users. If licensed for concurrent
  use, the Student Lab license can be installed on a network or on a server.
  The Student Lab license may also be installed on individual machines
  or student laptops in a bring your own device environment up to the number
  of users allowed by the license.
The Student Lab license is an annual license. Annual licenses expire
  one year from the date of purchase. Annual licenses ensure all users
  have access to the latest version of Stata.
All Student Lab users have access to
PDF documentation
.
A
Prof+ Plan
is a great
  complement to any Student Lab.
View the Student Lab license terms and conditions.
