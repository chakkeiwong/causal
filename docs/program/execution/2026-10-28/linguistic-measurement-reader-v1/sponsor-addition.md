# Measure the intervention before funding the language workflow

A banking assistant that proposes rewriting a complaint response must distinguish the writer's intent, the customer's perception, a classifier label and the actual editing procedure. The inspected linguistic-effects preprint explicitly qualifies its directional attenuation argument by classifier quality and homogeneous effect direction. Its observational CFPB example uses outcome-matched complaints and extreme politeness scores; it supplies a design reference, not known causal truth for all bank customers. Public complaints and generated outcomes can justify a prototype before an expensive bank experiment, provided their roles and selection limits remain explicit. [@exec28_lm_pryzant]

An independently authored, exactly enumerated example shows why this matters. Two equally weighted populations have true effects of USD20 and minus USD10 per account. A proxy with 77.5 percent overall accuracy produces an adjusted contrast of minus USD4 although the true average is plus USD5. For a stipulated 10,000 accounts, that is minus USD40,000 versus plus USD50,000 before costs. The example uses heterogeneous effects and stochastic measurement; it is neither a refutation of the source's qualified result nor an observed TextCause failure. The investment implication is to test conditional label errors and intervention meaning alongside predictive accuracy. [@exec28_lm_reference]

Direct reuse has specific unresolved inputs. The pinned LLM-CD and LLM-and-SCD trees lack root permission notices, while LCM's code notice does not establish the identities, provenance and terms of its externally linked pretrained models. Each needs component evidence and an admitted environment before runtime adoption. A CLI or MCP response should expose unavailable artifacts and execution failures explicitly. A separately reviewed public prototype can proceed where a particular external artifact remains unavailable. These are precise adoption conditions, not reasons to suspend independent proposal work.
