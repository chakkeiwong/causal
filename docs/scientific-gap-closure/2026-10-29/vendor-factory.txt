What is a Digital Worker's function?
A Digital Worker is a multi-agentic system that owns a complete business workflow from start to finish.
It does the whole job end-to-end.
Collapse
A Digital Worker completes the full process with judgment at every step. It's built from multiple specialised AI agents working together as a team.
Each agent handles one part of the job (e.g. gathering data, analysing it, checking quality, producing the output).
Same process + outputs. Automated.
Expand
Business Leaders are accountable for architecting the workflow & for the outputs/deliverables.
A Digital Worker automates everything in-between. It works inside your existing systems and tools (CRM, ERP, email, databases).
Humans always stay in control.
Expand
Digital Workers escalate edge cases, flag low-confidence decisions, and pause for approval wherever judgment or accountability matters.
Every action is captured in a full audit trail, always available.
