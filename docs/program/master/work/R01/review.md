# R01 author review and disposition

The changed information design passed the preregistered valid-law screen. The
replicated randomized method’s simultaneous one-sided lower coverage bounds
are 0.9680, 0.9540, 0.9651, 0.9623, 0.9651 and 0.9843 for baseline, short
calendar, low audit, near-rank, exact-rank and persistence cells. Every
request is finite. The inherited stacked challenger fails the same screen in
every valid cell, with lower bounds from 0.870 to 0.924. The adverse cells
false exogeneity, wrong inclusion and label errors fail as expected and remain
in the denominator.

The replicated method reports monetary widths and costs: the ordinary cells
use 24 independent calendars of 50 origins and about 1,500 linked labels per
request; the exact costs are in the request summaries. Its estimator is
conditional on true randomized action mean/variance, truthful audits, known
inclusion and independent calendars. This qualifies D01 and D09 only for that
scoped information design. D10 remains conditional progress because no bank
width/loss threshold or price was supplied. No universal correction, bank
usefulness or ROI follows.
