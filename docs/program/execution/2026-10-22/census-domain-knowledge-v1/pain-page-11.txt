               3.3.3. System-Wide parameters
                   Across KG-RAG, LLM, and RAG systems, we use Mistral 7B-Instruct
               v0.3 [28]. We selected Mistral 7B-Instruct v0.3 as our LLM because it is open
               source, providing fine-grained control over system prompts and hyperparam-
               eters, while controlling for unknown variables characteristic of proprietary
               systems. Furthermore, since Mistral 7B-Instruct v0.33 was released prior to
               the publication of the expert causal graph [12], the ground truth could not
               have been included in the training data of the LLM.
