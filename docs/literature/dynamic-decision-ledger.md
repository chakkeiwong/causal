## Funding: which dynamic inference is justified?

The inherited DML partialling-out score and the Newey–West covariance result
are the primary building blocks. Ciganovic et al.'s 2026 time-series paper is
the closest general dependent-learning route. Its Assumption 2.4 explicitly
requires conditional plug-in stability; it cannot be invoked solely because
training excludes the evaluated block. The selected independent-pilot finite
basis supplies a direct remainder/rate argument instead. The price innovation,
fixed stability margin and pilot invariance remain substantive restrictions.

External-IV projections (Stock–Watson, Casini) address private anticipation
using additional exclusion information and possibly a different complier
target. Lag augmentation addresses persistence under its innovation model;
it cannot cure an invalid external instrument. Multiway DML/panel moments
address different sampling dimensions, while system GMM adds initial-law
restrictions. MIDAS/factor/cointegration alternatives change the observation
or dynamic law. The manuscript now states which information would cause a
method change rather than presenting these as interchangeable estimators.

## Cohorts and donor inference

Callaway–Sant'Anna provides the cohort target and normalized DR representation;
the project differentiates both normalizers and estimated cohort shares.
The distinction between double robustness of the point functional and of
inference at misspecified nuisance limits is retained. Abadie 2021 supplies
the donor interpretation and warns against treating observational placebo
ranks as randomized assignments. The selected exact sharp-null test uses a
declared randomized regional pilot and refits donors for each assignment.

The closest substantive challenger is Chernozhukov–Wüthrich–Zhu's conformal
construction, located in Abadie's bibliography and acquired as real arXiv
v10. Sections 2–3, Assumptions 1–5, Theorems 1–2 and Appendix D distinguish
exact exchangeability from approximate dependent inference under prediction
consistency or stability/mixing. Its sharp-path hypothesis is not the generic
heterogeneous cohort ATT. Synthetic DiD and augmented SC change donor fitting
and robustness; they do not remove the need to choose an inference design.

Version trace: Callaway's preserved December 2020 manuscript is linked to its
2021 DOI. The conformal PDF archive label says May 2021 but its title page

explicitly cites the preserved manuscript rather than claiming an exact
publisher version. The source's assumptions are the ones actually inspected.
Stopping rationale: assignment-rank inference and temporal-residual conformal
inference are the relevant competing uncertainty mechanisms for the chosen
donor decision; there is no promise to implement every donor estimator.

## Observation and linkage

Slawski's 2025 audit correction is the direct starting point for the mismatch
issue. Its bibliography identifies Guha–Reiter–Mercatanti (2022), a closer
challenger for whole probabilistic maps. Both its arXiv v2 and actual
Bayesian Analysis publisher PDF were acquired. The final paper explicitly
discusses the disjoint linking/covariate assumption and conditioning changes
when variables overlap; technical comparison uses that final text. The
strong-ignorability, no-duplicate and linked-population restrictions are
carried into the method decision. Outcome feedback can be coherent under
the joint model; it cannot be imported into an independently calibrated
frequentist experiment without changing its inferential argument.

The selected pair-assignment construction inverts a two-response mixing
matrix per stratum and propagates calibration through the complete dynamic
curve. It is a project model, not a claim that Guha or Slawski proved this
exact longitudinal estimator. A near-singular mapping is withheld. Source
fixtures preserve ambiguity and an actual wrong acceptance under false
metadata. No cited linkage method establishes semantic truth by itself.

Version trace: Slawski's Crossref title query did not reliably find its arXiv
preprint; that is an index limitation, not evidence of no successor. The
publisher Guha PDF resolves a concrete earlier-version limitation. The

Stopping rationale: independent-audit correction, whole-map joint posterior
modeling and unresolved-set output cover the decisive choices for the bounded
observation problem. General linkage under outcome-dependent mismatch is
not represented as solved.
