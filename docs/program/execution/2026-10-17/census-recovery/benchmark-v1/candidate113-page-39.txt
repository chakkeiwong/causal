B.5    Computational Cost
The total evaluation required 1,152 API calls across three models. At Groq’s inference
pricing (free tier for open models at time of evaluation), the monetary cost was zero.
Total wall-clock time was approximately 18 hours due to rate limiting (30-second
delays between calls). Metric computation for all 1,152 instances completed in under
2 minutes on a standard laptop (Intel i7, 16GB RAM).
