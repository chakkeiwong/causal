# Reviewed preparation of the four matched engine studies

The four design obligations concern the program's selected engines: pcalg in R, y0 in Python, Tetrad in Java and the grf C++ core through its maintained R front end. This is a proposal-stage evaluation design. Native adapters, installation, statistical runs and admission remain separate operations with their existing prerequisites. In particular, the native grf alias and the Tetrad decision do not currently grant integration permission; a passing design cannot bypass them.

1. Bind each existing adapter contract and source audit to its authenticated engine revision. Use the corrected pcalg successor, not its superseded matrix/CI assumptions. Preserve the y0 conditional-ID restriction, Tetrad failure-transparent CI wrapper prerequisite and grf target distinctions.
2. Construct independent finite oracles: exhaustive small Gaussian Markov classes and covariance/effect calculations; rational structural intervention versus observational front-door evaluation; and grf score identities, distinct population targets and analytic known-law moments. The oracles never import the engines or generate final trial data.
3. Specify every regular sampling law, sample size, target, setting, adverse regime, denominator and resource limit. Separate deterministic conversion/semantic conformance from sampling error. No confidence coverage is assigned to a discovered graph or symbolic functional.
4. Compute fixed exact binomial screening boundaries and power before any native result. Reserve a family error budget of 0.05 over 24 one-sided prototype screens, of which 22 are active and two remain unused. The screens can support bounded prototype progression, not nominal 95% calibration or general superiority.
5. Review each design against its two acceptance criteria, replay the finite oracles, verify artifact/source identities and save an individual receipt. Before a future registration, require a complete engine/environment lock, independent generator review, adapter admission, fixed fresh randomness custody and cost feasibility. A missing registration input does not block the proposal design or independent research.

## Preparation review

The proposed study remains matched by scientific question: PC implementations share population laws and full endpoint targets but retain their different orientation settings; y0 is compared by functional semantics; grf is compared on all-population AIPW, not average CATE or overlap targets. Cross-language runtime ranks across these different tasks would be meaningless. Finite reference checks cannot be promoted to native conformance. Resource failure remains in the requested denominator. No smaller sample, seed replacement, successful-only report or threshold revision may rescue a failed registered attempt.

Exact deterministic panels and known-law screens justify a prospective prototype experiment. They do not supply private-bank truth, external acceptance, current vendor prices or deployment reliability. A statistically failed screen is a useful retained negative finding. A pending engine/environment build is local to its future trial. Continue all independent proposal obligations.
