90.4     What the fresh comparisons support
The first fresh comparison uses 600 requests in each of seven laws. Its joint-regression horizon
interval covers 550/600 at 720 dates, with Monte Carlo interval [.8916, .9375]. This is a retained
calibration failure. At 1200 and 4800 dates coverage is .935 and .94; the persistent ρ = .96
case gives .9467. Changing the covariance does not remove all finite-regression error. In the
weak-audit law only 127/600 requests yield intervals, of which 121 cover; conditional coverage
is about .953, but all-request coverage is .202. Reporting only the conditional fraction would
hide the information shortfall. Omitting the quadratic action-mean term gives .2633 coverage;
correlating the action innovation with the structural disturbance gives zero. These are false
scientific declarations that a numerical API cannot detect.
   The exact pilot calculation suggests another route: retain an independent pilot but let
M = 4T . This satisfies the selected sufficient rate and avoids claiming that the small joint-
regression failure has been repaired. A separately frozen fresh study compares the inherited
pilot, a fourfold pilot with Bartlett covariance and a fourfold pilot with horizon covariance.
For the latter, the fitted score converges in mean square to the true-nuisance score, so each of
its fixed-H lag moments converges to the moment in (90.4). The vanishing pilot remainder
supplies the required centering at the desired target. An imperfect pilot held fixed need not
have zero score covariance beyond H; the growing-pilot argument is asymptotic. The last
procedure has coverage .9600, .9417 and .9333 across the three designs in Table 90.3. Each
Monte Carlo interval includes .95, but the point estimate at the largest evaluation sample size
remains below nominal. Its mean SE is 64,431 compared with empirical SD 65,867. Relative to
the same-draw inherited baseline, the coverage changes are 4/600, 0/600 and 1/600; descriptive
paired Monte Carlo intervals include zero. Clear superiority is not established.
   At 720 dates the old pilot was already fourfold; only covariance differs. The 1200/4800
designs require three/twelve times the inherited pilot history. Mean fit-and-inference time for
the horizon route is about 1.35/1.57 times the baseline in those two cases, excluding acquisition.
Thus a justified information rate has a real requirement even where nominal calibration is
statistically compatible. The independent pilot must also represent the same economic law. A
long history drawn from a different assignment regime does not satisfy that requirement.

100.11       A public prototype buys an option to make a later
             investment
A proposal need not know bank ROI before it can justify a small prototype. It does need an
account of which later decision the prototype could change. Let G be the eventual net value
of constructing a selected capability, after all subsequent implementation and operating costs
in a declared horizon. The choice today is to build or decline. A pilot supplies a signal S,
costs K today and delays the later value by a discount factor d ∈ (0, 1]. Under an explicitly
stipulated joint probability model, the two option values are
                W0 = max{0, EG},         Wpilot = −K + d E[max{0, E(G | S)}].
The maximum inside the expectation represents the option to decline after an unfavorable
signal. It must correspond to a feasible stop decision: costs already committed before the
signal cannot be removed by a retrospective choice. Pilot cost is excluded from G because it
is subtracted separately. The law of S describes how the prototype informs the commercial
and scientific state; a benchmark score is not automatically such a signal. (Causal banking
proposal, 2026ag)
   For an exact planning example, let G be USD 100,000 or minus USD 60,000 with equal
probability. Immediate construction has expected net value USD 20,000. Stipulate a pilot
signal with sensitivity and specificity both 4/5. Positive and negative signals each occur
with probability 1/2. Conditional expected construction values are USD 68,000 and minus
USD 28,000, so the pilot policy builds only after a positive signal. Expected value before pilot
cost and delay is USD 34,000. This also follows directly from
