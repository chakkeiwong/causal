On the event β ∈ C1−α , this set contains the true θ whenever the stated restriction is correct.
That inclusion proves its coverage. The region must respect the cluster/time dependence of the
design, and an asymptotic normal region inherits its approximation limits. The conditional,
hybrid and fixed-length methods in Rambachan and Roth (2023, Sections 3–4) offer more
powerful procedures under their own regularity and restriction classes; they are not equivalent
to endpoint substitution.
   Passing a pretreatment trend test cannot establish the extrapolation restriction. Low power
leaves large violations plausible, and using the same test to select which studies or specifications
to report changes the sampling problem. A useful banking analysis reports which financial
conclusion changes as M or M grows, why those departures are operationally plausible, and
which new measurement or experiment would narrow them.

causal model class. Their combination must be defined jointly; independently varying several
knobs and taking the largest separate effect need not bound their combined effect. A policy
