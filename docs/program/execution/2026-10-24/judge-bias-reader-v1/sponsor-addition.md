
### Judge reliability needs scientific correctness and coverage

The inspected MM-JudgeBias preprint separates sensitivity to missing or
mismatched evidence from stability under meaning-preserving changes. Its
1,804-pair vision-language study supplies a test-design reference, not a
bank causal-acceptance result. The proposed evaluator adds exact causal
correctness and refusal oracles, preserves task identities and reports both
conditional agreement and full-request coverage. An authored example has
90% agreement among 50 scored cases but only 45 correct scored judgments
among 100 submissions. A constant score can be perfectly stable while
accepting invalid causal claims. [@exec24_judgebias_original; @exec24_judgebias_reference]

Public or generated fixtures can establish the merit of this evaluation
before costly bank work. Exact original identities, model configuration,
perturbation validity, exclusions, attempts and full costs remain part of
the proposed experiment. The authentic preprint does not stand in for an
unacquired final conference PDF or native benchmark reproduction.
