## What a panel comparison actually buys the bank

A platform rollout and a fee concession can share customers without sharing an intervention date. An apparently common ATT can mean a contrast against a changing baseline, a combined intervention effect, or an average over different adoption cohorts. The proposal requires those meanings to survive the statistical adapter and CLI/MCP response. The primary comparisons explain why a common chart is insufficient. [@exec28_panel_roth; @exec28_panel_tsai; @exec28_panel_wing]

Three synthetic examples make the consequences auditable. With zero effect and a group gap growing by 50 dollars per period, changing from an earliest-period baseline to a pre-period average produces an apparent adoption-date jump. With a 40-dollar platform effect and a minus-30-dollar concession effect, exposure differences of three quarters versus one quarter reduce the naive estimate to 25 dollars; interactions can conceal bias by cancellation. Two stacks with treated counts 10 and 30 and control counts 90 and 10 turn a 50-dollar target into 115 dollars through unequal trend weights. Corrective control weights 5/18 and 15/2 restore 50 dollars in the specified balanced example. These are exact authored calculations, not measured customer economics. [@exec28_panel_reference]

The practical program specifies both event clocks, preserves original unit identities, distinguishes joint from separate effects, retains cohort support and baselines, and propagates dependence through aggregation. The selected multiple-event original has inconsistent second-stage expressions; our comparison follows its explicit appendix derivation and retains later-edition and delegate verification as obligations. Simultaneous adoption lacks the separate-effect anchor in that design. Corrective weights cannot manufacture an absent control or independently validate confidence intervals. [@exec28_panel_tsai; @exec28_panel_wing]

Pinned sources distinguish five reuse candidates. The Roth example is a diagnostic template with unpinned imports; the stacked R/Stata tutorial needs balance, cohort and rights checks; fastdid has a verified author/repository association but needs delegate and edition review; nonabsdid documents presentation harmonization across different estimators; and pampe offers a donor-regression counterfactual comparator. These are conditional integration choices. Documented speed is not a measured benchmark, and MIT or GPL declarations do not settle every dependency's terms. Native, coverage, source and visual obligations remain open. [@exec28_panel_software]
