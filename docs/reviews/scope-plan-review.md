# Review of the scientific scope completion plan

8 October 2026. Author review before execution; not independent validation.

The seven V4 gaps are mapped to T02 (G1), T03 (G2), T04 (G3/G4), T05 (G5),
T01 throughout (G6), and T06 (G7). T07/T08 require connected writing and
reconstruction, not simply the presence of artifacts.

## Defects anticipated and addressed

1. **Another convenient latent example.** A larger randomized HMM would
   leave the private-logger problem intact. The selected new route retains
   state-dependent assignment and an independent emission-validation sample.
   Its scientific cost is a justified measurement model; this is disclosed
   and compared with observed-history and proximal alternatives.
2. **An interval swap without an estimand check.** Lag-augmented ordinary LP
   results do not automatically qualify external-IV curves. The plan keeps
   these targets and innovation restrictions distinct and requires a matched
   comparison before any recommendation.
3. **Favorable simulation as a theorem.** Generators and comparisons are
   fixed before execution. Failures remain in the denominator and earlier
   results remain in the text. Qualification does not imply uniform validity.
4. **Panel methods conflated by table shape.** Common calendar shocks and
   short independent dynamic panels have different information regimes.
   Separate moments, covariance and initial-condition assumptions are required.
5. **A learned DAG made artificially decisive.** Temporal discovery retains
   graph marks and assumptions. The effect step can remain unresolved; it
   cannot orient a PAG for convenience or call selection-conditional intervals
   unconditional confidence.
6. **Supplied semantics relabelled automatic.** The new observation operation
   must generate candidates and use held-out renamed schemas; oracle inclusion
   is measured rather than assumed by the generator. Name scores remain hints.
7. **Software census substituted for integration.** Selected substantive
   causal-library operations must execute against matched numerical references.
   No need to runtime-qualify all 44 reviewed projects.
8. **Public data treated as known causal truth.** The financial application
   checks reproducibility and design, not coverage. Its identifying assumptions
   remain substantive; simulations provide the known-truth comparisons.
9. **More sources substituted for scholarship.** Searches are tied to method
   decisions and traced into arguments. The proposal must explain selection,
   not maximize citations or pages.
10. **Scale measured in irrelevant rows.** Measure fitting and uncertainty
    with statistical dimensions retained; distinguish host measurements from
    distributed architecture and adoption economics.
11. **Administrative completion hiding unfinished science.** Historical R/S
    phase evidence is retained, while the active T program states unresolved
    scholarly obligations. Final closure requires G1–G7 dispositions with
    manuscript arguments, evidence and defended exclusions.
12. **Approval churn and unsafe rule broadening.** Preserve the existing
    bounded collector. Use a local environment for selected dependencies and
    escalate only an observed network/filesystem boundary; no broad interpreter
    approval or sandbox change is part of this program.

## Disposition

The plan is executable within the authorized research workspace. Sources and
available package versions determine exact numerical comparators; any change
must preserve the scientific question and be recorded. Public-source failure
permits an audited alternative source or an explicit exclusion, not a fabricated
paper. T01 can continue alongside technical derivations from already preserved
primaries, but new source-dependent claims wait for technical inspection.

The principal execution risk is again stopping at a narrow implementation.
The final review must read the argument and method hierarchy for each broader
capability, not infer their adequacy from passing tests or phase names.
