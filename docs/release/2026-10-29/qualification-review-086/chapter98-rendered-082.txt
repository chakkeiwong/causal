98.1      The banking question and the observation law
A bank can observe a stable association between an offer and a subsequent outcome yet remain
unable to price the effect of changing the offer. The missing information can persist even
with an exactly known observational distribution. This chapter develops a restricted temporal
confidence construction and shows what an additional direct intervention would contribute.
The mathematical construction and exact calculations are project derivations, not a fitted
bank result. Causal banking research project (2026ah,aj)
   In the preserved temporal graph study, all valid cells covered and retained their declared
true graph in 512 of 512 requests. Weak relevance and unrestricted latent observation each
produced 512 whole-line sets. The persistent cell produced 507 finite sets and five whole-line
sets. Under false exclusion, the true graph was retained zero times despite numerical effect
coverage. These findings distinguish numerical coverage from informative effects and from a
valid causal restriction. Causal banking research project (2026ak)

  Define CO to contain every tuple (r, G, λ, Σ) for which r ∈ I, G is in the declared family, its

All nuisance coefficients and the continuous lag remain in the region. Let

proof uses a single coverage event at the fixed true lag, and the union includes that lag. It
does not declare every wrong-lag confidence calculation valid or justify discarding the true lag
by a separate selector.

ν ≥ 14,400. The predecessor used 240 transitions; this particular finite bound cannot give it

numerical implementation would need separate validation. No improvement in cost or useful
width is claimed from replacing a lag grid with this conservative continuum argument.

98.4      An equal-law witness and what an exclusion actually buys
Let Z, U, E be independent standard Gaussian innovations and set

               εZ = Z,    εA = Z + U,     εY = τ εA + (2 − τ )Z + (1 − τ )U + E.

For every real τ , the observed innovation is exactly (Z, Z + U, 2Z + U + E)′ , with covariance

The confounded disturbance has variance 1 + (1 − τ )2 and covariance 1 − τ with U ; its
conditional noise variance is always one. Every world is a coherent positive-definite Gaussian
law. Attaching the same scalar lag and invariant initialization makes the entire observed
temporal law identical for every τ , while a contemporaneous intervention on A changes Y at
slope τ . The equality is algebraic for all real effects, not merely an equality across the finite
checked examples.
   The restriction c = 0 reduces this family to τ = 2; strong observed relevance ΣZA = 1 then
gives the familiar ratio. But relevance alone does not establish exclusion: all other worlds
have the same strong observed correlation with Z. Merely observing more of this law, using a
different discovery optimizer or asking an LLM to orient an edge cannot choose among these
effects. No theorem here rules out all additional measurements; it states exactly what the
declared observational law omits.
   At zero relevance, even exclusion can leave every effect feasible. Take εA = U and εY =
τ εA + (1 − τ )U + E = U + E, with Z independent. Now c = 0, ΣZA = ΣZY = 0, and the
effect is still arbitrary. A weak-instrument ratio that quietly deletes zero cannot solve this
information problem.


98.5      A direct intervention that separates effects without iden-
          tifying every edge
Consider independent experimental episodes at a common fixed pre-action history. Replace
the action equation by A = a0 + δR, where δ > 0 is known and R ∈ {−1, 1} is assigned with
m episodes per arm independently of innovation roots. Keep the other structural equations
unchanged. Under the restricted linear Gaussian model and an invariant τ , the outcome has
the form
                                            iid
                       Y = µ + τ δR + ξ, ξ ∼ N (0, σI2 ),  σI2 > 0.
The unknown µ absorbs the fixed history and baseline action; the unknown variance absorbs
the remaining contemporaneous causes. The episodes must be independent; taking adjacent
dates from one continuing customer trajectory does not satisfy this experiment. Identical
variance in the two arms and a common transported effect are additional model assumptions.

98.7      When a valid temporal region is still an unsuitable study
          design
The restricted continuous-lag argument separates mathematical coverage from decision-useful
precision. At the true common scalar lag, invariant Gaussian initialization and independent
innovations justify the projected variance argument. A union over candidate lags need only
retain the true lag on that event; incorrect lags need not themselves produce independent
residuals. This is a useful restricted result. It does not establish a confidence procedure for
an arbitrary lag matrix, unknown order, nonstationary hidden histories or a general learned
partial ancestral graph. These differences also determine whether a proposed numerical study
is worth conducting Causal banking research project (2026ai).
   Consider the displayed Chebyshev construction with nine variance directions, relative
tolerance t = 1/4 and observational error allocation αO = .02. For a Gaussian sample variance
with ν residual degrees of freedom, the relative variance is 2/ν. Chebyshev and a union bound
therefore give
                                                                        18
                       Pr(at least one relative deviation exceeds t) ≤ 2 .
                                                                       νt
To make this bound at most .02 requires ν ≥ 14,400, or 14,401 transitions under the stated
residual convention. With 240 transitions and 239 degrees of freedom, the untruncated
expression is approximately 1.20502 and gives no useful coverage bound. The specified whole-
line fallback is honest about this limitation. Increasing the number of Monte Carlo replications
cannot make that per-request fallback decision-useful Causal banking research project (2026ai).
   A sharper pivot is a concrete development route. At the restricted true lag, an exact chi-
square variance pivot can replace the Chebyshev band while preserving the continuous union.
Ordinary development quantiles at 239 degrees of freedom and the nine-way .02 allocation
give variance multipliers approximately [.76731, 1.34533]. These values indicate potential
improvement; they are not certified release endpoints. The implementation must enclose the
tail quantiles, observable inputs and propagated covariance constraints, preserve conservative
fallback under timeout, and profile the continuous-lag calculation. Sampling a lag grid or
accepting a point optimizer’s failure does not certify the whole continuum Causal banking
research project (2026ai).

different obligations: availability, effect coverage, and scientific validity of the graph class
