. 14 14 14 15 15 ii Chapter 1 Public evidence for a causal banking proposal The immediate deliverable is a product proposal: a defensible account of the decisions a shared causal library could improve, the methods and software worth assembling, a bounded prototype, and the experiments worth funding next. A bank deployment, accepted integration and measured commercial return are later milestones. Requiring private assignment logs, independent bank audits and production owners before completing this proposal reverses the investment sequence. Those expensive inputs should be requested only when less expensive evidence has established both technical promise and a consequential uncertainty that they can resolve. Public data can provide much of the evidence for that first decision. It can expose realistic variable definitions, missingness, reporting clocks, financial magnitudes and computational workloads. Public experiments can exercise estimation under a documented assignment design. Semi-synthetic data can combine empirical covariates with generated treatment assignments and potential outcomes, making error measurable. Entirely synthetic histories can isolate feedback, confounding, graph equivalence and observation errors. These sources complement one another because no single source supplies realistic bank operations, observed counterfactual truth and unrestricted reuse at once. The proposal therefore replaces a premature demand for authentic bank qualification with a public-evidence portfolio. It does not replace an unknown bank effect with the effect from another population. The claim being evaluated changes from “this system improves this bank’s decisions” to “these capabilities are technically credible, reproducible and sufficiently promising to justify this specific next experiment.” A negative comparison, a useful refusal, or a narrowed capability can be a successful research deliverable when it changes that investment decision honestly. 1.1 What constitutes evidence Let S = 1 denote membership in a public study and S = 0 membership in the future bank population. A public randomized study may identify τ 1 = E[Y (1) − Y (0) | S = 1]. It does not by itself identify τ 0 . One possible transport argument would require comparable interventions and outcomes, sufficient effect modifiers X, overlap between populations, and 1 2 Causal analysis for a universal bank stability of the conditional causal response. Under those additional assumptions, Z τ 0 = {m 1 (x) − m 0 (x)} dP (x | S = 0), m a (x) = E[Y | A = a, X = x, S = 1]. The expression explains the later data requirement: a target population distribution and a credible account of effect modification. It is not a finding that these assumptions hold. A banking example makes the distinction concrete. Learning how advertising holdouts support an incremental-conversion estimate can validate treatment coding and an estimator’s workflow. It does not show the deposit value of a bank telephone campaign, whose product, population, contact policy and financial payoff differ. Five evidence classes are kept distinct throughout this survey. It covers funding and bank panels; household finance and marketing; lending and mortgage histories; experimental and semi-synthetic estimation; discovery; linkage; and language-model reasoning. A source register preserves exact downloaded bytes, SHA-256 identities, inspected passages, supported claims and access limitations. Dataset documentation supports dataset descriptions; this survey does not infer a scholarly result from a paper title or repository abstract. 1.3. The 401(k) extract contains 9,915 households, eligibility, participation, financial assets and nine declared controls. The existing analysis distinguishes an eligibility average effect from a participation instrumental-variable target and compares score algebra with DoubleML under identical fitted inputs. The extract lacks survey-design fields sufficient to establish design-valid covariance; conditional exchangeability, instrument assumptions and bank transport are unresolved. These are informative prototype limitations, not reasons to discard the example (DMLonGitHub application-data repository, 2026). The monetary example uses 203 quarterly observations from 1959Q1 through 2009Q3. It reproduces local-projection coefficients and Bartlett covariance against a numerical reference, and makes the information-clock ambiguity explicit. An aggregate money-stock response to a Treasury-bill rate change is not a bank’s deposit-offer effect. It is nevertheless a useful public demonstration of time-series conventions, dependence-aware computation and sensitivity to period and control choices (statsmodels dataset contributors, 2026). A new descriptive inspection downloaded the UCI Bank Marketing archive, checked its four CSV files and preserved their identities. The two full datasets contain 41,188 and 45,211 observations; their smaller files contain 4,119 and 4,521. The 41,188-row version has 4,640 subscriptions and 36,548 non-subscriptions. Its default field contains 8,597 unknown values and education contains 1,731. Empty-cell counts alone would therefore conceal missing information. The full and smaller versions overlap and must not be used as independent replications (UCI Machine Learning Repository, 2026a; Causal banking research project, 2026). That inspection is a completed data-profile exercise. It is not a new treatment-effect estimate, a fitted benchmark or a preregistered experiment. It establishes that an openly licensed, modest-size financial dataset is immediately usable for schema, timing and refusal demonstrations. Chapter 2 Dataset choices and their causal roles 2.1 Funding responses and bank panels 1. FDIC Summary of Deposits. The custodian describes an annual survey of branch-office deposits as of 30 June. Institutions with branches submit the survey; institutions with only a main office are exempt from that reporting requirement. The natural observational unit is a branch-year, nested within an institution and local market. This makes the source attractive for deposit concentration, branch-network histories and a proposed event-study workflow. Before estimation, the chosen release must establish identifiers, ownership changes, deposit units and coverage. A branch closure or merger is not automatically an exogenous treatment: local demand, financial distress and strategic selection can drive both the event and deposits. Annual June observations cannot recover a daily customer offer response. Decision: shortlist for a public panel prototype, with causal event-study claims conditional on a separately justified design (Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation, 2026b). 2. FDIC Bank Data API financial and structural series. The inspected API specification exposes institution, financial, historical-change and deposits routes. This gives an acquisition route for an institution-time dataset and the structural history needed to avoid treating a merger as organic growth. Financial fields and history must be joined using a frozen field dictionary and defensible continuity rules. First compare accounting identities, dates and missingness; then examine a declared event or exposure. Bank and calendar dependence belong in the uncertainty design. No bank-quarter dataset or causal effect was downloaded or estimated in this survey. Decision: pair with the deposits source after a bounded schema and vintage inspection; do not pool institution, holding-company and branch units without an explicit aggregation map (Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation, 2026a). 3. Federal Reserve FR Y-9C. The report collects consolidated holding-company balance sheets, income statements and supporting schedules quarterly. The official description says microdata are public with exceptions. The unit is a consolidated holding company, which is different from an insured bank or a branch. It can support funding composition and institution- level stress examples when thresholds, reporting changes and consolidations are accounted for. It is not a customer-level dataset and does not supply the bank’s internal pricing decision. Decision: reserve as a second panel source; prefer one coherent institution panel initially and add holding-company aggregation only for a question that requires it (Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, 2026c). 4. Federal Reserve H.8. H.8 supplies estimated weekly aggregate commercial-bank 4 2.2. Households, acquisition and credit decisions 5 balance sheets and bank-group series, with downloadable data routes. It is inexpensive context for system-wide deposits, credit and common-calendar shocks. The aggregation is useful for a forecasting or conditional-response example, but it removes customer and institution assignment information. Group composition, revisions and seasonal adjustments need attention. Decision: adopt as a contextual and time-series workflow source; require a separately documented shock design before attaching a structural causal interpretation (Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, 2026a). 5. Archived macroeconomic extract. The existing 203-quarter dataset is already local and has an executed, bounded projection study. Its advantages are a fixed historical snapshot, small resource requirements and an existing independent numerical comparison. Its limitations are age, aggregation and unresolved policy information. Decision: reuse this baseline now; a newer series can be a versioned extension, with release vintages and units preserved. Replacing it merely because newer numbers exist would not supply an identification argument (statsmodels dataset contributors, 2026). 2.2 Households, acquisition and credit decisions 6. SIPP-based 401(k) extract. Eligibility, participation and financial wealth yield a compact financial example with two distinct estimands. The existing workflow can show adjustment- set declarations, orthogonal scores, overlap diagnostics and why a participation effect needs stronger assumptions than an eligibility contrast. Unknown survey design and variable timing remain explicit. Employer selection and saving preferences are plausible unmeasured confounders. Decision: retain as the principal existing public household-finance example; add sensitivity and target-definition tests without relabeling observational assumptions as verified randomization (DMLonGitHub application-data repository, 2026). 7. Survey of Consumer Finances. The 2022 public file is described as 4,595 families represented by 22,975 rows across five implicates. The Federal Reserve supplies separate replicate weights and warns that ignoring both imputation and complex sampling yields incorrect standard errors. This is valuable for wealth, debt, balance-sheet heterogeneity and the product’s treatment of uncertainty across completed datasets. It is normally a triennial cross-section, not a customer transaction panel. A credible exercise would reproduce a published descriptive quantity with proper imputation and survey variance before attempting an assumption-dependent causal example. Decision: shortlist for observation construction and household heterogeneity, selecting a documented pre-cutoff file; do not concatenate implicates and count them as independent families (Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, 2026b). 8. UCI Bank Marketing. The data concern telephone campaigns at a Portuguese bank and term-deposit subscription. The full additional version has 20 inputs, 41,188 examples and a documented date ordering from May 2008 to November 2010; the older full version has 45,211 rows. The custodian states CC BY 4.0 terms. Contact duration is an especially clear timing test: it is not available when deciding whether to place the call. The files do not establish a random treatment policy, a representative uncontacted group or an entity key sufficient for a repeated-customer causal panel. “The client subscribed” is an outcome; it is not itself an intervention. Decision: adopt immediately for data profiling, business-question translation, leakage detection and principled refusal. Reject a claimed incremental campaign effect from this file alone (UCI Machine Learning Repository, 2026a; Causal banking research project, 2026). 9. UCI Default of Credit Card Clients. This 30,000-row Taiwan dataset has 23 predictors, including credit limits, payment status, bills and payments over April–September 6 Causal analysis for a universal bank 2005, followed by a default label. The custodian states CC BY 4.0. The histories are useful for checking feature clocks, censoring assumptions and the distinction between a repayment and a bank action. A payment is partly a customer’s response, not an assigned servicing treatment. The file does not disclose the complete sequence of contact, limit-change or relief decisions and their logging probabilities. Decision: use as an openly reusable credit-history and prediction baseline; use its admissible covariates in a clearly labeled generated-action study if desired. It cannot establish the causal effect of a collection policy (UCI Machine Learning Repository, 2026b). 10. Public HMDA application records. The inspected CFPB historical public schema includes lender identity, year, geography, application action, loan amount, income and pricing fields. It supports a lending-funnel example: application, denial, origination and conditional loan terms are distinct objects. Conditioning a rate analysis on origination changes the selected population. Public disclosure transformations, missing fields and policy-specific coverage require the selected year’s dictionary. The current publication endpoint returned HTTP 403; the accessible, cutoff-pinned historical schema is evidence about that schema, not proof of the 2024 file’s contents. Decision: shortlist for application and selection analysis after current-year access is resolved. Do not present it as a monthly repayment panel or individual discrimination ground truth (Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, 2026). 2.3 Loan servicing and repeated outcomes 11. Freddie Mac Single-Family Loan-Level Dataset. The official page describes origination and monthly performance data, including prepayments and credit events, and directs users to register and sign in. It also states that data are unaudited and subject to change. Free noncommercial, academic/research and limited uses remain subject to terms; commercial redistribution requires an agreement. Longitudinal loan histories can demonstrate competing events, outcome horizons, cohort splits and incomplete observations. Modification flags do not supply the full decision state, all feasible offers or known action probabilities. Decision: strong conditional candidate for realistic servicing histories. Review the permitted use and a small fixed cohort before committing resources; use an unrestricted synthetic history route meanwhile (Freddie Mac, 2026). 12. Fannie Mae Single-Family Loan Performance Data. The custodian describes selected fixed-rate loans with origination and dynamic monthly performance, alongside a HARP mapping between original and refinanced loans. Its stated population excludes several mortgage categories, so it is not the entire mortgage market. Registration is required; third- party redistribution and external commercial uses are restricted without written consent. The HARP mapping is valuable for discussing how an apparent loan termination may be a linked refinancing, but it does not resolve endogenous refinancing selection. Decision: alternative to Freddie Mac, not a mandatory second acquisition. Select one source by access, variable coverage and cohort quality; retain the other as a robustness extension only when it answers a consequential question (Fannie Mae, 2026). 13. NHEFS observational follow-up. The inspected causallib loader distinguishes a smoking-cessation/weight-change analysis and a survival variant with follow-up and censoring. It explicitly records optional exclusion of missing outcomes and uses cessation as the exposure. This is an adjacent-domain teaching example for baseline treatment, observation restrictions and survival semantics. It is not a series of randomized bank servicing decisions. Decision: keep as an optional inexpensive survival comparison after checking upstream data terms and cohort construction. Use a generated sequential decision process for time-varying treatment feedback rather than pretending this baseline-exposure example supplies it (causallib contributors, 2.4. Experimental, panel and semi-synthetic benchmarks 7 2026). For a sequential policy, histories H t , actions A t , subsequent states and censoring need a declared order. Identification by a longitudinal g-formula requires consistency, positivity and sequential exchangeability given the available history. Monthly rows satisfy none of those assumptions merely by being longitudinal. An observed modification may follow a private hardship assessment absent from the release. Therefore the first servicing prototype should combine realistic public outcome histories, where access permits, with generated actions and outcomes whose causal law is fully declared. The causaldata documentation describes nsw_mixtape as a 445-row experimental extract with earnings and baseline covariates. A small public earnings experiment is useful for effect-versus-prediction semantics and a design- based baseline. Before claiming an identified effect, recover the original assignment and sample-selection description and bind the exact version. A public mirror of the data was acquired here, but no fitted study or new trial validation was performed. Decision: preferred small experimental candidate, conditional on those provenance and rights checks (causaldata contributors, 2026). 15. MatchIt LaLonde comparison sample. It is not the all-randomized experimental comparison. This is a valuable adverse example for an agent asked to “load LaLonde and estimate the RCT effect”: the response must disambiguate the dataset first. The inspected author documentation specifies 4,802 empirical covariate rows and 58 covariates from the Collaborative Perinatal Project. CausalBench perturbational gene-expression data. The documented benchmark offers observational, partially interventional and fully interventional regimes on large biological datasets. It is attractive because intervention labels allow evaluations that pure observational graph recovery cannot supply. Nevertheless, perturbations are biological operations, measured expression is an imperfect system representation, and validation resources do not amount to a complete known causal graph. Different auxiliary resources carry different licenses, including a noncommercial component. Decision: optional external-domain discovery challenge after a small declared synthetic graph suite. Use held-out intervention prediction or explicitly scoped reference metrics; do not call a learned biological network banking graph truth (CausalBench authors, 2026). 21. FEBRL linkage datasets. The inspected loader describes synthetic identity records and returns known links reconstructed from generator identities. FEBRL1 has 500 originals and 500 duplicates; FEBRL4 has two tables of 5,000 records each with known correspondences. These can test duplicate handling, blocking recall, false matches and propagation of uncertain identity. Generator IDs that encode correspondence must be hidden from matching features. A financial outcome or transaction history added to these names is an additional synthetic construction. Decision: preferred optional linkage benchmark once data-specific terms are checked; existing project-generated identities remain sufficient for an immediate prototype. Neither establishes the error rate of a real bank audit (Python Record Linkage Toolkit contributors, 2026). 22. PaySim. The author description explains that mobile-money transactions are generated by a simulator, initially from a service schema and later using aggregate transaction information. Realistic financial-looking records are still synthetic. This source is useful for workload shape and event-stream concepts, but its fraud labels do not identify the effect of a bank intervention. Code and linked dataset terms need separate checks. Decision: optional engineering fixture, deprioritized because the existing project simulator already provides transparent decision and observation mechanisms (PaySim authors, 2026). 23. CLadder. The benchmark documentation describes 10,112 balanced generated causal questions, underlying model metadata and variants with ordinary, counterintuitive and nonsense variable names. Those variants help distinguish causal reasoning from memorized domain associations. Public model metadata can support a logical reference; it cannot be allowed into the evaluation prompt when it contains the answer. Decision: shortlist for offline task design and later bounded live evaluation after dataset terms and contamination controls are fixed. Public benchmark performance is a diagnostic, not evidence that the model owns the business assumptions (CLadder authors, 2026). 2.5. Discovery, observation construction and language models 9 24. Corr2Cause. The documented generator builds graphs, conditional-independence statements and verbalized causal-relation questions; a three-class supplement distinguishes entailed, underdetermined and contradicted claims within an equivalence class. Variable renaming and paraphrases offer useful robustness contrasts. Decision: pair selected tasks with CLadder and new controlled banking briefs. Require the system to retain equivalent diagrams and request missing identifying information instead of forcing a direction. Source review is complete for these descriptions; no live-model scores were produced (Corr2Cause authors, 2026). The surveyed datasets cover concrete components of discovery, but none establishes a universal “business text to true causal graph” route. A useful graph prototype should return candidate structures, provenance for asserted edges, explicit temporal restrictions and the consequences of ambiguity for the requested intervention. On generated examples, structural metrics can be scored against the generator. On empirical observations, equivalence and uncertain measurement must survive into the effect analysis. A proposal can include a live-model evaluation design before paying for it; a claimed live-model capability requires an actual identified backend and held-out execution. 3.2 P1: demonstrate that the problem is correctly formulated The first study asks whether the prototype preserves the scientific question as data move through it. For 401(k), write two briefs: the effect of eligibility on assets and the effect of participation. The reference specification distinguishes their assumptions and estimands. For Bank Marketing, ask for pre-contact subscription prediction and the incremental effect of making a call. The former can be a prediction exercise with an explicit information cutoff. The latter requires the system to identify absent assignment/control information and propose a design. The baseline is a reviewed template plus deterministic schema/timing checks. The proposed assistant must output the population, unit, time zero, feasible action, outcome horizon, available covariates, candidate diagrams, adjustment or instrument assumptions, and missing evidence. Score each field separately and record whether uncertainty is retained. Do not score an invented graph as correct merely because it looks economically plausible. A deliberately misleading brief that proposes controlling for call duration should trigger a clock objection. The completed archive profile provides an input fixture, not final task performance. Freeze the briefs and reference alternatives before evaluating a model or agent. Public benchmark text may be familiar to a model; use disjoint graph templates and new names/numerical mechanisms for the held-out portion. Record prompted assistance and reviewer intervention in the denominator of workflow cost. 3.3 P2: make dependent financial computation reviewable Retain the existing macroeconomic result as an inherited baseline. For an FDIC extension, first select a release window whose final included publication predates the cutoff, a bounded set of institutions and a documented event definition. Acquire field dictionaries, structure changes and release information before fitting. Separate branch-level deposits from consolidated bank financials. Entity continuity, mergers and geography should be reproducible transformations rather than unrecorded analyst choices. The first result should reproduce descriptive totals and a declared conditional panel asso- ciation. A causal event study is an additional design: define event time, comparison units, anticipation, interference, common shocks and the identifying assumption. A conventional two-way fixed-effect coefficient and a group-time effect can target different weighted objects under staggered adoption; matching only the formula name is insufficient. Include a falsification or negative-control design where justified, but do not interpret a nonsignificant pretrend as proof of parallel trends. Evaluate numerical parity, retained sample/unit counts, missingness, memory, elapsed time and recovery from an interrupted local job. These metrics support engineering feasibility. Bank service levels and network recovery remain future target-platform questions. The local workload must preserve the complete calculation, including reconstruction and uncertainty, rather than reporting only a fast inner reducer. 12 3.4 Causal analysis for a universal bank P3: check effect estimators against the right reference Begin with a generated randomized experiment that has a declared assignment probability and known response law. Compare a difference in means, a prespecified regression adjustment and the proposed score estimator. Under unequal assignment, a naive mean contrast can remain interpretable under the actual design, while precision comparisons must account for the allocation. In observational semi-synthetic cases, require the declared adjustment information and vary overlap, response nonlinearity and heterogeneity. For ACIC, preserve real covariates and simulated quantities as separate objects. Report ATE error, heterogeneity error when conditional truth is supplied, interval availability, width, repeated-sampling coverage, overlap and runtime. Never tune on evaluation potential outcomes. Use the same scenarios, training information and splits for each method, and retain failures as outcomes. The NSW extension should report uncertainty for the experimental estimate and preserve the exact extract identity; its estimate is not a noiseless label for another method. Select scenarios and replication counts prospectively from the precision needed to distinguish meaningful failure. For example, at nominal coverage 0.95, 400 independent repetitions give a Monte Carlo standard error near 0.011; that is a planning calculation, not proof that observed 94% coverage is acceptable. A simultaneous acceptance rule across the selected scenarios must be specified before final runs. Current open calibration findings are not erased by switching to a public benchmark with unknown truth. 3.5 P4: distinguish realism from sequential identification A servicing prototype should represent a fixed policy and a policy learned on independent training histories. At each decision time, disclose the available state, chosen action, action propensity, subsequent outcome and censoring. Include an adverse case with a private hardship variable that affects both actions and repayment, and a case with weak support for the proposed policy. A useful result may be a refusal, a broad identified set or a warning that an off-policy value cannot be estimated reliably. Real mortgage histories, if lawfully acquired, improve the realism of delinquency and termination clocks. They should initially support descriptive transitions and competing-event handling. If actions are then generated conditional on recorded histories, label the resulting policy comparison semi-synthetic; if future outcomes are also generated, disclose that response law. Policy value under that law is a simulation result. The proposed bank experiment is justified only when an unresolved assumption about genuine actions or response materially changes the policy recommendation. 3.6 P5: carry ambiguity through graph and identity operations For discovery, use small graphs where observational equivalence classes can be enumerated. The baseline returns the entire compatible class; a discovery method or language model must not receive credit for an unjustified single orientation. Evaluate orientation precision, valid-class retention and the resulting intervention-effect set, including empty, disconnected or unbounded answers when they are mathematically appropriate. Add interventional information in a separate condition to test whether it resolves the intended ambiguity. For observation construction, start from known entity histories and then corrupt identifiers, timestamps or links. Keep the hidden generator mapping outside the analysis interface. The baseline should be a competent low- cost workflow: a reviewed form, a deterministic graph/identification solver where applicable, and the selected statistical package. The proposed agent should be evaluated for improvements over that workflow, including errors it introduces and time spent correcting them. Measure invalid assumptions, clock violations, unsupported orientations, identification mistakes, tool/schema failures, hallucinated results, uncertainty fidelity, successful abstention and end-to-end completion. Record calls, tokens, elapsed time and human review effort only when they are actually measured. Live backend identity, version, budget and custody are necessary for a live performance claim; they are not prerequisites for the literature survey, offline fixtures, architecture proposal or protocol. If a live evaluation is unaffordable, narrow the claim and retain a costed protocol rather than fabricating results. 3.8 Common study controls Each new final study needs a prospectively frozen question, input hashes, methods, splits, scenarios, estimands, assignment/observation assumptions, numerical tolerances, resource limit and stopping rule. Exploratory profiles such as the UCI row inspection remain exploratory and may inform that registration. Existing fitted results cannot be relabeled fresh evaluation data. A source-access failure blocks only the affected dataset branch. An initial resource envelope should be modest: local public files, one selected data source per role, no paid API by default, bounded CPU jobs and recorded memory/time. Exact limits should be set after an input-profile pilot, before final outcomes are examined. The plan does not attach invented calendar dates or currency costs to unmeasured work. A baseline that performs as well as the proposed component is evidence to reuse the baseline, simplify the design or reject that component. Chapter 4 When a bank experiment becomes worth its cost 4.1 Proposal acceptance and later qualification Proposal acceptance means the scholarly argument is coherent, source-grounded and sufficiently complete; the software and agent landscape has been compared seriously; the integration design is concrete; the public/synthetic demonstrations are reproducible within their claims; and the next investment decision follows from an explicit comparison and cost model. Open research can be retained with a scoped conjecture, failure result and next test. A general theorem is not promised where only a restricted construction is known. The three major manuscript repairs remain obligations: systematic software coverage and reuse/commercial comparisons; the developed treatment of language models and agents; and CLI, MCP, library and workspace integration. The public-data survey supplies their evaluation substrate. It does not finish those chapters or automatically close any scientific gap. The software and language-model page/depth commitments also remain; pages in this public-data section are not counted twice toward them. Later bank qualification asks a different question: do the assumptions, precision, workflows and economics hold for a specific institution? That stage may need genuine offer histories, logging information, independent labels, owner-reviewed semantics, production constraints and prospective outcomes. The relevant input should be requested only for the capability and target population under test. An internal bank pilot is not required to demonstrate that the proposal has a reproducible public baseline and a sensible experiment design. 4.2 A value-of-information decision Let d ∈ {stop, build, pilot} denote an investment choice, θ unresolved technical and economic conditions, and U (d, θ) its net value. Let E denote current public/prototype evidence and Z the possible result of a proposed experiment. A planning expression for the expected value of sample information is   EVSI = E Z|E max E{U (d, θ) | E, Z} − max E{U (d, θ) | E}. d d An experiment merits consideration when its expected decision improvement exceeds its full incremental cost, including data preparation, customer burden where relevant, security 14 4.3. Conditions for moving to expensive evidence 15 review, labeling, analyst time and opportunity cost. This is a project decision framework, not an empirically estimated ROI. Where a probability model for θ is unjustified, use a scenario table and sensitivity bounds instead of a precise-looking EVSI number. A simple illustrative calculation shows the sequencing. Suppose, purely for planning, a small bank study costing 20,000 could avert an unsuitable 200,000 build in 20% of scenarios, with no other benefits or costs. Its gross avoided-spend value would be 40,000 and its net would be 20,000 under those assumptions. If the study changes the build decision in only 2% of scenarios, the same gross value is 4,000 and it is not justified on that ground. These are stipulated arithmetic examples, not quotations, bank probabilities or forecasts. The decision should identify the uncertainty that matters. If a public benchmark shows repeated action-clock mistakes, improve the formulation layer before requesting confidential data. If a correct estimator is too slow on modest data, fix or replace it before commissioning a platform pilot. If all plausible effects lead to the same action, a costly precision improvement may have little value. If policy value reverses when a missing hardship variable is plausible, a carefully scoped bank measurement study can have high value even when the public-data implementation is technically sound. 4.3 Conditions for moving to expensive evidence An actionable next-experiment recommendation specifies the proposed intervention, target population, candidate baseline, decision-loss threshold and the smallest information addition that could alter the decision. It also states an explicit stop or redesign outcome. A bank experiment should not be recommended merely to fill a missing-evidence cell. For funding, the missing addition may be time-stamped offers and an assignment mechanism, not more aggregate deposits. For servicing, it may be feasible-action and hardship information at each decision time. For discovery, it may be one feasible intervention that distinguishes competing graphs. For observation construction, it may be a representative independently resolved sample of uncertain identities with known inclusion probabilities. Public data help design each measurement and estimate its computational burden, while the bank supplies the later population-specific truth. Authentic institutional approval, independent acceptance, actual workflow prices and pro- duction service levels remain future qualification requirements. A proposal can be complete without them. It cannot honestly claim those outcomes without obtaining them. 4.4 Remaining survey and prototype gaps The survey has established a usable portfolio and a concrete public-data acquisition/profile, not six executed new studies. Current-source web snapshots still need exact data-file vintages for future analyses. Several benchmark datasets need data-specific rights checks. Mortgage histories require registration and use review; the HMDA publication endpoint was inaccessible in this run. The Criteo license is a material constraint for a commercial proposal. These affect individual options; project-generated alternatives and the already local public data keep the prototype work moving. No inspected public financial source supplies the complete logged bank servicing action process or independent real-bank linkage truth. That finding is limited to the surveyed sources. It calls for transparent semi-synthetic experiments now and a justified targeted measurement later, rather than a claim of impossibility. Public reasoning benchmarks also cannot establish a 16 Causal analysis for a universal bank live agent’s quality without an actual controlled run, nor resolve the business owner’s normative choice of outcome and feasible intervention. Finally, public data do not repair the open mathematical arguments for persistent depen- dence, hidden states, graph-equivalence effect sets or arbitrary learning procedures. Those questions remain scoped research obligations. The proposal can present negative findings and restricted supported regimes, compare alternatives and describe what evidence would change the recommendation.
