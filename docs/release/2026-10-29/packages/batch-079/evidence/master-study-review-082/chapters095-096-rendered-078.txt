Chapter 95

What additional information buys

     A causal banking service must return a decision quantity together with the information
     and uncertainty that make it defensible. Funding responses, sequential servicing, temporal
     relationships and verified customer histories reveal different limits on that information.
     A funding experiment uses supplied action randomization and independent calendars
     to remove a fitted-ratio mean shift. A nonlinear servicing extension evaluates a fixed
     observable policy using truthful state audits and supplied action probabilities. Both meet
     a preregistered minimum-coverage screen on selected synthetic grids with conservative
     99% critical values. A joint pair/calendar/gold-label experiment still fails finite calibration
     in important regimes. A corrected temporal graph union retains effects but can return
     the whole real line. A corrected sparse learner fails qualification at higher dimension or
     support. Full linked-outcome streaming agrees with its dense reference and survives real
     local process failures, without a consistent measured performance gain. These are research
     findings from specified experiments. Their use in a bank requires independent review,
     credible assignment and observation information, suitable operating performance and a
     defensible economic case.


95.1     One decision, several kinds of evidence
A bank deciding whether to raise a deposit rate, change a collections policy or act on a
discovered relationship needs a target with units and timing. It also needs an observation
model: a row may not identify the customer whose balance matters; a public signal may omit
the private state used by a historical decision maker; an apparent edge may permit several
intervention effects. Computation cannot supply missing identity, assignment or exclusion
information.
   The experiments ask whether changing the available information improves a stated banking
comparison. A negative result can explain which information is missing. An uncertainty
set can be statistically defensible yet too wide to distinguish a profitable action from a loss.
Scientific validity and useful precision therefore need separate attention.
   Every table cell has 512 fresh Monte Carlo requests. Covers uses all requests, including
refusals. MC lower is a simultaneous one-sided exact-binomial 95% lower bound, Bonferroni-
adjusted over all method/cell comparisons in the study. The minimum screen is .95. These
screens do not prove uniform coverage or validate supplied scientific assumptions. Primary
methods use frozen 99% critical values or covariance regions; some challengers use 95% critical
values. Width and coverage have a disclosed tradeoff.

95.6      A learner whose code matches its argument
The residualized IV score is
                     E[(Z − mZ (X)){Y − mY (X) − θ(A − mA (X))}] = 0.
Independent-training nuisance errors shift the conditional moment by E[δZ (δY − θδA )]. This
is the inherited orthogonal-score principle Chernozhukov et al. (2018); dependent separation
and actual selection need their own proof.
   The corrected procedure has two outer calendar blocks separated by a gap. Inside each
training block a further separated split selects a shrinking lasso grid. Inner training controls
standardization; selected models are refit using only outer training data. Evaluation labels
never tune penalties. Bartlett products stay within evaluation folds, never across the omitted
gap. Actual feature/support overrides are honored.
   For fixed dimension, a correct linear nuisance, positive Gram eigenvalues and fixed geometric
mixing give root-sample moment/coefficient rates. A finite shrinking grid preserves that rate,
including at selection boundaries. Products are then second order. A sufficiently increasing
gap and growing covariance window supply a conditional asymptotic route. Growing dimension
additionally needs restricted-eigenvalue and approximation/product-rate control. Prediction
accuracy alone proves none of these. The grid has 20 or 60 controls, support three or eight,
640 origins and ordinarily a 40-origin gap.

95.7      Keeping uncertainty visible in the reported result
Each A-D result carries actual bounds or a refusal reason, units, assumptions and capability
disposition. Finite intervals, disconnected sets, whole lines, empty sets and unavailable
computations remain distinct. Computed sets without bounds reject. Null endpoints denote
infinity only in the explicit extended-real schema, never zero or absent evidence.
   The full linked method streams both pilot and evaluation pseudo-outcomes. Chronological
pilot halves are compressed into augmented QR factors and online target moments. Validation
uses the same fixed penalty losses, followed by full-pilot refit; bounded passes then compute
estimates and covariance. Twelve dense-equivalence cases span chunks 1/37/257, low audit,
identity rank and persistence. Relative tolerance 10−9 and absolute USD 10−6 assess numerical
equivalence, not bank usefulness.
   Complete workloads include input generation, tuning/refit, inference, serialization and total
process memory. At 120,000 evaluation and 480,000 pilot origins, dense and streamed runs
take about 1.08 and 1.71 seconds, with roughly 409 MiB peak RSS each. The streaming fit is
slower. Input generation dominates memory; reduced auxiliary storage does not imply a large
total RSS reduction. No consistent performance advantage is claimed, and a dense fallback is
retained.
   Recovery binds code/input hashes, seed and request count. Local locking, fsync, atomic
record rename and a final manifest prevent partial publication. Real process deaths during
fitting, a partially written checkpoint and final publication recover the same 12 requests on
restart, replay without duplication and reject changed identities. This is local single-process
evidence; multi-host coordination and bank service levels need their platform operators.


95.8      Financial value and unresolved evidence
If ∆L is held-out improvement in bank decision loss over an accepted workflow, then

     Net value = ∆L − Caudit − Ccalendar − Cintegration − Ccompute − Creview − Coperation .

The research supplies synthetic precision and local computing measurements, not bank values
for these costs. Linked histories, correctness labels and full-state audits are different services.
A label count is not a market price; a faster fit is not ROI. Width, availability and decision
loss need matched information and actual prices.
  Verified customer histories provide a restricted way to learn funding responses, and the
nonlinear servicing study supports one specified policy comparison. Several causal graphs
can still fit the same observations. Assignment, audit inclusion and label accuracy require
substantive evidence. Joint dependence and high-dimensional learning retain adverse findings,
while local computing results do not establish bank operating performance. Useful precision
and the full financial case depend on the institution and decision.
   A bank study would need the relevant decision makers and data owners to establish
assignment rules, observation quality, required precision and costs. The information requested
should be specific to the proposed study.

From research output to a bounded bank decision
A deployment question must first specify the unit, decision time, available records and loss
from a wrong action. The funding construction requires certified randomization and link
inclusion, plus independent calendars whose economic shocks do not overlap. If those inputs
cannot be supplied, its calibrated synthetic interval is not a substitute for a bank answer. The
servicing construction requires a frozen observable policy, truthful representative state audits
and the actual historical action probabilities; otherwise the reported fixed-policy value has a
different target or can be severely biased.
   For discovery, a bank must specify the scientific restrictions that remove observationally
equivalent intervention effects. If its accepted restrictions still permit the whole real line,
the service should return that set and withhold a directional recommendation. For shared
calendars and dependent learning, the present finite failures require a new variance design or
stronger information before qualification. A platform may transmit these limitations faithfully,
but cannot turn them into positive scientific evidence.
   Finally, independent reviewers must reconstruct each claimed regime and bank owners
must measure useful precision, decision loss and complete acquisition and operating costs.
Only a bounded release meeting those conditions should enter an institutional acceptance and
investment decision. An adverse scientific or economic conclusion is a legitimate output of this
program.

A bank can increase its account count without acquiring an independent observation of a
common calendar shock. It can also add monthly observations without redrawing a persistent
identity audit. These two limitations affect different parts of funding uncertainty. A useful
analysis must first state the joint observation law, then determine which uncertainty can be
bounded from its actual information.

sample confidence set when variance and persistence bounds are supplied, together with an
information counterexample to a uniform precision claim when persistence is unrestricted. The
exact multiplier moments, covariance references and numerical illustrations are reproducible

96.5      What additional information would change the decision
The finite region separates valid uncertainty from useful precision. Bounds on unknown
variances and persistence supply a model under which the probability statement is established,
but the resulting set can still be unbounded. For a generated prototype those bounds may be
stipulated and tested as assumptions. For a later bank claim their scientific origin must be
established, or their estimation must receive its own valid uncertainty budget. A successful
calculation cannot certify them.
   The earlier 5,632-request study of the additive pair/calendar/gold law found finite under-
coverage in scarce-information and persistent-calendar regimes despite favorable balanced-case
results. Those fixed findings motivate this alternative construction; they do not validate it.
The same study’s wrong-inclusion, shared-audit and endogenous-label cases remain outside the
law used here. A valid set under independent symmetric audits cannot be transported to those
mechanisms by changing a covariance estimator. (Causal banking research project, 2026ab)
   There are three distinct next routes. A conservative region can serve as a transparent
reference in a generated prototype, with exact quadratic and set-projection checks. A sharper
mixture-likelihood or test-inversion method might retain validity with less conservative bounds,
but it must account for the unknown variances and dependence parameters. A changed
information design can add independently audited pairs, independent representative gold labels
or genuinely independent calendar clusters. Each addresses a different part of the uncertainty.
More gold labels alone cannot remove a shared-calendar obstruction, while more dates alone
do not make a persistent audit independent.
   A prototype should preserve unbounded and disconnected results, measure the cost of
certified projection, and compare these information choices under the same scientific target.
The comparison can initially use generated mechanisms without costly bank data. A later
bank experiment is justified when its information could narrow a decision-relevant ambiguity
enough to change an action. The present argument supports that conditional choice; it does
not promise a universally precise funding estimate. The next servicing argument confronts a
different information cost: learning action and observation probabilities while preserving the

A servicing team wants to compare an assistance rule with ordinary collection. Its historical
action probabilities may not have been retained, and some customers disappear from the
analytical records before their next repayment is measured. These are two estimation problems
only after a more basic scientific question has been answered: did the recorded history include
enough information to remove action and observation selection? A highly accurate model of
recorded actions cannot answer that question on its own.
  This chapter develops a restricted route with estimated probabilities, a policy selected
on separate training histories, and an explicit finite-sample confidence statement. It also
constructs two observationally indistinguishable worlds in which that route answers different
causal questions. The confidence construction and finite examples are project derivations.
The longitudinal treatment/observation identification principle is inherited from Hernán and
Robins; the importance of independent policy evaluation and cumulative, rather than current-
action, ratios is also explicit in Jiang and Li. Their results do not themselves prove the
composition below. (Hernán and Robins, 2020; Jiang and Li, 2016; Causal banking research
project, 2026ad,ae)

                       Observed pair (A, Y ) (0, 0) (0, 1) (1, 0) (1, 1)

   Even the private-state assignment in world H has overlap: its probabilities are .2 and .8.

probabilities. It passed its selected valid cells, while wrong probabilities covered only one of
512 requests and policy leakage returned no finite outputs. The present finite-table route
makes estimated-probability uncertainty explicit after restricting to sufficient observed histories.
It removes no hidden-state requirement from that predecessor and claims no new repeated-
sampling qualification. (Causal banking research project, 2026af)
   The separate proposed continuous-state servicing experiment has Gaussian innovations and
a quadratic reward. Its rewards are unbounded and its history table is not finite. Consequently
it cannot use this bounded-table theorem as written. A prospectively declared bounded utility
would define a different target; alternatively, a valid tail/moment bound and a continuous-
model estimation argument are needed. Quietly capping realized losses after evaluating the
results would answer a changed question.
