Chapter 32

What the evidence changes about
the proposed service

The four journeys distinguish a wrong target, insufficient treatment support, causal ambiguity
and uncertain measurement. Those failures demand different responses. A common service
should make the distinctions visible while reusing the appropriate statistical procedures. Its
initial scope follows from the evidence below; it does not require every possible capability to
be implemented at once.


32.1      A result should change a design choice
The existing 3,000-replication generated offer study is evidence about target alignment. Under
one specified design, the residual projection averages 26.447 when the equal-population benefit
is 50. Its interval covers its own target in 97.2% of repetitions and the population target in
none. At the stipulated cost of 40, interpreting it as the population effect rejects the beneficial
offer in all 500 repetitions of that scenario. The design consequence is to put the population
weights and target in the result, and to compare implementations only after aligning those
quantities. A more precise projection is not a repair for this interpretation error.
   The separate 6,000-replication sparse-support study addresses inference after the target is
aligned. Pooling yields 94.7% conditional coverage under equal normal variances but 52.2%
under the tested unequal-variance case. Cellwise Student intervals joined by a union bound
preserve the conditional coverage guarantee in the normal model at the cost of very wide
intervals. Thus the initial implementation should show cell support and retain an unsupported
or inconclusive result. If useful precision requires unjustified pooling, the next expenditure
should improve assignment support or the information collected. These are existing generated
experiments, not evidence of superiority by a named external package.
   The public UCI timing study answers a different question. On 41,188 archived contact
records, later-archive prediction has AUC 0.630 using the candidate pre-call feature set and
0.793 when duration is added. The latter feature is unavailable before the call, so its predictive
gain cannot justify pre-call targeting. Differences between early and late archive outcomes
also matter for evaluation. The correct service response is to preserve availability dates and
the evaluation period. Neither score estimates the effect of making a call. Chapter 28 gives
the executed design, comparators, results and qualifications.
   The retained public 401(k), monetary, panel and linked-record analyses provide further
empirical and methodological context. Their sampling, instrument, design and observation re-
strictions remain attached to their results in the detailed evidence part. A working independent-

32.2     Choose the smallest comparison that can reverse the
         recommendation
The first comparison should take the same fixed offer question through expert composition
and a minimal shared integration. The cases should include correct assignment, heterogeneous
effects, unequal treatment support, unavailable predictors, misrecorded receipt and a dependent
sampling request outside the accepted route. The generated law supplies truth where that
is possible; a public case supplies realistic data handling without pretending its causal truth
is known. A valid refusal counts as a completed scientific answer when the information is
insufficient.
   An independent evaluator should judge target correctness, assumption visibility, uncertainty,
interpretation and avoidable errors. Time must include correction, interpretation and unsuc-
cessful attempts. The estimate itself is not a success metric when its target differs from the
request. The proposed comparison is not yet an executed analyst study; its purpose is to
discover whether the common representation is worth its expense. If both workflows reach
equivalent answers at equivalent cost, improve the existing workflow. If the integration reduces
consequential errors or repeated work, a narrow bank-data pilot becomes a defensible next
step.


32.3     What the newer studies add to that choice
The public earnings contrast tests the complete chain from observed rows to uncertainty and
an explicit spending hurdle. A positive contrast alone does not establish that a cost is covered.
The servicing comparisons show why a fitted policy can offer narrow but misleading uncertainty
when dropout or state assumptions fail. The institutional panel separates persistent level
adjustment from a coherent economic unit; the linkage study makes the cost of a justified
error budget visible. These results explain which information the proposed study description
must carry, rather than establish the benefit of buying the service.
   The follow-up studies make that recommendation more specific. Random invitations can
leave selective completion, and a later report can describe a different outcome from the
historical one the bank needs (Section 14.5). A more complete graph search can still select
the wrong relationships from a finite sample (Section 20.15). Where compatible graphs imply
different actions, more computation does not replace identifying information (Section 69.30). A
useful study may therefore recommend better records, a different experiment or no decision yet.
Its cost must include the effort of reaching that conclusion, including unsuccessful attempts;
