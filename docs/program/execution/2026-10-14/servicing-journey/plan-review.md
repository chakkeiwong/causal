# Servicing workflow: reviewed local execution plan

This plan completes `IN.JOURNEY.DESIGN.servicing` and supplies the prospective
protocol for `IN.JOURNEY.LOCAL.servicing`. It concerns the proposed product's
meaning at an interface and deterministic local references. It does not approve
the rejected `SCI.DESIGN.R03S` sampling experiment. All 24 request cases are fixed
before the local run; each is issued through the library and CLI initially and
after restart, followed by one changed-horizon conflict on each route. The total
is 98 calls, including refusals and invocation failures.

## Decision, information and scientific target

The business question is whether an observable assistance rule improves the
net contribution of customers eligible before the first servicing decision.
Fix two decision periods. Before decision t, only the recorded history H_t is
available. Assistance A_t follows; retention C_t then determines whether the
subsequent reward and next history are recorded. Retention is coded one and
dropout is absorbing. The complete-data reward remains defined after analytical
dropout, but the observed score must stop without reading an unobserved future.
Sale, repayment, default and closure that change economic outcomes are not
automatically dropout. The clock audit here tests feature timestamps only;
outcome follow-up and state truth require separate evidence.

The restricted scientific argument targets V(pi), expected total bounded
reward per originally eligible customer. The business contrast is
V(pi)-V(never-assist). A policy frozen on an independent training cohort targets
the value of that realized policy, not the value of the learning algorithm
averaged over training sets and not an unrestricted optimal value. No numeric
confidence interval for this contrast is implemented by the interface study.
The separate [-4,4] fixture tests transport of a supplied single-policy utility
range; it must not be interpreted as a confidence interval for the contrast.

The sampling unit is an entire independent customer trajectory, with arbitrary
dependence within that trajectory. A split by rows or months would leak history
and violate the independent evaluation premise. Shared calendar shocks and
household links are explicit unsupported sampling regimes. The finite-table
proof requires sufficient dated cells, true support floors, bounded rewards,
and independent complete histories. Neither a declared unit nor a fitted action
model establishes these restrictions.

The observed prefix identity weights by every action and retention factor up to
the reward, including current retention. It requires consistency, sequential
exchangeability and noninformative analytical dropout, in addition to support.
Estimated logger and retention probabilities need their own error budget. The
existing project argument gives such a budget for a fixed finite table, including
empty-cell radius one. It does not license arbitrary discretization, unrestricted
learners or the unbounded Gaussian/quadratic P4 design. The exact example has an
evaluation-only radius 8.242685524 at n=1000 against a total target range of width
8; with illustrative denominator errors its radius is 9.615113642. These numbers
explain the negative design decision, not a new empirical coverage finding.

Private confounding and informative dropout remain information obstructions.
The preserved two hidden-state worlds have identical observed records but
intervention effects 0.6 and zero. The two missing-outcome worlds have identical
observed records but cohort means 0.25 and 0.75. Larger samples of the same
observations cannot resolve those distinctions. A future route must declare a
different information law or restrict the causal target, then revisit precision.

## Exact boundaries of the local execution

The installed canonical service exposes graph syntax, clock consistency and
supplied quadratic sensitivity sets. It has no servicing estimator or policy
optimizer. The study therefore calls explicit proposed engine names and
expects unsupported results. The policy methods tested are supplied logger,
estimated logger, independent selected policy and unknown optimum. Sequential
g-formula identification and a mistaken single back-door policy request must
also remain unsupported. A DAG syntax result, even for a declared latent
confounder, must preserve the graph and claim only execution-level syntax.

Six contradicted-premise requests cover private confounding, informative dropout,
zero support, selection on evaluation outcomes, common calendar dependence and
unbounded reward. They should be refused with no numerical result. These are
checks of a caller's explicit contradictions. The runtime cannot detect an
undisclosed false assumption from these fixtures. A correct refusal counts as
conformance and as an unavailable estimate; it is never a successful policy
evaluation. Assignment-truth auditing remains unsupported.

The deterministic inequality cases are x^2-16<=0, -x^2+1<=0, x^2+1<=0 and
-x^2-1<=0. Their independent mathematical references are respectively [-4,4],
two unbounded rays, the empty set and the whole line. These fixtures exercise
topology, units, null unbounded endpoints and sensitivity-without-coverage.
They neither fit data nor produce sampling confidence. Unsupported estimates
keep a null result: unknown information must not become an empty identified set.

The clock cases have event<=available<decision, plus equality at the decision,
a missing availability time and a reversed event order. A changed sign after
artifact import must fail the context check. Corrupted artifact bytes must fail
integrity even after restart. Two changed-horizon requests reuse an existing key
and must be rejected, with no old-context result. Input artifacts are saved before
fault injection and their references reused; this avoids the historical draft
runner's error of trying to reimport corrupted content.

Every fixture is schema-checked before registration without invoking the
service. The execution records every full request and response atomically.
Each route uses a new isolated operator root. After a process interruption,
already journaled slots are skipped, while an unjournaled call may be safely
replayed under the same frozen identity. Such an ambiguous duplicate is not
qualified as exactly-once processing; the study checks ordinary restart/replay,
not process-kill atomicity or multi-host recovery. CLI exit codes and all response
fields except request_id are compared across four executions per case.

Separately, the existing exact reference program is imported and its calculation
replayed without overwriting the prior result. Equality to that preserved result
is required. Its intervention path sums, backward recursion and observed-prefix
density change are independent formulas within a single author-controlled
reproduction. This is not independent scientific acceptance. The runtime does
not consume those generated truth tables, and no new random trajectory is drawn.

## Full workflow costs and proposed consumer behavior

The run measures all invocation wall time, including process startup, replay
and failed requests, plus active runner time for importing/journaling. Reference
reproduction time is reported separately. Parent and child peak RSS are separate
process maxima, not simultaneous process-tree memory. Interpreter startup before
the timer, idle time between segments, authoring, review and manuscript work are
excluded and explicitly unpriced.

A transparent planning scenario maps one utility unit to USD100 for 1,000
eligible generated customers. Exact values 2 and 11/8 imply incremental reward
USD62,500. Assistance cost is already subtracted in the reward; subtracting it
again would double count. Assume an additional full-workflow cost USD10,000,
comprising data/rights/cleaning 1,500; fitting and selection 1,000; adapter and
integration allocation 2,000; human scientific/semantic review 2,000; computation,
storage and recovery 500; monitoring/operations 1,000; and evaluation/privacy/
governance allocation 2,000. The stipulated net is USD52,500; the break-even
additional cost is USD62,500. At an assumed USD75,000 cost the net is negative
USD12,500. These are utility-mapping scenarios, not price observations, bank ROI
or confidence bounds. Cohort size, conversion, outcome transport and every
cost component would need validation before a bank decision.

A proposed consumer contract would require explicit population, action
feasibility, horizon, information clock, fitting/evaluation split, causal premises,
uncertainty meaning and complete cost categories. It would propagate all set
components and withhold an adoption recommendation when required evidence is
missing. No consumer double is executed in this operation, no sibling repository
is modified or run, and no foreign-language adapter is asserted to exist.

An authentic owner-run trial is conditional later work. It would require a
dated eligible cohort and feasible actions, documented action/retention mechanisms,
outcome follow-up, independent audit truth when used, an agreed loss/width rule,
the frozen method and split, owner trace, actual full costs and signed disposition.
The proposal can proceed with public/generated evidence while retaining those
claims as conditional. Live model calls, commercial licenses and external owners
are unnecessary for this fixed local execution.

## Author review and continuation

The plan was reviewed against both design criteria. The scientific chain is
explicit, the negative R03S decision is retained, all known unavailable methods
remain unavailable, and the numerical references are distinct from interface
execution. Review corrected a potential ambiguity by declaring [-4,4] a supplied
single-policy range rather than the range or interval of the policy contrast.
It also separated reference runtime from service timing and eliminated assistance
cost double counting. The remaining boundaries are limitations of the proposed
capability, not blockers to this conformance design.

Freeze the fixture, code, schemas, environment, preserved scientific evidence and
this review; record the design; register the local protocol; execute all 98 calls;
replay the exact reference; review every mismatch, failure denominator, parity
check and cost measurement; record pass, negative or inconclusive according to
the frozen rules; then immediately select the next eligible operation. A passing
result awards only this local operation, with no original scientific gap closure.
