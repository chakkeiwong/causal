\hypertarget{selection-rather-than-accumulation}{%
\section{Selection rather than
accumulation}\label{selection-rather-than-accumulation}}

Acquiring every dataset would create cost without proportionate
evidence. The recommended portfolio has six study families, each tied to
a product decision. Documentation, assumptions, a runnable baseline and
an explicit failure result are more valuable than an impressive count of
downloaded files. A source that is registration-gated or unsuitable for
one estimand must not block another family. This is a portfolio design, not a claim that every listed acquisition or native trial has occurred. The public NSW analysis in Chapter~\ref{gap29:public} develops the experimental branch; Chapters~\ref{gap29:servicing} and~\ref{gap29:policy-uncertainty} develop generated sequential-policy comparisons; Chapter~\ref{gap29:observation} develops generated linkage uncertainty. The generated institution panel in Chapter~\ref{gap29:funding} tests continuity errors but is not the proposed FDIC acquisition. These completed studies supply evidence for the corresponding choices while their broader extensions retain the requirements below.

P1 and existing P2 examples can proceed with local inputs. P3--P6 have
project-generated routes that do not depend on external accounts, paid
model APIs or bank records. Their results must be labeled synthetic
where appropriate. Using public covariates in a simulator improves
distributional realism but does not validate its action or response law.
A proposal can include a live-model evaluation design before paying for
it; a claimed live-model capability requires an actual identified
backend and held-out execution.

\hypertarget{p1-demonstrate-that-the-problem-is-correctly-formulated}{%
\section{P1: demonstrate that the problem is correctly
formulated}\label{p1-demonstrate-that-the-problem-is-correctly-formulated}}

The first study asks whether the prototype preserves the scientific
question as data move through it. For 401(k), write two briefs: the
effect of eligibility on assets and the effect of participation. The
reference specification distinguishes their assumptions and estimands.
For Bank Marketing, ask for pre-contact subscription prediction and the
incremental effect of making a call. The former can be a prediction
exercise with an explicit information cutoff. The latter requires the
system to identify absent assignment/control information and propose a
design.

The baseline is a reviewed template plus deterministic schema/timing
checks. The proposed assistant must output the population, unit, time
zero, feasible action, outcome horizon, available covariates, candidate
diagrams, adjustment or instrument assumptions, and missing evidence.
Score each field separately and record whether uncertainty is retained.
Do not score an invented graph as correct merely because it looks
economically plausible. A deliberately misleading brief that proposes
controlling for call duration should trigger a clock objection.

The completed archive profile provides an input fixture, not final task
performance. Freeze the briefs and reference alternatives before
evaluating a model or agent. Public benchmark text may be familiar to a
model; use disjoint graph templates and new names/numerical mechanisms
for the held-out portion. Record prompted assistance and reviewer
intervention in the denominator of workflow cost.

\hypertarget{p2-make-dependent-financial-computation-reviewable}{%
\section{P2: make dependent financial computation
reviewable}\label{p2-make-dependent-financial-computation-reviewable}}

Retain the existing macroeconomic result as an inherited baseline. For
an FDIC extension, first select a release window whose final included
publication predates the cutoff, a bounded set of institutions and a
documented event definition. Acquire field dictionaries, structure
changes and release information before fitting. Separate branch-level
deposits from consolidated bank financials. Entity continuity, mergers
and geography should be reproducible transformations rather than
unrecorded analyst choices.

The first result should reproduce descriptive totals and a declared
conditional panel association. A causal event study is an additional
design: define event time, comparison units, anticipation, interference,
common shocks and the identifying assumption. A conventional two-way
fixed-effect coefficient and a group-time effect can target different
weighted objects under staggered adoption; matching only the formula
name is insufficient. Include a falsification or negative-control design
where justified, but do not interpret a nonsignificant pretrend as proof
of parallel trends.

Evaluate numerical parity, retained sample/unit counts, missingness,
memory, elapsed time and recovery from an interrupted local job. These
metrics support engineering feasibility. Bank service levels and network
recovery remain future target-platform questions. The local workload
must preserve the complete calculation, including reconstruction and
uncertainty, rather than reporting only a fast inner reducer.

\hypertarget{p3-check-effect-estimators-against-the-right-reference}{%
\section{P3: check effect estimators against the right
reference}\label{p3-check-effect-estimators-against-the-right-reference}}

Begin with a generated randomized experiment that has a declared
assignment probability and known response law. Compare a difference in
means, a prespecified regression adjustment and the proposed score
estimator. Under unequal assignment, a naive mean contrast can remain
interpretable under the actual design, while precision comparisons must
account for the allocation. In observational semi-synthetic cases,
require the declared adjustment information and vary overlap, response
nonlinearity and heterogeneity.

For ACIC, preserve real covariates and simulated quantities as separate
objects. Report ATE error, heterogeneity error when conditional truth is
supplied, interval availability, width, repeated-sampling coverage,
overlap and runtime. Never tune on evaluation potential outcomes. Use
the same scenarios, training information and splits for each method, and
retain failures as outcomes. The completed NSW comparison in Chapter~\ref{gap29:public} reports uncertainty and preserves its exact extract identity. Its estimate is not a noiseless label for another method. An ACIC extension still needs its separately selected scenarios and input-rights review.

Select scenarios and replication counts prospectively from the precision
needed to distinguish meaningful failure. For example, at nominal
coverage 0.95, 400 independent repetitions give a Monte Carlo standard
error near 0.011; that is a planning calculation, not proof that
observed 94\% coverage is acceptable. A simultaneous acceptance rule
across the selected scenarios must be specified before final runs.
Current open calibration findings are not erased by switching to a
public benchmark with unknown truth.

\hypertarget{p4-distinguish-realism-from-sequential-identification}{%
\section{P4: distinguish realism from sequential
identification}\label{p4-distinguish-realism-from-sequential-identification}}

A servicing prototype should represent a fixed policy and a policy
learned on independent training histories. At each decision time,
disclose the available state, chosen action, action propensity,
subsequent outcome and censoring. Include an adverse case with a private
hardship variable that affects both actions and repayment, and a case
with weak support for the proposed policy. A useful result may be a
refusal, a broad identified set or a warning that an off-policy value
cannot be estimated reliably.

Real mortgage histories, if lawfully acquired, improve the realism of
delinquency and termination clocks. They should initially support
descriptive transitions and competing-event handling. If actions are
then generated conditional on recorded histories, label the resulting
policy comparison semi-synthetic; if future outcomes are also generated,
disclose that response law. Policy value under that law is a simulation
result. The proposed bank experiment is justified only when an
unresolved assumption about genuine actions or response materially
changes the policy recommendation.

\hypertarget{p5-carry-ambiguity-through-graph-and-identity-operations}{%
\section{P5: carry ambiguity through graph and identity
operations}\label{p5-carry-ambiguity-through-graph-and-identity-operations}}

For discovery, use small graphs where observational equivalence classes
can be enumerated. The baseline returns the entire compatible class; a
discovery method or language model must not receive credit for an
unjustified single orientation. Evaluate orientation precision,
valid-class retention and the resulting intervention-effect set,
including empty, disconnected or unbounded answers when they are
mathematically appropriate. Add interventional information in a separate
condition to test whether it resolves the intended ambiguity.

For observation construction, start from known entity histories and then
corrupt identifiers, timestamps or links. Keep the hidden generator
mapping outside the analysis interface. Compare exact-oracle, hard-match
and uncertainty-aware routes. Report missed candidate pairs, false
matches and the change in a downstream financial target. High pairwise
matching accuracy does not guarantee a small monetary bias when the rare
high-value records are the ones mismatched. FEBRL can add a public
fixture; it cannot supply the error distribution of the future bank's
audit process.

\hypertarget{p6-evaluate-the-assistant-as-part-of-a-workflow}{%
\section{P6: evaluate the assistant as part of a
workflow}\label{p6-evaluate-the-assistant-as-part-of-a-workflow}}

Use public data and controlled problems for the sequence from business
brief to diagram, identification query, tool execution and explanation.
The baseline should be a competent low-cost workflow: a reviewed form, a
deterministic graph/identification solver where applicable, and the
selected statistical package. The proposed agent should be evaluated for
improvements over that workflow, including errors it introduces and time
spent correcting them.

Measure invalid assumptions, clock violations, unsupported orientations,
identification mistakes, tool/schema failures, hallucinated results,
uncertainty fidelity, successful abstention and end-to-end completion.
Record calls, tokens, elapsed time and human review effort only when
they are actually measured. Live backend identity, version, budget and
custody are necessary for a live performance claim; they are not
prerequisites for the literature survey, offline fixtures, architecture
proposal or protocol. If a live evaluation is unaffordable, narrow the
claim and retain a costed protocol rather than fabricating results.

\hypertarget{common-study-controls}{%
\section{Common study controls}\label{common-study-controls}}

Each new final study needs a prospectively frozen question, input
hashes, methods, splits, scenarios, estimands, assignment/observation
assumptions, numerical tolerances, resource limit and stopping rule.
Exploratory profiles such as the UCI row inspection remain exploratory
and may inform that registration. Existing fitted results cannot be
relabeled fresh evaluation data. A source-access failure blocks only the
affected dataset branch.

An initial resource envelope should be modest: local public files, one
selected data source per role, no paid API by default, bounded CPU jobs
and recorded memory/time. Exact limits should be set after an
input-profile pilot, before final outcomes are examined. The plan does
not attach invented calendar dates or currency costs to unmeasured work.
A baseline that performs as well as the proposed component is evidence
to reuse the baseline, simplify the design or reject that component.

The investment sequence therefore purchases information in stages. Start with the smallest study that can expose a consequential error or establish a useful restricted capability; retain its adverse outcomes; then identify what a new source, method or bank experiment would resolve. The following banking studies put those comparisons into mathematical and numerical detail. Their exact constructions, fitted experiments and local implementations must keep their distinct claims when they inform the common product decision.
