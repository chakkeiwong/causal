# Skeptical review of the developmental rewrite plan

29 October 2026. Author/coordinator review, not independent approval.

## Decision

Proceed with the preservation contract in the plan. A coherent successor is feasible without discarding the extensive evidence. The plan is acceptable for execution only with the following safeguards, which are incorporated in its final form.

## Objections and resolutions

**The earlier gap assessment is already partly outdated.** A later scientific continuation adds substantive comparisons and studies. Freezing only the 1,795-page edition would discard progress. Resolution: the active 1,813-page continuation is the content baseline; the reviewed edition is an additional protected reference. Incorporate the 9,000 generated replications, UCI timing comparison and four interface cases as existing evidence, not newly executed work.

**A short new front section could leave the same book underneath.** Resolution: reorganize the complete input tree around an explicit main argument, give the four banking journeys complete worked treatments, connect comparative choices to them, revise inherited transitions and remove confirmed administrative interruptions from the reader narrative. Keep the detailed comparative material in the same complete monograph with an explained function. Do not claim that every paragraph has been rewritten.

**Literal equation preservation can protect mistakes and miss lost assumptions.** Resolution: record exact before/after spans and their scientific dispositions, check inline mathematics and surrounding prose, and allow reviewed corrections. Unchanged source hashes prove preservation only for unchanged material. Edited spans require semantic review as well as counters.

**Moving important material to a folder could masquerade as preservation.** Resolution: compiled reachability is mandatory. Each original input must be reachable once at its intended successor location, or have an explicit consolidation/correction entry whose scientific content is demonstrably retained. Archive availability alone cannot pass.

**A deletion count can conceal a lost negative finding.** Resolution: publish the full deleted/replaced passages, reasons and destinations. Independently review every content-changing operation after assembly. Known failures and limitations are protected content. Any unaccounted substantive loss blocks release.

**Rewriting could invent confident recommendations.** Resolution: use the existing primary-source audits and declared evidence classes. Choose a bounded first prototype under explicit conditions; distinguish a source-based candidate from an executed package comparison. An unresolved choice remains unresolved. No new live-model benefit, source coverage or institutional price is inferred.

**A financial example could imply bank validation.** Resolution: implement a small, fully specified generated or finite-population case, label its target and assumptions, and carry estimation uncertainty through an explicit cost map. Its purpose is to demonstrate the complete analysis and expose failure, not estimate an actual bank return.

**The updated sponsor could overstate the monograph.** Resolution: generate a new sponsor source tied to the successor's actual findings, alternatives, proposed expenditure and reversal conditions. Leave the earlier sponsor unchanged and do not claim deployment or ROI.

**Review can recurse indefinitely or exceed the image safety limit.** Resolution: one plan review and one terminal review; targeted corrections between them. The six-preview session allowance is exhausted, so no further images are forwarded. New PDFs will be compiled, text/layout inspected and previews prepared. Fresh visual/human review remains an explicit acceptance limit, not a reason to leave the authorized rewrite unfinished.

## Execution instruction

Freeze the baseline and create the content map immediately. Then write the opening and four-journey argument before expanding the comparison and economics. Run the deletion audit on the final candidate and publish its outcome alongside the PDFs. Do not ask the user to approve the already-authorized reversible work.
