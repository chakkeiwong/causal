# Eligibility, treatment receipt and the value of a credit rule

A bank considering a credit eligibility change needs the value of that rule for its eligible population. An instrumental-variable coefficient may instead describe the customers whose receipt changes with encouragement. A regression-discontinuity estimate may concern an arbitrarily small neighborhood of a score threshold. The comparison must preserve those differences before choosing an engine. The recommendation is to use separate ordinary-IV, weak-IV, continuity-RD and local-randomization profiles, with explicit uncertainty and failure contracts. The present comparison uses inspected originals and exact authored examples; it does not certify all software in these families or an implemented adapter.

Let Z denote eligibility, D actual receipt and Y fixed-horizon dollar loss. Under consistency, no interference, independent encouragement, exclusion, monotonicity D(1) >= D(0), and a positive first stage, define delta_D = E[D|Z=1] - E[D|Z=0] and delta_Y analogously. Then delta_D is the complier share and delta_Y/delta_D is the complier mean loss effect. Independence of an encouragement is a distinct design from deterministic eligibility at a score cutoff. The latter needs a justified continuity argument with support approaching the cutoff, or a stated local-randomization assignment law. Exclusion and monotonicity are substantive assumptions; neither an overidentification p-value nor a first-stage statistic supplies them. \citep{exec22_riv_reference,exec22_riv_ivmodel}

## A reproducible banking consequence

The complete stipulated population has complier mass 2/5 with losses (200,50), always-taker mass 1/5 with losses (200,150), and never-taker mass 2/5 with losses (200,200). Losses are potential responses under no receipt and receipt. Independent fair encouragement yields receipt means 1/5 and 3/5, loss means 190 and 130, and a complier loss effect of -150 dollars. The population effect is -70. With a stipulated 100-dollar receipt cost, the complier margin is +50, while universal receipt versus none has margin -30. Compliers are latent response types, so the positive complier margin does not identify an implementable policy that selects those customers. An encouragement costing 10 dollars has incremental net value -delta_Y - 100 delta_D - 10 = +10. Its uncertainty requires the joint reduced-form and first-stage covariance. These exact values are reproduced by the authenticated independent reference and are neither package estimates nor observed bank returns. \citep{exec22_riv_reference}

A direct encouragement benefit of 20 dollars changes the ratio to -200 while the complier treatment effect remains -150. Allowing defiers of mass 1/5 with effect -50, alongside compliers of mass 2/5 with effect -150, instead yields ratio -250. These cases show why a strong computational solution cannot repair an invalid causal interpretation. In the Python reference, separately full-rank columns z and d = epsilon z + u can have projected Gram value 4 epsilon squared. With y = 2d + z/100, ratios at epsilon = 1/100 and 1/1000 are 3 and 12, and the zero case is singular. A small exclusion violation is amplified by weak relevance. \citep{exec22_riv_reference,exec22_pyiv_reference}

## Weak identification and score support need different remedies

For jointly Gaussian reduced-form and first-stage estimates with known covariance entries (v_Y,c,v_D), invert (deltaYhat - b deltaDhat)^2 <= q(v_Y - 2bc + b^2 v_D). This remains a coefficient-null statement even at zero first-stage mean. At q=4 its coverage is approximately 95.45%, not exactly 95%. The exact reference gives the sets (-infinity,-1] union [1,infinity), [1/3,7/3] and the whole real line for three declared inputs. Searching only [-1/2,1/2] in the first case produces an empty grid result, not an empty global confidence set. Infinite endpoints, excluded gaps, search exhaustion and missing results must remain different values. This known-covariance construction does not assert robust validity for arbitrary dependence or reproduce an upstream finite-sample procedure. \citep{exec22_riv_reference,exec22_riv_ivmodel,exec13_ivrd_stata}

Suppose scores occur only at -1 and +1, with eligibility at zero and observed losses 100 and 80. The continuous models m0(r)=100, m1(r)=80 and m0(r)=m1(r)=90-10r agree on observed outcomes but imply cutoff effects -20 and zero. Replicating those scores does not identify the missing limits. If each potential-mean slope is substantively bounded by 10, the cutoff effect set is [-40,0]; with a 10-dollar treatment cost the net set is [-10,30]. Mass-point adjustments cannot create observations near zero, and score-density balance cannot rule out a second discontinuous policy. An alternative local-randomization design needs its own justified assignment law. In a fixed six-customer window with losses (0,0,0,100,100,100), exactly three assigned uniformly, the extreme absolute contrast has sharp-null p-value 2/20 = 0.1. Selecting a window after examining outcomes is a different inference problem. \citep{exec22_riv_reference,exec22_riv_rdrobust,exec22_riv_rddensity,exec22_riv_rdlocrand}

## Reuse and the next evidence purchase

Retain original R/Python workers as conditional candidates rather than porting only coefficient formulas. An ordinary-IV path, a qualified weak-IV inversion path and two distinct RD design profiles should share typed requests but retain separate scientific contracts. A commercial host and an open package installed on it have separate rights and version obligations. rdrobust's CRAN 4.1.1 publication at 23:20:02 UTC on 7 October falls after the 15:59:59 UTC client cutoff; the authenticated 4.0.0 distribution remains the cutoff reference. Package-level GPL/NCSA/alternative-license findings do not clear transitive dependencies or the actual distribution arrangement. \citep{exec22_riv_rdrobust,exec22_riv_gpl2,exec22_riv_gpl3,exec22_linearmodels_reuse,exec13_ivrd_survey}

The next prototype should use public/generated inputs with independently known targets, strong/weak/zero first stages, valid/invalid exclusion, dependence, weighted missing rows, coarse scores, competing policies, numerical failures and disconnected sets. Freeze the package environment, scenarios, target, sample law, seeds, tolerances, replication budget and stopping rule before final draws. Include every requested fit in the denominator. Compare semantic correctness and useful uncertainty before runtime, then account for conversion, failed runs, diagnostics, review, licenses and maintenance. No measured bank ROI or native qualification follows from the present proposal comparison. The scientific argument and worked examples are already integrated in the current monograph; this deliverable reconciles the dedicated comparison obligation without claiming that the broader coverage cells are complete.
