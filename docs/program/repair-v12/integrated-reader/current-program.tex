The canonical state has 21 dispositions among 40 packages. P12, IN01,
IN02, IN04 and LA01--LA05 have scoped passing receipts. LA06 is
inconclusive because its final live study inputs are unavailable. The
original eleven V11 dispositions remain preserved. M00--M03 established
the controls. R01 qualified a selected funding construction; R03
qualified a selected nonlinear fixed-policy construction. R02's
persistent map/calendar construction, R04-v3's temporal graph-to-effect
construction and R05-v2's dependent learner have negative results in
material regimes. E01-v2 qualified local linked streaming and recovery;
I01 integrated these findings. None of the ten original gaps has met
every scientific and external gate.

The required standard remains a connected scholarly argument from a
bank's decision and information clock through model, assumptions,
identification or an identified set, estimation, uncertainty,
diagnostics, numerical behavior and financial consequences. Notation
must be explained; substantive alternatives and adverse examples must be
worked through. The named FinMarketing monograph is a standard of
development and argument. It is not evidence that an integration works.
The new page allocations respond to the user's explicit requested scope;
they do not replace the standard of scholarship.

Every cited scholarly work needs its genuine PDF in docs/papers/, named
``Title FirstAuthorSurname(year).pdf''. Every scholarly, software and
official documentation citation needs a source identity, version/date,
local copy, SHA-256, inspected technical anchors and supported claims.
Metadata can establish identity and discovery; a package name or
abstract cannot substantiate an algorithmic claim. Unavailable originals
remain in an omissions register. Inherited results, new derivations,
synthetic examples, hypotheses and verified implementations must be
distinguished. Reproducible banking examples and inspection of the
rendered PDF are acceptance requirements.

There are four separate decisions: author scholarship delivered; a
capability qualified in a declared regime; independent/bank/platform
acceptance; and a signed investment/adoption decision. A critical survey
can be excellent while concluding that a method fails. Neither that
survey nor an API can repair missing identification, establish authentic
audit truth or accept itself independently.

\hypertarget{mr01-search-every-causal-area-and-decide-what-to-use}{%
\subsection{MR01: search every causal area and decide what to
use}\label{mr01-search-every-causal-area-and-decide-what-to-use}}

The current software chapter and its 44-project register are a starting
corpus. They do not provide the requested multi-chapter survey or full
commercial comparison. SW01 retains 110 original search leads and one
discovered pgmpy row: 111 catalog entries, 77 audit entries of differing
scopes, and 22 new pinned technical reviews. Its 285 acquisition
registrations include 239 saved, 24 failed and 22 unexecuted.
Twenty-three of 102 cells have partial individual reviews; none is
closed. A lead is neither a verified citation nor an assertion that a
current package exists.

The coverage contract crosses seventeen areas with six dimensions: R,
Python, Java, C++, other relevant implementations and commercial
products. All 102 cells must receive a supported disposition. The areas
include IID, latent and temporal discovery; identification and
counterfactuals; effect and heterogeneous-effect estimation;
experiments, IV and RD; longitudinal and survival analysis; dynamic
policies; panel/DiD; synthetic controls; dynamic panels/time series;
networks and interference; mediation and sensitivity; transport and
missingness; Bayesian/SCM and state computation; trustworthy
observations; and computational workflows.

SW01 will first record search terms, engines/registries, search date,
cutoff, result identities, screening decisions and unavailable sources.
Search families include method names and aliases, official package
registries, upstream code and release histories, primary method papers,
vendor manuals, and backward/forward citation checks. Language wrappers
and native engines must be distinguished; aliases cannot inflate the
inventory. Two documented passes cover both the method families and
language/vendor dimensions. A cell without an eligible candidate needs
the searches and exclusion reasons, not a blank. Current mutable
documentation cannot be silently presented as its 7 October version.
Later material is a separately dated delta with an explicit scope
decision.

The aim is defensible systematic coverage, not an impossible claim to
enumerate every unpublished or inaccessible program. Each cell records a
search stopping rule, exclusions and residual coverage limits. Essential
unavailable evidence keeps the affected claim or repair open. Public
technical manuals may support a documented capability even when a paid
binary is unavailable; they cannot support claims about measured
performance, usability or numerical agreement.

SW02--SW05 develop the mathematical and operational comparison. Each
substantive package dossier explains the target, population,
intervention, assumptions, sampling law, information clock,
identification, graph/effect output, uncertainty and sensitivity,
diagnostics, supported runtime and failure behavior. It then works
through an appropriate banking example and compares substantive
alternatives under the same target. A panel fixed-effect regression is
not automatically an identified treatment effect; a selected graph is
not an effect confidence region; a predictive forest is not
automatically a causal forest. The chapter must explain these
distinctions through operations and examples.

SW06 compares commercial products by product, edition and dated version.
For each proposed operation it records the vendor's documented claim and
evidence level, our actual state, the capability difference, and a
build/buy/partner/omit recommendation. Our state is one of implemented
and qualified in scope, implemented but conditional, proposed, or
absent. The comparison can conclude that a vendor is stronger. ``Not
evaluated'' is distinct from ``absent'', and a proposed feature is not a
delivered competitive advantage. Pricing is recorded as public,
quote-required, unavailable or actually measured; no invented total cost
or vendor contact is part of this program.

SW07 assigns every admitted open-source catalog entry an action: adopt
directly, integrate through an adapter, port a selected operation, use
as a research reference, defer, or reject. A decision needs technical
evidence, a substantive alternative, licensing and maintenance evidence,
a bank use case, a reason and a revisit trigger. Direct adoption
requires semantic fit and an acceptable dependency contract. An adapter
must preserve target, units, information clock, graph ambiguity and
uncertainty. A port additionally needs license permission, an upstream
behavioral oracle, numerical/statistical equivalence checks and a
maintenance owner. Rewriting an algorithm is not presumed better than
using it.

IN03 implements selected paths in this repository. SW08 then qualifies
the selected R, Python, Java and C++ engine paths under a registered
design with independent small references. Native bindings and subprocess
boundaries receive separate cost and failure evidence. All requests,
including crashes, unsupported operations and unavailable runtimes, stay
in the record. An unavailable language path forbids an executed claim
for that path. It does not justify inventing a run or concealment in the
critical survey. SW09 reconciles the findings into ten connected
chapters and closes MR01 only after substantive review.

\hypertarget{mr02-language-models-within-a-causal-workflow}{%
\subsection{MR02: language models within a causal
workflow}\label{mr02-language-models-within-a-causal-workflow}}

LA01 maps primary literature and packages across fourteen task families.
These include business translation, variable definitions and
measurement, evidence and expert disagreement, diagrams, IID and
latent/temporal discovery, experimental queries, identification,
data/code work, longitudinal decisions, estimation and validation,
reporting/monitoring, agent architecture and evaluation/cost. Search and
source-audit rules are the same as for MR01. Favorable findings, failed
replications, counterexamples and benchmark limitations all belong in
the map. A generic agent framework must not be credited with causal
algorithms it does not implement. A remembered paper title is only a
discovery lead until verified.

LA02 starts with four banking journeys: funding response, sequential
servicing, discovery followed by an intervention query, and trustworthy
observation construction. For each, the system must translate a business
request into a decision maker, available actions, target population,
outcome and horizon, estimand, observation/availability clock, feasible
intervention and required information. Ambiguous business wording should
produce alternatives or abstention. It must not invent a unique
``correct'' causal question.

For example, ``reduce delinquency without reducing value'' leaves the
customer population, servicing action, timing, loss horizon, cost of
contact, constraints and feasible comparison unresolved. An LLM may help
expose those choices and draft an estimand. It cannot certify that the
bank's logging policy is known or that records contain all time-varying
confounders. The casebook will compare alternative formulations and show
how their identification requirements differ.

Every proposed diagram edge must have a statement of variables and time,
evidence type and anchor, assumptions, counterevidence and review
status. Data-supported relations, domain hypotheses and language-model
suggestions remain different objects. Expert disagreement generates
explicit alternative graphs. Deterministic checks catch temporal
contradictions, accidental collider adjustment, impossible interventions
and discarded endpoint ambiguity. Passing those checks establishes
internal consistency, not causal truth.

LA03 organizes discovery methods by role: language-only proposals,
empirical discovery, retrieved domain evidence, priors/constraints,
hybrid search, active expert queries and experiments. Each mechanism
receives a primary-source account, its inferential role, a banking
example and a counterexample. CPDAG/PAG equivalence, latent confounding
and temporal restrictions must survive suggested orientations.
Downstream identification and effect sets expose the consequence of
graph errors. Agreement among prompts or agents cannot become a
calibrated confidence statement without a justified statistical
procedure.

The remedies are specific and testable. Unsupported edges require
anchored retrieval and review; temporal leakage requires explicit
availability clocks; prompt instability requires repeated perturbed
evaluations and abstention; benchmark memorization requires held-out
tasks and renamed/restructured cases; graph uncertainty requires
equivalence-aware scoring and effect-set propagation. Retrieval quality
and agreement can still fail, and the chapters must say where.
Observational equivalence requires extra information or bounded
conclusions; more fluent language cannot remove it.

LA04 covers design, identification, preparation, linkage, estimator
selection, code generation, diagnostics, sensitivity, reporting and
assumption monitoring. Each task receives a useful agent role, a
non-agent baseline, required evidence object, failure mode and limited
remedy. Reporting must distinguish a theorem, an assumption, a synthetic
run, an unexecuted suggestion and an external finding. Retrieved content
is data rather than authority to invoke tools. Artifact and permission
boundaries must be explicit in the actual integration design.

LA05 builds an auditable local prototype on the shared operation
contract. It carries the problem, graph alternatives, source evidence,
tool calls, validation and refusals end to end. Replayed or mock model
responses can qualify contract behavior; they cannot qualify live model
quality. LA06 freezes models/versions, prompts, tools, inputs, split,
costs, repetitions, seed, stopping rule and metrics before any final
empirical evaluation. Comparators include non-agent workflows, data-only
discovery, language-only suggestions and retrieved/hybrid variants where
justified. Metrics cover formulation validity, assumptions, equivalence-
aware graph quality, downstream effect error/coverage, abstention,
reviewer time and full workflow cost. Missing model access or expert
labels remains missing.

LA07 integrates eight chapters. Business translation, diagram
construction and discovery receive the greatest combined allocation. The
result should explain what assistance is useful, why failures occur and
which remedies have evidence, without presenting agentic orchestration
as a solution to D01--D10.

\hypertarget{mr03-one-semantic-core-and-actual-consumers}{%
\subsection{MR03: one semantic core and actual
consumers}\label{mr03-one-semantic-core-and-actual-consumers}}

The saved nine-repository documentation index is historical evidence.
The fresh seed inventory observes eighteen workspace directories; that
observation alone does not establish eighteen usable integrations. IN01
must inspect applicable instructions, revision or content identity,
actual entrypoints, documented schemas and role of each repository,
grouping worktrees with their projects. Every directory receives a
relevant, adjacent, unsuitable or deferred disposition with an inspected
reason. Standalone notebook, batch, CLI and service uses are included.
All writing, fixtures and adapters stay in causal.

The inventory will examine likely statistical and banking consumers,
protocol servers and research utilities on their actual interfaces. A
familiar directory name is not enough to assert a function exists.
Instructions, source inspection, documentation snapshots, local contract
doubles, actual library/CLI/MCP runs and signed consumer acceptance are
separate evidence levels. Any run that would modify a sibling's files,
environment, services or dependencies remains deferred unless a safe
isolated execution can be established within the authorized scope.

IN02 defines one operation layer for problem specification, graph
objects, identification, design, estimation, sensitivity, audits and
reporting. Requests carry units, population, intervention,
time/availability, assumptions and version. Results preserve provenance,
unsupported/error states and finite, disconnected, unbounded, empty or
refused sets. Library calls, CLI JSON/exit codes and MCP tools/resources
map to the same validated operations. An API that turns an unbounded
confidence set into a finite interval fails conformance even if its
transport is successful.

Large inputs use authorized artifact handles with content identity and
declared custody. The contract covers schema/capability negotiation,
bounded job budgets, polling, cancellation, retries, idempotency,
checkpoints and recovery. The MCP specification and SDK version must be
locally audited before implementation; an MCP-shaped JSON response is
not a tested MCP server.

IN03 implements representative reviewed language adapters. IN04
implements the actual CLI/MCP surfaces and calls the server from a real
client. IN04 depends on the common contract, not completion of LLM
research. LA05 uses that same contract; IN05 brings the strands together
in registered whole-workflow trials. It covers all four bank journeys
and standalone usage, with at least three relevant repository contracts
selected after IN01. Real eligible clients and engines, read-only
inspection and contract doubles remain individually labeled.

Fault trials include incompatible versions, invalid graphs, unit/clock
mismatch, missing artifacts, permission errors, timeouts, interrupted
work and partial publication. Comparisons include scientific output,
uncertainty, error propagation, recovery and full cost before speed.
Maintainer adoption and target-bank platform acceptance remain X03,
regardless of author conformance results. IN06 develops four operator
and architecture chapters from the actual evidence, with diagrams,
client examples, failure behavior and explicit adoption limits.
