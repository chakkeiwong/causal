## Reconstructed arguments and resulting decisions

1. **Dependent responses.** The intervention replaces one structural shock;
   the denominator fixes its impact scale. The stationary Gaussian model
   implies summable product covariances and a quadratic-form CLT, rather
   than assuming a score CLT without a law. Bartlett PSD follows from moving
   sums; its stated bandwidth follows from the stronger Gaussian moment
   bound, not Newey–West's different little-o condition. Weak-IV residual
   tests avoid division by the weak denominator. Analytic propagation
   derivatives agree with numerical differentiation. Near-unit and invalid-IV
   failures remain visible and prevent a finite-sample certification claim.
   Direct moments are the flexible principal route; the proxy VAR is a
   compulsory structural challenger only when finite-order dynamics are
   scientifically defended. Bandwidth sensitivity is diagnostic, not an
   automatic selection-based coverage theorem.

2. **Learning and panels.** The separated-block construction spells out
   absolute regularity, the lag/horizon-expanded gap, coupling error, nuisance
   product rates and the stronger accumulated fitted-HAC error condition.
   The oracle CLT now includes a big-block/small-gap Lindeberg argument.
   Finite sparse fitting is distinguished from an empirical proof of the
   population rate assumptions. A factor model cannot replace sparse causal
   adjustment merely because it predicts well. Fixed bank weights produce
   date scores, and the common-shock experiment explicitly retains the HAC
   undercoverage even after the gross iid-row mistake is removed. Integrated
   level responses use telescoping stationary changes and full covariance;
   VECM/rank and lag-augmented projection alternatives retain their different
   assumptions and targets.

3. **Longitudinal fitting.** Whole trajectories are the independent units.
   Fixed policy contrasts retain within-customer covariance. The finite
   nuisance-table rates make the earlier longitudinal remainder negligible.
   Backward weighted-intercept targeting solves the relevant residual
   equations and respects continuation bounds. It is not mislabeled as the
   earlier unweighted clever-covariate fluctuation. The evaluation-fold
   update has fixed finite dimension; the text does not generalize that
   argument to arbitrary adaptive regression. Deterministic policies expose
   larger errors than moderate stochastic changes. Direct/IPS point errors
   are compared without inventing standard errors that ignore fitting.

4. **Controlled latent inference.** The unknown binary emission model used
   in fitting is identified through three signal views under randomized
   exogenous actions. Inversions, eigenvalue separation, state-label
   invariance, initial-law recovery and reward recovery are explicit.
   Population recovery is checked independently. The observed likelihood,
   posterior-count score, EM constraints, optimizer refinement, information
   matrix and fixed-policy monetary recursion refer to the same model.
   Hidden-path enumeration and direct likelihood derivatives agree.
   Consistency/CLT require an approximate global maximizer, interior truth
   and nonsingular information; multistart fitting cannot establish them.
   Weak emissions do not imply weak full information when rewards remain
   informative. The path-law value bound holds the actual observed-history
   policy fixed; it does not transfer a fully observed bound without proof.

5. **Graph to effect inference.** Nine Gaussian variance pivots give one
   simultaneous covariance event. All DAG rejection and effect interval
   maps are conservative on that event, with no separate post-selection
   inference claim. Faithfulness is not required for retaining the true
   Markov graph in this small class. Retained graphs can include infeasible
   members, so the result is an outer set. Interval operations round outward,
   but quantile/variance computation is not formally certified arithmetic.
   The temporal coefficient-box route has its own structural VAR1 and
   martingale argument; it does not qualify general PCMCI/LPCMCI. Hidden
   confounding and an omitted lag produce wrong effects despite good
   observational or numerical fit. No threshold can repair their premises.

6. **Observation construction.** The finite grammar includes key, clocks,
   revision, aggregation and entity hypotheses. Supplied constraints have
   explicit scientific meaning; ranking never eliminates a material
   alternative. Oracle inclusion plus valid constraints implies correct
   acceptance, and the manuscript states precisely that conditional result.
   Revisions are selected before event filtering; strict availability is
   checked against the independently frozen oracle. Ambiguous stocks/flows
   and household links change effect estimates by about ten units and cause
   abstention. The accounting example uses extra independent information.
   The engine does not infer exclusion, exchangeability or a calibrated
   entity posterior from metadata.

7. **Interfaces and computation.** Version 0.4 represents curves, covariance,
   interval unions, graph sets and fixed-policy values explicitly. Finite
   mass kernels validate support, normalization and information order.
   Lowering is restricted to the actual mathematical recipes. Unit/sign
   transformations propagate covariance and unbounded set topology;
   clock/weight/sampling changes can be rejected. Six standalone requests
   execute with data hashes and typed results. Exact sibling fields were
   read and copied locally; local mapping checks are not sibling execution
   or adoption. Installed DuckDB/Polars revisions, Lasso loss normalization
   and SciPy optimization have independent small references, separate from
   the 44-project document review.

8. **Cost and investment.** Complete workflows include reconstruction,
   fitting, inference, sensitivity/resampling and report serialization.
   Fixed banks do not multiply the independent macro sample. The larger
   latent cost is dominated by refits, not the first likelihood evaluation.
   The 19-refit timing experiment is not a precise bootstrap-coverage study.
   The same scientific workload must be priced for expert composition;
   CPU timing cannot substantiate the illustrative analyst-hour savings.
   The economic conclusion remains staged construction contingent on reuse
   and actual matched-task cost evidence.

## Defects found and repaired during this review

- The fitted-HAC RMS statement used an unjustified additive root-n term.
  It now uses a deterministic high-probability population error bound,
  conditional mean-square expectation and Markov's inequality.
- Graph interval operations initially used ordinary endpoint arithmetic.
  They now round outward, while ordinary sample variance/quantile computation
  remains expressly uncertified. JSON infinities became typed null endpoints.
- The temporal graph confidence construction was stronger than the initial
  generic temporal-discovery description. The selected Gaussian VAR1/no
  contemporaneous-edge class is now stated as a distinct bounded route;
  general temporal discovery is kept exploratory.
- A weighted-intercept target update was liable to be confused with the
  earlier clever-covariate implementation. The score equation and its
  finite-dimensional justification are now given explicitly.
- Duplicate stock records at the same entity/time were initially summed.
  That candidate is now rejected; distinct events are not silently merged.
- The standalone runner now checks the exact reward family, action/signal
  support, feasible state actions, column order, curve grid and policy
  definition. Family names cannot silently authorize a different estimand.
- A DuckDB parameterized CREATE VIEW and a wrong latent-fit keyword failed
  in the pilot. Both failures and their repairs are preserved; final runs
  use the observed APIs. A missing JSON serialization conversion in the
  earlier finite checks was also repaired before a passing receipt.
- BLAS and DuckDB were limited to one thread but Polars was initially not.
  The timing evidence was archived and rerun with Polars explicitly limited;
  the reported table uses the corrected full runs.
- A broader controlled value horizon changed code input hashes. Original
  receipts were preserved and unchanged statistical specifications rerun;
  hashes were not merely refreshed without execution.
- The source/build audit correctly rejected stale manuscript/check inputs.
  Affected exact checks and the final document build were rerun before
  closure. A successful identity audit alone does not assess scholarship.
- The enlarged rendered targeting equation exposed a missing TeX backslash
  before the fluctuation parameter epsilon. The symbol was corrected;
  the fitted update was already implemented correctly. A new build and
  rendered inspection replace the preliminary PDF identities.
