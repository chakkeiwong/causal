\chapter{LLM-assisted causal discovery: disciplined assistance around an uncertain graph}
\label{ch:llm-causal-discovery-l4}

A banking request rarely arrives as a graph. It arrives as a sentence such as ``does an early arrears review improve recovery?'' and a collection of tables whose columns have operational names but incomplete histories. A language model can help turn the sentence into candidate variables, clocks, mechanisms and alternative diagrams. It cannot supply the bank's assignment law or turn a plausible story into an identified effect. The proposal therefore treats an LLM as an evidence-organising assistant around a protected scientific core.

Let $D$ be the observed records, $T$ the retrieved or supplied technical text, and $M$ a declared model, prompt, decoding and tool configuration. A graph proposal is an output of $(D,T,M)$, not an independent sample from the banking process. The proposal must record the variable dictionary, unit, time zero, intervention, outcome, horizon and admissible information before asking for edges. A candidate graph $G$ is retained as a hypothesis until its meanings, assumptions and observable implications are reviewed.

\section{From a business sentence to competing causal questions}

Suppose the business sentence concerns an offer made at an account's first qualifying arrears date. The unit is the eligible account at that date; $A$ is the offer, and $Y$ is discounted net recovery for 180 days. A first target is $E[Y(1)-Y(0)]$ in the declared eligible population. Acceptance and eventual restructuring terms are different actions. A note entered on day 11 cannot be a day-10 covariate merely because its event date is earlier. The assistant should produce a typed brief with unresolved fields rather than guess them.

A useful output is a set of diagrams. One may contain predecision distress $U$ causing both offer assignment and recovery; another may treat a complaint note as a post-offer collider; a third may distinguish branch policy from customer-level assignment. Each diagram is checked for identification and implied independences separately. If the resulting effects differ, the correct recommendation is to obtain the evidence that distinguishes the diagrams or to report a set of effects. Choosing a familiar DAG because it produces a number is a scientific failure.

The literature supplies complementary roles. CausalAgent and ORCA illustrate agent coordination and shared relational state; CausalSmith makes statements, proof dependencies and compiler results explicit; graph-validation studies show that prompting can assist review but does not establish graph truth. Their reported completion or demonstration rates are source findings, not bank performance \cite{v12_la_2602_11527,exec13_census_orca,exec13_census_causalsmith,exec13_census_graph_validation}. The inspected OCI workflow is closer to the estimation boundary: a human principal reviews specifications, diagnostics and notebooks. It still changes the target when overlap trimming is applied, so the target and retained population must be printed in every report \cite{v12_la_2607_22443}. These distinctions prevent a structure-learning demonstration from being mistaken for a validated effect estimator.

\section{What the assistant may and may not infer}

Language-only answers can propose domain mechanisms and identify missing questions. They cannot reveal whether an omitted branch policy or customer characteristic generated a particular observational association. A response to ``is pricing unrelated to risk given branch?'' is a language response process, not a conditional-independence test on records. Agreement among repeated calls may reflect shared context, cache or model priors. The numerical core must carry its own observations, null, sampling law and calibration.

Retrieval can add a policy definition or an owner-approved timing constraint, but it can also return contradictory or irrelevant text. Retrieval quality and scientific support are therefore separate fields. A missing passage means ``not retrieved'' unless the corpus and search protocol justify a stronger claim. Evidence-grounded auditing work demonstrates this distinction: a gated pipeline can reduce false alarms while missing some omissions, and its small human pilot is preliminary and in-sample. The banking report should expose retrieval status, assumption status and expert adjudication separately \cite{v12_la_2609_30867}.

Text extraction has the same boundary. Under independent centered Gaussian disturbances with $\operatorname{Var}(U)=\operatorname{Var}(\epsilon_A)=1$, if true distress is $U$, an action is $A=U+\epsilon_A$, an extracted score is $W=U+\epsilon_W$, and recovery is $Y=\tau A+U+\epsilon_Y$, then conditioning on $W$ leaves residual confounding when $\operatorname{Var}(\epsilon_W)=s>0$. The population coefficient is $\tau+s/(1+2s)$. With $\tau=-1/5$ and $s=1$, it is $2/15$: the sign reverses although the score is measured without sampling error. This is a project calculation under a declared Gaussian law, not evidence that every text representation fails. It shows why predictive extraction accuracy cannot be used as a substitute for causal measurement validation \cite{exec13_census_text_review_arxiv}.

\section{Discovery outputs and graph uncertainty}

The core output should preserve CPDAG or PAG uncertainty, endpoint marks, variable bijections, isolated nodes and an explicit list of unsupported composite encodings. A proposed orientation is an assumption addition, not an automatic consequence of a language explanation. If a language model converts a PAG to a DAG for an intervention analysis, the conversion must be recorded and the original equivalence class retained. An effect identified in one member of the class is not identified for the class without a justification.

For a candidate graph $G$, the report contains: (i) the business-to-variable mapping and clock; (ii) the graph set and provenance of every edge; (iii) the identification result for the declared estimand; (iv) diagnostics such as positivity, missingness and measurement quality; and (v) unresolved alternatives. The states \texttt{identified}, \texttt{nonidentified}, \texttt{unknown}, \texttt{unsupported}, \texttt{malformed} and \texttt{failed} are distinct. A blank graph or empty JSON object is not a successful abstention. The agent may recommend an operation, but only fixed whitelisted operations may execute it; retrieved text and agent messages are never executable code.

A deterministic baseline is essential. The baseline is an analyst-reviewed brief, a versioned dictionary, fixed graph fixtures and reviewed numerical code. The assisted path receives the same information and is compared on target preservation, clock violations, unsupported certainty, graph ambiguity, identification decisions and complete failure accounting. If a critic selects only reports with good diagnostics, coverage is reported both over all requests and over selected requests; selection is not conditional coverage.


\section{An exact graph fixture and the cost of extra orientation}

A three-variable Gaussian fixture makes the distinction between a statistical
constraint and a semantic assertion visible. Let $A$ denote the offer, $B$ an
intermediate engagement measure and $C$ a recovery measure in a generated
model. In the project chain fixture, the correlation matrix has
$\operatorname{Cov}(A,B)=\operatorname{Cov}(B,C)=1/2$ and
$\operatorname{Cov}(A,C)=1/4$, with unit variances. Then
\[
 \operatorname{Cov}(A,C\mid B)=\tfrac14-\tfrac12\tfrac12=0.
\]
In the project collider fixture the two adjacent covariances remain $1/2$,
but $\operatorname{Cov}(A,C)=0$ and
$\operatorname{Cov}(A,C\mid B)=-1/4$. Gaussianity converts zero covariance to
independence in these fixtures. Under causal sufficiency, acyclicity and
faithfulness, the two patterns support different equivalence classes. Neither
pattern validates the variable meanings, excludes selection, or proves that
real banking data follow either fixture. A language explanation may suggest
why a collider is plausible; the formal core computes the implications and
retains the assumptions that licensed them.

When the output is a CPDAG or PAG, a report should state which endpoints are
invariant and which are unresolved. A business decision may depend on an effect
that changes across members of the class. The project then needs a domain
restriction, an intervention, or a set-valued decision analysis; forcing a
single DAG is not an estimation method. ORCA's reported use of a consensus PAG
followed by conversion to an actionable DAG illustrates exactly where an
additional decision enters the workflow. That conversion may be useful for a
scenario analysis, but its consequences must be tested across alternative
orientations and attributed to the added assumption \\cite{exec13_census_orca}.

\section{Comparators, diagnostics and failure accounting}

The empirical comparator is not merely an unassisted prompt. It includes a
fixed analyst specification, a numerical graph algorithm under its declared
assumptions, and a reviewer who can reject a bad variable or edge. A language
assistant can improve the time needed to assemble that specification or find
contradictory evidence. The comparison must count those gains alongside
reviewer corrections, failed requests and the consequences of an incorrect
effect decision. For the prototype, the same public or generated cases are
fed to assisted and unassisted workflows with the target and truth hidden
from the agent. Scoring distinguishes vocabulary mapping, clock validity,
recovered skeleton, endpoint marks, identification, effect error, interval
coverage and scientific refusal. An orientation score conditional on
true-positive edges is not a whole-graph score; the missed and false edges
remain in the denominator for decision-level analysis.

Two adversarial tests are especially revealing. First, keep the mechanism
fixed while changing names from recognizable banking terms to neutral codes;
then keep familiar names while reversing the generated mechanism. The former
probes reliance on labels, and the latter probes whether a familiar story
survives contrary data. Second, provide a postdecision note whose event date
precedes treatment but whose availability date follows it. The assistant must
refuse the note as a pretreatment adjustment variable. Neither test proves
bank truth, but both expose failures that a fluent explanation can hide.

The proposed initial implementation adapts fixed reviewed graph and estimate
operations; it does not admit arbitrary notebook text, an unrestricted package
namespace or a free-form MCP tool. Direct scoped PC integration with
causal-learn is a candidate, with FCI reviewed separately. A source and
conversion decision exists, but executable admission is deferred until a
complete dependency environment, endpoint semantics and failure handling are
checked. R pcalg and Java Tetrad remain independent comparison paths. The
assistant may compare their declared outputs, but cannot conflate a PC
bidirected conflict with a latent-confounding PAG edge or a CI-test threshold
with whole-graph confidence.

\section{Public evidence and the prototype boundary}

Public and generated evidence can establish that the typed workflow preserves known truth in declared regimes, rejects invalid clocks, retains graph ambiguity and handles malformed results. It cannot establish authentic bank semantics, assignment evidence, transport or production reliability. CausalDS provides a useful benchmark pattern because it separates narrated scenes, observed proxies, private structural truth and deterministic scoring, with abstention for nonidentified tasks. Its source scorer records both uncapped and capped interval losses, so both must remain visible: capping can reverse a method ranking. These are design requirements, not a live comparison of model providers \cite{v12_la_2607_08093}.

The narrow prototype should therefore adapt the reviewed specification and critic artefacts behind bounded operations, audit the generator and scorer, and run a preregistered public/generated task set. A later live provider study adds model/version, custody, prices, prompts and independent judgments. A bank study is justified only when the prototype isolates a decision-relevant uncertainty and a positive value of information. Until then, the scientifically useful answer may be an effect, an interval, a set of graph-specific effects or a refusal with the missing evidence named.
