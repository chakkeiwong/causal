# Scientific completion program

Proposed 8 October 2026, Asia/Shanghai. Publication cutoff: 7 October 2026;
new retrievals carry their actual access dates. This program closes the
[comparative gaps](../reviews/remaining-gaps-v3-2026-10-08.md) against the
FinMarketing, BayesFilter and CIP standards. This file preserves the proposed
work and its exit rules. Execution is now recorded as complete at author-reviewed
proposal scope in [Mission Control](../mission-control.md), the
[phase receipts](phase-results.md) and the
[completion review](../reviews/scientific-completion-author-review.md).

The deliverable is a sustained research monograph and a scientifically
specified reusable product. Its central capabilities remain large-scale
causal time-series analysis, longitudinal and partially observed decisions,
causal discovery and automatic data organization. The proposal must serve
both neighboring financial applications and an independent product.

## Working principle and scope

Organize the next work around a common scientific argument and four worked
problems. The field survey retains all 14 domains of the
[theory map](theory-map.md). Worked problems force the connections to be
complete; they do not replace the broader scholarly survey. Bounded numerical
support is legitimate, but excluding a central capability merely to pass a
completion check is not.

Each supported operation must connect:

economic decision and information → causal model and diagram → identification
or identified set → statistical functional → estimator and learning problem →
sampling uncertainty and sensitivity → numerical method → financial
consequence → reusable software operation.

Keep the substantial graph, IV, semiparametric and longitudinal derivations.
Extend or correct them where the selected dynamic regimes change their
assumptions. Avoid another wholesale reset, a page-count target or a new
catalogue of methods without decisions.

Separate supported analysis, conditional research analysis and unsupported
requests. Supported analysis needs an explicit model, observation and
sampling regime, identification argument, selected estimator and justified
uncertainty. Conditional model fitting must retain unresolved identification
obligations. A timeout or missing adapter is never nonidentification.
Narrowing an initial claim requires explaining the lost banking use and
credible alternatives; it does not close the corresponding survey question.

## Four connected worked problems

These are proposed study specifications and method priorities, not claims
that a theorem, package or bank dataset already supports them. S01 settles
the exact model and sampling classes. Later changes require a stated reason
and review of the affected arguments.

| Problem | Question and initial experiment | Methods to decide between | Required adverse comparison |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Structural responses | Deposit, funding-contribution and lending responses over horizons 0–12 to an explicitly normalized policy-rate shock. Begin with a stationary finite-dimensional linear system and external instrument; distinguish one series from a bank panel with common shocks. | LP-IV versus structural VAR propagation; dependent orthogonal nuisance learning versus a low-dimensional reference; sparse versus factor control reduction. | Weak/invalid instruments, persistence/cointegration, common shocks, omitted lags, release revisions, aggregation, pointwise versus simultaneous coverage. |
| B. Sequential decisions | Discounted collections value under two prespecified eligible assistance policies, first using recorded histories and then a declared controlled latent model. Distinguish independent customers from shared calendar shocks. | Sequential augmentation/targeting versus g-computation and cumulative weighting; controlled finite-state likelihood fitting versus a justified numerical alternative. | Poor overlap, censoring, private logger information, weak emissions, misspecification, common shocks and policy selection using evaluation data. |
| C. Discovery to an intervention query | Which intervention conclusions survive the admissible graph class for balances, pricing, liquidity and repayment? Start with small enumerated reference classes and develop a supported stationary temporal class. | Conditional-independence search with background restrictions; latent-variable and functional-model alternatives under their own assumptions. | Near-unfaithfulness, hidden common causes, truncated history, contemporaneous ambiguity, screening and aggregation. |
| D. Automatic observation construction | Which histories are justified by customer, account, action, outcome and release-vintage records, and how do alternative interpretations change A–C? | Constrained candidate generation and validation as the initial mechanism; statistical linkage/ranking with a specified calibration model where justified. | Revisions versus repeated actions, uncertain households, delayed availability, stock/flow confusion and endogenous missingness. |

These problems share notation and observation objects, and reuse synthetic
mechanisms where appropriate. A customer policy effect and a macro structural
shock remain different targets; explain their relationship without forcing
them into the same estimating equation.

### S01: intellectual architecture and literature synthesis

Create `docs/program/scientific-specification.md` and four study dossiers
under `docs/studies/`. Each specifies economic units, decision clock,
diagram/model, observation law, estimand, sampling experiment, methods,
uncertainty target and open questions. Define evaluation contrasts before
examining final method results.

Reconcile the existing source-question matrix rather than create an
unconnected bibliography. For each unresolved choice:

1. Inspect foundational primary derivations, assumptions and relevant
   correction/version chains, using saved copies first.
2. Follow references and subsequent developments with a targeted update
   through the client cutoff. Record queries, selection and access limits.
3. Compare statistical objects and guarantees in common notation; expose
   counterexamples and gaps between a theorem and package defaults.
4. Write a provisional primary-method/challenger/extension decision and
   rejection reasons. Update it in response to experiments.

Prioritize dependence and nuisance learning, persistence/cointegration,
controlled latent identification and estimation, graph/effect uncertainty,
and observation/linkage mechanisms. Retain the larger synthesis connecting
graphs, IV/control functions, inverse/proxy problems, semiparametric ML,
g-methods, panels, state-space models and decision learning.

Every new scholarly citation requires a genuine PDF named
`docs/papers/Title FirstAuthorSurname(year).pdf`, source identity/version/date,
URL, SHA-256, inspected anchors and supported claims. Software and official
sources require preserved local copies and the same trace. Unavailable
originals remain omissions. Metadata discovers sources; it does not support
technical claims. S01 exposes obligations and cannot declare the literature
complete before S02–S05 arguments survive scrutiny.

### S02: dependent time series is the first technical priority

Derive A's normalized response moments, estimator and stacked cross-horizon
score. State sufficient dependence, moment, rank and remainder conditions
for each selected regime. Specify actual bandwidth, block/gap, horizon and
nuisance-rate choices from applicable results. An "appropriate CLT" or
"suitably slow bandwidth" is not an exit result. Construct simultaneous
bands or test inversion; treat weak normalization separately.

Develop stationary, near-unit-root, integrated/cointegrated and break cases
substantively. Derive how differencing, error correction or lag augmentation
changes the target and inference. Choose supported cases and explain the
others; a unit-root pretest alone does not certify a subsequent interval.

Develop one scalable causal route with learned/reduced controls. Compare
sparsity and factor restrictions, information removed, approximation error
and cost. Specify blocked training, lag/feature selection and the needed
dependence argument. For panels, distinguish information from entities,
dates and clusters, including common shocks.

Hold shock, normalization, observation frequency and horizons fixed across
comparisons. Compare LP and VAR only where their identification restrictions
support the common target. Deliver connected derivations, algorithms,
source anchors and an adverse experiment before large performance runs.

### S03: longitudinal and POMDP estimation on one decision clock

Retain the existing fixed-horizon gradient/remainder and specify the actual
backward fitting, training/tuning, eligibility/censoring, policy covariance
and inference procedure. Compare augmentation, targeting, g-computation and
weighting on the same target. Explain the supported common-shock extension
without transferring an independent-trajectory theorem by notation.

Start the latent route with an explicitly identified controlled finite-state
model and hidden-path likelihood reference. Known or externally calibrated
emissions provide a constructive baseline with unknown transition/reward
parameters. Investigate an unknown-emission route with a source-specific
controlled identification argument and logging design. An uncontrolled HMM
theorem cannot certify it. If that route fails, state the additional
information needed and retain conditional research status while completing
the identified POMDP estimation baseline.

Specify initial, transition, signal, reward and logger laws; parameter
constraints and label equivalence; scaled forward/backward recursion;
derivatives or another justified optimizer; starts and stopping rules.
Choose a concrete parameter-to-value uncertainty construction and develop
an error decomposition for the actual belief process. A fully observed
Markov bound needs a proved transfer before use for a POMDP.

Compare the chosen numerical route with a meaningful alternative. Explain
the different approximation targets of Gaussian, particle and variational
methods and specify the BayesFilter interface. Implement selected routes,
while giving substantive treatment to the alternatives.

Begin with fixed policies. For a learned policy, freeze its actual
history-to-action mapping after training and use a justified separate
evaluation construction. State which fitting/selection steps a bootstrap
repeats and what conditional coverage it claims. Parameter uncertainty is
not automatically uncertainty for a selected optimal policy.

### S04: discovery uncertainty must reach the decision

Use small finite graph classes with known reference effects, then choose the
supported temporal class and observation/dependence conditions. Specify CI
tests or score objectives, conditioning sets, lag limits, background
knowledge and stopping behavior. Functional and latent-variable alternatives
must retain their distinct restrictions and graph objects.

Construct graph-set/effect-set inference in a bounded regime from applicable
primary results. Independent discovery and effect samples may help, but
splitting does not itself give graph confidence coverage. Account for graph
and effect error, multiplicity and any uniformity conditions. When these
cannot be justified, return exploratory graphs and effect sensitivity
without a confidence interpretation.

Identify a measurement, instrument or experiment that could distinguish
decision-relevant alternatives. Evaluate the effect of screening and lag
truncation on the final query; edge-recovery accuracy alone is insufficient.

### S05: an explicit automatic organization mechanism

Define a grammar of candidate units/keys, event and availability clocks,
revision rules, aggregation and linkage hypotheses. Use metadata, temporal
order, accounting identities and observed violations to eliminate
incompatible candidates. Rank the remainder by a specified auditable rule;
an arbitrary score is not a probability of semantic correctness.

Abstain when materially different interpretations cannot be distinguished.
Retain alternatives and display changes in eligible populations, histories,
sampling units and estimands. Analysts supply economic assumptions that
records cannot identify. Statistical linkage probabilities need a model
and calibration, with downstream propagation consistent with that meaning.

Evaluate on synthetic raw records with known observation construction.
Report accepted-mapping errors, useful coverage and abstention, followed by
downstream effect/value error against the oracle view. Include cases with
no unique automatic answer. Inspect dataml read-only for interface mapping.

### S06: mathematical interfaces and selected software

Specify syntax/semantics for model, observation, intervention, population,
functional and sampling objects. Define kernel operations, variable/measure/
support types, graph endpoints and allowed transformations. Identification
traces carry assumptions; hashes bind bytes but do not validate semantics.

Extend result types to scalar/vector effects, horizon-indexed curves with
joint uncertainty, identified sets, graph/equivalence objects, policy values
and numerical error. Distinguish confidence coverage, posterior probability
and sensitivity. Specify a bounded mapping from supported requests to
estimators, inference and backend settings, with explicit unsupported cases.

Select a small backend set after the scientific comparisons. Pin versions
and APIs, inspect behavior/licenses, and run small cases against independent
references. Expose required scores, folds, weights, masks and graph marks.
Qualify operations actually tested, not whole packages. Earlier feasibility
probes may disprove a proposed route; final compatibility is assessed here.

Map exact FinMarketing, dataml, BayesFilter and MacroFinance interfaces by
read-only inspection. Build local examples/adapters in this workspace,
including changed clock, currency, endpoint and weight-role cases. Specify
the standalone study workflow using the same scientific contracts.

### S07: discriminating experiments and complete cost measurement

Reuse exact checks and banking studies where they test the chosen
construction. Each new experiment declares the generating law, causal truth,
data available to fitting, tuning, comparisons, seeds and failure accounting
before final runs. Add tests only for a scientific claim or decision needing
new evidence.

Assess bias, variability, interval/set coverage and width, graph/effect-set
behavior, value error and decision regret where appropriate. Retain
misspecification, poor support, rejections and optimizer caps. Report
unconditional failure rates rather than summarizing only successful fits.
Check financial units and accounting separately.

Set repetitions by Monte Carlo precision: approximately 500 independent
replications at coverage 0.95 give a standard error near 0.01. Expensive
cases may use fewer with their uncertainty reported. Pilots determine the
resource plan. Preset claim-specific tolerances before final runs; favorable
simulation coverage is not a general theorem.

Measure reconstruction, fitting, multiple starts, discovery, uncertainty,
sensitivity and reporting together. Vary entities, dates, variables/lags,
states and resampling counts, with hardware limits established by pilots.
Record wall time, memory, I/O, retries and error against the qualified
reference. Demonstrate at least one complete scalable time-series route;
fast score reduction alone cannot close this phase. Explain any changed
scientific assumptions or approximation errors due to acceleration.

Revise the investment comparison using equivalent-workflow costs. Keep
unmeasured salaries, adoption and bank benefits explicit. An unfavorable
result changes the method or product proposal.

### S08: integration and substantive review

Write scholarly prose throughout the technical phases. Final integration
checks notation and the connections among diagrams, IV/proxies, learning,
longitudinal processes, state-space models, panels and discovery. Method
choices should follow from those arguments. Process receipts stay outside
the product narrative.

In a separate author-review pass, reconstruct the central estimation and
uncertainty arguments from stated assumptions and primary texts. Try
observationally equivalent models, unsupported histories, weak signals,
singular parameters and adverse transformations. Call this independent
review only if an independent reviewer actually participates.

Compare substantive passages with the three reference monographs again.
Ask whether a competent reader can recover the construction and understand
the alternatives. A section heading or passing script cannot establish it.

After repairs, revise sponsor/catalogue claims, rerun affected scientific
checks, close source/claim traceability, compile and inspect the rendered
PDF, then create the new delivery. The freeze guard must require S00–S08
and associated reopened R obligations. Author completion remains distinct
from user acceptance and bank validation.

## Acceptance rules against premature closure

For each required construction, the review must answer:

1. Is the target defined in mathematical, economic and information units?
2. Do identification assumptions apply to the actual model and fitting data?
3. Are estimation, training/tuning and numerical computation reconstructable?
4. Is uncertainty justified for the actual sampling and selection process,
   including necessary rates, conditioning and error control?
5. Does a substantive alternative change assumptions, cost or performance,
   and is the chosen hierarchy justified?
6. Does an adverse case establish a limit with a truthful product response?
7. Are sources, project derivations, experiments and implementation evidence
   distinguished and connected to the precise claim?

A missing answer necessary for advertised support keeps the phase open.
Name the missing mathematical step or evidence; "more validation needed"
is insufficient. Nonidentification can complete a scholarly argument but
cannot advertise an identified estimator. Finite checks and simulations do
not prove general theorems.

## Execution controls and first checkpoint

Existing authorization covers local research, planning, manuscript edits,
read-only sibling inspection and the registered public-source collector.
Use its exact allowed form with `login: false`; do not broaden permissions
or relax managed controls. This plan needs no additional approval.

Before package probes, inspect available environments and prepare exact
pinned dependencies and an isolated local environment. If installation or
another action needs additional permission, request the smallest supported
capability with the concrete batch of actions. A public-source download
does not authorize arbitrary downloaded-code execution. No external
messages, sibling edits or deployment are part of this program.

Mission Control records evidence, failures, repairs and exact continuation.
Checkpoint reports state which mathematical question was resolved, which
method choice changed, what remains unproved and what is ready to read.
Progress is not a percentage of pages or citations.

The first substantive checkpoint is S01 plus S02's structural-response
construction: study specifications, source-to-argument map, selected
comparisons, and one complete dependent inference route with an adverse
example. Continue under existing authorization; this is a reviewable
deliverable, not a routine permission stop.

Calendar/runtime estimates follow scope and numerical pilots. The plan
sets dependency and acceptance order without inventing a duration for
unresolved derivations. The original plan delivery covered planning and
status correction only; subsequent execution and closure have separate
dated phase receipts.
