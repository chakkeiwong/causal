# C++ network experiments need assignment and state contracts

The RGCR research implementation addresses a specific network-experiment design, while NetworKit, SNAP, Repast HPC and the C-native igraph library supply different graph and simulation components. Their proposed uses are conditional on explicit graph identity, exposure, assignment, random-state and failure contracts. The comparison binds 64 software originals and a genuine 2020 author manuscript; it does not claim complete ecosystem coverage or native qualification. [@exec25_cnet_rgcr; @exec25_cnet_networkit; @exec25_cnet_snap; @exec25_cnet_repast; @exec25_cnet_igraph; @exec25_cnet_paper]

Exact public examples show why these contracts matter. Positive node-level exposure probabilities do not guarantee a nonempty realized exposure arm: a source-style zero extension of a ratio estimator gives mean one-half for an authored constant effect of one. A selected weighted-graph restoration path can change normalized exposure from nine-tenths to one-half by omitting weights. Copied random streams and distributed replicas also need scientific accounting. These are bounded mathematical and source findings, not observed installed-platform failures. [@exec25_cnet_choice]

The proposed investment starts with finite assignment enumeration, graph round-trip checks, empty-arm refusal, deterministic draw allocation and distributed-state fault tests. RGCR rights and its older SNAP dependency require separate resolution; graph-library licenses and complete installed dependencies must be reviewed for the intended distribution. Public known-law evidence can justify a later bank experiment without claiming authentic bank validation. [@exec25_cnet_choice]

A hypothetical USD30,000 evaluation costs USD30 per 1,000 attempts, USD50 per 600 completions or USD75 per 400 accepted outputs. All denominators belong in the comparison. Unknown bank graph truth and unrestricted dependent-data inference remain information or research limits; the 52 bounded discovery leads do not close them. [@exec25_cnet_choice]
