Chapter 53

Synthetic controls and the choice of
a comparison

When one region or institution changes policy, a weighted combination of unaffected units
may offer a comparison. Its usefulness depends on donor information, pre-intervention fit
and the assumptions linking that fit to the untreated future. The methods differ in how
they extrapolate and how they represent uncertainty; those differences matter for the bank’s
decision.
   The chapter compares three ways to obtain the missing trajectory: constrained donor
matching, augmentation beyond the donor hull, and model-based reconstruction. It first
distinguishes the commercial host from the estimator it runs and examines how panel wrappers
define the target. Detailed R method studies then precede the wider R, Python and cross-
language comparisons. The question throughout is whether the same donor information,
intervention clock and uncertainty reach the bank’s decision after fitting and conversion.


53.1     A commercial host does not settle synthetic-control se-
         mantics or rights
A bank comparing a treated regional portfolio with other regions needs a credible untreated
path, not merely an optimizer that returns donor weights. A commercial statistical host
may run a community estimator, a vendor-native command or a general optimization routine.
These are different implementation, support and rights arrangements. The proposed common
service should compare those arrangements explicitly while preserving donor eligibility, timing,
missingness, pre-fit and the uncertainty protocol.
   The selected Synth-for-Stata repository is an author-maintained community implementation
inside the commercial Stata host. Its inspected command identifies version 0.0.8 and declares
Stata version 13.0. The selected commit, ae99fd1ed100e8f5ea38386f18f02e61a90956e6, is
dated 30 April 2026. Twelve authenticated texts cover its command, help, loss helper, package
manifest, optimizer interface, component notices and selected test artifacts. No Stata exe-
cutable, plugin binary or compiled Mata library was run here. A host compatibility declaration
is not a verified installed edition, entitlement or vendor-supported causal capability. (Synth
contributors, 2026)

53.1.1    Freeze the donor universe and information used to select weights

The selected help describes a convex weighted donor combination. Donors default to all panel
units other than the treated unit; an explicit donor list must contain at least two units. That
syntactic rule cannot establish that another region is unaffected by the intervention, has
comparable measurement or supplies a credible untreated trajectory. Predictor averages ignore
missing entries, while a wholly missing predictor is rejected. Consequently, two units can
contribute averages over different observed dates. Predictor-specific windows can also override
the common predictor period. (Synth contributors, 2026)
  Consider an authored regional-portfolio example, with outcomes measured in thousands of
USD. Two donors and the treated region have trajectories

               D1 = (90, 110, 120),    D2 = (110, 130, 140),    T = (100, 120, 150).     (53.1)

The first two dates precede intervention. Equal donor weights reproduce both preperiod
treated values exactly and predict 130 at the third date. Stipulate that this donor average is
the treated region’s true untreated path. The corresponding treatment effect is then 20, or
USD 20,000. This truth is an assumption of the example, not something established by pre-fit
alone. (Causal banking proposal project, 2026ak)
   Now suppose the second donor also receives a spillover of 20 at the final date. Its observed
final value becomes 160. The same weights predict 140 and the apparent treated gap falls to
10. Alternatively, selecting weights using the treated postintervention outcome can choose 1/4
for the first donor and 3/4 for the contaminated second donor. The selected synthetic path is
then
                              Te = (105, 125, 150),    T3 − Te3 = 0.                      (53.2)
The intervention has not disappeared from the stipulated world; contamination and postout-
come selection have changed the comparison. A lower postperiod gap cannot certify a better
causal counterfactual. (Causal banking proposal project, 2026ak)
  The source makes this timing check operationally relevant. Default predictor and MSPE
periods precede treatment. For an explicitly supplied MSPE period, however, the inspected
command checks that the requested dates occur in the panel, without imposing a separate
before-treatment inequality in that branch. The help allows later predictor dates only under a
substantive unaffectedness condition. The proposed adapter must therefore declare and check
the scientific window; passing a command’s membership validation is a narrower result. (Synth
contributors, 2026)

where xit contains covariates with a justified intervention-time meaning, ft represents common

Covariates caused by the intervention, anticipation, spillovers to donors, or changing loadings

those substantive conditions.

factors in two pre-periods and one post-period be

                           f1 = (1, 0)T ,   f2 = (2, 0)T ,   f3 = (1, 1)T .                     (53.6)

Donor loadings (0, 1) and (1, 1) generate observed paths (0, 0, 1) and (1, 2, 2). A treated

53.2.3    Defaults, missingness and uncertainty must travel together

In the selected wrapper, the default estimator is gsynth. Legacy EM=TRUE selects ife only if
the estimator argument was omitted. The default requested inference is parametric for the
gsynth route and bootstrap for the IFE or matrix-completion routes. A parametric request
for the latter routes is changed to bootstrap with a warning; the legacy name nonparametric
maps to bootstrap. The wrapper also sets unit fixed effects, five CV folds and tolerance
10−3 , whereas the direct engine’s inspected defaults include two-way effects, twenty folds and
tolerance 10−5 . Direct-engine adoption therefore requires explicit configuration rather than
assuming identical defaults. (Yiqing Xu and package contributors, 2026b,a)
   The engine’s gsynth route forces a never-treated donor pool and rejects treatment reversals.
Too few pre-treatment observations or excessive missingness can remove units; the returned
result must disclose this change in population. A package family’s support for switching
treatments does not override the restrictions of this selected method. Missing-cell masks also
enter fitting and effect aggregation, so reporting only the number of input rows conceals the
denominator actually used. (Yiqing Xu and package contributors, 2026a)
   For parametric uncertainty, the selected engine requires the isolated never-treated pool. Its
automatic error mode uses an autoregressive strategy when cells are missing and an empirical
strategy otherwise; explicitly requested empirical or wild modes require a complete panel.
In inspected case-bootstrap code, units or cluster identifiers are resampled, with support-
conditioned retries in unit-resampling branches. Ordinary jackknife with a cluster argument
emits a warning that clustering is ignored. These are different sampling mechanisms; a
generic confidence-interval label cannot make them interchangeable. (Yiqing Xu and package
contributors, 2026a)
   The inspected average-effect uncertainty uses the standard deviation of bootstrap draws
with missing values removed and, by default, a normal interval. The basic-pivot option
has a different construction. Any future qualification must preserve attempted, valid and
failed draws, resampling units, selection/refitting behavior and reasons for unavailable output.
Dropping failed draws from a standard-deviation calculation is not evidence that inference
remains valid. Dependence across branches, common calendar shocks, model misspecification
and post-selection uncertainty each require a declared law and a specific argument. This
source comparison has executed neither native package and makes no empirical coverage claim.
(Yiqing Xu and package contributors, 2026a)

53.5.1    The dispatcher determines the scientific comparison

More than one finite treatment time selects multisynth, where a supplied progfunc is ignored
with a warning. One treatment time selects the single-outcome or multiple-outcome route
according to the formula. Several treated units at one date are therefore different from several
treatment dates. The inspected single-outcome prediction averages treated outcomes; it is not
automatically an exposure-weighted portfolio effect. The banking request must distinguish
an equally weighted branch, account or region from an exposure-weighted aggregate. (Eli
Ben-Michael, 2026)
   The single-outcome defaults are ridge augmentation, SCM weights and no unit fixed
effect. Preparation splits observations at the intervention date and uses each unit’s maximum
treatment flag. This persistent-treatment representation is not a general treatment-reversal
contract. A proposed adapter must validate unit-time uniqueness, pre/post unit alignment,
missingness, calendar meaning and persistence. A rectangular matrix does not establish
those causal conditions. Covariates are aggregated before treatment, but their measurement
availability still needs an information clock. Full staggered and multiple-outcome engines
remain separate review targets. (Eli Ben-Michael, 2026; Causal banking proposal project,
2026cu)


53.5.2    Negative weights represent extrapolation

For the selected single-treatment ridge route without auxiliary covariates, let X be the n0 × p
donor matrix after centering each feature on its donor mean, and x the centered treated vector.
With simplex weights wsc and penalty λ > 0, the inspected update is

                          waug = wsc + X(X ′ X + λIp )−1 (x − X ′ wsc ).

Since 1′ X = 0, the adjustment sums to zero. Individual weights need not be nonnegative.
This argument concerns the specified centered route; covariates, fixed effects and supplied
feature transformations must have their own matched contracts. (Eli Ben-Michael, 2026; Causal
banking proposal project, 2026cu)
   In an authored example, centered donors are (−1, 1), the treated value is two, wsc = (0, 1)
and λ = 2. The initial imbalance is one and X ′ X + λ = 4. The adjustment (−1/4, 1/4) gives
weights (−1/4, 5/4), remaining imbalance 1/2 and absolute weight sum 3/2. Donor revenues
of 80 and 120 thousand USD produce a counterfactual 130; treated revenue 150 gives a gap 20.
The simplex gap would be 30. Neither gap is identified by arithmetic alone: donor eligibility,
stable extrapolation and absence of relevant interference need justification. Truncating the
negative weight and renormalizing changes the estimator; it is not a formatting adjustment.
(Causal banking proposal project, 2026cu)
   The constrained solver introduces an auxiliary outcome path tied to donor weights by linear
equalities. With a symmetric positive-semidefinite metric, its objective agrees with donor

54.3      Synthetic controls and the source of uncertainty
When a policy changes for one region, branch group or business line, a donor combination can
form a counterfactual trajectory. The donor definition, pre-treatment period, scaling, fitting
criterion and post-treatment horizon are part of the scientific design. The weight optimizer is
one component of that design. A small fitting loss does not by itself explain why post-treatment
prediction error should have a particular distribution.
   The inspected Synth interface takes preprocessed predictor and outcome matrices and
reports donor/predictor weights and optimization losses. This makes it a candidate for a
controlled weight-construction operation. The selected passages do not supply a universal
placebo or confidence-coverage guarantee. The product should report fitting loss and donor
dependence as diagnostics and attach only an uncertainty procedure whose assumptions and
target have been separately stated. (Synth contributors, 2026)
   augsynth’s selected dispatcher distinguishes single-outcome, multiple-outcome and staggered
settings, with distinct balance and regularization controls. Selecting the family name is
therefore insufficient to select a method. Section 53.5 develops the selected ridge, tuning and
uncertainty paths. Its findings do not qualify every staggered or multiple-outcome request, and
native admission still requires matched tests. The author-repository snapshot was available
after a CRAN mirror lookup failed; that establishes source access for the inspected revision,
not a CRAN release history. (augsynth contributors, 2026)
   scpi’s selected documentation explicitly distinguishes in-sample and out-of-sample uncer-
tainty and exposes donor constraint sets, solvers and simulation choices. A generic scalar
“standard error” field would compress these components into a misleadingly uniform object.
The proposed integration retains the constraint set, solver outcome, simulation identity and
the assumptions used for prediction error. It also records failures: an infeasible or numerically
failed solve does not become a zero effect, and changing the constraint set to obtain a solution
changes the reported procedure. (scpi contributors, 2026)
   For banking examples, donor contamination is a substantive concern to assess: a nominally
untreated branch may be affected by shared pricing, national communications or customer
movement. This is a proposed design diagnostic, not an observed defect in a package or dataset.

of spillovers solely from a donor weight vector.
  A Bayesian structural time-series analysis offers a different account of counterfactual
prediction and its uncertainty. The next study explains what its posterior draws represent and
why a precise predictive difference can still fail to identify the effect of the banking action.

54.4.1    The target and the identifying restriction

Let the intervention begin after date T0 , let H be the declared post-intervention horizon, and
write Yt (0) for the outcome that the treated branch group would have produced without the
campaign. The realized path contrast is ∆t = Yt − Yt (0). A posterior for this missing path uses
the observed pre-intervention response and the control history X1:T0 +H . In the state-space
formulation of Brodersen and colleagues,

                                     Yt (0) = ZtT αt + εt ,                                 (54.1)
                                      αt+1 = Tt αt + Rt ηt .                                (54.2)

Here αt is a latent state, Zt maps it to the scalar response, Tt evolves that state, and Rt
maps state innovations into it. The observation and state innovations have the specified
Gaussian laws; trend, seasonality and regression components determine the matrices and their
parameters. Posterior simulation integrates over the stated parameter, state and variable-
selection uncertainty. The inherited method supplies a joint predictive law for the entire
missing response path, not merely separate forecasts at each date. (Brodersen et al., 2015)
   For causal attribution, the control series must remain unaffected by the intervention, and the
pre-period relationship must continue to describe the untreated post-period response. Neither
an accurate pre-period fit nor a posterior excluding zero proves these restrictions. National
advertising, shared customer acquisition channels, a branch closure or a simultaneous pricing
change can make the proposed donor history inappropriate. A post-period control may be
available for retrospective analysis while being unavailable when a campaign is first planned.
The proposal must distinguish these two information sets before using retrospective fit to
justify a prospective decision. (Google CausalImpact maintainers, 2026a; Brodersen et al.,
2015)
  An exact construction shows the consequence. Let At = 0 for eight pre-period days and
At = 1 for four campaign days. Set x0t = 10 + t and suppose the observed daily application
count and control index satisfy

                         Xt = x0t + 15At ,     Yt = 100 + 2x0t + 20At .

In this first law, the campaign increases applications by 20 per day and also changes the
control index by 15. The pre-period relationship is exactly Yt = 100 + 2Xt . Continuing that
relationship into the post-period gives

                            Yt − (100 + 2Xt ) = 20 − 2(15) = −10.

The same twelve observed rows arise under a second law in which the exogenous control
path is the observed Xt , the campaign leaves it unchanged, and the response equation is
Yt = 100 + 2Xt − 10At . Under that law the effect is genuinely negative. The exogenous control
path is allowed to shift on the campaign date; no random timing assumption excludes that

possibility in this construction. Thus the identical observations, including perfect pre-period
fit, support opposite effects under different control-invariance assumptions. This is a project

   More generally, with pre-period slope β, donor contamination γAt and response effect τ At ,
the continued linear contrast is τ − βγ. This decomposition makes a sensitivity analysis
interpretable: an owner can state a plausible range for donor spillover and inspect when the
sign changes. It does not estimate that range from the same ambiguous history. Similarly,
an unmodeled untreated post-period shift δ would enter the contrast as τ + δ − βγ. A richer
state model can represent some changes, but cannot learn from missing untreated outcomes
which part of a coincident break was caused by the campaign.

The existing TFP implementation is a separate candidate that should be compared before
undertaking such a port. Its backend source, license closure and native comparison remain
deferred. Shared import names are insufficient package identity. (Causal banking research
proposal project, 2026a; TFP CausalImpact Authors, 2026)
   Alternatives should be selected by the question. A simple seasonal forecast is a transparent
predictive baseline; a donor-weight procedure emphasizes an explicitly restricted comparison
group; a panel design is preferable when its assignment and population assumptions match the
decision; and a randomized rollout can address causal ambiguity that predictive refinement
leaves intact. The earlier survey describes the respective packages. A comparison should align
the branch group, outcome units, horizon, available information and uncertainty meaning before
ranking losses. A package that answers a different estimand is not a losing implementation of
the same request.
   The research owner must justify the donor and stability restrictions and declare the target.
The adapter owner must supply the compatible environment, dependency notices, calendar
and conversion checks, resource handling and complete failure outputs. A reviewer must
assess native-versus-adapter behavior and the prospectively specified study before any fitted
performance claim is made. The present decision grants no integration capability. Reconsider
it when a compatible pinned environment and matched native oracle exist, or when a changed
scientific question, dependency revision or target platform changes the appropriate baseline.
Unavailable native execution is local to this candidate and does not prevent work on other
proposal branches. (Causal banking research proposal project, 2026a)


54.5     Fitting weights and target weights
fect’s selected documentation distinguishes weights used in counterfactual fitting from weights
used to aggregate treatment effects. The latter do not automatically implement inverse-
probability or doubly robust confounding correction. A fitting weight changes the optimization
or fitting population; an aggregation weight changes the reported average. The two can be
numerically similar vectors and still represent different operations. (fect contributors, 2026)
