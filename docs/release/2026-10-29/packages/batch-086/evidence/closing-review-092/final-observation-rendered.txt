21.1      What the bank records determines what can be learned
A causal estimator sees records, while the intervention acts on customers, contracts and markets.
Let X ∗ denote the scientific variables, R their observation indicators, L an entity-linkage
assignment, and O the stored records. An observation kernel K maps the full law into the
recorded law:                     Z
                          pO (o) = K(o | x∗ , r, l) p(x∗ , r, l) d(x∗ , r, l).            (21.1)

The target θ may depend on interventions in the law of X ∗ , not merely on the distribution of
O. Identification therefore asks whether all admissible full models with the same pO give the
same θ. A cleaning transformation T (O) cannot distinguish full models that already agree
on O: their pushforward laws under T also agree. This elementary argument explains why a
more complete-looking table cannot by itself resolve missingness, measurement error or causal
ambiguity.
   Useful automation instead makes the observation assumptions explicit, checks their ob-
servable implications and preserves information for an appropriate estimator. The aim is to
automate reproducible construction of an analysis view while retaining a scientific record of
what that view means. Data lineage proves how a variable was computed; it does not prove
that the variable blocks a confounding path.


21.2      Event, availability and revision clocks
For a decision at time t, let Ft−bank be the information actually available immediately before

assignment. A deployable policy gt and its inputs must be measurable with respect to that
information. This is different from having an event timestamp earlier than t. A January income
field entered in March may describe January but was unavailable to a February decision. A
revised arrears balance may replace an older value in today’s warehouse while the historical
policy acted on the older version.
   Represent a record by its entity, event time, availability time, validity interval, revision
identity, value, unit and source. An as-of view at t first restricts records to availability before
t, then chooses the applicable version for the scientific event interval. For example, a deposit
balance dated 31 January, posted on 2 February and corrected on 10 February supplies no
input to a 1 February offer; it supplies its initial value on 5 February and its corrected value

on 12 February. Whether the outcome should use the eventually reconciled balance or the
historical published balance is a separate estimand choice.
   There are also two scientifically different uses of retrospectively measured variables. An
eventual audit can validly measure a truly predecision confounder for an explanatory analysis
under its measurement model, even if that measurement was unavailable to the historical
policy. It cannot then be used as a prospective policy feature. Conversely, a postdecision
credit score that incorporates the offered credit limit is a descendant of treatment; its event

causal time and available information.
   For longitudinal evaluation, rolling outcomes extend beyond their origin. If Yt,h aggregates
cash flows through t + h, training rows with earlier origins can still use outcomes that occur
after an evaluation decision. A prospective split needs outcome availability as well as feature
availability. An offline estimator of a stationary law may use a different split justified by
dependence assumptions, but it answers a different validation question.

21.3.1      A missingness law that no imputer can learn from these records

Suppose P (R = 1) = 1/2 and observed binary outcomes are evenly split. One full model has
P (Y = 1 | R = 0) = 0 and mean 1/4; another has P (Y = 1 | R = 0) = 1 and mean 3/4. They
have exactly the same recorded law (R, RY ). The general bounded-outcome identified interval
is

      P (R = 1)E[Y | R = 1] ≤ E[Y ] ≤ P (R = 1)E[Y | R = 1] + P (R = 0),         0 ≤ Y ≤ 1.

Every value between the endpoints can be attained by a suitable unobserved outcome dis-
tribution. Filling the gaps with the observed mean selects 1/2 through an assumption;

prediction validation on observed cases cannot distinguish these models. A sensitivity parame-
ter δ(x) = E[Y | X = x, R = 0] − E[Y | X = x, R = 1] instead yields the explicit correction
E[(1 − π(X))δ(X)] to the MAR mean. Its admissible range can be connected to bounded
outcomes or external follow-up data.
   Missingness also changes graph discovery. Test-wise complete cases for different triples
need not come from one common observational law; conditioning on observation can open
collider paths. Tu et al. (2019, Section 3, Assumptions 1–4) studies this problem under
causal sufficiency, faithfulness/observability restrictions and specific constraints on missingness
indicators, including no self-masking. Its correction procedure is not a general MNAR solution.
If customers with the greatest unrecorded distress avoid reporting their distress, the no-self-
masking premise is precisely the questionable restriction.

   For an exact example, let true receipt be randomized with probability 1/2, and let P (Y =

rounding or arbitrary informative missingness. The scientific contribution is a joint restriction
on cleaning, target estimation and error rates. Applying PCA to any bank table and then
using an ordinary standard error is not that procedure.


21.5     Entity uncertainty and the unit of intervention
A household may own several customer identifiers, an SME may merge, and a branch portfolio
may transfer to another legal entity. The unit for assignment can therefore differ from the
unit for reporting and the unit over which interference occurs. A household-level promotion
analyzed as independent accounts can both misstate treatment versions and exaggerate sample
information. A stable identifier is not evidence of a stable economic unit across a merger.
180                                                            Causal analysis for a universal bank


   Let L denote a candidate linkage and θL the effect estimate or parameter under it. If a
justified joint probabilistic model supplies linkage weights, its posterior uncertainty satisfies

                   Var(θ | O) = E[Var(θ | O, L) | O] + Var(E[θ | O, L] | O).                (21.4)

The second term disappears when a most-likely linkage is treated as certain. This is the law of
total variance, not a guarantee that an arbitrary matcher’s confidence scores are calibrated
posterior weights. For example, two equally weighted linkage models with effect means 0 and 2
and conditional variance 1/4 give total variance 5/4. Reporting 1/4 misses the between-linkage
component.
   An alternative is sensitivity across a defensible set of linkages, returning a union of their
identified/estimated effect sets. This does not require invented posterior probabilities but
can be wide. Averaging linked rows first generally differs from averaging the resulting causal
functional because propensity scores, conditional laws and target weights are nonlinear. The
entity service should preserve uncertain links, historical validity and their effect on the target
population.


21.6      Which transformations preserve the scientific question?
A one-to-one covariate transformation preserves its sigma-field: σ(f (X)) = σ(X) when the
inverse is measurable. Conditional exchangeability and conditional expectations can then be
translated without losing information. A coarsening generally cannot. A special exception
is a balancing score under the assumptions derived in Section 8.6; arbitrary low-dimensional
embeddings do not enjoy that property.
   Outcome transformations also change the target. An effect on E[log Y ] is not the log of an
effect on E[Y ]. Portfolio sums, account averages and balance-weighted averages assign different
units and weights. In finance, a useful reward must reconcile interest margin, fees, credit losses,
funding cost, servicing cost and capital charges over the same intervention horizon. Internal
transfers can be a gain to one branch and no gain to the banking group. Accounting identities
should be retained as constraints, not supplied as independent variables to a faithfulness-based
discovery procedure.
   For example, if ending deposits equal starting deposits plus net flows, then the three variables
are deterministically related. Their covariance is singular and their independences need not
behave like a generic noisy DAG model. Choose primitive quantities or an explicit constrained
measurement model. Likewise, mechanically constructing an interest income variable as balance
times rate does not establish either economic causal direction from a discovered association.
   At large scale, an immutable analysis recipe can specify event/availability filters, revision
policy, entity map, action history, eligibility, outcome windows, units and observation indicators.
A generated query should be checked for future information, join multiplicity, unsupported
follow-up, unexpected missingness and reconciliation to source totals. Partitioning by enti-
ty/date and streaming score reductions can preserve this recipe; downsampling, aggressive
screening and global imputation can change the scientific information. Their consequences
must be tested separately from query execution time.


21.7      A finite candidate search with explicit abstention
An initial automatic organization operation can be specified without claiming to infer arbitrary
economic meaning. Let a candidate observation plan be c = (k, e, a, r, g, u): record key k,

event and availability clocks e, a, revision relation r, aggregation g, and entity map/unit u.
The input includes a finite grammar C, raw versioned records, decision identifiers and times,
declared units, and constraints with their provenance. The grammar is itself part of the
scientific input. A method cannot recover an interpretation that the grammar excludes.
   The implemented reference grammar has two timestamp assignments, two record-key
hypotheses (source/record or source/entity/record), latest-version versus append semantics,
stock versus flow aggregation, and account versus a supplied candidate household map.
This gives 32 initial candidates. The engine tests key uniqueness, finite values, currency
compatibility, declared column roles, and any supplied chronology, revision-publication or
accounting constraints. For example, e ≤ a can distinguish two timestamp assignments only
if the analyst has declared that this feed records realized events before their publication.
Scheduled future events do not obey that restriction. An external closing-minus-opening
ledger identity can distinguish flow summation from a last stock only if that identity and its
independent measurement are actually supplied.
   For each surviving candidate and each requested decision time t, first keep versions with
a < t. Within its record identity, select the largest eligible version; then restrict its economic
date to the requested half-open interval [b, t). A correction moving an event outside the interval
must not resurrect the older version. Distinct decision identifiers and distinct events remain
distinct even when their amounts match. A flow is the sum of distinct eligible increments. A
stock is its latest eligible economic observation; component accounts at that instant can be
summed only under the supplied additive household interpretation. Missing support returns
a missing value, not a fabricated zero. The output retains the source/entity/record/version
identifiers of every selected term.

Hints order inspection; they cannot eliminate a surviving material alternative. Partition the
survivors by equality of requested values, lineage, aggregation meaning and economic unit.
Return incompatible if none survive, accept the current requested view if one class remains, and

   This construction has a simple conditional correctness statement. If the true plan belongs to
the grammar and every hard constraint is valid for it, that plan survives. If all surviving plans
agree on the required view, the accepted view equals its oracle counterpart. No probabilistic
calibration is needed for this implication. Incorrect metadata or an incomplete grammar
invalidates its premise. A large hint score cannot estimate the probability that those premises
are true. Under ambiguity, fit the scientific functional separately in each view and retain the
resulting sensitivity set; do not average the tables or treat hint scores as linkage probabilities.


21.7.1     An evaluation that reaches the effect estimate

The synthetic experiment fixes the oracle before constructing candidates. For 240 customers
draw independent standard-normal X, W , assign an offer with probability expit(.7X), and
generate Y = 20A + 30X + ϵ, ϵ ∼ N (0, 25). The predecision record contains X; a later revision
contains X + 2A and is unavailable at the decision. OLS on (1, A, X) estimates the constant
effect of 20 in this specified law. Using the eventual revision instead gives the population

equation Y = −40A + 30(X + 2A) + ϵ: the nominal cleaning choice changes the adjustment
variable into a treatment descendant.
   Across 300 repetitions, labeled revisions and the valid chronology case accept the oracle
view in every run, with effect RMSE about 0.68–0.69. Retrospective revision adjustment has
RMSE about 60. In the unlabeled aggregation case an earlier independent record W leaves
last-stock X and flow-sum X + W as materially different possibilities. In the linkage case
pairing two accounts changes the available confounder to their sum. Both cases abstain in
every run and retain the oracle. Forcing the top hint-ranked candidate gives RMSE 10.19 and
10.59, while the mean spread between alternative estimates is 9.77 and 10.13. Those spreads
are observation sensitivity, not confidence intervals.
   An accounting case has increments W and X −W , with an independently declared reconciled
total X. The constraint selects the flow plan in every run, with RMSE 0.70. The additional
total provides the distinguishing information; the algorithm did not discover it from column
names. Zero wrong acceptances among these fixtures is conditional evidence for this grammar
and generator. It does not establish population error rates for previously unseen bank feeds.
With zero errors in 300 trials, the two-sided 95% Wilson upper bound is about 1.27% for the
particular repeated experiment, not zero.
   The bounded search costs O(|C|RD) in a direct implementation with R records and D
decisions. Indexing by entity and availability time, caching common candidate projections
and expressing eligible-version selection in a columnar engine reduce repeated scans without
changing the semantics. Probabilistic record linkage is a separate extension requiring a
calibrated joint linkage/outcome model or an explicitly conservative linkage set. The practical
stopping point is a reproducible observation plan or a short list of decision-relevant alternatives
that an analyst can resolve with additional metadata or measurement.


21.8      Automation with a defensible stopping point
The data layer can propose schemas, map candidate variables, construct as-of joins, expose
revisions and generate reproducible views. It can identify an ambiguous action timestamp
or a proxy measured after the decision and explain which analysis is affected. It cannot
establish exclusion, exchangeability, missing-at-random or a unique household definition solely
from column names. Those inputs belong to the scientific model and may remain alternative
hypotheses.
   The output is consequently more than a clean matrix: it is a defined population and
observation law, an analysis view with provenance, and explicit unresolved measurement or
linkage alternatives. Passing that object to discovery, longitudinal estimation or state-space
fitting preserves the connection between the bank’s intervention and the numbers ultimately
reported.
  The following studies develop that observation object in stages. Candidate schemas first
expose which semantic facts the bank must supply. Audited linkage then adds sampling
uncertainty to the recovered effect. Whole candidate histories reveal where an inverse becomes
weak; persistent identities show what richer audits can buy. The final covariance comparison
can improve a confidence set’s precision while leaving its identification requirements intact.

The preceding finite grammar supplies an exact conditional reconstruction operation. A
reusable product must also explain where candidates come from. The new profiler extracts
datetime columns as possible clocks, string columns as possible identifiers, small nonnegative

c, then selects the last eligible revision within its record identity, then applies the event-time

its eligible records. Chronology tevent ≤ tavailable is imposed only for a feed explicitly declared

21.9.1     Conditional correctness and missing interpretations

Suppose a correct plan c⋆ is generated, all hard constraints and analyst answers are true
of it, and accepted plans agree on the requested material observation. Then c⋆ survives
every filtering step and the accepted observation equals its projection. This induction proves
the conditional correctness claim. It does not prove candidate inclusion, answer truth or
population-level semantic recovery. Consequently the workflow records proposed hypotheses,
supplied facts, rejected reasons and unresolved views independently. A contradiction triggers
a new candidate/search request; it must not silently promote the highest-ranked survivor.
   The test fixtures rename and reorder every field, include future revisions, and vary stock/flow
interpretation and alternative value roles. Across 80 repetitions in each supported case, the
oracle observation survives and is resolved without wrong acceptance. One oracle-supplied
answer resolves stock and flow cases; two resolve the value-role ambiguity. An intentionally
unrepresented three-field record key yields incompatibility in every run, with no false acceptance.
These are synthetic heterogeneous schemas with trusted fixture answers, not measured analyst
accuracy or burden in a bank. The downstream adjusted-effect spread determines question

Consider a deposit feed with business date, warehouse arrival date and revision number, plus
a separate account/customer ownership table. A row for business day 10 may arrive on day
20, after the day-15 decision. A business-date filter then exposes a revision unavailable to the
decision maker. Joint ownership creates another ambiguity: copying an account balance to

both owners changes the economic unit and can double exposure. These are observation-model
questions before any causal estimator is called.
   Five source-owner fixtures are specified separately from the interpretation code. A current
trusted arrival-clock statement excludes the late revision and gives balance 90. Conflicting
trusted arrival/business-clock statements retain balances 90 and 110; missing clock documenta-
tion does the same. A customer request with two joint owners and no allocation rule abstains.
Finally, a plausible but incorrect accepted migration specification yields the unique wrong
balance 110. The algorithm reports conditional acceptance in that case, and the external
fixture evaluation records wrong acceptance. The result is not hidden by defining every supplied
answer as correct.
   Verified provenance means that the stated owner supplied the assertion, not that the
assertion’s economic meaning is true. Schema profiles can generate candidate clocks and entity
relations; they cannot establish first availability or ownership allocation by themselves. An
analyst question must therefore request the missing semantic fact and identify its source. If
pilot outcomes rank useful questions, the ranking and answers are frozen before final evaluation.
The executed final linkage experiment below uses predeclared strata and independent audit
information, with no final-outcome question selection.


21.11.1     Whole candidate maps and a recoverable mixture

A scalar replacement rate is inadequate when plausible candidate entities have different
responses. Use two candidate portfolio histories at each origin, with the entire response
vector assigned consistently under each map. In linkage stratum s, the two histories are
correctly ordered with probability ps and swapped otherwise. This is a one-to-one pair
assignment, not independent per-column linking. The match event is independent of the
complete potential histories conditional on the declared stratum and matching information.
That strong noninformative-linkage restriction is part of identification; a name-match score
is not evidence for it. The executed map is redrawn by origin, while retaining all horizons
within that origin together. It models an independently reconstructed candidate window,
not a persistent customer alias across the entire calendar. A fixed erroneous crosswalk or
time-dependent matching mechanism would need its own joint map law and covariance; the
following evidence does not qualify them.

21.14.1     A global calculation over audit uncertainty

If neither audit interval contains one half, each reciprocal rank 1/rj ranges over a compact
interval. Their product is a rectangle. The upper support function U is convex in these
reciprocal coordinates because it is the sum of a linear function and a norm; L is concave. Every
point in a rectangle is a convex combination of its vertices. Convexity gives U (u) ≤ maxv U (v)
and concavity gives L(u) ≥ minv L(v). Consequently the four corners give the global enclosing
interval. No local optimizer or finite effect grid can silently remove a tail.
   When an audit interval touches one half, the implementation conservatively retains the
whole line. Some such cases admit a sharper disconnected result; the original rectangle routine
retains those rays and remains a challenger. The ellipsoid implementation does not claim
sharpness in this case. A singular empirical response covariance also receives a declared
conservative fallback, rather than a pseudoinverse labelled as a four-dimensional confidence
pivot. Special-function inversions and endpoint rounding use ordinary floating-point arithmetic.
The global geometric argument is exact; a certified bound on floating-point error has not been
supplied.
   If the true response is in E and both true audit probabilities are in their intervals, the
true financial contribution belongs to the projected set. The union bound therefore gives
asymptotic coverage at least 1 − α under the stated response approximation, without a lower
bound on linkage rank. The set can be valid and financially uninformative.


21.14.2     What the comparisons diagnose

The preserved 400-draw strong-calendar design permits a retrospective comparison because
each draw retains all four means and their covariance. The median bounded width falls from
1,498,486.81 for the rectangle to 1,202,977.31 for the ellipsoid; coverage is 400/400 and 399/400.
Moderate and reversed designs also have smaller median bounded widths in this comparison.
Every retained weak and singular ellipsoid remains the whole line. These observations do not
establish uniform dominance or confirmatory calibration on newly selected calendar data.
   The regular strong-design interval covers 370/400 = .925. Supplying the true audit probabil-
ities gives 372/400 = .930, and using the oracle first-order linearization gives 371/400 = .9275.
Thus estimated audit probabilities alone do not explain the failure; the response approximation
and its finite sample behavior remain important. These arms identify contributors, not a
unique causal diagnosis of the coverage error. With freshly simulated Gaussian primitive
responses of known covariance, strong-design regular coverage is 1920/2000 = .960, while both
conservative projection constructions cover all 2,000 draws. That primitive experiment isolates
geometry from the original calendar covariance estimation; it cannot retrospectively validate
the latter.
   The practical alternatives are consequently clear. Use the original rectangle when preserving
disconnected topology matters; use the registered ellipsoid when covariance is well qualified
and its outer interval is suitable; retain the regular calculation as a disclosed research route;
or purchase the outcome-linked information in Section 21.13. None may be selected after
observing which produces the narrowest favorable answer.
