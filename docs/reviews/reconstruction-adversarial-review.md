# Reconstruction: adversarial author review

8 October 2026. This is an author review of the commissioned survey and product
design. It is not independent review, user acceptance or bank validation.
The final delivery manifest binds this record to the reviewed sources and PDFs.

## Review criterion and scope

The benchmark comparison in reference-standards-2026-10-08.json remains the
quality contract. FinMarketing requires target-specific learner comparisons
and a derived dynamic-choice bridge; BayesFilter requires probability laws,
information and numerical targets before backend selection; CIP requires
identification and observation restrictions before parameterization. The
reconstruction is assessed against those structures, not against page counts.

The full chapter argument was read across the reconstruction sessions.
This final pass revisited the connections among model, observation, target,
estimator, uncertainty and proposed operation. Primary-source inspections
accumulated in the reading notes and source-question matrix are reused,
with additional direct checks of Hall–Horowitz, Alexandrovich et al.,
Bennett–Kallus and Ryalen et al. described below. A finite identity or a
source anchor is not a proof of every general theorem stated in a primary.

## Substantive findings and repairs

| Finding | Consequence | Repair and evidence |
|---|---|---|

| The NPIV rate display used an unqualified L2 norm after a conditional-expectation Hilbert-space construction. | The source's Lebesgue integrated risk could be confused with an L2(P_A) theorem. | Named L2([0,1],dx), retained the positive normal-operator distinction and re-read Hall–Horowitz PDF pp.7–10, A.1–A.5 and Theorems 4.1–4.2. |
| The longitudinal chapter's finite-action scope was implicit in sums and ratio bounds. | A deterministic point intervention under continuous treatment could inherit an invalid density-ratio argument. | Declared finite actions at the decision clock, explained singular continuous point policies and connected to the modified-policy chapter. |

No backend execution is asserted. |
| Current numerical claim artifacts were listed but not hash-bound by the active audit. | A current manuscript could reference stale study or component evidence. | Audit now checks study/component/protocol input hashes, claim artifact hashes and build inputs/outputs, including general package warnings. |

The marked-reward example is a loss-of-information counterexample, not a
same-full-observed-law nonidentification claim. The bridge repair preserves
the primary theorem's assumptions; it does not add a new identification theorem.

The repeated source checks confirm the stationary fixed-K, distinct-emission,
full-rank conditions for Alexandrovich's 2K+1 observation theorem. They do
not extend it to private controlled logging. Ryalen's bounded-outcome proof
uses consistency and a mean-one martingale in an enlarged filtration;
ordinary symmetric sequential independence is not substituted for it.

## Evidence review

The reconstructed studies were rerun with preserved adverse results, then
checked by separate finite-path likelihood/count enumeration, sequential
score recursion, repeated-cell covariance and direct financial accounts.
The same-data convergence follow-up is narrower than refitting every capped
replicate. The ten-million-row measurement was rerun after fitting finished
to avoid that simultaneous load. Its workload excludes source I/O, temporal
reconstruction and nuisance/latent estimation. Generated tables were read
against the recorded values; build receipts bind their displayed bytes.

The publication audit verifies every active citation against saved source
bytes and trace records. Original unavailable g-formula/MSM corrections,
Newey–Powell and other historical acquisition gaps remain in the omissions
register. Later preserved primaries support the particular results used;
they are not passed off as the missing originals. No agent-written paper
substitutes or invented source inspection are accepted.

## Author conclusion

The previously missing central connections are now developed as sustained
arguments: diagram construction to IV; identified functionals to orthogonal
learning and inverse problems; longitudinal histories to controlled
state-space likelihoods and belief-state value. The product is derived
from those arguments and retains their unsupported cases. This closes the
commissioned reconstruction at author-reviewed proposal scope. The final
source/build checks pass, and actual rendered inspection is recorded in
reconstruction-visual-inspection.json for both current PDF hashes. It does not claim
universal methodological coverage or automatic equality to the reference
monographs by any numerical measure.

Future product construction, independent review, bank data qualification,
external-package runtime tests, accepted sibling integrations and measured
adoption remain separate work. They are not substitutes for the scholarly
repairs above, and their absence does not masquerade as an unfinished proof
inside the supported proposal scope.
