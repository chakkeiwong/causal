## Julia, Scala and Rust missingness contracts

The proposed product can conditionally reuse Impute.jl for local numerical completion, Spark for fitted numeric surrogates, and Polars for typed column and temporal operations. Thirty-five retained originals establish a bounded comparison of these roles. A completed table does not establish the observation, treatment-assignment or population assumptions required for a causal effect. The interface must preserve original masks, customer identities, types, availability times and the authorized fitting population. [@exec25_otm_imputejl; @exec25_otm_spark; @exec25_otm_polars]

The source inspection makes the next implementation work concrete. Impute.jl's nearest-neighbor initialization follows typed median/mode defaults despite a mean-fill comment; self identity, duplicate distances and retained initialization need qualification. Spark excludes NaN during fit but can retain it during transform when another sentinel is configured, and restores the original numeric type. Its quantile rank tolerance is not a monetary value-error bound. Polars distinguishes null from NaN and exposes operations whose customer partitions and time interpretation remain caller obligations. These findings motivate explicit adapter contracts rather than a generic promise that all missing values have been corrected. Exact authored examples demonstrate the consequences without claiming native benchmark results. Positional interpolation gives five where coordinate interpolation gives one for irregular times; global forward fill can cross customer boundaries; an integer cast can change a fitted mean of 7/3 to two. An illustrative source/target effect difference of 0.8 at USD10 per unit over 10,000 accounts gives USD80,000. These are proposal design examples, not observed bank gains. [@exec25_otm_choice]

Public generated laws and typed fixtures can qualify mask conversion, donor randomness, neighbor identity, training-only fitting, integer casts, temporal leakage and refusal behavior in independent branches. The prototype owner must bind each result to exact artifacts, dependencies, scientific oracles and full costs. Selected MIT-style and Apache 2 declarations do not clear the whole installed distribution. Wider languages, uninspected candidates, numerical delegates and native qualification remain open. None of the components supplies a universal repair for unrestricted missing-not-at-random observations or unsupported target populations.
