Master program V14: complete coverage with independent execution
12 October 2026. Literature cutoff: 7 October 2026.
The program is unfinished. The present working monograph has 375 pages, but the three requested repairs remain
incomplete: the software, LLM/agent and integration parts occupy physical spans of 42, 42 and 12 pages. None of
the ten original capability gaps is fully qualified. Twenty-three of 48 packages have recorded outcomes, including
negative and inconclusive results. Those outcomes must be interpreted within their stated scope.
The immediate planning defect was real. The previous controller required the entire software census before accepting
any family comparison, every family comparison before reuse decisions, and the entire reuse package before an
individual adapter. Its fixed priority repeatedly selected SW01. Saying that independent work could proceed did not
change these executable barriers.
This revision replaces 16 broad dependency edges across nine unresolved packages. It gives source reviews, individual
reuse decisions, commercial comparisons, chapter development, adapters, registered trials, scientific arguments and
external intake their own prerequisites. The final package receipts remain substantive acceptance decisions; completing
a small operation does not close a scientific gap or award product acceptance.
The detailed evidence audit, machine work graph and review of every old dependency are part of this program. The
PDF includes the full audit as an appendix. Current execution uses python3 tools/program.py status, next and
check. The historical controller and README are bound to old receipts and are preserved. Mission Control and the
canonical state point to this revision.

The standard and the four completion decisions
The deliverable is a connected causal-banking monograph, a reusable product proposal and evidence for the capabilities
it actually claims. It must cover four journeys: funding responses; sequential servicing; discovery followed by an
intervention query; and trustworthy observations. Its six research/product streams remain identification and learning,
dependent time series and panels, longitudinal and controlled-state decisions, discovery and uncertain observations,
reusable operations and computation, and financial validation and economics.
Each claimed use must connect the business decision and information clock to a causal model and assumptions,
identification or an identified set, estimation and learning, uncertainty and diagnostics, substantive alternatives,
numerical error and monetary consequences. The named FinMarketing monograph is a standard for a developed
scholarly argument. It is neither a page-padding target nor evidence that a new integration already works.
Every scholarly citation needs its genuine PDF under docs/papers/, named Title FirstAuthorSurname(year).pdf.
Every cited source, including official software documentation, needs identity, version/date, saved bytes, SHA-256,
inspected technical anchors and supported claims. Metadata establishes identity and discovery only. Unavailable
originals stay in the omissions register; an author-written summary cannot replace them. Sources retrieved after the
cutoff must have their applicable historical revision established or be reported as a separate dated delta. Banking
arithmetic must reproduce.
The four decisions are deliberately separate. Author completion requires a reviewed argument, evidence and limitations,
and can include negative results. Capability qualification requires valid and useful output in a declared model, sampling
and information regime. External acceptance requires authentic independent, bank, consumer and operator evidence.
Investment/adoption requires actual decision-value, cost and owner acceptance. A source inspection, compiled PDF,
passing API test or completed plan cannot supply the later decisions.

Work lane 1: software evidence and scientific comparisons
The 102 source cells are independently addressable. For each, inspect the existing search record and candidate
identities; complete complementary primary routes and relevant citation/repository chaining; record cutoff, duplicates,
inclusion/exclusion and bounded stopping rules; acquire genuine originals; inspect exact technical behavior; and write
a reviewed disposition with alternatives and limitations. A cell with no eligible candidate needs a documented search.
An inaccessible essential source prevents its claim from closing, while other cells continue.
The 17 area comparisons begin from already inspected evidence. Their specific questions are in the audit: graph
classes, identification algorithms, estimands, sampling units, nuisance learning, survival/competing events, policies and
logging, cohort/time aggregation, donor uncertainty, instruments/common shocks, interference, mediation/sensitivity,
transport/missingness, latent states, observation truth and complete computation. Each comparison needs explained
assumptions, diagnostics, a banking example, substantive alternatives and typed failure behavior. It must distinguish
documentation, inspected implementation, executed behavior and scientifically qualified inference.
Final review of an area waits only for that area’s needed language/source dispositions. Commercial analysis is a
separate lane, so missing a vendor manual does not stop a noncommercial area review. Discovery evidence can feed
discovery chapters and one adapter while panel or longitudinal searches continue.

Work lane 3: commercial comparison

Scheduling, review and continuation
There is one coordinator and no spawned editing agents. Logical independence does not require concurrent agents.
The machine graph contains separately addressable evidence obligations, not hundreds of sequential phases. Reusable
cell, decision, trial and chapter templates keep the work consistent. Final manuscript and product results remain
integrated.
The scheduler selects the least recently served lane that has ready work, then uses attempt count, priority and stable
ID within that lane. The lanes are software, reuse, commercial, LLM, integration, research, manuscript, external and
release. The first cycle therefore combines source review, a representative reuse decision, commercial acquisition, an
LLM task, integration design, scientific argument, chapter development and intake/release preparation. A long source
census cannot monopolize continuation.
Use bounded units and short tool output. Before an operation, inspect its exact contract and evidence. Do the
work, review the result, repair justified findings and run appropriate checks. Record a pass only for that stated
scope. A negative or inconclusive disposition can be recorded without pretending a blocked trial ran. A review that
rejects reuse may complete the decision but cannot enable an adapter. An essential missing source keeps the affected
acceptance open.
After recording a result or a blocker, the controller immediately reports the next eligible operation across all lanes.
Continue it without asking for phase or checkpoint permission. A checkpoint saves state; it is not a stopping point.
Stop dependent work only for a real missing prerequisite, invalid argument, runtime/permission constraint or absent
authentic input, record the evidence and exact trigger to resume, and continue unrelated work. If no operations are
eligible, report the remaining real blockers and needed input precisely.
For a long operation, progress records bounded inspected evidence and the exact next action without awarding
completion. It also rotates lane selection, so an unfinished literature census cannot monopolize the session. Saving
this checkpoint is followed by continued work in another eligible lane.
