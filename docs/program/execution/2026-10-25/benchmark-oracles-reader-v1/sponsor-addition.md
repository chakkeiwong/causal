## Qualify the scientific oracle before funding the agent trial

Public evidence can justify a prototype only if its reference preserves the intended decision. Independent finite models demonstrate the instrumental-variable gap: identical observed distributions and a ratio of 1 coexist with population effects of -1/2 and 1/4. A stipulated 10,000-account example at USD100 per outcome unit implies increments of USD-500,000 and USD250,000 before costs. This establishes ambiguity, not a bank estimate or measured benchmark failure. A prototype must preserve ambiguity, change its target explicitly or identify the missing information. [@exec25_bor_sources; @exec25_bor_reference]

Three reference states are required: identified, not identified and unknown. Backend failure or nullable metadata cannot silently become a scientific label. Refusal, malformed output and unknown truth have separate denominators. Interval validation covers endpoints, truth, confidence level and overflow. The retained implementation reports raw and capped loss; an exact witness shows why both matter, with raw means of 10 versus 1 changing to capped means of 0.1 versus 1. [@exec25_bor_sources; @exec25_bor_reference]

The public trial should bind native source and dependency versions, causal and scoring fixtures, visible task information, and complete request, failure and cost counts. This work supplies independently derived requirements and reproducible examples. Native conformance, generator verification, artifact rights, independent review and banking value remain unqualified. The finite findings justify a bounded experiment without establishing a completed product. [@exec25_bor_reference]
