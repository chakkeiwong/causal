B.1      S TATES , F ORMULAS , AND D EDUCTIVE C LOSURE

Following the Objective Information Theory (OIT) foundation Qiu & Xu (2025), the state of an ob-
ject or system at a time point (or interval) is the semantic valuation of a set of well-formed formulas
over a given domain and interpretation. The framework relates a semantic domain SO of statements
to a carrier domain SC that represents them Xu (2026a). In CPUNeSy, the language model is the
carrier and the symbolic core operates on the statements it proposes.
Definition 1 (Statement universe and proof system Xu (2026d)). Fix a finite ambient universe SO of
candidate statements and an effective proof system PS such that for every finite Γ ⊆ SO and every
s ∈ SO , the judgment Γ ⊢ s is decidable. The induced deductive closure is

which is reflexive (Γ ⊆ Cn(Γ)), monotone, and idempotent (Cn(Cn(Γ)) = Cn(Γ)), with Cn(Γ)
finite for finite Γ.
