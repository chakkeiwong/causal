\section{The standard and the four banking journeys}

The product standard follows one decision through its information clock:
banking target, causal and observation model, identification or an identified
set, estimation or learning, uncertainty and sensitivity, numerical error,
and monetary consequence. An interval is useful only when its assumptions,
set topology, units and refusal meaning travel with it. Author completion,
scoped capability qualification, authentic bank acceptance and investment
adoption are separate gates.

The four journeys are: (A) a structural funding response; (B) a sequential
servicing decision with a private state; (C) discovery followed by an effect
query; and (D) construction of trustworthy observations and reusable
operations. The earlier 259-page reader and its preserved supplements contain
the inherited model, source roles and baseline evidence. This release adds
the Wave 1 execution records without rewriting those predecessors.

\section{Funding: what additional information changes}

Let $F_t$ be a focal balance response history and let two candidate histories
be stored in uncertain order. The old correctness-count observation cannot
separate the two focal means at identity rank $p=1/2$. A truthful sampled
origin audit $R_t$ with known $q_t>0$ supplies the focal identity. With
$B_t$ the candidate average, the vector pseudo-outcome
\[
 Z_t=B_t+\frac{R_t}{q_t}(F_t-B_t)
\]
has conditional mean $F_t$. This is new information; more old counts do not
create it. Under a continuous action with known randomized mean and variance,
an independent pilot can be used in a centered control score. Replicating
entire calendars removes the fitted ratio denominator and estimates the
remaining calendar variance across independent calendar means.

The R01 protocol froze 24 independent calendars, 50 origins per calendar,
an independent fourfold pilot, a 99 percent Student critical, six valid cells
and three premise-violation cells. The finite screen was a simultaneous
one-sided lower bound of .95 in each valid cell; every request remained in
the denominator. The replicated score passed in all valid cells:

\section{Servicing: fixed policy value with a private state}

The R03 extension adds a nonlinear $A_t\tanh(S_t)$ reward term to a stable
private-state transition. The public observation is noisy, while a supplied
logger probability is bounded away from zero and one. Representative truthful
full-state audits identify the selected17 likelihood family. For an observable
policy $\pi$, sequential change of measure gives
\[
 V(\pi)=\mathbb E\left[\sum_{t=0}^{H-1} .97^t W_t R_t\right],\qquad
 W_t=\prod_{j\le t}\frac{1\{A_j=\pi_j(O_j)\}}{p_j(A_j\mid S_j,O_j)}.
\]
The direct estimator has no latent-filter quadrature error. Its uncertainty is
the sample variance over independent complete trajectories. A 99 percent
critical was frozen before draws.

All six valid cells passed the simultaneous .95 lower-bound screen, with
lower bounds .963--.981. The 95 percent challenger was narrower and failed
the same screen. Wrong logger probabilities covered 1/512; evaluation-policy
leakage produced no finite output. Weak emission, scarce audits, poor and
strong overlap, and transition misspecification remained visible. This
qualifies a fixed policy in the selected17 family with truthful audits and
supplied logger records. It does not qualify an unknown optimal policy,
estimated logger uncertainty, arbitrary POMDPs or bank truth.

\section{Discovery: why a selected graph is not an effect region}

R04 uses a finite stationary temporal Gaussian class with observed
encouragement, action and response histories and a latent disturbance. A
simultaneous covariance region is propagated through every retained graph.
In the observational latent class, direct-effect members imply an
unrestricted $\R$ effect set. A covariance witness constructs distinct latent
systems with the same positive-definite observed covariance and arbitrary
direct effects. Hence observational temporal data alone cannot identify the
requested effect. Randomized encouragement, exclusion or measured
sufficiency can contract the set, but each is added scientific information.

The exact finite-member fixture passed containment and covariance-witness
checks. The first registered run then failed before request publication
because mathematically valid infinite endpoints were sent to a JSON writer.
That failure is retained. A reviewed successor encodes unbounded endpoints as
null, but the common summary decoder still needs a versioned repair before a
fresh study can be counted. D03 remains unresolved; no PAG or unrestricted
temporal guarantee is claimed.

\section{Learning and operations}

R05 fixes a sparse linear nuisance family, block separation, a lasso grid and
a HAC score. Its dense cross-fit reference agrees on the small fixture, but
the sparse method's lower coverage bounds range from .879 to .929 across the
ordinary valid cells. The weak-IV cell has wide, biased intervals and is not a
qualification. D06 receives conditional progress for the implemented family;
D09 remains unresolved. Arbitrary learners and nonstationary bank panels
remain open.

E01 supplies the implementation boundary. Four consumer fixtures preserve
finite intervals, unions, whole lines, unavailable outputs, assumptions,
units and capability disposition. Dense and streamed linked funding agree to
$3.5\times10^{-10}$ currency units and $3\times10^{-11}$ standard-error
units while retaining 37 pseudo-outcome rows. A forced local interruption at
request 13 recovers 40 unique requests atomically with no duplicates. This is
single-process local evidence; it does not qualify multi-host service levels,
target platforms or bank interfaces.
