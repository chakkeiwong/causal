# Remaining gaps after V10

V10 completes the planned local comparison and selected streaming construction.
It does not complete the general bank product. The standard remains connected
identification-to-estimation-to-uncertainty reasoning for a declared banking
target, primary-source trace, reproducible financial examples, adverse-case
diagnostics, credible calibration and inspected delivery. General usefulness
also needs independent challenge, authentic bank validation, accepted
interfaces, target-platform evidence and measured economics.

The registered 16,000-request synthetic grid supplies selected evidence that
fourfold/eightfold pilots outperform joint regression at one baseline. It also
finds .927/.933/.934 all-request coverage with small audits, intervals in only
451/2,000 near-rank requests and severe failure under false exogeneity.
Doubling pilot history has no demonstrated benefit. The selected streaming
pilot is dense-equivalent in independent numerical checks and cuts large-case
local memory to .544/.599 of dense use; elapsed ratios .906/1.071 do not show
a reliable speed improvement. Neither fact establishes bank validity or
general calibration.

| Remaining gap | Route to address it | Missing evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Finite-sample calibration and useful decision precision | Derive a correction or a changed audit/evaluation design, register it before fresh draws, and compare all-request coverage, width, bias, availability and cost. | No general correction is known. Retain the small-audit, persistence, near-rank and false-exogeneity failures. |
| Independent pilot feasibility and economics | Identify an invariant independent history source and a bank precision threshold; compare fourfold and alternatives on held-out matched cost/quality. | Additional pilot rows, representative audits and gold labels are unpriced. |
| Other causal and observation models | For nonlinear private states, PAG effects, persistent/endogenous identity maps or arbitrary learners, establish their own identification, sampling rates, covariance and numerical bounds. | V10's fixed dictionary and origin-swap premises do not transfer automatically. |
| Production operation | Run complete matched requests, concurrency and fault recovery on the intended storage and compute platform. | Local single-host pilot measurements do not measure partial-fit recovery, service levels or total costs. |
| Institutional acceptance | Execute the preserved V9/V8 external protocol with independent reviewers, bank source/audit owners, interface maintainers and workflow evaluators. | None has supplied signed results or adopted this proposal. |

The [D01--D10 register](../program/deferred-gaps-2026-10-10.md) gives a
separate continuation action for each obligation. A failed or inconclusive
investigation can be author-complete while the capability remains open.
