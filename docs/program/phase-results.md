### Scientific findings preserved

The deposit design uses 96 branches over 30 months with fixed adoption cohorts.
Its estimated monthly funding contribution is about negative USD 5,219 per
treated branch despite a positive balance effect. The cash break-even at a
USD 30 million untreated balance is about USD 4.293 million additional funds.
Whole-branch uncertainty is conditional on this synthetic rollout.

The eight-decision collections study uses independent model-training and
policy-evaluation trajectories. It compares direct, cumulative importance and
doubly robust values, paired policy contrasts and an incorrect current-action
shortcut. The 160-dataset coverage study retains undercoverage, including
0.86875 for zero-Q/true-logging and 0.9125 for true-Q/wrong-logging.

Unknown-emission controlled-state estimation fits initial, transition and
observation laws. Hidden action selection reverses the omitted-action
policy-gain sign in the main observational sample. All 30 selected
omitted-action repetition fits and seven joint fits hit their 120-iteration
cap; one bootstrap fit is capped. A same-data extension to 2,000 maximum
iterations meets the stopping criterion and retains a gain near negative
USD 22.40 for the omitted-action fit versus positive USD 13.61 for the joint
fit. This checks a specific optimization concern, not global optimality or
general observational identification.

The bank-firm study contrasts a pooled coefficient near -1.08 with a
borrower-effect coefficient near -2.92 against a generating -3. The
1,200-panel common-shock experiment produces interval coverage near 0.227
with row inference, 0.693 with entity clustering and 0.944 with crossed
clustering. These are declared synthetic laws, not bank performance evidence.

The temporal fixture returns 160 then 90 dollars when revisions are selected
before economic windows; adverse orderings yield different accounts.
Score merging agrees with independent centered batch covariance, including
repeated intersection rows. Three fresh processes per size measure generated
two-dimensional scores at 100,000, one million and ten million rows; the
largest median is about 1.208 seconds and 123 MiB. Model fits and source reads
are excluded, and dense intersection storage limits cardinality.

Equal-output construction economics derives a threshold of 26.737 completed
equivalent studies annually under the stated three-year assumptions. The
platform loses at low reuse and on the 4/16/30 adoption ramp; it saves about
USD 2.073 million present cost at 36 studies each year. These costs and effort
savings are assumptions awaiting bank measurement.
