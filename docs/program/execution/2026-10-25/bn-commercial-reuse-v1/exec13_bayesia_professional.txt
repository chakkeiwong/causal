BayesiaLab Professional — Licensing Options & Pricing
Context
Built on the foundation of the Bayesian network formalism, BayesiaLab is a powerful desktop application with a highly sophisticated graphical user interface. It provides scientists with a comprehensive “lab” environment for machine learning, knowledge modeling, diagnosis, analysis, simulation, and optimization.
Supported Platforms
BayesiaLab supports a range of operating systems:
Windows XP, Vista, 7, 8, 10, 11 (32-bit and 64-bit systems)
macOS/OS X: Mountain Lion (10.8) or later, Intel and Apple Silicon
Linux/Unix
BayesiaLab always has to be installed on your local hardware. BayesiaLab is
not available as a remotely-hosted Software-as-a-Service (SaaS).
Configurations
We offer a wide range of licensing options for BayesiaLab Professional to suit your specific business requirements, including
License Purchase
and
License Rental
.
Independently of purchase or rental, BayesiaLab can be licensed per seat (
Single-User/Single-Machine License
) or for shared use in an organization (
Floating Token License
)
Additionally, the
Elastic Licensing
program offers a high degree of flexibility with several prepaid plans.
For students and faculty of accredited institutions, we offer attractively priced
Academic Editions
and
Education Packages
of BayesiaLab.
Non-profit organizations and government entities may be eligible for institutional discounts on certain products. Please get in touch with us for details.
