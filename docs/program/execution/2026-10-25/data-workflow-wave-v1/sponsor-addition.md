## Observation data and workflow execution as a scientific contract

The proposed product needs an observation contract and an execution contract before it can make a credible claim about causal results. A financial unit, source record, proposed identity match and physical job attempt must remain distinct. The selected data.table, targets, Dask, Snakemake, Beam, Arrow, Taskflow, DataFrames.jl and Dagger implementations provide useful components across five language ecosystems. Their 83 authentic pinned source files support bounded comparisons; five discovery queries do not establish complete market coverage, and no downloaded implementation was executed. A two-by-two duplicate-key join can double USD300 of loan exposure to USD600 while remaining at a row-expansion limit. An event-time lookup can select a revision unavailable at the decision date. In a separate authored linkage example, an 80% match probability between possible losses of USD100 and USD1,000 gives expected loss USD280, not the USD100 top-match plug-in. Applying that plug-in to 10,000 hypothetical accounts understates expected loss by USD1.8 million. These are reproducible constructed consequences, not bank loss estimates or observed package defects. They justify public tests of cardinality, clocks, missing identities and retained linkage uncertainty. [@exec25_dww_choice]

The preferred architecture conditionally adopts mature engines behind explicit worker contracts. R pairs tabular workers with targets where appropriate; Python compares task-graph and file-workflow roles; JVM workers declare their Beam/Spark runner and sink; C++ separates Arrow data representation from Taskflow execution; Julia separates DataFrames joins from Dagger fault recovery. A portable scientific schema and finite oracles are better initial porting targets than whole mature engines. Root license declarations, task names and source pins do not establish complete artifact rights, installed-version compatibility or supported commercial equivalence. Workflow retries need explicit logical identifiers, attempt histories and external-effect reconciliation. A repeated USD100 instruction after lost acknowledgement can create USD200 of effects unless the sink enforces an appropriate idempotency protocol. A constructed two-request example costs eight units over four attempts; dividing only successful-attempt costs by completions reports one unit rather than four. Neither figure is a measured service quote. The investment design must count failures, retries, unresolved outcomes and complete resource and engineering costs before claiming savings. The next proposal milestone is a preregistered public suite with finite join and clock oracles, uncertain-linkage cases, seeded logical replication, numeric cancellation, worker failures and sink acknowledgement faults. CLI/MCP requests should bind immutable inputs, scientific target, artifact profile and logical job identity, with typed results and diagnostic evidence. This comparison creates no sibling-repository integration or native execution guarantee. Missing assignment or availability truth cannot be recovered by a faster scheduler; it requires information or justified restrictions. [@exec25_dww_choice]
