set projections and coverage counts, and confirms 2,532 matching available

The projected set covered 600/600 requests in each valid-law baseline,
small-audit, near-rank and persistent design. Those results are not a blanket
success. Median finite widths were about USD 1.231 million, USD 1.406 million
and USD 3.426 million in the baseline, small-audit and persistent cases,
approximately 2.47, 2.82 and 2.56 times the corresponding regular intervals.

remained; their median width was about USD 63.7 million. The regular method
had 132/600 available intervals and 130/600 all-request coverage. The new set
does not turn weak audit information into useful decision precision.

Under the registered false-exogeneity law, regular coverage was 122/600 and
projection coverage 591/600. The latter's selected-law coverage was purchased
with roughly USD 1.367 million median width and does not validate the violated
scientific premise. A different violation can fail; no general correction or
causal guarantee is claimed. Exact-binomial Monte Carlo intervals describe
only the registered synthetic laws. No bank threshold or pilot/audit price
has been supplied to judge whether any finite width is worthwhile.
