## Policy learning needs separate optimisation and evaluation services

The R comparison now distinguishes policytree's supplied-reward tree optimisation, DynTxRegime's backward Q-learning steps, DTRreg's model-based sequential estimation and polle's fixed or adaptive policy evaluation. Each is a candidate for an original R worker under an explicit method contract. Their package names do not establish bank identification, calibrated uncertainty or unrestricted optimality. Model, history, action-domain, source and rights restrictions remain attached to the proposed service. \citep{exec24_policiesr_policytree,exec24_policiesr_dyntx,exec24_policiesr_dtrreg,exec24_policiesr_polle}

Exact authored examples show why this matters economically. Selecting between two zero-value policies with independent errors of minus or plus one dollar produces an expected development maximum of half a dollar, while fresh value remains zero. A four-stage path with probability one in ten thousand requires weight ten thousand; capping its unit-reward contribution at one hundred reduces its expectation to one hundredth. A quadratic dose optimum beyond observed support depends on an unverified extrapolation, and compressing relevant history can reduce achievable value from one to one half. These are declared finite laws, not observed package performance. \citep{exec24_policiesr_reference}

The proposed public/generated prototype compares fixed-policy value, selected-policy value and constrained optimisation separately. Its CLI/MCP artifacts preserve history and feature schemas, action identities and domains, support, sample membership, uncertainty, refusals, aborted or failed fits and full costs. Twenty attempts costing USD 100 with five accepted numeric deliveries cost USD 20 per accepted delivery; correct refusals are scored separately. Unknown logging, unsupported actions and unrestricted hidden state require additional evidence or justified restrictions. Conditional original-worker integration is preferable to an unmotivated whole-library port. \citep{exec24_policiesr_reference}
