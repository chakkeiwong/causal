Corollary 1 (Reliability under premise erasure). Under the fixed-cache availability model of Defi-

The three accuracy conditions concern derivation demand (Proposition 2), certified-source coverage
(Theorem 5), and slip- versus bias-dominated grounding (Lemma 1). Separately, the corpus audit
uses criteria C1–C4. On the 14,414-unit AFP corpus and 503-unit regulatory corpus, C1, C2, and

      S2. Fixed canonical order. The canonical scan order of Definition 3 is public and fixed; kernel

      S3. Channel independence. The bridge channel errors (proposal failure rate µ, verification
          miss rate η, formalization failure rate εf r ) are independent across derivation steps and ex-
          posed premises. For the formalization channel this applies to the slip component only; the

          separately (Definition 8, Lemma 1).
      S4. Stationary amortized cost model. Stationary request stream; storage rate α; recomputa-
          tion rate β; ρ = α/β; storage cost σ normalized to one step-equivalent per unit.
      S5. Canonical-witness erasure regime. Designated-parent map, hereditary derivation DAG
          (Definition 5); premise erasures i.i.d. with rate ε.
