40.1     What a complete connection must preserve
A treasury analyst may obtain a state estimate from one package, an intervention contrast
from another and a value calculation from a third. The final number answers the original
question only if these exchanges preserve its meaning. An exposure measured in millions, a rate
expressed in basis points, an outcome available after a decision and a value computed under
a different continuation policy can each change that meaning while leaving a syntactically
valid table. The integration problem therefore begins with the banking decision and ends with
the consumer’s displayed result. Successful transfer of a numeric array is one intermediate
condition.
  Let the target declaration be

                                  T = (P, π, π0 , t0 , H, F, u, c),

where P is the population, π and π0 are the intervention and comparator, t0 is the decision
origin, H the outcome horizon, F the available information, u the measurement unit and c
the cash-flow convention. This is a proposed semantic record, rather than a claim that every
inspected repository already serializes these fields. A target also needs its scientific premises:
the observation and assignment laws, graph or model class, sampling units, and the evidence
supporting any restrictions. Those premises are referenced alongside T ; a short tuple is not a
replacement for their mathematical definition.
  Write D for a consumer’s data and M (D) for the representation sent to the causal operation.
A correct adapter must give a declared mapping g for which

                           τconsumer (D; T ) = g{τprovider (M (D); T ′ )}                  (40.1)

under the stated model. For a unit conversion, g may be a fixed affine function. For a
population change, time aggregation or policy substitution, there is usually no such automatic
identity. The adapter must then require a new analysis or an additional argument. This
requirement is useful even when both packages use identical field names: an “effect” can denote
an association, a structural response or a policy-value difference.
   The result mapping must preserve uncertainty as well as the target. For a fixed known
function g and a confidence set C for τ , the event {τ ∈ C} implies {g(τ ) ∈ g(C)}. Thus applying
g to the whole set preserves the coverage lower bound. This elementary implication does not

make a supplied interval a valid confidence set, and it does not apply to unaccounted uncertain
exchange rates, estimated exposure weights or a transformation chosen after inspecting the
result. A joint confidence region for all uncertain inputs, or an explicitly conditional scenario,
is needed in those cases. An identified set, a sensitivity range and a confidence set must keep
different result types even when each happens to be printed as two endpoints.
   Three connections consequently need separate judgments. Source inspection can establish
that a field or command is declared. An author-operated trial can establish that a selected
version transports and interprets a fixture correctly. Acceptance by a consumer or an institution
requires its own decision and evidence. The cases below use inspected interfaces, existing local
runtime evidence and new transparent calculations at their respective levels. They do not
claim that sibling maintainers have adopted this library.

40.3     Information clocks before feature exchange
A transaction can occur before a decision and still be unavailable to the decision maker. Let
vj be the event time for feature j, aj its availability time and dt the action time. The relevant
information set is generated by records available before dt , not by every record whose event
time precedes it. Late corrections, external bureau arrivals and retrospective entity resolution
all create this distinction. Reconstructing the final database as though it were visible at every
earlier date gives a different policy from the one the bank could have used.
  FinMarketing’s inspected FeatureSnapshot compares timestamp and sequence pairs and
requires
                              (amax , sa ) ≤ (c, sc ) < (dt , sd ),
where c is the feature cutoff and s is a within-timestamp sequence. Its selected records also
distinguish training, validation, test and serving partitions, bind a fitted transformation to a
snapshot, and keep outcome names separate from feature names. The inspected SourceBatch
includes owner, use basis, permission and source digest, with a declared synthetic or licensed-
public boundary (Causal banking research project, 2026k). These declarations give a concrete
proposed mapping. They do not establish that the supplied timestamps or ownership claims
are true.
   Consider an illustrative decision pair (100, 4) and cutoff (100, 3). Availability at (99, 9)
or (100, 3) is admitted; availability at (100, 5) or (101, 0) is rejected. The four exact checks
are reproduced with the other chapter examples (Causal banking research project, 2026j).
Dropping the sequence field makes the third case appear simultaneous and loses the needed
ordering. Converting UTC to a local display time must not change the comparison. When a
source does not supply the required ordering, the adapter should request a coarser defensible
decision clock or report unavailable information, rather than inventing the missing sequence.
   Filtering introduces the same issue at a less visible boundary. A state estimate using
observations through t can support a decision after those observations arrive. A smoothed
state using later observations cannot be substituted into that historical policy without changing
its information set. Both arrays can have the same dimensions and plausible values. The
consumer therefore needs the conditioning horizon and observation-availability rule, in addition
to a state name and numeric uncertainty. This requirement connects the workspace interfaces
directly to the longitudinal assumptions developed earlier in the monograph.

40.4      Funding: from a structural rate response to value
Suppose treasury asks for the value of a ten-basis-point reduction in a funding action over four
quarters. Three objects are required. The consumer must specify the action and exposure. A
scientific analysis must establish what change in paid funding rate that action would cause
under its model. A valuation step must then translate that response into dated cash flows.
A forecast of market rates alone supplies neither the intervention response nor the exposure
response to the bank’s decision.
   The inspected MacroFinance inventory directs active numerical work to BayesFilter and
describes the root migration adapter as a compatibility facade (Causal banking research
project, 2026ag). In the re-inspected BayesFilter declarations, BayesianSSMProblem binds
the model manifest, data signature, static shape and coordinate convention; FilterProgram
separately names the filter, required model capabilities, target policy and approximation
semantics (Causal banking research project, 2026k). A proposed funding adapter should
retain these distinctions. An exact structural model, a deterministic approximate filter and
a fixed-randomness numerical procedure need not imply the same likelihood or uncertainty.
Importing a historical local sampler because it returns a familiar array could bypass the
selected authority entirely.

basis points, annual effective discount rate r = 0.04 and an initial implementation cost
K = 40,000 USD. Let θ denote paid-rate pass-through in basis points per action basis point,
held constant across four quarters. Assume balances are invariant to the action, cash accrues
uniformly within each quarter and is paid at quarter end, and no additional fees or risk effects
occur. The present value of savings is then

that baseline with an automated connection. No general MacroFinance–BayesFilter–causal
adapter has been qualified by the arithmetic example.

finite-range proof is unavailable; truncation, tail control or another error metric would have to

   For an actual exchange, the causal-side mapping should therefore retain the following fields
in distinct roles. The model/data/coordinate identity specifies which conditional distribution
is being approximated. The filter identity and approximation semantics identify the numerical
procedure. Its diagnostics describe what was observed in that procedure, while an error
statement must specify a norm, target and validity regime. Finally, the value mapping gives
the payoff, its horizon and any bounds used to translate numerical error. A convergence trace
is useful diagnostic evidence but cannot silently become a total-variation certificate. When
no justified error bound is available, the consumer should display a conditional approximate
result and the missing error qualification.
   A similar distinction applies to posterior output. A posterior distribution computed under
a structural model may support Bayesian decision analysis conditional on that model and
its priors. Passing posterior quantiles into a field labeled frequentist coverage changes the
asserted guarantee. Passing a likelihood under a merely predictive model into an intervention-
value operation changes the scientific interpretation even earlier. The proposed common
contract should keep these tags and premises with the object, so a consumer can compare a
conditional structural calculation with a design-based alternative without presenting them as
interchangeable evidence.
   This suggests a useful progression for the first numerical-provider trial. Start with a finite
model whose state law and value are exactly enumerable. Check the declared approximation and
the monetary mapping against that reference. Next vary numerical settings while keeping the
target fixed, recording error, memory and time separately. Only then consider a richer model
with an explicit replacement for the finite reference. Such a sequence tests the connection’s
mathematical meaning at low cost. It leaves the intervention assumptions and the usefulness
of its precision visible for a later substantive decision.

40.7      Discovery: carry ambiguity through to the financial deci-
          sion
A model or discovery provider can return candidate diagrams without identifying the effect
requested by a business user. The proposed DynareMCP exchange would retain variable defini-
tions, equation and observation timing, and distinctions between structural and measurement
variables. The inspected documentation supported selected CLI/in-process operation while
withholding strict stdio qualification (Causal banking research project, 2026ag). Thus even
the transport requires a selected implementation and trial. More fundamentally, neither the
presence of structural equations nor the name of a discovery method proves that an imported
edge has an intervention interpretation.
  A two-variable example isolates the issue. Let binary variables A, Y have joint probabilities
                                         Y =0 Y =1
                                     A=0  0.4  0.1 .
                                     A=1  0.1  0.4
One causal model generates A fairly and then generates Y with probability 0.2 + 0.6A, using
an independent uniform disturbance. Its graph is A → Y , and E{Y | do(A = 1)} − E{Y |
do(A = 0)} = 0.6. A second model generates Y fairly and then A with probability 0.2 + 0.6Y .
Its graph is Y → A, and intervening on A changes no equation for Y , giving effect zero.
Multiplication of the two factors verifies exactly the same joint table in both models (Causal
banking research project, 2026j).
   In the absence of additional information distinguishing these two models, the proposed
consumer must preserve both effects. If one additional recovery is valued at 100 USD and an
intervention costs 20 USD per person, the two net values are 40 and −20 USD. Their signs
differ. Choosing the arrow that produces a favorable investment case would therefore answer a
different, more restricted question. The set {0, 0.6} describes these two specified models; it is
not asserted to exhaust all latent-variable models or to be a sampling confidence region. Its
convex hull [0, 0.6] is a conservative outer summary, but loses the fact that interior effects do
not occur in this particular two-model calculation.
   This example is deliberately free of sampling error. More observations from the same joint
law cannot distinguish the two constructions. A real time ordering, an intervention, or a
justified scientific restriction can change the admissible class. For example, a documented action
that genuinely precedes the outcome would rule out this literal reverse-time model, although it
would not by itself exclude hidden common causes. A language model’s preferred wording, a
graph rendering package or a high prediction score supplies none of those restrictions. Evidence
used to remove a graph must be retained with the result and with the specific variables and
time indices it concerns.
   The discovery-to-effect exchange should consequently carry a class object: node identities
and measurement definitions, endpoint semantics, permitted latent variables and time lags,
restrictions with sources, and the exact analysis target. A causal operation can return effects
across admitted graphs when its identification domain supports them. Otherwise it should
return the supported partial result and a reason for withholding the effect. A consumer that
accepts only a single DAG needs an explicit narrowing decision; selecting one representative
for display must not narrow the scientific object passed to estimation. The Java and R adapter
discussions earlier in the book develop this same requirement for specific graph representations.
   MathDevMCP has a possible supporting role at the derivation boundary: a client could
send a located identity, its assumptions and a requested verification operation, then retain the
returned evidence. The inspected project distinguishes specialist verification from generated

derivations and has restricted-use terms (Causal banking research project, 2026ag). A computer
algebra check of the two factorizations can confirm their arithmetic. It cannot certify that
a bank’s actual data were generated by either model. The simplest exact enumeration is
sufficient for the present counterexample; a general mathematical service would need to justify
its additional complexity.

mapping error, the variance of their sum is 400; if they are independent, it is 200. The marginal
correctness description is identical. An exchange that gives each record its own independent

error bar would discard consequential shared uncertainty. The adapter should retain common
origin, household, calendar and mapping identifiers when the selected analysis requires them.
A common identifier indicates a possible dependence structure; it is not itself an estimated
covariance or a proof of an independence assumption.
   For the proposed trustworthy-observation journey, the consumer first states the target
population and information clock. The source provider then exports records, inclusion and
label declarations, unresolved ownership or matching alternatives, and accepted/rejected
counts. The causal layer checks whether its method supports that information structure and
returns either an analysis with the retained limitations or a precise missing-information result.
Its displayed output must show when the conclusion is conditional on supplied selection or
truth assertions. Authentic ownership and bank gold labels remain requirements for later
claims about a bank; the present proposal can demonstrate these logical distinctions using
public or generated information without pretending to have obtained that validation.
   Research-assistant can help locate documentation and original papers for these declarations
through its inspected read-only source-navigation route (Causal banking research project,
2026ag). A source summary should point back to a genuine local document and the inspected
technical passage. It cannot replace the paper, prove a data owner’s assertion or authorize a
new use of the records. This separates a useful navigation integration from the much stronger
and unsupported claim of automated scientific truth reconstruction.


40.9     Population changes are new analyses
Two consumers can agree on an action and outcome while referring to different customers. A
marketing sample might overrepresent a low-risk stratum, while a servicing portfolio contains
mostly a high-risk stratum. Changing the population field in a copied result would not transport
the effect. The inspected FinMarketing comparison requires equality of population, action,
comparator, origin, horizon, finance owner and capital convention when mapping an estimate
into value; its comment explicitly distinguishes a unit conversion from reaggregation across
populations or horizons (Causal banking research project, 2026k). This gives the proposed
adapter a useful boundary: a new population needs a new target identity and a scientific
bridge.
   For a simple bridge, let X have two strata, let P describe the source population and Q the
target population, and suppose the stratum-specific causal effect τ (x) is invariant between
them. This is an explicit transport assumption in addition to identification of the source
effects. If Q(x) > 0 only where P (x) > 0, then
                                  X                 X           Q(x)
                           τQ =       Q(x)τ (x) =       P (x)         τ (x).              (40.9)
                                  x                 x           P (x)

The equality is algebraic under the stated assumptions. It does not establish that equal
stratum names represent equal measurements, that treatment versions match, or that effects
are in fact invariant. Different credit limits, collection actions or outcome definitions would
require a richer bridge.
   Suppose the stratum effects are 10 and −10 USD per account. With source weights (0.8, 0.2)
the source average is 6 USD. With target weights (0.2, 0.8) the target average is −6 USD.
Density ratios (1/4, 4) reproduce the second value from the source distribution exactly (Causal
banking research project, 2026i). The sign reversal arises entirely from the population change,
despite identical conditional effects. A common API and identical units would not prevent it.
If the second stratum is absent from the source, the target result is not obtained by capping
390                                                             Causal analysis for a universal bank


the missing ratio. One must restrict the target, supply additional information or retain a
bound under stated restrictions.
   For a FinMarketing or SMEwallet consumer, a proposed export should therefore identify the
unit of analysis, stratum construction, population counts or weights, intervention versions and
availability of the variables used to transport. If estimated effects and estimated population
weights are combined, their joint uncertainty matters. Splitting the two estimates across
repositories does not make their errors independent. A consumer can instead request a
conditional planning calculation with fixed supplied weights, but the response should preserve
that qualification. The public-data and transport chapters discuss alternatives when the
desired bank population is not yet observed; a bank-specific transported claim is not needed
to develop this proposal.


40.10       Portfolio composition and the ownership of cash flows
Separate funding and servicing effects also need not add. Consider a generated net-value table
for one common account, with funding action F and servicing action S:

                      Y (f, s) = 100 + 20f + 15s − 30f s,       f, s ∈ {0, 1}.

All entries are stipulated economic net values at one common horizon. The funding-only effect
is 20, the servicing-only effect is 15, and their joint effect is 5. In general the decomposition is

       E{Y (1, 1) − Y (0, 0)} = E{Y (1, 0) − Y (0, 0)} + E{Y (0, 1) − Y (0, 0)} + I,        (40.10)
                            I = E{Y (1, 1) − Y (1, 0) − Y (0, 1) + Y (0, 0)}.               (40.11)

Here I = −30. Adding two correct isolated analyses and reporting 35 would omit the interaction.
The example does not assert that this particular interaction occurs in a bank; it demonstrates
information the proposed portfolio consumer needs before combining answers.
   Suppose the funding population has 100 accounts, the servicing population 60, and 20
accounts receive both actions. Assume the table applies to each account, memberships are
fixed before treatment, and accounts do not affect one another. The total incremental value is

                                80(20) + 40(15) + 20(5) = 2300,

equivalently 100(20) + 60(15) + 20(−30). The naive separate total is 2900. Both evaluations
and the equivalent decomposition are reproduced exactly (Causal banking research project,
2026i). Origin identities are consequently needed for more than deduplication: they establish
which units receive the joint policy. If linkage is uncertain, both membership and the relevant
interaction can be uncertain. If there are spillovers, the no-interference assumption used by
this sum must be replaced rather than hidden in the aggregation code.
   Before adding monetary outputs, the consumer must also identify whose cash flow is being
counted. An internal transfer of 3 USD recorded as income by a branch and cost by treasury
sums to zero for the consolidated bank. The branch target and bank target are different
even though both are expressed in USD. Similarly, a principal repayment may be relevant to
liquidity while not being income under the declared net-contribution measure. A state-space
quantity called a balance and a financial quantity called contribution cannot be combined
merely because both have currency units.
   The inspected FinMarketing CashEntry distinguishes revenue, cost, principal, liability,
internal transfer and continuation, and retains owner, cash date, economic basis and inclusion
in value. Its Value validation restricts owner and currency to the target, disallows principal,
40.11. Standalone analysis and the three transport surfaces                                  391


liability and internal transfer from the net-contribution measure, and requires continuation
to start after the explicit cash horizon (Causal banking research project, 2026i). These
are concrete source declarations for a proposed valuation bridge. They do not establish all
accounting policies or cross-consumer correctness. CardNPV’s continuation convention still
requires its own mapping, and a loan cash-flow valuation may legitimately include principal
under a different explicitly declared target.
   A proposed combined workflow should therefore retain event and account identity, economic
owner, category, horizon and intervention membership before aggregation. A manual reference
can reconcile one small joint scenario across the consumer records. An automated adapter
should reproduce that result and retain the same exceptions when an owner, horizon or
intervention changes. When a bridge cannot be justified, separate displayed values with a clear
non-additivity qualification are more informative than a fabricated total. The resulting next
experiment is specific: determine whether the consequential joint response or population bridge
is adequately supported, rather than repeating isolated analyses whose individual accuracy
cannot resolve it.


40.11      Standalone analysis and the three transport surfaces
The same target restrictions apply when no sibling repository is involved. A notebook, a
batch script and a compatible MCP client are three ways of submitting an analysis; none
supplies missing causal premises. The library and CLI chapter gives a complete supported
request and its observed output, including conditional identification and set-valued sensitivity
results. Its thirteen generated comparisons and associated lifecycle checks establish a bounded
local reference. Earlier MCP trials supply evidence for the specific inspected local protocol
and environment, rather than universal compatibility with every MCP client or HTTP
deployment (Causal banking research project, 2026g,ah).
   For notebook use, the analyst should construct the target and scientific context explicitly,
save the full request and response, and derive any displayed scalar from the response’s declared
type. The funding scenario above is naturally a small local calculation: all cash assumptions
and coefficient interpretations are visible. A notebook cell that keeps only the midpoint of a
value range would make its positive result appear more certain than the underlying information
supports. If a graph analysis returns the two-model effect set, a chart should preserve the
two alternatives or explicitly label an outer interval, rather than reporting their average as an
identified estimate.
   For batch use, a manifest should give one immutable request identity per business question
and retain process exit status separately from the scientific disposition. The common CLI
accepts the operation and a versioned JSON request; the complete executable listing appears
in the preceding chapter. A batch coordinator should distinguish malformed input, unavailable
dependencies, supported refusal and a computed result. A nonzero process status does not
imply a scientifically zero effect; a zero process status does not imply identified benefit. The
aggregate report must keep counts of all submitted requests, including those with unavailable
results. Reporting average value only over successful calls changes the population whenever
success depends on difficult cases.
   For MCP use, the client first negotiates the actual protocol and discovers the available
operations. It should then submit the same bounded scientific request it would submit locally,
keeping human choices about targets and premises separate from tool mechanics. A tool can
return a well-formed unsupported or refused result. An agent must preserve that result and
the reason, rather than changing the sampling law or omitting the disputed premise to obtain
a number. The proposed graph and policy workflows may need operations outside the current
392                                                            Causal analysis for a universal bank


local implementation. Discovery of a tool name is not evidence that it supports those domains.
   The operating store is another boundary. Artifact handles are references under a declared
local custody root, not portable permissions to read any file with the same name. A consumer-
side export should preserve the content digest, source role and permission context while
mapping custody explicitly. The proposed client should not pass an arbitrary sibling path
as if every service could access it. For a service wrapper, ownership, authorization, schema
negotiation and request/result lifecycle must be designed for the actual deployment. Existing
local stdio evidence does not establish network isolation, multi-tenant behavior or a bank
service level.

40.13       Choosing the first connection and the next experiment
For this proposal, a reviewed manual artifact exchange is the baseline for each selected consumer.
It forces the target, units, clock and provenance to be written down before automation hides
them. The first automated connection should address a frequent, stable exchange whose
scientific interpretation is already defensible. FinMarketing’s source and target declarations
and dataml’s artifact-oriented CLI provide plausible observation interfaces; MacroFinance with
the selected BayesFilter authority provides a plausible funding route. Their priority follows
from the banking questions, not from an assertion that the mappings are complete.
  A planning comparison can make the choice explicit. If a manual process costs cm per
admitted request, an automated route costs ca , and design, verification and maintenance have
present cost F , then the cost advantage over n otherwise equivalent requests is

                                        n(cm − ca ) − F.

The expression requires equivalent output quality, exception handling and retention; it is not
a measured return on investment. If cm ≤ ca , volume alone does not pay for the automation.
If an adapter reduces meaningful translation errors, their expected loss can be added only
with an explicit error model, baseline and uncertainty. Runtime for one arithmetic call omits
human specification, source review, data transfer, failures and revision cost.
   The immediate informative experiment is therefore a small matched workflow trial on public
or clearly generated requests. Measure the manual and automated routes against the same
independently declared targets, preserve every refusal and failed request, and include the
adverse cases in Table 40.2. Record where the actual consumer changes or drops a field.
This can justify building a broader adapter without the cost of an authentic bank study. A
394                                                          Causal analysis for a universal bank


later bank experiment is warranted when the unresolved question concerns representative
assignment, ownership, precision, operating fit or value that the public trial cannot establish.
   The reusable contribution is the target-preserving workflow, including its failure states.
The exact examples are reproducible with tools/workspace_examples_20261013.py, using
a fresh output path; they require only the Python standard library and execute no sibling
code (Causal banking research project, 2026j). They demonstrate why the proposed interfaces
need the information specified here. Actual adapter conformance, consumer acceptance and
bank applicability require their own evidence, attached to the selected implementation and
the claim being made.
