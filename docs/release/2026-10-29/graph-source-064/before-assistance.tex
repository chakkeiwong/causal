\chapter{Which tasks merit language assistance?}
\label{rewrite:assistance-tasks}

Assistance should be selected by the task it improves. A system that answers
questions about a supplied causal mechanism has not thereby shown that
it can discover a defensible mechanism for a new bank. The evidence
synthesis in Section~\ref{scisyn:assistance} and the detailed studies
support a restricted comparison, with a questionnaire and ordinary search
as serious alternatives. They do not establish a measured saving for this
proposed product.

\section{Formulation and variable construction}
For formulation, the useful output is a precise intervention, alternative,
population, outcome, horizon and information date, together with unresolved
choices. The retained Kıcıman and causal-learn+ discussions motivate
question clarification and graph critique. Their descriptions support
a prototype candidate, not the truth of bank-specific assumptions.
\citep{v12_la_2305_00050,v12_la_2606_23608}
The strongest elementary comparator is a structured questionnaire completed
by an economist. Measure material omissions found and the time required
to correct the final question. The deposit distinction among assignment,
receipt, acceptance and price is an explicit test case. An eloquent
answer that silently chooses one is a failure.

Variable construction requires a separate assessment. A model can suggest
a record field or an extraction rule, but measurement validity depends
on the relation between that field and the scientific variable. The
retained text-and-causal-inference review discusses text used for adjustment;
it does not prove that a modern embedding removes confounding.
\citep{exec13_census_text_review_arxiv}
Compare any proposed extraction with a documented coding rule and
independently labelled examples, preserving missing and ambiguous cases.
The observation journey shows why high predictive accuracy or apparent
completeness is insufficient. Begin with auditable field definitions and
retrieval assistance; an autonomous scientific measurement claim remains
unsupported without independent validation.

\section{Diagrams and numerical discovery}
For diagram work, require evidence and timing for each proposed relationship
and retain competing explanations. LACR's reported retrieval effects vary
across its examples. The retained inspection records both improvement and
deterioration, including the ASIA skeleton comparison and the SACHS
comparison when PC output is added. This is evidence for evaluating the
retrieval choice, rather than assuming that more context improves the graph.
\citep{v12_la_2402_15301}
Compare the assistant with ordinary search plus an economist and, where
its assumptions fit, a numerical discovery procedure. The inputs must
be matched. Assess false orientations, missed common causes and the
resulting effect or action, rather than relying solely on edge counts.

The first assistant should propose a diagram and competing explanations
for review, then call a named discovery or identification method under
explicit assumptions. Numerical discovery contributes its own evidence;
textual plausibility cannot turn an unresolved equivalence class into
a validated graph. The three-model pricing example is a useful adverse
case because one unjustified orientation changes the financial sign.

\section{A positive workflow result and its limits}
The retained ORCA study gives a more direct reason to test assistance in a
completed workflow. Twelve participants used a counterbalanced within-subjects
design; the baseline assistant already had tools, step gates and minimal
state. The paper's Figure 5 reports total task durations of 1,395 seconds
for the baseline and 1,107 seconds for ORCA, a reported reduction of about
21\%. Table 1 reports completion rates 0.75 and 0.83. This is positive
published evidence for a structured workflow on the study's relational,
generated causal tasks. It is not a measured benefit for this banking
proposal. \citep[pp.~3--5]{exec13_census_orca}

The interpretation changes when the outcome definitions and all attempted
analyses are retained. Completion means producing an ATE, regardless of
accuracy; discovery success requires the treatment and outcome nodes to be
present. The paper's confidence-interval metric is described as an estimated
ATE falling within a ground-truth interval, so its reported 0.083 and 0.50
must not be presented as conventional repeated-sample confidence coverage.
The full-data condition comparison for absolute ATE error has $p=0.321$;
after excluding configuration-error outliers, the reported OLS comparison
has $p=0.020$. Both belong in an assessment of completed work because
configuration errors are among the failures a service is supposed to avoid.
The abstract's claimed 42-percentage-point completion gain also differs
from the displayed 0.75-to-0.83 comparison; this synthesis uses the stated
table comparison rather than reconciling that discrepancy by invention.
\citep[pp.~1, 5, 17--18]{exec13_census_orca}

The proposal should therefore test whether shared intermediate results and
question clarification save full effort while preserving scientific quality.
It should retain all configuration failures and report separately the time
spent by the analyst, tool execution and correction. The external study
supports that experiment's plausibility. Its small participant sample,
specified tasks, outcome definitions and analysis exclusions prevent a
claim that the same benefit is established across bank studies.

\section{Identification, execution and reporting}
CLadder evaluates formal causal questions under constructed mechanisms;
its reported results concern historical models and that supplied task.
It does not measure elicitation of a new bank mechanism.
\citep{v12_la_2312_04350}
For identification, compare assistance with a defined formal procedure,
requiring the assistant to preserve both the answer and its conditions.
An appropriate refusal must explain the missing restriction, not simply
return an empty result. The benchmark literature and the retained
mechanism-clustering critiques give reasons to keep failed cases and
uncertainty denominators in the evaluation.

For execution, the existing local interface study shows equality for four
constructed requests, not model-driven analysis quality. A scripted
workflow is the credible baseline. Compare any assistant on request
construction, faithful tool use, errors detected and correction effort.
Do not give it credit for a calculation performed by the formal tool
while omitting the costs of choosing or correcting the request.

For reporting, a deterministic template is a strong comparator because
it can already state the estimand, assumptions, interval and scope.
The assistant's proposed value is explaining why the result changes a
decision or why it cannot yet support one. Check that explanation
against the actual result and the retained sources. A report that turns
a set crossing zero into a positive recommendation fails even if its
prose is persuasive. No local live-model evaluation establishes an
incremental benefit for this reporting task.

The preferred first assisted task is therefore question clarification
with evidence retrieval and explicit alternatives, evaluated against
the questionnaire. Identification and numerical results remain delegated
to defined procedures. Expansion to autonomous discovery, variable
construction or recommendations requires task-specific evidence. The
frontier evidence includes the three additional inspected originals discussed
in Section~\ref{gap29:frontier}. The preceding inventory recorded 85 inspected
and 28 unread entries. The financing-and-exports and epistemology originals
now developed in Section~\ref{closure30:formulation} bring this edition to
87 inspected and 26 unread. The financing study supports a bounded extraction
and explanation comparison; its scores do not identify a lending effect.
The epistemology discussion supplies no separate intervention estimate. These
readings sharpen the limits of assistance without establishing live banking
benefit. The remaining omissions still limit broad claims. A live comparison
requires a fixed model version, held-out questions, matched information,
independent adjudication and a budget.~\citep{rel18li,rel18mugleston}
