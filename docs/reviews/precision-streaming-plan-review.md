# Pre-execution review of the V10 continuation

10 October 2026. Author review of the plan; not independent acceptance.

The V9 plan is complete and its failures remain the baseline. Repeating its
unchanged studies would not close D09 or D06. The proposed continuation is
accepted with these resolutions before final evaluation:

1. Select an eight-cell grid that changes evaluation information, audit size,
   persistence and near-rank information without claiming factorial coverage.
   Nested 4T/8T pilots allow a paired information-cost comparison. Both satisfy
   the existing sufficient rate; neither is called a demonstrated repair.
2. Set 2,000 requests per cell using stated binomial precision near .95. The
   worst-case precision is looser; show it. Paired effects may remain unresolved.
   Do not select repetitions, penalties or covariance from final coverage.
3. Preserve all-request coverage when the audit rank guard refuses inference.
   Conditional coverage alone cannot qualify a weak-information design.
4. Register false exogeneity separately. Numeric declarations cannot verify the
   causal innovation law; streaming agreement cannot rescue a false model.
5. Preserve exact pilot semantics: chronological halves, training variances,
   averaging across target columns, first minimizing penalty and refit on the
   entire pilot. A different tuning loss would be a new statistical procedure.
6. Avoid subtracting large raw second moments for training variance. Merge
   centered block moments. Obtain residual losses from QR factors to avoid
   cancellation in a Gram-matrix residual formula. Check dense coefficients,
   loss ordering, financial contrasts and both covariance choices.
7. Compare awkward block sizes including one row, split boundaries and chunks
   shorter than the horizon. Test invalid pilot size, rank, scale, counts,
   declarations, nonfinite values and existing refusal statuses.
8. A full dense comparison of every Monte Carlo request would waste work.
   Qualify numerical equivalence with independent fixtures first; keep the
   final procedure fixed. Pilot paths and input specifications are bound into
   every journal, and summaries must reconstruct exactly from saved requests.
9. Measure the full pilot plus inference process, not only QR. Two sizes and
   reversed execution order expose fixed costs; no speed gain is acceptable.
   Keep preparation in a separate process and verify a small supervisor RSS.
10. Archive V9 before changes, update scholarly and operational entry points,
    inspect the new PDFs and bind a current manifest. External acceptance and
    broader mathematical extensions remain explicitly deferred.

Disposition: execute J00–J05 under these conditions. Coverage compatibility is
conditional evidence, not proof of calibration; file identity is not independent
scientific review. No known universal correction or institutional evidence is
being silently substituted by this plan.
