\section{What an elicited Bayesian prior actually buys}
\label{exec18:bayesian-advice}
A bank considering an LLM-assisted deposit diagram needs more than an answer to whether the proposed arrows look plausible. It needs to know which uncertainty distribution the answers define, how that distribution interacts with transaction data, and which observations could discredit the combination. Videira's corrected master's thesis provides a useful concrete case: it combines elicited edge probabilities with DiBS, then considers feedback to a score-based ancestral-graph generator. These are different graph domains and different uses of advice. The thesis was defended on23 February2026 and the university records publication on19 March2026, before the declared literature cutoff. The comparison here uses the genuine corrected thesis, including its experimental tables and appendices; it does not independently reproduce either upstream system~\citep{exec18_videira_thesis}.

\subsection{Response frequency is an elicitation quantity}
The first construction queries each variable pair ten times and estimates frequencies of three responses: one direction, the other direction, or no relation. The text replaces probabilities one and zero by0.99 and$10^{-10}$, respectively, then uses products of edge and nonedge factors as a graph prior. It reports30 starting particles and3000 DiBS iterations. These choices specify a finite elicitation and fitting procedure; they do not establish that a frequency of0.9 is a90\% probability that the causal assertion is true~\citep{exec18_videira_thesis}.

To see the distinction, fix a prompt and a response mechanism which selects one arrow with probability$9/10$. The mechanism can be identical in a world where that arrow is true and a world where it is false. Repeating the prompt estimates the response mechanism in both worlds, without distinguishing their truth. More draws reduce sampling variation in the response-frequency estimate under an appropriate sampling law. They do not identify adviser accuracy, and correlated draws can reduce even that benefit. In a banking proposal, frequency should therefore be stored as an elicitation statistic together with its prompt, model identity and sampling scheme. A calibrated truth channel needs additional evidence on suitably independent labeled questions~\citep{exec18_bayesian_reference}.

The semantic categories also require repair before direct adoption. The thesis's first prompt offers no statistical association as one response and direct causal directions as the alternatives. No direct edge and no statistical association are different statements. For example, an offer and a subsequent balance can be associated through a pre-existing customer-risk variable even if neither directly causes the other. A proposed adapter must define whether it queries adjacency, ancestry, conditional independence, or an intervention effect, then permit outcomes appropriate to that question. A forced three-way answer cannot silently supply all four kinds of information. This is a limitation of the inspected wording, not a newly observed performance result~\citep{exec18_videira_thesis}.

\subsection{Graph admission changes the distribution}
An edge-factor expression is not yet a distribution over admissible causal graphs unless its support and normalizer are specified. For a deliberately small example, let the two possible arrows between two variables be independent Bernoulli indicators with probability$1/2$ each. The unconstrained model gives four configurations equal probability. Rejecting the two-cycle leaves the empty graph and the two single-arrow graphs, each with conditional probability$1/3$. Consequently
\begin{equation}
 \Pr(A\to Y\mid\text{acyclic})=\frac{1/4}{3/4}=\frac13,
 \label{exec18:dag-conditioning}
\end{equation}
although the original edge parameter was$1/2$. The exact reference enumerates these four configurations. A consumer must distinguish the elicited edge parameter from a marginal under the admitted joint graph law~\citep{exec18_bayesian_reference}.

The thesis samples30 graph realizations for its reported graph metrics and rejects cyclic realizations. That makes the admitted count and the conditional sampling rule important. The inspected account does not establish a distribution-free uncertainty guarantee from those30 draws. Nor can one assume that independent priors on the two opposing directions preserve a mutually exclusive pairwise response model. A reuse design should record the pair distribution, the graph construction, the legal support, rejection counts and any subsequent renormalization explicitly. The earlier joint-graph discussion explains why individually plausible edges need not form a coherent accepted graph~\citep{exec18_videira_thesis}.

\subsection{The reported comparison is mixed}
The thesis evaluates Sachs, MAGIC-7, MAGIC-ORIGINAL and an authored Summer construction. MAGIC-7 removes genetic variables from the larger dataset and is deliberately treated as a case with omitted confounding; the selected DAG learner's assumptions are therefore an essential part of interpreting its performance. The following are reported posterior mean expected structural Hamming distances, abbreviated E-SHD, from Table3.2. Lower is better for this metric. They are source-reported means, not new runs or a ranking of general banking suitability~\citep{exec18_videira_thesis}.

The GPT-prior differences relative to baseline are$-1.70$, $2.31$, $7.05$ and$0.25$, respectively. The DeepSeek-prior differences are$3.30$, $2.48$ and$-2.49$ where reported. These exact differences of printed means reproduce separately; they are not significance tests. The printed error bars are not converted here into standard errors or confidence intervals because that interpretation requires their actual experimental denominator and dependence structure~\citep{exec18_bayesian_reference}. On MAGIC-ORIGINAL, the reported mean AUROC also declines from0.62 to0.60 with the GPT prior. On Summer, the DeepSeek prior improves both reported metrics. The defensible conclusion is a conditional opportunity with adverse examples, rather than uniform improvement from adding an LLM~\citep{exec18_videira_thesis}.

Two source statements deserve particular care. The discussion calls AUROC0.5 the worst possible value. A perfectly reversed binary ranking instead has AUROC0, as the two-observation exact reference demonstrates. The final discussion also describes the combined method as better than the original prior in terms that are broader than the table supports. The proposal follows the tabulated, dataset-specific evidence. It retains the author's concern about known datasets in model training, sparse graphs favoring some SHD comparisons, and violated data-generating assumptions. A favorable prior cannot by itself repair a misspecified likelihood~\citep{exec18_videira_thesis,exec18_bayesian_reference}.

\subsection{Feedback to an ancestral-graph generator has another contract}
The second construction uses an ancestral-graph GFlowNet with a score-based reward, graph-validity constraints and expert feedback. The thesis describes four relationship categories, a confidence parameter for each queried pair, and a categorical channel that places probability$\pi$ on the reported true category and$(1-\pi)/3$ on each alternative. It uses repeated model responses to supply the confidence. Its distributional assessment uses20 generated five-node graphs with500 observations each. The separate feedback experiment uses reduced Sachs graphs and reports three simulations in the figure caption~\citep{exec18_videira_thesis}.

For a transparent conditional calculation, take prior category probabilities$(0.4,0.3,0.2,0.1)$, channel accuracy$\pi=0.7$, and feedback selecting category2. The unnormalized posterior weights are$(0.04,0.21,0.02,0.01)$, summing to0.28, so
\begin{equation}
 \rho(\cdot\mid f=2)=\left(\frac17,\frac34,\frac1{14},\frac1{28}\right).
 \label{exec18:feedback-update}
\end{equation}
This is a valid update under the stipulated channel. If0.7 merely measures repeated-answer consistency, the assumed likelihood is not thereby verified. Moreover, pairwise relationship marginals do not determine every dependence in the joint graph distribution. An adapter needs to specify the graph-level update and validate its scientific interpretation~\citep{exec18_bayesian_reference}.

The thesis reports improved E-SHD as feedback accumulates in its selected Sachs experiments, while noting worse unreported DeepSeek trials. Those observations do not prove monotone improvement or convergence to the true graph for arbitrary advisers. The selected reward is a transformation of a fit score; accurate sampling from that reward establishes fidelity to the chosen target, not causal truth or frequentist graph coverage. The relation vocabulary also needs an explicit reconciliation: the mathematical description uses ancestry while parts of the elicitation wording request direct relationships. The source records both descriptions; the proposal does not silently equate them~\citep{exec18_videira_thesis}.

\subsection{A bounded reuse and evaluation decision}
The thesis is valuable as a design and adverse-result source. Its AppendixA supplies an R data-generation listing and AppendixB gives DSPy field declarations. These are not a complete pinned package, trained generator, reproducible prompt transcript or dependency manifest. In particular, the R listing draws random structural parameters before its displayed seed call, and requests2000 observations while the body describes2001. Without the missing initialization or saved parameters, that listing alone does not reproduce the exact structural model. A preserving reproduction should retain these discrepancies, specify a seed before every relevant draw, save the realized parameters, and declare its actual sample count as a new experiment~\citep{exec18_videira_thesis}.

The proposed disposition is therefore to adopt the separation of elicitation, graph admission and updating as a design pattern; retain DiBS and ancestral-graph methods as distinct backend candidates; and defer executable integration until exact source versions, code rights, prompts, graph encodings and experiment artifacts are inspected. The thesis's CC BY4.0 notice supports attribution to its text but is not automatically a license for every external dependency or model service. No downloaded code is executed here.

A useful inexpensive prototype compares a data-only learner, elicited advice alone and the combined method under the same graph law and budget. It should include correct and wrong advice, correlated repeated answers, causal-sufficiency violations, unfamiliar variable names, rejected graph samples and full attempted-query costs. Graph scores should be supplemented with the resulting intervention-query decisions and abstentions. In a deposit application, choosing the wrong adjustment relation can change the monetary target even when only one edge is wrong. The evaluation must preserve that consequence. Authentic bank labels and costly experiments follow only after a scoped public/generated comparison shows merit; no current evidence warrants a universal improvement, calibrated semantic truth or bank-adoption claim.
