# Remaining gaps after the joint-candidate follow-up

The standard remains the [master program's](../program/master-program.md)
connected banking target-to-identification-to-estimation-to-uncertainty
argument, inspected primary sources, reproducible financial examples,
substantive alternatives and honest diagnostics and limits. A completed
author proposal is distinct from a qualified reusable bank product,
independent review, authentic validation and adoption. The frozen V10
[D01--D10 register](../program/deferred-gaps-2026-10-10.md) remains the full
obligation list; the two 11 October supplements add evidence without rewriting
that historical register.

**D09: useful calibrated precision remains open.** The first supplement
represented audit ambiguity without a rank cutoff. This follow-up uses
candidate covariance and narrows every one of 2,937 paired finite sets in six
selected fresh designs. It does not create more finite sets at the original
near-rank size: 558/600 are still whole-line. Eight times as many audit
labels reduce that count to 105/600, but finite transformed median width is
about USD 15.4 million. This is additional unpriced information, not a free
algorithmic cure. The exact audit component cannot make the response bands
finite-sample exact or guarantee useful financial precision. Next, agree a
decision-width threshold and authentic audit/pilot prices; derive and register
any new simultaneous region or information design before independent draws.
Retain all-request coverage, finite availability, width, bias and cost under
small audit, near rank, persistence and false-law stress. Do not promote the
preserved unqualified run.

**D04: causal information remains open.** In the false-law cell the two sets
covered 597/600 and 561/600, yet the action innovation restriction was false.
The checked pathwise equivalent laws have the same observations but financial
targets about USD 387,575 apart when the nuisance mean may adjust. Obtain
documented assignment/exogeneity evidence, randomized or instrumental
variation, or another defensible identifying restriction and representative
truth; then specify and test the changed observation law. More identity audit
labels or narrower confidence boxes alone cannot distinguish those two laws.

**Other scientific scope, D01--D03 and D05--D06, is unchanged.** Singular
individual contrasts need outcome-linked truth or a new identified likelihood;
nonlinear private-state, general temporal-graph, persistent identity/calendar
and arbitrary-learner extensions each need their own identification, sampling,
rate and numerical arguments. The selected streaming pilot is not a general
learner or multi-host qualification.

**External D07/D08/D10 work needs external inputs.** Name an independent
reviewer, source and audit owners, platform operator and interface/workflow
participants. Freeze authentic bank inputs and truth, a precision threshold,
representative history/audit/gold prices, a target hardware/fault protocol
and held-out matched decisions. Run and sign the preserved
[external validation protocol](../program/external-validation-v9.md) before
claiming coverage in that bank, adopted interfaces, operational service levels
or savings. None of these facts is supplied by another synthetic run or PDF.
