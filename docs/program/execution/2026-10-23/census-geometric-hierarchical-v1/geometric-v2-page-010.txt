5     Identification and Estimation
The goal of causal modeling is to understand the effects of interventions. We observe the world as
it is, but want to predict the result of a change. Causal inference methods reason from observation
to intervention by first asking what we could learn from infinite data, and then approximating it
from the finite data we have at hand. The usual logic is:
