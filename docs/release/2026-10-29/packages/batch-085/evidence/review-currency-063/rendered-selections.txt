What the bank should buy first

A bank considering a better deposit rate must pay for money it already holds as well as money
the offer attracts. A collections team must account for how today’s contact changes tomorrow’s
information. A discovery algorithm may return several explanations of the same observations,
with opposite implications for a pricing decision. An apparently clean customer table may still
conceal misrecorded treatment or incorrectly linked accounts. These are four different reasons
why a persuasive numerical answer can fail to improve a decision.
   An increase in one account’s recorded balance can be a transfer from another account at
the same bank. The funding outcome must aggregate the intended household and distinguish
new external money from internal movement. Competitor repricing can also change balances
during the same period. A lender’s threshold comparison concerns the applicants affected at
that margin, while a treasury shock analysis must separate the external shock from the bank’s
own response. These economic distinctions determine which comparison is useful before a
statistical model is chosen.
   The proposal is to make a small class of defensible studies easier to conduct and compare.
The first investment should be a comparative integration using established methods, a precise
study description and a report that preserves uncertainty. A capable economist using existing
tools is the reference alternative. The proposed service earns a place only if the completed
study is better or cheaper at equivalent quality. Broad replacement of the bank’s statistical
estate is not justified by the evidence presented here.
   That conclusion follows from the examples, rather than from an assumed advantage of
a common interface. In the constructed offer study, a population balance effect is carried
through sampling uncertainty into annual contribution. The servicing calculation shows why a
correctly fitted regression can still answer the wrong policy question. The graph example gives
identical observed laws with different intervention values. The observation example shows how
additional measurement knowledge can reverse a financial conclusion. Each case tells us what
a service must preserve and what it must decline to infer.
   The evidence has different roles. Established causal results justify inference under stated
assumptions. The project’s exact derivations and generated studies expose target errors,
weak support and unresolved models. Public data reveal realistic timing and measurement
difficulties. Local interface executions establish that particular requests retain their meaning
through particular clients. None of these substitutes for an independently reviewed study on
authentic bank information. Their combined value is to identify a useful and bounded next
experiment before undertaking that more expensive work.

How to read the argument

The opening develops an annual deposit decision and four worked journeys. Their cross-
references distinguish exact supplied-law calculations, fitted public and generated evidence,
and unresolved identification. The longitudinal and panel foundations are followed by the
servicing and funding studies; the observation foundations lead directly into the linkage
bounds. The graph journey points to the restricted temporal confidence construction and
its additional-information argument. The discovery foundations also develop the completed
search-repair comparison in Section 20.14: a computational stopping rule must not be mistaken
for a scientifically established relationship.
   The evidence and method-choice part compares statistical targets before selecting software.
Chapter 26 is the authoritative software treatment: developed comparisons, the seventeen-area
selection, commercial alternatives and a target-weight illustration sit together. Chapter 27
brings formulation, measurement, benchmark evidence and task selection into one assistance
treatment. The subsequent detailed parts supply source-specific derivations and comparisons
for readers examining a particular method or operating route.
   The final investment part distinguishes the value of information from the value of a common
analytical product. Chapter 101 brings the annual benefit account, multi-year cost examples,
institutional variation and value-of-information calculation into one conditional decision. Its
recommendation is tested against expert composition and existing commercial capability.
PDF bookmarks expose the detailed sections; chapter and section references carry the reader
between the opening question and the supporting result.
   Throughout the opening case, assignment of an offer, delivery of that offer, acceptance and
the resulting price are separate quantities. Balances and contribution have different symbols
and a stated one-year horizon. Other journeys introduce their own units and decision dates
explicitly. A number from a constructed example is not a bank forecast. A method documented
in an inspected source is not thereby an executed integration. These distinctions are essential
to interpreting the proposal.
  Research coverage retains the cutoff of 7 October 2026. Editions, acquisition dates, inspected
anchors and omissions remain in the source records. The comparative recommendation does
not claim that every literature or language cell is complete. The unanswered questions that
could change the first investment are stated where that choice is made.

47.17.2     Centering the influence function before weighting and clustering

The selected uncertainty delegate sharpens the distinction between a policy value and the
uncertainty of that value. The inspected ife revision declares version 0.2.5 in July 2026 and
GPL version 3 or later. It stores a point estimate, a vector called the influence function,
weights, cluster identifiers and a critical value. Arithmetic operations propagate derivatives
through the vector. This is a useful interface for retaining the dependence between two policies
evaluated on the same observations. It does not construct the correct influence function from
an arbitrary prediction vector. The separately inspected lmtp revision permits ife versions
from 0.2.0; its resolved dependency is not observed here. The delegate’s own change log records
changes to cluster handling in 0.2.1 and 0.2.5, so the lower version bound cannot identify the
behavior of a particular installation. (Williams and contributors, 2026; Nicholas Williams,
Ivan Diaz and contributors, 2026)

47.17.3     A paired contrast must retain its scale and sampling identities

The delegate’s arithmetic is useful once the incoming influence representation is correct. A
difference subtracts corresponding contributions; a ratio uses the numerator derivative and the
denominator derivative jointly. This retains their covariance. Before combining two objects,
the inspected code requires equal vector lengths and identical weight and ID arrays. Those
checks help, but cluster labels do not uniquely identify the individuals within a cluster. The
delivery contract must bind each influence contribution to the same original observation across
fits, as well as to its cluster and weight. Reordering records while regenerating positional IDs
can pass an array-level check while changing the pairing. Such a change alters uncertainty
without changing either marginal point estimate. (Williams and contributors, 2026)
   The newly inspected lmtp contrast path computes relative-risk and odds-ratio contrasts
on the log scale, obtains a symmetric interval there and then exponentiates its endpoints. It
also exponentiates the point estimate, but leaves the returned std.error column on the log
scale. That is usable when documented; interpreting every numeric column as being on the
reported ratio scale is a conversion error. For a ratio of 1.5 and log-scale standard error 0.2,
the delta-method ratio-scale standard error is 0.3. A conventional normal critical value gives
an exponentiated log interval approximately [1.014, 2.220], compared with [0.912, 2.088] for a
symmetric interval formed on the ratio scale. Neither arithmetic construction proves finite-
sample coverage, especially near a weak denominator. The delegate’s rejection of denominators
smaller than machine epsilon is a numerical guard, not a test that a denominator is statistically
separated from zero. (Williams et al., 2026; Williams and contributors, 2026; Causal Banking
Research Project, 2026b)
   The caller warns when a numeric reference is used because an estimated reference treated as
known loses its uncertainty. Its print method warns about unadjusted multiple comparisons.
The delegate permits a configurable critical value but prints a fixed 95% interval label. A
bank-facing result therefore needs the actual contrast, scale of every uncertainty field, critical
value, sampling unit and comparison family. A method name or a formatted confidence label
should not supply these facts by implication. (Williams et al., 2026; Williams and contributors,
2026)
   The recommendation is conditional reuse of the arithmetic interface after qualifying the
incoming centered influence function, mean-weight normalization, individual pairing and
independent clusters. Compare a known outcome shift, a deterministic reward increment,
unequal cluster sizes, a single-cluster request and log-versus-ratio output against independent
laws before relying on a standard error. A simpler independent implementation of the declared
influence calculation is a serious alternative for a narrow initial worker; it must meet the
same statistical requirements. Native dependency resolution, the numerical variance delegate,
full survival handling, learner behavior and random-number coordination remain separate
qualification obligations. These source findings strengthen the admission conditions for the
longitudinal reference; they do not establish an executed package failure rate, calibrated bank
uncertainty or an observed business benefit. (Williams and contributors, 2026; Williams et al.,
2026; Causal Banking Research Project, 2026b)

47.20.4     Deterministic histories must change the fitting population

A simulator can enforce a structural rule while estimating the stochastic part of that rule on the
wrong records. The selected pygformula documentation describes a covariate restriction as a
known value outside specified histories. It directs the fitting step to exclude those deterministic
histories, then directs simulation to assign their known value. For a bank, eligibility may make
a service-completion indicator identically zero: the completion model then describes eligible
accounts, rather than an average that includes accounts unable to receive the service. This is
a distinction between two conditional distributions, even before any causal effect is considered.
(pygformula contributors, 2026b)
   The documented input is a list of restrictions, each beginning with the covariate’s name. In
the selected fitting helper, the list used to identify restricted covariates repeats the whole first
restriction instead of extracting each name. Its subsequent membership test compares a name
with those lists, so the documented input does not enter the intended filtering branch. The
simulation helper does extract the names and assigns deterministic values on the corresponding
histories. The constructor forwards the supplied restrictions directly. Thus the two steps
implement different populations on this selected path; this conclusion follows from inspected
source, not from an executed package experiment. It is specific to this revision and schema,
and does not assert that every fitted model changes numerically. (pygformula contributors,
2026b)
  An exact constructed likelihood shows when the distinction matters. Let E indicate eligibility,
Z completion and X a binary predictor. At a post-baseline fitting time, each value of X has
ten eligible records, eight with Z = 1 and two with Z = 0, and ten ineligible records with the
known value Z = 0. Use the logistic model

                             logit Pr(Z = 1 | X, E = 1) = β0 + β1 X.

Restricting estimation to eligible records gives fitted probability 4/5 in both predictor cells.
Pooling the deterministic zeros gives 2/5 in both. For either fit, the intercept and slope scores
vanish at the corresponding constant probability; both cells contain successes and failures, and
the information matrix is positive definite. These are finite, unique likelihood solutions, not
an appeal to an intercept-only formula that the input parser might handle differently. The
predictor is deliberately uninformative within eligibility; no claim is made about a model that
separately represents every eligibility history. (Causal Banking Research Project, 2026c)
   If half the simulated accounts are eligible and the other half are correctly set to zero,
the completion mean is 2/5 under restricted estimation but 1/5 under the pooled fit. A
stipulated USD 100 receipt contingent on completion and USD 30 cost per account give
expected margins of USD 10 and USD −10. This is a contract-payoff calculation under an
invented law, not an estimated causal service benefit. Increasing simulation size converges
more precisely to the respective fitted laws; it cannot repair their different fitting populations.
The proposed worker should therefore retain the exact fitting row set for each transition, verify
deterministic assignments separately, and withhold this restriction profile until a corrected
native path reproduces the declared law. An explicitly fitted stochastic-subpopulation model
is a substantive alternative, provided that its conditioning histories and standardization
population remain visible. (Causal Banking Research Project, 2026c; pygformula contributors,
2026b)

47.20.5    Censoring labels and competing risks define different denominators

The input clock places covariates on interval k and the outcome on interval k + 1. The selected
outcome helper uses nonmissing outcomes at nonnegative times; with explicit competing
events it also requires no current competing event. A censoring-name argument is not passed
to that helper. The censoring model instead supports the observed natural-course inverse
probability weighted comparator. Supplying that model therefore does not automatically
select the outcome likelihood rows. The documentation permits missing or zero outcomes on
a survival censoring row under its stated fine-interval qualification; for an end-of-follow-up
outcome it requests a missing value. These conventions concern which event information is
observed, not merely how a missing field is displayed. (pygformula contributors, 2026b)
   For a deliberately coarse interval, suppose 100 accounts supply eight observed defaults,
72 observed nondefaults and 20 outcomes unknown after censoring. A constant outcome
likelihood over observed outcomes gives 8/80 = 1/10; coding the unknown outcomes zero
gives 8/100 = 2/25. A stipulated contract paying USD 10,000 on default and charging USD
900 has model-based margins USD 100 and USD −100, respectively. Neither choice alone
identifies the complete finite-population risk: the 20 unknown outcomes permit a proportion
between 2/25 and 7/25. Interpreting the observed-outcome fit as a target risk additionally
requires an appropriate conditional independent-censoring assumption, support and model
specification. This construction does not contradict a small-interval approximation; it shows
why that approximation must be justified before applying the convention to coarse monthly
data. (Causal Banking Research Project, 2026c; pygformula contributors, 2026b)
  A competing event supplies different information. Consider 100 accounts with 20 pre-

ordered first, its probability is q = 1/5 and the conditional default probability among the 80
