## Numerical and implementation findings

- Exact HMM likelihood and all posterior count families agree with exhaustive
  latent-path sums within 4e-15, including the private logging law.
- The 375-path sequential check verifies correct-Q and correct-logging mean
  routes and recursive/expanded agreement. Both-wrong and current-only values
  differ from truth; the latter is a different incoming-history law.
- The longer main omitted-action fit meets the stopping criterion and still
  reverses policy gain. The repeated finite-budget fits retain 30/30 omitted
  and 7/30 joint caps; one bootstrap fit is capped. Neither table is called
  a certified global optimum or bank identification result.
- Deposit break-even is independently checked by subtracting the cash accounts.
  Reuse break-even is checked by substituting the threshold into cost difference.
- Score merges agree with independently centered batch calculations, including
  three repeated rows per entity/date intersection. Indefinite covariance is
  flagged, not silently clipped by the reusable reducer.
- The complete revised study suite was rerun after diagnostics/code changes.
  The old six-family examples replay within 1e-8; four valid contracts and ten
  invalid mutations pass their existing checks. All results are synthetic.
