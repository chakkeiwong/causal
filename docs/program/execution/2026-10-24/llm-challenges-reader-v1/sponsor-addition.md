## General LLM evidence becomes a controlled prototype question

The inspected 72-page Kaddour and colleagues survey is a July 2023 opinionated
review. It organizes prompt, knowledge, evaluation and reproducibility risks;
it does not establish a 2026 capability ranking. Its causal-reasoning discussion
reports different results on different tasks, and its behavioral and synthetic
data discussion preserves failures and representativeness concerns. Underlying
studies require original-source review before technical adoption.
\citep{exec24_llmchallenges_original}

Four exact authored examples connect those concerns to investment choices.
Three reviewers with a shared failure event have majority error 12.52%, against
9.4582% under an incorrect independence calculation. A generated borrower mixture
implies USD 5 net value per reminder, while another mixture under the same
stipulated response law implies negative USD 15. A two-factor prototype has a
20-percentage-point interaction, so isolated component gains do not predict
its combined result. With 100 initiated requests, 60 numeric outputs and 36
accepted numeric results, conditional acceptance is 60% but delivery per request
is 36%; stipulated full cost of USD 100 is approximately USD 2.78 per accepted
numeric result. These are constructed witnesses, not model measurements or prices.
Correct refusal remains separately scored.\citep{exec24_llmchallenges_reference}

The proposed next investment is a bounded comparison of deterministic templates,
retrieval and agent assistance on public documents and known constructed causal
worlds. Preserve task and version splits, clocks, source identities, refusals,
failed attempts and complete costs. CLI and MCP artifacts should carry these
semantics across translation, graph construction and identification. Public
tests can justify a later bank experiment; simulated people cannot supply bank
representativeness, and open weights cannot alone establish reproducibility.
