               5. Discussion
                   In this work, we developed and evaluated knowledge systems to perform
               knowledge-augmented causal discovery. These methods can aid in building
               causal graphs by combining data-driven approaches with LLMs leveraging
               their internal knowledge, external documents, and/or insights extracted from
               a KG to reason over causal relationships. Our results suggest that KG-RAG
               can effectively propose plausible causal relationships, and combining these
               knowledge systems with causal discovery algorithms can provide insight into
               a larger range of variables and relationships. Furthermore, the results sup-
               port the application of LLM-driven knowledge systems to aid in the causal
               modeling of cLBP through unifying domain knowledge and data driven anal-
               ysis.
