## Scientific design commitments

The joint target is a population mean action response, averaged over both
independent persistent pairs and a stationary common calendar. Pair audit
selection uses known probabilities; a separate representative gold sample may
estimate a symmetric label-error rate. The augmented score is formed per
pair/date. A two-way projection separates pair, calendar and interaction
variation. The primary covariance retains the pair and calendar terms
conservatively; it requires a declared joint asymptotic regime and nondegenerate
first-order variation. It is not an arbitrary two-way-clustering theorem.

Audit allocation minimizes a stated variance objective at a matched expected
budget using an independent fully audited pilot. Allocation is frozen before
evaluation. More audits do not remove common-calendar uncertainty; planning
must report that floor and the cost of estimating label error.

Funding calibration comparisons retain the old procedure. New bandwidths and
sample sizes are specified before fresh draws. Known-audit and oracle-response
arms diagnose contributions but cannot be deployed when their information is
unavailable. No procedure is selected from the final intervals or coverage.

For ordered Gaussian Z,A,Y episodes, the new sufficient-information operation
uses the exact conditional regression pivot. Valid randomized encouragement
uses a test inversion that permits zero first-stage rank. These are new
prespecified observed-law procedures, compared with the V7 covariance rectangle;
they are not post-hoc intersections of two 95% regions. The unrestricted latent
class keeps its constructive whole-line answer.

The nonlinear extension adds an action-specific transition innovation variance
to the audited model. The policy remains fixed independently of evaluation.
Audited transitions in both action groups identify the variance parameters.
Observation, logging, reward and initial-state contributions stay in the joint
likelihood. Numerical agreement and local regular intervals have explicit limits.

The learner extension fixes a dictionary including the previously omitted
interaction. Chunked QR preserves the dense least-squares problem without a
full expanded fitting matrix. Temporal gaps and score covariance remain part
of the statistical operation. Larger dictionaries and arbitrary learners are
excluded without new rate and approximation arguments.

## Execution and stopping rules

Use independent pilot and final seeds, save each replication or small batch,
and retain all unavailable fits. Report Monte Carlo uncertainty, widths and
costs, not coverage alone. Failed assumptions and failed computations have
different outcomes. A negative study completes an investigation but does not
qualify a capability. Freeze repaired procedures before any fresh follow-up.

Tool output and waits remain short; longer jobs run as resumable processes
with progress files. Do not repeat unchanged V7 studies. Do not contact other
people or change sibling repositories. External validation remains pending
until named participants, authentic data and target infrastructure exist.
