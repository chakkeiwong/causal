Review corrections and restrictions:

* ORCA's abstract completion gain is inconsistent with Table 1. The text uses
  the rounded table values and records the discrepancy. It reproduces the
  source's unusual CI-metric wording without silently converting it into
  standard repeated-sampling coverage. Its node-presence success metric,
  uncertain-edge orientation and sensitivity to removing configuration
  failures remain explicit. No runtime or library admission is claimed.
* CausalEval's prose/table numerical discrepancy, different sample sizes,
  repeated questions and mixed human-comparison provenance are retained.
  Tool gains are reported by benchmark, with adverse cases, not as a pooled
  universal improvement. Existing CLadder/CausalDS oracle concerns survive.
* Susanti--Holsmoelle assesses pairwise text relations. The narrower task and
  different annotation/training budgets prevent a graph-validation or
  cost-equivalent-superiority claim.
* Causal Agent's iterative tool interface is compared with a one-run code
  baseline; its cost sample excludes effect-level tasks. Its synthetic
  domain labels, effect tolerance and QRData subset are not bank validation.
* Augur is retained as forecasting/hypothesis generation; graph consensus,
  internal summary consistency and cycle removal do not identify an effect.
  Its explicit sufficiency and missed-lag limitations are not omitted.
* CausalCollab is a directly relevant longitudinal method. Its conditional
  theorem is not presented as independently reproduced or as a cure for
  unrecorded intent and support failure. The text distinguishes a functional
  mixture of text versions from an implementable rewriting policy, and
  identifies model-generated counterfactuals/outcome labels.
* CausalSmith's compiler claim, statement fidelity and scientific relevance
  are separated. Run-level self-ratings and declaration counts remain
  author-reported. No proof environment was installed or executed.
* Keith, Egami and Geraldo Bastías provide methodological foundations;
  none is mislabeled a verified modern LLM implementation. Sample separation
  does not certify construct validity, exchangeability or treatment-version
  sufficiency.
