## A scientific loop with testable boundaries

The May 2025 Zhang and colleagues perspective organizes scientific assistance around observation, hypothesis, experiment and automation. Its 45-page preprint supports that organizing argument and its cautions about reasoning, translation and trust. The related journal record has a different title; its original remains unavailable, so the cited version is the actual preprint. Descriptions of underlying tools are leads for separate primary review. \citep{exec24_scimethod_original}

An assistant can solve the wrong problem faithfully. Extracting eventual repayment loses the difference between USD 10,000 repaid on day 5 and on day 25 when the decision concerns ten-day cash. A formally checked cost constraint of at most USD 100 fails an intended constraint strictly below USD 100 at the boundary. Exact authored examples also show majority error 0.00856 for five independent judges with individual error 0.1, but error 0.1 when they share the same mistake. Agreement is therefore not a general scientific-confidence guarantee. These are stipulated mathematical witnesses, not measured model failures. \citep{exec24_scimethod_reference}

The proposed prototype compares fixed workflows, retrieval assistance and bounded planning on frozen public/generated tasks, using independent answer/refusal oracles, complete failures and fixed search budgets. Public evidence can justify the merit of later bank experiments. It cannot establish transport by itself: a stipulated public choice yielding USD 8 rather than USD 3 becomes USD -4 rather than USD 3 in another environment, giving USD 7 regret. Question, diagram, identification and experiment artifacts should share exact targets and provenance through CLI/MCP calls. This is a proposed contract, not implemented integration. \citep{exec24_scimethod_original,exec24_scimethod_reference}

A stipulated USD 240 workflow across 40 attempts, 30 tool completions and eight accepted answers costs USD 6, USD 8 and USD 30 respectively. Six correct refusals remain a separate outcome. Such complete accounting makes a prototype investment assessable without claiming bank ROI, general algorithmic confidence or independent acceptance. The journal-original omission remains open despite the useful related-preprint inspection. \citep{exec24_scimethod_reference}
