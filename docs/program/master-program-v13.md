## What must count as completion

There are four separate decisions. Author completion requires developed argument,
reviewed source evidence and delivered scholarship, and can include negative
findings. Capability qualification requires identification, justified uncertainty,
numerical behavior and useful outputs in an explicit regime. Independent, bank
and operating acceptance require people and authentic evidence beyond this
workspace. Investment requires measured full costs and decision value and a
signed owner decision. Neither a compiled book nor a passing interface test
automatically satisfies a later gate.

For every substantive method the argument must connect a banking decision and
information clock to target population, intervention, model, assumptions,
identification or a set, estimation, sampling/numerical uncertainty, diagnostics,
alternatives and financial implications. Explain notation and work through an
adverse case. Distinguish inherited results, project derivations, synthetic
examples, hypotheses and verified implementations. The named FinMarketing
monograph remains the standard of development and connected argument; its length
and existing interfaces establish no capability here.

Every scholarly citation needs the genuine locally named PDF. Every cited source,
including software and manuals, needs identity, version/date, local bytes,
SHA-256, inspected anchors and supported claims. Metadata establishes discovery
and identity only. A post-cutoff snapshot is a dated delta unless its historical
version is established. Source unavailability belongs in the omissions register;
it is neither a negative finding about a vendor nor permission to invent a source.

## Review before research, then execute in dependency order

RG1 reviews candidate remedies and stopping rules before final-study design.
Its output is a feasibility assessment, not a scientific qualification. It must
separate an obtainable information design from a convenient oracle and explain
why a candidate can or cannot address each failed regime. R02S, R03S, R04S and
R05S then develop explicit arguments, independent small references and reviewed
fresh protocols. Research readiness authorizes that argument work; it does not
authorize presenting exploratory output as final qualification.

For R02S, an uncertainty set for the numerator and audit/rank parameters may be
propagated jointly without dividing by an estimated near-zero rank. This only
helps if each component set is valid under the actual persistent sampling law.
Supplying known calendar covariance does not solve estimated-covariance inference.
For R03S, observed-history policy evaluation must be separated from hidden-state
optimal control; positivity and logger estimation can obstruct identification.
For R04S, observationally equivalent models need separating information, not
another graph-selection algorithm. For R05S, residualization needs quantitative
remainder and dependence bounds; an arbitrary learner cannot inherit a rate
from a convenient linear model.

All final studies freeze sampling units, truth, methods, tuning, alternatives,
adverse cases, seeds, stopping rules, numerical tolerance, metrics and acceptance
before execution. Registration must precede the first final request. Failures,
refusals, numerical timeouts and whole-line outputs remain visible. Scope
comparisons must show whether a new study really addresses previous failure
regimes. Passing a narrow oracle diagnostic cannot close an original gap.

An empirical lower-confidence-bound screen is a qualification decision, not a
proof of uniform coverage. With a claimed minimum of 95%, a method whose true
coverage is exactly 95% is unlikely to clear a simultaneous lower bound above
95%. Review operating characteristics before final registration. Preserve the
95% standard; choose a justified method/design and adequate simulation precision,
or report that the claim has not been qualified. Do not lower the threshold or
widen intervals after inspecting final outcomes. Wider intervals must also meet
predeclared usefulness criteria; bank width/loss thresholds require the bank.
