# Scientific scope completion program

Authorized for planning, thorough review and execution by the user on
8 October 2026. Source cutoff remains 7 October 2026. This program addresses
the seven findings in `../reviews/remaining-gaps-v4-2026-10-08.md`.

## Objective and acceptance standard

Produce a connected survey and product proposal whose selected capabilities
carry an economic question through observation, causal identification,
statistical estimation, inference, numerical implementation and reusable
behavior. FinMarketing supplies the standard for statistical derivation;
BayesFilter for likelihood/derivative/numerical consistency; CIP for economic
interpretation and source-specific method choice. Length, source count and
passing identity checks cannot satisfy this acceptance test.

Preserve the broad survey. Deepen representative routes that expose the hard
parts of the requested product instead of reducing every question to the
previous convenient experiment. No single recipe is evidence for all panels,
POMDPs or discovery models. Conversely, the proposal need not solve general
nonidentification, implement every estimator or deploy at a bank.

## Scientific dossiers

### A. Persistent transmission and bank panels

Keep external-shock responses distinct from reduced-form innovation responses
and sustained policies. Compare the existing direct-moment/HAC route with
explicitly specified dependence-aware alternatives on the same response
target. A lag-augmented projection is a primary alternative only under the
innovation and dynamic restrictions that justify it; its published marginal
result is not a generic weak-IV or simultaneous-band theorem. Derive any
joint construction separately, including its effect on power.

The common-shock panel must count calendar information correctly. Develop
the large-N/short-T dynamic-panel contrast separately: valid lag moments,
initial conditions for system moments, instrument compression, one/two-step
weighting and covariance. Compare weak/persistent and invalid-moment cases.
Mixed frequency, factors, cointegration and breaks receive a source-specific
selection hierarchy explaining what extra information each route uses.

Register generators, seeds, replications, confidence targets and comparisons
before observing results. Retain initial undercoverage. A failed interval
cannot be repaired by selecting a favorable seed, increasing its nominal
level after the fact or changing its target. Empirical calibration supports
only the tested regimes; unsupported cases remain explicit.

### B. Observational servicing and measurement-supported latent states

Use privately selected actions, not only randomized logging. A tractable
identified route has validated full-rank emission measurements: the observed
unnormalized signal/action/next-signal matrix recovers action-specific state
mass, logger and transitions. Derive this factorization and its failure when
measurement rank or invariance fails. When emissions are estimated from an
independent labelled validation sample, include that sample in the joint
likelihood and uncertainty rather than treating calibration as known.

Fit a multi-state controlled model retaining action, signal and reward terms;
verify its analytic score against the likelihood actually evaluated. Diagnose
local curvature, label alignment, optimization failures and calibration
strength. Evaluate fixed history-measurable policies with value covariance.
Compare a model omitting the private logger and explain why good predictive
fit is insufficient. Source-specific proximal value identification is a
substantive alternative when its proxies and completeness can be defended.

For observed-history inference, add continuous covariates and an explicit
censoring process, derive the resulting weighted recursive score, and preserve
whole trajectories in fitting/evaluation splits. Any learned policy must be
fixed using separate training information before evaluation. Numerical tests
do not establish generic machine-learning nuisance rates.

### C. Discovery under plausible temporal structure

Use a multivariate financial-mechanism generator with lagged and contemporaneous
effects; include hidden-common-cause and omitted-lag variants. Inspect and run
selected PCMCI+/LPCMCI operations at recorded installed versions. Compare
their actual assumptions, tests, endpoint types and output interpretation.

Discovery is followed by a supported effect query or an explicit unresolved
query. Do not convert a PAG to a DAG by erasing circle/bidirected endpoints.
Any coefficient interval conditional on a selected graph must retain that
conditioning; split samples reduce reuse but do not prove the graph correct.
Use background knowledge and alternative plausible completions to show what
would make a financial decision robust or identify useful additional data.

### D. Observation candidate generation and resolution

Generate candidate keys, time roles, version order and aggregations from
schema profiles and constraints. Names may rank candidates but cannot justify
economic truth. Evaluate renamed/reordered heterogeneous fixtures, ambiguous
semantics and feeds outside the supported grammar. Track oracle inclusion,
wrong acceptance, abstention, question count and downstream effect changes.

Select analyst questions according to the ambiguity they resolve for the
requested observation/target. Linkage must retain alternative entities or a
separately calibrated match model. Incorrect or insufficient metadata and
nonidentification remain observable outcomes, not silent cleaning decisions.

### E. Shared operations and public empirical finance

Demonstrate shared objects on distinct static financial studies and the new
longitudinal/discovery paths. A supported identification expression determines
required nuisance fits and score/inference objects; the dataset does not
select its own identifying assumptions. Qualify a substantive DoubleML
operation against an independently calculated score using the same folds and
predictions, then compare genuine alternative nuisance choices separately.

Use a publicly available household-finance application, initially the archived
401(k) eligibility/wealth data used in the DML literature, subject to verified
source availability. Reproduce the estimand, controls and sample definition.
Distinguish eligibility, participation, ATE and constant-coefficient targets.
No numerical effect from that study is automatically transferable to a bank
campaign. Public empirical work complements known-truth synthetic experiments.

Measure full fitting and uncertainty cost, varying the dimension that matters:
dates and lag/horizon for responses, states/trajectory length/calibration and
refits for latent models, variables/lags for discovery. Timing on this host
does not prove distributed throughput; derive the partition/reduction
requirements and distinguish measured from proposed scale.

## Review rules and authority

The review must challenge target changes, action/observation order, untestable
assumptions masquerading as diagnostics, omitted calibration uncertainty,
graph selection, nuisance fitting, repeated use of data and computational
approximations. Document repairs and residual limitations before marking phases
complete. Unsupported generality can be removed only with a mathematical and
product explanation, not by quietly shrinking the acceptance criterion.

Work remains in this repository. Public reads use the existing registered
collector and approved command forms. Dependencies are installed in a local
environment, with versions and license/source identity recorded; no sibling
repository is modified. Existing authorization covers execution. Ask only for
an actual permission boundary encountered, grouped where feasible; do not
broaden security policy to avoid a required review.

Sources and search receipts distinguish discovery metadata from technical
evidence. Existing qualified sources are reused after inspecting the relevant
argument; new PDFs use `Title FirstAuthorSurname(year).pdf`. Current searches
stop at the agreed cutoff and record access date, result limits, inspected
anchors, correction/version checks and unavailable originals.

The first technical checkpoint must contain a real inferential comparison and
a controlled-observational identification/estimation derivation. Continued
work follows the recorded dependencies without another permission request.
