Chapter 89

Reliability when identities, calendars
and models are uncertain

A deposit intervention can affect many customers on the same date, while one customer’s
uncertain identity persists across dates. These are two different sources of repeated information.
Auditing the same identity every month does not produce independent identity evidence, and
adding customers on one calendar does not remove uncertainty about a common funding
shock. The scientific question is therefore not simply whether a database has enough rows.
It is which independent dimensions inform the intervention target, what information the
audits buy, and whether the resulting interval is precise enough to support the decision. This
chapter develops selected extensions with those distinctions intact. Its new arguments are
project derivations; its known-truth examples are synthetic. They do not establish a generally
validated bank product. The argument first separates persistent pair information from calendar
information and independent gold labels, then asks what audit spending can improve. Funding
calibration, finite graph restrictions, nonlinear state models and richer learners test different
ways to gain information or reduce error. Their comparison keeps the scientific law, numerical
implementation and decision-useful precision separate.

89.4      Sharper confidence in a finite latent information class
Suppose independent Gaussian episodes have ordered variables (Z, A, Y ). The information
class is supplied scientifically. Under observed sufficiency, the coefficient of A in E[Y | Z, A] is
the causal target throughout the declared finite class. Gaussian linear regression with intercept
gives an exact conditional tn−3 interval when the design has rank three. This uses causal
invariance across the class, rather than selecting a graph and then ignoring its uncertainty. A
broad latent observational class still supports every real effect through the earlier same-law
witness. More precise computation cannot remove that lack of information.
  Under valid randomized encouragement and exclusion, the true null effect θ makes Y − θA
have zero Z regression slope. Its Gaussian reduced-form error is independent of Z even when
the treatment residual is confounded with outcome error. The exact null-test approach is the
Gaussian specialization of the weak-IV principle discussed by Staiger and Stock (1994); no
lower bound on the first-stage coefficient is needed.

To verify the pivot, regress Y − θA on (1, Z): its slope is y − θa, residual sum of squares is the

slope and independent scaled residual variance give the Student distribution. Membership of

   Equation (89.5) is a quadratic sublevel set. A positive leading coefficient gives the interval

independent-looking 95% answers would require its own simultaneous confidence argument.

zero encouragement-set coverage, and falsely supplied sufficiency under hidden confounding
likewise fails. An exact statistical pivot cannot make an incorrect causal restriction true.
General PAG confidence and dependent histories remain distinct problems.

The partial-linear IV score retains the Robinson form of Chernozhukov et al. (2018), with
nuisance conditional means (ℓ, r, m):

lie in the dictionary. Fixed-dimensional OLS then has root-sample error under these selected

   Two held-out blocks are separated by gap 100. Each trains on the other; windows and

89.8      Evidence required before institutional use
Independent scientific review must reconstruct the target, assumptions, identification, un-
certainty and limitations, including negative comparisons. Authentic bank validation then
needs assignment and source-clock owners, representative independent audit truth, known
selection probabilities, a frozen question and held-out evidence. An unknown-truth bank study
cannot simply reproduce synthetic truth-coverage counts. It needs assignment diagnostics,
placebos, audit sensitivity and financial reconciliation appropriate to its design, together with
a decision-relevant precision requirement.
  Application acceptance requires consumer maintainers to preserve qualifications, unbound-
ed/disconnected sets and refusals under their actual interfaces. Workflow economics require
matched complete analyst tasks and quality, active labor, rework, audits and infrastructure
