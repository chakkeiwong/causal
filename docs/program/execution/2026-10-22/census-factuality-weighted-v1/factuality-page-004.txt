2     FACTUALITY ISSUE
In this section, we describe the issue of factuality in large language models, as well as the impact.

2.1    Large Language Models
There is no well-accepted and exact definition of large language models in the literature [21, 101, 312].
We mainly consider the decoder-only generative pre-trained language modes with emergent abilities,
such as ChatGPT [192] and LLaMA [254, 255]. We also include some work that is based on models
with encoder-decoder architectures, such as T5 [213]. We do not talk about work that is only based
on the Encoder-only models, such as BERT [53] and RoBERTa [156], in this survey. To be specific,
our survey includes the following LLMs:
• General Domain LLMs: GPT-2 [211], GPT-3 [17], ChatGPT [192], GPT-4 [193], GPT-Neo [15],
  OPT [304], LLaMA [254], LLaMA-2 [255], Incite [43, 253], Claude [38], Falcon [3], MPT [249],
  Vicuna [33], FLAN-T5 [36], BLOOM [222], Baichuan & Baichuan2 [285], PaLM [34], Gopher [62],
  Megatron-LM [230], SAIL [163], Codex [27], Bard [85], GLM & ChatGLM [297], InternLM [248],
  StableBeluga [166], Claude [38], Alpaca [247], New Bing [177], Ziya-LLaMA [300], BLOOMZ [185],
  Chinese-LLaMA [46], Phoenix [31], and others.
• Domain-specify LLMs: BloombergGPT [278], EcomGPT [143], BioGPT [164], LawGPT [189],
  Lawyer LLaMA [104], ChatLaw [45], BioMedLM [259], HuatuoGPT [299], ChatDoctor [142],
  MedicalGPT [283], Bentsao (Huatuo as its original name) [267], Zhongjing [287], LLM-AMT [271],
  DISC-MedLLM [10], Cohortgpt [87], Deid-gpt [157], Doctorglm [282], MedChatZH [243], K2 [52],
  HouYi [8], GrammarGPT [64], FoodGPT [209], ChatHome [277], and others.

2.2    Factuality
By factuality in LLMs we refer to the capability of large language models for generating contents
that follow factual information, which encompasses commonsense, world knowledge and domain
facts. The factual information can be grounded to reliable sources, such as dictionaries, Wikipedia
or textbooks from different domains 1 . A series of work have discussed whether LLMs can serve as
knowledge bases to store factual knowledge [2, 196, 293]
