41.1      A business sentence can contain several questions
“Should we increase the deposit offer?” sounds like a single question, but it leaves several
choices unresolved. The intended action could be a change in the quoted rate, the eligibility
rule, the message shown to a customer or the allocation of a fixed promotional budget. The
outcome could be a new deposit, retained balances, funding cost or total relationship value.
The population could be existing customers, applicants who received an offer or everyone
eligible under a proposed rule. Each choice defines a different causal problem.
   A language assistant can expose these alternatives and organize the evidence needed to
resolve them. It should not choose the easiest target to estimate and silently treat that target
as the owner’s decision. In the project funding case, the response of balances to a 25-basis-point
increase and the change in net funding cost over thirty days are retained as separate candidates.
Their business motivation is shared; their outcomes and decision consequences are not identical.
The unresolved choice is useful information, not a failure that must be hidden by returning a
number.
   The co-pilot roadmap discussed by Alaa and colleagues motivates this progression from a
scientific question through assumptions, statistical targets and interpretation. Their illustrative
medical examples attend to eligibility, baseline time, treatment and follow-up. The inspected
paper also reports that generic uncued answers can omit relevant design concerns. Applying
those principles to banking is a project proposal; the medical examples do not validate banking
clocks or interventions.Alaa et al. (2024)


41.2      A question as a collection of commitments
The project represents a question as

                                Q = (U, P, A, π, π0 , Y, H, ν, ℓ, I).                        (41.1)

Here U is the unit of analysis; P is the target population; A is the feasible action set; π and
π0 are the policy and comparator; Y is the outcome; H is the horizon; ν is the measure used
to average over the population; ℓ is the loss, including its units; and I specifies information
available at each action. This notation defines the project’s declaration contract. It is not an
identification theorem.

   The unit needs particular care. An account can be an observation while a customer is the
decision unit and a household is the unit within which actions interact. An assistant can ask
which role each identifier plays, but a column called customer ID does not certify ownership or
stable linkage. If several accounts share a budget or pricing rule, an account-level action may
also be an infeasible description of the intervention. The feasible action set should reflect what
the responsible institution can actually change.
   The comparator must be as explicit as the intervention. “No treatment” might mean no
new contact, continuation of the current contact policy, no rate increase, or a different offer
under a fixed budget. A dynamic policy can include future actions conditional on history, so
its comparator is also a history-dependent rule. An effect relative to one comparator cannot
be reused for another solely because the same treatment variable appears in the dataset.
  The outcome and loss should also remain separate. A change in balances has units of
currency held over a horizon; funding expense combines balances, rates and time. A larger
deposit balance can have a positive acquisition interpretation and an unfavorable net-cost
implication. If g(a) is a specified gross benefit and c(a) is a specified cost, the decision may
depend on E[g(a) − c(a)], not on E[g(a)] alone. The definitions and valuation assumptions
must be supplied before that subtraction is meaningful. The assistant should make missing
costs visible rather than invent prices.

41.4     Information is governed by an availability clock
The date on which an event occurred differs from the date on which an analyst or policy could
observe it. For a recorded variable V , write eV for event time and aV for availability time.
The finite project fixtures require eV ≤ aV , and a policy acting at time t can use the value
only if aV ≤ t. A derived feature becomes available no earlier than its required inputs. These
rules prevent an apparently historical feature from leaking future information into a decision.
   In the servicing fixture, an account condition occurs at day ten but becomes available
at day eleven. A rule acting on day eleven may use it; a rule acting on day ten may not.
The distinction matters even when both values later appear in the same warehouse table. A
language assistant should request the recording and availability convention, rather than infer
it from a field name or a sorted timestamp.
   Advance announcements and forecasts require different variable definitions. A forecast
observed today of next month’s condition is a current forecast variable, not a measurement of a
future realized condition with an impossible availability time. Revised historical data similarly
require a vintage or revision convention. The project’s finite validator does not implement
every revision protocol. It checks the stated recorded-value contract and leaves more complex
clocks as explicit extensions.
  Equal timestamps do not determine direction. Two columns aggregated to the same day
may hide a finer action sequence or a common cause. The assistant can propose a finer clock
and identify the evidence needed to support it. It cannot establish temporal precedence by
placing one variable first in a diagram. When the granularity is insufficient, the ambiguity
should survive into the model class.


41.5     An available covariate is not automatically an adjustment
         variable
In a sequential servicing model, A0 changes an intermediate condition L1 , which affects both
a later action A1 and the outcome Y . The same L1 is a mediator for the first action and a
common cause of the second action–outcome relation. Its appropriate use depends on the
target. Including every available column in one regression does not preserve all possible total,
direct and policy effects.
   The project declaration therefore distinguishes a policy-history purpose from an ordinary
adjustment purpose. A simple directed-path diagnostic flags a post-treatment descendant
proposed as an ordinary total-effect control. It does not declare all post-treatment conditioning
invalid. Controlled direct effects and longitudinal procedures need their own question and
formal analysis. A warning from a declaration validator is not a replacement for an adjustment
criterion or an identification algorithm.
   Observation processes can alter the target in a similar way. Suppose only successfully
linked records enter an analysis and both action and repayment affect successful linkage. The
selection indicator is part of the causal problem, and an effect among linked records may differ
from an effect in the original population. A record-link score is an observed proxy; it is not
verified ownership. The observation fixture deliberately retains a latent ownership variable, a
selection node and an unresolved population choice.

41.6      The handoff to formal analysis
The assistant’s useful output is a reviewable declaration and a record of what remains unresolved.
A selected declaration contains one explicit target, its population measure, the intervention
and comparator, clocks, feasible actions, outcome and loss. An ambiguous declaration retains
candidate targets without covertly selecting one. A contradicted assumption cannot be left as
an inconsequential note while the calculation proceeds as if it held.
  The local project validator exercises these distinctions with four synthetic banking cases and
adverse contract tests. It checks declared identities, types, references and selected clock/graph
conditions. It does not authenticate an institution, certify a reviewer, determine whether a
causal assumption is true, or implement every MAG/PAG/CPDAG validity condition. A
declaration ready for formal analysis is ready for the next conditional operation, not a qualified
bank recommendation.
   This boundary also explains the limited scope of the current executable prototype. A
declaration can describe a dynamic regime while the selected operation supports only a
static binary population ATE. The correct handoff then reports unsupported target mapping.
Translating that request into a static regression would produce a result at the cost of changing
the user’s question. The appropriate remedy is to qualify a longitudinal operation or obtain
an explicit new target decision, with the original question preserved.


41.7      A complete translation example: the incentive question
Suppose a manager asks an agent to “find the incentive uplift and choose the best offer.” The
sentence leaves at least three mathematical objects open: a binary offer effect, a continuous
local slope, and a finite-dose policy value. The agent can help by presenting these alternatives
with their populations, units and action constraints. It should not select whichever object
happens to be exposed by the first available package.
   Let A be incentive intensity in a declared unit and let Y be retained net value over a fixed
horizon. The finite question is ∆(a1 , a0 ) = E{Y (a1 ) − Y (a0 )}. A local derivative, where
it exists, is µ′ (a). A covariance slope averages features of the response under an exposure
distribution and need not equal either object. The inspected grf and dose-response interfaces
make this a practical interoperability issue, not merely a linguistic distinction.grf project
(2026b); causaldrf project (2026b)
   For the stipulated curve µ(a) = 20a − 3a2 , a uniform exposure on [0, 2] gives covariance
slope 14, while moving from dose one to dose two changes the mean by 11 CNY. An agent
that labels 14 as the value of the specified change has mistranslated the problem even if it
calls its numerical tool correctly. The exact project calculation makes the error detectable
without a live model experiment.Causal banking research project (2026be)
   A useful translation record would therefore state: eligible existing customers; the information
available before the offer; current dose one and feasible dose two; a fixed retention horizon;
treatment and outcome currency units; and whether incentive expense is already included
in Y . If expense is excluded, the decision value is ∆(a1 , a0 ) − {c(a1 ) − c(a0 )}. Subtracting
it again when Y already measures net value would double-count cost. The record also needs
account closure/default definitions and a sampling unit for uncertainty.

41.8      A servicing policy is a rule with an information boundary
For sequential servicing, an action rule uses Ht , the information actually available at decision
time. A data field recorded later may describe an earlier event while remaining unavailable
to the operating policy. An agent assembling history features must preserve both event and
availability times. Otherwise the proposed policy uses information the bank would not possess
when acting.
   The causal query should distinguish evaluating a specified rule, choosing among rules and
claiming an optimal hidden-state policy. A longitudinal simulator can evaluate supplied regimes
under its assumptions. Its ability to accept a custom history function does not identify private
customer states or make an unknown logger known. The inspected pygformula class exposes
useful histories, event and regime options, but its source interface alone establishes neither
policy optimality nor uncertainty under arbitrary dependence.pygformula project (2026b)
   A concrete policy-selection hazard can be shown without an asymptotic argument. Suppose
two equally valuable policies each have independent evaluation error equal to minus one or
plus one with equal probability. The maximum of the two errors is minus one only when both
are minus one, and plus one otherwise. Its expectation is therefore 34 − 41 = 12 . Reporting the
selected policy’s same-data evaluation is optimistic. If the selected policy is evaluated with a
fresh independent error of mean zero, the evaluation error has mean zero conditional on the
selection. Exact enumeration verifies both claims.Causal banking research project (2026be)
   This example does not prove that a particular split is sufficient for a dependent servicing
panel. It explains why the selection and evaluation laws must be specified. An agent can
preserve the split, freeze candidate rules and refuse reuse of a held-out set for tuning. A
scientific argument must still justify independence or the required dependence control. Training
a policy and then narrating its favorable development score is not an independent evaluation.


41.9      Translation outputs that deserve abstention
Some business questions should produce a request for more information or a set of alternatives.
If “the customer” may mean the legal entity, the beneficial owner or a linked household,
choosing one silently changes both the population and interference structure. If the intended
action is not operationally feasible, the mathematically optimal action is irrelevant. If the loss
function and costs are unknown, the agent can compare statistical targets but cannot assert
an investment recommendation.
   Abstention should be specific. It should identify the missing definition, record the alternative
interpretations and explain which downstream operation depends on the answer. That is
different from a generic refusal to analyze. For an incentive query, the missing item may be
treatment units or whether cost is already included. For servicing it may be the information
clock or the policy class. For interference it may be network direction or the allocation policy.
These are concrete commitments that owners can resolve and numerical tools can then preserve.

41.10      A formulation workflow that preserves unresolved alter-
           natives
A language model can make a business question more precise by exposing the decisions that its
wording leaves open. Its useful output is a set of explicit candidate questions, with evidence
for their variables and clocks, followed by a reasoned choice of a supported analysis. Fluency
alone does not determine which candidate the bank intended. A formal causal engine also
cannot repair a mistake that was made before the graph, intervention and target reached the
engine.
   Consider a manager who asks, “Which customers should receive a retention invitation next
month, and how much value will it create?” One interpretation is the effect of offering the
invitation to every currently eligible customer. Another is the effect among customers who
historically received it. A third is the value of a targeting rule under a fixed contact budget.
The first two are averages over different populations. The third also requires a feasible policy
class, the information available when the rule acts, a comparator policy and a cost definition.
A model that estimates historical recipients’ average effect and calls it the expected value of
the new targeting rule has changed the question.
   The formulation record should therefore contain the eligible population and exclusions,
the sampling unit, invitation and comparator, actual receipt if distinct, the decision time,
permissible information, outcome horizon, monetary units and accounting treatment of contact
cost. It should distinguish invitation from successful contact and from subsequent product use.
Those variables may be causally related, but they are different interventions. It should also
retain alternatives that remain unresolved: all eligible customers versus historical recipients, a
single action versus a sequence, and gross revenue versus net value.

41.10.3    Concrete package choices and the formal baseline

The inspected pywhy-llm path is a candidate for eliciting hypotheses and candidate variables. Its
IdentificationSuggester.suggest_backdoor delegates to a language-model confounder sug-

preserve cited source passages and unresolved alternatives instead of interpreting a generated
factor list as an approved adjustment set. (pywhy-llm contributors, 2026)
   For oci-agent, the useful candidate is the separation of a specification, a deterministic
analysis notebook and a critic report. The selected source has a general notebook runner and
a target-label issue requiring correction. A bounded banking interface should expose fixed
reviewed operations with resource limits and typed outputs. The proposal does not adopt a
general notebook executor as the causal MCP surface. Dependency resolution, failure handling

   CausalDS is a candidate for generated scene and task design, but its grader needs a semantic

Unknown identification due to a missing backend is not scientific nonidentification. A malformed

   The deterministic formulation baseline has three layers. A schema check verifies that
population, unit, intervention, comparator, horizon, clock and scale are present. A semantic
check compares these fields with the supplied brief and dictionary: a post-contact duration
cannot become a pre-contact covariate, an invitation cannot silently become actual uptake, and
a requested all-eligible effect cannot become a recipients-only effect. A formal check evaluates
identification under the explicitly supplied candidate graph and returns an expression, model-
relative nonidentification, unsupported scope or an unresolved input. These checks validate
different things. A well-formed schema does not prove the story is true, and a correctly
evaluated graph does not prove its edges.
   For a generated benchmark, the reference can enumerate a finite set of admissible formu-
lations Q(b) for brief b. An explicitly resolved brief may have one acceptable target. An
ambiguous brief may have several and require the system to request or retain the missing
distinction. A baseline can return those alternatives directly from the supplied reference
fields and use exact calculations such as the example above. This establishes a transparent
lower-cost comparator and exposes what a language model is expected to add: interpretation
of unstructured language, evidence retrieval and useful clarification, rather than numerical
work already handled by a deterministic engine.

41.10.4     Evaluation before a costly bank study

The initial evaluation can use public data dictionaries and openly documented financial
examples alongside generated causal mechanisms. Public data support questions about
available variables and clocks; they usually do not supply intervention truth. Generated
mechanisms can supply that truth for the declared model. A reference author should create
matched examples that vary one decisive feature: invitation versus uptake, all eligible versus
treated population, pre-decision versus post-decision measurement, complete variables versus
noisy proxies, or a single action versus repeated treatment. The target-change example supplies
one exact reference family. This is a proposed evaluation design; it is not a reported live-agent
comparison.
   Train/development and final evaluation should be split by the underlying scenario or
structural model, not merely by paraphrases of the same question. Otherwise a system can
learn the reference target from exposed near-duplicates. All requests should remain in the
evaluation denominator, including ambiguous requests, timeouts, unsupported estimands,
malformed answers and justified nonidentification. Report target and clock fidelity, unjustified
resolution of ambiguity, false causal answers, valid abstention, numerical error where a target
is actually identified, interval availability and coverage where ground truth permits them, and
total resource use. A high completion rate alone rewards an agent for confidently answering
questions that should remain unresolved.
   Retrieval, structured prompting and an actor/critic loop should be compared against the
same deterministic baseline and fixed task families. A retrieval ablation tests whether source
passages improve interpretation. A critic ablation tests whether additional review catches errors,
while reporting the added calls, time and newly introduced mistakes. A graph-perturbation
comparison tests whether a system notices that an assumption changed. Repeated runs
reveal stability. These studies need their actual model, tool, prompt, data and stopping-rule
identities frozen before final evaluation. Agreement across repeated runs is useful evidence
about stability; it is not evidence that the agreed answer is causally correct.
   Several gaps remain scientific. A general method for recovering stakeholder intent from
incomplete language is not established. The immediate remedy is to preserve admissible
alternatives and make the missing decision explicit. A general guarantee that retrieval or
debate validates causal edges is also absent; the current studies justify investigating those tools
with factual and graph-specific references, not treating consensus as truth. Identification from
noisy proxies, dependent observations or repeated interventions requires the corresponding
statistical model and method. Finally, whether an assisted workflow saves bank analyst time
or changes an investment decision requires a later workflow study after a bounded prototype
shows merit. Those gaps narrow the claims and motivate specific experiments; they do not
prevent writing or testing the proposal’s public/generated formulation contract.

Four banking translations before
formal causal analysis

A request such as “Should we improve retention?” is not yet a causal question. The action
may be a rate offer, a service contact, a product change or a budgeted policy. The unit may be
an account, customer, household or branch. The outcome may be balance retention, funding
cost, repayment or relationship value. An assistant should expose these alternatives and ask
for the missing decision; a formal engine cannot repair a target chosen incorrectly before the
graph is built. The four translations below keep the business motivation while making different
estimands visible. Their formal discipline follows the separation of a target trial, its eligible
population and its observed emulation in Hernán and Robins (2020); the banking choices
themselves are proposals, not observations.


42.1     Deposit pricing: acquisition, retention or net funding cost
Suppose a treasury manager considers a 25-basis-point deposit-rate increase. Translation A
asks for the effect of offering the rate to every eligible account on thirty-day average balance.
Translation B asks for the effect on balances among accounts that historically received similar
offers. Translation C asks for net funding cost, including paid interest, incentive expense,
repricing of existing balances and any value assigned to retained relationships. Translation D
asks for a fixed-budget allocation policy that chooses which accounts receive the offer. These
are four populations or outcomes, not four estimators of one effect.

42.3      Credit underwriting: approval threshold or feasible deci-
          sion rule
A credit team may ask whether lowering an approval threshold changes loss. Translation A is
the effect of offering credit to applicants between the old and new thresholds. Translation B is
the effect on repayment conditional on approval. Translation C is the effect on portfolio loss
including volume, utilization and capital cost. Translation D is a policy value under a capacity
or risk budget. Approval is an action selected by a threshold, while repayment is observed
only under selected applicants; conditioning on approved cases changes the target.
   The unit is an application at the decision date. The action must specify amount, term and
price, not only a binary approval label. Potential outcomes include repayment, loss, utilization
and capital usage over a fixed horizon. A threshold change can alter composition, interference
through capacity and future collection intensity. The information contract must distinguish
bureau vintage, application-time fields, later delinquency and revised labels. Diagnostics
include support around the threshold, manipulation or anticipatory behavior, policy feasibility,
missing outcomes and whether the threshold is a rule or a human override.
  A regression discontinuity or instrumental design requires its own continuity, exclusion
and assignment assumptions; a predictive score does not supply them. If the target is an
approval-policy value, the report should retain the policy constraints and loss function rather
than output a conditional repayment coefficient. A public or generated threshold mechanism
can test translation and clock preservation before requesting bank outcome truth.
   To see the selection issue, let D(z) denote approval under threshold policy z and let Y (1)
denote repayment under a specified offered loan. The mean E[Y (1) | D(z) = 1] describes
the applicants selected by z; it is not E[Y (1)] for all applicants. Changing z can change
that conditioning population even if no individual repayment response changes. Near a score
cutoff c, the discontinuity lims↓c E(Y | S = s) − lims↑c E(Y | S = s) may have a local causal
interpretation only under the design’s continuity and manipulation restrictions; it is not
the portfolio effect of moving the entire threshold. A threshold policy must count declined
applications with their opportunity cost and define the loan terms and missing counterfactual
repayment law. The local-design distinction is consistent with the audited discontinuity and
target-trial sources (Hernán and Robins, 2020).

42.5      The formulation record and formal handoff
The declaration should contain Q = (U, P, A, A0 , Y, H, ν, ℓ, I): unit, population, action, com-
parator, outcome, horizon, averaging measure, loss and information clock. It should preserve
candidate questions when the business sentence is ambiguous. A schema check verifies fields
and types; a semantic check compares them with the brief and dictionary; a formal check
applies the supplied graph and method. A passed schema is not causal truth, and a failed
backend is not nonidentification.
   The order of handoff matters. First, freeze Q and the candidate alternatives with a human
owner. Second, attach each proposed graph arrow to a source, temporal ordering and an
uncertainty label. Third, ask the formal engine which estimand, if any, follows from the
supplied graph and assumptions. Fourth, run estimators only for supported targets and return
precision, sensitivity and refusal states. An arrow suggested by retrieval is a hypothesis until its
substantive owner accepts it; conversational consistency is not graph accuracy (Kıcıman et al.,
2023; Grace Sng, Yanming Zhang and Klaus Mueller, 2024). Formal reasoning benchmarks
and narrated synthetic cases help check the translation boundary, but their scores do not
validate bank assignment truth (Jin et al., 2023; Leban and Sun, 2026).
   The deterministic baseline is an analyst interview plus fixed arithmetic and graph fixtures.
An assisted system can retrieve evidence, draft alternatives, propose a graph and prepare typed
calls. It should be scored on target fidelity, clock fidelity, preserved ambiguity, valid abstention,

42.5.1    Text measurement must preserve the causal question

A servicing analyst may ask an assistant to extract financial distress from call notes and then
adjust for the extracted score when comparing repayment plans. This can be useful work.
It also combines three different claims: that the score measures distress, that the note was
available before the plan decision, and that conditioning on the score removes the relevant
confounding. An accurate summary of the note establishes none of the latter two claims. A
later note may describe a reaction to the plan; a note written only after a complaint may select
on both treatment and outcome. The proposed formulation interface must attach a time and a
measurement interpretation to each extracted variable before adding it to an adjustment set.
   Keith, Jensen and O’Connor review text used to measure otherwise unobserved confounders.
Their discussion explicitly distinguishes confounders, colliders and mediators and asks whether
one document can encode several of these at once. It also identifies error in measured con-
founders as an unresolved problem. A richer language representation can improve measurement,
but the review provides no guarantee that a representation eliminates confounding. The appro-
priate comparison therefore includes simple dictionaries, supervised extractors, human-coded
measurements and a language model, with the same permitted document dates and the same
effect target. Katherine A. Keith, David Jensen and Brendan O’Connor (2020)

Freeze the measurement rule before evaluating its effects

Egami and colleagues make the measurement rule itself explicit. Let g map a document into a
category or vector. For a textual outcome, E[g(Y (1)) − g(Y (0))] is a target defined by that
particular rule. Searching over rules using the same treatment assignments and documents later
used for effect estimation can induce dependence and select apparent effects. Their proposed
separation of codebook discovery from effect estimation addresses that problem, while their
text-as-treatment discussion imposes a further sufficiency restriction on the representation.
Naoki Egami, Christian J. Fong, Justin Grimmer, Margaret E. Roberts and Brandon M.
Stewart (2018)
   For a language-based measurement pipeline, the rule includes the model version, prompt,
retrieval corpus, preprocessing, extraction schema and response aggregation. Freezing only a
classifier filename while changing the prompt after reading the effect estimates does not freeze
g. The prototype should develop the rule on a designated development set, record its identity,
and evaluate it on held-out units. If several calls concern one customer or conversation, those
calls should remain together in the split. This is a proposed implementation of the separation
principle, not a claim that sample splitting alone ensures valid inference for dependent histories.
   The held-out review asks whether the labels retain their intended meaning, whether extrac-
tion errors differ by treatment and outcome, and whether missing documents have a defensible
observation model. It must preserve negative findings. Rewriting the measurement rule to
rescue a disappointing effect requires a new evaluation set or an explicitly exploratory analysis.
Neither a fixed rule nor a held-out set supplies unmeasured assignment information or repairs
a post-treatment adjustment error.

relationships from missing variables. This is a methodological contribution to diagram review,
not an implemented LLM discovery algorithm. It supports an assistant that makes assumptions
