Chapter 46

Channels, doses and sensitivity to
assumptions

A bank may know that a policy works on average and still need to choose which mechanism
to change, how large an intervention to offer and how much confidence to place in the causal
assumptions. These are related decisions, but they require different targets. We begin with
channel attribution and feasible mediator interventions, then compare natural-effect estimation
and sensitivity methods across implementations. The final dose analysis asks how to choose
an amount rather than merely fit a response curve. Throughout, changes in assumptions and
changes in sampling uncertainty remain distinct.


46.1     Commercial mediation software and the meaning of a
         channel
A credit team may know that a servicing offer improves repayment while still not knowing
whether the improvement travels through customer understanding, available liquidity or a
change in staff behavior. That distinction matters when the proposed investment changes only
one channel. An effect attributed to a mediator is not automatically the effect of a feasible
intervention on that mediator. Commercial mediation software supplies developed estimation
and reporting workflows, but the comparison must begin with the intervention, counterfactual
target and observation clock.
  The selected Stata manuals and Mplus chapter document concrete capabilities. The
StataNow two-mediator manual is a separate product feature from the one-mediator command.
The selected Mplus chapter has March 2017 PDF metadata and supports a historical technical
profile; it does not establish current feature absence or exact licensed entitlement. Sources
acquired after the literature cutoff cannot, through an internal date alone, authenticate their
exact bytes on that cutoff date. The available comparison therefore supports scoped design
choices while retaining unresolved versions and commercial alternatives. (StataCorp, 2026t,s;
Muthen and Muthen, 2017)

46.2.2    Preserve target labels, population and model-object semantics

The inspected mediate frontend accepts fitted mediator and outcome models, explicit treatment
and control values, simulation count, bootstrap choice and uncertainty options. If supplied
categorical treatment values do not match factor levels, it warns and falls back to the first two
levels. That is an observable source contract, not an acceptable silent conversion for a banking
API. A request for labels none and offer cannot be treated as a request for declined and
offered without a verified mapping. The proposed adapter refuses unmapped labels and
returns the exact values used when a mapping is declared. (Tingley,Yamamoto and mediation
contributors, 2025; Causal banking proposal project, 2026du)
   When dropobs is enabled, the source merges the model frames and refits on shared rows. It
warns about possible cluster alignment problems with missing observations. The observation
population therefore belongs in the result. Mediator identifiers {a, b, c} and outcome identifiers
{b, c, d} intersect at only {b, c}. The common sample represents half the union, not an
unchanged four-customer target. An adapter must retain the original row identities, model-
frame membership, missingness rule and refitted sample, and must not use row counts as
a substitute for matching identities. (Tingley,Yamamoto and mediation contributors, 2025;
Causal banking proposal project, 2026du)
   The source treats prior model weights as sampling weights, warns when a binomial mediator
model could invite a trial-count interpretation, and checks matching weights between the
relevant model frames. Effects (0, 10) with weights (1, 9) average to 9, while equal weights
give 5. Importing binomial trial totals as population weights can therefore change the target,
not just its standard error. Dependency objects and their classes must retain this meaning
through CLI or MCP serialization. (Tingley,Yamamoto and mediation contributors, 2025;
Causal banking proposal project, 2026du)

46.6.5    Neural bounds need both a theorem and numerical certificates

NeuralCSA generalizes the sensitivity family using two conditional normalizing flows. The
retained primary paper assumes independent observations, consistency, positivity and latent
unconfoundedness. Its key sufficiency theorem concerns a transformation-invariant generalized
treatment sensitivity model, an invertible map that exactly reproduces the observational
outcome distribution, and optimization over the full admissible distribution class. That result
motivates the two-stage construction. It does not say that any fitted flow, finite Monte Carlo
sample and stopped optimizer produces certified extrema. (Frauen et al., 2024)
   The selected implementation samples latent variables, evaluates sensitivity constraints
and updates a constrained objective using multipliers and a penalty. For supremum-type
restrictions, sampled extrema can miss a small violating set. If a violation occupies probability
0.001 and 100 independent probes are drawn, the probability of seeing none is 0.999100 ≃ 0.905.
This is an authored diagnostic, not an assessment of the paper’s experiments. A feasible
maximization iterate is generally a lower bound on the optimal maximum, not by itself a
valid upper bound on the true causal query. A proposal for certified output therefore needs a
feasibility assessment, a justified optimization-gap bound or analytic outer envelope, and a
separate account of density-estimation and sampling uncertainty. (DennisFrauen/NeuralCSA
contributors, 2023; Frauen et al., 2024; Causal banking proposal project, 2026gb)
  The source inspection also finds that a public sensitivity-check helper calls a three-argument

46.7.1    What a linear path product means

Let A be a servicing assignment, M an intermediate response, and Y the outcome over a fixed
horizon. A recursive linear structural model can be written as

                    A = εA ,     M = aA + εM ,        Y = cA + bM + εY .

If these equations are valid structural assignments, intervening on A changes the conditional
mean of Y with slope c + ab. The direct path has coefficient c, while the path through M
has product ab. This algebra does not itself identify the coefficients from observed data.
Interpreting regression estimates as structural coefficients needs restrictions on disturbances,
measured covariates, timing, selection and measurement. In particular, a model that fits an
observed covariance matrix is not automatically an identified intervention model. (Causal
banking proposal project, 2026fo)
   For an authored example, set (a, b, c) = (2, 4, 3) and disturbance variances to one. With
independent disturbances the indirect effect per unit of assignment is eight and the total
is eleven. If instead Cov(εM , εY ) = 1/2, while assignment remains independent of these
disturbances, regression of Y on A and M yields mediator slope 4.5 and direct coefficient two.
The regression path product is then nine, although the structural indirect effect remains eight.
The total remains eleven: a correct total can conceal an incorrect decomposition. A proposed
Java adapter must distinguish a supplied structural parameter from a regression estimate and
retain any correlated-error assumptions. (Causal banking proposal project, 2026fo)

46.10.6     Reuse decisions and public qualification before bank experiments

We choose reference use now: causaldrf for transparent GPS adjustment, CausalGPS for design
and balance contracts, WeightIt for point and longitudinal weighting, drc for fitting and inverse
targets, and DoseFinding for experimental contrasts. An original R worker is a conditional
future route when it preserves objects, diagnostics and uncertainty. A whole-library port lacks
a demonstrated benefit. We reject automatic curve-to-causal and balance-to-identification
admission; native admission awaits exact distributions, dependencies, rights and semantic
evidence. (Causal banking proposal project, 2026dc)
   Declarations differ. causaldrf declares MIT plus its copyright file, R at least 3.1.2 and
imported/optional packages; CausalGPS declares GPL-3, R at least 3.5, Rcpp compilation and
learning/smoothing dependencies. WeightIt declares GPL at least version 2, R at least 4.1 and
method-dependent optional dependencies. drc declares GPL-2 and R at least 4; DoseFinding
declares GPL-3 and compiled code. These are declarations, not transitive distribution clearance
or a legal conclusion. Rights, maintenance owners, installed versions and optional paths must
be resolved for the actual worker. (causaldrf contributors, 2022; CausalGPS contributors, 2026;
WeightIt contributors, 2026; drc contributors, 2026; DoseFinding contributors, 2025)
   Public qualification can precede expensive bank access. Freeze version, transformation, in-
tervention density, population and dose region. Generate known-outcome experiments covering
confounding, nonlinear balance, weak overlap, varying scales, dependence and misspecification.
Compare analytical targets with the pinned worker. Test invalid identifiers, empty/extreme
weights, trimming, missing fields, unsupported covariance types, selection, serialization and
process failure. Refit estimated stages under the justified resampling law, retain failed requests,
and distinguish pointwise from simultaneous claims. Tuning and final assessment must not
reuse the same outcomes. (Causal banking proposal project, 2026dc)
   The scientific owner supplies causal assumptions and sampling units; the adapter owner
preserves units, measures, histories, objects and refusal states; the rights/platform owner
qualifies the distribution; the evaluator registers independent targets and complete costs.

gives 24, 30 and 40 USD per respective denominator. These are not measured prices or
adoption returns. Unmeasured confounding, absent support, arbitrary dependence and policy-
