5. Fine-tuning analysis: Investigating whether causal reasoning capabilities can
   be improved through targeted fine-tuning on causal datasets, and whether such
   improvements transfer across domains.
6. Closed-source model evaluation: Extending evaluation to include state-of-the-
   art closed-source models (GPT-4, Claude) to provide a more complete picture of
   LLM causal reasoning capabilities across the model landscape.

9.3 Practical Implications
Expert systems deploying LLMs in causal decision support (e.g., clinical diagnosis
support, root-cause analysis, policy evaluation) should:
• Prefer Socratic prompting for counterfactual queries and effect prediction tasks;
• Implement mandatory adversarial causal screening before deployment in domains
  where spurious correlations are common (epidemiology, economics, social science);
• Provide domain-specific causal knowledge (through retrieval augmentation or fine-
  tuning) for biological and social domains where general-purpose causal reasoning is
  weakest;
• Use task-adaptive prompting that selects the strategy based on the type of causal
  question being asked.
