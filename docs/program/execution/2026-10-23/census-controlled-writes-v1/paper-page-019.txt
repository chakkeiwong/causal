Definition 6 (Bridge channel model). The carrier–core interface is modeled by three error chan-
nels: (i) proposal unreliability µ: each step of the intended derivation is corrupted or missing in the
carrier trace with probability at most µ; (ii) verification miss rate η: a valid step fails verification
with probability at most η; (iii) formalization-failure rate εf r : each exposed premise fails formaliza-
tion with probability at most εf r . Channel (iii) consists of an interface-dependent bias component
and an i.i.d. slip component (Definition 8), and the independence of channel events (Assumption 2,
S3) applies to the slip component only.
Definition 7 (Grounding interface). A grounding interface specification is a triple I = (Σ, L, Ω),
where Σ is a scoped predicate vocabulary with explicit signatures (arity and argument roles), L is a
role legend, i.e. a partial injective map from constants to case/entity roles, and Ω ⊆ Σ is a declared
source-predicate whitelist whose ground instances the carrier may propose directly. The whitelist
determines which predicates the model may propose. Their instances still require source checking.
A grounding operator τI , implemented by the carrier, maps each query x to a set Ĝ(x) of ground
atoms over Σ; τI implements the vocabulary and role constraints specified by I.
Definition 8 (Bias–slip decomposition). Fix an interface I, a query distribution, and a gold
grounding G(x) per query. The i-th carrier call produces Ĝi (x) = G(x) ⊕ BI (x) ⊕ Si (x)
(symmetric difference of ground-atom sets), where the bias component BI (x) is determined by
I and hence identical across calls, and the slip components Si (x) are i.i.d. across calls. A
component is answer-critical for x if it changes the answer derived from the grounded premises.
Write βans (I) := Prx [BI (x) is answer-critical] and σc := Prx [Si (x) is answer-critical]. The
formalization-failure rate of Definition 6(iii) satisfies εf r (I) ≤ βans (I) + σc , with equality on
disjoint error events; the bias component is perfectly correlated across traces, the slip component
independent (S3).
Definition 9 (Certified deductive rounding). Given premise base B, a trace tr, an edge extractor
E, and a verifier V for a decidable fragment, the verification procedure, called certified rounding
and denoted RE,V (B, tr), deletes every unverifiable edge, discards conclusions without a complete
verified derivation from B, and returns the certified source (Scert , PScert ). A certificate gate admits
into the core only units whose derivation edges all verify.
Definition 10 (Certified depth; recovery depth). Ddcert (q | B) is the minimum derivation depth of
q over derivations whose every edge verifies under V . Letting π be a minimum-depth derivation of
q, the recovery depth drec (q) is the maximum, over edges of π pruned by rounding, of the certified
depth needed to re-derive the pruned subgoal from B.
Definition 11 (Canonical cache key). Under the canonical order of Definition 3, every predicate
unit u has a unique normal form; key(u) is its serialization. Units colliding under key are logically
equivalent, so the key is a sound cache index for certified content.
Lemma 2 (Certified rounding yields a deductive source). For a finite carrier trace and a sound,
decidable fragment verifier, whenever the rounding operator of Definition 9 retains a nonempty set
of units, its output (Scert , PScert ) is a deductive source in the sense of Definition 2.
The proof is given in Appendix D.3. If no unit survives verification, the gate rejects the proposal.

Connection to the main guarantees. Proposition 2 gives the coverage guarantee, Lemma 3
bounds certification overhead, Lemma 1 separates shared bias from independent slips, and Proposi-
tion 3 governs caching. Their proofs appear in Appendices D.4, D.5, D.6, and D.8, respectively.
Remark 1 (Observed joint-error diagnostic). The joint outcomes describe how errors co-occur
across calls, the behavior considered in Lemma 1. Broad-interface L1-HARD has 9 both-wrong
pairs; the restricted interface has none and four correct/wrong pairs. Two calls cannot identify βans
and σc separately or establish independence. The restricted vocabulary blocks direct conclusion
proposals, while false allowed leaf assertions remain possible (Appendix G).
The interface and rounding operator determine what can enter the certified representation. Sec-
tion 3.2 states the guarantees for source admission and certified derivation, together with the voting
bound. Appendix C gives the coverage, kernel-storage, caching, and recovery guarantees.
