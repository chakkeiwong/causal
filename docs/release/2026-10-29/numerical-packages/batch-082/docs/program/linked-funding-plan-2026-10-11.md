and verified. D01/D09 now need new information: the previous transformed
rectangle remains whole-line in 558/600 original near-rank cases. This plan

## Target and new observation law

Retain the same focal intervention value, fixed financial weights, evaluation
history and independent fourfold pilot. At each origin an independent audit
indicator R reveals the true focal candidate across all response horizons
with known inclusion q in (0,1]. The sampled label is exact; unaudited labels
are absent. Audit indicators are conditionally independent across origins,
given the full histories and their declared inclusion probabilities. The
bank would have to verify this information and pay for it in both histories.

Write F for the focal horizon vector, B=(Y1+Y2)/2 and
Z=B+R(F-B)/q. Conditional on the complete history E[Z]=F. Consequently
partialling out the correct action and Z conditional means identifies the
same focal slope without dividing by 2p-1. The independent pilot fits Z and
action using the inherited fixed dictionary, chronological tuning and refit.
Evaluation estimates the two stratum slopes and their fixed half-weighted
financial sum. The alternative inverse-probability estimator uses Z=RF/q.
It has the same expectation but no guaranteed finite-sample variance ordering.

For oracle action residual v and scalar monetary response, the additional
mean-zero term is v(R/q-1)(F-B). Conditioning on all complete histories shows
that its covariance with latent scores and with audit terms at other dates
is zero. Its lag-zero contribution is E[v^2(1/q-1)(F-B)^2], with the stratum
normalizers included. The latent score retains the inherited finite-horizon
innovation covariance. With fixed q bounded away from zero, stable fixed-
dictionary pilot rates and M=4T, the nuisance product and action-square
remainders are o(T^-1/2). The selected estimator therefore uses a pointwise
normal scalar interval with the untapered horizon covariance. No exact,
uniform, shrinking-q or false-exogeneity coverage claim is made.
