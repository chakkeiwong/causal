# LA03 author review

12 October 2026. C1–C3 pass for a source-grounded comparative draft and
analytical examples, not an empirical model comparison or MR02 closure.

The methods matrix distinguishes thirteen mechanisms and connects each to
audited primary sources, a scientific role, a counterexample and a bounded
remedy. The failure register gives thirteen concrete evaluation routes without
claiming those proposed experiments have run. The chapter develops the
comparisons in banking terms, including targets, assumption changes,
information clocks, latent dynamics and complete-request cost.

The response-count review resolves LA01's covariance concern in one explicit
model. For iid categorical responses, the variance of the same-batch difference
is [pN+pY-(pN-pY)^2]/n. Under a binary symmetric null with n=100, the inspected
independent-proportion denominator has half the true variance; the nominal
one-sided 1% rule first rejects at 59 NO responses and its exact rejection
probability is 0.04431304005703379. This is an independently coded finite sum,
not execution of causal_GPT or a live response trial. Real model response
dependence and bank conditional-independence validity remain separate issues.

For C2, the chapter retains CPDAG/PAG ambiguity and the union of graph-specific
effect sets. The elementary graph-retention/within-graph coverage union bound
states its two required premises explicitly; neither is claimed for arbitrary
LLM discovery. The inherited R04 negative result remains open. LA02's equal-law
two-DAG example demonstrates distinct effects without deleting the less fluent
alternative.

For C3, every remedy has an evaluation route and a stated limit. Source
grounding does not prove applicability; agent agreement does not supply
independent observations; response calibration does not prove causal
calibration; finite predictive success does not identify arbitrary mechanisms.
Temporal discovery, sequential batches and longitudinal policy targets remain
distinct. Paper claims, project derivations and static software observations
are labeled separately.

The draft compiles into the interim LLM reader. That compilation is not final
page acceptance: this session's image budget is exhausted, and a fresh session
must perform rendered visual inspection. The current chapter is also shorter
than the final L4 allocation. LA07 retains the full eight-chapter coverage and
96–112 substantive-body-page obligation. No final-repair or visual-review
claim is made by recording this draft package.
