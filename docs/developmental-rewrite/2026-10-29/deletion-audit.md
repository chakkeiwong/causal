# Post-execution preservation and deletion audit

29 October 2026. This audit was run after the last manuscript content edit and final compilation. Its scope is the 1,813-page scientific continuation and all of its reachable sources, not only the earlier 1,795-page review copy.

## Result

**Pass: no unaccounted substantive deletion.** Every one of the 444 inherited TeX files has a mapped successor, is reachable exactly once, and appears in the completed TeX recorder. All 1,511 protected files and source snapshots retain their recorded hashes. The final PDF is 1,836 pages; page growth is an observation, not evidence of preservation or quality.

The complete original supporting treatments remain inside the delivered PDF. They were not preserved only in an archive. All inherited citation occurrences and labels remain. All inherited numerical tokens in body sources remain. Mathematical passages, including inline mathematics, are unchanged except for the explicitly reviewed opening target and notation corrections below. The audit reconstructs each successor file from the frozen source by replaying the exact edit ledger; any unrecorded difference fails the check.

## Limits and failures retained

The first audit read the TeX recorder while the last build pass was still writing and withheld a pass. Repeating it sequentially after build completion confirmed every inherited input. The early source payload lost backslash escapes; it was repaired before successful assembly. The first build exposed an overflowing servicing equation and three-digit contents-number widths; those defects were corrected and rebuilt. A rendered CLI option became an en dash; a literal listing restored the copyable double hyphen. The final semantic review restored the opening's internal-transfer and competitor examples. None of these failures was reclassified as a scientific result.

A bibliography style file was discovered outside the initial TeX-input traversal. Its separate observed binding and copy are in additional-asset-binding.json; this later observation is not backdated to the baseline freeze.

This is an author/coordinator audit with exact preservation checks and a separate semantic review pass. It is not independent scientific approval of every retained claim. Six new compressed previews were rendered and saved but not forwarded because the session had already exhausted its six-image allowance. Fresh visual and human editorial acceptance remain pending. The detailed, very large supporting corpus has not received a new sentence-by-sentence rewrite or a full mathematical referee review.
