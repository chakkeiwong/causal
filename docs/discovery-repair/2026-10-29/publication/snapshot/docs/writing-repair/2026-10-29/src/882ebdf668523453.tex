\section[Bayesian SCM and hidden-state workers]{Bayesian structural models and hidden states: a common contract across five language families}
\label{sec:exec25-bsw}

A bank selecting a Bayesian or hidden-state worker must distinguish the model it is willing to defend from the computation it wants to perform. A posterior can be numerically stable for a model that does not identify the effect of a servicing intervention. A state smoother can be accurate for retrospective explanation while using information unavailable at the original credit decision. A graph may represent a joint probability law without specifying what changes under an intervention. The proposed product therefore needs a common scientific contract across R, Python, Java, C++ and other relevant languages, with backend-specific computation and refusal rules. The comparison extends the already reviewed PyMC, NumPyro, bnlearn, Dynamax, graphical-model and state-space records with pinned Stan, RStan, NIMBLE, Keanu and Turing originals.~\citep{exec23_pymc_sources,exec23_numpyro_sources,exec24_bnlearn_sources,exec19_dynamax_source,exec24_libdai_source,exec24_opengm_source,exec25_bsw_stan,exec25_bsw_rstan,exec25_bsw_nimble,exec25_bsw_keanu,exec25_bsw_turing,exec25_bsw_choice}

The new acquisition retains 57 original files across five repositories, with immutable Git identities and local integrity records. Four bounded discovery queries do not establish ecosystem saturation. The selected Keanu revision is from September 2021 and is treated as a historical reference, not evidence of current supported service. The other selected revisions precede the 7 October 2026 cutoff. Declared development versions and dependency compatibility ranges are not proofs that particular distributed binaries were acquired or installed together. Reused audits retain their original identity, date and scope; they are not counted as newly collected evidence. No downloaded implementation was executed, and no new scholarly paper is admitted through a package's bibliography or comments.~\citep{exec25_bsw_choice}

\subsection{The posterior is conditional on an identification decision}

Let $D$ be the retained observations, $M$ a candidate structural model, $\theta$ its parameters, and $Z_{1:T}$ a latent-state path. A numerical worker may target
\[
 p(\theta,Z_{1:T}\mid D,M)
 \ \propto\ p(D\mid\theta,Z_{1:T},M)
 p(Z_{1:T}\mid\theta,M)p(\theta\mid M).
\]
This expression identifies the conditioning information and prior but does not specify whether the intervention contrast is identified from the observed law. For an exact witness, take a fair binary $U$ and compare $M_1:A=U,Y=A$ with $M_0:A=U,Y=U$. The observed pairs coincide under the two models. The intervention effects differ: one under $M_1$ and zero under $M_0$. Equal observational likelihoods leave posterior model odds equal to prior model odds. Prior odds of $3:2$ give a posterior mean effect of $3/5$, entirely dependent on that unresolved model choice. More draws from either model cannot create the missing assignment information. This is a mathematical limitation under the stated models, not a defect in a sampler.~\citep{exec23_pymc_reference,exec25_bsw_choice}

The interface should consequently return two separate statements: a computational assessment conditional on the declared model, and a scientific assessment of the intervention assumptions. Sensitivity across observationally indistinguishable models is more informative than reporting one narrow interval without its model restriction. Additional measurements, a justified assignment design, or an explicitly narrower target can change this situation. Universal identification of an unrestricted hidden state from arbitrary observed banking fields is not a promised repair. A model-writing LLM may retrieve candidate restrictions and explain their consequences, but cannot establish them by producing valid model syntax.~\citep{exec23_numpyro_reference,exec24_bnlearn_contract,exec25_bsw_choice}

Posterior parameter uncertainty also differs from state and observation uncertainty. Under a $\operatorname{Beta}(2,3)$ prior and six successes plus four failures, the posterior is $\operatorname{Beta}(8,7)$. Its mean is $8/15$ and variance $7/450$. For two future Bernoulli trials sharing this unknown probability, the posterior predictive count variance is $119/225$. Plugging in the mean probability gives only $112/225$, omitting $7/225$. The law of total variance explains the difference: uncertainty about the common probability creates dependence between future outcomes. This exact reference checks more than a posterior point estimate and gives a small public oracle for whether a worker propagates the uncertainty promised by its result type. It does not validate the causal interpretation of the success indicator.~\citep{exec25_bsw_choice}

\subsection{R: explicit sampler configurations and component-specific diagnostics}

RStan and NIMBLE offer different proposed worker routes. The inspected RStan metadata declares a development version, an R interface to Stan, GPL version 3 or later for RStan, and dependencies including StanHeaders. The separately inspected StanHeaders metadata has a BSD three-clause declaration. The standalone Stan revision reviewed here declares version 2.40.0, whereas the selected RStan and StanHeaders metadata declare 2.39.0.9000. These are independent source pins; compatibility of a particular combined installation must be qualified rather than inferred from the shared project name. A direct R worker can retain RStan's model and diagnostic objects; a port must separately reproduce model compilation, unconstrained transformations, generated quantities and numerical diagnostics.~\citep{exec25_bsw_rstan,exec25_bsw_stan}

The inspected automatic-warning routine and explicit diagnostic helper illustrate why a common label is insufficient. Missing divergence or tree-depth columns contribute zero to the automatic warning counts, and the fixed-parameter algorithm bypasses that warning routine. This does not mean an unavailable diagnostic was measured and passed. The automatic energy warning uses parameter count divided by sample energy variance, while the explicit BFMI helper uses average squared successive energy increments divided by sample variance. For the illustrative energy sequence $(0,10,0,10)$ and one parameter, sample variance is $100/3$. The former ratio is $3/100$, and the latter is $9/4$. At the selected threshold $1/5$ they give different flags. The proposal should record the function, formula, inputs and missing-diagnostic status; it should not silently relabel every quantity as the same universal convergence certificate. These are exact consequences of the inspected formulas, not native results obtained from a fitted bank model.~\citep{exec25_bsw_rstan,exec25_bsw_choice}

The same routine applies its selected R-hat and effective-sample-size thresholds. Such diagnostics are useful evidence about sampled chains, but an apparently satisfactory chain can remain conditional on an inadequate model or an unvisited mode. A two-mode target with equally weighted means $-10$ and $10$ has mean zero; a collection restricted to the positive mode has mean ten. Replicating that restricted behavior across chains does not correct the omitted mode. The public experiment should include deliberately separated modes and known moments, alongside ordinary conjugate examples, and retain all attempts rather than only chains that returned attractive diagnostics.~\citep{exec25_bsw_rstan,exec25_bsw_choice}

NIMBLE's selected configuration distinguishes sampled nodes, monitored nodes, conjugate updates and scalar or block updates. Its posterior-predictive grouping checks the downstream sampling set; the corresponding sampler simulates downstream stochastic and deterministic nodes, recalculates stochastic log probabilities and updates saved state. The build path can reorder predictive samplers after other updates, but refuses certain custom orders and option-dependent mixtures of predictive and nonpredictive nodes. In the inspected default multivariate assignment branch, partially observed multivariate-normal nodes receive a specific partial-normal sampler; other partially observed multivariate distributions are refused there. This bounded statement concerns that branch, not every possible user-written NIMBLE algorithm. A bank adapter must preserve which values are posterior states and which are newly generated outcomes.~\citep{exec25_bsw_nimble}

Chain construction and continuation have separate meanings. Without supplied initial values, the selected \texttt{runMCMC} path uses the current model values for the first chain and the preceding chain's endpoint for later chains. It has explicit per-chain seed handling. This is not a claim that every such run is invalid, but it is a reason to avoid describing the chains as independently initialized without evidence. The wrapper does not expose the low-level continuation option. At the lower level, first execution forces a reset; later continuation can retain adaptation and append draws, while a separate flag can clear retained draws. Nonzero burn-in is refused during continuation, and a changed thinning interval can warn if a prior save cycle is incomplete. Persistence of these live objects is not itself a demonstrated durable crash-recovery protocol.~\citep{exec25_bsw_nimble}

For a causal R workflow, bnlearn remains a distinct alternative when its supported fitted graphical-model operations match the target; its reviewed graph surgery, Gaussian fitted paths and other fallback classes must not be conflated. For state-space work, bssm's R interface and C++ kernels offer another route with separately reviewed innovation thresholds, posterior subsampling and importance weights. RStan, NIMBLE, bnlearn and bssm should therefore be selected by the model, intervention and uncertainty contract, not ranked as interchangeable implementations of a generic Bayesian command. Conditional direct reuse with typed adapters is the default proposal; a cross-language reimplementation requires a demonstrated operational benefit and a much larger conformance obligation.~\citep{exec24_bnlearn_sources,exec24_bnlearn_contract,exec25_cdyn_bssm,exec25_cdyn_choice,exec25_bsw_choice}

\subsection{Python: model surgery, posterior prediction and state information clocks}

The previously inspected PyMC and NumPyro records already provide detailed primary evidence, so the repair here is a comparative scientific contract rather than another claim of fresh acquisition. PyMC distinguishes observed-variable transformation from intervention and controls whether stored random or deterministic quantities are frozen or regenerated. NumPyro uses execution handlers, distinguishes conditioning, substitution and intervention, and has its own predictive substitution and discrete-inference routes. A converter must state which model variables remain conditioned on historical evidence, which mechanisms change, which outcomes are regenerated, and whether the requested result is a posterior average, a decoded state, or a cross-world counterfactual. Name and shape matching alone cannot establish equivalence.~\citep{exec23_pymc_sources,exec23_numpyro_sources}

The existing exact references give common tests of those differences: association versus intervention under hidden confounding, recomputed versus retained deterministic expressions, posterior averaging versus a most-probable-state decision, and distinct cross-world couplings with identical intervention marginals. The implementation should carry those references into one request schema and require each worker to state which requests it supports. General cross-world identification should be refused when the model has not supplied a defensible coupling, even if a backend can mechanically reuse random values.~\citep{exec23_pymc_reference,exec23_numpyro_reference,exec25_bsw_choice}

Dynamax and PyDLM occupy a different role. The reviewed Dynamax HMM interface uses initial, transition and emission factors; it does not automatically introduce an observed-action logger into that factorization. Its filtering and smoothing information sets differ. Its EM helper returns a parameter/objective sequence under a specified iteration budget, which is not a global convergence or causal-identification certificate. The reviewed PyDLM path has its own missing-step and innovation conventions, while dynamic-feature availability remains a caller obligation. These records justify model-specific state workers and public finite-path comparisons, not a universal causal interpretation of every hidden credit factor.~\citep{exec19_dynamax_source,exec19_dynamax_reference,exec25_pydyn_pydlm,exec25_pydyn_choice}

An exact information-clock example makes this operational. Let a binary customer state persist for two periods, with prior probability $1/2$. Each observed signal agrees with that state with probability $3/4$, conditionally independently. After a positive first signal, filtering gives probability $3/4$ that the state is one. After two positive signals, the retrospective probability for the first-period state is $9/10$. If an action pays USD100 in state one and loses USD400 in state zero, its first-period expected value is USD$-25$, while the future-informed calculation gives USD50. Acting on the latter as though it were available at the first decision can change the action. Applying that mistaken rule to 10,000 otherwise comparable first-signal cases gives an illustrative expected loss of USD250,000 under the first-period law. The numbers are invented exact examples, not measured bank performance; the point is that a correct smoother can answer the wrong decision-time question.~\citep{exec25_bsw_choice}

Python qualification should therefore compare supported finite models against enumeration, preserve exact observation and action timestamps, and separate state filtering, smoothing, prediction and posterior parameter integration. GPU or compilation claims need actual device and full-cost measurements. Existing PyMC and NumPyro diagnostic and continuation evidence should remain backend-specific; matching a field called ESS does not prove the same definition, chain layout or diagnostic availability. The current source evidence supports conditional worker routes, while full ecosystem coverage, installed dependency rights and native qualification remain incomplete.~\citep{exec23_pymc_sources,exec23_numpyro_sources,exec25_bsw_choice}

\subsection{Java: observation status, adaptation and retained samples}

The selected Keanu original provides a historical Java probabilistic-programming comparison. Its network separates latent vertices from observed vertices and includes both in the joint probability calculation. The inspected \texttt{observe} method sets a value and observation status; it does not itself remove incoming factors. The probability calculator still evaluates probabilistic vertex contributions. Accordingly, observing an action is not established as structural intervention by that method. A normal value setter also leaves an already observed vertex unchanged. A model-conversion test must verify the graph and observation state rather than assume that setting a Java value has replaced a mechanism. Numeric deterministic vertices face specific observation restrictions in the selected method.~\citep{exec25_bsw_keanu}

The default Metropolis--Hastings configuration uses a prior proposal and single-variable selection. The selected NUTS setup obtains continuous latent variables, expects a supported gradient model and checks the initial joint probability. These choices differ in support and proposal geometry. One should not advertise a universal mixed-state NUTS route from the existence of both APIs. Moreover, the selected NUTS default adapts for 1,000 steps, while its generic sample generator defaults to dropping zero initial steps. The adapter must deliberately align adaptation and retained inference draws. The presence of an adaptation setting does not imply that the returned samples have automatically excluded it.~\citep{exec25_bsw_keanu}

Sample accounting is explicit in the generator: the requested count includes transitions that will be dropped or skipped, and retained positions start at zero after the initial drop. With eleven transitions, two dropped and thinning interval four, the remaining positions $0,4,8$ give three retained draws, not the floor of $9/4$. This is a useful boundary fixture for translating budgets between libraries. It also shows why an API parameter called sample count should not be mapped without its definition. General NUTS statistics are disabled by default in the selected builder, but post-adaptation divergence storage and warnings have a separate branch. The correct contract preserves both facts; it would be inaccurate to infer either complete diagnostic availability or complete silence from the general statistics flag alone.~\citep{exec25_bsw_keanu,exec25_bsw_choice}

Tetrad and JDemetra+ supply substantive Java alternatives in different roles. The reviewed Tetrad temporal operations construct lag identities and enforce selected restrictions; those graph operations are not themselves Bayesian parameter integration. JDemetra+'s reviewed state-space paths propagate process covariance through missing measurements and distinguish diffuse initialization and regression uncertainty. A selected overload's forecast mean and covariance clocks still need qualification. The Java Smile statistical library is also distinct from BayesFusion's SMILE engine; a shared spelling cannot transfer vendor causal features, entitlement or licensing to a different project. Conditional reuse should retain these boundaries, with historical Keanu maintenance and runtime costs explicitly unresolved.~\citep{exec25_jdyn_tetrad,exec25_jdyn_jdplus,exec25_jdyn_choice,exec24_smile_choice,exec25_bsw_choice}

\subsection{C++: service success, probability targets and approximation error}

Stan's selected adaptive diagonal-metric NUTS service illustrates why process success, service return codes, retained draws and scientific acceptance must all be inspected. Configuration failures and propagated sampling exceptions return different codes. Inside the inspected adaptive-run helper, however, an exception during initial step-size search is caught, logged and followed by a void return before sample headers and draws. The outer service can then reach its normal return. This is a source-level conditional path, not a reproduced native incident. Its qualification test should provoke the relevant branch and check the log, return code, expected draw count and diagnostic files together. A wrapper that checks only a zero code could miss an empty scientific result.~\citep{exec25_bsw_stan}

When sampling proceeds, warmup, adaptation shutdown, adapted-state writing and retained transitions have distinct stages. The selected diagnose service compares automatic derivatives with finite differences at the evaluated point. That local derivative check does not establish global exploration, a proper posterior, correct missing-data assumptions or causal identification. The product should expose these as different evidence types with different acceptance criteria. Tests on conjugate laws and multimodal finite references complement local numerical checks rather than being replaced by them.~\citep{exec25_bsw_stan,exec25_bsw_choice}

Pathfinder supplies another computational choice whose approximation and failure denominators must remain visible. In the inspected multi-path branch without PSIS resampling, no successful path leads to a software error, while partial path success can produce a warning and an overall normal return. Successful-path draws alone cannot establish that all requested initializations or regions were adequately represented. The public trial must retain requested paths, attempted paths, failures, surviving draws, approximation configuration and full costs. Approximate optimization-based draws and MCMC output should be compared against the same declared target and calibrated oracle; they are not interchangeable merely because both yield arrays called posterior samples.~\citep{exec25_bsw_stan,exec25_bsw_choice}

The already reviewed libDAI and OpenGM records clarify other C++ boundaries. libDAI operates on factorized probability models, with exact and approximate inference choices, conditioning operations, factor-order rules and numerical limits. Conditioning through clamping is not causal surgery without a structural interpretation. OpenGM supplies graphical-model representations and optimization/inference interfaces with solver-dependent capabilities; a minimizing assignment is not a collection of posterior paths, and an inherited base marginal interface does not prove that every concrete solver computes marginals. Optional commercial solver dependencies also require separate rights and costs.~\citep{exec24_libdai_source,exec24_libdai_choice,exec24_opengm_source,exec24_opengm_choice}

The bssm and structural-VAR kernels add state and forecast alternatives with their own assumptions. The reviewed bssm filter's thresholds, process-noise propagation and response-versus-state prediction semantics should remain visible. The reviewed structural forecast source conditions on supplied forecast restrictions and retains path-dependent quantities; a Gaussian conditional forecast is not an intervention merely because the analyst calls the restriction a policy. The appropriate C++ integration is therefore conditional use of explicitly identified computational targets, with factor permutations, observation/intervention transformations, stopping states and uncertainty outputs qualified separately. A wholesale port of a high-level probabilistic language would duplicate compilation and inference semantics without a demonstrated proposal benefit.~\citep{exec25_cdyn_bssm,exec25_cdyn_bsvars,exec25_cdyn_choice,exec25_bsw_choice}

\subsection{Julia and other languages: component composition does not prove a valid joint kernel}

The selected Turing source declares version 0.49.0 and a Julia 1.10.8 compatibility requirement, alongside separately versioned DynamicPPL, AbstractMCMC, AdvancedHMC and other dependencies. The reviewed HMC wrapper resolves adaptation and initial-draw removal for a fresh chain. When an initial state is supplied, the selected branch requests zero additional adaptation and zero initial dropping. That is an in-memory continuation contract, not demonstrated persistence across changed model code, dependency versions or interrupted serialization. A public restart fixture must bind the model, data, transformations, sampler state, RNG lineage and actual runtime artifacts.~\citep{exec25_bsw_turing}

Gibbs composition needs an additional scientific and numerical review. The inspected implementation conditions each block on current non-target values and checks appearances, disappearances and missing reported values. Its documentation separately warns that support changes across blocks can destroy irreducibility even when each conditional update has the right invariant law. A binary selector that places a continuous variable on disjoint intervals can trap separate blocks. The variable-set checks do not prove that the joint chain can traverse the intended support. A changed block design or a supported joint update may repair a particular model, but a general repair cannot be inferred from interface compatibility alone.~\citep{exec25_bsw_turing}

The analytical \texttt{GibbsConditional} component draws from user-supplied conditional distributions and refuses use outside the intended Gibbs context. The callback's correctness is a substantive obligation. For a small finite joint law, the qualification oracle can enumerate the full conditionals and compare the callback to them before any long run. For continuous conjugate examples, the beta reference supplies exact moments. These checks establish a bounded model-specific route; successful construction of the callback does not certify that arbitrary user distributions are compatible full conditionals of the intended joint model.~\citep{exec25_bsw_turing,exec25_bsw_choice}

The particle path makes the time contract equally concrete. The inspected implementation advances model executions at likelihood statements, maintains particle-specific random generators and refuses models requiring threadsafe evaluation in the selected path. Particle Gibbs retains a reference trajectory; ordinary sequential Monte Carlo and conditional sweeps therefore have different roles. The ESS-based resampling comparison is less-than-or-equal to the configured threshold times particle count, and a nonfinite total weight is explicitly refused. Availability of a particle count or a completed sweep is not proof that the latent sequence is observable or that an action-logging mechanism has been modeled.~\citep{exec25_bsw_turing}

Particle weight ESS is also not MCMC ESS or an ancestor-diversity certificate. Weights $(0.9,0.1)$ give $1/(0.9^2+0.1^2)=50/41$. Two equal weights give two, even if the two particles descend from one identical ancestor. The output should retain weight concentration and genealogy as separate diagnostics. Nor does a log-normalizer estimate acquire every property of a normalizer estimate: random estimates $1/2$ and $3/2$ with equal probability have mean one, but mean log $\tfrac12\log(3/4)$ differs from log mean zero. This elementary example motivates repeated public calibration of the reported quantity without asserting any uninspected theorem about the selected particle implementation.~\citep{exec25_bsw_turing,exec25_bsw_choice}

StateSpaceModels.jl and gretl remain distinct, already reviewed alternatives for classical state-space computation. The selected Julia fitting paths retain fitted hyperparameters, numerical penalties and particular missing-coordinate handling, while gretl has its own missing-step propagation and dataset-layout assumptions. A fitted state-space likelihood with plug-in parameters should not be described as full Bayesian model averaging without an additional parameter-integration mechanism. Other language families and candidates remain outside this bounded comparison; their omission is recorded rather than interpreted as absence of relevant software.~\citep{exec25_odyn_ssm,exec25_odyn_gretl,exec25_odyn_choice,exec25_bsw_choice}

\subsection{A public prototype portfolio and a defensible integration decision}

The repair is an executable portfolio of independent public qualification tasks. The first task enumerates observationally equivalent structural models and verifies that a worker's reported effect remains conditional on its chosen model. The second compares filtering and smoothing on a finite hidden-state law with distinct event, availability and decision clocks. The third checks conjugate posterior and predictive moments, including the dependence created by shared parameter uncertainty. The fourth exercises missing diagnostics, empty outputs, partial approximation failures, adaptation removal and sample-count conventions. The fifth checks conditioned versus intervened graphs, factor permutations and deliberately incompatible or trapped block updates. None requires authentic bank data merely to justify why the proposed experiment is useful.~\citep{exec25_bsw_choice}

Every trial needs a scientific oracle and a refusal oracle, a declared backend version, dependency and license record, full attempt denominator and total cost. The scientific owner defines identification assumptions and state meaning; the numerical owner defines supported model classes and diagnostics; the integration owner preserves types, graphs, clocks and random-state lineage; the operator measures compilation, adaptation, sampling, prediction, storage and recovery. These are proposed responsibilities to be assigned, not evidence that a production team or service already exists. Direct adoption should preserve a library's supported native semantics. A process adapter is preferable when it can retain those semantics with explicit conversion and custody. Porting is justified only by a concrete runtime requirement and a credible parity budget. Unsupported causal claims and ambiguous observation/intervention requests should be rejected with the missing assumptions stated.~\citep{exec25_bsw_choice}

The selected license declarations support further review, not whole-distribution clearance: Stan and NIMBLE have BSD-style declarations, RStan has GPL version 3 or later with a distinct StanHeaders notice, and Keanu and Turing have MIT declarations. Numerical delegates, compilers, optional components, complete installed artifacts and commercial alternatives retain their separate obligations. The five language-cell decisions are consequently inconclusive for complete coverage and native admission, while recording concrete conditional reuse and public-test routes. Known source and adapter gaps can be addressed independently. Unknown assignment truth, unrestricted hidden-state identification, arbitrary cross-world dependence and universal uncertainty calibration require information, restrictions or narrower claims. A well-computed posterior remains conditional on a defensible model and information set.~\citep{exec25_bsw_stan,exec25_bsw_rstan,exec25_bsw_nimble,exec25_bsw_keanu,exec25_bsw_turing,exec25_bsw_choice}
