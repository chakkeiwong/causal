{\large Sponsor decision brief --- author research release\par}
11 October 2026, Asia/Shanghai\end{center}

The local research and integration wave is complete. The proposed bank product is not accepted. The program now has evidence-based dispositions for eleven of sixteen work packages, with five institutional packages awaiting external inputs and, for final acceptance, successful capability gates. None of the ten gaps has met every institutional closure condition.

The opportunity is a common causal-analysis service for funding response, sequential servicing, intervention queries after discovery, and trustworthy observations. Its output must pair a decision quantity with units, timing, assumptions, uncertainty and the conditions under which it should refuse. The required standard runs from the banking question through identification, estimation and uncertainty to reproducible financial consequences. Independent scientific review, bank validation and investment approval are additional gates.

\section*{Results that change the decision}
\textbf{Funding: a scoped success with a demanding information contract.}
A supplied randomized action law, truthful sampled links and genuinely independent calendars remove a fitted-ratio mean error. All six valid synthetic cells meet the predeclared minimum-coverage screen. This does not create independent calendars from one time series or validate the bank's assignment mechanism. Baseline median interval width is about USD 748,000; the persistence cell is USD 1.975 million against synthetic net value USD 752,000. Statistical coverage alone does not make that precision useful.

\textbf{Servicing: fixed-policy inference in a selected nonlinear family.}
Truthful representative state audits, actual historical action probabilities and independent customer trajectories support a fixed observable policy. Five valid cells and one transition-misspecification robustness cell pass the screen. Baseline median interval width is USD 735 per trajectory; scarce audits increase it to USD 2,339 against value about USD 1,174. An incorrect historical action probability produces only one covered request out of 512. Unknown optimal-policy inference remains open.

\textbf{Three material scientific limits remain.}
Joint pair/calendar/gold-label inference fails the selected finite screen under persistence and other regimes. Temporal graph unions retain ambiguity correctly but can return the whole real line. Corrected sparse learning misses the screen at higher dimension and larger support; omitted nonlinear nuisance structure fails outright. These negative findings are retained, not counted as successful capabilities.

\textbf{Local implementation works within its measured scope.}
Full linked-outcome pilot and evaluation streaming matches dense computation. Result records preserve finite, disconnected, unbounded and unavailable answers. Real local process failures recover without duplicate requests or premature publication. At 120,000 evaluation origins and 480,000 pilot origins, dense/streamed workloads take about 1.08/1.71 seconds and about 409 MiB each. There is no consistent performance gain and no bank-platform acceptance.

\section*{The investment case remains conditional}
\[
\text{Net value}=\text{held-out decision-loss improvement}-\text{all incremental costs}.
\]
Costs include linked histories, gold labels, independent calendars, implementation, compute, scientific review, analyst work, rework and operation. These are different services and must be priced separately. No actual bank prices, useful-width threshold, measured analyst saving or institutional return has been supplied. Faster computation would not by itself establish economic value.

A reasonable next decision is whether to resource a bounded validation effort with named owners and explicit information requirements. This brief does not authorize deployment, approve investment or claim that broader capabilities have been solved.

\section*{Reading and evidence}
The research extension contains the mathematical argument and all five final result tables. The combined reader preserves the 259-page predecessor and appends the current extension. The earlier 16-page sponsor proposal remains a historical baseline; this dated brief states the current evidence and limitations.

There are 22,528 final requests across five current studies. Saved journals reconstruct their summaries; fifteen representative first/middle/last requests were regenerated. Corrected graph and learner studies use fresh frozen attempts, and the corrected engineering work includes actual crash trials. The original exploratory requests and superseded claims remain available for audit. Independent likelihood, score and quadrature checks for servicing occurred after evaluation; that timing is disclosed.

Coverage screens use simultaneous Monte Carlo lower bounds at a .95 minimum, with 99\% primary intervals or regions and separate width/availability reporting. These are selected synthetic regimes, not uniform guarantees. Literature coverage retains the 7 October 2026 cutoff. Local primary PDFs and inspected claim anchors are hash-audited; no comprehensive new literature search is asserted.

The deliverable is a reviewed author research proposal with scoped positive and negative results. Independent review, authentic bank evidence, target-platform acceptance and actual economics determine whether it can become an accepted product.
