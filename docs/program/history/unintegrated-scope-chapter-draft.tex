\chapter{Scope completion: method choice under the difficult regimes}
\label{ch:scope-completion}

The preceding chapters establish bounded operations. The remaining question is
whether those operations are adequate for the broader product proposed for a
banking group. This chapter closes that question by taking the adverse regimes
seriously. It selects a primary method and a substantive challenger for
persistent responses and panels, privately logged longitudinal decisions,
temporal discovery, observation construction, and reusable software. The
comparisons are conditional experiments and a public financial reproduction;
they are not evidence of a universal estimator or of a bank's causal truth.

\section{Dependent responses need a method hierarchy}
\label{s5:dependent-hierarchy}

Consider a structural response $\theta_h=\Theta_{h,i1}/\Theta_{0,11}$ to a
shock selected by an external instrument $Z_t$. The direct moment vector for a
fixed horizon set $\mathcal H$ is
\[
 m_t(\theta)=Z_t\left((X_{i,t+h})_{h\in\mathcal H,i\in\mathcal I},
 X_{1t}\right)'\begin{pmatrix}1\\-\theta'\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The zero of $\E m_t(\theta)$ is the normalized response only when the
instrument is relevant for the target innovation and excluded from every
other innovation, including leads and lags. The denominator is therefore part
of the estimand. It is not a first-stage statistic that can be discarded after
the curve has been plotted.

For stationary Gaussian linear dynamics with a fixed stability margin, the
score array has summable product covariances. A Bartlett estimator
\[
 \widehat\Omega_L=\widehat\Gamma_0+
 \sum_{\ell=1}^L\left(1-\frac{\ell}{L+1}\right)
 (\widehat\Gamma_\ell+\widehat\Gamma_\ell'),
 \qquad
 \widehat\Gamma_\ell=n^{-1}\sum_{t=\ell+1}^n
 (m_t-\bar m)(m_{t-\ell}-\bar m)'
\]
is positive semidefinite by the moving-sum representation. Its consistency is
conditional on the stated bandwidth, moment and dependence rates
\citep{newey1986}. The residual-test set obtained by inverting
\[
 n\{\bar m_j-\theta_j\bar m_d\}^2
 \leq c_{\alpha,j}^2(\widehat\Omega_{jj}-2\theta_j\widehat\Omega_{jd}
 +\theta_j^2\widehat\Omega_{dd})
\]
does not divide by a weak $\bar m_d$. It can consequently be disconnected or
unbounded. This is the right result object near weak normalization.

Lag-augmented local projection is a different target and a different
assumption. For a VAR with lag polynomial $A(L)$, the regression of
$y_{1,t+h}$ on $(y_t',\ldots,y_{t-p}')'$ includes one extra lag so that the
coefficient on $y_t$ estimates the reduced-form response under the
innovation restrictions of the lag-augmentation theorem
\citep{lpaug2020}. It is not an external-IV estimate merely because both
procedures produce a horizon curve. The uniform result covers a specified
root class and horizon growth; it does not license an arbitrary cointegrated,
weak-instrument or nonlinear bank process. Simultaneous significance bands
also require an argument beyond the marginal interval in that source
\citep{inoue2023}.

Prewhitening is a covariance estimator, not an identification device. If a
score process obeys $u_t=A u_{t-1}+e_t$ and $I-A$ is nonsingular, a zero-frequency
covariance estimate can be recoloured by $(I-A')^{-1}$. An estimated unstable
or ill-conditioned prefilter is a failure diagnostic. It cannot repair an
invalid instrument. The implemented comparator therefore reports the
prefilter radius and recolouring condition and retains its interval only as a
dependence alternative.

The preregistered response study makes the hierarchy concrete. With 400
replications and 26-coordinate simultaneous sets, the short Bartlett window
covered .940 in the stationary case, .905 under persistent instrument noise,
and .758 near the unit boundary. A four-times window covered .928, .935 and
.933 in those same cases and .973 in the long near-unit case. Prefiltering
covered .935, .923 and .775. In the invalid-instrument case the corresponding
coverages were .240, .363 and .273. The larger window is a defensible
challenger in this fixed family, but its better result is not a theorem for
future banks. The product should expose both windows, report the sensitivity,
and withhold a calibrated recommendation when the stability or exclusion
diagnostics fail.

The scalar lag-augmented comparison covered .920 at $\rho=.8$, .943 at
$\rho=.985$ and .928 at the unit root in 400 replications. These are marginal
intervals for a scalar AR response, not evidence that LP inference solves the
external-IV curve problem. The distinction prevents a method name from
silently changing the intervention.

\section{Dynamic panels: differencing and system moments are not interchangeable}
\label{s5:dynamic-panel}

For the short panel
\[
 y_{it}=\alpha_i+\rho y_{i,t-1}+\beta x_{it}+v_{it},\qquad
 \Delta y_{it}=\rho\Delta y_{i,t-1}+\beta\Delta x_{it}+\Delta v_{it},
\]
the lag $y_{i,t-s}$ is an instrument for the differenced equation only under
the corresponding serial-correlation and initial-condition restrictions. The
valid moments are
\[
 \E[y_{i,t-s}\Delta v_{it}]=0,\quad s\geq2,
\]
with different admissible lags for predetermined and endogenous $x$. The
moment count grows as $(T-2)(T-1)/2$ for the simple AR case
\citep{arellano1991}. Instrument compression is therefore an inferential
choice: it changes the weighting problem and can trade finite-sample bias
against variance.

System moments add level equations instrumented by lagged differences. They
need restrictions linking initial deviations to individual effects and are
especially attractive only when persistent levels make differenced instruments
weak. The two-step covariance also needs a finite-sample correction because
the estimated optimal weight contributes uncertainty; the Windmeijer correction
is not a cosmetic standard-error switch. These restrictions and corrections
are discussed in the modern account of initial conditions and persistent
series, which also records the weak-instrument failure of differenced GMM
\citep{blundellbond2023}.

The implementation uses collapsed, scaled moments and one- and two-step
linear GMM as a transparent reference. In 400 replications with $N=800$ and
$T=8$, difference GMM covered .955 and system GMM .958 in the stationary
case. Under an initial-condition violation, difference GMM retained .950
coverage while system one-step fell to .033 and system two-step to zero. With
serially correlated shocks, every nominal interval had zero coverage and the
coefficient bias was about .42 for the action regressor. Those failures are
the method decision: system moments are not enabled by default, and the
product must require an initial-condition declaration, AR diagnostics and
instrument-count report before exposing them.

The panel route is separate from the common-shock route. If 96 banks share
one date shock, the date is the information unit even when the table has
millions of account rows. Date aggregation and HAC are required; row-iid
standard errors are invalid. Factors, cointegration and breaks are challengers
with their own restrictions, not automatic repairs for a common shock. The
source-led method record and the study receipt retain all these regimes and
their failures.

\section{Private logging and measurement-supported latent states}
\label{s5:private-logger}

An action chosen using a hidden state is part of the observation law. Let
$S_t$ be a $K$-state customer state, $O_t$ a signal, $A_t$ an action and
$R_t$ a reward. With invariant emission matrix $E_{os}=P(O_t=o\mid S_t=s)$,
logger $q_s(a)=P(A_t=a\mid S_t=s)$ and transition $T_a$, the observed
unnormalized two-signal/action matrix is
\[
 J_a(o,o')=\sum_s\mu_s q_s(a)E_{os}T_a(s,s')E_{o's'}.
\]
Writing $M_a=E\operatorname{diag}(\mu\odot q(a))T_aE'$ and a left inverse
$L$ of $E$ gives
\[
 L M_a L'=\operatorname{diag}(\mu\odot q(a))T_a.
\]
The action-conditioned matrix must not be normalized as if the logger were
randomized; doing so removes the state selection factor and changes the
experiment. If the signal matrix is full column rank and every state-action
mass is positive, the row sums recover $\mu\odot q(a)$, and division recovers
$T_a$. Near rank deficiency, the two inversions amplify error. This is a
sufficient finite route, not a generic POMDP identification theorem.

The validation sample in the new construction supplies labelled pairs
$(S,O)$ from an independently audited measurement process. Its emission
likelihood is included jointly with the trajectory likelihood. The observed
trajectory score differentiates the forward likelihood, including logger,
transition and reward factors; it is checked against exhaustive hidden-path
enumeration. The fixed-policy value is then a functional of the fitted model,
with the policy held fixed while refitting. Information-based and independent
two-sample sandwich uncertainty are reported separately.

The 80-repetition experiment used 600 trajectories and either 90 or 600
validation labels. With 90 labels, 85% of model-valid fits covered the true
fixed-policy contrast; with 600 labels, coverage was 87.5%. Several fits hit
the parameter boundary, so they are retained as unavailable rather than
silently contributing a narrow interval. Omitting the private logger produced
bias of about -36.5 to -41.1 currency units. The result demonstrates why a
predictive hidden-state fit cannot substitute for an action assignment model.

Proximal dynamic-regime identification is a substantive alternative when
two proxy processes satisfy sequential latent randomization and completeness.
The bridge equations identify the joint counterfactual law needed for a
dynamic regime; they are stronger than saying that two proxies predict
repayment \citep{zhangprox2024}. When rewards are missing not at random, a
next-state shadow variable can support a bridge only under exclusion,
relevance, positivity and completeness. The resulting conditional-moment
problem is an NPIV problem with its own regularization and error budget
\citep{wei2026}. The product therefore returns a proxy/MNAR query as
identified only when those declarations and diagnostics are supplied.

\section{Discovery must preserve unresolved structure}
\label{s5:discovery}

PCMCI+ and LPCMCI are useful temporal search operations, but they return
different endpoint semantics under different conditional-independence and
latent-confounding assumptions. The inspected temporal latent-confounder
route assumes a stationary SVAR, causal Markov and faithfulness; its output is
a dynamic PAG, not a DAG with missing arrows \citep{rohekar2023}. The installed
Tigramite 5.2.10.1 operations retain the graph, $p$- and value-matrices, lag
window, test family and search limits. A circle or bidirected endpoint is
carried into the next operation rather than coerced to a directed edge.

The generated six-variable financial mechanism includes contemporaneous
effects, two lags and a hidden common cause. In the sufficient case, PCMCI+
and LPCMCI achieved descriptive adjacency recall one, with precision near
.93. In the hidden case they retained unresolved marks and precision fell to
.88 and .82. When the second lag was omitted from the search, full-window
recall fell to .929 even though the reported in-window recall was one. These
are diagnostics of the declared generator, not graph-confidence probabilities.
The effect query is therefore conditional on a supplied contemporaneous order
and lag model; under hidden common causes it returns an unresolved status.

An intervention answer over a discovered graph is the set
\[
 \Theta(P,\mathcal G)=\{\theta(M):M\in\mathcal M,
   \mathcal K(M)=P,\ G(M)\in\mathcal G\}.
\]
Graph stability is evidence about a search procedure, not a confidence level
for this set. A useful product query either returns an effect set with its
model class or states which additional measurement, order restriction or
intervention would reduce it.

\section{Observation generation and linkage uncertainty}
\label{s5:observation-generation}

The finite 32-plan grammar in Chapter~\ref{s4:organization-algorithm} is a
valid conditional operation but not a complete account of automatic data
organization. The new profiler extracts candidate clocks, entity fields,
version columns, value columns and key combinations from schema types and
decision-unit membership. It then materializes each candidate with strict
availability and revision order. A candidate outside the key-size bound is
reported as unsupported. The system asks an analyst a question only when the
answer can reduce the remaining decision-relevant effect spread; a name score
never supplies the answer.

Renamed and reordered fixtures retained the oracle in all 80 repetitions. A
single question resolved the stock and flow cases; two resolved a value-role
ambiguity. The three-field-key fixture returned incompatible rather than
inventing a key. This is useful behavior precisely because the last case is a
failure certificate for the current grammar.

Linkage is another causal measurement process. If $M$ indicates a mismatch,
an audit-corrected score has the schematic form
\[
 \widehat\tau_{\rm corr}=\widehat\tau_{\rm naive}/(1-\widehat\alpha),
\]
only under a mismatch model in which the replacement record is independent
of the treatment/outcome conditional on the observed covariates and the audit
sample is representative and truthful. Those assumptions match the linkage
analysis of \citet{slawski2025}; they are not properties of a fuzzy join.
In 400 generated linkage samples the naive interval never covered, while the
joint audit correction covered .955. Treating a biased audit rate as if it
were representative covered .015. The product must retain match alternatives,
audit provenance and the linkage variance instead of exporting one hard ID.

\section{Reusable scientific objects and a public financial check}
\label{s5:composition}

The common layer now has a small executable composition. An
`AdjustmentQuery` records action, outcome, controls, population unit, units
and sampling law. A supplied DAG yields an adjustment trace; cross-fitting
returns out-of-fold nuisance predictions, a score and overlap diagnostics.
The same score is passed to the installed DoubleML IRM 0.11.4 operation with
identical folds and predictions. Coefficients and standard errors agree to
less than $5\times10^{-13}$ in the matched checks. This validates algebraic
reuse for this operation; it does not qualify every DoubleML model or a panel
split.

The public 401(k) extract has 9,915 complete rows after declared missingness
removal. Eligibility for participation is one target; actual participation
instrumented by eligibility is another. With the stated controls, three folds
and three fixed seeds, the eligibility ATE ranged from 7,968 to 8,932 archived
dollars across the two learner families. The participation LATE ranged from
11,879 to 12,947 dollars per complier. These are a reproducibility check of
the design, not a new causal claim: the extract's survey design, instrument
interpretation, inflation and transport to a bank require separate work.

This composition also exposes a practical limit. The same result type can
carry an estimate and covariance, but cannot make an iid covariance valid for
calendar shocks, certify the DAG, or turn a historical eligibility variable
into a bank offer. Those declarations remain in the request and in the
result's evidence state.

\section{Decisions and remaining boundaries}
\label{s5:scope-decisions}

The completed comparisons support five product decisions. First, response
operations expose direct HAC, a longer-window challenger and weak-instrument
sets, with no automatic near-unit certification. Second, system GMM is an
explicit challenger requiring initial-condition and instrument diagnostics.
Third, hidden-state value estimation requires an assignment/measurement
factorization or a declared proxy bridge; a predictive filter alone is
insufficient. Fourth, discovery output is a PAG/structural set with an
unresolved effect status when latent or omitted structure matters. Fifth,
organization proposes and tests observation plans but abstains when schema
evidence does not cover the required interpretation.

These decisions close the seven review gaps at proposal scope without claiming
unrestricted solutions. Uniform inference at arbitrary unit roots, general
latent POMDP identification, confidence regions for arbitrary large PAGs,
semantic recovery outside the candidate grammar, independent bank validation,
and production distributed deployment remain explicitly unsupported. The
proposal is stronger because each boundary follows from a target, an
assumption, a comparison and an observed failure.
