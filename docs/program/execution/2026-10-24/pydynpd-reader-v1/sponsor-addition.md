## Dynamic panels require a moment and calendar contract

The pydynpd review supplies a concrete Python candidate for restricted linear dynamic-panel analysis. The genuine 2023 original describes difference/system GMM and automatic model search; its historical benchmarks do not establish current commercial parity. Primary source inspection identifies an ordering contract in panel construction, categorical time spacing, distinct estimator/diagnostic steps, pseudoinverse and nominal instrument-count conventions, skipped search exceptions and an iterative loop without an explicit cap. These are source-level findings, not observed native failures. The current disposition is reference-only, with a conditional original-worker route after qualification. \citep{exec24_pydynpd_paper,exec24_pydynpd_source}

The scientific contract comes first. Lag moments require justified orthogonality, system moments add initial-condition restrictions, and high persistence can weaken instruments. A correctly solved invalid moment can return coefficient two when the structural coefficient is one. Repeated regressors can identify only their sum. A common shock replicated across one hundred accounts retains variance one although diagonal-only accounting suggests 1/100. These exact examples explain why rank, independence and nonrejected diagnostics must not be inferred from a regression table. \citep{arellano1991,blundellbond2023,exec24_pydynpd_reference}

The proposed library/CLI/MCP worker binds immutable data, a regular calendar and sorted keys, explicit instruments, covariance and diagnostic steps, the entire candidate-search ledger, and bounded termination. Generated/public panels should test row-permutation invariance, gaps, invalid/weak moments, missingness, common shocks and failed searches before bank trials. A targeted repair is preferable to an unqualified wholesale port; direct MIT metadata does not resolve the full environment or rights. Source version declarations disagree, so the exact commit remains the review identity. \citep{exec24_pydynpd_source}

Economic interpretation also needs a declared regime. These are authored examples and motivate a matched study, not observed bank returns. \citep{exec24_pydynpd_reference}
