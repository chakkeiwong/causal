Chapter 30

A shared service for economic
analysis

A banking study connects a feasible action to its financial consequence through a defensible
account of customer behaviour. The underlying event records have changing identities and
reporting delays. An economist supplies restrictions on assignment or shocks; a learner
estimates conditional distributions; a validator needs a sampling argument; a decision maker
needs the value of a feasible policy. A shared product is useful when it preserves the meaning
of the question across those transformations.
   The proposed product is a library and a standalone analytical workspace built around the
same definitions of populations, interventions and outcomes. Its first release should expose
a bounded collection of well-defined operations. More general discovery, inverse problems
and latent control remain important research capabilities, but their outputs must retain the
assumptions and unresolved alternatives developed in the preceding chapters. The construction
below derives this boundary; it is a specification, not a claim that the entire product already
exists.


30.1     Define the study before choosing an estimator
Write a request as
                                   Q = (M, K, g, ν, θ, E, F),                             (30.1)
where M is a class of causal models, K the observation operator, g the intervention, ν the target
population, θ the requested functional, E the sampling experiment and F the information
available to the decision rule. For a model M ∈ M, the observed law is P = K(M ) and the
causal answer is θ(M ; g, ν). The observation operator includes selection, measurement, clocks,
aggregation and record linkage when relevant. The sampling experiment specifies independent
households, randomized branches, a dependent calendar series, or another explicit design.
   For deposit retention, the request might ask for the difference in six-month discounted net
funding contribution under two invitation rules for households eligible on a specified date.
It must distinguish invitation from receipt, and baseline eligible households from eventual
activators. If the action is instead an increase in offered interest, both customer balances and
the price paid on existing balances change. These are different interventions and financial
functionals even when the same table is used.

30.2     Identification returns a mathematical object or an explicit
         limit
Define the identified set

                         ΘQ (P ) = {θ(M ; g, ν) : M ∈ M, K(M ) = P }.

An identification routine receives the model and observation declarations; it does not receive
permission to add whichever assumptions make a preferred estimator available. A singleton
set can be represented by an observed-law functional Ψ(P ). A non-singleton set may admit
bounds, a union of functionals, or only a nonidentification witness. The useful result types are
therefore distinct.
Identified expression.
An expression for Ψ together with the assumptions and transformations that derive it. Numer-
ical evaluation may still be unsupported.
Identified set or outer bound.
A set with its model class, support, restrictions and sharpness status. A conservative outer
bound is useful, but must not be labelled sharp without a compatible-model construction.
Nonidentification witness.
Two allowed models with the same observed law and different targets, or a valid source-specific
failure certificate such as a hedge in the applicable ID model.
Unsupported query.
The registered algorithm does not handle the graph class, intervention, observation process or
requested target. This makes no claim that the target is unidentified.
Incompatible specification.
The declarations are logically inconsistent or violate a deterministic support/schema require-
ment. A statistical model-check rejection should separately carry its test and error level.
   These types follow from Chapter 6 and the ID/IDC construction of Shpitser and Pearl
(2006). Failure to find a back-door set is not a nonidentification certificate. Nor is failure
of an optimizer. For example, the front-door expression is identified under its restrictions,
but a backend implementing only covariate adjustment should return an identified expression
with no available estimator. Conversely, the two invitation models of Section 7.3 agree on

   The companion scientific protocol gives this separation a concrete design form. A request
binds the model, observation, target and sampling specifications by content hash. A result
separately records computational completion, identification and inferential availability. A scalar
confidence set is an ordered union of intervals, including empty, disconnected and unbounded
sets; posterior credible sets have a different type. Derivations, bounds and failure witnesses
have distinct artifact roles. The supplied schema fixtures check these representation invariants.
They do not execute an identification algorithm or establish coverage. The following versioned
extension supplies explicit vector, curve and graph-set types for the bounded constructions
developed in this monograph. A general graph probability distribution remains a different
object.


30.4      A bounded mathematical language and a concrete lower-
          ing rule
The specification must be stronger than a collection of file hashes. A variable declaration
contains its name, support, physical unit, causal role and clock. A population declaration
identifies the economic unit and the role of its weights. An observation declaration identifies the
immutable view, revision relation, availability convention and sampling frequency. The model
specifies a structural class and an assumption set. A target specifies the action, outcomes, fixed
horizons or policies, normalization and population. These declarations together determine a
statistical experiment; no single field can substitute for the others.
  For finite mechanisms an executable expression grammar is particularly transparent. A
mass kernel has ordered parent variables U , ordered child variables V , declared finite supports
and an array of shape |XU | × |XV |. Every parent slice must be nonnegative and sum to one
over the child axes. A generative product j Kj (xj | xpa(j) ) requires one mechanism per child
                                          Q

and a topological order. Marginalization sums named random axes; substitution fixes an axis
without summing it. Replacing an action mechanism by g(a | h) requires that its information
variables precede the action. This operation evaluates the supplied causal factorization; it
does not establish that an arbitrary observed kernel can be intervened upon.
   The local finite-mass interpreter implements product, marginalization and mechanism
replacement, checking support, normalization and information order. For a binary confounded
example it recovers the interventional mean 0.5 and rejects a policy using a future outcome. A
general expression language would additionally need density measures, positive-denominator
restrictions and numerical integration error. The initial lowering rules do not pretend that
this finite interpreter implements continuous ID or general latent identification. Instead they
recognize the complete constructions already derived and reject mismatched target/sampling
combinations.

30.4.1    Results that can carry the actual uncertainty

Protocol version 0.4 supplements the historical scalar protocol. A scalar is a one-coordinate
result; vectors and response curves have named ordered axes and one physical unit per flattened
coordinate. A response curve orders outcome before horizon. Its covariance has that same
coordinate order and already includes the sample-size divisor. A confidence object carries its
method, level, coverage target and an interval union for each coordinate. For a simultaneous
rectangular set, the Cartesian product of these unions is the confidence region. Empty,
disconnected and unbounded coordinate sets remain representable; an unbounded endpoint is
a typed null, not a JSON infinity or a large arbitrary number. Pointwise coverage cannot be
relabeled simultaneous by an adapter.
   A graph result retains ordered nodes and its graph class. A DAG set stores directed binary
adjacency matrices. CPDAG/PAG endpoint matrices use zero for absence, one for a tail, two
for an arrowhead and three for a circle; the latter is permitted only for a PAG. Basic endpoint
validation is not a complete PAG-recognition algorithm. The temporal reference instead stores
lower and upper coefficient matrices with edge marks “present” or “zero or nonzero”. Its graph
class is not a posterior distribution over graphs. The effect union is explicitly an outer set,
and uncertainty about the causal-sufficiency assumption is not encoded as its confidence level.
   Computational completion, conditional identification and inference have separate status
fields. Numerical error bounds, when available, use the same output units. A null numerical
bound means no certified bound was provided; it must not be interpreted as zero error. Solver
diagnostics and all requested failed repetitions remain attached evidence. Hashes bind those
arrays and declarations to a computation, while scientific validation determines whether their
asserted premises are plausible.

30.4.2    Allowed conversions and explicit rejection cases

For a fixed known diagonal scale matrix S, the converted effect and covariance are S θb and S Vb S ′ .
Interval endpoints reverse when a scale is negative; the order of disconnected components
also reverses. A rate denominator changing from percentage points to basis points divides a
normalized response by 100. A fixed currency multiplier can be applied in the same way. A
time-varying exchange rate generally changes the reward functional and requires rebuilding it;
a monthly stock cannot be converted into daily flows by relabeling an axis.
   Population weights, inverse behavior probabilities, inverse observation probabilities and
bootstrap frequencies have different mathematical roles. The request validator rejects their
substitution. It also rejects an iid row experiment for the dependent response route, a split
inside a purportedly independent trajectory, inclusive availability when the request requires
strict predecision information, and a learned policy supplied to fixed-policy inference. A
valid clock conversion can preserve timestamp/sequence order exactly; changing that order
requires a new observation analysis. These checks enforce declared meaning, not the truth of
exchangeability or instrument exclusion.


30.5      Concrete sibling boundaries and the standalone study
Read-only inspection of the actual sibling APIs changes the integration design. FinMarketing’s
CausalData.from_record requires pretreatment features, mature outcomes, binary actions
and independent groups. Its FeatureSnapshot orders availability, cutoff and decision by
timestamp/sequence pairs. The local mapping preserves those pairs, horizon and dependence
unit. It does not coerce a dynamic curve into FinMarketing’s scalar Estimate; that requires a
richer result consumer. A causal response to a macro shock also cannot inherit its independent-
group standard error merely because the source rows are account records.
   dataml’s LineageGraph.append accepts run, dataset, partition and metadata, while its cor-
rection schema requires a resolvable assumption and a numerical confidence field. Observation
candidates therefore map to lineage metadata with their assumptions and alternatives. An
uncalibrated hint score does not populate correction confidence, and ambiguous candidates do
not generate an automatic correction. This preserves dataml’s distinction between an original
source and an assumed overlay.
   BayesFilter’s SSMStaticShape, SSMDataSignature and BayesianSSMProblem can record
dimensions, data/mask identities and parameter coordinates. For the binary controlled family,
the local mapping adds action identity, randomized-logger semantics, reward, finite state
support and the full observation order to its model manifest. A generic Bayesian SSM
descriptor alone does not supply those causal meanings or a controlled categorical filter
implementation. Its numerical target and derivative still need to refer to the same likelihood,
as required by Chapter 12.
   Two MacroFinance records distinguish the measurement roles: DirectedPairMeasurement
and ObservationContract. They distinguish directed quote pairs, maturity, observed rows
and derived diagnostics. MacroFinance’s convention is xm = −χ; reversing a pair reverses
the quoted wedge sign. The local mapping preserves these identities and requires a separate
causal role for the measured variable. A triangle diagnostic is not automatically an identified
structural cycle intervention. The inspected MacroFinance BayesFilter facade also references
a different import root from the workspace checkout, so inspection alone cannot establish
runtime compatibility between them.

When a stationary mixing argument is invoked, its mixing and moment restrictions must be

restrictions and decision adequacy. A converged point estimate can coexist with unsupported
uncertainty. A wide but valid confidence set can be adequate for a decision if every contained
model favors the same affordable action.

30.9     Discovery should improve a decision-relevant model class
The discovery workspace begins with mechanisms and timing. It proposes variables and
lags from the available data, records business restrictions, and returns a DAG, CPDAG,
PAG or named functional-model object with its endpoint semantics intact. Metadata lineage
is displayed separately from causal ancestry. An upstream database dependency does not
establish an economic intervention edge.
  The next operation asks a specific causal query across compatible models. For the chain
example of Section 20.9, the observed Gaussian covariance and CPDAG imply effects {0, 1/4}
within the declared linear causally sufficient class. The product should expose both values,
explain the orientations that produce them, and show which credible timing information
or intervention would distinguish them. Returning the effect of one arbitrarily oriented
representative would turn an unresolved scientific question into a spurious estimate.
   General PAG completion is expensive and may still leave latent nonidentification within
each completion. The first bounded route should therefore use verified generalized-adjustment
criteria on supported graph classes (Perković et al., 2018), small enumerated classes for exact
examples, and unresolved results beyond those capabilities. Search limits, CI test assumptions,
variable screening and lag windows must be retained. A timeout is computationally unresolved,
not a certificate that no adjustment set exists.
   A useful experiment recommendation should name the ambiguity it resolves. For deposit
pricing, a randomized encouragement may distinguish a price response from latent liquidity
selection. For collections, a new predecision signal may improve a bridge’s measurement rank.
For treasury, a higher-frequency observation may remove an aliasing ambiguity. Their cost and
decision value can be compared; edge-count improvement alone is not the economic objective.


30.10      Acceptance is a collection of scientific obligations
Each registered operation needs a reviewable construction dossier. It contains the exact target
and restrictions, an identification derivation or witness, an estimator/inference derivation, the
qualified backend version and transformation, and reproducible positive and negative examples.
Exact finite laws should be used when they expose semantic errors that a large benchmark
can conceal.
  For adjustment, compare the estimator’s population expression with explicit intervention
enumeration, and include a confounded pair of models that observation cannot distinguish. For
IV, hold the observed law fixed while changing the unobserved outcomes of never-takers; ensure
that the requested ATE remains unresolved although LATE is returned. For longitudinal
analysis, verify the recursive score under feedback, then change the policy’s dependence on the
propensity and require the corresponding gradient change.
  For controlled states, enumerate hidden paths to check the likelihood, filter and sufficient
30.11. Executable composition across distinct scientific questions                            259


counts with and without the logger; compare interventional value against independent dynamic
programming. For discovery, round-trip all endpoint marks and propagate every supported
completion into its effect set. For data organization, delay and revise events across the decision
boundary and verify exactly which history the policy receives. For distributed inference,
repartition the same scores across workers and preserve folds, cluster sums and accepted
uncertainty sets.
   Repeated-sample experiments then assess bias, coverage or policy regret under named laws,
including failures of support, rank, stationarity and model specification. Their purpose is to
test the implemented construction and characterize its operating range. They do not prove
that the bank’s unobserved confounding assumption holds. Bank data validation subsequently
evaluates the actual information, process and financial account.
   This division makes a staged product possible without shrinking its scientific ambition. A
bank can use rigorous observed-history analyses while researchers develop controlled latent and
discovery-to-effect routes in the same representations. Promotion of a method then requires its
missing mathematical and empirical obligations to be discharged, rather than a new wrapper
around an attractive estimator name.
   The implemented examples that follow test this proposed composition at three levels. A
static adjustment query shares scores with an invitation ratio. A dynamic funding study carries
clocks, linkage uncertainty and financial units through two consumers. The final comparison
preserves uncertainty and refusal across distinct scientific families. Each provides evidence for
specific shared operations within the wider specification.

30.11.1     A substantive package adapter with a matched reference

DoubleML 0.11.4 provides an installed IRM ATE operation (Bach et al., 2022; doubleml
contributors, 2026). The adapter supplies the exact same folds and nuisance predictions as the
independent score calculation, uses unnormalized inverse weights and propensity truncation at
.01, and checks the resulting estimate and standard error. Across the six public learner/split
runs and the second bank-contact example, coefficient discrepancy is zero and standard-error
discrepancy is at most 4.55 × 10−13 . This is a meaningful test of target, weight and covariance
semantics under matched inputs. Comparing independently fitted learners would conflate
those semantics with nuisance variation; the public study reports that second comparison
separately.

bleML supplies the qualified static semiparametric operation, while Tigramite supplies qualified
temporal search. The shared layer adds observation clocks, identification traces, preserved
result states and comparisons across libraries. Controlled likelihoods, dependence-sensitive
moments and generated observation plans still need explicit reference constructions because a
generic estimator interface does not supply their assumptions. Unrestricted PAG identification,

test is not distribution clearance. The standalone design exposes these operations through
explicit requests and preserved artifacts. Sibling integrations map existing domain fields to
those requests, with adapters tested here; accepted execution inside the sibling repositories
remains separate work.

30.12.1     A financial set is a different public result

The same public operation accepts the projection procedure in Section 21.12. Its result carries
a finite list of interval components, confidence level and the separate audit/response coverage
conditions. A missing lower or upper endpoint explicitly means an infinite endpoint; it never
means a missing estimate to be filled by zero. The treasury view shows the monetary set,
while the application view divides each component by the fixed positive account count. A
disconnected set remains disconnected after this transformation. There is no invented point
estimate or symmetric standard error when the information only supports a set.
   The regular and projection procedures are selected explicitly, and the procedure, confidence
level, fixed error allocation and sum/difference coordinates enter the scientific identity. A
caller cannot choose the narrower interval after inspecting both and inherit either procedure’s
coverage claim. Displaying them as sensitivity comparisons is different from a prespecified
inferential choice. The candidate-coordinate projection is retained as a mathematical challenger,
not an automatic width selector.
   The input domain is part of the calculation. The public operation checks finite observations
and economic quantities, complete response dimensions, the declared driver/stratum basis,
full pilot-training rank, both evaluation strata, residual price variation, binary calibration
records and an integer bandwidth below the calendar length. Conflicting copies of the same
outcome revision and nonfinite extraction clocks are rejected. Regular inverse-map inference
also needs audit records in both strata. Projection can instead use [0, 1] for an unaudited
stratum, often producing an uninformative set. An unsupported variance or nonfinite financial
result cannot carry a successful computed status. These checks verify numerical and structural
compatibility; they do not establish the source owner’s truth, pilot independence, the causal
innovation restriction or audit representativeness.
   An executed adversarial case removes all audits from one stratum. The regular operation
rejects the missing calibration, while projection returns the appropriate set under its larger
probability region. Other retained cases change the inference procedure and confidence level,
remove an evaluation stratum, introduce nonbinary audit labels, break the pilot basis, and
supply conflicting revisions. Their failures identify the particular information or procedure
that must change before a result can be requested again.

The preceding constructions support reuse because they expose compatible scientific meanings,
not because every problem is converted into the same regression. A request declares its
population, intervention normalization, unit, information clock, sampling law, inference method
and fixed monetary scale. The persistent-funding request invokes the independent-pair audited
moment. The servicing request invokes the identified nonlinear model and a policy fixed

independently of evaluation. The discovery request retains its latent class or its separately
justified encouragement restriction.
   Each result distinguishes a numeric calculation from its uncertainty claim. The inference
description contains the method, nominal level, finite-sample or asymptotic interpretation,
assumptions, numerical status and adverse empirical evidence. Both a treasury account and a
customer-facing application retain that description. The latter may divide a set by a fixed
external account count, preserving disconnected components and unbounded endpoints. An
estimated exposure denominator instead requires a joint influence argument.

consumers dropped the scope field even though the underlying calculation carried it. The
revised request makes the inference method explicit before fitting. The retained regular method
discloses its adverse calibration; neither it nor the projection is selected after comparing
favorable widths. Legacy callers preserve their original procedure while receiving the fuller
description. The change preserves the original numeric results and changes what their recipients
are told.
   The local examples execute two eligible requests within each family and send each result
to both consumers. The second funding request changes horizon and sample size; the second
servicing request changes the fixed observable policy; the second discovery request supplies
valid encouragement information. These are new scientific inputs and receive new identities.
The identity binds the request, data and implementation, but it does not certify the truth of a
supplied assumption.
   Incompatible requests fail at meaningful boundaries. A pair-level persistent map cannot
borrow a calendar HAC covariance. A current audited hidden state cannot become an input
to the deployed policy. An account-date cannot replace the independent customer unit. A
selected graph cannot inherit confidence from an outer structural union without carrying the
union’s information conditions. Wrong clocks, empty populations and unsupported inference
combinations are rejected. These refusals express missing scientific arguments and information,
not a generic software inconvenience.
   Direct expert composition can call the same qualified kernels and satisfy the same unit,
provenance, uncertainty and reporting obligations. The shared product is valuable only if
it helps preserve those obligations across repeated tasks. The local matched computation
compares identical public output bytes; it does not measure analyst savings or establish
acceptance by FinMarketing, BayesFilter, MacroFinance or dataml. Their inspected interfaces
remain concrete integration proposals until their owners qualify and adopt the mappings.
