Integrated master program for causal banking
Version 11.0 · 11 October 2026 · Literature cutoff: 7 October 2026
The full product is unfinished. We have a substantial author-reviewed research base, selected
executable constructions and reproducible negative results. We do not yet have a generally calibrated,
independently accepted, validated bank product. This program replaces a sequence of isolated
continuations with one scope, one dependency graph, one evidence register and explicit decisions
about each unresolved capability.
The goal is a common causal-analysis product for a universal bank, supported by a connected
mathematical monograph and an investment case. It must address four banking journeys: structural
funding responses; sequential servicing decisions; discovery followed by an intervention query; and
construction of trustworthy observations. Funding is one journey, not a substitute for the others.
The six original streams remain identification and learning; dependent time series and panels;
longitudinal and controlled-state decisions; discovery and uncertain observations; reusable operations
and computation; and financial validation and economics.

What the required standard means
For each claimed use, a reader must be able to follow the banking decision and information clock
through a causal model, assumptions, identification or an identified set, estimation and learning,
uncertainty and sensitivity, numerical error, and the monetary consequence. The implementation
must preserve this meaning, including ambiguity and refusal. A working optimizer or an API
response cannot substitute for identification or justified uncertainty.
The named FinMarketing monograph supplies a standard of motivated and developed argument,
explained notation, alternatives and substantive banking examples. Its length is not a target. The
existing reference comparison also preserves the earlier BayesFilter and CIP comparisons. Their
existence does not establish that an integration works here.
Inherited results, project derivations, synthetic evidence, hypotheses and verified implementation
must remain distinguishable. Every cited scholarly work requires its genuine locally saved PDF
and inspected primary technical anchors; all cited sources require identity, version/date, hash and
supported claims. Search coverage retains the client cutoff and recorded omissions. Financial
examples must reproduce. A deliverable must be compiled and visually inspected.
There are four separate gates:
  1. Author work completed: the question, argument, evidence and limitations have been
     reviewed and delivered. A negative finding can satisfy this gate.
  2. Capability qualified in a declared regime: identification, uncertainty, numerical behavior
     and useful output satisfy predeclared criteria for that target, information and sampling law.
     Passing file checks is insufficient.
  3. Authentic bank and operating acceptance: independent review, owners, assignment/audit
     truth, real data, interfaces and target-platform trials supply evidence that this workspace
     cannot manufacture.
  4. Investment and adoption: matched decision quality and actual total costs support a signed
     institutional decision. Local runtime is not an ROI estimate.
This program gives bounded acceptance criteria for the four journeys. The original ambitions
of general nonlinear inference, arbitrary latent graphs, arbitrary learners and universal semantic
reconstruction remain recorded as research obligations. Qualifying one family does not close those
unrestricted ambitions.

Eligibility permits independent work; no agents are spawned and no external contacts are authorized
by this plan. Bank/platform dependencies cannot be satisfied with author-generated fixtures. The
input contracts list all seven missing owner/input groups.
The exact calendar critical path cannot be honestly dated before derivation outcomes, pilot runtimes,
owner availability and data access are known. Work is scheduled by evidence dependencies. Each
research package has one reviewed candidate/design per attempt. Derivation and fixture work
precede final study registration. A failed candidate is retained, receives an explicit disposition, and
can lead to a reviewed new attempt. There is no indefinite unrecorded seed, bandwidth or audit-size
search.

Scientific execution rules
R01 starts from the linked score and separates fitted-pilot/ratio error from calendar variance.
Studentization or a changed independent pilot/evaluation design are candidates to justify, not known
successful cures. R02 addresses the missing joint sampling experiment. R03 begins with a declared
selected16 extension and a policy fixed independently of evaluation; unknown optimal-value inference
is a separate research claim. R04 begins with a finite stationary latent temporal class and an exact
small oracle. R05 begins with a specified sparse learner under an explicit dependence law. These

choices make work concrete without claiming universality.
Every attempt must review the target, observation law, clock, sampling unit, identification, uncer-
tainty statement, numerical error, information cost and substantive alternatives together. Primary
sources can supply inherited premises, not prove the project’s new composition. The capability
contracts and evaluation protocol define the detailed gates.
Before any final draw, freeze method/code/input identities, a fresh seed, all scenarios, repetitions,
the coverage/availability/width acceptance rule and a fixed stopping rule. Negative and unavailable
requests stay in the denominator. Old outcomes may motivate a new question; they cannot also
serve as its fresh qualification data. Do not widen intervals or change windows after inspecting final
results and call the original design successful.
For a finite minimum-coverage claim, an interval merely containing the nominal level is insufficient.
The protocol must state a justified inferential guarantee and its empirical qualification rule. A
default finite-grid screen uses simultaneous lower Monte Carlo bounds at the claimed minimum,
reports width and availability separately, and does not prove uniform validity. If only an asymptotic
statement is justified, label it conditional/asymptotic and leave finite qualification open. Bank
usefulness needs the bank’s own precision/loss criteria. No tolerance is silently lowered to pass a
study.
E01 can improve computation for the existing independent-origin procedure while its calibration
remains conditional. Dense equivalence and crash recovery do not improve its scientific status. Keep
process records in this program and in reviews; the product narrative should explain mathematics,
banking use, findings and limits.

Executing and resuming
Run from the repository root:
python3 tools/causal_program.py status
python3 tools/causal_program.py next
python3 tools/causal_program.py check
python3 tools/causal_program.py baseline
The first two report actual evidence-based disposition and ready work. The ordinary check is read-
only: it validates dependencies, evidence identities, sources, current PDF hashes and per-document
labels. The baseline command replays the original four nested delivery verifiers through preserved
historical controls. It is the full historical artifact check.
Use template PACKAGE to obtain the criterion receipt schema. A research protocol is frozen
with register PACKAGE --spec PATH. Complete work with record PACKAGE --receipt PATH,
recording pass, negative or inconclusive findings against every criterion and inspected evidence
hashes. External input uses intake INPUT --receipt PATH; an author’s report cannot satisfy
an external gate. The tool enforces evidence structure and identity; substantive correctness and
authenticity still require the named reviewer.
Failed or inconclusive work may be reopened with a reviewed revision using reopen PACKAGE
--revision PATH. The old contract, result and registration are preserved. Resolved downstream
work prevents a casual reopen and requires a reviewed program revision, so a changed premise
cannot silently invalidate an accepted conclusion. The revision must explain the obstruction, what
information/argument changes, and why another attempt is warranted.
State changes are atomic and retain prior snapshots. Continue eligible work without permission
checkpoints. A stream disconnection is a recovery event: read state, inspect saved journals and

Release and review policy
The program review challenges scope, evidence gates, failed-study branches, external dependencies
and the archive migration. It is author review, not an independent review. The current release
delivers an executable master and its preparation wave; it does not assert that R01–R05 or bank
qualification have already passed.
After each wave, integrate findings into this single program and its canonical state. Do not launch
an unrelated supplement as the default next action. A new scientific attempt must identify the gap
and work package it advances, retain prior failures, and satisfy the common acceptance rules. A
reviewed negative conclusion is progress; it remains an open capability gap.
Historical controls are available under the pre-master archive. The four scientific deliveries retain
their original bytes and manifests. Use the new canonical baseline command for their historical
verification; running an old verifier directly against the newly active state intentionally mixes different
versions.

Required result fields
Each scientific result must preserve the versioned target, units and financial weights; observed
information and availability clock; model/sampling/assignment/ audit assumptions; identification
status; fitted method and training selection; interval or set topology; uncertainty type and scope;
sensitivity/diagnostics; numerical tolerances and failures; provenance; and capability disposition.
The independent pilot belongs in input identity. Unknown truth cannot become a boolean assertion
merely because an input validator accepts its shape.
Separate statuses are required for: conditional point estimate; justified confidence set; whole-
line/disconnected ambiguity; unsupported scientific regime; unavailable numerical output; and
operational failure. A consumer must not change monetary units, discard qualifiers or relabel every
successful serialization as a supported causal answer.

Evidence at acceptance
Author work requires the connected derivation, primary-source/claim audit, independent reference
calculation, reproducible banking example, substantive challenger, adverse cases and reviewed PDF.
Capability qualification additionally requires the scoped scientific and empirical rules in the evaluation
protocol. Bank acceptance requires X01-X03; economic usefulness requires X04/F01.
No table or test count closes an unrestricted research question. The current gap rows in state.json
retain a remaining_generalization field even when all bounded closure tasks pass. The executable
tracker reports bounded acceptance separately from unrestricted_product_claim, which remains
false.
For D01 and D04, unchanged observations sometimes admit incompatible effects or meanings.
The program must either obtain new information, return the identified set, or explicitly propose a
changed target with the lost banking use explained. For D02/D03/D05/D06, a specified model/learner
extension is executable research; a promise covering arbitrary families is not an honest acceptance
contract.


Common scientific evaluation and resumption protocol
The master governs every new R01-R05 attempt. Existing final requests remain baseline evidence.
They are never relabeled fresh evaluation data. The protocols and journals already delivered stay
frozen.

Argument before draws
   1. State target, units, clock, observation law, assignment/audit law, sampling unit, model class
      and exact uncertainty claim. Define what extra information is required and who could supply
      it.
   2. Derive identification, influence/error terms, nuisance rates, dependence and numerical er-
      ror for that same experiment. Inspect relevant primary technical anchors; retain source
      identity/version/hash/claim records and unavailable-source omissions.
   3. Implement an independent small reference: enumeration, analytic moments, dense algebra,
      high-accuracy likelihood or certified structural bounds. Exercise misleading declarations and
      invalid/unavailable outputs.

   4. Review the derivation and reference before choosing a final procedure. Record a failure or
      unresolved theorem as such; no final study is required to disguise an unresolved identification
      problem.
   5. Freeze a protocol and all substantive input/code hashes using the canonical register command.
      The protocol must contain task_id, task_contract_sha256, target, model, observation_law,
      sampling_unit, method, assumptions, uncertainty_claim, alternatives, adverse_cases, metrics,
      acceptance_rules, replications, seed, stopping_rule, derivation_review and input_sha256. The
      derivation_review is a path/hash/inspected-anchor record.
The registered stopping_rule is fixed_design_no_outcome_stopping. Replications and seed are
integers. Compute repetition requirements from the exact binomial Monte Carlo precision and
multiplicity family before final draws. The program does not prescribe another arbitrary 600-request
study. Freeze a tolerated Monte Carlo error for the actual claim, justify the finite count and report
the realized intervals even when the target precision is missed.

Acceptance dimensions
For a claim of minimum finite coverage c (normally 0.95), require a scoped scientific argument
and a prespecified empirical rule. The default empirical screen requires a simultaneous one-sided
exact-binomial lower bound at least c in every claimed valid-law cell. State the multiplicity family
and confidence level before draws. A bound merely containing c, or conditional coverage among
the few finite outputs, is insufficient. This screen can be inconclusive for a procedure whose true
coverage is exactly nominal; such a result is not a proof of failure. It does not establish uniform
validity between grid cells.
An asymptotic argument can justify a conditional research output while finite qualification remains
open. Do not silently replace the finite claim with asymptotic wording and mark the original gap
closed. A predeclared scientific scope revision needs a separate reviewed attempt.
Report all-request coverage; conditional coverage; finite/disconnected/ whole-line/unavailable counts;
width and its distribution; point bias; reported SE versus empirical SD; sensitivity; actual labels
and history rows; complete runtime, peak memory, serialization and recovery; and information
acquisition assumptions. Report paired differences with their sampling uncertainty when methods
share requests. Keep oracle diagnostics separate from deployable methods.
Coverage alone does not establish a useful product. Whole-line answers can be correct and useless.
Availability and width criteria must be frozen for the declared research task. Bank width/loss/risk
thresholds and actual costs are external inputs, not values invented to obtain acceptance. No
precision tolerance is lowered after results. Calendar variance floors must appear in audit design and
economics.

Journals, failures and evidence
Before evaluation, write a launch record referencing the registered protocol hash and actual start time.
A final receipt references that registration, a hash-bound complete journal and explicit retention
of failed/unavailable requests. The engine rejects a final experiment whose stated start predates
registration. Clock/identity checks are auditable controls, not independent proof of when a person
ran a computation.
Use immutable request IDs from design/version/seed/cell/repetition, bounded batches and atomic
result publication. On disconnect, inspect the journal and resume missing IDs under the existing
registration. Never replace an old partial run, silently change a seed or count retries as fresh requests.
Reconstruct saved summaries from request rows before qualification.

Disposition and continuation

without fabricated results. Its scientific capability remains open.

fied_in_declared_scope, conditional_progress, unresolved or external_pending. A passing package
with conditional progress does not qualify that gap. Negative/inconclusive work cannot qualify
a gap. The final decision gate requires the explicit qualification dispositions from all required
predecessors, in addition to their package outcomes.

Intake format

manifest. Bank data stay in approved custody; no raw customer identifiers or confidential datasets

data. The script checks shape and identities, not signature authenticity, scientific truth or legal
