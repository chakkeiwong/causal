A     Event-study plots based on in-sample imputation
Another approach to constructing event-studies for imputation estimators, which is imple-
mented in the fect and did2s packages, reports averages of the form Yit ´ Ŷit for all relative
time periods r, where Ŷit is the same as for BJS described above. Li and Strezhnev (2025)
study this approach in detail, and argue that it systematically under-estimates the magni-
tude of the pre-trends. The issue stems from the fact that the pre-treatment outcomes for
treated units are used to form Ŷit , and thus Yit ´ Ŷit is an in-sample prediction error for
pre-treatment observations. When applied to the non-staggered setting, the results in Li
and Strezhnev (2025) imply that this in-sample approach yields pre-treatment coefficients
equal to NN0 β̂rBJS,new for r ă 0, where N0 is the number of untreated units. Thus, the pre-
treatment coefficients β̂rBJS,new are multiplied by the factor NN0 ă 1, which is very small when
most of the units are treated. Appendix Figure 1 illustrates this attenuation by plotting the
event-studies produced by fect and did2s in our simulated data: there is a clear jump in
the coefficients around the treatment date.
