## Additional execution review

1. **Audit selection is part of the estimator.** C02 uses pair-level known
   Bernoulli inclusion and actual focal action/outcomes. Inverse inclusion
   weights identify a population mean; an audited-only average changes the
   target under unequal inclusion. All dates in a pair stay together. Known
   label-error correction requires an externally supplied, identified error law.
2. **Projection must enclose its search.** For a positive-definite four-response
   covariance, fixed nonzero ranks give analytic ellipsoid extrema. Over a
   rectangle in reciprocal ranks, the upper support function is convex and the
   lower is concave, so extrema occur at corners. This supplies a global result
   without a local optimizer. When a rank interval touches zero, retain an
   explicit conservative whole-line fallback. It sacrifices precision, not
   coverage. Singular response covariance also receives a declared fallback.
3. **Old draws remain useful evidence.** Their saved means and full covariances
   permit a new projection comparison without regenerating the unchanged
   stochastic experiment. The new procedure is registered before evaluation;
   this is a retrospective comparison on retained draws, not fresh confirmatory
   validation. A fresh primitive-law simulation checks independent behavior.
4. **Nonlinear identification precedes filtering.** Full labelled trajectory
   audits identify transition, signal, logger, reward and initial parameters
   under full rank and support. Joint observed/audit inference must account for
   both samples. The exact Gaussian limit removes both transition nonlinearity
   and private logger dependence. A cheap approximation may fail; such failure
   is a legitimate comparison outcome, not a reason to hide the reference.
5. **Graph confidence retains the whole declared class.** Use finite Gaussian
   episodes and a simultaneous covariance region. Structural parameter sets
   may be enlarged conservatively to analytically tractable moment constraints.
   Record that relaxation; never describe it as sharp. Hidden confounding can
   yield the whole line. Randomized encouragement changes the identifying
   restrictions and must be declared before evaluation.
6. **Engineering preserves the sample.** Stream/checkpoint operations carry
   immutable data, fold and procedure identities; partial writes and duplicate
   contributions cannot change the statistical sample. Dependent sparse learning
   is a separate qualified family, not an automatic extension of pair linkage.
7. **Qualification requires evidence, not warnings alone.** Run targeted public
   output, numerical, adverse, restart and composition checks. Explain remaining
   calibration or identification failure in the manuscript and result object.

This review finds a feasible bounded route for the authorized work. It does not
predict useful precision, optimizer success, calibrated finite-sample coverage,
faster execution or adoption. Those are recorded outcomes. New substantive
failures will amend this record with the exact failed condition and continuation.
