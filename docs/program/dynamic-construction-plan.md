## Acceptance and design

The revision must connect a bank question, available information, identified
functional, statistical experiment, selected procedure, uncertainty, numerical
behavior and reusable result. It must explain substantive alternatives on the
same question. A new reader must be able to reconstruct that chain. Neither
additional pages nor passing receipt checks constitute this judgment.

Two economic dossiers anchor the work. **Funding** asks for the balance and
funding contribution response to a one-period price intervention, with common
calendar shocks, persistent balances, learned controls and fixed financial
aggregation. **Servicing** asks for the value of assistance under private
historical decisions and imperfect affordability measurements, including a
policy trained on one information set and evaluated on another independent
sample. Discovery and source reconstruction supply qualified information to
these dossiers; they are not independent demonstrations of convenient scores.

The existing broad foundations remain. New sections deepen selected routes
and repair nearby incomplete derivations. Unsupported generality is removed
only through an explicit mathematical and product decision. In particular,
nonlinear private-state fitting remains research unless a likelihood,
derivative and value approximation can actually be qualified. Exact Gaussian
and finite-state constructions remain part of the initial scientific scope.

## Funding experiment and inferential decisions

Use a fixed-weight branch aggregate observed on a monthly decision clock.
The price intervention acts at one origin; future assignment retains its
natural rule. The primary stationary construction allows an observed
persistent common driver, persistent balances, independent action innovations,
and common plus branch-specific outcome innovations. Nuisances condition on
pre-action balances and the common driver. The primary statistic counts dates,
not branch/date rows, as the increasing information dimension.

Nuisance family, penalties, horizon and covariance bandwidth are fixed using
an independent pilot or external protocol before the evaluation sample.
Derive the orthogonal score, pilot remainder and the calendar long-run
covariance of the complete response vector. State mixing, moment, overlap/
innovation and pilot-rate conditions. The fixed financial contrast uses the
full covariance. Compare to a direct implementation on identical inputs and
to an adverse calculation that ignores common information. Post-evaluation
break/model/bandwidth selection is outside that interval's claim; a changed
regime requires a new target and independent evaluation, or explicit selection
inference, rather than silently reusing it.

Complete the normalized panel ATT influence terms, including estimated cohort
shares and reused controls. For synthetic controls select a finite assignment
randomization test under an explicit sharp null; derive the exchangeability
and refitting requirements. Explain why it is not a generic confidence
procedure for one observationally chosen treated bank. Mixed frequency,
system GMM, external IV and synthetic control retain distinct targets and
information requirements in the method-choice dossier.

## Servicing experiment and inferential decisions

Preserve the V5 private-logger failures. Diagnose standardized errors,
availability, bias, empirical spread versus reported standard errors, boundary
directions and the consequence of increasing data. Do not infer a cause solely
from an information condition number or a larger number of starts. New
follow-ups retain every fit and use disjoint registered seeds.

The primary alternative acquires representative audited *trajectories* of
predecision affordability states, rather than only a small emission-label
sample. Define states operationally from contemporaneous verified information;
label error and auditing selection are additional measurement restrictions,
not facts established by simulation. Derive row-frequency/reward estimators
and whole-trajectory influence functions, preserving covariance among
initial law, emissions, transitions and rewards. The historical private
logger remains part of the observed experiment; intervention evaluation
removes it. Compare costs and efficiency with the existing hidden likelihood.

Train a finite-horizon signal-history belief policy on an independent pilot;
freeze its entire decision tree before evaluation. The policy cannot consult
audited evaluation states. Evaluate the fixed tree and contrast, with sampling
uncertainty for all fitted kernels. Label the resulting interval conditional
on training, rather than an interval for the unknown globally optimal value.
On the same mechanism show why observed-history ignorability fails under
private logging, what proxy exclusions/completeness would supply, and what
observing or randomizing the action-relevant information changes.

## Graphs, source uncertainty and composition

Choose an explicitly finite unrolled DAG and observed window. Marginalize all
latent and outside-window nodes required by that declared mechanism; derive
the maximal ancestral representation via inducing paths or an equivalent
oracle construction. Adjacency is scored against that target. Arrowheads
assert nonancestry, tails assert ancestry, and circles assert neither: score
soundness and informativeness separately. A generating MAG is not itself the
PAG of its equivalence class. Do not demand that an algorithm orient
unidentifiable endpoints. Search limits and finite-window boundaries remain
part of the evaluation.

Write source fixtures independently of the candidate generator, with signed
metadata, superseded documentation, joint ownership, publication clocks and
revisions. A unique feasible interpretation can still be wrong under false
metadata. Report such failures and useful abstention, not only success under
oracle answers. Linkage retains whole candidate maps and separately audited
calibration; fixed-map alternatives remain a set when probabilities are not
identified. Metadata questions use pilot information only. The final response
and financial interval include the evaluated mapping/calibration uncertainty
under stated assumptions.

The product has two public consumers: a treasury analyst and an application
builder. Both consume the same versioned observation, target, sampling,
calibration and inference objects. Cache identity includes those scientific
inputs. Replacing an independent-date assumption with a dependent one, or a
balance stock with a flow, must either recompute a justified result or reject
the request. No sibling edits or deployed workbench are prerequisites.

## Literature, computation and review controls

Read existing primaries before adding citations. Follow specific theoretical
decisions backward to their premises and forward to versions/corrections and
closest substantive challengers. Record queries, limits, inspected anchors
and why further searching would or would not change the choice. Unavailable
originals go in the omissions register; metadata never supports a theorem.
All new scholarly citations require genuine PDFs in the requested naming
format and full source/hash/anchor/claim records.

Register final experiments before running their random draws; use pilots for
numerical feasibility, preserve failed pilots, and record any design repair.
Use full per-replication records. Coverage uncertainty and unavailable fits
are reported; passing a threshold does not prove a theorem. Partition at the
statistical independence boundary, retain deterministic pilot/fold allocation,
and reduce raw sufficient contributions so serial and parallel computations
evaluate the same experiment. Never treat numerical parity as scale or
statistical validity by itself.

Work uses the current sandbox and narrowly registered public collector.
Existing authorization covers research, local edits and builds. No permission
rule expansion or new approval is needed merely to begin. An encountered
managed-policy boundary will be reported concretely. No agents are spawned.
Mission Control records exact continuation after each substantive checkpoint.
