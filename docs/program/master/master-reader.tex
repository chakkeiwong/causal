\hypertarget{integrated-master-program-for-causal-banking}{%
\section{Integrated master program for causal
banking}\label{integrated-master-program-for-causal-banking}}

Version 11.0 · 11 October 2026 · Literature cutoff: 7 October 2026

The full product is unfinished. We have a substantial author-reviewed
research base, selected executable constructions and reproducible
negative results. We do not yet have a generally calibrated,
independently accepted, validated bank product. This program replaces a
sequence of isolated continuations with one scope, one dependency graph,
one evidence register and explicit decisions about each unresolved
capability.

The goal is a common causal-analysis product for a universal bank,
supported by a connected mathematical monograph and an investment case.
It must address four banking journeys: structural funding responses;
sequential servicing decisions; discovery followed by an intervention
query; and construction of trustworthy observations. Funding is one
journey, not a substitute for the others. The six original streams
remain identification and learning; dependent time series and panels;
longitudinal and controlled-state decisions; discovery and uncertain
observations; reusable operations and computation; and financial
validation and economics.

\hypertarget{what-the-required-standard-means}{%
\subsection{What the required standard
means}\label{what-the-required-standard-means}}

For each claimed use, a reader must be able to follow the banking
decision and information clock through a causal model, assumptions,
identification or an identified set, estimation and learning,
uncertainty and sensitivity, numerical error, and the monetary
consequence. The implementation must preserve this meaning, including
ambiguity and refusal. A working optimizer or an API response cannot
substitute for identification or justified uncertainty.

The named FinMarketing monograph supplies a standard of motivated and
developed argument, explained notation, alternatives and substantive
banking examples. Its length is not a target. The existing
\href{../reviews/scholarship-reset-2026-10-08.md}{reference comparison}
also preserves the earlier BayesFilter and CIP comparisons. Their
existence does not establish that an integration works here.

Inherited results, project derivations, synthetic evidence, hypotheses
and verified implementation must remain distinguishable. Every cited
scholarly work requires its genuine locally saved PDF and inspected
primary technical anchors; all cited sources require identity,
version/date, hash and supported claims. Search coverage retains the
client cutoff and recorded omissions. Financial examples must reproduce.
A deliverable must be compiled and visually inspected.

There are four separate gates:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Author work completed:} the question, argument, evidence and
  limitations have been reviewed and delivered. A negative finding can
  satisfy this gate.
\item
  \textbf{Capability qualified in a declared regime:} identification,
  uncertainty, numerical behavior and useful output satisfy predeclared
  criteria for that target, information and sampling law. Passing file
  checks is insufficient.
\item
  \textbf{Authentic bank and operating acceptance:} independent review,
  owners, assignment/audit truth, real data, interfaces and
  target-platform trials supply evidence that this workspace cannot
  manufacture.
\item
  \textbf{Investment and adoption:} matched decision quality and actual
  total costs support a signed institutional decision. Local runtime is
  not an ROI estimate.
\end{enumerate}

This program gives bounded acceptance criteria for the four journeys.
The original ambitions of general nonlinear inference, arbitrary latent
graphs, arbitrary learners and universal semantic reconstruction remain
recorded as research obligations. Qualifying one family does not close
those unrestricted ambitions.

\hypertarget{reconciled-baseline}{%
\subsection{Reconciled baseline}\label{reconciled-baseline}}

The current research reader is
\href{../causal_foundations_with_linked_audits_2026-10-11.pdf}{259
pages}: the 245-page V10 monograph and three preserved supplements of 4,
4 and 6 pages. The \href{../sponsor_reconstruction.pdf}{16-page sponsor
proposal} is the V10 baseline; it has not been revised to claim the
later supplements or the new program.

The linked-outcome supplement supplies 4,800 fresh synthetic requests
under a specified independent-origin audit law. Both linked methods
returned finite intervals in every request. At exact rank, the augmented
method covered 559/600 requests, or 93.17\%; its individual Monte Carlo
interval {[}90.84\%, 95.05\%{]} includes 95\%. That does not establish
general calibration, or establish a general calibration defect. The
earlier V10 small-audit failures remain separate evidence.
Persistent-case median width is about USD 1.413 million. No supplied
bank precision threshold makes this useful by declaration.

False exogeneity, wrong inclusion probabilities and noisy labels
produced 148/600, 378/600 and 528/600 covers respectively in the linked
study. The ordinary linked design expects about 1,500 resolved origin
labels across pilot and evaluation; the low-audit design about 600. The
count comparator uses a different service of 3,200 correctness labels.
Prices and superiority are unknown. These are conditional synthetic
findings, not bank observations. Detailed anchors are in the
\href{../reviews/remaining-gaps-linked-funding-2026-10-11.md}{latest gap
review} and
\href{../reviews/linked-funding-author-review-2026-10-11.md}{author
review}.

Four original manifests and 7,609 unique artifact identities have been
verified through a preserved historical control overlay. The canonical
V10 tracker had missed all three supplements. Its global LaTeX-label
scan also reported a false collision between two separately compiled
documents and overwrote a frozen check receipt. The failure is retained;
the original 244-byte receipt was reconstructed only after matching its
existing SHA-256 exactly. Current checks use a namespace for each
compiled document and do not overwrite that historical evidence. No
research PDF or original manifest was rewritten.

\hypertarget{scientific-execution-rules}{%
\subsection{Scientific execution
rules}\label{scientific-execution-rules}}

R01 starts from the linked score and separates fitted-pilot/ratio error
from calendar variance. Studentization or a changed independent
pilot/evaluation design are candidates to justify, not known successful
cures. R02 addresses the missing joint sampling experiment. R03 begins
with a declared selected16 extension and a policy fixed independently of
evaluation; unknown optimal-value inference is a separate research
claim. R04 begins with a finite stationary latent temporal class and an
exact small oracle. R05 begins with a specified sparse learner under an
explicit dependence law. These choices make work concrete without
claiming universality.

Every attempt must review the target, observation law, clock, sampling
unit, identification, uncertainty statement, numerical error,
information cost and substantive alternatives together. Primary sources
can supply inherited premises, not prove the project's new composition.
The \href{master/capability-contracts.md}{capability contracts} and
\href{master/evaluation-protocol.md}{evaluation protocol} define the
detailed gates.

Before any final draw, freeze method/code/input identities, a fresh
seed, all scenarios, repetitions, the coverage/availability/width
acceptance rule and a fixed stopping rule. Negative and unavailable
requests stay in the denominator. Old outcomes may motivate a new
question; they cannot also serve as its fresh qualification data. Do not
widen intervals or change windows after inspecting final results and
call the original design successful.

For a finite minimum-coverage claim, an interval merely containing the
nominal level is insufficient. The protocol must state a justified
inferential guarantee and its empirical qualification rule. A default
finite-grid screen uses simultaneous lower Monte Carlo bounds at the
claimed minimum, reports width and availability separately, and does not
prove uniform validity. If only an asymptotic statement is justified,
label it conditional/asymptotic and leave finite qualification open.
Bank usefulness needs the bank's own precision/loss criteria. No
tolerance is silently lowered to pass a study.

E01 can improve computation for the existing independent-origin
procedure while its calibration remains conditional. Dense equivalence
and crash recovery do not improve its scientific status. Keep process
records in this program and in reviews; the product narrative should
explain mathematics, banking use, findings and limits.

\hypertarget{executing-and-resuming}{%
\subsection{Executing and resuming}\label{executing-and-resuming}}

Run from the repository root:

\begin{verbatim}
python3 tools/causal_program.py status
python3 tools/causal_program.py next
python3 tools/causal_program.py check
python3 tools/causal_program.py baseline
\end{verbatim}

The first two report actual evidence-based disposition and ready work.
The ordinary check is read-only: it validates dependencies, evidence
identities, sources, current PDF hashes and per-document labels. The
baseline command replays the original four nested delivery verifiers
through preserved historical controls. It is the full historical
artifact check.

Use \texttt{template\ PACKAGE} to obtain the criterion receipt schema. A
research protocol is frozen with
\texttt{register\ PACKAGE\ -\/-spec\ PATH}. Complete work with
\texttt{record\ PACKAGE\ -\/-receipt\ PATH}, recording pass, negative or
inconclusive findings against every criterion and inspected evidence
hashes. External input uses \texttt{intake\ INPUT\ -\/-receipt\ PATH};
an author's report cannot satisfy an external gate. The tool enforces
evidence structure and identity; substantive correctness and
authenticity still require the named reviewer.

Failed or inconclusive work may be reopened with a reviewed revision
using \texttt{reopen\ PACKAGE\ -\/-revision\ PATH}. The old contract,
result and registration are preserved. Resolved downstream work prevents
a casual reopen and requires a reviewed program revision, so a changed
premise cannot silently invalidate an accepted conclusion. The revision
must explain the obstruction, what information/argument changes, and why
another attempt is warranted.

State changes are atomic and retain prior snapshots. Continue eligible
work without permission checkpoints. A stream disconnection is a
recovery event: read state, inspect saved journals and resume the
existing registered attempt; never redraw silently. Real blockers are
failed/missing scientific premises or unavailable external evidence.
Report those precisely and continue other ready work.

\hypertarget{release-and-review-policy}{%
\subsection{Release and review policy}\label{release-and-review-policy}}

The \href{../reviews/master-program-review-2026-10-11.md}{program
review} challenges scope, evidence gates, failed-study branches,
external dependencies and the archive migration. It is author review,
not an independent review. The current release delivers an executable
master and its preparation wave; it does not assert that R01--R05 or
bank qualification have already passed.

After each wave, integrate findings into this single program and its
canonical state. Do not launch an unrelated supplement as the default
next action. A new scientific attempt must identify the gap and work
package it advances, retain prior failures, and satisfy the common
acceptance rules. A reviewed negative conclusion is progress; it remains
an open capability gap.

Historical controls are available under
\href{history/pre-master-2026-10-11/docs/program/master-program.md}{the
pre-master archive}. The four scientific deliveries retain their
original bytes and manifests. Use the new canonical baseline command for
their historical verification; running an old verifier directly against
the newly active state intentionally mixes different versions.

The master governs every new R01-R05 attempt. Existing final requests

\hypertarget{argument-before-draws}{%
\subsection{Argument before draws}\label{argument-before-draws}}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  State target, units, clock, observation law, assignment/audit law,
  sampling unit, model class and exact uncertainty claim. Define what
  extra information is required and who could supply it.
\item
  Derive identification, influence/error terms, nuisance rates,
  dependence and numerical error for that same experiment. Inspect
  relevant primary technical anchors; retain source
  identity/version/hash/claim records and unavailable-source omissions.
\item
  Implement an independent small reference: enumeration, analytic
  moments, dense algebra, high-accuracy likelihood or certified
  structural bounds. Exercise misleading declarations and
  invalid/unavailable outputs.
\item
  Review the derivation and reference before choosing a final procedure.
  Record a failure or unresolved theorem as such; no final study is
  required to disguise an unresolved identification problem.
\item
  Freeze a protocol and all substantive input/code hashes using the
  canonical register command. The protocol must contain task\_id,
  task\_contract\_sha256, target, model, observation\_law,
  sampling\_unit, method, assumptions, uncertainty\_claim, alternatives,
  adverse\_cases, metrics, acceptance\_rules, replications, seed,
  stopping\_rule, derivation\_review and input\_sha256. The
  derivation\_review is a path/hash/inspected-anchor record.
\end{enumerate}

The registered stopping\_rule is fixed\_design\_no\_outcome\_stopping.
Replications and seed are integers. Compute repetition requirements from
the exact binomial Monte Carlo precision and multiplicity family before
final draws. The program does not prescribe another arbitrary
600-request study. Freeze a tolerated Monte Carlo error for the actual
claim, justify the finite count and report the realized intervals even
when the target precision is missed.

\hypertarget{acceptance-dimensions}{%
\subsection{Acceptance dimensions}\label{acceptance-dimensions}}

For a claim of minimum finite coverage c (normally 0.95), require a
scoped scientific argument and a prespecified empirical rule. The
default empirical screen requires a simultaneous one-sided
exact-binomial lower bound at least c in every claimed valid-law cell.
State the multiplicity family and confidence level before draws. A bound
merely containing c, or conditional coverage among the few finite
outputs, is insufficient. This screen can be inconclusive for a
procedure whose true coverage is exactly nominal; such a result is not a
proof of failure. It does not establish uniform validity between grid
cells.

An asymptotic argument can justify a conditional research output while
finite qualification remains open. Do not silently replace the finite
claim with asymptotic wording and mark the original gap closed. A
predeclared scientific scope revision needs a separate reviewed attempt.

Report all-request coverage; conditional coverage; finite/disconnected/
whole-line/unavailable counts; width and its distribution; point bias;
reported SE versus empirical SD; sensitivity; actual labels and history
rows; complete runtime, peak memory, serialization and recovery; and
information acquisition assumptions. Report paired differences with
their sampling uncertainty when methods share requests. Keep oracle
diagnostics separate from deployable methods.

Coverage alone does not establish a useful product. Whole-line answers
can be correct and useless. Availability and width criteria must be
frozen for the declared research task. Bank width/loss/risk thresholds
and actual costs are external inputs, not values invented to obtain
acceptance. No precision tolerance is lowered after results. Calendar
variance floors must appear in audit design and economics.

\hypertarget{journals-failures-and-evidence}{%
\subsection{Journals, failures and
evidence}\label{journals-failures-and-evidence}}

Before evaluation, write a launch record referencing the registered
protocol hash and actual start time. A final receipt references that
registration, a hash-bound complete journal and explicit retention of
failed/unavailable requests. The engine rejects a final experiment whose
stated start predates registration. Clock/identity checks are auditable
controls, not independent proof of when a person ran a computation.

Use immutable request IDs from design/version/seed/cell/repetition,
bounded batches and atomic result publication. On disconnect, inspect
the journal and resume missing IDs under the existing registration.
Never replace an old partial run, silently change a seed or count
retries as fresh requests. Reconstruct saved summaries from request rows
before qualification.

Preserve implementation failures and their effect on qualification. If
code or inferential design changes after final draws begin, disposition
that attempt, retain its protocol/code/journals, and reopen a reviewed
successor with a fresh registration. Unchanged source-only reporting
corrections require substantive review; they do not automatically
require expensive simulations again.

\hypertarget{disposition-and-continuation}{%
\subsection{Disposition and
continuation}\label{disposition-and-continuation}}

Every work-package criterion receives pass, fail or inconclusive plus an
inspected path/hash/anchor and a substantive finding. A passing research
package requires a registered evaluation and all promised outputs. A
negative or inconclusive package may end at a reviewed analytical
obstruction without fabricated results. Its scientific capability
remains open.

Every non-control receipt also contains gap\_dispositions for all its
assigned gaps: qualified\_in\_declared\_scope, conditional\_progress,
unresolved or external\_pending. A passing package with conditional
progress does not qualify that gap. Negative/inconclusive work cannot
qualify a gap. The final decision gate requires the explicit
qualification dispositions from all required predecessors, in addition
to their package outcomes.

The engine retains the prior contract/result/registration on reopen, and
rejects reopening underneath resolved dependent work. A reviewed
whole-program revision is required in that case. Other ready branches
proceed without a permission checkpoint. Revision is warranted by a new
argument, corrected defect or changed information, not by disappointment
with an honest result.

A reopen revision contains task\_id, prior\_contract\_sha256, reason,
new\_information\_or\_argument, a hash-bound review, and new\_contract.
Retain the same task ID, kind and gap obligations, but use new versioned
output paths. Old inputs, journals and reviews remain immutable and are
still checked.
