     3.3.2     Results
         We show in Figures 3.5 to 3.8 the true adjacency matrices and prior distributions of
     Sachs, MAGIC-7, MAGIC-ORIGINAL and Summer respectively. The priors used in each
     experiment are represented as heatmaps, where each entry represents the probability of
     the respective edge. On the true adjacency matrices, the color green represents the edge
     exists, and the color dark-blue means the absence of the edge. As for the prior distribu-
     tions’ heatmaps, green describes a 99% probability and dark-blue a 1𝑒 − 10% probability.
         For each dataset (Sachs, MAGIC-7, MAGIC-ORIGINAL and Summer) and prior (GPT4o
     and DeepSeek) we run DiBS with 30 starting latent particles and 3000 iterations. We refer
     the reader to Section 1.3.3 for a summary of technical details on the framework.
