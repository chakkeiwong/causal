                                    mechanisms is imperative. This study used natural language processing (NLP) models to pre-select
                                    candidate risk factors for dementia from 5505 baseline variables in the UK Biobank. We then applied

                                    pronounced, its potential value in dementia management remains non-negligible. This study
                                    advances our understanding of dementia mechanisms and highlights the potential of NLP and
                                    machine learning for the causal discovery of complex diseases from high-dimensional data.
