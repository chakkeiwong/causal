# Pre-execution review of V6.1

9 October 2026. Author review, not independent review.

1. **An audit threshold is not identification-robust inference.** Do not call
   the Wilson-screened delta interval uniformly valid. Retain its actual
   conditional and all-request coverage; projection is a separate operation.
2. **Finite audit validity does not make HAC finite-sample exact.** Prove the
   inclusion first; state exactly which probability bound is asymptotic.
   Stable finite-dimensional candidate scores can remain regular when the
   mixing matrix loses rank, but the causal inverse does not.
3. **Do not manufacture precision by choosing p>1/2.** Preserve both sides
   unless a separately justified restriction supplies the sign. Reversed maps
   are a legitimate test case. At p=1/2 compatible means leave the component
   contrast unidentified.
4. **Set topology is scientific information.** Analytic extrema, infinities
   and Minkowski sums replace a finite parameter grid. Test singleton rank,
   touching/overlapping bands, separated rays and exact endpoints.
5. **Do not count stratum rows as independent dates.** Derive financial
   candidate contrasts from the complete date influence and HAC covariance.
   Bonferroni permits cross-stratum dependence; it does not erase it from
   each contrast's variance.
6. **Financial reduction must precede scalar uncertainty correctly.** Include
   all within-curve covariance and the cost of induced first-month balances.
   Margins, baseline and weights remain fixed; estimating them changes the
   experiment.
7. **Missing audit and missing response information are different.** With no
   audits, p ranges over [0,1]. With no evaluation stratum or no price
   variation, the selected response procedure is unsupported. Neither may
   become `computed` plus NaN. Validate finite arrays, dimensions, strata,
   binary audit labels, fixed basis and bandwidth before estimation.
8. **Calibration bias is not rank weakness.** Exact binomial coverage assumes
   representative accurate independent audits. This continuation does not
   solve selected, correlated or misclassified audits, persistent crosswalks,
   endogenous matching or semantic truth.
9. **Reuse must bind the inference procedure.** Changing delta to projection,
   confidence level or error allocation changes scientific identity. Consumers
   must preserve disconnected/unbounded sets under a fixed positive rescaling.
10. **Coverage alone can reward an uninformative procedure.** Report finite
    frequency, component count and finite width alongside coverage, including
    exactly singular regimes and unavailable regular results. Keep all draws.
11. **Source fidelity.** Existing weak-IV and sensitivity primaries motivate
    inversion/projection. They do not prove this new linkage procedure; its
    proof and implementation must stand on their displayed construction.
12. **Do not turn a focused repair into blanket certification.** The review
    closes the discovered connection and failure at their stated scope. It
    cannot establish all possible scholarship, user acceptance, bank validity
    or independent review. The stale V6 delivery-status sentence is corrected
    while preserving its historical original.

Decision: execute the plan with these restrictions. No new access or approval
is required for the local derivation, implementation, studies and build.
