## Causal-Copilot reference use and a bounded adoption experiment

The named Causal-Copilot repository, its README V1/V2 labels and linked demo
belong to one documented project lineage. Its April 2025 paper and May 2026
source snapshot remain distinct evidence: the later commit is not established
as the experiment release. The proposal retains the workflow architecture as
a reference and makes a concrete component decision. It does not admit an
installed adapter. Dialogue, selection and reporting offer useful comparators,
while graph ambiguity, edit authority, temporal semantics and truth-assisted
scoring require explicit contracts. [@exec17_copilot_paper; @exec17_copilot_code; @exec25_copilot_choice]

A bounded public/generated prototype would compare a fixed method, a frozen
upstream workflow and the proposed contract-wrapped workflow under matched
information. Generated structural models provide scientific oracles; public
observational data provide workflow stress cases without invented causal
truth. Tests cover variable and lag mapping, unknown versus absent edges,
protected edits, stale graph versions, provider failures, cancellation and
duplicate delivery. A truth-assisted compatibility score remains separately
named. Scientific thresholds and sample size must follow a selected decision.
No native trial is reported here. [@exec17_copilot_exact; @exec25_copilot_choice]

Two inspected MIT notices do not settle all dependencies, assets, data or
provider rights. Exact environment, current support, experiment binding and
maintenance ownership remain conditions for adoption. In a purely illustrative
USD 6,000 trial with 120 requests, 100 completions and 80 admissible results,
costs are USD 50, 60 and 75 respectively. Full workflow cost and scientific
admissibility must be compared before expensive bank experiments. The scoped
reference decision closes one reuse decision without granting native
capability or closing either generic agent umbrella. [@exec17_copilot_rights; @exec25_copilot_choice]
