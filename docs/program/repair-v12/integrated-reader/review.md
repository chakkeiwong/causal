# Review of the integrated working monograph and program refresh

12 October 2026. Author review: source integration and continued draft
development completed; full repair and release acceptance remain open.

## What changed and why

The current main manuscript incorporates the original foundations, all four
scientific supplements, five developed software chapters, eight LLM/agent
chapter drafts and four integration chapter drafts. It is one compiled book
with continuous chapter/equation numbering, a single contents sequence and one
bibliography. The 268-page I01 PDF, its components, all completed receipts and
their evidence remain byte-identical.

The new frontier chapter compares language as domain knowledge, direct causal
judgment, soft/hard search guidance, temporal assistance, interactive review and
workflow orchestration. Claims use already inspected primary sources; published
results are not represented as this project's experiments. The new economics
chapter derives a decision rule, the value of perfect and imperfect information,
review costs, a break-even threshold and the limits of using uncalibrated model
confidence. Its exact rational-arithmetic record has independent enumeration
and gain-minus-loss checks. Probabilities and costs are stipulated, not observed.

Four new integration chapters develop mathematical interchange, actual local
library/CLI/MCP use, dated workspace consumer designs, and operations. They
separate supplied-moment calculations from arbitrary estimation, unsupported
back-door attempts from general nonidentification, legacy MCP from modern
protocol support, local recovery from distributed guarantees, and inspected
consumer interfaces from executed adoption. The new monetary set example
preserves disconnected tails and reverses endpoints correctly under loss signs.

## Plan and implementation review

The main risk was bypassing V12 acceptance by delivering an integrated PDF.
The working reader, manifest and current program say that all three repairs
remain open. No package result, original D gap, prerequisite, page allocation,
source cutoff or acceptance criterion changed. The four integration drafts use
completed IN01/IN02/IN04 evidence and do not bypass IN03/IN05. The eight LLM
drafts cover the topic sequence, but do not satisfy the depth or review gate.

The current program view preserves the full V12 scope and adds current
dispositions, chapter progress and exact continuation. Its wrapper calls the
unchanged V12 controller for work/receipt validation and adds the whole-book
source checker. The historical bibliography and controller cannot be casually
extended because accepted evidence binds their hashes. The new audit verifies
every citation in the working book without rewriting that historical register.
Publication uses the existing locked writer, which snapshots the previous state
and preserves all earlier deliveries. A registered working PDF cannot be
overwritten by its builder; later changes require a versioned successor.

The chapter progress register maps every one of the 22 required allocations.
Several software allocations share a draft chapter; these mappings are not
additive page credit. The inherited software survey is excluded from the new
physical body totals. A substantive duplication and coverage review remains
necessary, especially where old and new software/interface material overlap.

## Evidence and verification

The book has 345 physical pages and 45 numbered chapters. Its new repair spans
are 18 software pages, 39 LLM/agent pages and 12 integration pages. Its audit
binds 228 cited records, TeX inputs, bibliography files, figures, project
constructions and explicit supplement transformations. Citation keys for new
software are namespaced; supplement labels are namespaced; frozen prose and
tables are integrated through recorded transformations.

The final LaTeX build has no undefined citations/references, duplicate labels,
missing-character warnings or overfull boxes. Two underfull paragraph notices
come from inherited material. Text extraction confirms the new chapter content,
key decision arithmetic, original negative findings and one bibliography.
All 345 pages contain text, and extracted word boxes lie within physical page
bounds. These checks do not establish visual quality or absence of collisions.

The source refresh detected four changed sibling records among the previous
53 inspected sources. Forty-nine still match, including the technical originals
used in new integration claims. Changed FinMarketing/MacroFinance worktree
instructions and MOONeural README/metadata are excluded from new technical
claims and recorded in workspace-source-drift.json. The earlier inventory is
preserved as a dated observation. No sibling files were changed or executed.

Build failures and repairs are retained in the execution notes: BibTeX's
relative-output protection, an overly broad bibliography record discriminator,
one long inherited funding display, and control characters in pdftotext's XML
representation of mathematical glyphs. The final text/bounds review records
the XML sanitization separately; it does not infer that rendered glyphs were
visually inspected.
