                --- REPORT STRUCTURE ---
               You will be given a report from experts of chronic lower back pain
               to help answer the given question. A report will consist of three
               types of relevant context:
               Knowledge Graph Communities: Each community will consist of a
               title, a summary, and a list of key findings.
               Chunks: Segments of text from the original source documents.
               Knowledge Graph Relationships: Facts extracted from the original
               source documents and organized into a relationship as apart of the
               Knowledge Graph. Each Relationship included will follow a knowledge
               graph triplet structure:
               (’entity id’: ’entity description’)--[’relationship type’:
               ’relationship description’]-->(’entity id’: ’entity description’)
               Note that the reports will be given in markdown format.
